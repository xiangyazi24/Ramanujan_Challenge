# 5. What class does the irreducible non-symmetric-square operator belong to?

The phrase “sporadic CMF” is not a canonical classification theorem in the CMF paper. At the present project state, the safest classification is:

Its irreducibility says there is no nontrivial rational submodule over \mathbb Q(n). The failure of a symmetric-square representation says only that this rank-three module is not obtained from a rank-two module by the functor \operatorname{Sym}^2, even after the allowed scalar twist.

It does not imply that the operator is outside all infinite CMF families. Rank-three generalized hypergeometric modules, parameter-contiguous modules, and two-measure multiple-orthogonal-polynomial modules all generate infinite CMF families. The question is whether L_{27} is rationally gauge-equivalent to one of them.

At present there is no such identification in the repository. Hence it is better to say
