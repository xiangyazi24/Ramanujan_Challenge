# 7. Audit conclusion

The proposed ingredients are sufficient, with two qualifications:

- “Fourier second moment” means mass plus the normalized second moment. The first and third moments are not needed because parity kills every odd polynomial term.

- The pole blocks require both Laplace transforms and a justified Fubini interchange. No higher pole order occurs for n ≤ 2 because F - F'/2 produces at most double poles.

This is therefore a finite exact normalization proof, independent of the recurrence and asymptotic machinery. Its only analytic infrastructure is the already established Fourier transform, standard exponential/Laplace integrability, one second-moment extraction, and one Fubini/geometric-series calculation.
