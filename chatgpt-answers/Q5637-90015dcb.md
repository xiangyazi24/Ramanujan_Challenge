## 5.2 Constant-term/diagonal representation

The constant-term representation explains global boundedness and gives the p-Lucas law through Frobenius. Modulo p, however, the condition p|b_r is cancellation among nonzero terms whenever the folded index satisfies 2r<p. No termwise factorial carry identifies the target prime.

A Subspace-Theorem argument would need the coefficient b_n to be the value of a fixed polynomial at a fixed-dimensional S-unit point. Constant-term extraction instead sums a number of monomials growing with n; converting it to a torus evaluation does not preserve a fixed-dimensional S-unit orbit whose height is O(n) and whose gcd contains exactly the old-prime part.
