The three levels must be separated.

## 1. Three different statements

Let

[

### (A) Real Rogers equality

[

5(R(a)+R(b)+R(c)+R(d))-3R(1)=0.

]

This says only that the regulator of (xi) vanishes in the real Rogers coordinate.

### (B) Ordinary pre-Bloch equality

A finite Abel certificate proves

[

xi=0

]

in the pre-Bloch group (mathcal P(F)), where relations are generated by the five-term relation.

This is strictly stronger than (A).

### (C) Extended Bloch equality

A regulator-compatible proof requires a lift

[

including flattening/parity data.

This is the correct object for Rogers dilogarithm identities modulo (pi^2mathbb Z).

## 2. Why zero regulator does not imply zero class

For a number field (F) with (r_1=2,r_2=5), the Borel regulator has a kernel containing torsion.

The theorem used in the P3.1 approach is the Suslin regulator theorem: after tensoring with (mathbb Q), the Bloch regulator is injective. Therefore

[

operatorname{Reg}(xi)=0

]

only implies that the image of (xi) is torsion.

It does not imply (xi=0).

For the alpha field, (w_2(F)=120). Thus a possible situation is

[

120xi=0,qquad xineq0,

]

while every real Rogers regulator vanishes.

## 3. What a pure Abel certificate must prove

A certificate using only Abel five-term and Euler complement must prove an equality in the extended group:

[

widehatxi

=sum_i n_i(text{lifted five-term relation})

+sum_j m_j(text{complement relation})

=0.

]

For an ordinary Rogers identity modulo (pi^2), it is enough to prove

[

Nwidehatxi=0

]

for a known torsion bound (N), then determine the regulator.

But this is different from proving the exact pre-Bloch identity.

## 4. How to disprove existence of a pure five-term certificate

A computable obstruction is the class of (xi) in the quotient

[

mathcal P(F)/langletext{five-term relations, complements}rangle.

]

More concretely:

1. Choose generators given by PGL(2,F)-orbit representatives of the arguments.

1. Build the integer relation lattice generated by all lifted five-term relations.

1. Compute Smith normal form.

1. Reduce the target vector.

If the target has a nonzero component in the Smith cokernel, then no pure five-term certificate exists in that generated presentation.

This is the correct interpretation of the previous "one missing rank" result: it is evidence of a possible torsion/cokernel component, not evidence that the search was insufficient.
