# 4. The reflection-zero block: exact resolution

The reflection collision is not fatal to the expected value M4=3, but it is fatal to the naive multiplicity-one proof.

## 4.1 What reflection produces locally

For every mirror pair {a,-a}, both tensor squares contain a local trivial character:

```plain text
L_a tensor L_(-a)=1.
```

There are m=2h-2 such pairs. Therefore reflection produces

```plain text
m local trivial lines in Sym^2(W_h),
m local trivial lines in wedge^2(W_h).
```

In wedge^2(V_h), the break-0 factor wedge^2(T_h)=1 supplies one more local trivial line.

## 4.2 What reflection produces globally

Q6644 identified the reflection correspondence as the third universal global contraction. This remains correct. But it does not mean that every mirror-pair local line is globally invariant.

Once V_h is geometrically irreducible and symplectic, Schur's lemma gives

```plain text
dim Hom_G(V_h,V_h^vee)=1.
```

Thus the alternating global invariant line is unique.

The unique global line is some linear combination inside the local zero-wild block of dimension m+1. The quotient wedge^2_0 retains m zero-wild directions. For full Sp monodromy these remaining directions lie in one irreducible primitive representation and are mixed with the nonzero wild-character directions by the global group.

This is the decisive distinction:

```plain text
local trivial under I_infinity
  does not imply
trivial under G_geom.
```

## 4.3 Why the argument cannot simply discard the mirror block

A proper global submodule could, in principle,

```plain text
- lie partly or wholly in the zero-wild block;
- contain selected nonzero pair-sum character lines;
- contain selected subspaces of the 2-dimensional cross blocks;
- mix all three kinds.
```

SUM-DISTINCT does not rule out any of these possibilities. One must prove that I_0 moves every such candidate out of itself. That is a relative-position statement, not a statement about either local conjugacy class separately.

# 6. Why FULL-SUM-DISTINCT still does not prove irreducibility

## 6.1 Multiplicity two survives

The cross term is

```plain text
T_h tensor W_h
 = direct-sum_(a in A_h)
     AS_a tensor (T_h tensor eps).
```

Since rank(T_h)=2, every AS_a has multiplicity 2. No condition on sums of critical values can remove this multiplicity.

Thus the wild representation is not multiplicity-free.

## 6.2 The zero-wild block is large

Even after quotienting the symplectic line,

```plain text
wedge^2_0(V_h)
```

has zero-wild multiplicity m=2h-2. The symmetric square has at least the m mirror lines plus the rank-3 tame factor Sym^2(T_h).

Thus the largest uncontrolled isotypic block is the zero block.

## 6.3 The Jordan form at 0 contains no relative-position information

Suppose the I_infinity wild decomposition of one tensor square is

```plain text
R = direct-sum_lambda E_lambda.
```

A global submodule R' must be stable under wild inertia, so

```plain text
R' = direct-sum_lambda R'_lambda,
R'_lambda subset E_lambda.                  (6.1)
```

SUM-DISTINCT identifies the E_lambda and makes many of them one-dimensional. But to rule out R', one must know how a generator of I_0 acts between these spaces:

```plain text
pr_mu rho(tau_0) pr_lambda.                 (6.2)
```

The Jordan form of rho(tau_0) determines none of the blocks in (6.2). Conjugating tau_0 changes all of them while preserving exactly the same local type at 0.

Hence the known local type can be positioned so that it preserves a selected sum of infinity blocks, or positioned so that it mixes them. Local conjugacy classes alone cannot distinguish these cases.

## 6.4 Arithmetic Frobenius cannot repair the geometric argument

The target M4 and tensor-square irreducibility are geometric. Arithmetic Frobenius is not an element of G_geom.

Even if Frobenius permutes all pair-sum characters transitively, a geometric submodule may be carried to a different conjugate submodule by Frobenius. The direct sum of its Frobenius orbit may be arithmetically stable while the original proper geometric submodule still exists.

Therefore Frobenius-orbit transitivity is not a substitute for geometric mixing.
