## 1.3 What “Calabi–Yau type” does imply

A fourth-order Calabi–Yau operator has a rank-four self-dual, symplectic-type structure. In operator terms, the exterior square of a generic rank-four system has rank six, but the preserved alternating form supplies an invariant line; the scalar exterior-square equation therefore drops to order five. This fourth-order/fifth-order relation is the natural setting of Yifan Yang’s pullback, discussed in Almkvist,

Calabi–Yau differential equations of degree 2 and 3 and Yifan Yang’s pullback.

Accordingly, the representation-theoretic test appropriate for #209 is:

- formal self-duality;

- a one-dimensional invariant in \Lambda^2;

- an order-five exterior-square equation;

- symplectic monodromy and a polarization.

It is not the geometric-progression test for a rank-three symmetric square.

A rank-four system could in principle be a symmetric cube of a rank-two system. That is a different hypothesis from the failed symmetric square. It must be tested using the full fourth-order local system, not the three Poincaré roots of a coefficient recurrence. A decisive local test is the Jordan form: if a finite singularity has conifold monodromy

then it cannot be \operatorname{Sym}^3(J_2(1)), because the latter is the single regular block J_4(1). No symmetric-cube identification for #209 is supplied by the AESZ or Almkvist papers.

# 7. Direct answers

### (a) Is AESZ #209 a symmetric square, Rankin–Selberg convolution, base change, or Hadamard product?

Not a symmetric square. No Rankin–Selberg or base-change identification is known. The exact construction visible in its coefficients is the central-binomial Hadamard/Kummer lift (1.1). Its intrinsic CY representation type is rank-four symplectic, with an order-five exterior square/Yifan-Yang companion.

### (b) What explains 3\to4\to3 and q_n/W_n\sim Cn^{3/2}?

The first “3\to4” is a quadratic pullback/direct-image trace and concerns recurrence shift order; the natural differential direct image has larger rank. It acquires a genuine extra 1/64 sector from x=64. There is no established “4\to3” quotient. The n^{3/2} ratio is a half-integral local-exponent difference and rules out a finite rational Ore intertwiner. A Kummer/Gamma twist plus a nonlocal projection would be required.

### (c) What are the critical values of a weight-four level-11 newform, and is one \zeta(2)+\zeta(3)?

For a specified weight-four eigenform the critical integers are 1,2,3. The eta product \eta^2\eta(11\tau)^2 is weight two and has only the critical value at 1. No known level-11 weight-four critical value equals \zeta(2)+\zeta(3), and pure modular-period theory does not predict such an additive mixed-weight identity.
