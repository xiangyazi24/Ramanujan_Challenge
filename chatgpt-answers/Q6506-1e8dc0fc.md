## 2.2 Why real oscillation and Carleman arguments do not transfer

A real Sturm/oscillation proof uses an ordered field, signs, positivity of a self-adjoint quadratic form, and variation diminution. None survives canonically over \mathbf F_p.

The finite-field remnants are only:

- order-two unique continuation: two consecutive zeros force the entire solution to vanish;

- nonzero Casoratian and hence no adjacent projective collision;

- algebraic degree, gcd, resultant, and Plücker constraints;

- reflection and restart identities.

There is no finite-field analogue of “number of sign changes,” no monotone Prüfer angle, and no analytic argument principle controlling zeros of a length-p nonautonomous solution.

More decisively, the Green-kernel axioms alone cannot imply E1. Given almost any sequence of nonzero projective points with adjacent points distinct, one may choose vector lifts recursively to realize any prescribed nonzero adjacent determinants. Those vectors satisfy some second-order nonautonomous recurrence, while their projective multiplicities can be as concentrated as desired. Thus rank two, skew-symmetry, diagonal boundary data, and local uniqueness do not control global zero energy. The explicit polynomial word P(n),n^3 must be used.

Verdict: the Green formulation is the correct coordinate system, but its present quantitative content is exactly the continuant/root machinery already banked. A new finite-field doubling/Carleman principle would have to use the arithmetic variation of the explicit transfer word, not merely the fact that D solves a second-order equation.
