# Hazards that matter

1. alpha*k means α_k, not multiplication by k. The Lean coefficient is ctAlpha27 k; there is no additional factor k.

1. Index shift: the repository recurrence parameter is k, while every Zudilin polynomial is evaluated at k+2. Do not call ctAlpha27 (k+2) or shift the sequence again.

1. Asymmetric factor two: ctAlpha27, ctBeta27, and ctGamma27 contain a factor 2; ctDelta27 does not. Hence only the last normalized coefficient has denominator 2 * zudilinA (k+2).

1. Signs: homogeneous signs are +α, -β, +γ, -δ. Solving for E(k+3) gives +M/A, then -(k+2)N/A, then +R(k+2)(k+1)^3/(2A).

1. Certificate orientation: the pointwise right side is shifted minus unshifted. If the contour theorem is stored as unshifted equals shifted, use .symm before supplying hcert.

1. Kernel location: the shifted term is ctSPhi27 ... (t+1) * kernel(t), not kernel(t+1). Kernel periodicity belongs in the prior certificate-shift proof.

1. Integral linearity: integral_congr_ae needs no integrability, but MeasureTheory.integral_add and integral_sub do. Omitting any of the six hypotheses risks silently using the Bochner integral's non-integrable value 0 rather than proving the intended identity.

1. Normalization scalar: factor out 1/(2π); do not divide by it. This avoids an unnecessary Real.pi_ne_zero or complex nonzero premise. There is no extra Complex.I in the repository's real-parameterized fixed-line formula.

1. Type: ZudilinSatisfiesRec is declared only for ℕ → ℚ. A Barnes integral is ℕ → ℂ, so state the complex scalar extension rather than attempting ZudilinSatisfiesRec E directly.

1. Uniqueness: proving the recurrence does not by itself identify the Barnes sequence with (zudilinCombinedError n : ℂ). Since the recurrence has order three, equality still requires exact agreement at n=0,1,2 (or an equivalent three-value uniqueness theorem). The normalization factor is critical in those initial checks.
