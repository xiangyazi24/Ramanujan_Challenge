The correct finite-facet invariant is not a rectangle but a moving cone adapted to the two-dimensional stable/unstable splitting.

## 3. Projective invariance calculation

Write the homogeneous row vector as

[

E_n=(-s_n, r_n, t_n),qquad s_n,r_n,t_n>0.

]

The projective coordinates are

[

x_n=r_n/s_n,qquad y_n=t_n/s_n.

]

Because every entry of M(n) is positive,

[

(s,r,t)mapsto(s,r,t)M(n)

]

keeps the positive octant. The nontrivial part is proving that the normalized ratios remain inside the moving cone.

For each facet L_i(n)\ge0, compute

[

L_i(n+1)(E_nM(n)),s_n.

]

After substituting the explicit polynomial matrix, the denominator disappears and the result is a polynomial in n and the cone variables. The cone constants are chosen so every coefficient is nonnegative.
