# 17. What would count as real progress

The collision route should be reopened only after proving one of the following actual-Apéry statements.

1. Primitive depletion: condition (P).

1. Weighted marked cover: conditions (C1)–(C2).

1. Superlinear non-reflection expansion: hypothesis (E).

1. A global fixed-gap resultant: for each h, one nonzero integer carrying all relevant h-gap collisions at logarithmic height substantially below the sum of the individual continuant heights. No such identity is currently known; ordinary products have the zero-slack ledger above.

1. A horizontal Mellin/crystalline correlation theorem: a two-characteristic estimate which controls the moving events p\mid B_{r_q} and is not merely a vertical bound on |Z_p|.

The following do not suffice:

- a uniform numerical bound such as the measured |Z_p|\le12;

- reflection pairing;

- the existence of many target primes dividing B_n;

- rank or CRT reconstruction;

- additional comparison solutions with the same local zero line;

- reflected continuants before saturation;

- generic second-order recurrence expansion;

- a count of edges without a weighted cover or a superlinear lower bound.

For the actual Apéry cubic, however, no theorem presently forces a large fixed-n target star to generate enough non-reflection cross-incidences. The exact obstruction is the primitive/doublet component: its vertices have no guaranteed collision witness, and exact computations show that doublets dominate the active vertical population. Rich fibers also supply only possible witnesses, not alignment with another star residue.

Therefore the sharpest rigorous next target is not another determinant identity. It is a horizontal theorem of the form (P)+(C), or the stronger expansion bound (E). Either would use the exact cubic through the canonical continuants; neither follows from the currently known recurrence, Lucas, reflection, or small-fiber facts.
