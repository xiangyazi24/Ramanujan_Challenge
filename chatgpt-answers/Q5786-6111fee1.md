# 7. What this proves about nuisance height

For the explicitly classified Kummer/common-shell factors, maximal saturation gives only (O(1)) candidate integers near (n) and (n/2). Their total logarithmic height is therefore (O(log n)). The same is true of a fixed number of endpoint factors such as divisors of (n+j) with bounded (j).

Thus the geometric and Kummer part of the desired construction is positive:

1. all targets in a linear-size core survive;

1. only (O(1)) balanced blocks are needed;

1. the deterministic moving nuisance tail has (O(log n)) height after full saturation.

However, this does not yet prove that the total evaluated nuisance gcd has (o(n)) height. Outside the flat-cell interval, primes can still divide all evaluated minors through a boundary-shell cancellation, a primitive Plücker relation, or a high-difference coincidence. The fixed-radius scan has already shown that one must not replace this issue by a fixed-degree Fitting polynomial.
