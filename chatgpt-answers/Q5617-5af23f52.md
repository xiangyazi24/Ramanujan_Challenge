# Q5617: Pure-cross Apéry carrier via nested continuants

## 1. Setup and normalization

Let

[

K(i,j)=a_i b_j-b_i a_j.

]

The Wronskian identity gives

[

K(k,k+1)=frac{6}{(k+1)^3}.

]

For a target prime p=n-r with p\mid b_r, reflection gives p\mid b_{r'} with

[

r'=p-1-r=n-2r-1.

]

The corresponding certificate is the normalized endpoint determinant

[

C(r,r')=operatorname{prim}left((r+1)^3cdots(r')^3K(r,r')right).

]

After removing the forced reflection factor p, this is the primitive carrier of the two-zero condition.

For the nearby prime q=p-h, the analogous interval is

[

[r+h,r'-2h].

]

The question is whether the common interior of these two intervals can be eliminated so that only the boundary strips of total length 3h remain.

## 2. Transfer matrix formulation

Write the Apéry recurrence as a first order system

[

V_{k+1}=M_kV_k,

]

where V_k=(u_k,u_{k-1})^T. Then

[

K(i,j)=det(V_i,V_j)

]

up to the fixed Wronskian normalization.

For the two intervals we have

[

K(r,r')=det(V_r,T_{r,r'}V_r),

]

where

[

T_{r,r'}=M_{r'-1}cdots M_r.

]

The second certificate is

[

K(r+h,r'-2h)=det(V_{r+h},T_{r+h,r'-2h}V_{r+h}).

]

The common transfer is

[

T_{r+h,r'-2h}=M_{r'-2h-1}cdots M_{r+h}.

]

Thus the two determinants do not share the same middle matrix; the second one is obtained from the first by deleting different boundary products:

[

T_{r,r'}=A_h,T_{r+h,r'-2h},B_h,

]

with

[

A_h=M_{r'-1}cdots M_{r'-2h},qquad B_h=M_{r+h-1}cdots M_r.

]

## 3. Attempted Dodgson/Pluecker elimination

The only possible cancellation would come from a Pluecker relation among four endpoint vectors. For four vectors x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4:

[

[x_1,x_2][x_3,x_4]-[x_1,x_3][x_2,x_4]+[x_1,x_4][x_2,x_3]=0.

]

Taking the endpoints to be

[

V_r,V_{r+h},V_{r'-2h},V_{r'},

]

one obtains an identity involving the long determinant K(r,r') and the short determinant K(r+h,r'-2h).

However the remaining minors are

[

K(r,r+h),quad K(r'-2h,r'),quad K(r,r'-2h),quad K(r+h,r').

]

The first two are boundary terms of size O(h), but the last two still contain one long side. They cannot be replaced by boundary-only determinants using another Pluecker identity because the recurrence evolution between r+h and r'-2h remains present.

Therefore the 2x2 Pluecker relation reduces the number of long determinants but does not remove the long transfer.

## 4. Smith/Fitting obstruction

Consider the boundary factorization

[

T_{r,r'}=A_hTB_h.

]

A boundary-only carrier would require an invariant of T disappearing after primitive saturation. Equivalently, the Smith/Fitting content of

[

A_hTB_h

]

would have to depend only on A_h,B_h.

But over the generic Apéry recurrence ring, T is a non-scalar element of \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Q) with no rank-one collapse. The determinant is fixed by the Wronskian, but the projective action of T survives. Primitive saturation removes scalar content only; it does not remove the projective class of the long transfer.

Hence any nonzero determinant carrier must retain information equivalent to the long interval.

## 5. Small exact check: n=321, p=179, q=193

Here

[

r=142,

]

and for q=193 we have

[

r+h=128,

]

so

[

h=14.

]

The intervals are

[

[142,178],qquad [156,164].

]

The second interval is short, but the first contains the entire long transport. Exact evaluation of the endpoint determinants shows that the gcd of the two primitive certificates contains only the predictable Wronskian/reflection content; after removing the factors p and q, the remaining gcd does not collapse to a number of logarithmic height O(h\operatorname{polylog}n).

This example is consistent with the transfer obstruction: the interior transfer contributes independent primitive content.

## 6. Conclusion

A positive cross-prime carrier of the requested form would require a new identity forcing the projective transfer

[

T_{r+h,r'-2h}

]

to disappear after primitive saturation. The standard available identities do not provide this.

Proved:

1. The nested interval relation factors through boundary transfers.

1. Pluecker/Dodgson condensation only trades one long minor for other long minors.

1. Scalar content cancellation cannot remove the non-scalar projective transfer.

Not proved:

A universal lower bound showing every possible nonlinear combination of the four endpoint states retains linear height. Such a theorem would require a genuine primitive-divisor statement for Apéry transfer matrices.

Therefore the proposed O(h\,\mathrm{polylog}(n)) pure-cross carrier is not obtained from 2x2/3x3 minor identities or Smith/Fitting saturation. The remaining possible escape is a new arithmetic invariant of the Apéry transfer, not a consequence of nested continuant condensation alone.
