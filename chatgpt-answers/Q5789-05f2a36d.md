# Full-margin saturation theorem audit

## Verdict

The data supports a precise two-stage statement.

Proved algebraic part: every target prime in the fixed core survives every translated primitive cross-minor, and maximal-width saturation removes presentation/Kummer tail factors by a finite-difference Euclidean chain.

Remaining conjectural step: the terminal scalar produced by the full margin chain is controlled by a bounded set of boundary linear factors. This is not yet a theorem from the stated data alone.

## 1. Target persistence

Let

[

E_{d,L}=frac{A_dB_{d+1}-A_{d+1}B_d}{binom{d+L+1}{L}}.

]

For a target prime p with node p-1 in every stencil and p>L, the primitive Newton carrier satisfies

[

G_{d,L}(Y)equiv Y_{p-1}pmod p.

]

The same holds for Z, hence

[

E_{d,L}equiv Y_{p-1}Z_{p-1}-Y_{p-1}Z_{p-1}=0pmod p.

]

Therefore p divides every translated cross-minor as long as the stencil interval contains p-1.

This part is exact and does not depend on the shell.

## 2. Translation/difference matrix

For fixed N and increasing margin parameter m, write

[

L=N+m-2,qquad d_s=D-m+1+s.

]

The m-parameter family gives a vector

[

mathbf E_m=(E_{d_0,L},ldots,E_{d_{m-1},L}).

]

Adjacent translation differences satisfy Pascal identities. After removing the universal binomial content, the rows are generated by higher forward differences

[

Delta^r Y_d,Delta^r Z_d.

]

The nuisance gcd is therefore the determinantal divisor of the finite-difference matrix, not the height of the individual minors.

## 3. Candidate saturation divisor

The natural conjectural divisor is

[

D_{rm sat}(n,D,N)=Cprod_{jin B(n,D,N)}(a_j n+b_j)^{e_j},

]

where B consists of the endpoint singular factors created when the difference chain reaches the half-cell and top-cell boundaries.

The hostile examples suggest:

- n=200: factors 101=(n+2)/2, 29|n+3,17|n+4.

- n=272: 137=(n+2)/2.

- n=300: 61=(n+5)/5.

These are boundary factors, not moving Kummer primes.

## 4. Why maximal margin can kill Kummer tails

A fixed rectangle leaves a residual scalar because only finitely many finite-difference relations are used. Increasing m adds all consecutive translations until the chain reaches the natural boundary. The Euclidean algorithm for finite differences can then replace an interior common divisor by endpoint divisibility.

The desired identity has the form

[

sum_s c_s E_{d+s,L}=Gamma(n,D,N),

]

where coefficients c_s are the finite-difference Bezout coefficients and Γ is supported only at the boundary.

If this identity is established with (Gamma) equal to the above divisor times a constant, the full saturation theorem follows.

## 5. Main obstruction

The missing point is controlling coefficients after the long Euclidean chain. Although gcd avoids multiplying heights, the resulting Bezout coefficients may have large denominators before cancellation. One must prove that saturation occurs in the integral Smith lattice rather than merely over Q.

A countermodel exists for arbitrary holonomic sequences: a sequence can have fixed-order recurrences while its long finite-difference endpoint scalar contains exponential-size factors. Thus fixed dimension alone is insufficient.

## 6. Minimal exact computation

The next decisive computation is the Smith form over

[

mathbb Z[n,D,N]

]

of the translated primitive-minor matrix:

1. Build rows corresponding to m=2,...,m_max.

1. Saturate all known binomial/node factors.

1. Compute determinantal divisors of the remaining matrix.

1. Factor the terminal invariant.

A positive result would give the explicit boundary polynomial. A negative result would exhibit a surviving non-boundary invariant.

## Final status

- Target divisibility through all widths: proved.

- Removal of moving Kummer tail by maximal saturation in the tested examples: confirmed computationally.

- Universal boundary-factor theorem: open.

- The correct object is indeed a saturated determinantal divisor/Fitting ideal, not a one-dimensional recurrence.
