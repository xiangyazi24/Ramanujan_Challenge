## 15. Precise scoped obstruction

The following statements are now exact.

### Obstruction A — monomial orbit collapse

All (operatorname{GL}_3(mathbb Z))-equivalent models have identical shell sequences and identical block carriers. Their gcd is the original carrier.

### Obstruction B — period mutation is not shell mutation

A genuine period-preserving mutation does not preserve the finite torsion grids defining (C_M^L(d)). Therefore the mutation theorem gives no coefficientwise or shellwise Bézout identity.

### Obstruction C — exact support saturation

After intersection with the known block primorial, the gcd of any number of model carriers is exactly (R_I(n)), the target product. Thus the multi-model algebra has recovered the unknown object rather than bounded it.

### Obstruction D — local target rank remains one

The existing two-model prefix/commutator audit has one primitive target factor and no second target equation; its mutation commutator is a unit after saturation at an actual target. Products or resultants cannot claim an extra local factor without new arithmetic.

Individual carriers, origin-free differences, and standard resultants have logarithmic height (O(n)) or (O(n^2)). P3.2 needs (o(H)) in each (H=n^{1/3}) block.

## 17. Final route-selection verdict

The multi-model construction is mathematically real:

- every model gives an exact primitive block carrier;

- model differences cancel the common origin coefficient exactly;

- the block-prime radical of the gcd is exactly the target product;

- at (n=321), that factor is (7{,}289{,}417).

But the construction does not yet solve the height problem:

- monomial models are identical at shell level;

- the proposed explicit (PhitoLambda) pullback is false for the displayed normalizations;

- genuine mutation preserves periods, not finite shells;

- the unrestricted (Phi)-carrier and its full gcd with the (529)-digit (Lambda)-carrier have not been executed in the repository;

- the only rigorous general height bounds remain (O(n)) or worse;

- the earlier two-model commutator audit is locally rank one and exponentially tall after primitive normalization.

Therefore no common integer of logarithmic height (o(H)) is currently constructed. The exact obstruction is not that different Laurent models are independent; it is that their common target divisor survives only as the same one-dimensional marked scalar, while mutation gives no short characteristic-zero Bézout relation among the off-origin shell packets.
