## 11. Strategic conclusion

The constant-term model does produce a real block object. The key identity is not merely Lucas but

It shows that every target annihilates an entire coefficient packet of one fixed characteristic-zero Laurent power. Nevertheless, all currently natural ways of combining these packets stop at the known barrier:

```plain text
raw packet or trace       -> exp(Theta(n)) height;
CRT recombination         -> exactly b_n/b_a modulo the block primorial;
Newton extrapolation      -> valid divisibility, but no nonzero/o(H) bound;
packet determinant        -> growing rank and exp(Theta(n)) entries;
cyclotomic norm           -> varying fields, growing degree, one q-factor only;
formal packet polynomial  -> primorial or packet-product saturation.
```

Thus the strongest justified conclusion is a scoped no-go, not a global impossibility theorem:

Frobenius/Cartier alone, applied separately in each characteristic, cannot give the requested compression. A successful construction must prove a new cross-characteristic relation among the actual coefficients of \Phi^n along the moving shell spacings q-1.

The two most concrete next targets are:

1. prove the local-limit estimate (10.2);

1. find a signed WZ/creative-telescoping normalization of P_m(N) whose degree-H extrapolation has sub-H height while retaining (3.5).

Everything weaker that merely reuses the individual Cartier packets is already saturated.
