### What this proves, and what it does not

It proves a characteristic-zero identity between distinguished periods and an exact finite-field truncation identity. The square of a solution of a second-order differential equation is the characteristic signature of a symmetric-square construction, with the factor 1+x acting as a rational gauge.

It does not, by itself, prove any of the following:

- that a specified K3 family has Hasse invariant literally equal to the bare polynomial H_p²;

- that the full integral or ℓ-adic Apéry local system is isomorphic to Sym² of the Franel elliptic local system;

- that no quadratic, finite-order, or Tate twist occurs;

- that the equality of one distinguished period determines an arithmetic sheaf isomorphism.

If A_p(t) is realized as a K3 Hasse scalar in some specified Hodge trivialization, its pullback in the normalization above would be (1+x)^(p-1)H_p², not globally the bare polynomial H_p². ## 7. Elliptic versus K3: audited classification

### Proved and fully sourced

1. H_p is the scalar Hasse–Witt invariant of the Beauville-IV elliptic pencil E_x.

1. Its distinguished characteristic-zero period is the Franel generating series h(x).

1. The square h(x)², after pullback and the rational gauge 1+x, is the distinguished Apéry period.

1. On the Hodge line of the symmetric square of the elliptic variation, Frobenius acts by the square of the elliptic Hasse scalar, hence by H_p² in the chosen normalization.

### Plausible but not certified here

The classical Apéry ζ(3) differential equation is associated with a K3 family, and the period identity strongly suggests that its rank-three transcendental variation is a pullback/gauge form of a symmetric square. The standard classical references are:

- F. Beukers and C. A. M. Peters, “A family of K3 surfaces and ζ(3),” J. reine angew. Math. 351 (1984), 42–54, DOI 10.1515/crll.1984.351.42;

- J. Stienstra and F. Beukers, “On the Picard-Fuchs Equation and the Formal Brauer Group of Certain Elliptic K3-Surfaces,” Math. Ann. 271 (1985), 269–304, DOI 10.1007/BF01455990.

I was able to verify the bibliographic metadata, but not to inspect a reliable full primary text with the relevant page/equation in this audit. I therefore do not use these papers to assert a specific K3 equation, a precise Hasse polynomial, or an ℓ-adic symmetric-square isomorphism. Those remain items requiring a separate page-level audit.

### Not verified and not asserted

- A page-numbered primary-source theorem identifying one explicit Apéry K3 model with this exact symmetric-square ℓ-adic sheaf.
