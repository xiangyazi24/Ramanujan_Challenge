[

]

whose minimal polynomial is

[

h(t)=t^8+t^7-7t^6-6t^5+15t^4+10t^3-10t^2-4t+1.

]

The required isomorphism is the translation

[

boxed{w=t+1}.

Indeed, exact substitution gives

[

g(t+1)=t^8+t^7-7t^6-6t^5+15t^4+10t^3-10t^2-4t+1=h(t).

]

Therefore (g(w)=0) is equivalent to (h(w-1)=0), and

[

mathbb Q[w]/(g)cong mathbb Q[t]/(h)=mathbb Q(zeta_{17}+zeta_{17}^{-1}).

]

[

boxed{P=[1,1,0,0,0,0,0,0]}.

]

[

Q(w)=w-1,

]

with coefficient list

[

boxed{Q=[-1,1,0,0,0,0,0,0]}.

]
