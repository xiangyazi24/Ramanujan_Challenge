# Strategic feasibility audit: nonlinear determinantal/Fitting route

## Verdict

The determinantal route is the first nonlinear construction that is not immediately killed by the rank-one CRT obstruction. However, the strongest unconditional statement currently available is weaker than the desired P3.2 closure.

A realistic target lemma is:

After saturation by universal node/binomial factors, a fixed-size rectangle of primitive minors of the family E_{d+s,L+t} defines a nonzero Fitting ideal whose generators have bounded recurrence order and fixed algebraic degree in the state variables.

This does not yet imply a polynomial-height integer in n,d,L. The obstruction is that converting the finite-dimensional state representation into evaluated integers introduces transition products with exponential height.

## What is rigorous

Let the shell state be represented by a fixed-dimensional rational recurrence state v_d\in K^r. Then each carrier has the form

A_d=\alpha(d,L)v_d,

and similarly for B_d. Therefore

E_{d,L}=\det\begin{pmatrix}A_d&B_d\\A_{d+1}&B_{d+1}\end{pmatrix}/\binom{d+L+1}{L}

is a bilinear exterior coordinate of the combined state.

The exterior state lives in

\wedge^2(K^{r_Y}\oplus K^{r_Z}),

so it is finite dimensional. This proves:

1. the nonlinear family is holonomic;

1. fixed-order annihilators exist after Ore elimination;

1. a finite observability matrix can be formed.

These are formal consequences of finite state dimension.

## The missing step

The desired theorem would require a primitive integer

R(n,d,L)

with:

- all target primes dividing R;

- degree in (n,d,L) bounded independently of the block size;

- logarithmic height o(H).

Finite-dimensionality alone does not provide this.

If

v_{d+t}=T(d+t-1)\cdots T(d)v_d,

then even fixed dimension gives coefficient height

\log ||T(d+H-1)\cdots T(d)||=\Theta(H\log d)

in general.

Thus the nonlinear route succeeds only if the Fitting determinant cancels these transfer products or admits a bounded-height gauge.

## Proposed exact computation

The smallest meaningful symbolic test is not a recurrence guess. It is the Smith/Fitting computation of the primitive rectangle matrix.

For a rectangle

\mathcal R_{s,t}=E_{d+s,L+t},\quad 0\le s\le S,0\le t\le T,

construct the coefficient matrix in the exterior state basis:

M_{S,T}(d,L).

Compute over

\mathbb Q[d,L]

(or after clearing denominators over \mathbb Z[d,L]):

1. maximal minors;

1. gcd of minors (first Fitting ideal);

1. Smith invariants modulo the target prime p;

1. whether the primitive quotient removes all universal binomial factors.

The first decisive case is S=T=1 and then S=T=2.

## Possible positive outcome

A successful theorem would look like:

\operatorname{Fitt}_1(M_{S,T})=(R(d,L)),

where R is a bounded-degree polynomial and every target prime in the rectangle divides R.

The proof route would be:

1. build the exterior state;

1. compute its observability matrix;

1. saturate by known node factors;

1. prove the remaining determinant is nonzero;

1. bound its degree/height.

The nonzero step is algebraic. The height step is the real bottleneck.

## Negative countermodel

Finite-dimensional state does not imply bounded-height Fitting determinants.

Take a one-dimensional state

v_{n+1}=n^2v_n.

The state dimension is one, but

v_n=(n-1)!^2v_1,

and every nonzero observability determinant has factorial height.

Therefore any argument using only "fixed dimension" and "exterior power" is insufficient.

The Apéry shell requires an additional cancellation theorem specific to the matrices, not just holonomicity.

## Relation to known dead ends

This route is different from:

- linear CRT carriers: already rank one;

- pointwise zero-fiber estimates: insufficient;

- binomial window gcds: blocked;

- recurrence order bounds: insufficient without height control.

The nonlinear object is correctly identified as a saturated determinantal/Fitting ideal, but the decisive theorem is a low-height gauge/cancellation identity.
