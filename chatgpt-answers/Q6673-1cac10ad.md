\begin{theorem}[Noncollapse, all $h$]\label{thm:noncollapse-all}
Assume that $C_h$ is squarefree for $h\geq 2$. Then the squared-critical-value map on the reflection quotient $B_h$ is not constant.

\begin{proof}
Suppose otherwise that every squared critical value is equal to a constant $a$.

If $a=0$, then all $4h-4$ simple critical points lie in the fiber $f_h^{-1}(0)$. Each critical point contributes local multiplicity $2$ to this fiber, so its total degree contribution is at least
\[
2(4h-4)>3h=\deg f_h,
\]
which is impossible.

Now suppose $a=t^2\neq 0$. Reflection pairs the critical points and exchanges the two values $t$ and $-t$. Hence exactly $2h-2$ critical points lie over each of the two fibers over $t$ and $-t$. Every such critical point has multiplicity $2$, so each fiber has degree contribution at least
\[
2(2h-2)=4h-4.
\]
Since the fiber degree is $3h$, this forces $4h-4\leq 3h$, hence $h\leq 4$. The remaining cases $h=2,3,4$ are excluded by the exact integer certificate: the stored coefficients satisfy
\[
(m-1)a_1^2-2m a_2\neq 0,
\]
with evaluated signs $+,-,-$ respectively.
\end{proof}
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp primitivity criterion]\label{thm:primitive-morse-sharp}
Assume $C_h$ is squarefree and $\operatorname{Gal}(A_h/\mathbb Q)$ acts primitively on the $2h-2$ roots of the reflection quotient $A_h$. Then the squared-value fibers form a Galois-invariant partition. Primitivity forces this partition to be either discrete or indiscrete. Theorem \ref{thm:noncollapse-all} excludes the indiscrete case. Therefore $w_h$ is injective, $V_h$ is squarefree, $\Sigma_h=\varnothing$, and
\[
s_h=4h-4.
\]
Consequently $\operatorname{Mon}(f_h)=S_{3h}$ and the collision curve $G_h$ is absolutely irreducible.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Certificate protocol]\label{rem:certificate-protocol}
A two-prime machine certificate suffices for the required primitivity check. One good prime $q_0$ with $A_h$ irreducible modulo $q_0$ gives transitivity. A second good prime with a factorization pattern containing a prime cycle length $p>h-1$ gives a pure $p$-cycle after raising Frobenius to the least common multiple of the remaining cycle lengths. Such a cycle is incompatible with any block system because both the block count and block size are at most $h-1$. An alternative is an $(n-1)$-cycle with a fixed point, with fallback to exact cycle-block compatibility elimination over all divisors.

Cautions: irreducibility alone gives only transitivity; a transposition does not rule out wreath-type imprimitivity; squarefree discriminant alone is insufficient.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Complex variance warning]\label{rem:complex-variance}
The quantity
\[
(m-1)a_1^2-2m a_2
\]
resembles a variance expression, but the squared critical values are complex. Its sign is not controlled by positivity; indeed the computed signs are $+,-,-$ for $h=2,3,4$. Only nonvanishing is used.
\end{remark}

Combined with the parallel value-side route (where irreducibility of $W_h$ over $\mathbb Q$ implies the full chain in one step), the all-$h$ absolute irreducibility of the collision curves rests on two independent single-variable characteristic-zero statements, both machine-certified for small $h$ and certificate-programmable for large $h$.
