# Q5847: exact sign audit of the subresultant coefficients

The requested global sign claim on the stated rational box is **false as written**. The obstruction is in `cb`, not in the subresultant computation.

## Exact counterexample

Take the rational point

\[
 X=\frac1{100},\qquad L=\frac25.
\]

This satisfies

\[
0<X<\frac16,\qquad \frac25<L<\frac35.
\]

Write

\[
cb=-X(L+X)(L+X^3)P(X,L),
\]

where

\[
P=L^2X^2+2L^2-LX^4+3LX^3+2LX^2+3LX-L+2X^4+X^2.
\]

At the above rational point the exact numerator of `P` is

\[
P\left(\frac1{100},\frac25\right)
=-\frac{16950696}{250000000}.
\]

The denominator is positive, hence

\[
P<0.
\]

All three prefactors

\[
X,\quad L+X,\quad L+X^3
\]

are positive, so

\[
cb=-X(L+X)(L+X^3)P>0.
\]

Therefore the statement `cb < 0 on 0<X<1/6, 2/5<L<3/5` cannot be proved because it is not true.

## Consequence for the linear root

The subresultant equation

\[
ca\,r+cb=0
\]

still gives

\[
r_0=-\frac{cb}{ca},
\]

but the sign of `r0` cannot be fixed from the supplied box alone. A smaller box excluding the region near `X=0,L<1/2` is required.

## Machine-checkable Lean-style certificate of failure

The following rational certificate is sufficient:

```text
X = 1/100
L = 2/5

P(X,L) = -16950696/250000000

sign(P) = negative
sign(X*(L+X)*(L+X^3)) = positive
therefore sign(cb) = positive
```

Hence any Lean proof of `cb < 0` over the full announced rectangle must fail. The next repair step is to determine the correct endpoint box (or add the missing constraint on `(X,L)`) and rerun the interval proof for `ca`, `cb`, and `2*u*r0+1`.
