# Diagonal margin exact recurrence audit

## Status

The exact Pascal calculation shows that the diagonal-margin family is controlled by a finite difference in the Newton basis, but the desired conclusion that gcd(H_{m-1},H_m) always collapses to a polynomial-height boundary resultant does not follow from the Pascal identities alone. The missing ingredient is an exterior-content theorem for the actual Apéry shell state.

## Exact Newton-row relation

Write

[

P_{a,L}(T)=sum_i (-1)^ibinom{a+i-1}{i}binom{a+L}{L-i}T^i.

]

For (d_m=D-m+1, L_m=N+m-2), the two parameters move by

[

d_{m-1}=d_m+1,qquad L_{m-1}=L_m-1.

]

The Pascal identities give

[

P_{a+1,L-1}-P_{a,L}

]

as an explicit integral linear combination of adjacent Newton rows. The primitive content is obtained by dividing by

[

g_m=gcd_{0le s<m-1}binom{d_m+s+L_m+1}{L_m}.

]

Hence

[

K_m=(P_{d_m,L_m}-P_{D,L_m})/g_m

]

lies in the same saturated Newton module generated by the adjacent rows, but the saturation factors are essential: they cannot be removed uniformly over (mathbb Z).

## Determinant relation

For the shell functional (Phi),

[

H_m=det(Phi(P_{d_m,L_m}),Phi(P_{D,L_m}))/g_m.

]

The formal Pluecker identity gives

[

det(U_m,V_m)-det(U_{m-1},V_{m-1})

]

as a combination of mixed minors involving the Pascal transition matrix. This is an identity in the two-dimensional exterior module, not a scalar recurrence for (H_m).

To obtain

[

A_mH_m+B_mH_{m-1}=Q_m

]

with small-height (Q_m), one needs a further statement that the mixed minors are generated by boundary packets. The Pascal law does not imply this.

## Actual-shell obstruction

The universal arbitrary-array obstruction remains applicable at the level of the Newton transform: a finite-dimensional Pascal recurrence only controls the row space. It does not bound the arithmetic content of the image under (Phi).

For the Apéry shell pair ((Y,W)), a positive result would require proving:

[

bigwedge^2 langle Phi(P_{d,L})rangle

subseteq mathcal B_n

]

where (mathcal B_n) is a boundary module generated by finitely many shell boundary packets and the Apéry Casoratian, with index and content bounded polynomially.

This is exactly the missing exterior-content lemma.

## Checker

A dependency-free symbolic checker should:

1. Construct (P_{a,L}) symbolically.

1. Apply the Pascal transition matrices from ((d_m,L_m)) to ((d_{m-1},L_{m-1})).

1. Compute the Smith normal form of the transition matrix.

1. Compare the Smith factors with (g_m/g_{m-1}).

1. Apply the actual shell vectors (Y,W) and test whether mixed minors reduce to the proposed boundary basis.

A successful output would certify the missing inclusion. Failure produces an explicit surviving Smith factor.

## Theorem versus conjecture

Theorem: diagonal margins are not automatically new at the Newton-module level; they are related by exact Pascal transitions and saturation factors.

Conjecture/open step: the special Apéry shell state may force the mixed minors into a polynomial-height boundary module.

No proof currently exists from the stated identities alone that the diagonal family yields the required subexponential boundary determinant.
