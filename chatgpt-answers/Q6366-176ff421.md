# 9. Gap ledger

## Proved directly from the displayed formulas

- The fixed-z cyclotomic sum has the Weil bound 2\sqrt p.

- The raw complete x,y sum is O(p^{3/2}).

- The cover counts the (1+\eta(q(t)))-weighted split part.

- Additive zero detection does not linearize the global trace.

- The exact CRT density is |Z_p||Z_q|/(pq).

- Together with |Z_p|\ll p^{2/3}, this gives only the bounds in (7.3)--(7.6).

## [GAP-MON]

Prove that the pulled-back rank-2 hypergeometric sheaf has no geometrically constant constituent after every Mellin/branch Kummer twist. This upgrades the split-cover sum from O(p^{3/2}) to O(p).

## [GAP-DESCENT]

Construct a genuine deck-equivariant descent from the x-cover to the t-line. This is necessary to turn the split-weighted bound into a bound for the full cyclotomic lift of \tau_j or \sigma_j.

## [GAP-ZERO]

Find a p-adic/unit/norm statement converting the cyclotomic trace estimate into nonvanishing of its reduction. Complex Deligne bounds alone do not do this.

## [GAP-HORIZONTAL]

Obtain additive Fourier cancellation for 1_{Z_p} or an equivalent cross-prime incidence theorem. Nothing in the present complete complex sums supplies it.
