A mechanical proof should use these bounds:

1. On the strip, `0 < 1/2 ≤ z.re`, hence
   `‖z + j‖⁻¹ ≤ 2` for every denominator factor.
2. For every `r < n`,
   `‖z-(r+1)‖ ≤ (n+m+3) * (1+|z.im|)`.
3. The erased numerator has at most `n` factors, each cubed.
4. Its explicit derivative has at most `n` summands and at most `n-1`
   remaining cubed factors.
5. `ctPoleProductPrimeExplicit27` has `n+1` summands and `n`
   linear factors.
6. The degree-six `Qhat` and degree-five `QhatPrime` are bounded by a
   constant depending on `n` times `(1+|z.im|)^6`.
7. Substitute these into the explicit quotient-rule formulas; the common
   denominator and its square are uniformly bounded away from zero.
