```lean
  rw [ctNumerator_succ27 (k + 2) t,
    ctNumerator_succ27 (k + 1) t,
    ctNumerator_succ27 k t, ctAlphaC_eq27]
  -- Rewrite the source factorial in the goal and its nonzero proof together.
  rw [ct_factorial_succ_cast27 (k + 2)] at hden ⊢
  have hcancel := ct_cancel_exact_den27
    (d :=
      ((((k + 3 : ℕ) : ℂ) * ((k + 2).factorial : ℂ)) ^ 2) *
        ctPoleProduct27 (k + 4) t)

      ctAlphaBarC27 (((k + 2 : ℕ) : ℂ)) * ctNumerator27 k t *
        (t - ((k + 2 : ℕ) : ℂ) - 1) ^ 3 *
```

Why this works: after the factorial rewrite, the source denominator is literally


The outside common denominator contributes `((k+2)!)² P₄`, while `ctAlphaC_eq27` contributes `(k+3)²`. Thus the full prefactor is exactly the source denominator. `ring` only rearranges multiplication; it is no longer asked to cancel an inverse.
