# Q6559: Radon spectrum audit of bulk sqrt(2) shift and surface flat term

## 1. Bulk peak: random benchmark and the sqrt(2) question

Let

F(t,xi)=sum_{h,r} exp(2*pi*i*t*D(r,r+h)/p) exp(2*pi*i*xi h/p).

Under the null model, the p^2 summands for fixed (t,xi) are independent unit complex variables. Hence

E |F|^2 = p^2.

Therefore X=|F|/p has the Rayleigh law coming from a complex Gaussian:

F/p -> Z,   Z ~ CN(0,1),

so

P(X>x)=exp(-x^2).

There are N=(p-1)p approximately p^2 spectral points. The maximum satisfies

P(M<=u)= (1-exp(-u^2))^N

and the extreme-value centering is

u_p^2 = log N + y.

Equivalently

M_p^2 - log(p^2) -> G,

where G has the standard Gumbel distribution

P(G<=y)=exp(-exp(-y)).

Thus

M_p ~ sqrt(log(p^2)) = sqrt(2 log p).

There is no extra sqrt(2) in the complex-Gaussian convention. A measured ratio

M_p/sqrt(2 log p) ~= sqrt(2)

means that the effective number or effective variance of independent spectral modes is larger by a factor close to two.

Two mechanisms must be separated.

### Hypothesis A: mirror coherence

If there is an involution sigma on the summation lattice such that

D(sigma(r,h))=D(r,h)

and the Fourier phase is preserved,

xi sigma(h)=xi h,

then paired terms add coherently. The effective variable is

F = 2 sum_{orbits} zeta_j.

The variance doubles:

E|F|^2 becomes 2 p^2,

and the Gaussian scale becomes sqrt(2). The extreme value law becomes

M_p ~ sqrt(2)*sqrt(log(p^2))

up to lower order centering corrections.

For a true Apéry reflection this requires an exact symmetry after including the Fourier character. The relevant test is not only D symmetry but compatibility with h -> -h or the corresponding affine transformation induced by

r -> p-1-r.

### Hypothesis B: wrap-around contamination

The implemented probe uses

(r+h) mod p.

For h+r>=p, the second coordinate is no longer a genuine orbit point. Therefore the Casoratian identity does not apply automatically. The artificial boundary strip creates extra deterministic correlations.

This mechanism predicts a different experiment:

- Full cyclic probe: sqrt(2)-inflated bulk peak.
- Non-wrap probe with h<p/2 and r+h<p only: no artificial boundary pairs, so the ratio should return to approximately 1.

Therefore the decisive experiment is the truncated non-wrap Radon transform. If the sqrt(2) survives there, it is genuine mirror coherence. If it disappears, the effect is a coordinate artifact.

## 2. Surface term F(t,0): complete two-dimensional sum

For xi=0,

F(t,0)=sum_h A_t(h)
      =sum_{r,s} exp(2*pi*i*t D(r,s)/p).

This is a complete two-variable exponential sum over the Casoratian kernel.

Decompose the square into algebraic strata.

### (a) Diagonal stratum

s=r gives

D(r,r)=0.

Contribution:

S_diag=p.

This is only size p, hence after normalization contributes 1.

### (b) Mirror stratum

If the reflection relation gives

pi(p-1-r)=pi(r),

then the projective determinant

D(r,p-1-r)

vanishes.

If the cyclic implementation identifies this line exactly, it contributes another approximately p terms.

Contribution:

S_mirror ~= p.

### (c) Remaining bulk

The complement is

sum_{s notin {r,mirror(r)}} exp(2*pi*i*tD(r,s)/p).

If no additional algebraic locus exists, cancellation should reduce this to square-root scale:

O(p).

Hence the total surface size should be

F(t,0)=C_surface p + O(p^{1+o(1)}),

with C_surface determined by the number and multiplicity of forced zero/low-complexity strata.

The observed value

|F(t,0)|/p ~= 7-8

therefore cannot be explained by the diagonal alone. It indicates either:

1. several hidden structured strata,
2. coherent accumulation of many low-degree fibers,
3. a normalization effect from the cyclic extension.

## Final judgment

The bulk phenomenon is not a generic Gumbel anomaly. The random benchmark predicts the constant exactly, and the observed sqrt(2) factor points to either a variance-doubling symmetry or a boundary-induced correlation.

The required separating test is the non-wrap transform. It distinguishes a true Apéry mirror involution from cyclic-coordinate pollution.

The surface term is a separate object: a complete two-dimensional exponential sum. A flat value near 8 is evidence for a finite collection of rigid algebraic strata, not for a new growing obstruction. A discrepancy after removing all known diagonal/mirror strata would be direct evidence for an undiscovered trace-layer identity.
