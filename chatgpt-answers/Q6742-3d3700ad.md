# Q6742: slowly varying cocycle / integrability audit

## Verdict

The discrete-Painleve / zero-entropy direction is structurally interesting, but it does not currently produce the required rigidity statement. The reason is not that the recurrence lacks arithmetic structure; rather, the known linear collision growth is already explained by the second-order recurrence and Casoratian degree budget, and does not by itself imply the existence of a bounded-degree invariant surface.

The strongest certified conclusion is a negative one:

There is no justified derivation from the supplied facts of a bounded-degree rational invariant I(r,xi) whose fibers contain all Z_d. A Painleve-type interpretation would be an additional theorem, not a consequence of the current collision identities.

## 1. The slowly varying matrix idea

The transfer matrix is

M_s = [[0,1],[-beta_s,alpha_s]].

The entries are rational functions. Exact finite differences are rational identities:

beta_{s+1}-beta_s = ((s+1)^3(s+2)^3 - s^3(s+3)^3) / ((s+2)^3(s+1)^3)

and similarly alpha_{s+1}-alpha_s is a rational function with numerator of bounded degree.

However, mod p there is no metric notion of "small". The only invariant meaning of a perturbation estimate is algebraic degree control. The product

T_{r,d}=M_{r+d-1}...M_r

therefore remains a matrix with entries of controlled polynomial degree in r, which is exactly the existing N_d degree budget.

The naive m/r perturbation does not descend as an analytic estimate over F_p.

## 2. Fixed point correspondence

For fixed d, the return condition can be written as

xi_r in Fix(T_{r,d}).

The fixed point locus W_d is algebraic. This is a useful reformulation:

returns = incidences between the orbit graph and W_d.

But W_d has degree growing with d. Standard finite-field incidence theorems require bounded complexity. With d around sqrt(p)L, the degree is already mesoscopic and the constants become too large.

Decomposing W_d into vertical slices gives only the existing bound:

number of r solutions <= deg(N_d)=3(d-1).

Thus generic incidence machinery does not beat the degree budget.

## 3. Does composition through one bounded correspondence help?

This is the promising part, but the desired conclusion does not follow automatically.

The fact that T_{r,d} is a d-fold product of companion steps says the system is generated by a low-degree local rule. It does not imply that the d-fold composition has dynamical degree one in the algebraic-dynamics sense.

The observed bound

C_d <= 3(d-1)

comes from the scalar Casoratian solution N_d(r), not from a classification of the correspondence dynamics. It is therefore evidence for low complexity, but not yet a zero-entropy theorem.

A genuine integrable reduction would require an explicit object such as:

- a rational invariant I(r,xi);
- an invariant fibration preserved by the nonautonomous map;
- a bounded-degree surface containing all collision triples (r,d,xi).

No such object is currently derived.

## 4. What can actually be extracted

The sharpest unconditional partial statement is:

For every d, the fixed-point incidence curve W_d has collision intersection size at most 3(d-1), because the return determinant is N_d and deg N_d=3(d-1).

For a dyadic block d in (D/2,D], summing gives the old degree scale:

sum C_d << D^2.

The missing improvement is exactly a family-averaged cancellation statement over d. The fixed-point reformulation identifies the target object but does not produce cancellation.

## 5. Relation to the padding obstruction

A true invariant-surface theorem would be qualitatively stronger than the previous run-rigidity attempt. It could attack scattered padding because it couples different gaps d.

Run-rigidity only kills long runs inside one Z_d.

An invariant surface would potentially constrain the whole family {Z_d} simultaneously.

At present, the gap remains: scattered variable-gap pairings do not violate any known fixed-d degree statement.

## 6. Deliverable theorem status

Proposed theorem:

"The Apery transfer correspondence has algebraic entropy zero and all collision sets lie on bounded-degree invariant fibers."

Status: OPEN.

The currently proved replacement is:

"The collision sets are zeros of the two-variable Casoratian family N_d(r), with linear-in-d degree growth and a second-order recurrence in the gap variable."

This is a rigidity statement, but not enough to invoke Lang-Weil/Bombieri counting in the required family direction.

## Final answers

(i) Least confident step:

The least confident step is the dismissal of hidden integrability. The available evidence does not prove an invariant exists, but a deeper Gauss-Manin/isomonodromic description could reveal one. The exact obstruction is absence of a derived formula, not a contradiction.

(ii) Blind spot in the question:

The question may conflate two notions of low complexity: low degree of each return polynomial N_d and low algebraic entropy of the nonautonomous projective cocycle. The former is proved; the latter is a much stronger dynamical statement. Bridging them is precisely the missing theorem.
