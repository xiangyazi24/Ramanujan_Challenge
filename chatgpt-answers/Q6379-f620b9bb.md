## Bottom line

The algebraic factorization used by the project is supported exactly, with the same positive factor q(t)=1-34t+t^2, once one says explicitly that S_p is the normalized version of CFVZ’s existential B_p and that chi_p=(-6/p) is project notation. The identities hold globally in \mathbb F_p[t] for every prime p>3, with no exceptional finite t.

CFVZ does not supply the stronger interpretation suggested in the question: there is no paper-defined S_p trace function, no fixed rank-2 \ell-adic sheaf underlying it, and no stated quadratic twist of such sheaves. The exact primary-source content is a Kummer/function-field Galois calculation, a quadratic-cover square-class descent, and a classical second-order/{}_2F_1 ingredient. Any sheaf-theoretic twist statement requires an additional source or a new construction.
