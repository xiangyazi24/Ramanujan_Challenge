# Independent audit: T-adic families versus the tame Teichmüller Mellin aspect

## Executive verdict

[FACT] The Liu–Wan, Davis–Wan–Xiao, and Ren–Wan–Xiao–Yu T-adic theories interpolate finite characters of an additive pro-p Galois group such as Z_p or Z_{p^ell}. Their analytic variables are specializations T = chi(1)-1, or several such variables, where chi has p-power order and increasing p-power conductor.

[FACT] Ren's Newton slopes for twisted Artin–Schreier–Witt Towers is the closest paper to the requested tame aspect. It inserts a Teichmüller multiplicative twist omega^u, but it fixes u and varies the conductor of a separate additive character chi: Z_p -> C_p^*. Its main slope-stability theorem is therefore not a theorem in the variable u mod q-1.

[FACT] The modular eigencurve weight space for Z_p^* does contain all tame finite-order characters, but it is a disjoint union of p-1 open disks, one disk for each tame character. The spectral-halo theorem of Liu–Wan–Xiao fixes one tame component and varies the wild/cyclotomic coordinate on that disk. The pure tame characters are the T=0 centers of different disks, not points moving along one connected analytic parameter.

[FACT] Gabber–Loeser's Mellin transform constructs an ell-adic character scheme and coherent Mellin complexes, with coefficient characteristic ell != p. Katz's finite-field Mellin theory varies the finite multiplicative characters themselves and proves ell-adic/complex equidistribution. Neither theory constructs a defining-characteristic overconvergent F-isocrystal over the full tame character set with a theorem controlling the mod-p atom at zero.

[DEDUCTION] There is a tautological “single family” containing all omega^{-r}: the finite étale character scheme of F_p^*, or the disconnected full p-adic weight space. This does not create analytic coupling between the p-1 fibers. On a split finite étale scheme, an arbitrary subset of points can be the zero locus of a regular function. Thus merely packaging the twists into a family gives no bound on the number of nonordinary fibers.

[VERDICT] None of the audited theorems implies

for the stated fixed rank-3 Mellin system. A new theorem is required: a defining-characteristic, cross-tame-component residual anti-concentration theorem, or an equivalent uniform complexity/degree bound for the Hasse invariant as a function of the tame character.

This is a negative applicability verdict, not a claim that such a theorem is impossible.

# 3. Liu–Wan: the original T-adic exponential-sum variable

## Primary source

Chunlei Liu and Daqing Wan, T-adic exponential sums over finite fields.

- arXiv:0802.2589

- Primary arXiv PDF

- Algebra & Number Theory 3 (2009), 489–509

- DOI 10.2140/ant.2009.3.489

## Exact parameter

Definition 1.1, arXiv PDF p. 1. The paper takes a locally constant character psi of the additive group Z_p of order p^m and sets pi_psi=psi(1)-1. Its classical sums are p-power-order additive exponential sums.

Definition 1.2, arXiv PDF pp. 2–3. For a Laurent polynomial f, the T-adic sum uses the universal factor

Specialization T=pi_psi recovers the sum attached to psi. Thus the universal variable interpolates all finite additive characters of p-power order.

## Main theorem statements relevant to this audit

Theorem 1.4, arXiv PDF p. 3. The T-adic C-function is T-adic entire in the L-function variable; the T-adic L-function is T-adic meromorphic.

Theorem 1.5, arXiv PDF p. 3. The T-adic Newton polygon lies above the combinatorial q-Hodge polygon.

Theorems 2.8 and 2.9, arXiv PDF p. 7. For a nontrivial additive character psi, the specialized Newton polygon lies above the T-adic Newton polygon, which lies above the Hodge polygon. If equality with the Hodge polygon occurs for one nontrivial psi, the Laurent polynomial is rigid/T-adically ordinary and equality holds for all nontrivial additive psi.

The hypotheses are a Laurent polynomial over the unramified p-adic coefficient ring, with the coefficient conventions specified in the paper; nondegeneracy is invoked for the standard finite-degree polynomial conclusions.

## Applicability verdict

[NOT APPLICABLE TO r-ASPECT] Every quantifier “for all nontrivial characters” in these results concerns additive characters of Z_p of p-power order. It does not concern the p-1 multiplicative characters of F_p^*.

The distinction is structural:

- pi_psi=psi(1)-1 is small because psi(1) is a p-power root of unity;

- omega^{-r}(x) lies in mu_{p-1} and changes in a prime-to-p torsion direction;

- there is no specialization of the Liu–Wan variable T that changes r mod p-1 while keeping the additive character fixed.

The Hodge/Newton theorem therefore supplies no count of tame exponents r at which a different fixed Mellin trace becomes divisible by p.

The introduction, arXiv PDF p. 2, defines the p-adic weight space for continuous characters of Z_p^* and states that it is a union of p-1 open unit disks for odd p, indexed by characters of the torsion subgroup Delta.

## Applicability verdict

- choose a tame component eta;

Thus “the eigencurve varies p-adic characters” is too coarse a statement. It varies the connected pro-p coordinate after the tame character has been fixed.

## Exact obstruction for the present question

The desired event is residual:

Katz's theorem controls the normalized Frobenius conjugacy class after an ell-adic and then complex realization. Reduction at a prime above the defining characteristic is not a continuous class function on the compact monodromy group and is not determined by the complex value.

A nonzero algebraic integer may have a perfectly generic complex value and still lie in mathfrak p. Therefore complex/ell-adic equidistribution does not imply residual anti-concentration.

[VERDICT-KATZ] Katz can potentially prove zero density for literal equality of the algebraic/complex Mellin trace to zero, if the limiting trace measure has no atom there. It does not prove zero density for a nonzero trace that is divisible by p.

# 12. Why Newton-versus-Hodge theorems do not count nonordinary tame twists

A Newton polygon theorem and a zero-count theorem have different quantifiers.

The audited T-adic results have the form:

- fix f and often fix a tame twist u;

- vary an additive p-power-conductor character chi;

- bound or eventually determine the Newton polygon of L_f(omega^u,chi,s).

The desired statement has the form:

- fix the trace system and fix the absence of an additive-conductor variable;

- vary u=-r through all residues modulo p-1;

- count the u for which one specified Frobenius trace is divisible by p.

There is no logical implication between them without a theorem uniform in u that also identifies the first Hasse invariant.

Even a hypothetical statement

every u-twisted Newton polygon lies above one common Hodge polygon

would not bound the number of u at which equality fails. A lower bound can be strict at any prescribed subset of parameters unless the parameter dependence of the relevant coefficient is controlled.

Likewise, an identity for the Newton polygon of a product over all u records the multiset union of slopes. It does not identify which factor contributes a slope jump or how many first traces are zero modulo p.

### Terminology caution

For a general rank-3 F-crystal, trace divisible by p is not automatically synonymous with nonordinary. One needs an integral Frobenius polynomial and a theorem identifying the relevant first Hasse invariant or unit-root criterion with that trace reduction.

[UNSURE-PROJECT-NORMALIZATION] The project may have or be constructing this comparison for A_-; none of the audited T-adic or eigencurve theorems supplies it automatically.

# Final verdict

The literature separates into three genuinely different directions:

1. Additive T-adic towers: analytic variation of p-power conductor, not tame exponent.

1. Cyclotomic/eigencurve weight space: analytic variation inside one disk after fixing the tame component.

1. Finite-field Mellin character aspect: all tame characters vary, but in ell-adic/complex cohomology and equidistribution, not in a defining-characteristic family controlling reduction modulo p.

One can place all omega^{-r} in a finite étale scheme or in the disconnected union of p-adic weight disks. That is not enough. None of the audited Newton/Hodge or halo theorems bounds the number of r for which the rank-3 Mellin trace is zero modulo p.

Therefore the desired implication

does not follow from the existing cited theorems without a new residual anti-concentration or sublinear-complexity theorem in the tame character aspect.
