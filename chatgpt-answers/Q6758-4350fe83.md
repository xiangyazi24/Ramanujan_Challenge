## 2. Fixed d equation H_{r+d}-H_r = 0

The equation is

1/(r+1)+...+1/(r+d)=0 mod p.

For fixed d it is a rational equation whose numerator has degree at most d. Therefore the algebraic bound is O(d), but for d growing like sqrt(p)L this is too large. Existing reciprocal-sum and incomplete Kloosterman methods generally estimate exponential sums over reciprocal variables, not the exact zero set of a moving harmonic interval.

Garaev-Shparlinski results on congruences with intervals give strong bounds for several reciprocal-type systems, but not this one-dimensional translated interval family. citeturn0academia27

Verdict: fixed d has elementary degree control; averaged mesoscopic d is not covered.

## 3. Additive energy of the harmonic sequence

The energy quantity

E(H)=#{(a,b,c,d): H_a+H_b=H_c+H_d}

is the natural global object behind collision statistics. The literature contains exponential-sum estimates and distribution results for harmonic sums, but I did not find a theorem giving the required localized distance energy

#{(r,d): d in (D/2,D], H_{r+d}=H_r} << D^(2-eta).

Known harmonic distribution results do not provide the missing distance parameter.

Verdict: global additive information is partial; the strip estimate is open.

## 4. Distance distribution of collisions

Exact target:

#{(r,s): s-r in (D/2,D], H_s=H_r}

I found no theorem matching this shape. Existing papers address:

- distribution of values H_r;
- exponential sums involving H_r;
- representations by reciprocal sums;
- congruences in short intervals.

They do not give a localized return-time theorem for the harmonic walk.

Verdict: absent.

## Derksen/Lucas calibration

Derksen's p-automatic structure theorem applies to zero sets of constant-coefficient linear recurrence sequences over characteristic p. The harmonic walk is not such a recurrence: the increment 1/r is nonconstant, and the collision condition uses translated reciprocal intervals.

The Apery situation is even more structured but also more nonautonomous. A Lucas congruence for b_n gives digit structure for the scalar Apéry sequence, but in the window r<p there is only one base-p digit. Thus the digit factorization does not immediately produce a mesoscopic collision description.

The second solution c_n is the obstruction: it behaves like a derivative/jet object, carrying harmonic corrections. The Casoratian

Delta_{r,d}=b_r c_{r+d}-b_{r+d}c_r

is a Wronskian-type quantity, not a scalar Lucas sequence.

## Sharp statement available today

The strongest unconditional analogue from the harmonic side is:

For each fixed d,

#{r : H_{r+d}=H_r} <= d.

Proof: multiply the equation by the denominator product

prod_{j=1}^d (r+j).

The result is a nonzero polynomial in r of degree at most d (the leading term does not cancel). Hence it has at most d roots over F_p.

This mirrors the Apéry fixed-gap degree budget C_d <= 3(d-1).

What is missing is a joint theorem beating the sum of these fixed-gap bounds.

## Missing lemma (one lemma from a power saving)

A sufficient lemma would be:

For D <= p^(1/2+o(1)),

sum_{D/2<d<=D} #{r : N_d(r)=0 mod p}

<< D^(2-eta).

Equivalent harmonic prototype:

sum_{D/2<d<=D} #{r : H_{r+d}=H_r}

<< D^(2-eta).

This requires cancellation between the d-families, not stronger estimates on individual d.

## Final verdict

The Apery wall is not merely an isolated recurrence issue. The harmonic calibration already reaches the same frontier: value distribution is known, reciprocal congruences are known, but localized distance collision energy is not.

Therefore the missing theorem is genuinely a quenched growing-gap statement.
