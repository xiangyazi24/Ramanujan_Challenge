# 10. Consequences of the reported computation

The scan through 2{,}000{,}000—mean |Z_p| about 1, maximum 12, reflection pairing, and maximum |T_n|=3—is strong evidence for the truth of the pointwise theorem. It does not feed the determinant mechanisms above in the direction needed for a proof.

1. Bounded zero-fiber size cancels in the Smith quotient. With |Z_p|\le12, one has q_p\le6, so the full determinant uses at most p^6 and its fake kernel at most p^5. The quotient is still p.

1. Minimal fibers are maximally saturated. If a target fiber is exactly one reflection orbit, its only two-zero continuant has the universal factor p; after division there is no target factor.

1. Many own-prime bad rows do not give one common rank drop. Equation (3.1) shows that a cross minor needs the horizontal event p\mid B_{r_q}, not the already known event q\mid B_{r_q}.

1. The empirical bound |T_n|\le3 cannot come from recurrence dimension. The simultaneous lattice (5.4) has rank two for an arbitrary number of target primes; its covolume simply becomes their product.

1. Valuation amplification is not height amplification. Every extra exterior or defect layer either repeats the same prime factor or introduces a new local coordinate. The normalized logarithmic ledger remains \log R_n.

# 11. Sharp boundary for future work

Within the mechanisms named in the question, the following statement is now rigorous:

Saturated recurrence no-go. After removing universal reflection factors, every target-prime divisor produced by a nonzero determinant of finitely many homogeneous Apéry solutions comes from either (i) a fixed determinant of initial states or (ii) a non-reflection second zero in the same characteristic. Higher homogeneous exterior powers vanish. Every finite V1 jet prolongation adds a fresh homogeneous state, so its new determinant quotients are local defect coordinates, not forced zeros. Giving the entire reflection-paired zero fiber changes the full and fake-kernel determinant indices by the same amount; their transverse quotient is exactly R_n.

This does not rule out every conceivable identity specially tailored to the exact cubic coefficient P(m). It identifies what such an identity must add. Any successful recurrence-centered proof must establish at least one of the following genuinely new assertions:

- a p-independent family of non-reflection collision pairs whose saturated continuants cover all targets with total gap o(n/\log n);

- a strong horizontal correlation theorem showing that many target primes divide the same small collection of canonical continuant values;

- an Apéry-specific well-roundedness/last-successive-minimum theorem forcing a short vector outside the exact fake-evaluation kernel;

- a characteristic-zero formula coupling the derivative and higher defect coordinates across varying primes;

- a growing Newton–Padé/interpolation construction with a proved sublinear transverse height, not merely a small determinant or many short kernel vectors.

The new zero-fiber data are vertical upper bounds. None of them supplies one of these horizontal or transverse statements. Therefore the sharp conclusion is a no-go for the proposed recurrence/determinant route, together with Lemma 8.1 as the exact narrow opening that remains.
