Theorem (scope of the certified modules). Let a family of observations be generated solely from the Apéry order-two recurrence, its projective mod-p dynamics, and the certified doubled-period Newton-jet extension. Then there is no universal sublinear-height integer invariant forcing a relation among distinct target primes p=n-r.

Equivalently, these modules prove only vertical constraints at fixed p; they do not solve the horizontal target-collision problem.

A successful continuation must add one of:

1. a characteristic-independent identity coupling different target primes;

1. a genuinely new p-adic digit that vanishes for every target;

1. a global height-controlled carrier involving many primes simultaneously.

The current doubled-period machinery supplies none of these.
