# Mathlib v4.29.0 audit for Ramanujan Challenge P3.1 Lean formalization

## Executive result

The unconditional P3.1 proof DAG splits into:

1. real analysis of the regulator integral;

1. algebraic verification of the A-polynomial/path data;

1. exact endpoint evaluation.

Mathlib v4.29.0 contains enough infrastructure for the first layer (interval integrals, derivatives, continuity, FTC-style arguments), but it does not contain a ready-made Rogers dilogarithm/polylogarithm package matching the required identity. The dilogarithm primitive and its analytic continuation must be implemented locally.

The Lean bottleneck is therefore not integral; it is the explicit special-function library and the exact algebraic endpoint certificate.

# Final assessment

Mathlib v4.29.0 gives a realistic foundation for P3.1, but the proof is not a direct application of existing theorems. The missing infrastructure is:

1. a controlled real dilogarithm/Rogers dilogarithm library;

1. explicit branch management for the 7_2 parameterization;

1. reusable Sturm/root-isolation certificates.

The FTC and algebraic normalization portions are Mathlib-compatible today. The correct strategy is to formalize the finite explicit identity DAG, not to attempt a general K-theory or regulator framework.
