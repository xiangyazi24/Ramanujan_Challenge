## Realizability

The witness need not be merely a formal tail profile. Because D=o(N), one can choose 3(d-1)/2 disjoint interior \tau_d-orbits whose four involved indices have all cross-mirror separations larger than D. Assign one projective value to the four positions in each selected orbit, distinct values to different selected orbits, and distinct values to all remaining reflection pairs. Then:

- each selected orbit contributes exactly two lag-d bases;

- no extra lag at most D is created by cross-mirror pairs;

- the only other collisions at lags at most D are the automatic even fixed points.

There are enough values in \mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p) and enough indices because only O(D)=o(N) positions are specially used. Thus the strictness example is compatible with the symmetry model, not merely with an abstract list of counts.
