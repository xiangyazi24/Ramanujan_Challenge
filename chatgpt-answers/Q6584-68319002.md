# Q6584: Moving finite-difference basis and cone obstruction analysis

## 1. Coordinate evolution in the moving basis

Let the row vector evolve by

[

E_{n+1}=E_nA(n).

]

The proposed basis is the column basis

[

C(n)=[c_0(n),c_1(n),c_2(n)],

]

with

[

E_n=x_nC(n)^{-1},

]

where x_n is the row of basis coordinates. Then

[

x_{n+1}=x_nB(n),qquad B(n)=C(n)^{-1}A(n)C(n+1).

]

Thus the sign question is not about positivity of entries of E_n directly; it is whether the orbit of x_0 remains inside a cone whose image under C(n)^{-1} implies

[

E_{n,0}<0<E_{n,2}.

]

The computed skeleton

[

B(n)=begin{pmatrix}

1&0&p_n\

-1&1&-q_n\

0&-1&r_n

end{pmatrix},qquad p_n,q_n,r_n>0

]

induces

[

(x_0,x_1,x_2)mapsto

(x_0-x_1,;x_1-x_2,;p_nx_0-q_nx_1+r_nx_2).

]

This is a finite-difference evolution, not a positive cone evolution.

## 2. Natural candidate cone and why it fails without extra inequalities

The natural variation cone is

[

K={x_0ge x_1ge x_2ge0}.

]

The first two coordinates are preserved:

[

(x_0-x_1)ge0,qquad (x_1-x_2)ge0.

]

However the third coordinate requires

[

p_nx_0-q_nx_1+r_nx_2ge0.

]

Writing finite differences

[

d_0=x_0-x_1,quad d_1=x_1-x_2,quad x_2=s,

]

we get

[

x_0=s+d_0+d_1,quad x_1=s+d_1,

]

and therefore

[

x_2'=p_nd_0+(p_n-q_n)d_1+(p_n-q_n+r_n)s.

]

Hence an invariant simplicial cone of this form exists only if the exact inequalities

[

p_nge0,qquad p_n-q_nge0,qquad p_n-q_n+r_nge0

]

hold for every n.

The computed sign skeleton alone gives only p_n,q_n,r_n>0. It does not imply the two decisive inequalities

[

p_nge q_n,

qquad p_n+r_nge q_n.

]

Therefore the finite-difference cone is not certified by sign regularity alone.

## 3. The correct obstruction

A rigorous obstruction is obtained by considering the extreme rays of the difference cone:

1. d_0>0,d_1=s=0 gives image third coordinate p_n>0.

1. d_1>0,d_0=s=0 gives image third coordinate p_n-q_n.

1. s>0,d_0=d_1=0 gives image third coordinate p_n-q_n+r_n.

If either

[

p_n-q_n<0

]

or

[

p_n-q_n+r_n<0,

]

then the corresponding extreme ray leaves the cone in one step. No refinement of the same finite-difference cone can repair this, because the obstruction is already visible on a one-dimensional face.

Thus the moving basis provides a useful diagnostic, but it does not by itself prove the Catalan bracketing.

## 4. Relation to total positivity and oscillatory matrices

The exterior-square computation proves positivity/sign regularity of 2\times2 minors. This implies preservation of sign variation under appropriate hypotheses (strict total positivity or oscillatory powers), but it does not automatically imply preservation of a chosen coordinate sign pattern.

The missing theorem would need the form:

For a sequence of matrices A(n) and cones K_n,

[

A(n)K_{n+1}subseteq K_n

]

with a certified flag cone corresponding to the desired sign variation.

Positive exterior minors only establish that ordered projective coordinates do not cross. They do not establish that the orbit remains between the two boundary rays corresponding to the Catalan constant.

A concrete certificate must therefore be a cone inclusion, not a variation-diminishing slogan.

## 5. Formalization-ready certificate

The appropriate Lean-level statement is:

Define rational polyhedral cones

[

K_n={xinmathbb R^3: L_{n,i}(x)ge0, i=1,2,3}

]

where rows L_{n,i} are explicit rational linear forms. Prove:

1. Initial membership:

[

x_0in K_0.

]

1. Transition preservation:

[

forall n,quad B(n)K_{n+1}subseteq K_n.

]

Equivalently it suffices to check the finite set of extreme rays of K_{n+1}:

[

L_{n,i}(B(n)v_j)ge0

]

for all generators v_j.

1. Output separation:

[

C(n)^{-1}K_nsubseteq{E:E_0<0<E_2}.

]

This is the required algebraic certificate.

## 6. Verdict

The moving Bernstein/difference basis reveals the correct geometry: the missing invariant object is a time-dependent simplicial cone, not a coordinate rectangle. However the currently known data (positive p_n,q_n,r_n and positive exterior minors) are insufficient.

A successful proof must additionally establish explicit rational inequalities equivalent to cone invariance, most naturally

[

p_n-q_nge0,qquad p_n-q_n+r_nge0

]

or replace the basic difference cone by a sharper n-dependent cone with verified extreme-ray inequalities.

Therefore:

- The variation-diminishing route is promising and gives the right formal target.

- Total positivity alone does not close the gap.

- The decisive missing certificate is a finite family of rational cone inequalities checked under B(n).

- Without such inequalities, sign-regularity cannot prevent loss of Catalan bracketing.
