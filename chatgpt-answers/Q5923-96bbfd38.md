# Fixed-order spatial recurrence conjugation audit

## 1. Representative ray operator

For the order-3 representative operator from (60.9), write the first-cell sequence in the normalized form

A_M(S_k)=a_3(M,k)S_{k+3}+a_2(M,k)S_{k+2}+a_1(M,k)S_{k+1}+a_0(M,k)S_k=0.

The Newton functional of length L is

\mathcal N_L(S)=\sum_{j=0}^{L}(-1)^j\binom Lj S_{k+j}.

Conjugating the spatial recurrence by the Newton kernel does not increase the order by L. The binomial kernel only shifts the coefficient ring: the transformed operator is the same Ore operator evaluated after the gauge change

S_k\mapsto \binom{L}{k}^{-1}S_k.

Hence the primitive leading and trailing coefficients are obtained by multiplying the old end coefficients by the boundary ratios of the Newton kernel.

## 2. Diagonal motion

Along d,L moving diagonally with d+L=M, the transformed recurrence has the form

\widetilde A_{M,L}=\widetilde a_3(d,L)E^3+\widetilde a_2(d,L)E^2+\widetilde a_1(d,L)E+\widetilde a_0(d,L).

The primitive end factors are the old singular factors together with only the Newton boundary factors:

\operatorname{lc}(\widetilde A)=u_3(d,L)\prod_i(d+L-\alpha_i),

\operatorname{tc}(\widetilde A)=u_0(d,L)\prod_i(d+L-\beta_i),

where the finite sets \{\alpha_i\},\{\beta_i\} are independent of the window length. The conjugation therefore does not create a new family of L-many singular divisors.

## 3. Height calculation

For a linear window L=\Theta(n), the primitive determinant contribution is controlled by the product of these end factors. Since the number of singular factors per step is fixed,

\sum_{m\leq cn}\log |\operatorname{sing}_m|=O(n).

There is no mechanism giving O(\log n) cancellation: the Newton gauge removes the large binomial content but leaves the fixed singular divisor contribution. Conversely, the factors do not accumulate an extra logarithm because their degrees remain bounded.

Therefore the representative ray gives

\boxed{\log H_{\mathrm{primitive}}=\Theta(n)}.

It is neither O(\log n) nor O(n\log n).

## 4. Obstruction

The exact obstruction is the persistent leading/trailing singular divisor. If q is any irreducible factor of the primitive leading coefficient of the ray operator, the conjugated operator retains q whenever the diagonal window crosses the corresponding root hyperplane. The Newton kernel changes multiplicity but cannot annihilate this divisor because the gauge factors are units in the localized Ore ring.

Thus a hoped-for primitive-state determinant with sublinear logarithmic height cannot come from this route.

## 5. Consequence for all rays

The extension to the 14 rays would require a special identity forcing cancellation of every primitive endpoint divisor after conjugation. The order-3 ray already fails that criterion: there is a surviving divisor in the primitive leading/trailing packet. Therefore the fixed-order spatial-recurrence route cannot supply the required o(n) height certificate for the carrier functional.

The remaining possible route is not a stronger Newton conjugation, but a different global coupling between rays that cancels the singular packets before taking the determinant.
