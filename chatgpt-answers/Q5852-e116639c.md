# P3.2 pure-cross carrier attack audit

## Verdict

The proposed pure-cross carrier mechanism does not currently produce the required `o(n)` height carrier. The obstruction is not a missing local identity: the local Apéry recurrence gives strong two-prime divisibility statements, but the available carriers remain of linear exponential height after summing short gaps.

The surviving statement is:

*For adjacent top-half targets `p<ell=p+h`, `h<=A log n`, the recurrence/Casoratian structure gives a natural carrier divisible by `p ell`, but every explicit carrier found so far has height comparable to the original Apéry state or factorial denominators, not `o(n)`.*

## Attempted carrier from the actual recurrence

Let

\[
 a_r b_{r+1}-a_{r+1}b_r=-\frac{6}{(r+1)^3}
\]

be the Apéry companion Casoratian. For a pure-cross pair

\[
p=n-r,\qquad \ell=n-(r+h),
\]

with

\[
p\mid b_r,\qquad \ell\mid b_{r+h},
\]

one obtains the standard two-point eliminant

\[
C_{r,h}=b_{r+h}U_r-b_rU_{r+h}
\]

where `U` is the rational companion coordinate chosen to clear the recurrence denominators. Then

\[
p\ell\mid C_{r,h}.
\]

However the size bound is

\[
\log |C_{r,h}|\leq \log |b_{r+h}|+O(h\log n),
\]

and the Apéry growth gives

\[
\log b_{r+h}=\Theta(r).
\]

Therefore this carrier has linear height, not `o(n)`.

## Why the short-gap sum does not repair it

For the requested window

\[
0<h\leq A\log n,
\]

there are only `O(log n)` possible gaps. Summing the above bounds gives

\[
\sum_{h\leq A\log n}\log |C_{r,h}|
 =O((\log n)n),
\]

which is worse than the required

\[
o(n).
\]

The factor `p ell` gained from the pure-cross condition contributes only

\[
\log(p\ell)=O(\log^2 n)
\]

per pair, so it cannot compensate for the linear carrier height.

## Counterexample to the proposed compression principle

A local carrier compression would need an additional identity

\[
\widetilde C_{r,h}=C_{r,h}/D_{r,h}
\]

where

1. `D_{r,h}` is forced by the Apéry initial state,
2. `D_{r,h}` removes essentially all exponential growth,
3. `p ell | \widetilde C_{r,h}` remains true.

No such denominator exists from the recurrence module alone. The normalized recurrence has unit polar coefficients and admits arbitrary squarefree simultaneous hole patterns in the abstract local model. Hence local divisibility does not imply a low-height global carrier.

This matches the previous separation analysis: fixed quotient/local endpoint towers reconstruct the same diagonal integer and do not create horizontal separation.

## Required missing ingredient

A successful proof would need a genuinely new identity of the form

\[
p\ell\mid H_{r,h},\qquad
\log |H_{r,h}|=o(n),
\]

where `H` uses the special Apéry initial state rather than only the recurrence module.

The known surviving route is therefore not a two-prime Casoratian carrier but a horizontal non-alignment theorem for the family of pure-cross zero sets.

## Conclusion

No explicit `o(n)` pure-cross carrier identity was found. The proposed mechanism fails at the height inequality step: all recurrence-generated carriers retaining the `p ell` divisibility have exponential Apéry height. A future positive result must exploit additional arithmetic of the initial Apéry solution beyond the P-recursive relation itself.
