## Anchored-star obstruction

For the anchored-star rank strata, the constant-term expansion has to be evaluated after imposing the actual Laurent constraints. A configuration count alone does not control the contribution because the star variables can share the same anchor and preserve a high-dimensional diagonal contribution.

The first unresolved formula is therefore the passage from the anchored-star constant-term sum to a nontrivial rank/dimension saving. In particular, the required statement is not merely

[

]

but an estimate showing that every surviving anchored-star contribution has Laurent dimension strictly smaller than the unrestricted contribution by a linear amount.
