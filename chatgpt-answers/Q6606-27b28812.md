\begin{theorem}[Q-sigma one-half bound]\label{thm:qsigma-half}
For the Apery projective orbit one has
$Q_p(\Delta)\leq (27/4)H^{5/2}$, where $H=\min(\Delta,p-3)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the renewal coordinates and split boxes of length $D$.
For a box containing $q$ renewal points, the pointwise Cauchy estimate is
$C(d,2)\leq qL_r+((q-1)/2)d$. Transitivity gives the injection
$T(h,k)\leq R_{k-h}$. The same-box contribution satisfies
$B_d\leq\min(H,q(D-d))$. Summing gives
$Q_H\leq qHS_1(D-1)+((q-1)/2)S_1(H)$ with
$q=\lceil H/D\rceil$. Taking $D=\lceil\sqrt H\rceil$ and the linear renewal
bound yields the stated constant.
\end{proof}

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}

Reindexing gives $\sum_g C_p(a,g)\leq R_aM_D$ and the reflected estimate.
Using $M_D\leq4D^{2/3}$ and summing the strip gives the bound.

\[

\begin{proof}
