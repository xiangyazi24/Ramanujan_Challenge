# Full-margin Apéry cross-minor saturation audit

The complete theorem target is a conditional endpoint-divisor statement: maximal first-cell margin saturation appears to remove the moving Kummer tail because the translated primitive Plücker minors form a long finite-difference chain whose determinantal divisor is controlled by boundary evaluations. The data does not prove this local divisor; it identifies the exact lemma required.

## Candidate local lemma

For the translated family

[

E_s=E_{d_0+s,L},quad 0le s<m-1,

]

with maximal margin (m), prove after removing the universal target factors that

[

gcd(E_0,ldots,E_{m-2})mid Cprod_{alphain A}(a_alpha n+b_alpha D+c_alpha N),

]

where (A) is a fixed finite set of boundary forms arising from the shell moment range (n-1,ldots,n+4), and (C) is an absolute content.

The observed factors fit this model: 61,101,151,191,13,67 are consistent with evaluations of fixed affine forms involving endpoint offsets and moment boundaries, but this remains a proof target.

## Dyadic assembly conditional on the lemma

Choose cores with geometric sizes (H_j). For each core require the smaller first-cell margin satisfy

[

m_jge cN_j.

]

The number of translated rows is then linear in the core width, so every target prime in the core is retained while nuisance contributes only the logarithm of the fixed boundary divisor. Summing over (O(log n)) scales gives

[

sum_j O(log n)=O((log n)^2)=o(n).

]

## Proof routes

The shortest route is not recurrence theory but a primitive Plücker lattice calculation:

1. Express the carriers as coefficient rows of the Newton matrix.

1. Apply horizontal and vertical Pascal identities.

1. Compute the saturated Smith determinantal divisor of the translated rectangle.

1. Show all interior columns are eliminated by finite-difference Bezout combinations.

1. Reduce the surviving divisor to endpoint columns.

The remaining gap is proving that endpoint columns have bounded polynomial height rather than exponential transition-product height.
