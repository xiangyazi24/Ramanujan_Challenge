## 2. The quadratic-form premise uses the wrong coordinate space

For any column vector `x`, one has

```text
x^T J g x = det(x,gx).
```

But this identity is relevant only if `g x` is the vector at the later time. Here it is not.

The transfer product acts on the time-state vector of one solution, for example

```text
w_n(y) = (n^3 y_{n-1}, y_n)^T.
```

It does not act on

```text
v_n = (b_n,c_n)^T,
```

which records two different solutions at one time. The two spaces are being silently identified in qQUAD.

If one applies `x^T J g x` to `x = w_r(b)`, the result is

```text
det(w_r(b), w_{r+h}(b)),
```

which is a same-solution area. It is not

```text
det((b_r,c_r),(b_{r+h},c_{r+h})).
```

The companion solution is missing.

One can conjugate the transfer product by `F_r`, but then the conjugating matrix itself contains the entire incoming orbit history. That merely reintroduces the original history/future coupling in a different coordinate system.

Verdict on the claimed quadratic form: FATAL. The correct group observable is the linear coordinate `-g_21` on the normalized transfer product.

This correction is not bad news. It makes the exact group-language reformulation cleaner:

```text
f_h(r) := Delta_{r,h} = - (T_{r,h})_21.
```

The obstruction is no longer the observable. The obstruction is the clocked path `h -> T_{r,h}`.

## 5. The true geometry of the difference curves

```text

N_h(-j)
```

Also

```text
[D_h(r)/(r+j)^3]_{r=-j}
```

Hence the principal polar term is

```text
f_h(r)
 = b_{j-1} b_{h-j} /(r+j)^3 + lower polar terms
```

at `r=-j`.

## 6. Is the map (r,h) -> (r,G_h(r)) injective?

No pointwise injectivity theorem follows from degree growth.

Over `Q`, the maps

```text
r -> G_h(r)
```

are distinct as rational or polynomial maps because their continuant entries have different degrees. This is a generic-function statement.

At one fixed `r in F_p`, equality can occur. If `k>h`, the cocycle identity gives

```text
G_k(r) = G_{k-h}(r+h) G_h(r).
```

Hence

```text
G_k(r)=G_h(r)
```

would mean

```text
G_{k-h}(r+h)=I
```

in the chosen normalization, or a scalar matrix in `PGL_2`.

This is an identity-return condition for a tail word. It imposes simultaneous continuant equations, but it is not ruled out by

```text
gcd(N_m,N_{m+1})=1,
```

because the off-diagonal conditions involve shifted polynomials such as `U_{m-1}(s)` and `U_{m-1}(s+1)`.

The determinant does not recover `h` either. In the unnormalized model,

```text
det G_h(r) = product_{j=1}^h (r+j)^6,
```

and sixth powers can collide. In the normalized `SL_2` model, every determinant is `1`.

The safe replacement for injectivity is only this:

```text
For fixed h!=k, the two maps are distinct over Q.
At a good prime, their pointwise intersections are cut out by
nonzero polynomial equations of degree O(max(h,k)).
```

A uniform bound on all self-intersections as `h,k` grow is itself a new family theorem. It cannot be assumed as part of the reparametrization.

## 7. What is the path variety really?

Use the normalized matrices `A_u`. Define the skew map

```text
Phi(u,g) = (u+1, A_u g).
```

Then

```text
Phi^h(r,I) = (r+h, T_{r,h}).
```

For one fixed `h`, introduce chain variables


```text
u_0 = r,
```

After denominators are cleared and the forbidden divisors are removed, this is a triangular complete intersection. In fact it is isomorphic to its starting line: every later variable is uniquely determined by `r`.

This sounds favorable but gives no cancellation. Imposing the endpoint observable


and pulling it back through the triangular graph gives exactly

```text
f_h(r)=a.
```

The terminal divisor therefore has degree `3h` and `h` triple poles. The complexity has not disappeared; it has moved from the recursion equations into the pullback of the endpoint hyperplane.

There are two further fatal points.

First, there is no single finite-type path variety with variable length. The family of all finite chains is a difference scheme or an ind-object. Deligne's theorem applies to a fixed finite-type variety and a fixed bounded-conductor sheaf, not to an object whose number of chain variables grows with `h`.

Second, the condition

```text
1 <= h <= H
```

is not algebraic of bounded complexity over `F_p`. If one adds the endpoint clock `u=r+h`, then the allowed clock differences satisfy

```text
u-r in {1,...,H}.
```

The corresponding algebraic set is the union of `H` hyperplanes, or is cut out by

```text
product_{j=1}^H (u-r-j)=0,
```

which has degree `H`. Finite fields have no archimedean notion of bounded height that converts this interval into a bounded-degree algebraic condition.

Verdict on an `h`-independent complete-intersection Weil bound: FATAL. The graph is simple only before the endpoint condition and clock truncation are imposed.

## 8. A cheap no-gauge obstruction

One possible hope would be that the skew cocycle is rationally conjugate to a constant group translation. In the unnormalized `GL_2` model this is impossible for an elementary reason.

Suppose there were a rational matrix `C(u)` and a constant `A in GL_2` with

```text
M_u = C(u+1) A C(u)^(-1).
```

Taking determinants gives

```text
(u+1)^6
 = det(A) det(C(u+1))/det(C(u)).
```

For a nonzero rational function `d(u)`,

```text
d(u+1)/d(u) -> 1
```

as `u -> infinity`. The right side above would instead grow like `u^6`. Contradiction.

Thus the `GL_2` cocycle is not a rational coboundary of a constant matrix.

Projectivizing and dividing by `(u+1)^3` removes this determinant obstruction, but it does not produce a conjugacy. A `PGL_2` gauge would have to solve a nontrivial rational difference equation and absorb the moving singular divisor. No such gauge is present in the project, and the growth of the pole set under iteration is direct evidence against a bounded-complexity one.

## 9. Averaging over r first does not produce a unipotent equidistribution theorem

The base map `u -> u+1` is unipotent, but the full skew map is not a unipotent translation on a homogeneous space. The fibre motion is multiplication by a changing semisimple matrix `A_u`.

There is also an exact shift identity showing what Weyl differencing in `r` really does. For `a>=1`, cocycle factorization gives

```text
T_{r,h+a} = T_{r+h,a} T_{r,h}
```

and also

```text
T_{r,h+a} = T_{r+a,h} T_{r,a}.
```

Therefore

```text
T_{r+a,h}
 = T_{r+h,a} T_{r,h} T_{r,a}^(-1).
```

Consequently

```text
f_h(r+a) = -[T_{r+h,a} T_{r,h} T_{r,a}^(-1)]_21.
```

For fixed small `a`, this is a linear combination of the four entries of the long word `T_{r,h}`, with coefficients supplied by two short endpoint words. It is not a rational phase of degree `O(a)`. The long `h`-dependence remains in the middle matrix.

The pole calculation gives the same warning. The principal coefficient of `f_h` at `r=-j` is

```text
b_{j-1} b_{h-j}.
```

A shift difference `f_h(r+a)-f_h(r)` can lose its long pole set only if a long list of nontrivial multiplicative identities among these Apery coefficients causes principal-part cancellation. No such identity is known or implied by the Casoratian.

### The best generic fixed-h estimate

For good primes and `t!=0`, the rational function `t f_h(r)` has `h` poles of order `3` and is regular at infinity. Since `p>3`, it is not of Artin-Schreier form. The standard conductor calculation gives a complete-sum bound of shape

```text
|sum_{r regular} e_p(t f_h(r))|
 <= (4h+O(1)) sqrt(p).
```

The nonwrapping window differs from the complete regular domain by only `O(h)` points, so the same scale holds there.

Summing this by the triangle inequality gives only

```text
|B_t(H)| << H^2 sqrt(p).
```

This is the honest generic theorem currently available from one-variable Weil. To reach

```text
|B_t(H)| << sqrt(pH) p^eps
```

one needs cancellation simultaneously in the clocked family, not another application of fixed-h Weil.

### Why the standard external frameworks do not apply

The relevant theorem classes all assume fixed complexity:

1. Green-Tao quantitative polynomial-orbit theorems concern polynomial sequences in a fixed nilpotent group and a fixed nilmanifold. The present fibre group is `SL_2`, and the sequence is a noncommutative time-dependent product, not a polynomial nilsequence of bounded step.
2. Tao-Ziegler inverse theorems over finite fields describe functions with large fixed-order Gowers norm by fixed-degree polynomial phases. Here the degree/conductor of `f_h` grows with `h`; applying enough Weyl differencing to eliminate it would require growing order.
3. Bourgain-Gamburd type expansion concerns random walks or convolutions generated by a fixed set. The word `A_{r+h-1}...A_r` is deterministic, uses each clock label once, and has no stationary convolution operator.
4. Short trace-function estimates require a trace function of bounded conductor. Here the conductor is at least linear in `h` because the pole divisor contains `h` moving points.
5. Translation by `1` on `F_p` has pure additive characters as eigenfunctions and no spectral gap. There is no black-box finite-field unipotent mixing theorem that forces cancellation for an arbitrary observable.

## 11. The one lemma to prove first

Before attempting family cancellation of Frobenius traces, prove a component-budget theorem for the covers

```text
f_h: P^1 -> P^1,
f_h(r) = N_h(r)/D_h(r).
```

A precise target is:

```text
[PATH-COVER-RIGIDITY]

For good characteristics and h!=k, the covers f_h and f_k have no common
nontrivial geometric quotient. Equivalently, the generic fibre product
f_h(x)=f_k(y) is geometrically connected.

For h=k, the number of geometric components of
f_h(x)=f_h(y) is bounded by an absolute constant.

More generally, if c_{h,k} is the relevant geometric component count, then

sum_{h,k<=H} (c_{h,k}-1) << H.
```

Small explicit exceptions such as `h=1` should be listed rather than hidden; for a cubic power map the self-fibre product already has several components over the algebraic closure.

Why this lemma comes first:

1. Every extra component contributes a main term of size about `p` to `N_coinc(H)`.
2. `[COINC]` allows only an aggregate excess of order `pH`.
3. Therefore a positive density of cross-pairs with extra components would make `[COINC]` false, independently of all Weil-error cancellation.
4. The lemma is algebraic and attackable with the exact pole divisor, the renewal identity, and adjacent coprimality.
5. If it is proved, the problem cleanly separates into an algebraic main-term theorem and a genuinely analytic family-trace variance theorem.

The pole divisor

```text
3[-1] + 3[-2] + ... + 3[-h]
```

is the best starting invariant. A common quotient must organize two consecutive pole blocks of different lengths into compatible fibres. The renewal identity can then be used to exclude hidden decompositions that pole combinatorics alone does not see.
