# 1. Formal calculation in u,r,X

Use
\[
T=1-r^2,\quad U=u,\quad V=u/X,\quad W=(1-uX)^{-1}.
\]

The quadratic relation gives
\[
r^2+\frac ru-1=0.
\]
Differentiating:
\[
2r\,dr+\frac{r}{u^2}du+\frac1u dr=0,
\]
so
\[
dr=-\frac{r}{u(2ur+1)}du.
\]
Since
\[
T=1-r^2,
\]
we have
\[
d\log T=-\frac{2r}{1-r^2}dr.
\]
Substitution of the quadratic relation reduces this to a rational multiple of \(du/u\).

The individual Rogers differentials are:

\[
dR(U)=\frac12(\log u\,d\log(1-u)-\log(1-u)d\log u),
\]

\[
dR(V)=\frac12(\log(u/X)d\log(1-u/X)-\log(1-u/X)d\log(u/X)),
\]

\[
dR(W)=\frac12(\log W d\log(1-W)-\log(1-W)d\log W).
\]

For W:
\[
W=(1-uX)^{-1},
\]
therefore
\[
1-W=-\frac{uX}{1-uX}.
\]
Hence
\[
d\log W=-d\log(1-uX),
\]
and
\[
d\log(1-W)=d\log u+d\log X-d\log(1-uX).
\]

These are exact identities. No branch choice has entered.

# 2. Required logarithmic product identities

The cancellations require identities among shape logarithms. The algebraic identities are:

\[
V\cdot X=u,
\]

\[
W^{-1}=1-uX,
\]

\[
(1-W)=-uXW,
\]

and from the r relation,

\[
T=1-r^2=\frac r u
\]
(using \(r^2+r/u-1=0\)).

Therefore:

\[
\log T=\log r-\log u
\]
modulo \(2\pi i\) periods.

The formal differential calculation can only use
\[
d\log T=d\log r-d\log u.
\]

# 3. Coefficient calculation

Before imposing gluing relations, the sum
\[
S=dR(T)+dR(U)+dR(V)+dR(W)
\]
has the form

\[
S=A(u,X)d\log u+B(u,X)d\log X,
\]
where A and B contain logarithmic factors:

\[
A=\frac12\left(\log\frac{T}{1-T}+\log\frac{U}{1-U}+\cdots\right),
\]

\[
B=\frac12\left(\log\frac{V}{1-V}-\log\frac{W}{1-W}\right).
\]

The B coefficient does not vanish identically by algebraic simplification alone. It vanishes only after the tetrahedral edge equations are inserted.

The required gluing relations are therefore of the form

\[
\sum_j \epsilon_j d\log z_j=0,
\]

and

\[
\sum_j\epsilon_j d\log(1-z_j)=0.
\]

Without these, the chart is just four rational functions and not a triangulation certificate.

# 4. Substitution u=(L+X^3)/(X(L+X)), X=M^2

Compute
\[
\log u=\log(L+X^3)-\log X-\log(L+X).
\]
Thus

\[
d\log u
=d\log(L+X^3)-d\log X-d\log(L+X).
\]

With
\[
X=M^2,

\[

The desired final expression is
\[
\pm(\log X\,d\log L-\log L\,d\log X).

However the raw Rogers sum leaves residual logarithms involving
\[

The exact residue is the linear combination killed by the missing edge equations.

Therefore the answer is:

\[
S\neq \pm(\log Xd\log L-\log Ld\log X)

using only the chart identities.
