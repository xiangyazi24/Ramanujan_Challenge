# 10. Scope boundary and the only honest reopenings

The theorem does not claim that no arbitrary polynomial in the actual Apéry values can ever be small. It rules out the exact class in which:

1. nearby states are reduced using the rank-two recurrence;

1. recurrence-only denominator and singular factors are primitively saturated;

1. p- and q-divisibility is a formal consequence of the two endpoint zero specializations;

1. Archimedean smallness is forced by the universal dominant rank-one diagonal, equivalently by determinant or Plücker contraction.

A successful construction must therefore leave this class in a substantive way. The genuine reopenings are:

- an Apéry-initial-state-specific identity whose leading cancellation is not in the universal diagonal ideal;

- a second selective local equation coupling the opposite quotient residues, rather than another recurrence-only bracket;

- a singular-step invariant proved to retain prime-specific information after all universal Smith content is divided out;

- or a nonlocal arithmetic relation whose smallness uses special arithmetic of the companion approximation to zeta(3), with a quantitative proof strong enough to exclude an exponentially fine accidental cancellation.

Merely taking higher symmetric powers, multiplying more Casoratians, alternating more nearby rows, or using another complete Plücker contraction cannot work: after endpoint reduction these operations remain in Z[Delta], where the pure-cross intersection is exactly pq Z[Delta].
