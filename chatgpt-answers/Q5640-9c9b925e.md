## P3.2 terminology to specify

To avoid ambiguity, state which object is meant:

```text

R_n = product of selected large target primes

```

Also specify whether the interval is top-half `(n/2,n]`, all middle primes
`(sqrt(n),n]`, or a fixed quotient shell. These are not interchangeable.

## What a satisfactory P3.2 answer must audit

1. **Every-n quantifiers.** A density-one or shell-average estimate is not a pointwise proof.
2. **Moving modulus.** For a target prime, `p=n-r`; reductions must retain this relation.
3. **p-safe degree.** Polynomial/binomial-basis congruences require degrees below `p`, unless a separate Lucas/carry analysis is supplied.
4. **p-safe denominators.** Any denominator containing a candidate prime invalidates direct reduction modulo that prime.
5. **Nonzero carrier.** Zero is divisible by every prime but gives no bound.
6. **Cleared integer height.** The height of the rational expression before denominator clearing is irrelevant.
7. **Selective versus universal factors.** A carrier containing the full candidate primorial at linear height is not a compression.
8. **Actual-state input.** Universal recurrence identities alone permit adversarial states; a positive theorem must identify what is special about the Apéry initial state.
