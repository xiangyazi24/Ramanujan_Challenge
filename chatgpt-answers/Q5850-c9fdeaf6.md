# Q5850 Left seam attack audit

## Scope

This note analyzes the left-active maximal-margin case `d-1=floor((n-1)/2)` as a Cartier seam problem. The main conclusion is a separation between the exact finite-difference algebra and the remaining primitive-content theorem.

## 1. Left seam normalization

Let the carrier be

\[
G_{d,L}(Y)=\sum_{i=0}^{L}(-1)^i\binom{d+i}{i}\binom{d+L+1}{L-i}Y_{d+i}.
\]

At the left maximal margin the admissible indices satisfy

\[
d+i\le \lfloor (n-1)/2\rfloor+L,
\]

so the Cartier quotient-one and quotient-two sectors meet only through the endpoint terms. Splitting the sum into the two quotient classes gives

\[
G_{d,L}=B_0(d,L)+B_1(d,L)+B_2(d,L),
\]

where each B_j is the contribution of the residue class modulo the Cartier quotient. The interior terms telescope under the finite-difference operator; only the boundary residues survive.

## 2. Exact finite-difference shell form

For a sequence W with primitive shell support,

\[
\Delta^{L+1}W_d=\sum_{i=0}^{L+1}(-1)^{L+1-i}\binom{L+1}{i}W_{d+i}.
\]

The seam identity has the form

\[
G_{d,L}(W)=U_{d,L}(\Delta^{L+1}W_d)+\sum_{r\in\partial(d,L)} c_r(d,L)W_r,
\]

where \(\partial(d,L)\) consists only of the two Cartier boundary faces. The coefficients \(c_r\) are integral after the same binomial saturation used in the primitive carrier definition.

## 3. Primitive-content target

The needed lemma is:

> For maximal left margin, the saturated lattice generated by the quotient-one and quotient-two seam rows has primitive divisor dividing
> \[
> C\prod_{j=0}^{4}(n+j)^{e_j}\prod_{x\in\{d,d+L+1\}}(x+1)^{f_x},
> \]
> with absolute exponents bounded independently of n.

This is not proved by the finite-difference identity alone. It requires a Smith-form computation for the boundary coefficient lattice.

## 4. Hostile checks

The boundary audit should verify:

- n=300: left seam factors reduce to the same small-support content seen in the full-margin experiments.
- n=321: the quotient-two boundary terms contribute no new moving prime support.

These checks support the lemma but do not prove it.

## 5. Proof route

The shortest rigorous route is:

1. Write the seam coefficient matrix for quotient-one/two boundary packets.
2. Compute its primitive determinantal divisor over \(\mathbb Z[n,d,L]\).
3. Remove universal factorial/binomial content.
4. Prove the remaining resultant is a bounded-degree polynomial in the endpoint variables.

The missing step is therefore a primitive Plucker/Smith theorem for the left Cartier seam, not another local divisibility argument.

## Verdict

The left seam has the same favorable structure as the full-margin saturation route: interior cancellation is exact, and only boundary primitive content remains. The local lemma is plausible but is still a proof target; the experiments alone do not establish the endpoint divisor.
