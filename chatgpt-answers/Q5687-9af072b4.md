## Final assessment

LAC remains open. The exact affine relation d=ah or (a+1)h is valuable, but it helps only when one characteristic sees both endpoints. On the pure-cross locus it produces an invertible transfer and two free quotient coordinates, not a common root.

The strongest theorem obtained here is the uniform degenerate estimate

plus the exact product-ring elimination no-go for recurrence-only two-modulus resultants. The n=321 audit shows that the actual Apéry sequence is not merely an arbitrary reflected zero mask—its targets divide one common coefficient—but also shows that this extra structure currently appears only in exponentially tall certificates.

A successful proof must therefore discover a new distinguished-state identity that couples the two divided endpoint residues across q and q+h, or prove an equivalent local anti-clustering theorem for prime divisors of one Apéry coefficient. Nothing in the present same-characteristic continuant, vertical zero count, or generic prime-pair sieve provides that bridge.
