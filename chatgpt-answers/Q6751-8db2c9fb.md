## (ii) What the harmonic proofs use and where Apéry breaks

The harmonic increment sequence has a special property:

1. 1/n is a rational function of the endpoint variable.

1. Window sums can be converted into rational-function equations.

1. Completion transforms many averaged endpoint problems into classical exponential sums with multiplicative characters/Kloosterman structure.

The proof leverage is not merely that the increments are structured. It is that the whole family

(r,d) -> sum_{n=r+1}^{r+d} 1/n

has a low-complexity algebraic description in the endpoint variables.

For Apéry increments

6/(n^3 b_{n-1} b_n),

there is a telescoping identity inside b-nonzero blocks, but the denominator sequence b_n has no known one-digit product formula analogous to n. The p-Lucas/Gessel phenomenon is a high-digit decomposition:

b_{mp+r} relates to b_m b_r.

It does not linearize the local window n -> n+d inside one base-p digit. Therefore the transfer breaks exactly at the step where harmonic methods complete a rational endpoint sum into classical exponential sums.

The remaining possible replacement is not local rationality but a new family-averaged arithmetic cancellation theorem for the Apéry cocycle.
