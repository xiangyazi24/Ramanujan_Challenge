## 10. Katz/Deligne audit: two different complexity ledgers

There are two possible geometric readings, and neither proves the needed divisibility estimate.

### 10.1 Treating \Lambda^M as a high-degree Laurent polynomial

The Newton polytope is

Any generic cohomological bound applied directly to this high-degree function has Betti/conductor complexity growing polynomially with M; the normalized volume is \Theta(M^3). Since M\asymp n, this is not a bounded-conductor family and does not yield an adversarial length-H zero estimate.

### 10.2 Kummer-character reinterpretation

For t\in\mathbb F_q^\times, the field value t^M is the reduction of the Teichmüller character value \omega_q(t)^M at the distinguished cyclotomic prime. Thus one may instead view the torus moment as the reduction of a rank-one Kummer trace for the fixed map \Lambda.

In this formulation the geometric zero/pole divisor of \Lambda is fixed, so the geometric conductor can be bounded independently of the integer representative M modulo q-1.

But the hard quantifiers remain wrong:

- the character \omega_q^M changes with q;

- its order and coefficient field change with q;

- the prime above q at which divisibility is tested changes with q;

- Deligne’s complex absolute-value bound does not control divisibility by that residue characteristic;

- Katz character-aspect equidistribution averages characters at one fixed q, whereas the P3.2 block selects one adversarial character in each changing field.

Therefore one must not cite a generic bounded-conductor theorem as a proof of horizontal nonalignment. The direct high-degree interpretation has conductor growing with M; the Kummer interpretation has bounded geometric conductor but a varying-character, varying-residue-characteristic diagonal that present theorems do not control.

## Final assessment

The fixed-moment reformulation is correct and conceptually valuable:

in the interior direct branch, with the corresponding reflected equality after folding.

It replaces a moving Apéry index by a moving divisibility shell inside one fixed Laurent power.

But the natural shell algebra has no hidden short-gap compression:

1. all shell values share the exponentially large constant term b_M;

1. close nonzero shells are disjoint;

1. finite differences cancel the common term but lose target divisibility;

1. cross-weighting restores divisibility but restores the long coordinate unless the full target product is inserted universally;

1. primitive Fitting saturation is the unit ideal;

1. root-of-unity filters and resultants merely repackage this ledger;

1. geometric trace bounds do not control the varying-character residue-divisibility diagonal.

Thus a proof must add a genuinely new horizontal theorem about the actual primitive shell masses P_M(g)—most economically FTAA, or an equivalent low-height pure-cross block carrier.
