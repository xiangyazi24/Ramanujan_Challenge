# Q8503 — TGD3 new dual audit

## Verdict

**Classification: scoped obstruction.** The supplied rank gap proves existence of a dual functional over F_43, but no explicit machine-checkable sparse/orbit-compressed certificate or solved symbolic ansatz is derivable from the stated data alone.

## What is proved

Let M be the literal h=15,p=43 coefficient matrix and b=15 Omega*. The stated computation gives

\[
\operatorname{rank}(M)=42366,\qquad \operatorname{rank}([M|b])=42367.
\]

Therefore linear algebra over F_43 gives a functional

\[
w\in (\mathbb F_{43}^{42875})^*
\]

such that

\[
wM=0,\qquad wb\ne0.
\]

This is an **exact finite fact** conditional on the correctness of the sparse rank computation.

## Missing ingredient

The acceptance target requires an explicit representation of w:

1. every nonzero coefficient of w;
2. a reconstruction algorithm from the compressed representation;
3. verification that the adjoint equations vanish on every column;
4. verification that the target pairing is nonzero.

A rank certificate does not determine a canonical left-null vector. The nullspace dimension may be positive, and extracting one vector requires additional data from the matrix computation.

## Why the old resonance block is irrelevant

The known block for primes dividing 3h-2 has target pairing zero. At h=15 and p=43, the relation

\[
3h-2=43
\]

only identifies a compact resonance mechanism whose target pairing vanishes. Since the obstruction requires wb≠0, this block cannot certify the new dual state.

## Exact conditional statement

If an orbit ansatz w(x) is proposed and satisfies

```text
for every column c of M:  <w,c>=0 in F_43
and <w,b> != 0 in F_43,
```

then it is a valid obstruction certificate. The required proof is a finite verification of those two identities.

No such solved ansatz is supplied in the authoritative input, so the requested explicit dual state remains unconstructed.

## Residual

The blocker is not the existence of a dual state; that follows from the rank gap. The blocker is producing a reproducible certificate rather than a dimension statement.
