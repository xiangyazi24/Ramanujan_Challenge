# Computational-symbolic endpoint search

I tested the proposed endpoint-hit identities against the recurrence

\[
(2t+1)^3u_{t+1}-4t(68t^2+3)u_t+(2t-1)^3u_{t-1}=0
\]

with \(p=2m+1\) prime, over \(\mathbf F_p\). The search range was primes below 20000. The admissibility filter was:

* \(u_2,\ldots,u_m\neq0\);
* endpoint hit \(u_{m-1}=5u_m\).

For every surviving hit I computed

\[
S=\frac13-2\sum_{t=2}^{m}\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}.
\]

The surviving evidence does not support a nontrivial formula depending only on the final three values \((u_m,u_{m-1},u_{m-2})\). In particular, the obvious boundary-jet ansatzes

\[
S=\frac{A u_{m-2}+B u_m}{C u_{m-2}+D u_m},
\]

and their degree-2 polynomial variants fail at the first held-out endpoint hit.

## Adjoint/Lagrange search

For a second order recurrence, a standard discrete Green identity is obtained from an adjoint solution \(v_t\). Writing

\[
a_t=(2t+1)^3,\quad b_t=4t(68t^2+3),\quad c_t=(2t-1)^3,
\]

one has

\[
v_t(a_tu_{t+1}-b_tu_t+c_tu_{t-1})-u_t(a_tv_{t+1}-b_tv_t+c_tv_{t-1})
=\Delta_t(a_t(v_tu_{t+1}-u_tv_{t+1})).
\]

Thus an adjoint certificate would require a source term of the form

\[
\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}=\Delta_t F_t
\]

with \(F_t\) generated rationally from \(u\) and an adjoint solution.

I searched rational ansatzes

\[
v_t=\frac{P(t)}{Q(t)},
\]

and Riccati forms using \(R_t=1/u_t\). Low degree polynomial spaces (degrees up to 8 in numerator and denominator) give only the homogeneous adjoint solutions; no source matching \(t^2-1\) survives.

The obstruction is structural: after substituting the recurrence to eliminate \(u_{t+1}\), the coefficient equations for a telescoper reduce to an overdetermined linear system. The source vector is outside the column span of the operator generated by degree-bounded rational adjoints. Hence no low-degree rational certificate exists.

## Endpoint condition

The endpoint relation \(u_{m-1}=5u_m\) cannot be used to create a missing final recurrence step: the original recurrence is only valid for \(1\le t<m\). Any certificate using the equation at \(t=m\) is invalid.

The last legal telescoping boundary is therefore at \(t=m-1\), leaving a term involving \(u_{m-2},u_{m-1},u_m\). The endpoint relation reduces this to two variables but does not collapse the sum.

## Conclusion

The strongest surviving statement is negative:

> There is no bounded three-point endpoint-jet identity for \(S\), and no low-degree rational/hypergeometric adjoint certificate reducing \(S\) to the endpoint condition alone.

The computational search does not prove nonzero \(S\). A proof of the gap-one lemma still needs a different invariant (for example a global polynomial identity, a positivity argument after lifting, or a higher-order parameter derivative construction). Raw endpoint-hit data alone is insufficient as a certificate.
