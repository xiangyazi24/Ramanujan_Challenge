# Q1095: H=2 exact audit of the claimed Pade/Newton module map

## Verdict

The claimed canonical cokernel map in Q1092 does not exist from the stated data. The H=2 symbolic interpolation modules and the Newton carrier are built from different presentations. They can both encode the same Apéry values, but there is no functorial Smith/Fitting ideal containment without an explicit morphism of the presented modules.

The alleged minor proportional to

\[
b_0b_1-b_0b_2-b_1b_2
\]

is not a maximal minor of the degree-split interpolation matrices below.

## 1. Exact H=2 interpolation matrices

Let the node data be \(a_0,a_1,a_2\). The condition is

\[
P(s)=a_sQ(s),\qquad s=0,1,2.
\]

### a=0

Here \(\deg P\le0\), \(\deg Q\le2\). Write

\[
P=c_0,\qquad Q=d_0+d_1s+d_2s^2.
\]

The homogeneous equations are

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&-a_0&0&0\\
1&-a_1&-a_1&-a_1\\
1&-a_2&-2a_2&-4a_2
\end{pmatrix}
(c_0,d_0,d_1,d_2)^T=0.
\]

The kernel has the expected dimension one after removing the scalar normalization. Maximal minors are the 3x3 minors of this matrix. They are generated by combinations of

\[
(a_1-a_0),\quad(a_2-a_0),\quad(a_2-a_1)
\]

and products with the a_i.

### a=1

Write

\[
P=c_0+c_1s,\qquad Q=d_0+d_1s.
\]

The matrix is

\[
M_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&-a_0&0\\
1&1&-a_1&-a_1\\
1&2&-a_2&-2a_2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its 3x3 minors generate the kernel Plucker coordinates. Expanding gives

\[
\det(M_1[0,1,2])=a_0(a_1-a_2)+a_1(a_2-a_0),
\]

and the other minors are the corresponding affine variants. No term equals
\(a_0a_1-a_0a_2-a_1a_2\).

### a=2

Here \(\deg P\le2,\deg Q\le0\). Write

\[
P=c_0+c_1s+c_2s^2,\quad Q=d_0.
\]

The matrix is

\[
M_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&-a_0\\
1&1&1&-a_1\\
1&2&4&-a_2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The kernel vector is the usual quadratic interpolation numerator. Primitive evaluation at x is

\[
P(x)=d_0\sum_{i=0}^2 a_i\ell_i(x),
\]

where \(\ell_i\) are the Lagrange basis polynomials.

Again the minors are Vandermonde multiples. There is no distinguished quadratic expression in the three values.

## 2. Primitive numerator evaluations

For every degree split the primitive numerator is obtained from the kernel generator. If the kernel generator is \(v=(P,Q)\), then

\[
P(x)=\pi_P(v)(x).
\]

Changing the primitive normalization multiplies all evaluations by a unit/sign only. The ideal generated by all allowed primitive choices is therefore the ideal of maximal minors of the corresponding presentation matrix.

It is not canonically equal to the ideal generated by Newton coefficients.

## 3. Newton carrier presentation

For N and M=floor(N/2), the two-block Newton matrix has rows

\[
(1,s,s^2,\ldots;\ b_s, sb_s,s^2b_s,\ldots).
\]

Its final determinantal divisor is

\[
D_N=\gcd_{j<M}L_j(N)(b_N-b_j).
\]

This follows from the determinant expansion in the Newton basis: deleting the j-th interpolation column leaves the cofactor

\[
L_j(N)=(-1)^{M-1-j}\binom Nj\binom{N-j-1}{M-1-j}.
\]

The important point is that this matrix has rows indexed by the Newton evaluation problem at N, while the Pade matrices above have rows indexed by interpolation nodes 0,1,2.

No row/column map between these presentations has been supplied.

## 4. Concrete counterexample

Take

\[
(a_0,a_1,a_2)=(1,2,4).
\]

For the a=2 matrix, the maximal minors generate the Vandermonde ideal contribution

\[
I_{Pade}=(a_1-a_0,a_2-a_0,a_2-a_1)=(1,3,2)=(1).
\]

The claimed expression gives

\[
a_0a_1-a_0a_2-a_1a_2=2-4-8=-10.
\]

Thus the proposed "maximal minor" ideal would contain only multiples of 10, while the actual maximal-minor ideal is the unit ideal. The divisibility direction required by Q1092 would be

\[
I_{Newton}\subseteq I_{Pade}
\]

(or equivalently the reverse divisibility of generators), but the Pade side is already unit while the claimed generator is not.

Therefore no such containment can be inferred.

## 5. Correct dependency map

The valid implications are only:

\[
\text{interpolation presentation}
\longrightarrow
\text{maximal minors}
\longrightarrow
\text{Fitting ideals}
\]

and independently

\[
\text{Newton evaluation presentation}
\longrightarrow
D_N,\Gamma_N.
\]

A transport theorem would require an explicit chain map between these presentations. Without it, Fitting ideals are not comparable. The H=2 symbolic calculation is an obstruction to the claimed canonical identification.
