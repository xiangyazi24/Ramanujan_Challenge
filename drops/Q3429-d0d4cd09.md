# 1. Deriving the projected divided first ghost

Put

\[
B:=1-x\Lambda(u,v).

\[

\[

\tag{1.1}

\[

\[
\frac{B^{a+bp}-B^a\psi(B)^b}{p}

\sum_{\ell=1}^b

\[
