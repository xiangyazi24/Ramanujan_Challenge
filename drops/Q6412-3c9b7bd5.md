# 4. Reflection does not close the forest

The off-reflection condition is decisive at the first Witt grade.

The exact continuant reflection identity is

```text
N_h(-X-h-1)=(-1)^(h-1) N_h(X).                       (4.1)
```

Let

```text
x^vee=p-1-x-h                                         (4.2)
```

be the start of the reflected gap. Taylor expansion of (4.1) modulo `p^2` at an ordinary simple root gives

```text
w_h(x^vee)
  =(-1)^(h-1)(w_h(x)-N_h'(x)) mod p,                 (4.3)

N_h'(x^vee)=(-1)^h N_h'(x) mod p.                    (4.4)
```

Therefore the normalized Hensel displacement transforms by the exact affine involution

```text
rho_h(x^vee)=-1-rho_h(x).                            (4.5)
```

For a three-gap window, reflection reverses the gaps and swaps its two normalized Bocksteins:

```text
(Xi_1,Xi_2) -> (Xi_2,Xi_1).                          (4.6)
```

Equation (4.6) determines the reflected tuple from the original tuple. It is not a vanishing condition.

At the endpoint-jet level, the exact first-lift reflection theorem likewise gives an affine bijection between the jets at reflected actual zeros. Adding a reflected path therefore does one of two things:

```text
two path components exchanged by reflection:
  their quotient is a path;

one path component reversed with a fixed vertex:
  its quotient is again a path.                      (4.7)
```

A reflection-fixed **edge** would identify the two endpoints of one edge and could create a closure equation. Such an edge is exactly an adjacent pair satisfying

```text
z_i+z_(i+1)=p-1,                                     (4.8)
```

which is absent from `M4-OFF`.

Thus the reflection quotient of the first-Witt graph is still a forest. The only banked first-lift identity that cancels a private endpoint quotient is tied to a reflected/central interval; the off-reflection branch has deliberately removed that geometry.

Multiplying return transfers around the reflected full cycle does not restore it. The exact multiplier reciprocity in `full_cycle_bridge_reciprocity.tex` produces a prescribed unit, and the remaining full-period step crosses the singular rank-one index. It cannot be inverted to manufacture another condition.

## Corollary 4.1 — scoped first-Witt no-go

Any construction which uses only:

```text
- the three short-gap first-Witt edge equations;
- their adjacent Bocksteins or normalized Hensel differences;
- the exact reflection involution;

```

has no nonzero scalar eliminant on an off-reflection adjacency forest. In particular, such a construction cannot assign `a(Pcal)` independent extra `p`-adic charges to one packet.

This is a local-construction no-go, not a denial that the globally distinguished Apéry orbit may obey an additional correlation.
