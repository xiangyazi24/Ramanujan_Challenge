# Hostile audit of the exponent theorem

## Verdict

The proposed proof has a first invalid step.  The failure occurs **before** the Mellit--Vlasenko block grouping: the asserted suffix valuation bound

\[
v_p(L_{t_k}(n))\le k
\]
for every \(t_k=n\bmod p^{d-k}\) is false at the required generality.

The suffix Kummer statement is correct only with the stated carry interpretation, but the claimed uniform bound does not follow from the truncation length alone.  The upper addition in the second binomial coefficient can acquire a carry chain through the retained prefix.

## 1. The exact Kummer expression

For

\[
L_j=(-1)^{M-1-j}\binom nj\binom{n-j-1}{M-1-j},
\]

put

\[
H=n-M=\lceil n/2\rceil .
\]

Kummer gives

\[
v_p(L_j)=\kappa_p(j,n-j)+\kappa_p(M-1-j,H),
\]

where \(\kappa_p(x,y)\) is the number of carries in the base-p addition \(x+y\).

For \(j=t_k\), the first summand is zero because the suffix is removed exactly.  The entire assertion therefore reduces to

\[
\kappa_p(M-1-t_k,H)\le k.
\]

This is the missing lemma.  It is not implied by the bound \(Q<p^k\).

## 2. Why the proposed induction fails

The later implication

\[
p^{e-k}\mid b_{t_k}
\]
requires

\[
v_p(L_{t_k})\le k.
\]
If the carry count is larger, the Newton coefficient may absorb all the required valuation and gives no information about the Apéry suffix.

The Mellit--Vlasenko decomposition cannot repair this: it starts only after the suffix divisibilities have been established.

## 3. Boundary checks

### The case \(n<p^e\)

The proposed preliminary reduction to \(d\ge e\) is not automatic.  The exception analysis using \(L_0\) is insufficient because a unit coefficient only rules out the hypothesis when the second binomial factor is exactly the one being tested.  One must separately prove that the chosen digit truncations exist with the required half-cutoff condition.

### The case \(n=2p^d-1\)

Here

\[
M=p^d-1,
\]

and the statement about \(L_0\) being a unit is correct.  This is an excluded case, not a proof of the general claim.

### The inequality \(t_k<M\)

This part is valid except for the small boundary where the suffix length is comparable with the half cutoff.  For \(n=2m\), one needs

\[
n\bmod p^{d-k}<m.
\]

This follows when the removed prefix is nonzero, but is not a free consequence of the digit notation alone.

## 4. The MV grouping step

The proposed grouping says that blocks of final length \(\ell\le e\) contribute

\[
p^{\ell-1}b_{t_{\ell-1}},
\]

and longer blocks contribute \(p^e\).

This would prove the theorem if the suffix valuations were available.  However the missing carry lemma means the hypotheses needed for those block lengths are not established.

In particular, the MV coefficient estimate

\[
v_p(c_p(a))\ge \ell_p(a)-1
\]
controls the coefficient of a block, not the Newton coefficient valuations required to force the suffix Apéry values.

## Conclusion

The first invalid step is:

\[
\boxed{v_p(L_{t_k}(n))\le k.}
\]

The stated Kummer formula is correct, but the advertised carry bound is an additional theorem and is not proved by the suffix construction.  Therefore the exponent theorem is not established from the supplied ingredients.

A corrected proof would need a separate half-cutoff carry lemma for every truncation \(t_k\), with explicit treatment of the top prefix and the \(2p^d-1\) boundary.  The Mellit--Vlasenko expansion alone does not supply this missing input.
