## 10.1 Edge-local module factorization lemma

Consider any characteristic-zero transfer module with the following precise locality property.

- A generator is attached to one edge `p -> p+Delta`.
- Its unknown lower-state columns are supported only on recurrence states belonging to that edge's `Delta`-chain component.
- The fixed selected row key, fixed coefficients, and already-saturated universal restart/Lucas factors may appear as coefficients.
- No generator contains a new unknown column shared by two distinct `Delta`-chain origins.

Then, after permuting rows and columns by connected component, its presentation matrix is block diagonal:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_C^{\rm loc}
\cong
\bigoplus_{\nu\in\pi_0(G_\Delta)}
\mathcal M_{C,\nu}^{\rm loc}.
}
\tag{10.2}
\]

This is immediate from the support condition: no row has nonzero entries in the unknown columns of two different components.

Therefore its row module, syzygy module, Smith decomposition over a PID specialization, and Popov/minimal-approximant computation all factor componentwise.  Row operations can change a basis inside the direct sum but cannot manufacture a relation whose primitive support couples two blocks that had no shared unknown.

By `(10.1)`, every block contains only

\[
O(\log Y)
\]

edge steps.

Hence

\[
\boxed{
R_C\gg Y^{1/6}
\quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{one }R_C\text{-row local transfer block}.
}
\tag{10.3}
\]

The large matching consists of polynomially many edges spread among many short blocks.

This does **not** extend Q4166's bounded-row impossibility theorem automatically to `O(log Y)` rows; I do not make that unjustified inference.  What it proves is narrower and exact: an `R_C`-sized cancellation cannot come from edge locality alone.

## 10.2 What must be new

Any genuine growing-module proof must violate at least one locality bullet above.  Concretely it needs a generator that couples different chain origins, for example through

```text
a common unsaturated characteristic-zero state;
a nonlocal interpolation/syzygy in the base prime variable;
a selected horizontal trace/zero relation across characteristics;
or another global Apéry invariant not present in the bank.
```

The first two common fixed-row upper coordinates already tested in Q4166 do not supply such an elimination equation after mandatory saturation.  Thus simply placing all `R_C` edge transfer rows in one large matrix does not by itself create the missing coupling.

This is the **cross-origin obstruction**.
