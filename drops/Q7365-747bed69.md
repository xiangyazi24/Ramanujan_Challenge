# Q32 Apéry reflection versus reciprocal moments

## Conclusion

The requested nonvanishing does not follow from the known reflection identity alone. The exact reflection rewrite gives a terminal reciprocal-moment identity, but the singular recurrence endpoint and the Casoratian do not supply the missing rank statement.

Let
\(S_s(a)=\sum_{u=1}^a b_{a-u}u^{-s}\). For \(1\le u\le a-1\), put \(j=q-a+u-1\). Then
\(a-u=q-1-j\), so
\[b_{a-u}=b_{q-a+u-1}\pmod q.\]
The endpoint is separate because \(u=a\) gives \(b_0\). Hence
\[
\boxed{S_s(a)=a^{-s}+\sum_{u=1}^{a-1}b_{q-a+u-1}u^{-s}\pmod q.}
\]
Equivalently, with \(r=a-u+1\),
\[
S_s(a)=\sum_{r=2}^{a}b_{q-r}(a-r+1)^{-s}+a^{-s}.
\]

Define \(A_r=\sum_{i=2}^{r}b_{q-i}\). Discrete summation by parts gives
\[
\sum_{r=2}^a b_{q-r}w_r=A_aw_a+\sum_{r=2}^{a-1}A_r(w_r-w_{r+1}),
\]
with \(w_r=(a-r+1)^{-s}\). Therefore the reflection identity yields an exact Green-type expression, but no sign or independence information over \(\mathbf F_q\).

The Apéry recurrence becomes singular at the endpoint \(q\): the coefficient containing \(q^3\) vanishes modulo \(q\). This gives only a terminal relation, not three independent constraints on the block \((b_{q-a},\ldots,b_{q-1})\). Thus it cannot exclude
\[
S_1=S_2=S_3=0.
\]

Likewise, the Casoratian of the Apéry solution and a companion solution proves two-solution independence away from the singular point. It does not imply that one solution cannot satisfy three prescribed linear functionals. The needed implication would be a finite-field rank statement for the terminal block.

The concrete missing estimate is:
\[
(b_{q-a},\ldots,b_{q-1})\notin\ker M_a,
\]
where the three rows of \(M_a\) are the reciprocal weights
\[
(1^{-1},\ldots,a^{-1}),\quad(1^{-2},\ldots,a^{-2}),\quad(1^{-3},\ldots,a^{-3})
\]
after the reflection indexing permutation. Reflection identifies the block but does not prove this rank condition.

The cases \(b_a\equiv0\pmod q\) or \(b_{a-1}\equiv0\pmod q\) do not create contradictions: they only remove individual coordinates in the moment equations. No known recurrence identity converts these cases into a nonzero moment.

Therefore the exact result is the boxed reflection/Green identity above, and the remaining problem is a concrete arithmetic correlation estimate preventing the Apéry terminal block from lying in this three-dimensional reciprocal kernel. No generic monodromy or randomness argument is sufficient.
