\frac{3(q-t)}{91}+O(1).
\tag{7.1}

After imposing the uniform boundary depth `L_0`, the count is

\[
N_+^{\partial}(q,t)
=
N_+(q,t)-\frac{10L_0}{91}+O(1).
\tag{7.2}

Therefore

\[
\frac{N_+^{\partial}}{N_+}
=
1-rac{10L_0}{3(q-t)}
+O\left(\frac1{q-t}\right).
\tag{7.3}
\]

The fraction of unordered raw offset pairs surviving pointwise boundary trimming is

\[
1-rac{20L_0}{3(q-t)}
+O\left(\frac{L_0^2}{(q-t)^2}+\frac1{q-t}\right).
\tag{7.4}
\]

For minus,

\[
N_-(q,t)
=
\frac{q-8t}{14}+O(1),
\tag{7.5}
\]

and

\[
N_-^{\partial}(q,t)
=
N_-(q,t)-\frac{4L_0}{7}+O(1).
\tag{7.6}

Hence

\[
\frac{N_-^{\partial}}{N_-}
=
1-rac{8L_0}{q-8t}
+O\left(\frac1{q-8t}\right),
\tag{7.7}
\]

and the raw pair fraction is

\[
1-rac{16L_0}{q-8t}
+O\left(\frac{L_0^2}{(q-8t)^2}+\frac1{q-8t}\right).
\tag{7.8}
\]

Thus:

- when `q-t` on plus or `q-8t` on minus is `O(log q)`, the strips can overlap and the fibre is empty or genuinely thin;
- when those quantities are `Theta(q)`, the residual fraction is `1-O(log q/q)`.

The endpoint singleton layer changes only `O(1)` offsets beyond the already counted vacancy prefix. The low-zero theorem removes no positive proportion of `d` because it is a condition in the transverse `z(p)` coordinate.

## 7.2 A project cell of length Lambda

A translated lower-prime cell of length `L<=Lambda` corresponds to an offset interval of the same length. A logarithmic boundary strip intersects it in at most `O(log T)` integer offsets, and the additional endpoint layer in `O(1)` offsets. Therefore a deep cell has geometric residual proportion

\[
1-O\left(\frac{\log T}{L}\right).
\]

At `L=Lambda=T^(5beta/6)`, this is

\[
1-O\left(\frac{\log T}{T^{5\beta/6}}\right)
=1-o(1).
\tag{7.9}
\]

No lower bound on the number of primes in a cell is available or used, so this geometric ratio must not be advertised as an actual-prime ratio.

## 7.3 Actual leaves

Without a distribution theorem for actual leaves, one cannot assign a numerical density to the residual among actual leaves. The deterministic statements are the correct substitute.

For a full plus fibre of size `K`, at least `K-1` leaves are high-zero and outside the endpoint cap. Thus the proportion is at least

\[
1-rac1K.
\tag{7.10}
\]

For a full minus fibre, at least `K-2` are hard, giving

\[
1-rac2K.
\tag{7.11}
\]

The corresponding hard-hard pair proportions are at least

\[
\frac{\binom{K-1}{2}}{\binom K2}
=
1-rac2K

on plus, and

\[
\frac{\binom{K-2}{2}}{\binom K2}
=
\frac{(K-2)(K-3)}{K(K-1)}
=
1-O\left(\frac1K\right)
\]

on minus.

On DPRM, endpoint exceptions are already deleted, so both signs have at least `K-1` high-zero deep leaves and the plus formula applies to both.

For `K>H=Y^(1/2)`, the actual hard residual therefore occupies a `1-O(Y^(-1/2))` fraction of the vertices and pairs. It is essentially the whole rich fibre.

# 8. Natural limit of each technique

## 8.1 Low-zero orbit method

The hard stop is `|Z_p|=4`.

With at most three zeros, two required opposite-parity zeros force one reflection orbit plus the unique midpoint orbit. The midpoint equation fixes `p`, producing `(3.1)` or `(3.7)`. At four zeros, the two required zeros can occupy two distinct two-cycles:

\[
Z_p=\{r,p-1-r,a,p-1-a\}.
\]

No midpoint is forced and no affine equation determines `p`. The one-dimensional fixed-state affine line remains completely soluble. This is a genuine structural obstruction to extending the same orbit-count proof, not an expository stopping point.

Any extension must use arithmetic beyond the number and reflection-orbit structure of the zeros: for example a restriction on exact-four configurations, a horizontal common-divisor theorem, or a labelled pair identity.

## 8.2 Boundary-vacancy method

The exact stopping point is the threshold

\[
u=\mu_e(p).
\]

Below it, positivity gives `0<b_u<p`, so `p` cannot divide `b_u`. At or above it, Archimedean size is compatible with divisibility. Reflection only supplies the second boundary coordinate; it does not create further scarcity once both folded indices exceed the threshold.

Using parity-sensitive `mu_e`, the exact Apéry asymptotic, or sharper constants can improve `O(1)` and `O(log log q)` terms. It cannot turn a logarithmic boundary theorem into a macroscopic one. This is a hard limit of individual-value size alone.

## 8.3 Endpoint radical-spacing method

The original width-four/width-two statement was not the natural limit. The current all-k theorem and `(4.13)` already push the method as far as prime packing allows.

The remaining obstruction is that geometric packing gives only

\[
K\ll1+W/\log W.
\]

At a deep offset width `W` tending to infinity this is much larger than one. At the first DPRM depth `W\asymp J_*`, the gap from the two-prime threshold is of order

\[
\frac{J_*}{\log T}
=
\frac{T^{\beta/6}}{\log T}.
\]

The method controls where primes can fit. It does not control why the same fixed gcd should contain them. Further constant endpoint layers are available, but no amount of prime packing alone yields uniqueness on a macroscopic or polynomial-width interval. The remaining wall is genuinely horizontal arithmetic.

# 9. Re-aiming on the true residual

## 9.1 The exact critical size object

Let

\[
\widetilde G_\sigma(q,t)
\]

be the normalized fixed two-value gcd after removing the complete `q`-primary part and the finite fixed row-normalization ledger. Every actual lower leaf prime divides this one integer.

For any deterministic pre-support set `S_sigma(q,t)` containing every possible residual lower prime, let its two smallest distinct primes be

\[
\pi_{\sigma,1}<\pi_{\sigma,2}
\]

and put

\[
\Theta_{2,\sigma}=\pi_{\sigma,1}\pi_{\sigma,2}.
\]

Then the genuinely support-blind sufficient theorem is

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{S_\sigma(q,t)}
\bigl(\widetilde G_\sigma(q,t)\bigr)
<
\Theta_{2,\sigma}.
\tag{9.1}
\]

The clean but slightly stronger form is

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{S_\sigma(q,t)}
\bigl(\widetilde G_\sigma(q,t)\bigr)
<
P_0(q,t)^2,
\tag{9.2}
\]

or a power bound `<<q^(2-eta)`.

The critical exponent remains exactly two. The residual is macroscopic and still permits two primes comparable with `q`; the partial theorems do not lower their information cost.

## 9.2 A tailored two-part radical target

The low-zero theorem does allow a weaker **domain decomposition** than charging the whole raw shell at once.

Define a deterministic high-zero residual pre-support

\[
S_\sigma^{\mathrm{hi}}(q,t)
=
\{\ell:\ \ell\text{ prime in the prescribed shell and raw window},\ J_\sigma(q,t;q-\ell)\ge0,\ |Z_\ell|\ge4\},
\]

before testing the actual q6 two-row support or inherited masks.

A sufficient high/high theorem is

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{S_\sigma^{\mathrm{hi}}}
(\widetilde G_\sigma)
<
\Theta_{2,\sigma}^{\mathrm{hi}}.
\tag{9.3}
\]

This eliminates two high-zero common primes.

There remains only the explicit possible size-three prime `p_sigma^(3)`. If it is integral, prime, and in the raw window, a support-blind cofactor theorem of the form

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_{S_\sigma^{\mathrm{hi}}}
\left(
\frac{\widetilde G_\sigma}
     {(p_\sigma^{(3)})^{v_{p_\sigma^{(3)}}(\widetilde G_\sigma)}}
\right)
<
\pi_{\sigma,1}^{\mathrm{hi}}
\tag{9.4}
\]

eliminates a low/high pair.

The pair `(9.3)` plus `(9.4)` is more tailored than a full-shell bound. It charges only the deep high-zero residual and one explicit anchor. It does not improve the exponent needed for high/high pairs.

## 9.3 Labelled pair route

A non-factoring pair identity no longer needs to treat every formal pair. It needs only:

```text
high/high residual pairs,
plus
one explicit size-three/high pair family.
```

On plus, every residual bad pair contains a high-zero non-endpoint anchor. On minus the only exceptional crossed stratum is the deep size-three prime together with a high-zero endpoint prime. This is the exact reduced domain for a labelled identity.

The identity must be intensional and support-blind in construction. It cannot be a post hoc choice of coefficients whose existence is equivalent to nonexistence of the pair.

## 9.4 A genuinely weaker tail target

For the P3.2 rich tail, full pointwise SLUNI is stronger than necessary. Let

\[
K_\sigma^{\mathrm{hard}}(x,I)
\]

count actual lower leaves in one selected state/cell that satisfy

```text
|Z_p| >= 4,
folded depth >= J_*,
outside the endpoint layer.
```

In DPRM,

\[
K_\sigma^{\mathrm{hard}}\ge K_\sigma-1.
\]

Therefore either of the following weaker statements is sufficient for the rich-cell gate:

\[
K_\sigma^{\mathrm{hard}}(x,I)
\ll_J
H Y^{-\varepsilon},
\tag{9.5}
\]

or a PBSD/PGME estimate formed only from hard-hard pairs with any fixed positive exponent beyond the `beta/3` conversion threshold.

This hard-residual counting theorem is genuinely weaker than Q6-SLUNI. It permits isolated leaves and all `O(1)` partial-theorem exceptions, yet it still kills every fibre with `K>H` for large `Y`. For campaign routing, this is the cheapest new counting target exposed by the synthesis.

# 10. Standing trap checks

## 10.1 Equivalence trap

The following are reformulations, not mechanisms:

```text
the product of actual admissible primes is < P^2;
there is no pair in the exact actual residual;
every actual high-zero leaf has no partner;
a polynomial exists after observing the partner set and vanishes on it.
```

For sufficiently large `q`, the actual-window radical inequality `<P^2` is equivalent to Q6-SLUNI.

The support-blind shell radical `(9.1)` is different. Its shell is fixed before the actual leaf support is consulted and it charges common divisors that may fail the exact lower-zero labels or inherited masks. SLUNI does not imply it. It is a genuinely stronger arithmetic premise.

Likewise, a labelled pair identity is new only if a uniform source rule constructs it from recurrence data before knowing whether the pair exists.

## 10.2 Packaging trap

This synthesis never descends two lower characteristics into `F_q`, and it never asks a newly constructed scalar to be divisible by both lower primes.

If one reduces the lower marked equations functorially modulo `q`, each lower prime is a unit and its special-fibre marking disappears. That is unit erasure.

If one instead builds a characteristic-zero determinant, Fitting invariant, CRT object, or scalar carrier required to remember both primes, it must pay at least

\[
\log p_1+\log p_2.
\]

That is the marked-prime information floor.

A shell radical bound is a theorem about the prime support of an already fixed gcd. It is not proved merely by multiplying local carriers. Any proof that reconstructs it by a product divisible by all candidate primes has re-entered the packaging trap and gained nothing.

## 10.3 Assignment and normalization audit

- Raw minus-first is inside `mathcal L_sigma` throughout.
- Every raw-plus exclusion remains valid after assignment because assigned plus is a subset of raw plus.
- The low-zero theorem was proved with the complete assignment and masks retained.
- Every inherited shell, residual, primitive, and depth predicate can only shrink the candidate sets used here.
- No normalization by an unselected mass occurs.
- No lower bound on `M_sigma(T)` occurs.
- No finite scan is used as a premise.

# Bottom line

The three partial theorems are mutually consistent and compose in useful but limited ways:

1. boundary-vacancy tests the one forced size-three point and removes additional logarithmic state strips;
2. endpoint spacing is the pairwise/all-k lift of the same Archimedean zero-depth inequalities and has already been extended to the natural prime-packing limit;
3. low-zero plus endpoint leaves only `O(1)` exceptional vertices, and on DPRM only one.

They do not produce Q6-SLUNI because, after those `O(1)` exceptions are removed, the surviving deep high-zero support is still macroscopic and can formally carry many primes.

For full pointwise completion, the best current target is the support-blind residual-shell radical below the exact two-prime capacity, with a tailored high/high plus explicit-low-anchor decomposition if useful. A non-factoring labelled pair identity remains the fallback above capacity.

For P3.2 tail completion, re-aim more cheaply: prove PBSD, PGME, or a pointwise fibre cap only for the deep high-zero residual. The three partial theorems guarantee that this hard subfibre contains all but one DPRM vertex and all but one canonical edge, so such a theorem already closes the rich pair gate without proving global SLUNI.
