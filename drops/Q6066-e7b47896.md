## 4. Exact complexity of the favorable resultant sequence

\[

\[
N_c(X)=a_c\prod_{i=1}^{m}(X-\alpha_i),
\qquad m=3(c-1),

in a splitting field, with roots counted with multiplicity.  The resultant formula gives

\[
S_c(h)
=a_c^{3(h-1)}
\prod_{i=1}^{m}N_h(\alpha_i+c).
\tag{4.1}
\]

For each root, put

\[
u_i(h)=N_h(\alpha_i+c).
\]

The canonical continuant recurrence gives

\[
\boxed{
 u_i(h+1)
 =P(\alpha_i+c+h)u_i(h)
  -(\alpha_i+c+h)^6u_i(h-1).}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Hence

\[
\binom{u_i(h+1)}{u_i(h)}
=M_i(h)
\binom{u_i(h)}{u_i(h-1)}
\]

for an explicit \(2\times2\) matrix whose entries are polynomials in \(h\) over the splitting field.

Taking the tensor product over the \(m\) roots produces an exact first-order nonautonomous recurrence system of dimension

\[
\boxed{2^{3(c-1)}}
\tag{4.3}
\]

containing the product in `(4.1)` as one coordinate.  Sampling

\[

only composes a bounded number \(|s|\le12\) of these matrices per unit step in \(\Delta\); it does not reduce the tensor dimension.

## 6. Why no checked large-sieve theorem applies

There are four independent hypothesis failures.

### 6.1 No fixed-degree polynomial family

The degree of \(N_{d(p_2)}\) is

\[
3d(p_2)-3,
\]

and the Sylvester dimension grows with \(p_2\).  Polynomial root bounds and polynomial-value large sieves require a fixed polynomial or a uniformly bounded degree.  Neither is present.

### 6.2 No constant-coefficient nondegenerate recurrence

Even in `(4.2)`, the coefficients depend polynomially on \(h\).  Standard prime-divisor results for nondegenerate constant-coefficient recurrences do not apply.  A generic P-recursive hypothesis would be insufficient anyway: the first-order sequence \(u_{n+1}=(n+1)u_n\) has \(p\mid u_p\) for every prime \(p\).  Modulus-index diagonals can be structurally singular.

### 6.3 No bounded conductor

Any sheaf or trace-family representation obtained from the variable Sylvester matrix would have rank/conductor growing at least with the continuant degrees exposed above.  A large sieve for bounded-conductor trace functions cannot be invoked with conductor \(T^{\Omega(1)}\) or tensor rank \(2^{T^{\Omega(1)}}\).

The candidate primes are not all primes in a dyadic interval.  They already satisfy

\[
p_2\mid G.
\]

No banked theorem says that the prime divisors of this fixed selected-state cofactor are equidistributed relative to the moving recurrence family.  A large sieve over all primes cannot be restricted to an arbitrary radical support without a new covariance input.  The needed covariance is precisely `HC-AGCD`, not a consequence of the recurrence shape.

## 9. Smallest checked fibre restriction that gives a fixed power

No nontrivial theorem follows merely from saying that the private row is fixed, that the recurrence rank is finite, or that the number of charts is bounded.  The smallest clean restriction for which the **verified separated-resultant/Smith method** proves the requested degree is a bound on the weighted exact gap-word complexity.

### 9.1 Exact word assignment

For a nondegenerate four-position chart, let

\[
R_D^{\rm raw}
=\prod_{j=1}^{r(D)}S_{d_j(D),e_j(D)},
\qquad r(D)\le2,
\]

be the raw product of adjacent separated resultants.  Outside the fixed content ledger,

\[
p_2\mid R_D^{\rm prim}
\Longrightarrow
p_2\mid R_D^{\rm raw}.
\]

Assign a passing skeleton deterministically to the first factor in the displayed order that is divisible by \(p_2\).  This does not delete or identify any occurrence label.

Let

\[
\mathscr W_\alpha
=\{(d,e)\text{ assigned to some partner skeleton of }\alpha\}.
\]

For a word \(w=(d,e)\), define the exact numerical-prime weight cap

\[
\mu_\alpha(w)
:=
\sup_{\ell\asymp T}
\sum_{\substack{\beta:\ p_2(\beta)=\ell\\
                 w(\beta)=w}}
\lambda(\alpha,\beta).
\tag{9.1}
\]

Physical-row uniqueness makes the unweighted fibre at most one; `(9.1)` preserves whatever source/host weight the local consumer actually carries.

### Theorem 9.1 — fixed-word resultant bound

Uniformly for one literal anchor,

\[
\boxed{
H_{2G}(\alpha)
\ll_J
\frac1{\log T}
\sum_{(d,e)\in\mathscr W_\alpha}
\mu_\alpha(d,e)\,
 d e\log(2(d+e)).}
\tag{9.2}
\]

#### Proof

For one exact word, the separated root strips give

\[
S_{d,e}\ne0.
\]

Every distinct hard-shell prime assigned to that word divides \(S_{d,e}\).  Hence

\[
\#\{p_2\text{ assigned to }(d,e)\}
\log(c_JT)
\le
\log|S_{d,e}|.
\]

The checked resultant estimate gives

\[
\log|S_{d,e}|
\ll de\log(2(d+e)).
\]

Multiply by the exact cap `(9.1)`, sum over the exact words, and use

\[
\log(c_JT)\asymp_J\log T.
\]

No moving \(R_D(p_2)\) has been frozen.  Each skeleton is first grouped by its exact numerical word, and each fixed resultant is paid once. ∎

The proof uses the primary separated-resultant budget, not the forbidden pointwise \(O(T)\) height of a moving companion carrier.  If “no ordinary height” is read as forbidding even the canonical fixed-resultant size estimate, the only remaining unconditional case is a \(T\)-independent finite word set: every corresponding nonzero resultant is then a fixed integer and has no sufficiently large shell-prime divisor.

### 9.2 Minimal quantitative restriction

Define

\[
\mathcal C_\alpha
:=
\sum_{(d,e)\in\mathscr W_\alpha}
\mu_\alpha(d,e)\,
 d e\log(2(d+e)).
\]

The purely skeleton-level restriction

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_\alpha
\ll
T^{1/5-\eta+o(1)}}
\tag{HC-WORD}_\eta
\]

implies, by `(9.2)`,

\[
\boxed{
H_{2G}(\alpha)
\ll
\frac{T^{1/5-\eta+o(1)}}{\log T}.}
\tag{9.3}
\]

This is exactly the per-anchor degree required by Q6033 at

\[
\Delta\asymp T^{2/5}.
\]

`(HC-WORD)_eta` is non-equivalent to the target.  It is imposed before any lower-zero test and constrains every phantom skeleton's exact gap vocabulary.  It does not assert that any prime divides any carrier.

It is the smallest restriction for this checked resultant proof in the following precise sense: `(9.2)` shows that the method sees the partner fibre only through the weighted sum of the known resultant complexities \(de\log(d+e)\).  A smaller exponent would require a new arithmetic saving for the prime divisors of the resultant family, not a refinement of the existing kinematic ledger.

### 9.3 Convenient shallow-word form

Suppose

\[
\mu_\alpha(d,e)\le T^{o(1)},
\qquad
|\mathscr W_\alpha|\le S,
\qquad
d,e\le H.
\]

Then

\[
H_{2G}(\alpha)
\ll T^{o(1)}SH^2.
\]

A sufficient condition for `(9.3)` is

\[
\boxed{
SH^2
\ll
\frac{T^{1/5-\eta}}{\log T}.}
\tag{9.4}
\]

If the fibre is allowed the full box of \(H^2\) words, so \(S\le H^2\), it is enough that

\[
\boxed{
H^4
\ll
\frac{T^{1/5-\eta}}{\log T},
\qquad
H\ll T^{1/20-\eta/4}(\log T)^{-1/4}.}
\tag{9.5}
\]

This is a genuine fixed-power theorem, but it lies in a very shallow private-gap sector.  It does not touch the critical companion words of size \(T^{2/5}\).

### 9.4 Why the critical fibre fails the restriction

In the favorable plus/plus chart `(3.2)`, the complexity of one variable word is already

\[
(c_L+c_R)h(p_2)\log T.
\]

If

\[
h(p_2)\asymp T^{2/5}
\]

and \(c_L+c_R\ge2\), even **one** exact word has complexity

\[
\gg T^{2/5}\log T,
\]

which exceeds the allowed \(T^{1/5-\eta}\) by a fixed power.  In a disjoint or mixed-sign chart, products of two macroscopic gaps are larger still.

Q6011 obtains a fixed \(T^{-4/25}\) saving for an \(M\)-spaced packet because every characteristic supplies many distinct common roots of the same small vocabulary of resultants; the \(q\)-adic valuation pays each start.  A fixed Q6033 anchor has only one partner skeleton per numerical \(p_2\).  That multiplicity divisor is absent.  One cannot import the Q6011 exponent without adding a new many-start fibre hypothesis.

## 10. Exponent ledger

At the critical separation scale,

\[
D\asymp T^{2/5},
\]

the support-blind prime capacity of one anchor is

\[
J_{\rm blind}(\alpha)
\asymp
\frac{D}{\log T}
\asymp
\frac{T^{2/5}}{\log T}.
\]

Q6033 requires the relative gain

\[
T^{-1/5-\eta+o(1)}.
\]

Therefore the hybrid degree must be

\[
\boxed{
J_{2G}(\alpha)
\ll
\frac{T^{1/5-\eta+o(1)}}{\log T}.}
\]

The audited routes give:

| Route | Exact output | Critical gain |
|---|---:|---:|
| bounded C4 chart | affine gap word | none |
| fix private numerical state | variable-order recurrence family | none |
| favorable plus/plus overlap | two fixed-boundary resultant sequences | none |
| quotient-6 pin | fixed integer \(F_6(v)\) | returns to fixed gcd with \(G\) |
| hypothetical bounded polynomial | fixed value \(F(-p_1)\) modulo \(p_2\) | returns to fixed gcd with \(G\) |

## 11. Rejection tests

Any future claim that \(R_D\) is a useful bounded-complexity family must pass all of the following.

1. **Numerical private-state test.** It must say whether \(v=(q_1,t_1)\) is fixed numerically.  Sign, chart, and edge role alone do not determine it.

2. **Affine endpoint test.** The four lower positions must be exactly `(2.1)–(2.2)`, with slope set
   ```text
   private plus:  -13,-6,-13,-6;
   private minus: -13,-6,-1,-6.
   ```

3. **Variable-order test.** A formula for \(N_{d(p_2)}\) is not a fixed polynomial merely because \(d(p_2)\) is affine.  Its degree is \(3d(p_2)-3\).

4. **Sylvester-size test.** A resultant matrix whose dimension grows with \(p_2\) is not a bounded-degree resultant in the parameter \(p_2\).

5. **Primitive-normalization test.** Moving factorial, leading, center, and subresultant contents must be removed exactly; calling the quotient primitive does not bound its recurrence order.

6. **Pinned-root test.** The implication
   
   \[
   p_2\mid R_D\Rightarrow p_2\mid F_6(v)
   \]
   
   is false.  A resultant common root may be unrelated to the labelled skeleton start.

7. **Modulus-diagonal test.** If a polynomial in \(\Delta\) is proposed, substitute
   
   \[
   \Delta\equiv-p_1\pmod{p_2}.
   \]
   
   The resulting fixed gcd must still be controlled.

8. **Recurrence-sieve test.** The theorem invoked must allow polynomially varying coefficients, modulus-dependent sample index, and the exposed singular factors.  Constant-coefficient nondegenerate recurrence theorems do not.

9. **Conductor test.** Any trace/sheaf large sieve must provide a uniform rank and conductor after the moving continuant orders are inserted.

10. **Selected-modulus test.** The modulus weights are \(\mathbf1_{\{p_2\mid G\}}\), not a smooth set of all primes.  A joint distribution theorem with \(G\) is required.

11. **Occurrence test.** All source/host labels and weights must remain in the sum.  Grouping by exact word may use `(9.1)` but may not collapse physical occurrences silently.

12. **Non-equivalence test.** Imposing both omitted companion zero atoms recovers the actual row.  A hybrid theorem must still count phantoms.

13. **Exponent test.** The bound must reach \(T^{1/5-\eta}/\log T\), not merely save a logarithm or give \(T^{2/5-o(1)}\).

14. **Actual-orbit test.** The no-go models are interface/carrier shadows.  A successful theorem may use new distinguished-Apéry arithmetic, but it must state that new input explicitly.

## 12. Final conclusion

The exact companion-row kinematics do not turn `HC-2G-PDD` into a bounded-degree divisor problem.

After the bounded chart is fixed, the private same-support upper state remains a macroscopic numerical variable.  Once it is fixed too, the four putative lower zeros are affine in \(p_2\), but the carrier uses continuants whose **orders** are those affine gaps.  The favorable chart is a finite-rank recurrence only after passing to a tensor system of rank \(2^{3(c-1)}\), with \(c\) potentially macroscopic.  The other charts have several moving orders.

One exact low-complexity object does survive: the quotient-6 companion return gives the fixed pinned value

\[
p_2\mid N_{|6q_1+t_1-(6q_0+t_0)|}
       \bigl(\min(6q_0+t_0,6q_1+t_1)\bigr).
\]

Adding this test produces the strictly smaller `HC-PIN6-2G` hybrid.  It does not save a power because its shell-prime support may share the complete anchor radical \(G\), and the unpinned resultant does not imply the pin.

No checked polynomial, rational, finite-difference, recurrence-large-sieve, or bounded-conductor theorem applies at \(\Delta\asymp T^{2/5}\).  Even a hypothetical bounded polynomial would be evaluated at the fixed residue \(-p_1\) modulo \(p_2\) and would return to a fixed common-gcd problem.

The smallest clean additional fibre restriction for which the verified resultant machinery proves the needed fixed power is the weighted exact-word budget

\[
\sum_{(d,e)\in\mathscr W_\alpha}
\mu_\alpha(d,e)\,
 d e\log(2(d+e))
\ll T^{1/5-\eta+o(1)}.
\]

Under it,

\[
H_{2G}(\alpha)
\ll
T^{1/5-\eta+o(1)}/\log T.
\]

The natural critical companion fibres violate this restriction by a fixed power.  Closing them requires a genuinely new anti-covariance theorem between the selected-state radical \(G\) and the moving private-state subresultant family—precisely the arithmetic absent from Q5958, Q6008, and Q6033.
