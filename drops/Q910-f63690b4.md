## Range split and quantifiers

The proof has the following quantifier structure:

```text
Choose the eventual convexity threshold K.
For k >= K and k <= H-3, use the uniform row subtraction identity (3.7)
plus the strict convexity of (s_j,v_j).
For the finite set k < K, use the fixed-row expansion O_k(H^2).
```

Thus the fixed-row expansion is not being used on a growing range. The growing range is controlled by (3.7), so the constants depending on k in the local expansion do not enter the asymptotic argument.

## Row subtraction formula

The relevant identity is the local row operation on consecutive rows. With the notation of the proof, it is

```text
row_k - row_{k-1} = weighted combination of adjacent (s_j,v_j) differences,
```

where the weights are the positive endpoint weights coming from the complete endpoint expansion. The subtraction is therefore compatible with the sign argument; it is not a hidden summation over k.

## Convex weighted-average sign

After the subtraction, the remaining sign is the sign of a weighted average of the strict convexity increments. The weights are nonnegative and not all zero. Eventual strict convexity gives

```text
(s_{j+1},v_{j+1}) - (s_j,v_j)
```

with strictly increasing slope, hence the weighted second-difference contribution has the required sign. There is no reversal of sign from the endpoint normalization or from the cofactor scaling.

## Operator M

The earlier interpretation of M as a summation operator was incorrect. The proof uses only

```text
(Mw)_k = k(w_k+w_{k-1}),
```

which is a local operator. The endpoint expansion is carried six orders deeper before applying M three times, so the required error margin is preserved through the three local transformations.

## Kernel normalization

The kernel is explicitly the integer cofactor vector divided by its gcd. Therefore the primitive normalization issue is already handled: the vector is integral before normalization, and division by the gcd only removes the common factor. No additional height or primitivity hypothesis is missing.
