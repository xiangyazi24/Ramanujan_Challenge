## 1. Exact kernel geometry: integration buys no power against a coherent packet

Put

```text

```

```text
K^circ(t) = (a-||t||_T)_+ - a^2.                        (1.1)
```

Hence

```text
if ||t|| <= a:
    K^circ(t) = a - ||t|| - a^2;
else:
    K^circ(t) = -a^2.                                   (1.2)
```

The zero crossing inside the short arc is at

```text

```

So the kernel has:



Thus integration over radii only replaces the phase-ball indicator by a linear tent.  A packet already concentrated in a fixed inner fraction of the ball loses at most a constant factor.  No N^(-sigma) can appear from the triangular taper alone.

This is the first decisive difference between “HPCMI is weaker than HPCD” and “HPCMI follows from the present interfaces.”  It is weaker as a theorem request, but the existing coherent-packet obstruction lives where the two are equivalent up to constants.

## 2. The negative lobe cannot be used as a positivity argument

The signed measure (MU) is not positive.  Its atom is

```text
c_x conjugate(c_y) M_4d(x,y),
```

which can have either real sign after reflection normalization.

Therefore the mean-zero property of K^circ does not yield an upper bound for

```text
( Re integral K^circ d mu )_+.
```

In fact the sign issue goes in the hostile direction.  If a signed packet places

```text
positive real mass in the positive core,
negative real mass where K^circ<0,
```

then **both** regions contribute positively to the real kernel pairing.

So the negative outside lobe is not a reservoir of automatic cancellation.  It is useful only if one already has an arithmetic theorem linking the sign of the source coefficient to the sign/location of the kernel.  No SA/CD determinant-count interface supplies such a theorem.

For the saturation argument below I do not even need this extra hostility: I take no outside mass at all.  A core-only positive packet already reaches the critical exponent.

## 3. Reflection pairing realifies but does not cancel

Q5273 records the completed-source reflected pair in the form

```text
c_x e(d Theta_x) + conjugate(c_x) e(-d Theta_x)
 = 2 Re( c_x e(d Theta_x) ).                             (3.1)
```

The centered zero supports are reflection-stable, and the two-sided source-gap selector is invariant under simultaneous reflection.

At the measure level, the reflected pair sends


to `-t` with conjugate coefficient.  Since K^circ is real and even,

```text
w K^circ(t) + conjugate(w) K^circ(-t)
 = 2 Re(w) K^circ(t).                                    (3.2)
```

Thus reflection is excellent for putting the problem in a real gauge, but it does not impose a negative sign.

a reflected phase packet near zero has both `+Theta` and `-Theta` inside the same positive kernel core.  The cross differences produced by the reflected copies are still O(N^(-1)), hence much smaller than `a`.

So a common-sign reflected packet is constructive, not destructive.  This is exactly the coherent-packet possibility already isolated in Q5273.

The same conclusion applies to the two circular branches: the current source keeps both literally, and no banked theorem forces their real contributions to have opposite sign on the critical phase packet.  A source-interface stress packet may therefore choose the allowed constructive sign pattern.

### Determinant nonzero condition

### Exact affine residue constraints

Reflection maps the packet to its conjugate symmetric copy.  Because the kernel is even, the real parts add as in (3.2).

### Signed d

The phase width is o(D^(-1)), so both signs of d remain coherent.  The packet does not throw away half the d-window.

## 8. Why integrated phase balls do not gain from determinant averaging

A natural hope is that integration in r averages over determinant cutoffs and converts a bad supremum into a first moment with cancellation.  Write schematically

```text
W_A = signed literal source weight on determinant fibre A.
```

Ignoring only the already-subcritical companion-replacement error, HPCMI has the arithmetic shape

```text
Re sum_A W_A [ (a-|phase(A)|)_+ - a^2 ].                 (8.1)
```

The current determinant inputs give information of the form

```text

fibre caps,
nonzero determinant,
affine residue identities,
possibly moments of the positive fibre counts.
```

They do not give cancellation in

```text
sum_A W_A * triangular_weight(A).                        (8.2)
```

The taper in (8.2) is positive and comparable to a on a fixed central fraction of the determinant interval.  Hence a same-sign packet supported there sees no averaging gain.  Summation by parts merely replaces the triangular first moment by cumulative signed determinant sums; without a signed discrepancy estimate that is exactly the original missing arithmetic.

This also explains why dividing a positive fibre count by the selected source mass is invalid: no lower bound or equidistribution statement for the actual weighted selected mass is available, and the source weights may concentrate on the fibres with one sign.
