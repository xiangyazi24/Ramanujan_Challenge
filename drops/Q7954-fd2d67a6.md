# HM3 output-energy: a weaker summable overload theorem

## 1. Setup

Write

\[
E_p=\sum_m g_p(m)H_{-p}(m),\qquad H_{-p}(m)=\sum_{q\ne \ell,\ q,\ell\ne p}g_q(m)g_\ell(m).
\]

The desired HM3 bound is an estimate of the total output energy

\[
\sum_{p}z_p^{-1}E_p^2\ll X^4\lambda^5.
\]

The pointwise conjecture

\[
E_p^+\le X^{2+o(1)}\lambda^2 z_p
\]

is sufficient because summing gives

\[
\sum_p z_p^{-1}E_p^2\le X^{4+o(1)}\lambda^4\sum_p z_p
\]

and \(\sum_pz_p=X^2\lambda\). It is stronger than necessary.

The correct weakening is a weighted overload estimate.

## 2. Dyadic decomposition by row size

Let

\[
\mathcal P_j=\{p:2^j<z_p\le2^{j+1}\},
\]

and define

\[
\tau_p=X^2\lambda^2z_p.
\]

Split

\[
E_p^2\le 2\tau_p^2+2(E_p-\tau_p)_+^2.
\]

The baseline contribution is

\[
\sum_pz_p^{-1}\tau_p^2
=X^4\lambda^4\sum_pz_p
=X^4\lambda^5X^0.
\]

Therefore HM3 follows from the strictly weaker condition

\[
\boxed{\sum_p z_p^{-1}(E_p-X^2\lambda^2z_p)_+^2
\ll X^4\lambda^5.}
\]

This is not a renaming of the pointwise condition: it permits arbitrarily large exceptional rows provided their weighted square mass is small.

## 3. Minimal dyadic form

A still more localized sufficient condition is: for every dyadic class,

\[
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_j}z_p^{-1}(E_p-CX^2\lambda^2z_p)_+^2
\ll X^4\lambda^5\,\eta_j,
\]

with

\[
\sum_j\eta_j<\infty.
\]

The pointwise theorem would force every summand to vanish. The dyadic theorem only controls aggregate overload.

Equivalently, if

\[
N_j(T)=\#\{p\in\mathcal P_j:E_p>T X^2\lambda^2z_p\},
\]

then it is enough to have

\[
\sum_j2^{-j}\sum_{r\ge0}2^{2r}N_j(2^r)\ll X^4\lambda^5/(X^4\lambda^4),
\]

or

\[
\sum_j\sum_{r\ge0}2^{2r-j}N_j(2^r)\ll\lambda.
\]

## 4. What pair-Palm controls

The pair-Palm second moment gives control of

\[
\sum_mK(m)(K(m)-1),
\]

and hence pair intersections

\[
\sum_{p\ne q}|Z_p\cap Z_q|.
\]

This controls the average collision of two rows, but HM3 is a third-order output quantity. The implication is only for subranges where \(H_{-p}\) is dominated by pair multiplicity.

A direct Cauchy estimate gives

\[
E_p^2\le z_p\sum_mg_p(m)H_{-p}(m)^2.
\]

Therefore

\[
z_p^{-1}E_p^2\le\sum_{m:g_p(m)=1}H_{-p}(m)^2.
\]

Summing over p:

\[
\sum_pz_p^{-1}E_p^2
\le\sum_m\sum_pg_p(m)H_{-p}(m)^2.
\]

Expanding the square gives triple overlap terms. Pair-Palm controls only the diagonal where two of the three indices coincide. The genuine HM3 obstruction is the ordered triple correlation.

## 5. Nontrivial controlled regimes

### Large z_p

If \(z_p\ge Z\), the number of such rows satisfies

\[
\#\{p:z_p\ge Z\}\le X^2\lambda/Z
\]

by the first moment. Hence a crude bound

\[
E_p\ll X^2\lambda^2z_p+X^2\lambda z_p^{1/2}
\]

already implies the overload estimate on this range whenever

\[
Z\gg\lambda^{-2}.
\]

This removes the high-row tail.

### Small z_p

For \(z_p\le Z\), the threshold is small but the number of rows is large. Here codegree gives

\[
\sum_{p\ne q}\#(Z_p\cap Z_q)\ll X^2\lambda^2.
\]

This controls the part of \(H_{-p}\) coming from one forced companion row. It yields

\[
\sum_{z_p\le Z}z_p^{-1}(E_p-\tau_p)_+^2
\ll X^4\lambda^5
\]

provided the remaining triple term satisfies cancellation. Thus pair-Palm proves a genuine subrange but cannot close HM3 alone.

## 6. Apéry-compatible countermodel to pair control

Take a family of sets satisfying:

* \(|Z_p|\asymp p^{2/3}\);
* no adjacent residues;
* reflection symmetry \(r\leftrightarrow p-1-r\);
* pair codegree bounds;
* all pair intersections pseudorandom.

Now impose a hidden triple atom:

choose a set \(S\subset[1,X^2]\) of size \(X^2\lambda^3\), and force for each \(m\in S\) three primes \(p,q,\ell\) to contain m modulo their primes. Randomize all remaining residues.

The pair contribution changes by only

\[
O(|S|)=O(X^2\lambda^3),
\]

which is below the pair second moment scale, but the triple output contribution is

\[
\sum_mK(m)^3\gg X^2\lambda^3.
\]

Thus pair-Palm plus codegree cannot imply HM3.

The construction respects the combinatorial Apéry constraints (reflection and no-adjacency), but it is not claimed to arise from actual Apéry recurrences. Therefore any proof using actual zero-set structure must exploit more than pair information.

## 7. Conclusion

The weakest useful replacement for the pointwise theorem is the weighted overload inequality

\[
\boxed{\sum_p z_p^{-1}(E_p-X^2\lambda^2z_p)_+^2\ll X^4\lambda^5.}
\]

Pair-Palm second moments prove only:

1. large-z pruning by first moment;
2. small-z control of pair-generated overload;
3. diagonal portions of the HM3 expansion.

They do not control the genuine triple output tail. A new ingredient must control ordered triple geometry (gap-polynomial, Fourier cancellation, or a third factorial moment estimate).
