# Q7935 — anchored-star sign audit

## Status

The requested theorem is **not derivable from the stated HM3 inputs**. The proposed implication

\[
\sum_p E_p^* \gg X^3\lambda^3 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_p z_p^{-1}(E_p^*)_+^2\gg X^6\lambda^5
\]

is false at the level of generality claimed. The missing step is the conversion of a positive global cubic moment into rowwise positive output-prime energy. No such conversion follows without a quantitative bound on the negative part or a sign theorem for every row.

## Exact normalization

For the anchored model, the active rows have

\[
Z_p^*=2,\qquad z_p=2/p,
\]

except the explicitly removed collision primes. Therefore

\[
\frac1{z_p}=\frac p2.
\]

The ordered primitive triple correlation is

\[
C_X^{\times,*}=\sum_pE_p^*.
\]

There is no extra factor of two from exchanging the other primes: the definition with \(G^2-G_2\) already removes the diagonal and counts ordered pairs.

## What Cauchy actually gives

Let

\[
P=\{p:E_p^*>0\}.
\]

Then

\[
\left(\sum_pE_p^*\right)^2
\le
\left(\sum_p z_p^{-1}(E_p^*)_+^2\right)
\left(\sum_{p\in P}z_p\right)
+
2C_X^{\times,*}\sum_{E_p^*<0}|E_p^*|.
\]

The usual shortcut discards the second term. That is invalid unless

\[
\sum_{E_p^*<0}|E_p^*|=o(C_X^{\times,*}).
\]

The anchored factorial moment identities only control the signed total. They do not provide this negative-part estimate.

## Countermodel obstruction

Take any collection of row values with total mass

\[
\sum_pE_p^*=A\asymp X^3\lambda^3.
\]

Choose one row with

\[
E_{p_0}=A,
\]

and all other rows zero. This has the same global total but gives

\[
\sum_pz_p^{-1}(E_p)_+^2
\asymp X\,X^6\lambda^6
\]

only if the single row has size \(A\). Conversely distribute the positive mass over \(\asymp X/\log X\) primes:

\[
E_p\asymp X^2\lambda^3\log X.
\]

Then

\[
S\asymp
\frac X2\frac X{\log X}(X^2\lambda^3\log X)^2
\asymp X^6\lambda^6\log X,
\]

which differs from the claimed \(X^6\lambda^5\) scale. Thus even the Cauchy route needs the exact positive-mass distribution.

More importantly, allowing cancellation with negative rows permits fixed total mass while making \(S\) arbitrarily small. Therefore the global lower bound alone cannot imply the regression theorem.

## What would suffice

A valid replacement lemma is:

> If
> \[
> \sum_{p:E_p^*<0}|E_p^*|\le \eta X^3\lambda^3
> \]
> with \(\eta<1\) fixed, and
> \[
> \sum_p z_p\asymp\lambda,
> \]
> then
> \[
> \sum_pz_p^{-1}(E_p^*)_+^2
> \gg X^6\lambda^5.
> \]

Proof: write \(A_+=\sum(E_p)_+\), \(A_-=-\sum(E_p)_-\). The hypothesis gives \(A_+\gg X^3\lambda^3\). Weighted Cauchy gives

\[
A_+^2\le S\sum_pz_p,
\]

hence

\[
S\gg\frac{X^6\lambda^6}{\lambda}=X^6\lambda^5.
\]

## Finite scans

The reported values

\[
1618,\ 7878,\ 35305,\ 139968
\]

for \(X=128,256,512,1024\) are consistent with the stronger empirical statement
\(E_p^*>0\) for every active row. They are finite evidence only; they do not prove the sign theorem.

A rigorous proof would require an identity isolating the anchor contribution and proving every non-anchor orbit contributes nonnegatively (or proving the negative part bound above). The current anchored factorial moment and defect identities do not contain such a statement.

## Conclusion

The sharp regression statement is conditional:

\[
\boxed{\sum_pz_p^{-1}(E_p^*)_+^2\gg X^6\lambda^5}
\]

follows from a quantitative negative-part/sign lemma, but not from the existing global lower bound. The proposed unconditional sign theorem is presently unproved, and the smallest missing ingredient is exactly a bound on the negative output-prime contribution.
