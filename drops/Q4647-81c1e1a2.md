## 5.1 Cleared gap polynomials and cross-characteristic resultants

At the endpoint \(p\), the two zeros live in \(\mathbb F_p\).  At \(P=p+\Delta\), the shifted two zeros live in \(\mathbb F_P\).  A resultant controls a common root of two polynomials after reduction modulo **one and the same prime**.  It does not turn

\[

\]

into a prime divisor of \(\operatorname{Res}(F,G)\).

After confluence, diagonal evaluation modulo the foreign prime does produce the fixed companion values in (4.8)--(4.9).  But the forced Smith factor is exactly the zero constant term that makes raw diagonal divisibility automatic.  Removing it produces the optional residuals, and an actual support pair need not make them vanish.  Thus resultants exhaust the excess channel and stop at the pure-Smith summand.

## 5.2 Companion/Frobenius matrices

\[
