# 8. Why no listed mechanism proves the Palm theorem

## 8.1 Fibre one removes diagonals, not darkness

Q6077 lets each full tuple contribute at most once. It therefore validates (6.1) as an honest set incidence with no label square. But the sum may contain one dark point for each of many different full tuples. Fibre one gives no cancellation and no marginal coefficient lower bound.

## 8.2 Packed-row injectivity is compatible with maximal Palm mass

The trivial Palm upper bound in (7.1) can be saturated with all rows distinct. Injectivity therefore does not lower the `K` factor.

## 8.3 Hall acts before coefficient colouring

The Hall graph may have maximum expansion while every incident source vertex is dark. To prove (6.2) from Hall, one would need a new expansion theorem for the coefficient-good subgraph or a bound on the dark degree of every outer token. Neither is present.

## 8.4 Reflection only swaps defect types

The pattern `(1,0),(0,1)` proves exact compatibility. Reflection can at best show that the aggregate lower and upper one-sided Palm masses are comparable. Their sum can still have the full trivial size.

## 8.5 The actual `q0` five-block is zeroth order

The outer block establishes genuine physical marking and excludes projective phantoms. It does not determine the two divided coefficient marks of each distinct source lift. The Bockstein system has the wrong direction for positivity: it parametrizes those lifts rather than forcing one to hit the intersection of two prescribed coefficient loci.
