# Q1757 cold audit

## Verdict

Rejected as stated. The first failing line is the coefficient extraction claim

```
Q(m)=7 c_(p-1)-32 c_(p-3)=-7 M0+32 M2.
```

The differentiated recurrence part gives a valid relation for the derivative, but the conversion of the top canonical coefficients into moments has a missing factor and sign depending on the interpolation convention.

## 1. Differentiated defect

Write

\[
L B=(X+1)^3B(X+1)-C_A(X)B(X)+X^3B(X-1)=(X^p-X)Q(X).
\]

In characteristic p,

\[
(X^p-X)'=-1,
\]

so differentiation is

\[
3(X+1)^2B(X+1)+(X+1)^3B'(X+1)-C_A'(X)B(X)-C_A(X)B'(X)
\]
\[
+3X^2B(X-1)+X^3B'(X-1)
=-Q(X)+(X^p-X)Q'(X).
\]

At any field point x, the second term on the right vanishes. Therefore the usable equation is

\[
3(x+1)^2B(x+1)+(x+1)^3B'(x+1)-C_A'(x)B(x)-C_A(x)B'(x)
+3x^2B(x-1)+x^3B'(x-1)=-Q(x).
\]

This is not the same as applying L to B'.

## 2. Center evaluation

The center is m=-1/2. The symmetry is

\[
B(-1-X)=B(X),
\]

hence

\[
B'( -1-X)=-B'(X).
\]

At the gap point a=m-1, the zero conditions give B(a)=B(a+2)=0. The differentiated equations at a and a+2 relate

\[
B'(a-1),B'(a),B'(a+1),B'(a+2),B'(a+3)
\]

but after using reflection only one new derivative remains: the center derivative (equivalently the odd part around m). Thus the system does not by itself determine B'(a).

The claimed closed formula

\[
B'(m-1)=4Q(m)-6A_m
\]

requires eliminating the center derivative. That elimination is not justified by reflection alone.

## 3. Coefficient defect

Let

\[
Q=q_2X^2+q_2X+q_0.
\]

Comparing the top degrees of

\[
LB=(X^p-X)Q
\]

only gives the leading coefficient constraints. The asserted identities

\[
q_2=-32c_{p-1},\qquad q_0=-c_{p-1}-32c_{p-3}
\]

are incomplete because the shifts B(X+1), B(X-1) contribute binomial terms from several top coefficients. The p-characteristic cancellation removes some terms but does not identify q0 solely from c_{p-1},c_{p-3} without an additional normalization computation.

Consequently the moment identity

\[
Q(m)=-7M_0+32M_2
\]

does not follow.

## 4. Boundary of what is proved

The recurrence defect supplies Q. The gap equations supply relations among endpoint derivatives. Reflection supplies the antisymmetry of derivatives. The missing invariant is an independent equation controlling the center derivative (or equivalently the remaining coefficient of the canonical interpolation polynomial).

Therefore the lemma is rejected. The first failing equality is the asserted coefficient/moment conversion, not the differentiated operator identity itself.

No proof that Sigma is nonzero follows from the stated lemma.
