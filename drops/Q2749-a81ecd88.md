Q_N  = finite quasiperiod coordinate,
E_N  = first Taylor/connection correction.

The Q/E recurrences alone give neither. Their indices at a defining prime are \(N=(p-1)/2\), so eliminating them produces quantities depending on the characteristic-specific center, not one common \(C_q(n)\).
