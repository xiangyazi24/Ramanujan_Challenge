# 1. A discrete label-Nash inequality

The first new ingredient is elementary but exactly matched to the banked
moments.

## Lemma 1 — discrete first-moment/L2 Nash inequality

For every finitely supported nonnegative sequence \((r_a)_{a\ge1}\),
\[
 \boxed{
 \left(\sum_a r_a\right)^3
 \le 8
 \left(\sum_a a r_a\right)
 \left(\sum_a r_a^2\right).}
 \tag{1.1}
\]

### Proof

Put
\[
 X=\sum_a r_a,
 \qquad
 Y=\sum_a a r_a,
 \qquad
 Z=\sum_a r_a^2.
\]
For every real \(T\ge1\),
\[
 \sum_{a\le T}r_a\le T^{1/2}Z^{1/2},
 \qquad
 \sum_{a>T}r_a\le \frac{Y}{T}.
\]
Since \(Y^2\ge Z\), the choice
\[
 T=(Y^2/Z)^{1/3}\ge1
\]
is legal and gives
\[
 X\le2(YZ)^{1/3}.
\]
Cubing proves (1.1).  \(\square\)

Apply this at every physical start \(J\) to
\[
 r_a=R(J,a).
\]
Write
\[
 C_J=\sum_aR(J,a),
 \quad
 A_J=\sum_a aR(J,a),
 \quad
 B_J=\sum_aR(J,a)^2.
 \tag{1.2}
\]
Then
\[
 C_J^3\le8A_JB_J.
\]
Therefore
\[
 C_J^{3/2}\le\sqrt8\,(A_JB_J)^{1/2}.
\]
Cauchy in \(J\), followed by (0.3)--(0.4), gives
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_J C_J^{3/2}
 \ll
 \left(\sum_JA_J\right)^{1/2}
 \left(\sum_JB_J\right)^{1/2}
 \ll P^{2+o(1)}.}
 \tag{1.3}
\]

This is the exact extra moment that CC2q31 did not consume.

A useful audit point is that (1.3) is not a hidden use of the close-label
target.  It uses only the already-safe same-label square and the physical
first-return spacing moment.
