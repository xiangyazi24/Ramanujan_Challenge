## Verdict

The remaining obstruction is a genuine ternary automaton problem for the pair consisting of the Kummer carry pattern of `L_j(n)` and the Apéry residue pattern of `b_n-b_j`.

The reduction to a minimum valuation statement is exact:

```text
v_3(D_n)=min_{0<=j<M}(v_3(L_j(n))+v_3(b_n-b_j)).
```

No replacement of `b_n-b_j` by `b_j` is valid here. The known unit statement for `Gamma_n` does not imply the corresponding statement for `D_n`.

## Exact valuation decomposition

For every `j<M`, Kummer gives

```text
v_3(L_j)=C_3(n,j)+C_3(n-j-1,M-1-j),
```

where the two terms count ternary carries in the indicated additions. Hence

```text
v_3(D_n)=min_j(C_3(n,j)+C_3(n-j-1,M-1-j)+v_3(b_n-b_j)).
```

The first two terms are purely geometric. The third term is controlled only after computing the Apéry residues.

Using Apéry--Lucas,

```text
b_m mod 3 = product b_{m_i} mod 3
```

for ternary digits `m_i`. Since `b_0=1`, `b_1=5=2 mod 3`, `b_2=73=1 mod 3`, the mod-3 residue is the parity of the number of ternary digits equal to one. This gives a finite-state description of whether `b_n-b_j` is a unit modulo 3.

However, lifting this to valuation exactly one requires the joint mod-9 state; mod-3 information alone is insufficient.

## Exceptional families

## Non-exceptional case

The required proof would construct `j<M` satisfying simultaneously:

```text
v_3(L_j)=0
b_n != b_j (mod 3).
```

The carry-free part is a Kummer condition. The residue part is a ternary digit condition. A naive choice such as `j=n mod 3^s` is insufficient because it guarantees a carry-free suffix but does not guarantee a different Apéry residue.

Therefore the finite-state route needs a combined automaton whose state records:

1. ternary prefix/suffix carry status for both binomial factors;
2. the accumulated Apéry residue of `n` and `j` modulo 3;
3. the lifted residue modulo 9 when the mod-3 residues coincide.

No such completed certificate is currently available in the supplied statement.


outside the two exceptional families.

The current proof ingredients establish the valuation formula and finite patterns, but they do not yet prove the required all-length finite-state exclusion.
