## Local geometry

At the branch value,

\[
\Lambda=\frac{uv(w-1)(1-uvw)}{w(u-1)(v-1)}
\]

has the critical point

\[
u=v,\qquad w=2-u,\qquad u^2-2u-1=0.
\]

The logarithmic Hessian is nonzero:

\[
\det H_{\log}=(u-1)/2.
\]

Thus after choosing a local parameter t with t=0 at the branch root, the completed local equation has the formal Morse form

\[
xy+z^2=t
\]

(up to multiplication of t by a unit). The Hessian square class controls the Frobenius action on the rank-one vanishing cycle. This is the quadratic character appearing in the vanishing quotient. It does not identify that quotient with the exceptional divisor after resolution.

## Correct cohomology diagram

Let Y be the singular proper fibre, X~ its minimal resolution, and X_eta the geometric generic fibre. Under the missing regular semistable-model hypothesis, SGA 7 II Exp XV gives the invariant-cycle exact sequence

\[
0\to H^2(Y_{\bar k},Q_l(1))
\to H^2(X_{\bar\eta},Q_l(1))^I
\to 0.
\]

Equivalently

\[
H^2(Y_{\bar k},Q_l(1))\simeq H^2(X_{\bar\eta},Q_l(1))^I
\]

Frobenius-equivariantly.

The vanishing quotient is instead

\[
0\to H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I
\to H^2(X_{\bar\eta})
\to V_{van}\to0,

where dim V_van=1 for a single node. The Frobenius eigenvalue on V_van may involve the Hessian character. It is not part of H^2(Y).

## Resolution contribution

For a split A1 singularity, the minimal resolution introduces one exceptional curve E=P^1. The standard blow-up/resolution sequence gives

\[
H^2(\widetilde X_{\bar k},Q_l)
\simeq H^2(Y_{\bar k},Q_l)\oplus Q_l(-1).
\]

With arithmetic Frobenius, the eigenvalue on Q_l(-1) is p. With geometric Frobenius it is p^{-1}; all point-count formulas use arithmetic Frobenius convention and therefore the exceptional contribution is +p in the trace.

Hence

\[
\operatorname{Tr}H^2(Y)=\operatorname{Tr}H^2(\widetilde X)-p
\]

for arithmetic Frobenius.

## The divisor lattice

The nineteen global divisor classes must be handled relatively, not only fibrewise.

Required checks:

1. Construct divisors on the total resolved family over a punctured neighbourhood of alpha.
2. Extend their closures over alpha.
3. Check none passes through the moving conifold point.
4. Verify intersection matrix remains rank 19.
5. Verify Frobenius action is unchanged.

The displayed boundary calculation proves fibrewise independence of a rank-19 lattice on the resolved special fibre. It does not by itself prove relative extension. That is exactly the missing specialization lemma.

If the lemma holds, write L for this lattice and E for the exceptional class. Then

\[
\dim(L)=19,\qquad \dim(L+E)=20,
\]

and the transcendental part has dimension two before resolution and dimension two after removing L+E.

## Trace comparison

Let

\[
\theta_p(\alpha)=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Frob}_p|T_Y)
\]

where T_Y is the primitive complement of L in H^2(Y). Let T_X be the primitive complement of L+E in H^2(X~). Then the exact sequence gives

\[
T_Y\simeq T_X
\]

as Frobenius modules.

Therefore

\[
\theta_p(\alpha)=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Frob}|T_X)
\]

and the branch defect

\[
E_{br}=\theta_p(\alpha)-t_p(\alpha)
\]

is zero provided t_p is defined from the same primitive invariant stalk.

## Frobenius character clarification

The Hessian character is real and nontrivial in general. It belongs to the action on

\[
V_{van}=H^2(X_{\bar\eta})/H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I.
\]

It disappears from the invariant-cycle stalk. Therefore inserting a Legendre-symbol correction into E_br double-counts the vanishing cycle.

**Regular local-model lemma.** After the fixed boundary resolution, the toric family admits a projective regular model over Spec(O_{F_p[[t]]}) with special fibre having exactly one A1 singularity and with the nineteen divisor closures Cartier and disjoint from the node.
