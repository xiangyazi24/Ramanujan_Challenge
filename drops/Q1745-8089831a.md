# Q1745 corrected endpoint-pencil deformation audit

## Status

The requested bordered-determinant certificate is not established by the currently available argument. The previous Q1741-style symmetric continuant construction is invalid unless a diagonal scaling is supplied: the natural tridiagonal continuant has off-diagonal products carrying the cubic coefficients, hence j^6 factors after pairing. No such scaling identity has been proved here.

The correct arithmetic anchor is

\[
C(1)=34+51+27+5=117,
\]

and therefore

\[
8b_2=117\cdot5-1=584,\qquad b_2=73.
\]

The earlier value 29/2 is not compatible with the recurrence.

## Exact transfer formulation

For the centered pencil define

\[
U_0(\xi)=1,\qquad U_1(\xi)=\xi,
\]

and

\[
U_{t+1}=\frac{4t(68t^2+3)U_t-(2t-1)^3U_{t-1}}{(2t+1)^3}.
\]

The endpoint polynomial is

\[
E_m(\xi)=U_{m-1}(\xi)-5U_m(\xi).
\]

The cleared trace polynomial is

\[
F_m(\xi)=\prod_{t=2}^{m}U_t-6\sum_{t=2}^{m}(t^2-1)\prod_{s\ne t}U_s.
\]

A valid certificate must produce a matrix pencil M(ξ,λ) satisfying

\[
\det M(\xi,0)=E_m(\xi),\qquad
\partial_\lambda\det M(\xi,\lambda)|_{0}=cF_m(\xi)
\]

with an explicitly verified constant c.

## Local obstruction to the naive continuant route

A tridiagonal determinant with diagonal entries d_j and off-diagonals α_j,β_j satisfies

\[
D_{j+1}=d_{j+1}D_j-\alpha_j\beta_jD_{j-1}.
\]

Matching the recurrence requires

\[
\alpha_j\beta_j=\frac{j^6}{(j+1)^3j^3}
\]

up to the chosen normalization. The symmetric choice α_j=β_j forces square-root factors and does not reproduce the recurrence over general finite fields without an additional scaling. Therefore the previously suggested symmetric determinant is not a proof object.

## Small cases

For m=2:

\[
U_2=\frac{117\xi-1}{8},\qquad
E_2=\xi-5U_2=\frac{5-577\xi}{8}.
\]

The trace polynomial is linear in ξ after clearing denominators; any determinant identity must reproduce this exact pair.

For m=3 and m=4 the same transfer recurrence gives explicit rational polynomials. A proposed deformation must match both E_m and the λ-derivative simultaneously. No bordered matrix satisfying these constraints has been verified.

## Conclusion

The only rigorous conclusion at this stage is a scoped no-go for the unscaled symmetric continuant construction. It does not prove Σ≠0. A successful proof requires a new deformation with explicitly defined bordered vectors or a different algebraic identity. Finite computations alone cannot replace that missing certificate.
