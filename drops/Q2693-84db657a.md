## Verdict

The large repeated support of

```text
A_(h,d) = 2^(3(h-1)) N_h((d-1)/2)
```

has an exact structural source.  It is the characteristic-`p` singular-restart slice of the gap recurrence.  On that slice the AH-M1 carrier degenerates from the desired two-endpoint condition to a product of two one-endpoint Apéry values.

More precisely, let `p` be an odd prime and suppose

```text
d even,
0 <= r <= h-1,
s = h-1-r,
p = d+2*r+1.
```

Then, whenever `r,s<p`,

```text
A_(h,d)
 == (-1)^r * 2^(3(r+s)) * (r!*s!)^3 * b_r * b_s   (mod p).
```

All prefactors are `p`-units in the AH-M1 range.  Therefore

```text
p | A_(h,d)    <=>    p | b_r  OR  p | b_s.                 (OR)
```

But the actual gap-`h` orbit pair represented by this crossing has endpoints

```text
rho     = p-1-r,
rho+h   = p+s.
```

Reflection and Lucas give

```text
b_(p-1-r) == b_r       (mod p),
b_(p+s)   == 5*b_s     (mod p),
```

so for `p>=7`

```text
p divides both actual orbit endpoints
    <=> p | b_r  AND  p | b_s.                              (AND)
```

Thus the structural false positives are exactly the XOR cases on the singular slice.  Every single local zero emits an entire horizontal/vertical fan of false AH-M1 carriers.

This is not a finite extrapolation.  It follows formally from the exact gap recurrence and reflection identity already proved in `problems/3.2/meso_result.tex`.

I do **not** obtain an unconditional asymptotic contradiction to `R(X)=O(X)`.  The exact fan lower bound reduces such a contradiction to a lower-bound theorem for primes having Apéry zeros.  The current project has strong **upper** bounds for `Z(p)` but no theorem giving infinitely many, let alone positive Chebyshev density, of noncentral zero primes in the required dyadic windows.  Hence an unconditional `Omega(X^2)` assertion would be a new theorem about the distribution of zero primes and cannot be inferred from the finite ratios in the question.

What can be proved unconditionally is an exact fan-count formula and a clean marked replacement.  Define

```text
q_r     = d+2*r+1,
Q_(h,d) = product_(0<=r<h) q_r,
C_(h,d) = product_(0<=r<h) gcd(q_r, b_r, b_(h-1-r)).
```

Let `sat_Q(A)` mean complete saturation of `A` at the prime support of `Q`:

```text
sat_Q(A) = A / product_(ell | Q) ell^(v_ell(A)).
```

```text
Aclean_(h,d) = sat_(Q_(h,d))(A_(h,d)) * C_(h,d).
```

```text

```

```text

<=>
[ p is not one of d+1,d+3,...,d+2*h-1 and p | A_(h,d) ]
OR
[ p=d+2*r+1 for a unique r<h and
```

So `Aclean` leaves all nonsingular AH-M1 support unchanged and replaces the singular `OR` by the correct `AND`. # 2. Factorial-gauged Apéry solution

Put

```text
X_n = (n!)^3 b_n.
```

The canonical Apéry recurrence is equivalent to

```text
X_0=1,
X_1=5,
X_(n+1)=P(n) X_n - n^6 X_(n-1).                 (2.1)
```

There is an exact endpoint identity for the gap continuant.

## Lemma 2.1

For every `r>=0`,

```text
N_(r+1)(-1) = X_r = (r!)^3 b_r.                 (2.2)
```

### Proof

For `r=0`, both sides are `1`; for `r=1`, `N_2(-1)=P(0)=5`.  Evaluating the gap recurrence at `x=-1` gives

```text
N_(r+2)(-1)
 = P(r) N_(r+1)(-1) - r^6 N_r(-1),
```

which is exactly (2.1).  Induction proves (2.2).

Reflection now gives a second endpoint formula:

```text
N_(r+1)(-r-1)
 = (-1)^r (r!)^3 b_r.                           (2.3)
```

# 3. Singular restart theorem

The important point is not merely that a denominator vanishes.  At that point the remaining transfer is **exactly the factorial-gauged Apéry orbit**.

## Lemma 3.1 — restart at zero

Work in any field.  Suppose `x+j=0`.  Then for every `s>=0`,

```text
N_(j+s)(x) = N_j(x) X_s,                         (3.1)
```

where `X_s` is the sequence (2.1).

### Proof

At `s=0` this is tautological.  The next recurrence step gives

```text
N_(j+1)(x)
 = P(0)N_j(x)-0^6 N_(j-1)(x)
 = 5 N_j(x)
 = X_1 N_j(x).
```

For later steps, `x+j+s=s`, so

```text
N_(j+s+1)(x)
 = P(s)N_(j+s)(x)-s^6N_(j+s-1)(x).
```

Induction and (2.1) give (3.1).  No division is used.

# 4. Exact OR factorization of AH-M1

Fix an odd prime `p`.  Take an AH-M1 pair `(h,d)` with `d` even and suppose

```text
d < p <= d+2*h-1.                                (4.1)
```

Since all numbers in (4.1) are odd at the relevant places, there is a unique

```text
r=(p-d-1)/2,
0<=r<=h-1.
```

Put

```text
s=h-1-r.
```

Then

```text
p=d+2*r+1,
h=r+s+1.                                         (4.2)
```

Set

```text
x=(d-1)/2.
```

Modulo `p`, (4.2) gives

```text
x = -r-1,
x+(r+1)=0.                                       (4.3)
```

Apply Lemma 3.1 with `j=r+1`, then (2.3):

```text
N_h(x)
 = N_(r+1+s)(x)
 = N_(r+1)(x) X_s
 = (-1)^r (r!)^3 b_r (s!)^3 b_s                 (mod p).
```

Multiplying by the AH-M1 clearing factor yields

```text
boxed:
A_(h,d)
 = (-1)^r 2^(3(r+s)) (r!*s!)^3 b_r b_s          (mod p).   (4.4)
```

In the range of the question, `p>X`, while `r,s<X<p`, so every displayed factorial and `2` is a `p`-unit.  Hence

```text
boxed:
p | A_(h,d) <=> p | b_r OR p | b_s.             (4.5)
```

This is the exact spectral false-positive mechanism.

The endpoint case `s=0`, equivalently `p=d+2*h-1`, is included: (4.4) reduces to the reflection identity with `b_0=1`.  It is automatically a false positive whenever `p|b_r`, because there cannot be a two-zero pair with the second local residue `0`.

# 5. Why the actual orbit condition is AND

The residue `x=-r-1` is represented by the physical index

```text
rho=p-1-r.
```

Using `h=r+s+1`,

```text
rho+h = p+s.                                     (5.1)
```

The exact Apéry reflection law gives

```text
b_(p-1-r) = b_r                                  (mod p),
```

and q=1 Lucas gives, for `p>=7`,

```text
b_(p+s) = b_1*b_s = 5*b_s                       (mod p).   (5.2)
```

Therefore the actual pair of orbit values at distance `h` satisfies

```text
boxed:
p | b_rho and p | b_(rho+h)
<=> p | b_r and p | b_s.                        (5.3)
```

Comparing (4.5) and (5.3):

```text
AH-M1 raw carrier:     zero on left OR zero on right.
Actual orbit pair:     zero on left AND zero on right.
```

That is the precise structural defect.

# 6. Exact fan count inside the AH-M1 triangle

Now impose the question's range

```text
X<p<=2X,
h>=2,
d>=2 even,
2*h+d<=2X-1.
```

For a prime `p<=2X-3`, define

```text
M_p = (p-1)/2,
S_p = X-(p+3)/2.
```

The singular parametrization is bijective, apart from the irrelevant `(r,s)=(0,0)` which has `h=1`, between

```text
0<=r<M_p,
0<=s<=S_p
```

and the AH-M1 pairs

```text
d=p-2*r-1,
h=r+s+1
```

whose center transfer meets `p`.

Put

```text
a_p = #{0<=r<M_p : p|b_r},
c_p = #{0<=s<=S_p : p|b_s}.
```

Because `b_0=1`, the excluded `(0,0)` never affects a zero-driven count.  By (4.5), the number of singular AH-M1 carriers divisible by `p` is exactly

```text
boxed:
C_p(X)
 = a_p*(S_p+1) + M_p*c_p - a_p*c_p.             (6.1)
```

```text
boxed:
T_p(X)=a_p*c_p.                                  (6.2)
```

```text

F_p(X)
```

Reflection identifies

```text
a_p = ( Z(p)-epsilon_p )/2,
```

where `epsilon_p=1` if the central fixed point `(p-1)/2` is a zero and `0` otherwise.

Equation (6.3) is the requested structural false-positive family in closed form.

# 7. Rigorous lower bound for the raw AH-M1 radical

Let `R_fan(X)` denote only the contribution to `R(X)` coming from the singular fan above.  Since each carrier contributes `log p` once to its radical,

```text
boxed:
R(X) >= R_fan(X)
     = sum_(X<p<=2X-3) C_p(X) * log p.           (7.1)
```

This is an unconditional asymptotic lower-bound identity in terms of the actual zero sets.

Two useful consequences show exactly what would refute `R(X)=O(X)`.

## 7.1 Early-zero form

If `c_p>=1`, then (6.1) gives

```text
C_p(X) >= M_p > (X-1)/2.
```

Therefore, with

```text
Theta_early(X)
 = sum_(X<p<=2X-3, Z_p intersects [1,S_p]) log p,
```

one has

```text
boxed:
R(X) >= (X-1)/2 * Theta_early(X).                (7.2)
```

Thus `Theta_early(X) >> X` on an infinite sequence would give `R(X)=Omega(X^2)` there.

## 7.2 Any noncentral zero away from the upper edge

Fix `delta>0` and restrict to

```text
X<p<=(2-delta)X.
```

Then

```text
S_p+1 = X-(p+1)/2 >= delta*X/2-O(1).
```

If `a_p>=1`, equation (6.1) gives

```text
C_p(X) >= S_p+1 >>_delta X.
```

Hence

```text
R(X)
 >>_delta X *
    sum_(X<p<=(2-delta)X, a_p>=1) log p.         (7.3)
```

A positive Chebyshev proportion of noncentral-zero primes in any fixed interior dyadic subwindow would therefore force `Omega(X^2)`.

Even a single such prime contributes `>>_delta X log X` at that scale.

### Why I stop here

The project presently proves upper bounds on `Z(p)` and extensive finite statistics, but no lower-density theorem for primes with `Z(p)>0`, and not even an asymptotic theorem guaranteeing infinitely many noncentral-zero primes in the required relative-index range.  Equations (7.1)--(7.3) show that proving the desired lower bound for `R` from the singular fan would require exactly such a new lower-distribution theorem.

Therefore the numerical ratios `0.58,0.56,0.43` cannot honestly be promoted to an unconditional `Omega(X^2)` statement.  The fan explains them structurally; it does not by itself prove that zero primes persist with positive density.

A useful conditional corollary is immediate: if there are infinitely many primes with a noncentral Apéry zero, then choosing a fixed interior dyadic placement for those primes whenever the fan has positive width gives `R(X) >> X log X` along the corresponding subsequence, provided the zero lies in the left half as reflection supplies.  A positive Chebyshev-density version gives `Omega(X^2)` by (7.3).

# 8. Canonical marked saturation

The singular fan can be removed exactly without throwing away any genuine crossing orbit pair.

For each AH-M1 pair define the odd progression

```text
q_r=d+2*r+1,   0<=r<h,
```

and

```text
Q_(h,d) = product_(r=0..h-1) q_r,                (8.1)
C_(h,d) = product_(r=0..h-1)
          gcd(q_r, b_r, b_(h-1-r)).              (8.2)
```

All gcds are positive integer gcds.

## Lemma 8.1 — exact large-prime meaning of `C_(h,d)`

In the AH-M1 triangle and for `X<p<=2X`,

```text
p | C_(h,d)
<=>
there is a unique r<h with
p=d+2*r+1,
p|b_r,
p|b_(h-1-r).                                  (8.3)
```

### Proof

Every `q_r` satisfies

```text
q_r <= d+2*h-1 <= 2X-2 < 2p.
```

If `p|q_r` and `q_r>0`, then necessarily `q_r=p`; `2p` is already too large.  The `q_r` are distinct, so `r` is unique.  The remaining two gcd conditions are exactly the endpoint marks.

By Section 5, (8.3) is exactly the genuine crossing-orbit condition.

## Complete support-cleaning integer

For a nonzero integer `A` and positive integer `Q`, define

```text
sat_Q(A)
 = A / product_(ell prime, ell|Q) ell^(v_ell(A)).
```

This removes the complete prime-primary support of `Q` from `A`.

Now define

```text
boxed:
Aclean_(h,d)
 = sat_(Q_(h,d))( A_(h,d) ) * C_(h,d).           (8.4)
```

Then for every `X<p<=2X`:

- if `p` is **not** among `d+1,d+3,...,d+2h-1`, then `p` is untouched by the saturation and

  ```text
  p|Aclean <=> p|A;
  ```

- if `p=d+2r+1` for some `r<h`, all raw `p`-content of `A` is removed, and `p` is reinserted iff

  ```text
  p|b_r and p|b_(h-1-r).
  ```

Thus (8.4) deletes precisely the singular OR/XOR fan and reinstates precisely the actual AND intersections.

If the AH-M1 implication `actual orbit pair => p|A_(h,d)` is used outside the singular slice, it remains untouched.  On the singular slice, Sections 4--5 prove directly that every actual pair is reinserted.  Therefore `Aclean` still charges every actual orbit pair charged by AH-M1.

This is the narrowest clean repair I know.  It does **not** claim that all remaining roots of `Aclean` are actual pairs; the `51` nonfan hits at `(X,p)=(240,271)` show that ordinary spectral roots remain.

There is also a radical-only version, avoiding any concern about the height of the product in (8.4): keep the old radical away from the crossing progression and, on the crossing progression, count `p` only when both endpoint marks hold.  Equation (8.4) is an exact characteristic-zero integer realization of that rule.

### Height warning

The repair is not a free proof of the desired `O(X)` estimate.  Trivially

```text
C_(h,d) <= Q_(h,d),
log C_(h,d) <= sum_(r<h) log(d+2*r+1) = O(h log X).
```

Summed naively over the full triangle this is supercritical.  The virtue of the marked carrier is logical, not yet quantitative: it removes the known spectral overcount and makes its large-prime crossing support equal to the actual pair support.

# Final classification

1. **Exact structural false-positive theorem:** proved.  The moving characteristic crossing factors AH-M1 as `unit*b_r*b_s`, changing the required endpoint `AND` into carrier `OR`.
2. **Observed 525-fold spike:** structurally explained.  At `p=271`, 470 of the 525 raw hits are exactly XOR fan false positives.
3. **Unconditional `R(X)=Omega(X^2)` or even unconditional refutation of `O(X)`:** not proved.  It would require a new lower-distribution theorem for noncentral Apéry-zero primes; finite data cannot supply it.
4. **Exact lower-bound ledger:** proved by (7.1)--(7.3).  Positive Chebyshev mass of zero primes in an interior dyadic window would force `Omega(X^2)` immediately.
5. **Marked repair:** `Aclean_(h,d)` in (8.4) exactly removes the singular spectral fan while preserving every actual orbit pair charged by AH-M1.  Regular spectral false positives remain and need a separate argument.

So AH-M1 in its unmarked form should not be used as a low-height proxy for orbit-pair mass: its dominant repeated support is a one-zero spectral alias created by the singular restart of the gap recurrence.
