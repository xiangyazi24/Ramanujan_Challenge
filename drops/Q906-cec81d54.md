# Q906 audit repair: scope of the Q904 conclusion

## Verdict

The hostile objection is correct. The phrase "CRT-saturated local module" in Q904 was too strong as stated. The previous argument treated the local coordinates at different primes as freely variable, but that is not justified for the actual Apéry sequence. A fixed pair of sequences `(a_n,b_n)` is generated by one recurrence, so residues at different primes and folded indices may have hidden global relations.

Therefore the universal no-go claim is retracted. What survives is only a scoped statement about a particular elimination attempt.

## What Q904 actually established

The valid part of Q904 is the following narrow algebraic observation.

For a fixed target prime p and a row decomposition

\[
n=qp+r,
\]

with the stated hypotheses, the available jets give

\[
b_n=b_q(b_r+2qpD_r)\pmod {p^2},
\]

and, when `p | b_r`,

\[
\frac{b_n}{p}=b_q(\frac{b_r}{p}+2qD_r)\pmod p.
\]

The companion identity gives

\[
b_qp^3a_n-a_qb_n=p^3a_r\pmod {p^4}.
\]

The second identity shows that after removing the known rank-one component, the remaining first companion transverse term is controlled by `a_r`. These statements are local consequences at each target prime.

They do not imply that the tuple of all target-prime residues is an arbitrary product of local variables.

## Failed elimination attempt

The attempted construction was:

1. Introduce local coordinates

\[
X_i=b_{q_i},\quad Y_i=D_{r_i},\quad Z_i=a_{r_i}
\]

for target primes `p_i`.

2. Use the congruences at each `p_i` to eliminate the visible first-order terms.

3. Conclude that any remaining expression is a CRT combination of independent local coordinates and therefore cannot produce a fixed small-height integer.

Step 3 is the invalid step.

The variables are not independent. In the real problem,

\[
(a_{r_i},b_{r_i},D_{r_i},a_{q_i},b_{q_i})
\]

come from the same Apéry recurrence and the same global index geometry. Equal-gap conditions may relate the `r_i`; reflection/Casoratian identities may relate companion and Apéry coordinates; and the recurrence can impose nonlinear constraints not visible in the local congruence statements alone.

CRT only says that independent congruence classes can be combined. It does not prove that the residues produced by one fixed sequence realize independent classes.

## Why no universal countermodel is supplied here

A genuine repair in the direction of option (1) would require constructing a formal class of recurrence sequences preserving:

- the Apéry recurrence,
- initial normalization,
- Casoratian relation,
- the stated mod-p^2 Lucas jet,
- the mod-p^3/mod-p^4 companion laws,
- the same-row folded-index geometry,

while varying one target local coordinate. Such a construction is not provided by the Q904 argument and cannot be assumed from CRT.

Option (2), a theorem proving local surjectivity for the actual Apéry sequence, is also unavailable.

Therefore the correct conclusion is option (3): retract the global no-go and retain only the failed elimination statement.

## Remaining precise obstruction

To prove that no new cross-prime invariant exists, one would need an additional theorem of one of the following forms:

1. A rigidity theorem showing that the recurrence forces all candidate equal-gap jet coordinates into a lower-dimensional algebraic variety whose defining equations are known.

2. A local-global independence theorem for Apéry jets at finitely many primes.

3. An explicit elimination theorem showing that every fixed characteristic-zero expression constructed from the available jets reduces to combinations of already-known rank-one lifts.

Without one of these, the existence or nonexistence of a new characteristic-zero carrier remains open from the stated inputs.

## Final corrected statement

The following sentence from Q904 survives:

> The available two jet layers alone do not exhibit a valid elimination producing a fixed small-height cross-prime carrier.

The following sentence does not survive:

> The two jet layers form a CRT-saturated local module, hence no such carrier can exist.

The correct replacement is:

> The current elimination attempt fails because it requires an unproved independence assertion about Apéry jet coordinates across folded rows. The known local identities prove only local rank-one cancellation, not a universal cross-prime no-go theorem.
