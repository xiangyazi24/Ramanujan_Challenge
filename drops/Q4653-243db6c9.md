## 10.4 Classical sixfold large sieve

For six primes \(p_i\asymp X\), the complete CRT period is

\begin{lemma}[Hard folded-coordinate rigidity]
\label{lem:q4653-hard-fold-rigidity}
Let $X\ge2$, $X\le m<X^2$, and let $p$ be a prime such that
\[
 X<p\le2X,\qquad p>2\sqrt m,\qquad
 r:=m\bmod p\in\mathcal Z_p.
\]
Put $s_p(m)=\min\{r,p-1-r\}$.  Then $s_p(m)<X$ and
$p\mid b_{s_p(m)}$.  For fixed $m$ and $s$, at most one hard hit
prime has
\[
 s_p(m)=s,\qquad m\bmod p=s,
\]
and at most one hard hit prime has
\[
 s_p(m)=s,\qquad m\bmod p=p-1-s.
\]
Consequently at most two hard hit primes at a fixed row have the same
canonical folded coordinate.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In the first orientation $p\mid m-s$.  Here $m-s>0$: if $m=s$ then
$p>m$, while canonicity gives $p\ge2m+1$, contradicting
$p\le2X\le2m$.  Thus two distinct hard primes in this orientation

\[
0<m-s<m<4m<pq,

\[

\end{proof}

 \ell_t=\left\lfloor\frac{t^2}{8C_0L_X}\right\rfloor.

 \sum_pz_p(z_p-1)\ge\frac{E^2}{L_X}-E.
