# Quotient formulation and coefficient audit

Work in
\[
A=\mathbf F_p[z]/(z^{m+1}-z).
\]
The map from even functions in the centered variable is obtained by writing
\(z=t^2\). Since \(t\mapsto t+1\) is a permutation of \(\mathbf F_p\), the shifted even functions are still represented by elements of \(A\):

\[
P((t+1)^2),\qquad P((t-1)^2)
\]
are reduced by first expanding in \(t\), using evenness \(t^2=z\), and finally reducing powers by
\[
z^{m+1}=z.
\]
This reduction is the required invariant quotient operation; one cannot replace shifts by ordinary polynomial substitution in z because the intermediate odd powers cancel only after combining the three terms.

Let
\[
G(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{m}g_i z^i,
\qquad P(z)G(z)=1\pmod {z^{m+1}-z}.
\]
For every field point with \(G(t^2)\neq0\), substitution \(C=1/G\) gives
\[
a_tG_tG_{t-1}+d_tG_{t+1}G_{t-1}+c_tG_{t+1}G_t
=-Q_{center}\delta_0G_{t-1}G_tG_{t+1}.
\]
The left side is a function of t invariant under t -> -t and hence corresponds to a unique element of A.

# Top coefficient equations

Write
\[
E(z)=a(z)G(z)G_{-}(z)+d(z)G_{+}(z)G_{-}(z)+c(z)G_{+}(z)G(z),
\]
where \(G_{\pm}\) denote the quotient representatives of the shifted evaluations. After reduction modulo \(z^{m+1}-z\), the coefficient equations at the top two degrees have the form

\[
[z^m]E=\alpha_m g_m^2+\beta_m g_m+L_m(g_0,\ldots,g_{m-1})
\]

and

\[
[z^{m-1}]E=\alpha_{m-1}g_m^2+\beta_{m-1}g_m+L_{m-1}(g_0,\ldots,g_{m-1}).
\]

The delta term contributes only through the cubic product
\[
-Q_{center}G(-1/4)G(-1/4+1)G(-1/4-1),
\]
with the centered representatives of the three neighboring points. Therefore it introduces no new free top coefficient beyond the same lower-coefficient evaluations.

The attempted conclusion that
\[
\Lambda=g_m+\frac23G(0)+2G(1)
\]
is forced nonzero does not follow from these two equations. The first unavoidable obstruction is that both equations contain the independent lower combination
\[
L_m(g_0,\ldots,g_{m-1}),
\]
whose value is not determined by \(g_m,G(0),G(1)\) alone.

# Exact small-prime check

For p=19 (m=9) and p=37 (m=18), the exact computation proceeds by:

```text
1. interpolate P(z) from the Apéry values C(t)=P(t^2);
2. compute G as the inverse of P modulo z^(m+1)-z;
3. reduce the three shifted terms in the quotient;
4. compare coefficients of the resulting identity.
```

The resulting coefficient systems satisfy the quadratic equations above, but the remaining lower-coefficient terms do not vanish identically. Hence no smaller polynomial gate for Lambda is obtained from the top two equations alone.

# Verdict

The quotient attack is valid as an algebraic reformulation, but the hoped-for closure on
\[
\Lambda=g_m+\frac23G(0)+2G(1)
\]
is not proved. The first obstruction is an explicit lower-coefficient coupling: the top coefficient equations retain \(L_m(g_0,\ldots,g_{m-1})\). A complete proof would require an additional identity eliminating that coupling; it is not a consequence of the delta-source equation and quotient reduction alone.
