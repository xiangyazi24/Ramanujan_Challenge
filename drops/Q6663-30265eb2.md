# 2. The missing structural step: primitivity

Irreducibility alone does not exclude a disconnected symplectically imprimitive group. This has to be checked rather than silently assumed.

We prove the needed statement directly from the local data.

## 2.1 General block setup

Suppose that V_h has a nontrivial system of imprimitivity

```text
V_h = V_1 direct-sum ... direct-sum V_k,
```

where the blocks have equal dimension d, kd=rank(V_h), and H_h permutes them transitively.

Let

```text
rho_blk : H_h -> S_k
```

be the block permutation action.

The connected identity component H_h^0 fixes every block, because a connected algebraic group has trivial image in the finite group S_k.

Every unipotent element of H_h lies in H_h^0. Hence

```text
rho_blk(nu_h)=1.
```

Since nu_h=T_0^3, if

```text
sigma = rho_blk(T_0),
```

then

```text
sigma^3=1.                                  (2.1)
```

## 2.2 The infinity inertia fixes all nonzero-wild blocks

Assume p>k. The wild inertia P_infinity is a pro-p group, so its finite permutation image in S_k is trivial. Consequently every block V_i is P_infinity-stable.

The nonzero wild characters L_a are pairwise distinct and one-dimensional. Therefore a P_infinity-stable block is the direct sum of:

```text
- some subset of the L_a lines;
- possibly a subspace of the rank-2 zero-wild part T_h.
```

The full inertia I_infinity preserves each rank-one summand L_a and preserves T_h. Thus any block containing an L_a line is fixed by I_infinity: if g sent that block to another block, the same invariant line L_a would lie in two distinct direct-sum blocks.

A block containing no L_a lies inside T_h. Since rank(T_h)=2:

```text
- if d>=2, there is at most one such block, so it is fixed;
- if d=1, there are at most two such blocks.
```

In the d=1 case the whole group is monomial in the block basis. But every invertible monomial matrix over characteristic zero is semisimple: on each permutation cycle its characteristic polynomial is X^m-c with c!=0, which is separable. Hence a monomial group contains no nontrivial unipotent element.

Since nu_h is nontrivial for h>=2, the case d=1 is impossible.

We conclude:

```text
rho_blk(I_infinity)=1.                      (2.2)
```

## 2.3 h=2

The nontrivial factorizations 6=kd with d>1 are

```text
(k,d)=(2,3) or (3,2).
```

For k=2, (2.1) forces sigma=1, so the block action cannot be transitive.

For k=3, transitivity and (2.2) force sigma to be a 3-cycle. Write T_0 cyclically as

```text
V_1 -> V_2 -> V_3 -> V_1.
```

Then the three restrictions of T_0^3 to V_1,V_2,V_3 are conjugate. Therefore

```text
rank(T_0^3-1)
```

is a multiple of 3. But its actual value is 1. Contradiction.

Hence V_2 is primitive.

## 2.4 h=3

The nontrivial factorizations 10=kd with d>1 are

```text
(k,d)=(2,5) or (5,2).
```

By (2.2), I_infinity acts trivially on the blocks. The block action is generated by sigma with sigma^3=1.

No permutation of order dividing 3 is transitive on 2 or 5 points. Hence V_3 is primitive.

Thus:

```text
[PRIM-2]
V_2 is primitive.

[PRIM-3]
V_3 is primitive.
```

This is the exact disconnected-group audit that was missing from the naive connected-subgroup argument.

# 3. The identity component acts irreducibly

Primitivity implies, by Clifford theory for the normal subgroup H_h^0, that the restriction to H_h^0 is homogeneous:

```text
V_h|H_h^0 = W^(direct-sum e)
```

for an irreducible H_h^0-module W. Equivalently,

```text
V_h = W tensor E,
dim(E)=e,
```

with H_h^0 acting trivially on E.

Since nu_h belongs to H_h^0,

```text
rank(nu_h-1)
 = e * rank(nu_W-1).                        (3.1)
```

## 3.1 h=2

The left side of (3.1) is 1. Therefore e=1.

Hence H_2^0 acts irreducibly on V_2.

## 3.2 h=3

The left side of (3.1) is 2. Thus e=1 or e=2.

Suppose e=2. Then dim(W)=5.

The H_3^0-invariant symplectic form on W tensor E forces W to be self-dual. Since dim(W)=5 is odd, its nondegenerate invariant form must be symmetric, while the form on E is alternating. Thus

```text
H_3^0 subset SO(W).
```

For a unipotent element of an odd orthogonal group in characteristic zero, every even Jordan block size occurs with even multiplicity. In dimension 5 the possible nontrivial Jordan partitions include

```text
5,
3+1+1,
2+2+1,
```

and in every case

```text
rank(nu_W-1)
```

is even and at least 2.

Equation (3.1) would then give

```text
rank(nu_3-1) >= 2*2 = 4,
```

contrary to rank(nu_3-1)=2.

Therefore e=1.

Hence H_3^0 acts irreducibly on V_3.

We have reduced both cases to connected irreducible symplectic subgroup classification.

# 4. h=2: connected subgroup classification in Sp_6

Let H be a connected semisimple subgroup of Sp_6 acting irreducibly on the six-dimensional standard space.

A short highest-weight and Weyl-dimension check gives only the following possibilities:

```text
1. Sp_6 on its standard module.

2. SL_2 on Sym^5(std), dimension 6.

3. SL_2 x SO_3 on std_2 tensor std_3,
   equivalently A_1 x A_1 on Sym^1 tensor Sym^2.
```

For completeness, the other apparent six-dimensional representations do not preserve an alternating form irreducibly:

```text
SL_3: Sym^2(std) has dimension 6 but is not self-dual.
SL_4: Lambda^2(std) has dimension 6 but is orthogonal.
Sp_4: its small irreducibles have dimensions 4,5,10,...,
      with the 5-dimensional one orthogonal.
G_2 and higher exceptional types have no 6-dimensional irrep.
```

Now compare unipotent Jordan types.

The actual element nu_2 has

```text
Jordan(nu_2)=2+1+1+1+1.                    (4.1)
```

## 4.1 Principal SL_2 candidate

Every nontrivial unipotent of SL_2 acts on Sym^5(std) as one block

```text
J_6.
```

This is incompatible with (4.1).

## 4.2 Tensor product candidate

On std_2 tensor std_3, the possible nontrivial unipotent Jordan types are obtained from

```text
J_2 tensor I_3 = J_2+J_2+J_2,
I_2 tensor J_3 = J_3+J_3,
J_2 tensor J_3 = J_4+J_2.
```

None equals (4.1).

Therefore the only possibility is

```text
H_2^0=Sp_6.
```

Since H_2 is already a subgroup of Sp_6 and contains H_2^0=Sp_6,

```text
H_2=Sp_6.                                   (4.2)
```

This can also be viewed as the characteristic-zero transvection criterion: a primitive irreducible symplectic group containing a transvection has full symplectic identity component. The explicit low-rank classification above makes the application self-contained.

# 6. Exact fourth moments

For the standard representation V of Sp_r with even r>=4,

```text
V tensor V
 = Sym^2(V)
   direct-sum Lambda^2_0(V)
   direct-sum 1.
```

The three summands are irreducible and pairwise nonisomorphic. Hence

```text
M4(V)
 = dim End_Sp(V tensor V)
 = 1^2+1^2+1^2
 = 3.                                        (6.1)
```

Applying (6.1) to (4.2) and (5.2) gives the exact results

```text
M4(G_2)=3,
M4(G_3)=3.                                  (6.2)
```

Thus the Q6658 residual

```text
Sym^2(V_h) irreducible,
Lambda^2_0(V_h) irreducible
```

is now a theorem for h=2 and h=3.

In particular, the large zero-wild blocks identified in Q6658 do mix globally in these two cases. The classification proves that mixing without requiring an explicit connection matrix.

# 8. What an exact numerical certificate would have to compute

A complete fourth-moment sum at one prime is not literally M4. It contains lower-weight errors and may also record arithmetic Frobenius acting on the geometric invariant space.

The pure trace must be used:

```text
T_h(t)=S_h(t)+1.
```

For q=p^m define

```text
A_(h,p,m)
 = (1/(q-1)) sum_(t in F_q^*) |T_h(t)/sqrt(q)|^4.
```

Then

```text
A_(h,p,m)
 = Tr(Frob_p^m | I_h) + O_h(q^(-1/2)),

I_h
 = (V_h tensor V_h tensor V_h^vee tensor V_h^vee)^H_h.
```

Therefore raw convergence near 3 is evidence, not an exact certificate.

There are two rigorous finite certificates.

## 8.1 Explicit-error rounding certificate

Produce an explicit cohomological constant B_h such that

```text
|A_(h,p,m)-Tr(Frob_p^m|I_h)|
 <= B_h p^(-m/2).                            (8.1)
```

Choose m so that the right side is less than 1/2. If arithmetic Frobenius is known to act trivially on I_h, then the exact rational value A_(h,p,m) identifies dim(I_h) by nearest-integer rounding.

Without trivial arithmetic action, take enough extension degrees to recover its finite-order eigenvalues.

## 8.2 Virtual moment L-function certificate

Let

```text
E_h(q)
 = #{(x,y,z,w) in U_h(F_q)^4 :
      f_h(x)+f_h(y)=f_h(z)+f_h(w)}.
```

Let

```text
n_h(q)=#U_h(F_q),
D_m=p^m E_h(p^m)-n_h(p^m)^4.
```

Form

```text
L_mom(Z)
 = exp(sum_(m>=1) D_m Z^m/m).               (8.2)
```

The total multiplicity of reciprocal roots or poles of weight 6, namely absolute value p^3, is exactly M4(G_h). After a finite constant-field extension that kills the arithmetic action on the invariant tensors, the desired top-weight factor is

```text
(1-p^3 Z)^(-3).                             (8.3)
```

The point counts need not enumerate q^4 tuples. Compute

```text
mu_m(a)=#{x in U_h(F_(p^m)): f_h(x)=a},
nu_m=mu_m *add mu_m,
E_h(p^m)=sum_s nu_m(s)^2.                   (8.4)
```

An explicit Betti bound for the compactified energy hypersurface tells the worker how many D_m values determine the rational function (8.2) by Newton identities or Pade reconstruction.

This is the correct exact numerical fallback if a future rank does not close by subgroup classification.

Several prime-field moments near 3, without (8.1) or (8.2), are not a proof.

# 10. Consequences and limits

## 10.1 Fixed-h trace laws

Every consequence in the project that assumes only

```text
G_geom(G_h)=Sp_(4h-2)
```

is now unconditional for h=2 and h=3, subject to the stated good-prime scope. In particular the pure normalized traces are governed by the standard trace law of

```text
USp(6) for h=2,
USp(10) for h=3.
```

The affine trace S_h differs by the boundary term -1, so exact finite formulas must continue to use S_h+1.

## 10.2 Pair independence for h=2 and h=3

The two connected simple groups

```text
Sp_6 and Sp_10
```

are nonisomorphic. A connected algebraic subgroup of their product with surjective projections has no nontrivial diagonal Goursat alternative. Hence the joint geometric monodromy for the pair (h=2,h=3) is the full product, once the usual no-constant-twist bookkeeping is imposed; the ranks already prevent an isomorphism of the two sheaves.

Thus fixed-pair independence for the pair 2,3 follows from the standard product-monodromy argument.

## 10.3 What does not follow

This result does not prove the growing-H large-sieve estimate from Q6612. Full monodromy for two fixed h values does not control a family with h growing with p.

It also does not prove Sp-full for h>=4. For those ranks, T_0^3 has h-1 separate J_2 blocks, and many more connected and tensor-induced subgroup types become possible. The Q6658 tensor-square/mixing route remains relevant there.
