# Q3425 — exact audit of the transverse `C47` Green source

## Verdict

The two q=6 first-Witt endpoint formulas do **not** identify a characteristic-zero source

\[
h\quad\text{with}\quad LY^{47}=h.
\]

What they identify exactly is a one-dimensional **endpoint residual**.  After the ordinary index-shift jet is restored, that residual is

\[
C_{47}(0,-2)
\]

in the two slope endpoint coordinates.  There is no current theorem identifying those two packet endpoint coordinates with two adjacent values, or with the lower Green trace, of one characteristic-zero Apéry sequence.  Consequently the inference

\[
h=\delta_{r+1}
\]

is not justified.

The genuine characteristic-zero first jet visible in the repository is the shifted-index jet `G`.  It satisfies a distributed inhomogeneous Apéry equation and accounts for the seam variable `D`; it does **not** account for `C47`.  Once that jet is separated, the endpoint algebra gives precisely the already-known p-divided numerator identity

\[
\boxed{
\frac{10T_7-T_4-5b_r}{p}
 \equiv 2C_{47}\pmod p,
}
\]

or equivalently

\[
\boxed{
10T_7-T_4-5b_r\equiv 2pC_{47}\pmod {p^2}.
}
\]

Thus there is no unconditional `Delta` attached to the present data, and hence no unconditional theorem that the actual `Delta` is a q=6 core unit.

There are, however, two sharp conditional unit statements.

1. If an additional localization theorem proves `h = delta_(r+1)` with the normalization used in the question, then

   \[
   \Delta=-\frac1{(r+1)^3}
   \]

   and this is a q=6 core unit.

2. If an additional theorem identifies the endpoint residual `(0,-2)` with the lower Green trace of the transverse source, then it forces

   \[
   \operatorname{Green}_b(h)=2,
   \qquad
   \operatorname{Green}_c(h)=0,
   \]

   so

   \[
   \Delta=2c_r,
   \]

   again a q=6 core unit.  This determines the Green-equivalence class of `h`, but not its support or its exact source sequence.

The present endpoint formulas prove neither additional theorem.  The exact theorem-level conclusion is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{`C47` is localized in endpoint space, not yet in Green-source space.}
}
\]

## 1. The actual characteristic-zero first jet

Put

\[
P(n)=34n^3+51n^2+27n+5
\]

and

\[
(Ly)_n=(n+1)^3y_{n+1}-P(n)y_n+n^3y_{n-1}.
\]

The characteristic-zero shifted family used by the cubic block audit is the formal solution `B_n(X)` of

\[
(n+1+X)^3B_{n+1}(X)
-P(n+X)B_n(X)
+(n+X)^3B_{n-1}(X)=0,
\tag{1.1}
\]

with the regular boundary normalization

\[
B_0(X)=1,
\qquad
(1+X)^3B_1(X)=P(X).
\tag{1.2}
\]

Write

\[
B_n(X)=b_n+XG_n+O(X^2).
\]

Then `G_0=0`, and differentiating (1.2) at `X=0` gives

\[
3b_1+G_1=P'(0)b_0+P(0)G_0.
\]

Since `b_0=1`, `b_1=5`, `P'(0)=27`, and `P(0)=5`,

\[
\boxed{G_1-5G_0=12,}
\qquad
\boxed{G_0=0,\quad G_1=12.}
\tag{1.3}
\]

This is the genuine characteristic-zero first-jet boundary equation.

Differentiating (1.1) at `X=0` gives, for every `n`,

\[
\boxed{
(LG)_n
 =P'(n)b_n
  -3(n+1)^2b_{n+1}
  -3n^2b_{n-1}.
}
\tag{1.4}
\]

Thus the exact first-jet source already present in the repository is

\[
\boxed{
h^{\mathrm{sh}}_n
 :=P'(n)b_n
   -3(n+1)^2b_{n+1}
   -3n^2b_{n-1}.}
\tag{1.5}
\]

It is a distributed source.  In particular, it is not a point source at `r+1`.  This is the source attached to the shifted-index/seam coordinate, hence to `D`, not to `C47`.

## 2. Where `C47` sits after the exact seam normalization

Set

\[
C_{47}:=J_4-5J_7
\tag{2.1}
\]

and absorb the slope-7 correction into the shifted jet scalar:

\[
\widetilde G_r:=G_r+J_7
              =2\epsilon\left(D+\frac{J_7}{2\epsilon}\right).
\tag{2.2}
\]

Then (1.6) becomes

\[
q_7\equiv\eta-\widetilde G_r\pmod p.
\tag{2.3}
\]

Since `J_4=C47+5J_7`, equation (1.7) becomes

\[
q_4
\equiv
5\eta-10\widetilde G_r-2C_{47}
\pmod p.
\tag{2.4}
\]

Thus the complete endpoint normal form is

\[
\boxed{
\binom{q_7}{q_4}
\equiv
\eta\binom{1}{5}
-\widetilde G_r\binom{1}{10}
+C_{47}\binom{0}{-2}
\pmod p.

\tag{2.5}

Equation (2.5) identifies the transverse direction exactly **as an endpoint vector**:

\[
\boxed{e_{47}^{\mathrm{end}}=(0,-2).}
\tag{2.6}

It does not identify a source sequence `h`.

The vectors in (2.5) have a transparent origin:

\[
(1,10)=((0+1)b_0,(1+1)b_1).

## 4. Why the endpoint residual does not determine `h`

To speak of a Green source one needs a characteristic-zero sequence `Y47` and an identity

\[
LY^{47}=h.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The endpoint formulas provide neither object.  They provide only the image

\[
E(Y^{47})=(0,-2)
\tag{4.2}
\]

under a two-coordinate packet endpoint map `E`, assuming a lift `Y47` exists at all.

The missing information is not cosmetic:

* `T_7` and `T_4` are truncated packet endpoint values from two different slope constructions;
* `J_7` and `J_4` are first-divided endpoint corrections;
* no current recurrence states that the sequence of `J`-corrections is acted on by the original Apéry operator `L`;
* no current declaration identifies `(T_7/p,T_4/p)` with `(Y_0,Y_1)` for one original-index sequence;
* no current declaration identifies that pair with the two lower Green traces of one finite source.

Therefore applying `L` to the two-entry vector in (2.6) is a type error unless an additional lift theorem is supplied.

### 4.1 The naive adjacent-boundary extension gives the wrong support

There is a useful diagnostic.  Suppose, as an extra convention, one declares `(0,-2)` to be actual adjacent initial data at the singular origin and extends it homogeneously for `n>=1`.

The unique extension is

\[
Y^{47}=-2c,
\]

because `(c_0,c_1)=(0,1)`.  For `n>=1`, `Lc=0`, but at the singular boundary

\[
(Lc)_0=c_1-5c_0=1.
\]

Hence this convention gives

\[
\boxed{LY^{47}=-2\delta_0.}
\tag{4.3}
\]

It does **not** give `delta_(r+1)`.

This is not asserted to be the project’s actual transverse lift; it is a proof that the endpoint vector alone does not force the proposed successor source.  One perfectly natural extension places the source at the singular boundary instead.

### 4.2 A lower-Green-trace interpretation determines moments, not support

There is another possible extra identification.  Let `w` be a finite interior source and let `Q` be its zero-top Green reduction.  With

\[
\mathcal G_b(w):=\sum_n b_nw_n,
\qquad
\mathcal G_c(w):=\sum_n c_nw_n,
\]

the standard Green formula gives the lower trace

\[
Q_0=\mathcal G_c(w),
\qquad
Q_1=5\mathcal G_c(w)-\mathcal G_b(w).
\tag{4.4}
\]

Consequently, **if** a future theorem identifies the transverse endpoint vector `(0,-2)` with `(Q_0,Q_1)` for the source `h`, then

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal G_c(h)=0,
\qquad
\mathcal G_b(h)=2.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

This fixes the class of `h` modulo

\[
\ker\mathcal G_b\cap\ker\mathcal G_c,
\]

but not `h` itself.  Any source `k` with both Green moments zero may be added without changing (4.5).

For example, a two-point representative of the class (4.5), localized at `r,r+1`, is

\[
\boxed{
h_{\mathrm{tr}}
=2(r+1)^3
 \bigl(c_{r+1}\delta_r-c_r\delta_{r+1}\bigr).}
\tag{4.6}
\]

Indeed the Casoratian gives

\[
\mathcal G_b(h_{\mathrm{tr}})=2,
\qquad
\mathcal G_c(h_{\mathrm{tr}})=0.
\]

But (4.6) is merely a representative of a Green-equivalence class.  It is not derived as the actual `C47` source by the q=6 endpoint formulas.

The proposed source `delta_(r+1)` has moments

\[
\bigl(b_{r+1},c_{r+1}\bigr),
\]

which are not the moment pair `(2,0)` in general.  Thus even under the lower-trace interpretation, `h=delta_(r+1)` does not follow without an additional normalization/localization identity.

## 5. What can actually be proved about `Delta`

Recall

\[
\Delta(h)
 :=\sum_n(c_rb_n-b_rc_n)h_n
 =c_r\mathcal G_b(h)-b_r\mathcal G_c(h).
\tag{5.1}
\]

Since the current endpoint formulas do not define `h`, they do not define an unconditional `Delta(h)`.  There is therefore no unconditional core-unit theorem at this stage.

The relevant conditional statements are exact.

### 5.1 Conditional successor-source theorem

If a separate theorem proves

\[
h=\delta_{r+1},
\]

then

\[
\Delta
=c_rb_{r+1}-b_rc_{r+1}.
\]

The companion Casoratian at `a=r+1` is

\[
b_rc_{r+1}-c_rb_{r+1}=\frac1{(r+1)^3}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Delta=-\frac1{(r+1)^3}.}
\tag{5.2}
\]

This is a q=6 core unit.  Indeed a legal target has

\[
0<r<p,
\qquad
p\mid b_r.
\]

The endpoint supercongruence in the target block audit gives

\[
b_{p-1}\equiv b_0=1\pmod {p^3}.
\]

Hence `r` cannot equal `p-1`; therefore

\[
1\le r\le p-2,
\qquad
p\nmid r+1.
\]

Thus

\[
v_p(\Delta)=0.
\]

This proves the unit claim **conditional on the missing localization theorem**.

There is also a normalization warning.  The raw endpoint residual is `-2C47`.  If a future source theorem absorbs that factor into the source and proves

\[
h=-2\delta_{r+1},
\]

then

\[
\Delta=\frac{2}{(r+1)^3},
\]

which is again a core unit for `p>5`.  The endpoint formulas do not decide which normalization is intended.

### 5.2 Conditional lower-trace theorem

Under the additional lower-Green-trace identification (4.5),

\[
\Delta
=c_r\cdot2-b_r\cdot0,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\Delta=2c_r.}
\tag{5.3}
\]

This too is a q=6 core unit.  At a target, the Casoratian at `a=r` gives

\[
b_{r-1}c_r-c_{r-1}b_r=\frac1{r^3}.
\]

Modulo `p`, using `p|b_r`,

\[
b_{r-1}c_r\equiv r^{-3}\not\equiv0\pmod p.
\]

Thus both `b_(r-1)` and `c_r` are p-units.  Since every q=6 core prime is greater than `5`, `2c_r` is a core unit.

Likewise the naive adjacent-boundary convention (4.3) would give

\[
\Delta(-2\delta_0)=-2c_r,
\]

also a core unit.  The values `-2c_r`, `2c_r`, and `-1/(r+1)^3` are different because the corresponding source identifications are different.  This is exactly why the source map must be proved rather than guessed.

## 6. The smallest missing theorem

To turn `C47` into an actual Green source, one needs a characteristic-zero lift

\[
Y^{47}=(Y^{47}_n)_n
\]

with all of the following properties.

1. **Endpoint realization.**  Under the actual packet-to-Green boundary map,

   \[
   E(Y^{47})=(0,-2)
   \]

   with the same normalization as (2.5).

2. **Operator equation.**  There is an exact source

   \[
   LY^{47}=h.
   \]

3. **Normalization.**  The factor `-2` is assigned explicitly either to the endpoint coordinate, to `Y47`, or to `h`; it cannot be silently discarded.

4. **Core-unit conclusion.**  Either prove the support localization

   \[
   h=\delta_{r+1}
   \quad\text{(or a fixed core-unit multiple),}
   \]

   or prove only the two Green moments needed by the cancellation:

   \[
   \mathcal G_b(h)=2u,
   \qquad
   \mathcal G_c(h)=0,
   \]

   for a known q=6 core unit `u`.

The moment theorem is strictly weaker than exact support identification and is already sufficient:

\[
\Delta=2u\,c_r
\]

is then a core unit by the target Casoratian.

No checked current identity constructs `Y47` or proves either the support theorem or the moment theorem.  The shifted-index family constructs `G` and the distributed source (1.5), but that is the `D` direction.  The `C47` direction remains an endpoint/Bockstein class.

## 7. Final theorem-level answer

Starting only from

\[
T_7/p=\eta-2\epsilon D-J_7,
\qquad
T_4/p=5\eta-20\epsilon D-2J_4,
\qquad
C_{47}=J_4-5J_7,
\]

the exact derivation is:

\[
\boxed{
\binom{T_7/p}{T_4/p}
=
\eta\binom15
-\widetilde G_r\binom1{10}
+C_{47}\binom0{-2}
\quad(\bmod p),
}
\]

followed by

\[
\boxed{
\frac{10T_7-T_4-5b_r}{p}
\equiv2C_{47}\pmod p.
}
\]

The characteristic-zero first-jet source that can actually be derived is

\[
\boxed{
(LG)_n
=P'(n)b_n-3(n+1)^2b_{n+1}-3n^2b_{n-1},
}
\]

and it is the seam/`D` source.  No corresponding characteristic-zero `Y47` is supplied for the transverse endpoint correction.

Therefore:

```text
actual h from the present derivation:        not identified
h = delta_(r+1):                             not proved
actual Delta:                                not yet defined
unconditional q=6 core-unit theorem:         not proved
conditional Delta for h = delta_(r+1):       -1/(r+1)^3, a core unit
conditional Delta from Green trace (2,0):    2*c_r, a core unit
exact unconditional endpoint equivalence:   (10*T7-T4-5*b_r)/p = 2*C47 mod p
```

So the proposed successor-Casoratian route is promising only after a new packet-to-Green first-jet lift theorem.  Without that theorem, it is exactly the old p-divided numerator written in Green-source language, not a localization of the transverse source.
