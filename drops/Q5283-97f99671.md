\tag{8.2}

\[
p=2h+mq.
\tag{8.3}
\]

D\bigl(S^2\mathcal V_N\bigr)^2N^{o(1)}\\
&\ll
N^{41/100}N^{-2+o(1)}S^4\\
&=
\boxed{N^{-159/100+o(1)}S^4.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{8.7}
\]

So Q5275's exponent is correct.

Two points are load-bearing:

1. the argument is a **modular residue-class fibre** argument, not an integer-zero-slope identity;
2. the half-residue must be kept exactly until (8.2) is derived.

If one were to replace (8.2) by an unsupported equality such as \(2h=p\), that would be the first invalid implication.  Q5275 does not need that step; its \(p=2h+mq\) parametrization is the correct one.

Finally, this zero sector is negligible compared with (E).  At \(S\le N^{1/2}\),

\ll
N^{6/5}S
\]
and
\[
N^{-159/100}S^4

by fixed powers of \(N\).

### Stronger normalization

The exact full-phase identities (4.4), (4.7) show that the raw-zero sector is not intrinsic.  If the half-residue is cancelled against CD8's parity/slow phase before the new tensor separation, the fast slope is \(J_\pm=\pm h\bar p\ne0\), so Section 8 can be omitted altogether.

Thus (8.7) is a valid auxiliary estimate, but not a necessary hypothesis or obstruction.

# 11. Is the \(S\le\sqrt N\) theorem really unconditional?

Yes, but there is a distinction worth making explicit.

The **printed** CD7 proposition assumes
\[
2G\le c_v\sqrt N.
\]
Therefore one cannot justify the full statement for \(S\le\sqrt N\) merely by writing “apply CD7 with \(G=S\).”

The endpoint proof above does not do that.  It reuses the CD8--CD10 mechanism and invokes the stable canonical actual-gap theorem directly.  For \(s,s'\asymp S\le\sqrt N\),
\[
|s'-s|\ll\sqrt N<q
\]
for every \(q\asymp_vN\) and large \(N\).  Hence the same-prime root bound is unconditional.  All later fibre and large-sieve steps are uniform in this larger constant range.

Thus the strongest safe formulation is genuinely
\[
\boxed{S\le\sqrt N,}
\]
not merely \(S\le c_v\sqrt N\), provided the proof includes the one-line appeal to the full nonwrapping gap-polynomial range rather than citing CD7 as a black box.

# 12. Insertion-ready LaTeX

The following is the version I would insert.  It uses the exact full-phase normalization and therefore avoids manufacturing a raw zero slope.  A remark after the proof records Q5275's optional \(N^{-159/100}S^4\) check.

```tex
\begin{proposition}[Completed-source endpoint-slack band]
\label{prop:ad-completed-endpoint-slack-band}
Let
\[
 D=N^{41/100},\qquad
 c_p=\frac{p-1}{2},\qquad c_q=\frac{q-1}{2},
\]
and let $I_S=(S,2S]\cap\mathbb Z$ with $1\le S\le\sqrt N$.
For the positive endpoint row put
\[
 g=c_p+c_q-s,\qquad s\in I_S,
\]
and for the negative endpoint row put
\[
 g=-c_p-c_q+s,\qquad s\in I_S,
\]
in the literal completed source expression
\eqref{eq:ad-completed-additive-slope-band}, retaining the original
condition $p\ne q$, the weights $u_pu_q$, the smooth factor
$\widehat w(Nd/(pq))$, all prime-color restrictions, and every bounded
$d$-independent source deletion.
Then, for either sign,
\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{0<|d|\asymp D}
   |A_{S,d}^{\rm end,\pm}|^2
 \ll_{v,w}
 N^{6/5+o(1)}S+N^{3/5+o(1)}S^3.}
 \tag{END}
\]
The estimate is uniform for an $N^{o(1)}$-summable separated family of
smooth source atoms.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $h=s+1$.  On the positive branch set $i=c_p+k$.  The two
literal physical zero indices are
\[
 i,\qquad q-1+i-s.
\]
Nonzero support forces $0\le i\le s$, and the Ap\'ery palindrome gives
\[
 \mathbf1_{\mathcal Z_q}(q-1+i-s)
 =\mathbf1_{\mathcal Z_q}(s-i).
\]
On the negative branch set $i=c_p-k$.  The two physical indices are
\[
 p-1-i,\qquad s-i,
\]
and palindrome in characteristic $p$ gives the same transformed
actual-zero pair $(i,s-i)$.  Thus, after pulling back every bounded
source mask, one obtains
\[
 C_{p,q}^{\pm}(s)
 =\sum_i m_{p,q,s}^{\pm}(i)
   \mathbf1_{\mathcal Z_p}(i)
   \mathbf1_{\mathcal Z_q}(s-i),
 \qquad |m_{p,q,s}^{\pm}(i)|\le1,
\]
with the multiplier independent of $d$.

The exact centered CRT phase \eqref{eq:ad-source-band-phase} becomes
\[
 (-1)^d e\!\left(\frac{d(k-1/2)}{pq}\right)e_q(dg\bar p)
 =e\!\left(\frac{di}{pq}\right)e_q(-dh\bar p)
\]
on the positive branch, and
\[
 (-1)^d e\!\left(\frac{d(k-1/2)}{pq}\right)e_q(dg\bar p)
 =e\!\left(-\frac{d(i+1)}{pq}\right)e_q(dh\bar p)
\]
on the negative branch.  Tensor-separate the product of the displayed
slow phase and $\widehat w(Nd/(pq))$ exactly as in the proof of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ad-completed-additive-slope-band}; its coefficient
$\ell^1$ norm is $N^{o(1)}$ and the rapidly decaying remainder is
negligible by the same crude source-row mass bound.

For one separated atom, the Gram overlap of the translated $q$-vectors
at two slacks $s,s'$ is at most
\[
 \#\{j,j'\in\mathcal Z_q:j'-j=s'-s\}.
\]
Since $S\le\sqrt N$ and $q\asymp_vN$, every nonzero occurring gap is
strictly less than $q$ for large $N$.  The actual gap polynomial gives
an $O(|s'-s|)$ off-diagonal bound.  Schur and Bessel therefore yield
\[
 \sum_{s\in I_S}|C_{p,q}^{\pm}(s)|^2
 \ll Z(p)(Z(q)+S^2).
\]

The additive slopes are
\[
 J_+=-h\bar p\pmod q,
 \qquad
 J_-=h\bar p\pmod q.
\]
They are nonzero because $1\le h<q$.  For fixed $q,J,h$, the prime $p$
lies in one residue class modulo $q$, which has $O_v(1)$ representatives
in the fixed-ratio shell.  Hence an equal-slope fibre has $O_v(S)$
pairs $(p,s)$.  Cauchy gives
\[
 \sum_{q,J}|\beta_{q,J}^{\pm}|^2
 \ll N^{o(1)}S\mathcal E_N
      (\mathcal E_N+S^2\mathcal V_N).
\]
The fractions $J/q$ are reduced and distinct fractions are

\[
 \mathcal E_N\ll N^{-2/5+o(1)},

 \mathcal V_N\ll N^{-1+o(1)}
\]
gives
\[
 \sum_{0<|d|\asymp D}|A_{S,d}^{\rm end,\pm}|^2
 \ll
 N^{6/5+o(1)}S+N^{3/5+o(1)}S^3,
\]
as required.
\end{proof}
```

Q5275's actual endpoint formulas avoid (1), use the correct shell family rather than (3), and its source-level setup allows arbitrary masks.  The only point worth making more explicit in the canonical insertion is (2), which is why the proof above normalizes the whole phase before applying CD9--CD10.
