Let `p>=7`.  Let `U_0,...,U_(p-1)` be the values of any nonzero solution of the Apéry recurrence modulo `p`, and let

```text
U_p(X) in F_p[X],
deg U_p<=p-1,
U_p(r)=U_r for 0<=r<p.                               (8.1)
```

Then

```text
deg U_p >= p-5.                                      (8.2)
```

### Proof

Define the polynomial recurrence defect

```text
L U(X)
  =(X+1)^3U(X+1)-P0(X)U(X)+X^3U(X-1).                (8.3)
```

For every `1<=r<=p-2`, the recurrence gives

```text
(L U_p)(r)=0.                                        (8.4)
```

Let `m=deg U_p` and let `a!=0` be its leading coefficient.  The coefficient of `X^(m+3)` in (8.3) is

```text
a-34a+a=-32a.                                       (8.5)
```

Since `p>=7`, this coefficient is nonzero.  Hence

```text
deg(L U_p)=m+3.                                      (8.6)
```

The nonzero polynomial `L U_p` has the `p-2` distinct roots in (8.4), so

```text
m+3>=p-2,
```

which proves (8.2).  QED.

Applying this to `b` and `c` gives

```text
deg B_p, deg C_p >= p-5.                             (8.7)
```

At the critical shell, the direct state selector therefore has complexity

```text
H^(5/2+o(1)).                                        (8.8)
```

There is an essential caveat.  In the fixed-word algebra one may reduce

```text

```

to degree `O(H)`.  The theorem does not rule out a future quotient-specific compression.  What is missing is a prime-independent integral description of the remainders (8.9) with a useful aggregate height.  Their coefficients currently retain the full-prefix dependence which the local short-word algebra had eliminated.

This is the sole possible escape left inside the exact-state mechanism.
