# Q936 hostile repair: status of the codimension-one and codimension-two regimes

## Verdict

The requested repair of Q932 cannot be completed as a theorem from the information currently available. The previous Q932 conclusions about support failure and subexponential height are invalid: the support argument was based on an undefined matrix and incorrectly treated an unproved height estimate as sufficient. The interpolation identity with integral denominator data does preserve the implication

\[

What can be certified from the stated exact scans is only a quadratic-height obstruction. A rigorous closed formula for the primitive-kernel normalization was not derived here, so no claim of \(\Omega(H^2)\) after gcd division is asserted.

## What would be required for a proof

For d=H-1 or d=H-2 one needs an exact description of the primitive kernel. In particular:

1. Define the fraction-free Newton matrix produced by `newton_coefficients` and `multiply`.
2. Compute the determinant/cofactor formula for the unique kernel vector.
3. Express the primitive normalization factor from `primitive_kernel` as an explicit gcd of minors.
4. Prove that the remaining primitive cofactor has determinant growth at least

\[
\exp(cH^2)
\]

for some c>0.

A bound of \(O(H\log H)\) is not relevant, because the target is \(o(n)=o(H)\), not merely subquadratic growth.
