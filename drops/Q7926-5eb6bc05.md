# Correction of the K3 point-count and primitive trace formula

## 1. Local replacement of a split A1 point

Let $Y/\mathbf F_p$ be a proper surface with one ordinary double point $y\in Y(\mathbf F_p)$, assumed split, and let
\[
\pi:\widetilde Y\rightarrow Y
\]
be the minimal resolution. The exceptional divisor is
\[
E\simeq \mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_p}.
\]

The point-count correction is purely geometric:
\[
\#\widetilde Y(\mathbf F_p)=\#Y(\mathbf F_p)-1+\#\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p)
=\#Y(\mathbf F_p)+p.
\]

The error in Q7924 was treating the resolution as if a new surface cohomology term of size $p^4$ appeared. For a surface the only Frobenius traces in the Lefschetz formula are in degrees $0,2,4$.

## 2. Grothendieck--Lefschetz formula

For arithmetic Frobenius $\operatorname{Fr}_p$ acting on etale cohomology,
\[
\#X(\mathbf F_p)=\sum_{i=0}^{4}(-1)^i\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^i_{\mathrm c}(X_{\overline{\mathbf F}_p},\mathbf Q_\ell)).
\]

For a smooth proper surface,
\[
\#\widetilde Y(\mathbf F_p)
=1+\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^2(\widetilde Y))-p^2.
\]

Here the convention is that arithmetic Frobenius has eigenvalues on $H^2$ of complex absolute value $p$. If geometric Frobenius is used instead, the trace is the inverse-root convention and the displayed formula is unchanged after replacing the operator by geometric Frobenius.

Thus the correct dimensionally consistent identity is
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^2(\widetilde Y))=
\#\widetilde Y(\mathbf F_p)-1-p^2.}
\]

Combining with the exceptional divisor correction gives
\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^2(\widetilde Y))=
\#Y(\mathbf F_p)+p-1-p^2.}
\]

There is no $p^4$ term: $p^4$ would have the wrong cohomological weight for a surface.

## 3. One-node invariant-cycle sequence

For a one-parameter degeneration with one ordinary double point, the SGA7 invariant-cycle sequence gives the additional vanishing-cycle contribution. Under the stated hypotheses (proper semistable family, one isolated split node, and no other singularities in the relevant open fiber), the invariant part satisfies
\[
0\rightarrow H^2(Y_{\overline{\mathbf F}_p})\rightarrow
H^2(\widetilde Y_{\overline{\mathbf F}_p})
\rightarrow \mathbf Q_\ell(-1)[E]
\rightarrow 0
\]
for the algebraic exceptional contribution.

The exceptional class has Frobenius eigenvalue $p$ in the arithmetic convention because
\[
H^2(E)(1)\cong \mathbf Q_\ell(-1).
\]

## 4. Rank-19 divisor lattice and primitive rank-2 trace

Assume the relative divisor closures
\[
D_1,\ldots,D_{19}
\]
are Cartier on a smooth projective model over the DVR, flat over the base, and remain independent after specialization. Let
\[
NS_{\mathrm{tor}}=\langle[D_1],\ldots,[D_{19}]\rangle.
\]

If the intersection matrix has rank $19$, then its orthogonal complement in $H^2$ has rank $2$:
\[
H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}:=NS_{\mathrm{tor}}^\perp,
\qquad \dim H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}=2.
\]

The equality of primitive traces is therefore
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(\widetilde Y))
=
\operatorname{Tr}(\operatorname{Fr}_p|H^2_{\mathrm{prim}}(Y))
}
\]
provided the exceptional class and all boundary divisor classes are removed before taking the orthogonal complement.

The Hessian character from the moving ordinary double point is not an extra rank-2 trace term. It occurs in the vanishing-cycle/local monodromy factor. In a quadratic branch parameter it records the discriminant character of the node; it controls the split/non-split local Picard-Lefschetz action.

## 5. TeX-ready corrected statement

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Split-node point count correction]
Let $Y/\mathbf F_p$ be proper with one split $A_1$ singularity and let
$\widetilde Y\to Y$ be the minimal resolution. If the exceptional divisor
is $E\simeq\mathbf P^1_{\mathbf F_p}$, then
\[
\#\widetilde Y(\mathbf F_p)=\#Y(\mathbf F_p)+p,
\]
and for arithmetic Frobenius
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\Fr_p|H^2(\widetilde Y))
=\#\widetilde Y(\mathbf F_p)-1-p^2.
\]
If the nineteen divisor classes are Cartier, flat in the family, and have
constant rank-$19$ intersection matrix, then their orthogonal complement is
rank $2$ and its Frobenius trace is unchanged by removing the exceptional
algebraic class. The node discriminant character appears only in the
vanishing-cycle/local monodromy factor.
\end{lemma}
```

## 6. References

The formal ingredients are the Grothendieck--Lefschetz trace formula and the invariant-cycle theorem of SGA7:

- P. Deligne, *La conjecture de Weil II*, Publ. Math. IHES 52 (1980), for Frobenius weights and trace conventions.
- A. Grothendieck, M. Raynaud et al., *SGA 7 I: Groupes de monodromie en géométrie algébrique*, especially Exposé IX on invariant cycles and vanishing cycles.

The family-specific claim that the nineteen toric/boundary divisor closures satisfy the Cartier, flatness, and rank hypotheses is separate: it must be checked in the boundary charts of the chosen compactification and does not follow from the displayed rational function alone.
