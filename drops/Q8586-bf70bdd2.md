## 1. Exact formulation

\[

\]

\[
G_X(m)=\#\{p: p\mid B_{q_p},\ p\mid B_{r_p},\ r_p\notin\{q_p,p-1-q_p\}\}.

The desired statement is
\[
\sum_{m<X^2}G_X(m)^3\le X^{2+o(1)}\lambda_X^3.

\[
\sum_{p_1,p_2,p_3}\#\{m: p_i\mid B_{q_{p_i}},\ p_i\mid B_{r_{p_i}},\ r_{p_i}\not\equiv q_{p_i},p_i-1-q_{p_i}\}.

Removing them leaves a folded pair whose two indices are not identified by the Apéry reflection involution. This removes the obvious rank-one Wronskian degeneracy, but it does not imply independence of different primes.
