# 11. Final theorem statement

The exact result established by the current identities may be packaged as follows.

> **Forty-step selected-resultant theorem.**  Let \((q,v,d,p)\) be an actual plus reverse-shift occurrence with \(p=q-d\), \(q\mid b_v\), odd height \(h=2r+1\), and \(p\mid J_h(6q)\). For sufficiently large shell primes, define \(D_h,\mathcal R_{40,h},\mathcal P_{40},\alpha_h,eta_h\) as in Sections 2--4. Then:
>
> 1. \(q\mid\mathcal S_{40}\), where
>    
>    \[
>    \mathcal S_{40}
>    =(\alpha_hD_h-\beta_h)J_h(6q)
>     +\alpha_h\mathcal R_{40,h}(6q).
>    \]
>
> 2. With \(p\)-safe Green clearings,
>    
>    \[
>    p\mid\mathcal S_{40}
>    \iff
>    p\mid G(r,\nu)
>    \ \text{or}\ 
>    p\mid J_{h+40}(6q).

> 3. The nonzero integer

>    =\bigl(J_{h+40}(6q)-\mathcal R_{40,h}(6q)\bigr)
>      \mathcal P_{40}
>    \]

>    
>    Hence it is not strong enough to prove
>    
>    \[
>    o_J\!\left(\lambda T^2/\log T\right).
>    \]

The positive output is therefore the exact resultant, the exact alignment dichotomy, and the nonzero joint integer.
