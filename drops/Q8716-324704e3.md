## Claim

The valuation statement cannot be reduced to the naive relative-Lucas lifting law
\[
 B_{pm+r}\equiv B_mB_r\pmod {p^{1+v_p(B_m)}}.
\]
The known failure at p=19,m=827 shows that the induction variable must include the full carry profile of the
Newton weights. The compensation mechanism is genuine: the weight valuations and Apéry valuations exchange
one unit of p-adic depth across strata.

For a prime p define
\[
\alpha_p(n,K)=\min_{0\le i\le K}\bigl(v_p(B_i)+v_p(w_i(n,K))\bigr).
\]
The desired theorem is equivalent to
\[
 v_p(B_n)\ge \alpha_p(n,K)
\]
for every prime p.

## Reduced state condition

Using Kummer's theorem,
\[
v_p\binom ab=\#\{\text{carries when adding }b,a-b\text{ in base }p\},
\]
the weight contribution is explicitly
\[
v_p(w_i(n,K))=c_p(n;i)+c_p(n-i-1;K-i),
\]
where c_p denotes the carry count. Hence the remaining problem is a finite-state valuation problem:

> For every p,n,K and every carry stratum S=(s_1,s_2), prove
> \[
> v_p(B_n)\ge \min_i\{v_p(B_i)+s_1(i)+s_2(i)\}.
> \]

The missing ingredient is therefore not the binomial side; it is a Dwork/Newton cross-congruence controlling
Apéry valuations on all base-p carry strata.

## p=19,n=21404,K=5351 mechanism

Here n=4K. The dangerous indices split into three strata:

* \((v_p(w_i),v_p(B_i))=(0,3)\),
* \((1,2)\),
* \((2,1)\).

No single stratum gives the conclusion by itself. The Newton divisor obtains depth three by taking the minimum over the combined quantity
\(v_p(w_i)+v_p(B_i)\). Thus the obstruction to a naive Lucas proof is exactly that a lost unit in the weight can be replaced by an extra unit in the Apéry factor.

## Attempted routes

1. Bezout identity route:

An identity of the form
\[
B_n=\sum_{i\le K} c_i(n,K)B_iw_i(n,K),\qquad c_i\in\mathbb Z
\]
would immediately prove the claim. No integral coefficient construction was found. Rational Newton interpolation identities exist, but denominator cancellation has not been established.

2. Dwork induction route:

The required induction must refine the state from \(m\mapsto(pm+r)\) to
\[
(m,r,\text{carry vector},v_p(B_m)).
\]
The supplied p=19 example rules out collapsing this state to only \(m,r\) and one valuation parameter.

3. Counterexample search:

No exact falsification was derived. The current evidence instead indicates that if the theorem fails, the first failure
must occur in a higher carry-depth state where the compensation minimum is insufficient.
