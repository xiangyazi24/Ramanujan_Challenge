Conclusion:

The data does not support the hypothesis that ord_p(a_p) is usually close to p-1. There are some maximally spread examples:

- p=7: order 6=p-1.
- p=31: order 30=p-1.

But the majority have order equal to a proper divisor, especially (p-1)/2. This is consistent with the CM/newform picture: the Frobenius traces a_p are not random elements of F_p^*, and their multiplicative orders are constrained by the arithmetic of the modular representation.

For the Apéry orbit question, the relevant conclusion is therefore weaker: large order occurs infinitely often and is evidence of spreading, but one should not expect the full-period monodromy to be a primitive element for most primes. The correct invariant is likely the projective Frobenius/Cartier action rather than ord_p(a_p) alone.
