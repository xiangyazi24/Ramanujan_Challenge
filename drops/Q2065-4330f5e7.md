```text

1+z,
```


The three logarithmic critical equations are

```text
x/(1+x) + t - 1 = 0,

y/(1+y) + (y*(1+z)+x*y*z)/E - 1 = 0,

z/(1+z) + (z*(1+y)+x*y*z)/E - 1 = 0.
```

The first gives

```text

```

Using


```text

```






Conversely these relations satisfy all three critical equations and keep `F` invertible.  This proves the isomorphism.

# 7. Saturation theorem for bounded interior GD / creative-telescoping expressions

The two-dimensional quotient also gives a clean uniform identity theorem.

Define `C_int` to consist of expressions obtained after:

```text
1. a bounded Laurent numerator is reduced modulo J_int;
2. a bounded number of log-derivative / Jacobian reductions is made;
3. powers of Lambda are shifted by bounded integer amounts;
4. coefficients are polynomial or rational functions of the certificate
   indices, with only structural denominators that are nonzero on the
   rank-interior sector;
5. finitely many such terms are added, with source tags retained in the
   coefficient ring.
```

Every reduced Laurent numerator is uniquely `a+b*y`.  Since

```text

```

each term is a linear combination of two adjacent powers of `[Lambda]`.
Under the real critical embedding `[Lambda]=alpha`, every expression in `C_int` is therefore a finite exponential-polynomial

```text
E(r)
 = sum_(nu=1)^M P_nu(r) * alpha^(L_nu(r)),
```

where `M` is bounded, `P_nu` is polynomial/rational in the indices, and `L_nu` is an integral affine form.

## Theorem 7.1

Suppose such an `E(r)` vanishes identically for all integer certificate indices in a full-dimensional cone of the B8 lattice

```text
sum_i c_i = 0,
sum_i c_i*r_i = 0.
```

Then, after restricting to that lattice, the terms split into groups for which their exponent forms `L_nu` are already equal up to constants.  In each group the corresponding polynomial coefficient sum vanishes identically.  In particular there is no cancellation between genuinely distinct exponent modes.

### Proof

After fixing `c`, only the second displayed relation constrains the variable indices; the first is a relation among the fixed colors.  Let `H` be the resulting integer affine lattice.  Group pairs for which `L_nu-L_mu` is constant on `H`.  If two remaining exponent forms are distinct on `H`, choose an integer direction `v` in the tangent lattice of `H` avoiding the finitely many hyperplanes

```text
(L_nu-L_mu)(v)=0.
```

Along an arithmetic line

```text
r(t)=r_0+t*v
```

inside the cone, the identity becomes

```text
sum_nu Q_nu(t) * alpha^(a_nu*t)=0
```

with distinct integers `a_nu` and polynomial/rational `Q_nu`.  Clear the fixed denominators.  Since `alpha>1`, the term with largest `a_nu` cannot be cancelled asymptotically by the others unless its polynomial coefficient is identically zero.  Remove it and iterate.  This proves the assertion.

Source tags are inert coefficients throughout, so the proof is coefficientwise in every tag.

### Consequence

Within `C_int`, the B8 balance can create only exponent-coincidence identities already forced by the linear index relation.  The literal example is the total-exponent tautology from Q2061.  It cannot create a new connected annihilator of the surviving `eta` mode.

This theorem includes the associated-graded effect of bounded logarithmic derivatives and bounded-order creative telescoping after interior Griffiths-Dwork reduction.  It does **not** include boundary logarithmic residues or a joint quotient of several different embeddings.

Rank-deficient monomial maps are also outside the theorem because the pulled-back critical locus need not remain zero-dimensional.
