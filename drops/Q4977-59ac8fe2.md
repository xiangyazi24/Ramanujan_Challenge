\(B_x^\circ(\ell_1)\) and \(B_x^\circ(\ell_2)\).  Reflection only changes
the finite branch ledger.

# 8. Bilinear dispersion before conditioning

Let \(y\) denote the outer data before the selected prime is fixed, and
let \(J_y(\ell_0)\in\{0,1\}\) be the authoritative selector.  Schematically,
\[
M_0
 =\sum_y\sum_{\ell_0}
 W_y(\ell_0)J_y(\ell_0),
\tag{8.1}
\]
\[
\mathfrak C_H
 =\sum_y\sum_{\ell_0}
 W_y(\ell_0)J_y(\ell_0)
 |\mathcal R_{y,\ell_0,H}|^2.
\tag{8.2}
\]
A dispersion theorem proved after deleting \(J_y\), for example
\[
\sum_{y,\ell_0}W_y(\ell_0)|\mathcal R_{y,\ell_0,H}|^2
 \le A
 \sum_{y,\ell_0}W_y(\ell_0),
\tag{8.3}
\]
does not imply
\[
\mathfrak C_H\le AM_0.
\tag{8.4}
\]
The selector may retain an exceptional root and discard the roots which
dilute (8.3).  This is not repaired by the factor
\(|u_{\ell_0}|^2\): the same factor appears on both sides, and there is
only one authoritative selected root per outer state.

A valid pre-conditioning route must prove the selected Carleson estimate
\[
\sum_{y,\ell_0}W_y(\ell_0)J_y(\ell_0)
 \frac1G\sum_{\tau,j}|S_{y,\ell_0,H}(\tau,j)|^2
 \ll N^{2/3+o(1)}M_0
\tag{8.5}
\]
at \(G=N^{7/15}\), or its \(\mathrm{RSLG}_\beta\) variant.  Equation
(8.5) is precisely a relative selected-weighted statement; it cannot be
manufactured by dividing an absolute large-sieve estimate by an upper
bound for \(M_0\).

# 9. The smallest missing source-native theorem

The cleanest next statement is \(\mathrm{RSLG}_{3/2}\):

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\left|
\sum_xw_x
 \sum_{\ell_1\ne\ell_2}
 \left(1-\frac{|r_x(\ell_2)-r_x(\ell_1)|}{G}\right)_+
 c_{x,H,\nu}(\ell_1)
 \overline{c_{x,H,\nu}(\ell_2)}
\right|\\
&\hspace{35mm}\ll_vG^{3/2+o(1)}M_0,
\qquad
1\le G\le\sqrt N.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{9.1}
\]

It must hold uniformly in the literal dyadic residual block and one term
of the canonical source separation.  It preserves:

```text
the distinguished selected prime and its actual zero labels;
both moving prime characteristics;
the actual centered factors, including their signs;
the selected atom w_x and the exact M0 normalization;
all orientations and masks;
the source multiplier, term by term.
```

It does **not** ask for arbitrary pair coefficients, all physical prime
pairs, or a global ratio-energy theorem.  By Sections 2--4 it gives
\[
\mathfrak C_H\ll N^{11/9+o(1)}M_0,
\qquad
L_{1,H}^{\circ}\ll N^{11/18+o(1)}M_0,
\tag{9.2}
\]
which is already strictly below \(47/75\).

The direct covariance target is the same statement with local exponent
\(10/7\):
\[
|\mathfrak T_H(G)|\ll G^{10/7+o(1)}M_0,
\qquad G=N^{7/15},
\tag{9.3}
\]
which gives \(N^{6/5+o(1)}M_0\).

The current fixed-gap theorem proves exponent \(2\), not \(3/2\) or
\(10/7\).  The missing content is cancellation of the two
own-characteristic centered factors **after conditioning on a short
actual gap in the distinguished characteristic**.

# 10. Insertion-ready unconditional LaTeX

The following statements are complete unconditional reductions.  The
open \(\mathrm{RSLG}_\beta\) estimate is not packaged as a theorem.

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Rooted translated-block reduction]
\label{lem:srg-rooted-block-reduction}
Let $w_x\ge0$ and $M_0=\sum_xw_x$.  For each selected root $x$, let
$c_x(\ell)$ be the literal centered residual summand of one smoothly
separated source component, and let
$r_x(\ell)\in[0,p_x)$ be its actual distinguished-characteristic zero
label.  Assume $p_x\asymp N$.  For $1\le G\le\sqrt N$, put
\[
 \mathfrak C=\sum_xw_x\left|\sum_\ell c_x(\ell)\right|^2
\]
and
\[
 \mathfrak L(G)=
 \sum_xw_x\frac1G\sum_{\tau=0}^{G-1}\sum_j
 \left|\sum_{r_x(\ell)\in[\tau+jG,\tau+(j+1)G)}c_x(\ell)\right|^2.
\]
After the finite signed nonwrapping branch split,
\[
 \mathfrak C\ll \frac NG\,\mathfrak L(G).
\]
Moreover
\[
 \mathfrak L(G)=\mathfrak D+2\Re\mathfrak T(G),
\]
where
\[
 \mathfrak D=\sum_xw_x\sum_\ell|c_x(\ell)|^2
\]
and
\[
 \mathfrak T(G)=
 \sum_xw_x\sum_{\ell_1\ne\ell_2}
 \left(1-\frac{|r_x(\ell_2)-r_x(\ell_1)|}{G}\right)_+
 c_x(\ell_1)\overline{c_x(\ell_2)}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For each translate there are $O(N/G)$ label intervals.  Cauchy's
inequality bounds the square of the sum by $O(N/G)$ times the sum of the
block squares.  Average over the $G$ translates.  A pair of integer
labels at distance $g$ lies in one common translated interval for exactly
$(G-|g|)_+$ translates, which gives the tent coefficient after expansion.
\end{proof}
```

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Literal fixed-gap bound in the rooted covariance]
\label{lem:srg-rooted-fixed-gap}
Retain the notation of
Lemma~\ref{lem:srg-rooted-block-reduction}.  Suppose that every nonzero
label $r_x(\ell)$ has $O_v(1)$ shell-prime lifts and that
$c_x(\ell)$ contains the actual condition
$r_x(\ell)\in\mathcal Z_{p_x}$, with all remaining factors bounded.
For every fixed signed $g$ with $1\le|g|\le\sqrt N$,
\[
 \left|
 \sum_xw_x
 \sum_{r_x(\ell_2)-r_x(\ell_1)=g}
 c_x(\ell_1)\overline{c_x(\ell_2)}
 \right|
 \ll_v |g|N^{o(1)}M_0.
\]
Consequently
\[
 |\mathfrak T(G)|\ll_vG^{2+o(1)}M_0.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For one root and one legal circular branch, two actual
$p_x$-zero labels at gap $g$ force
$N_{|g|}(r)=0\pmod {p_x}$.  The gap polynomial has degree
$3(|g|-1)$, so it has $O(|g|)$ starts.  Each start has bounded
candidate-prime multiplicity on both sides.  Keep the centered and source
factors signed until applying their pointwise bounds at the fixed-gap
sum.  Multiply by the same nonnegative $w_x$ and sum $x$.  The tent sum
over $|g|<G$ is $O(G^2)$.
\end{proof}
```

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Physical prime gaps are moving reciprocal columns]
\label{lem:srg-physical-gap-mobius}
Let
\[
 r_x(\ell)=\Delta_x\overline{p'\ell}\pmod p,
 \qquad
 C_x=\Delta_x\overline{p'}\pmod p,
\]
with $C_x\ne0$.  If $\ell_2=\ell_1+m$ and both labels are nonzero, then
\[
 m r_x(\ell_1)r_x(\ell_2)
 \equiv C_x\bigl(r_x(\ell_1)-r_x(\ell_2)\bigr)\pmod p,
\]
or equivalently
\[
 r_x(\ell_2)
 =\frac{C_xr_x(\ell_1)}{C_x+mr_x(\ell_1)}\pmod p.
\]
Thus a bound for additive zero gaps does not by itself bound close
physical candidate primes.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The defining congruence is
$r_x(\ell)\ell\equiv C_x\pmod p$.  Hence
$\ell\equiv C_xr_x(\ell)^{-1}\pmod p$.  Subtract the two congruences and
clear the two labels.
\end{proof}
```

```tex
\begin{lemma}[Two-sample rooted divisor switch]
\label{lem:srg-two-sample-switch}
For a selected root $x$, put
\[
 t_x(\ell)=\langle\Delta_x\overline{p'\ell}\rangle_P,
 \qquad
 t'_x(\ell)=\frac{p'\ell t_x(\ell)-\Delta_x}{P},
\]
and let $h_x(\ell)$ be the signed displacement.  If
$s_x(\ell)=\langle-\Delta_x\overline P\rangle_\ell$, write
\[
 t'_0+p'h_x(\ell)=n_x(\ell)\ell+s_x(\ell).
\]
For two candidates with
$k=h_x(\ell_2)-h_x(\ell_1)$ one has
\[
 p'k=n_x(\ell_2)\ell_2-n_x(\ell_1)\ell_1
       +s_x(\ell_2)-s_x(\ell_1).
\]
If $m=\ell_2-\ell_1$, this is
\[
 p'k=(n_2-n_1)\ell_1+n_2m+s_2-s_1.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first identity follows from
$Pt'_x(\ell)=p'\ell t_x(\ell)-\Delta_x$ reduced modulo $\ell$.
Subtract it for the two candidates and use
$t'_x(\ell)=t'_0+p'h_x(\ell)$.
\end{proof}
```

# 11. Final classification

## Unconditional advance

The residual covariance has been reduced, without changing its measure or
signs, to a tent-weighted short-gap covariance in the actual distinguished
characteristic.  The reduction identifies exactly why the source returns
\(4/3\): diagonal mass \(N^{2/3}M_0\), \(N/G\) label blocks, and the
available \(G^2M_0\) fixed-gap ledger.

## Smallest non-circular lemma

\(\mathrm{RSLG}_{3/2}\), equation (9.1), is sufficient for an accepted
rooted exponent \(11/18<47/75\).  The sharper
\(\mathrm{RSLG}_{10/7}\) gives the requested \(N^{6/5}\) covariance.
Both are selected-weighted, signed, local, and preserve the literal source
multiplier.

## Why the proposed alternatives stop

Physical prime gaps give moving Möbius columns rather than additive gaps.
Two-sample divisor switching leaves two independent quotient variables
and two different defining characteristics.  Pre-selection dispersion is
an absolute average and does not survive the authoritative selector
without a new selected Carleson estimate.
