# Local mod-p^2 structure of Newton carriers

## Theorem (second layer of a common Newton mask)

Let
\[
A_i=G_{d_i,L_i}(Y),\qquad
G_{d,L}(Y)=\sum_{t=0}^L(-1)^t\binom{d+t}{t}\binom{d+L+1}{L-t}Y_{d+t}.
\]
Assume every mask contains the node \(p-1\), and \(p>\max_i L_i\). Write
\[
j_i=p-d_i-1.
\]
Then
\[
A_i\equiv Y_{p-1}\pmod p.
\]
For every pair,
\[
\frac{A_i-A_0}{p}\pmod p
\]
is a linear form in the finite difference coordinates of \(Y\), and in general it is not a multiple of a single universal scalar.

The exact formula is obtained by differentiating the binomial weights at the unique term containing \(Y_{p-1}\). Put \(t_i=j_i\). The coefficient of \(Y_{p-1}\) in \(A_i\) is
\[
\alpha_i=(-1)^{j_i}\binom{p-1}{j_i}\binom{p+L_i-j_i}{L_i-j_i}.
\]
Modulo \(p^2\),
\[
\alpha_i=1+p\left(H_{j_i}-H_{p-1}-H_{L_i-j_i}+H_{p+L_i-j_i}\right)\pmod {p^2},
\]
where the harmonic numbers are interpreted in \(\mathbb F_p\). Thus the divided difference contains the extra coordinate
\[
Y_{p-1}\cdot
\left(H_{j_i}-H_{L_i-j_i}+H_{L_i-j_i}(p)-H_{p-1}\right).
\]
The remaining coordinates are the finite-difference contribution from nodes below the marked node.

Therefore the local Smith form modulo \(p^2\) has at most one guaranteed marked scalar. After one universal p-factor is removed, the next layer is not forced to vanish: it contains a harmonic-weighted copy of \(Y_{p-1}\) plus independent lower-node finite differences.

## Adjacent carriers

For the standard pair
\[
G_{d,L},G_{d+1,L},
\]
we have the exact identity
\[
G_{d,L}-G_{d+1,L}=(-1)^{L+1}\binom{d+L+1}{L}\Delta^{L+1}Y_d.
\]
If \(p=d+L+1\), the binomial contributes exactly one factor of \(p\). Hence
\[
\frac{G_{d,L}-G_{d+1,L}}p
\equiv
(-1)^{L+1}\frac1p\binom{d+L+1}{L}\Delta^{L+1}Y_d
\pmod p.
\]
The five hostile block computations are consistent with
\[
p\mid B,\qquad p\nmid\Delta^{L+1}Y_d,
\]
so the second layer is a unit, not another target equation.

## No second relation from p|Y_{p-1}

Suppose
\[
p\mid Y_{p-1}.
\]
The above expansion gives
\[
\frac{A_i-A_0}{p}\equiv
c_iY_{p-1}+\sum_{m<p-1}c_{i,m}Y_m\pmod p.
\]
The first term disappears, but the finite-difference terms remain. There is no implication that the divided rows vanish.

Countermodel: take an integer sequence with
\[
Y_{p-1}=0,\qquad Y_{p-2}=1,\qquad Y_m=0\ (m<p-2).
\]
Then the first layer satisfies the target condition, but the divided Newton rows are nonzero whenever the mask reaches \(p-2\). Therefore the implication
\[
p|Y_{p-1}\Longrightarrow p|(A_i-A_0)/p
\]
is false for general sequences.

## Apéry specialization

For \(Y_d=C_M(d)\), the same algebra applies. The additional Apéry structure would have to prove
\[
\sum_{m<p-1}c_{i,m}C_M(m)=0\pmod p
\]
whenever
\[
p|C_M(p-1).
\]
No such congruence follows from Newton interpolation, Cartier reduction, or the known first-cell factorization.

The Cartier congruence gives information on \(C_M(p-1)\) itself, not on the independent finite differences. The observed data with
\[
v_p(B)=1,\qquad p\nmid\Delta^{N+1}Y_{D-1}
\]
explicitly rules out the required extra digit in the adjacent-carrier direction.

## Height correction

A prime interval of length \(H\) has primorial logarithmic height
\[
\log\prod_{p\in I}p=\vartheta(x+H)-\vartheta(x)=\Theta(H),
\]
not \(H\log M\). This correction strengthens the accounting but does not create a missing second scalar.

## Conclusion

The exact mod-\(p^2\) layer is:

1. one harmonic correction to the marked value \(Y_{p-1}\);
2. independent finite-difference coordinates of the remaining mask;
3. no automatic Apéry congruence eliminating those coordinates.

Thus the local Newton stack remains rank one after saturation. A genuine P3.2 closure still requires a new Apéry-specific relation among the finite differences, not another universal carrier manipulation.
