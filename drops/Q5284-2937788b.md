## Bottom line

**Mathematical verdict:** the ordinary zero-extended translated-zero Gram extension is **valid at the scalar completed-source level for every fixed `G=N^gamma`, `gamma<1`**, after two load-bearing qualifications:

1. for arbitrary bounded `g`-dependent masks the masked Gram diagonal is **at most** `Z(q)`, not literally equal to `Z(q)`; and
2. the mask/coefficient atom must remain **independent of `d`** after the CD8 tensor separation. The argument does **not** transfer to a genuinely `d`-dependent HH/projected row merely because it is bounded pointwise.

The three Schur rows

\[
 Z(q)+G^2,\qquad GZ(q),\qquad Z(q)^2
\]

are all unconditional for the ordinary zero-extended source columns. CD10's equal-slope fibre and the additive-large-sieve transfer remain valid. With

\[
 \mathcal E_N^{(2)}:=\sum_{q\asymp_vN}|u_q|^2Z(q)^2
 \ll N^{4/15+o(1)},
\]

the resulting scalar source-row energy is indeed

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{0<|d|\asymp D}|A_{G,d}^{\rm src}|^2
 \ll N^{o(1)}\min\!\left(
 N^{6/5}G+N^{3/5}G^3,
 N^{6/5}G^2,
 N^{28/15}G,
 N^{281/100}
 \right).}
\]

**But the claimed strategic consequence is negative.** This does **not** enlarge the scalar four-color deletion past the already proved `97/300` cutoff. \[
 \frac35+3\gamma<\frac{157}{100}

 \gamma<\frac{97}{300}.
\]

So the global extension proves a narrow extra **row-norm fact** for `97/300 < gamma < 33/100` (the isolated band is already below `N^(159/100)` there), but **it deletes no new genuinely middle-macroscopic block from the four-distinct scalar Gram**. A new orthogonality/correlation input would still be required.

## Detailed derivation of the global Gram

For one fixed separated CD8 source atom and fixed `p,q`, set

\[
 v_g(k)=m_{p,q,g}(k)x_q(k+g),\qquad |m_{p,q,g}(k)|\le1,
\]

where `m` is independent of `d`. Then

\[
 C_{p,q}(g)=\sum_k x_p(k)v_g(k)
\]

(up to harmless conjugation convention). For `g,g'` in one signed interval,

\[
 |\langle v_g,v_{g'}\rangle|
 \le \#\{k:x_q(k+g)x_q(k+g')=1\}.
\]

Because `x_q` is zero-extended, after translating back by `c_q` this is exactly an **ordinary** pair count of zeros at integer difference `h=g'-g`. For `h!=0`, the actual gap theorem gives

\[
 A_q(|h|)\le3(|h|-1)\ll |h|,
\]

as soon as `|h|<q`; fixed `gamma<1` guarantees this for all sufficiently large `N`. For `h=0`, the masked diagonal is at most `Z(q)`.

Thus the three row-sum estimates are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sup_g\sum_{g'}|\langle v_g,v_{g'}\rangle|
 &\ll Z(q)+\sum_{1\le h\ll G}h
 \ll Z(q)+G^2,\\
 &\ll GZ(q),\\
 &\le \sum_{h\in\mathbb Z}A_q(h)=Z(q)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

The last equality is ordinary: every ordered pair of zero lifts has exactly one integer difference. Schur plus `||x_p||_2^2=Z(p)` yields

\[
 \sum_{g\in I_G}|C_{p,q}(g)|^2
 \ll Z(p)B_q(G),
 \qquad
 B_q(G)=\min\{Z(q)+G^2,\,GZ(q),\,Z(q)^2\}.
\]

Now retain CD10's definition

\[
 \beta_{q,J}^{(G)}
 =u_q\!\sum_{\substack{p\ne q,\ g\in I_G\\g\bar p\equiv J\pmod q}}
 u_pC_{p,q}(g).
\]

The equal-slope fibre has `O_v(G)` points, so

\[
 \sum_{q,J}|\beta_{q,J}^{(G)}|^2
 \ll N^{o(1)}G
 \sum_{p,q}|u_pu_q|^2Z(p)B_q(G).
\]

Using each of the three alternatives for `B_q(G)` gives respectively

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &G\mathcal E_N(\mathcal E_N+G^2\mathcal V_N),\\
 &G^2\mathcal E_N^2,\\
 &G\mathcal E_N\mathcal E_N^{(2)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The additive large sieve multiplies these by `N^(2+o(1))`. Since

\[
 \mathcal E_N\ll N^{-2/5+o(1)},\quad
 \mathcal V_N\ll N^{-1+o(1)},\quad
 \mathcal E_N^{(2)}\ll N^{4/15+o(1)},
\]

this is exactly the first three branches of the envelope. The absolute source-row bound supplies the fourth.

For reference, if `G=N^gamma`, the best exponent `e(gamma)` furnished by the minimum is

\[
 e(\gamma)=
 \begin{cases}
  6/5+\gamma,&0\le\gamma\le3/10,\\
  3/5+3\gamma,&3/10\le\gamma\le3/5,\\
  6/5+2\gamma,&3/5\le\gamma\le2/3,\\
  28/15+\gamma,&2/3\le\gamma\le283/300,\\
  281/100,&283/300\le\gamma<1.
 \end{cases}
\]

The crossings are exact:

- first two monomials in the original branch cross at `gamma=3/10`;
- that branch meets `N^(6/5)G^2` at `gamma=3/5`;
- `N^(6/5)G^2` meets `N^(28/15)G` at `gamma=2/3`;
- `N^(28/15)G` meets the crude `N^(281/100)` branch at `gamma=283/300`.

The envelope is monotone in `gamma`; there is no later range where it returns below the target.

## Why the four-color deletion does not improve

Let `A` be the band one wants to remove from a residual row `R`. CD15 writes the four-distinct scalar Gram as a finite expectation of colored inner products. Replacing `R` by `R-A` produces terms of the schematic form

\[
 \langle A,R\rangle,\qquad
 \langle R,A\rangle,\qquad
 \langle A,A\rangle.
\]

The source endpoint available in CD14--CD15 has energy

\[
 \|R\|_2^2\ll N^{161/100+o(1)}.
\]

Therefore Cauchy--Schwarz puts the cross terms at exponent

\[
 \frac{e_A+161/100}{2}.
\]

To beat the target `159/100`, one needs

\[
 e_A<2\frac{159}{100}-\frac{161}{100}
 =\frac{157}{100}.
\]

For `3/10<gamma<3/5`, which contains all candidate cutoffs near the existing one,

\[
 e_A=\frac35+3\gamma.
\]

Hence

\[
 e_A<\frac{157}{100}
 \iff
 \gamma<\frac{97}{300}.
\]

This is exactly the current CD12--CD15 threshold. By contrast, merely asking for the **band itself** to have energy below `N^(159/100)` gives

\[
 \frac35+3\gamma<\frac{159}{100}
 \iff
 \gamma<\frac{33}{100}.
\]

Thus the global Gram extension creates the narrow factual strip

\[
 \frac{97}{300}<\gamma<\frac{33}{100}
\]

where the isolated source band is sub-target, but the four-color cross term is not. Calling that strip “deleted” would be a logical error.

There is also no support-orthogonality shortcut: CD14 explicitly avoids support monotonicity for complex rows, and the scalar `d`-inner product has already summed the label variables. A disjoint label-gap support before completion does not make the completed scalar rows orthogonal in `d`.

## Insertion-ready unconditional repair

Because the connected repository does not expose the literal current-local CD24--CD26 text, I will not fabricate an exact tag-for-tag replacement. The following theorem/remark is, however, insertion-ready mathematically; tag numbers can be attached to the current file's existing CD24--CD26 slots.

```tex
\begin{proposition}[Global ordinary translated-zero source band]
\label{prop:ad-completed-additive-slope-band-global}
Let $1\le G=o(N)$ and let $I_G=(G,2G]\cap\mathbb Z$; treat the
negative band separately.  In the notation of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ad-completed-additive-slope-band}, assume that after
the CD8 smooth tensor separation the source multiplier
$m_{p,q,g}(k)$ is independent of $d$ and satisfies
$|m_{p,q,g}(k)|\le1$.  Prime-color restrictions and bounded
$d$-independent source deletions are allowed.

Put
\[
 \mathcal E_N^{(2)}
 =\sum_{q\asymp_vN}|u_q|^2Z(q)^2.
\]
Then
\[
 \mathcal E_N^{(2)}\ll_v N^{4/15+o(1)}
\]
and, uniformly for such source atoms,
\[
 \sum_{0<|d|\asymp D}|A_{G,d}^{\rm src}|^2
 \ll_{v,w} N^{o(1)}
 \min\!\left\{
   N^{6/5}G+N^{3/5}G^3,
   N^{6/5}G^2,
   N^{28/15}G,
   N^{281/100}
 \right\}.
\]
In particular this applies to $G=N^\gamma$ for every fixed
$0\le\gamma<1$ and all sufficiently large $N$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For fixed $p,q$ write
\[
 C_{p,q}(g)=\sum_km_{p,q,g}(k)x_p(k)x_q(k+g).
\]
Because $x_q$ is extended by zero to $\mathbb Z$, the Gram entry
between the $g$- and $g'$-translated $q$-vectors is bounded by the
ordinary integer-gap correlation of $\mathcal Z_q$ at
$h=g'-g$.  Its diagonal is at most $Z(q)$, and for $h\ne0$ the
nonwrapping gap-polynomial theorem gives
\[
 \#\{r,r'\in\mathcal Z_q:r'-r=h\}
 \le 3(|h|-1)\ll |h|,
\]
for $|h|<q$.  Since $G=o(N)$ and $q\asymp_vN$, this condition
holds throughout the band for large $N$.  Three Schur row sums give
\[
 \sum_{g\in I_G}|C_{p,q}(g)|^2
 \ll Z(p)\min\{Z(q)+G^2,\,GZ(q),\,Z(q)^2\}.
\]
Here the last row follows from
\[
 \sum_{h\in\mathbb Z}
 \#\{r,r'\in\mathcal Z_q:r'-r=h\}=Z(q)^2.
\]

Now group by the nonzero slope $J\equiv g\bar p\pmod q$ exactly as
in CD10.  A fixed $(q,J,g)$ admits $O_v(1)$ primes $p$ in the
fixed-ratio shell, so the equal-slope fibre has $O_v(G)$ elements.
Consequently
\[
 \sum_{q,J}|\beta_{q,J}^{(G)}|^2
 \ll N^{o(1)}G\sum_{p,q}|u_pu_q|^2Z(p)
 \min\{Z(q)+G^2,\,GZ(q),\,Z(q)^2\}.
\]
Using the three alternatives separately gives
\[
 N^{o(1)}\min\{
 G\mathcal E_N(\mathcal E_N+G^2\mathcal V_N),
 G^2\mathcal E_N^2,
 G\mathcal E_N\mathcal E_N^{(2)}
 \}.
\]
The unconditional pointwise bound $Z(q)\ll q^{2/3+o(1)}$ and CD6 give
\[
 \mathcal E_N^{(2)}
 \le N^{2/3+o(1)}\mathcal E_N
 \ll N^{4/15+o(1)}.
\]
The reduced slope fractions are $\gg_vN^{-2}$ separated, so the
additive large sieve costs at most $N^{2+o(1)}$.  CD6 now yields the
first three displayed energy bounds.  Finally the absolute source-row
bound
\[
 |A_{G,d}^{\rm src}|\le
 \left(\sum_p|u_p|Z(p)\right)^2
 \ll N^{6/5+o(1)}
\]
and $D=N^{41/100}$ give the uniform fourth bound
$N^{281/100+o(1)}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[No new scalar four-color deletion]
\label{rem:ad-global-gram-no-four-color-gain}
The preceding global source-row estimate does not by itself improve the
$97/300$ residual in
Corollary~\ref{cor:ad-source-gap-delete-97-300}.  Indeed, for
$G=N^\gamma$ with $3/10<\gamma<3/5$, the best band-energy exponent is
$3/5+3\gamma$.  In the CD15 four-color identity a deletion produces
cross terms with a source endpoint of energy $N^{161/100+o(1)}$.
Cauchy--Schwarz makes those cross terms $o(N^{159/100})$ only if
\[
 \frac35+3\gamma<\frac{157}{100},
\]
i.e. only if $\gamma<97/300$.  Although the isolated band itself is
$o(N^{159/100})$ for $\gamma<33/100$, the interval
$97/300<\gamma<33/100$ is not thereby deleted from the scalar
four-distinct Gram.  Any improvement past $97/300$ requires an
additional correlation/orthogonality input, not merely the ordinary
translated-zero Gram bound.
\end{remark}
```

This repair deliberately says **“diagonal at most `Z(q)`”**, keeps the `d`-independence hypothesis visible, uses only ordinary zero-extended differences, and states the no-gain conclusion needed to prevent the `33/100` row threshold from being mistaken for a four-color deletion threshold.
