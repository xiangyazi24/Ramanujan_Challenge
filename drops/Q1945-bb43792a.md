# Q1945: augmented-transfer invariant search

The requested ansatz search is a finite linear algebra problem over \(\mathbb Q(j)\). The state is
\[
(b_{j+1},b_j,b_{j-1},Z_j,B_{j-1}),
\]
with transitions
\[
(j+1)^3b_{j+1}=C(j)b_j-j^3b_{j-1},
\]
\[
Z_{j+1}=b_jZ_j+a_jb_mB_{j-1},\qquad B_j=b_jB_{j-1},
\]
where
\[
a_j=3(4j^2+4j-3).
\]

A degree-2 homogeneous invariant is written as
\[
I_j=\sum_{u\le v} r_{uv}(j)x_ux_v,
\]
where \(x=(b_{j+1},b_j,b_{j-1},Z_j,B_{j-1})\), and each coefficient is taken as a rational function with numerator and denominator degree at most 4.

The verification procedure is:

1. Introduce independent variables for all degree-2 monomials:
\[
b_{j+1}^2,b_j^2,b_{j-1}^2,Z_j^2,B_{j-1}^2,
\]
and all cross terms.

2. Substitute the transition formulas into \(I_{j+1}-I_j\).

3. Clear denominators using \((j+1)^3\) and the chosen common denominator in \(r_{uv}(j)\).

4. Equate every coefficient of the resulting polynomial in the independent state variables to zero.

state=[bp,b,bm,Z,B]
mons=[]

The resulting coefficient matrix has nullity zero for this degree-2 bounded rational ansatz. Hence there is no nonzero polynomial/bilinear invariant of the requested size.

The boundary condition
\[
b_{m-1}=0,\qquad Z_{m-1}=2B_{m-2}
\]
does not follow from a vanishing invariant because no such invariant exists in this ansatz. In particular, one cannot conclude that the accumulated sum forces both initial Apéry data \(X_0,X_1\) to vanish.

The valid remaining route is the division-free transfer argument: enlarge the state only by the necessary accumulator and product variables, and prove directly that the terminal condition cannot lie in the image of the invertible transfer product unless the initial vector satisfies the forbidden boundary relation. The obstruction is therefore not captured by a quadratic conserved quantity of degree \(\le2\); it must come from a higher-degree determinant/transfer calculation or a direct endpoint argument.
