# P3.2 pointed-simplicity / divided-Lucas gcd attack

## Verdict

There are two separate conclusions.

First, the displayed theorem in the question is **literally false** unless the already-declared bad characteristic `5` is removed from the quantifier.  The first exact counterexample is the structural one

```text
t=1,
b_1=5,
D_1=12,
e_1=5 D_1=60,
p=5>2t+1=3.
```

Thus

```text
rad_{p>2t+1} gcd(b_t,numerator(e_t)) = 1
```

must be replaced by

```text
rad_{p>2t+1, p>=7} gcd(b_t,numerator(e_t)) = 1,       (PS)
```

or equivalently `p>max(5,2t+1)`.

Second, after making that intended correction, I do **not** obtain a proof and I do not find a non-structural counterexample.  I do obtain a substantially sharper exact reduction which preserves the distinguished terminal node:

```text
(PS) is exactly the assertion that X_t=2t(t+1)+1
is never a multiple root modulo p of one specific Wilson polynomial v_t(X)
when p>2t+1 and p>=7.
```

Here

```text
v_0(X)=1,
v_1(X)=X,

(r+1)^3 v_(r+1)(X)
 = (2r+1)(r(r+1)+X) v_r(X) - r^3 v_(r-1)(X).       (1)
```

The exact identities are

```text
v_t(X_t)=b_t,
D_t=4(2t+1) v_t'(X_t),
e_t=20(2t+1) v_t'(X_t).                            (2)
```

All denominators of `v_t` and `v_t'` are units modulo every `p>2t+1`.  Therefore, for such a prime,

```text
p | b_t and p | numerator(e_t)

iff

v_t(X_t)=0 and v_t'(X_t)=0 mod p.                  (3)
```

This is a genuinely **pointed** separability problem.  It is not global squarefreeness of `v_t`: the global discriminant already has irrelevant large prime factors (for example the discriminant of `v_3` has a factor `191`, and that of `v_4` has the large factor `1792027`).

There is also an exact terminal unit:

```text
p | v_t(X_t)  ==>  p does not divide v_(t-1)(X_t)   (p>t).   (4)
```

Consequently an explicit terminal Bezout/contiguous identity of the form

```text
A_t(X) v_t(X)
 + B_t(X) * 4(2t+1) v_t'(X)

 = U_t v_(t-1)(X) + (X-X_t) E_t(X),                (PB_t)
```

with coefficients in

```text
Z[1/(5(2t+1)!)]
```

and `U_t` a unit there, would prove `(PS)` immediately.  This is the sharp algebraic target I recommend.  I do not find such a certificate.  The simplest low-degree terminal-ladder ansätze fail exactly, and the ordinary Christoffel-Darboux identity reduces a hypothetical double root only to an isotropic finite-field norm, which is not a contradiction.

The new useful coordinate is therefore the **specialized ordinary-derivative Wilson subresultant at the diagonal node**, not the global discriminant and not another unpointed Green norm.

Finally, even a proof of `(PS)` would **not prove reflection MR2**.  It would prove that every admissible one-prime lower-half reflection zero is locally simple and that its first divided-Lucas/Bockstein coordinate is a unit.  That removes local tangency/multiplicity.  It gives no correlation between two distinct characteristics `p` and `ell`, no new joint divisor beyond the already-paid `p*ell`, and no bound on the number of disjoint reflection-edge pairs.  The Q2080 DSAG/MR2 cross-prime gcd-height problem would remain.

```text
problems/3.2/research/scripts/q32_apery_reflection_p2.py.
```

The latter uses

```text
P(j)=34j^3+51j^2+27j+5,
P'(j)=102j^2+102j+27,
```

the exact differentiated recurrence

```text
(j+1)^3 D_(j+1)
 = P'(j)b_j + P(j)D_j
   -3j^2 b_(j-1) - j^3 D_(j-1)
   -3(j+1)^2 b_(j+1).                          (5)
```

The script also verifies the mod-`p^2` reflection formula and checks that the same `D_j` is the ordinary Gessel index derivative.

There is a factor-of-two mismatch in the formula stated in the question.  Put

```text
T_t=t(t+1),
phi_k(Y)=product_(j=0)^(k-1) (Y-j(j+1))/(k!)^2,
K_t(Y)=sum_(k=0)^t phi_k(Y)^2.
```

At the terminal node,

```text
phi_k(T_t)
 = binom(t,k) binom(t+k,k).                    (6)
```

Moreover

```text
1/(T_t-j(j+1))
 = [1/(t-j)+1/(t+j+1)]/(2t+1),
```

so

```text
(2t+1) K_t'(T_t)
 = 2 sum_(k=0)^t
     binom(t,k)^2 binom(t+k,k)^2
     (H_(t+k)-H_(t-k))
 = D_t.                                        (7)
```

Thus, with the repository normalization and the question's definition `e_t=5D_t`, the correct identity is

```text
e_t = 5(2t+1) K_t'(T_t),                       (8)
```

not `10(2t+1)K_t'(T_t)`.

The check at `t=1` is decisive:

```text
K_1(Y)=1+Y^2,

D_1=(2*1+1)*4=12,
e_1=60.
```

The expression with coefficient `10` would give `120`.

This normalization error does **not** affect the desired vanishing assertion for primes `p>=7`, since it is only a factor `2`, but it matters for an exact Bezout identity and for the characteristic-`5` exception.

# 2. Every denominator and the two structural exceptions

From (7), the harmonic denominators lie among `1,...,2t`.  If

```text
L_(2t)=lcm(1,...,2t),
```

then

```text
L_(2t) D_t is an integer,
L_(2t) e_t is an integer.                      (9)
```

Hence for every prime

```text
p>2t+1
```

the denominator of `D_t` or `e_t` is a `p`-unit, and saying

```text
p | numerator(e_t)
```

is unambiguous.

There are exactly two structural exclusions in this discussion.

### 2.1 Characteristic 5

The literal theorem includes `p=5` when `t=1`, and then

```text
5 | b_1,
5 | e_1.
```

So `p=5` must be removed explicitly.

### 2.2 The central ramification `p=2t+1`

If `p=2t+1` is prime, then `p` is not in the denominator in (7), while the explicit factor `(2t+1)` gives

```text
p | numerator(D_t),
```

Thus whenever this central prime also divides `b_t`, pointed simplicity must fail for the formal ramification reason.  For example

```text
b_5=819005=5*11*14891,
e_5=13276637,
gcd(b_5,e_5)=11.
```

The strict inequality `p>2t+1` correctly removes this case.

# 3. Exact Green identity

Set `e_j=5D_j`.  Multiplying (5) by `5` gives

```text
(j+1)^3 e_(j+1)
 = P(j)e_j - j^3 e_(j-1) + F_j,                (11)
```

where exactly

```text
F_j
 = 5[(102j^2+102j+27)b_j
     -3j^2 b_(j-1)
     -3(j+1)^2 b_(j+1)].                       (12)
```

Define

```text
W_j=(j+1)^3 (b_j e_(j+1)-e_j b_(j+1)).        (13)
```

Subtracting the `b` recurrence multiplied by `e_j` from (11) multiplied by `b_j` gives

```text
W_j-W_(j-1)=F_j b_j.                           (14)
```

Since

```text
W_0=e_1=60,
```

one gets the exact integer identity

```text
S_t
 = 60+sum_(j=1)^(t-1) F_j b_j
 = t^3 (b_(t-1)e_t-e_(t-1)b_t).                (15)
```

Now let `p>t`, `p>=5`, and `p|b_t`.  The Apéry recurrence gives the no-consecutive-zero fact

```text
p does not divide b_(t-1),                     (16)
```

and `p` does not divide `t`.  Therefore (15) gives

```text
S_t == t^3 b_(t-1)e_t mod p,                   (17)
```

which proves exactly

```text
e_t==0 mod p  iff  S_t==0 mod p.               (18)
```

No denominator is hidden in this equivalence when `p>2t+1` because of (9).

# 4. The pointed Racah/Wilson polynomial

The terminal triangular basis suggests using a **cross** kernel rather than the diagonal norm.  For a new variable `s`, put

```text
T_s=s(s+1),
R_t(s)=sum_(k=0)^t phi_k(T_t) phi_k(T_s).       (19)
```

Equivalently

```text
R_t(s)
 = _4F_3(-t,t+1,-s,s+1; 1,1,1; 1).            (20)
```

At the distinguished diagonal node,

```text
R_t(t)=K_t(T_t)=b_t.                            (21)
```

Differentiate only the second factor in (19).  Since the diagonal derivative differentiates both equal factors,

```text
R_t'(t)
 = (2t+1) * K_t'(T_t)/2
 = D_t/2
 = e_t/10.                                     (22)
```

Hence, for every `p>=7`, `p>2t+1`,

```text
p | b_t and p | numerator(e_t)

iff

R_t(t)=R_t'(t)=0 mod p.                         (23)
```

This already preserves the terminal label.  We are not asking whether all roots of a large Hasse polynomial are simple; we are asking whether one specified diagonal point is a multiple root of one specified degree-`t` polynomial.

For integral `s`, the hypergeometric expression is self-dual:

```text
R_t(s)=R_s(t).                                  (24)
```

That duality is the source of the next spectral form.

# 5. Spectral polynomial `v_t` and exact multiple-root equivalence

Put

```text
X_s=2s(s+1)+1.                                  (25)
```

There is a degree-`r` polynomial `v_r(X)` satisfying

```text
v_0(X)=1,
v_1(X)=X,

(r+1)^3 v_(r+1)(X)
 = (2r+1)(r(r+1)+X)v_r(X)-r^3v_(r-1)(X).       (26)
```

The Beukers/Wilson hypergeometric polynomial gives

```text
R_r(s)=v_r(X_s).                                (27)
```

Therefore

```text
v_t(X_t)=R_t(t)=b_t.                            (28)
```

Since

```text
dX_s/ds=2(2s+1),
```

(22) yields

```text
D_t=4(2t+1)v_t'(X_t),
e_t=20(2t+1)v_t'(X_t).                          (29)
```

This proves the claimed pointed equivalence (3).

### 5.1 Wilson identification

In standard Wilson notation,

```text
R_t(s)
 = W_t(-(s+1/2)^2; 1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2)/(t!)^3.    (30)
```

Indeed the Wilson numerator parameters are

```text
-t,
t+1,
1/2+i*i(s+1/2)=-s,
1/2-i*i(s+1/2)=s+1,
```

and all three denominator parameters are `1`.

Thus `(PS)` is a pointed simplicity statement for the completely symmetric Wilson family.

### 5.2 Denominators of `v_r`

If `c_r` is the leading coefficient of `v_r`, (26) gives

```text
c_r=(2r)!/[2^r (r!)^4].                         (31)
```

In particular every coefficient denominator of every `v_r` with `r<=t` is supported on primes `<=t` (the recurrence itself already gives this localization).  For `p>2t+1`, all coefficients in (26), all leading coefficients, and all derivatives are `p`-integral.

# 6. The exact terminal predecessor unit

The three-term recurrence gives a fact which is stronger than generic squarefreeness input and is precisely adapted to the distinguished node.

### Lemma

Let `p>t` be prime.  If

```text
v_t(X_t)=0 mod p,
```

then

```text
v_(t-1)(X_t) != 0 mod p.                        (32)
```

### Proof

If both `v_t(X_t)` and `v_(t-1)(X_t)` vanished, (26) at `r=t-1` would give

```text
(t-1)^3 v_(t-2)(X_t)=0 mod p.
```

For `t>1`, `p>t` makes `(t-1)` a unit, so `v_(t-2)(X_t)=0`.  Repeating backward forces

```text
v_0(X_t)=0,
```

contrary to `v_0=1`.  The case `t=1` is immediate.

This proves the lemma.

So, at an actual marked prime, the predecessor is a canonical available unit.  This is why `(PB_t)` is the right Bezout target: it would turn the derivative condition into an impossible vanishing of a known unit.

# 7. Why the obvious Wilson/Sturm attack still stops

Normalize `v_r` to monic polynomials `P_r`.  From (26),

```text
P_(r+1)(X)
 = (X+r(r+1))P_r(X)
   - [r^6/((2r-1)(2r+1))] P_(r-1)(X).          (33)
```

For `p>2t+1`, every off-diagonal coefficient in (33) is a `p`-unit.  Thus the corresponding tridiagonal matrix is irreducible/cyclic over `F_p`.

That fact alone does **not** imply a simple spectrum over a finite field.  A symmetric or symmetrizable irreducible tridiagonal matrix can have a defective eigenvalue when its eigenvector is isotropic for the relevant bilinear form.

Here the obstruction is visible without abstraction.  Differentiate (26) with respect to `X` and set

```text
y_r=v_r(X),
z_r=v_r'(X).
```

A direct subtraction gives the exact Christoffel-Darboux/Green identity

```text
(n+1)^3 [v_n(X)v_(n+1)'(X)-v_n'(X)v_(n+1)(X)]
 = sum_(r=0)^n (2r+1) v_r(X)^2.                (34)
```

At `X=X_t`, if `v_t(X_t)=0`, the case `n=t-1` is

```text
t^3 v_(t-1)(X_t)v_t'(X_t)
 = sum_(r=0)^(t-1) (2r+1)v_r(X_t)^2.           (35)
```

Because the predecessor is a unit, a bad pointed zero is equivalent to

```text
sum_(r=0)^(t-1) (2r+1)v_r(X_t)^2 = 0 mod p.    (36)
```

This is exactly the finite Green norm barrier already identified in the project.  Over `F_p`, a quadratic form of this dimension can be isotropic.  There is no Sturm sign argument, and `p>2t+1` does not turn (36) into a positive real inequality.

So a successful finite-field Sturm/Riccati proof must use additional arithmetic of the **specific terminal vector**

```text
(v_0(X_t),...,v_(t-1)(X_t)),
```

not merely irreducibility of the recurrence.

# 8. A genuinely new Wilson lane, and why it is not yet enough

Wilson polynomials have an exact **divided-difference** lowering relation.  For the symmetric parameters one has schematically

```text
D_W W_t(y^2;1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2)
 = -t(t+1) W_(t-1)(y^2;1,1,1,1),               (37)
```

where `D_W` is the Wilson central divided-difference operator.

For `p>2t+1`, the scalar `t(t+1)` is a unit.  This is the closest classical structure relation to the desired terminal Bezout identity.

But the pointed bad condition concerns the **ordinary derivative**

```text
v_t'(X_t),
```

not `D_W v_t`.  At the imaginary Wilson lattice point

```text
y=i(t+1/2),
```

the divided difference samples two shifted lattice values; an ordinary double root at `X_t` does not force that divided difference to vanish.  Therefore (37) cannot be substituted for the missing Bezout certificate.

This identifies the exact classical-special-function gap:

```text
ordinary derivative at the diagonal terminal node
        versus
Wilson divided difference with shifted parameters.                 (38)
```

A two-term structure relation resolving (38) modulo the ideal `(v_t,X-X_t)`, with denominator support `<=2t+1`, would prove `(PS)`.

# 9. The specialized subresultant/Bezout statement to prove

Let

```text
Lambda_t = Z[1/(5(2t+1)!)].                    (39)
```

All denominators above are units in `Lambda_t`.  The corrected pointed theorem `(PS)` is equivalent to the localized ideal statement

```text
(b_t,e_t) Lambda_t = Lambda_t.                 (40)
```

Equivalently, there must exist a Bezout identity

```text
A_t b_t + B_t e_t = U_t,                       (41)
```

with

```text
A_t,B_t in Lambda_t,
U_t in Lambda_t^x.                              (42)
```

Existence of (41) abstractly is merely a restatement of `(PS)`.  To count as a proof, one needs an **explicit/factorized** certificate whose right-hand scalar has no prime support above `2t+1`.

The strongest useful polynomial version is `(PB_t)`:

```text
A_t(X) v_t(X)
 + B_t(X) 4(2t+1)v_t'(X)
 = U_t v_(t-1)(X) + (X-X_t)E_t(X),             (43)
```

where

```text
A_t,B_t,E_t in Lambda_t[X],
U_t in Lambda_t^x.                              (44)
```

Evaluation at `X_t`, followed by the predecessor-unit lemma, immediately excludes a bad prime `p>2t+1`.

This is the requested terminal-node subresultant in a form which preserves the label.  It is strictly better targeted than

```text
Res_X(v_t,v_t')
```

because the global resultant pays for collisions at **all** roots.  Those irrelevant collisions really occur at large primes: exact small-degree discriminants already contain large factors such as `191` for `t=3` and `1792027` for `t=4`.

I tested the simplest low-degree polynomial-in-`t` two-term terminal-ladder ansätze against the exact recurrence; no identity appears through degree `6`.  That is not a no-go theorem for (43), but it rules out the most naive predecessor relation.

# 11. Strongest unconditional statement from the present attack

Without the missing pointed Bezout/separability theorem, the all-prime exclusion remains open.

One always has the elementary height bound

```text
b_t <= (t+1) 64^t,                             (45)
```

since

```text
binom(t,k)<=2^t,
binom(t+k,k)<=4^t.
```

Therefore, if `B_t^bad` denotes the set of nonstructural bad primes

```text
B_t^bad
 = {p prime: p>2t+1, p>=7,
              p|b_t, p|numerator(e_t)},        (46)
```

then unconditionally

```text
sum_(p in B_t^bad) log p
 <= log b_t
 <= t log 64 + log(t+1),                       (47)
```

and hence

```text
#B_t^bad = O(t/log t).                          (48)
```

Using the standard Apéry growth `b_t=(17+12sqrt(2))^(t+o(t))` improves only the constant in (47), not the qualitative statement.  No present elementary argument turns (48) into zero.

The exact advance here is structural rather than asymptotic:

```text
bad divided-Lucas prime
  <=>
```

# 12. What a proof of pointed simplicity would imply for reflection MR2

Assume the corrected theorem `(PS)` were proved.

For every lower-half reflection event with

```text
p>2t+1,
p>=7,
p|b_t,
```

we would then know

```text
p does not divide numerator(e_t).                (50)
```

Equivalently:

* the distinguished index zero is simple;
* the first reflected mod-`p^2` defect is nonzero;
* the first divided-Lucas/Bockstein coordinate is a `p`-unit after the fixed denominator is cleared;
* local primitive rank loss cannot be blamed on a repeated pointed zero.

This is valuable cleanup.  In fact it works **against** hopes for a free second Fitting layer: the first divided direction is forced to survive rather than vanish.

But `(PS)` is a one-characteristic theorem.  For two distinct reflection primes `p` and `ell`, it says only

```text
local p-coordinate is a p-unit,
local ell-coordinate is an ell-unit.             (51)
```

CRT still permits those two unit coordinates to vary independently.  Nothing in `(PS)` forces

```text
p*ell | C(p,ell)
```

for a new joint small-height scalar beyond the divisibility already defining the two vertices.

Consequently `(PS)` would **not** imply any of the following:

```text
MR2,
a bound for the factorial second moment of disjoint reflection edges,
the Q2080 DSAG four-polynomial gcd-height estimate,
a cross-prime resultant saving,
or a new simultaneous p*ell layer.               (52)
```

It would remove the local multiplicity/tangency exceptional set from those problems and make the residual sector cleaner, but the residual difficulty would still be horizontal correlation across **distinct characteristics**.

So the logical relation is

```text
pointed simplicity
    => clean/simple local reflection vertices
    !=> cross-prime MR2/DSAG.                    (53)
```

# 13. Recommended next theorem

The most sharply targeted next theorem is not global squarefreeness.  It is:

```text
POINTED_WILSON_BEZOUT(t):

For X_t=2t(t+1)+1, construct (43) explicitly with
A_t,B_t,E_t in Z[1/(5(2t+1)!)][X]
and U_t a unit in that ring.                    (54)
```

An equivalent specialized-subresultant formulation is:

```text
The primitive generator of

(v_t(X), v_t'(X), X-X_t)
   intersect Z[1/(5(2t+1)!)]

is a unit.                                      (55)
```

The certificate must be obtained **before forgetting `X-X_t`**.  Taking a global discriminant or scalar resultant first loses the terminal label and reintroduces the irrelevant large primes which the problem explicitly wants to avoid.

The Wilson structure relation (37) suggests the most concrete attack: derive an ordinary-derivative/central-divided-difference connection at the imaginary diagonal point `i(t+1/2)` and show that its terminal coefficient is a unit after localizing at primes `<=2t+1`.  That would be a genuinely new ingredient, not another use of the already-isotropic Green norm.
