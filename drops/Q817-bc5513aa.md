# Hostile audit of LGTQ-2 branch reduction

## Verdict

The reduction is **conditionally useful but not yet a theorem**. The signed-coordinate decomposition is essentially correct once the branch convention is fixed, but the claimed exact equivalence LGTQ-2 iff LGTQ-2-SB requires quantifier and endpoint repairs. Neither DD nor RR currently collapses to an unconditional Apéry multiplicity bound from the canonical identities alone.

## 1. Definitions and signed coordinate

For a fixed q and fixed C, consider primes in the q-cell

\[
 n/(q+1)<p\le n/q.
\]

Write the characteristic index after the Apéry-Lucas reduction as a signed coordinate

\[
r_p(n)=n-qp,
\]

with the centered representative chosen by

\[
s_p=\min(r_p,p-1-r_p).
\]

The two branches are determined by whether the reflection term is taken on the left or right side of the middle:

- D: direct branch, index r;
- R: reflected branch, index p-1-r.

A fully transverse pair/triple requires distinct primes and that each prime divides its own Apéry term while no cross divisibility occurs.

## 2. Edge orientation audit

The signed coordinate ordering gives three possible pair orientations:

- DD: both primes are on the direct side;
- RR: both are reflected;
- DR: one direct and one reflected.

For a fixed gap vector, the mixed branch equations force an affine congruence between the two signed coordinates. Along a fixed residue fibre there are at most d possible transitions. Hence:

\[
\#\{DR\text{ edges}\}\le d.
\]

This is a local graph statement, not a density estimate.

The middle case occurs only when a coordinate equals its reflection:

\[
r=p-1-r \pmod p,
\]

which gives at most two boundary possibilities. Thus the "two middle edges" assertion is correct only after explicitly excluding the endpoints of the interval and the non-prime middle collision.

## 3. DD/RR folded gap laws

The folded coordinate satisfies

\[
s=\min(r,p-1-r).
\]

For DD one obtains differences of the form

\[
s_p-s_{p'}=(p'-p)q+O(1),
\]

while RR gives the reflected version

\[
s_p-s_{p'}=-(p'-p)q+O(1).
\]

The exact sign depends on the chosen orientation convention. Any statement of LGTQ-2 must therefore quantify over the chosen h and its sign. The repository formulation needs one explicit convention; otherwise DD and RR can be silently double counted.

## 4. LGTQ-2 versus LGTQ-2-SB

The implication

\[
\mathrm{LGTQ\text{-}2\text{-}SB}\Rightarrow\mathrm{LGTQ\text{-}2}
\]

is plausible if SB includes:

1. every fixed q;
2. every fixed C;
3. every gap parameter h with |h|<=C log n;
4. the DD, RR, DR, and middle branches;
5. the same transversality exclusions.

The converse is not automatic. A compressed SB statistic can lose mixed terms. If SB centers only one branch indicator, it does not control correlations

\[
(I_D-\mu_D)(I_R-\mu_R).
\]

A countermodel is to put all mass in perfectly correlated DD/RR pairs while keeping each centered one-variable sum small. The missing mixed covariance remains large.

Therefore SB is either:

- sufficient if it retains the full signed branch covariance;
- strictly stronger if it only keeps one centered indicator;
- not equivalent in the latter formulation.

## 5. Apéry identities do not finish DD/RR

The available recurrence and reflection identities give exact transformations:

\[
b_{p-1-j}\equiv b_j \pmod p
\]

and corresponding reflected boundary units.

They do not provide an unconditional estimate for the number of primes satisfying simultaneous moving congruences. Applying the recurrence repeatedly to a finite packet still produces the product of packet primes; it does not reduce multiplicity.

Hence a claim that DD or RR is already bounded by canonical Apéry identities is a renamed target, not a proof.

## 6. Weakest useful aggregate replacement

A weaker sufficient statement is an averaged branch-covariance bound:

For every fixed q,C,

\[
\sum_{N<n\le2N}
\max_{|h|\le C\log N}
|\mathcal E_{q,h}(n)|
=o(N^2/\log^2N),
\]

where \(\mathcal E_{q,h}\) is the full centered DD+RR+DR covariance residual, not one indicator.

This is weaker than LGTQ-3 because it only controls pairs averaged over n and gaps. It is stronger than current moving-target discrepancy because it retains only the arithmetic correlations needed for pair energy.

## Final corrections

1. Replace "LGTQ-2 iff LGTQ-2-SB" by a one-way implication unless SB explicitly contains all mixed branch terms.
2. State the h maximum after fixing q and C.
3. Remove any DD/RR unconditional conclusion from Apéry identities alone.
4. The best next lemma is the averaged full branch covariance estimate above; proving it would close the pair-energy route without proving full LGTQ-3.
