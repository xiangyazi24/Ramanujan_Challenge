# 1. Audit of reciprocal-gap proof

Let a fiber be
\[
S_q=\{r_1<\cdots<r_m\}
\]
with consecutive gaps \(g_i=r_{i+1}-r_i\). Since adjacent repeats are forbidden,
\(g_i\ge2\).

Define
\[
T_q=\sum_i g_i^{-2}.
\]
If \(u_d\) counts consecutive gaps equal to \(d\), then \(u_d\le C_d\le3(d-1)\), hence
\[
\sum_qT_q\le3\sum_{d=2}^{p-1}\frac{d-1}{d^2}=O(\log p).
\]
This is correct. The endpoint issue is harmless: every consecutive pair is counted among all equal-label pairs with difference \(d\).

For a fiber of diameter at most \(L\), Holder gives
\[
m-1=\sum g_i^{2/3}g_i^{-2/3}
\le L^{2/3}T_q^{1/3}.
\]
This is correct.

Partition into blocks of length \(H\). For a block containing fiber points, the same Holder argument with span at most \(H\) gives
\[
(t-1)^3\le H^2T_{q,j}.
\]
No missing boundary term occurs because the proof only applies to consecutive fiber gaps whose two endpoints lie in the block. Cross-block pairs are handled separately by the adjacent-block estimate.

The estimate
\[
P_q(H)\le3m_q+3H^{4/3}J^{1/3}T_q^{2/3}
\]
is valid. The factor \(J^{1/3}\) comes from concavity:
\[
\sum_jT_{q,j}^{2/3}\le J^{1/3}(\sum_jT_{q,j})^{2/3}.
\]

Finally,
\[
A_p^{nw}(K)\le\sum_qm_qP_q(H)
\]
is valid after dropping the lower bound on the far gap. Using
\[
m_q\le1+p^{2/3}T_q^{1/3}
\]
and summing gives the stated
\[
A_p^{nw}(K)\ll p^{3/2}\log p.
\]

No endpoint, exponent, or block-crossing defect was found.

# 2. Can the logarithm be removed from only the four inputs?

The present method cannot remove it. The only global information on consecutive gaps is
\[
\sum_qT_q\ll\log p.
\]
Every subsequent Holder step consumes this quantity. To remove the logarithm one would need an additional fact such as

\[
\sum_qT_q=O(1),
\]

or a stronger weighted energy estimate controlling the distribution of the \(T_q\)'s.

The pair-cap extremal construction shows that reflection plus fixed-gap caps alone leave nearly quadratic short-window freedom. Therefore no argument using only pair caps and reflection can be expected to create the missing arithmetic cancellation.

I cannot prove that a logarithmic lower bound is attainable under all five hypotheses. The current data prove only:

* upper bound: \(A_p^{nw}(K)\ll p^{3/2}\log p\);
* no known improvement to \(O(p^{3/2})\) from these axioms;
* no valid logarithmic-loss counterexample.

# 3. Repair of Q7220 countermodel

The Q7220 construction needs one clarification: the large packet cannot simply be called one color on a subset. It must be completed to a full labeling.

The repair is:

1. Use the two reflected packets
\[
\mathcal P\cup(p-1-\mathcal P)
\]
from `pair_cap_extremal.tex` as a distinguished fiber.

2. Assign every complementary reflection orbit a fresh unique label.

3. Assign the middle point \((p-1)/2\) its own label.

Then:

* reflection symmetry holds globally;
* no adjacent equal labels holds globally;
* fixed-gap caps hold because the only nontrivial fiber is the packet fiber and its pair counts satisfy \(A(h)\le3(h-1)\); fresh fibers contribute at most one pair at their reflection gap;
* the full fiber-square energy is actually smaller than required because the distinguished fiber has size \(\asymp p^{2/3}\) and all other fibers have size at most two.

For
\[
K=\frac{\sqrt p}{\sqrt{\log p}},
\]
the packet contains \(\asymp p^{2/3}\) short consecutive four-windows. Each has a fiber size contribution \(\asymp p^{2/3}\), yielding
\[
A_p^{nw}(K)\asymp p^{4/3}
\]
for the raw packet construction, not automatically \(p^{3/2}\).

Therefore the stronger Q7220 claim \(\Theta(p^{3/2})\) is not established by the packet construction as written. The repaired construction is a valid obstruction to stronger claims from pair caps, but its verified scale is below the requested \(p^{3/2}\).
