\begin{conjecture}[Quenched triangular all-level L2 wall]\label{wall:l2}
The master WALL statement holds for
\[
\Delta_p(r,h)=b_r c_{r+h}-c_r b_{r+h}.
\]
For every admissible collection of boxes and product weights, the weighted
all-level triangular correlation has the square-root L2 bound predicted by the
quenched sieve. Its shadows are MESO-PAIR, T3/ATR, the sigma-ladder endpoint,
BLOCK-SUBQUAD, GPRV, and COINC. WALL-zero implies all these consequences except
COINC. Every all-level statement is subject to the gauge convention: the
chosen gauge must be fixed before translating zeros into incidences.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Galois/FAV wall]\label{wall:galois}
For the Artin representations attached to the gap family $M_h$ one has
\[
\sum_{h\leq p}|\operatorname{Tr}\rho_h(\operatorname{Frob}_p)-E_h|^2=O(p).
\]
GRH alone is insufficient, since the conductor contribution is of size at
least $p^5$. GRH together with this FAV estimate implies GPRV. After lifting,
this is equivalent to a bounded-conductor second moment subject to the
P1--P5 lift conditions.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Index-Mellin comparison wall]\label{wall:crystal}
There exists a finite-rank space $T$, fixed operators $\Phi_p$, and a
correspondence $K$ such that
\[
 C_h-\mu_h=\operatorname{Tr}(K\Phi_p^h|T)+e_h,
\qquad \sum_h|e_h|^2=O(p).
\]
The verified depth tower
$p^3c[p+v]=(p^3c[p])K_v(p)\pmod {p^7}$
provides index-cocycle evidence. The missing step is the cocycle-to-single-
Frobenius comparison: identifying index Frobenius with the $t$-Frobenius.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Clock compression carrier wall]\label{wall:carrier}
The universal two-clock carrier exists: the flat $\mathrm{GL}_2\times
\mathrm{GL}_2$ transfer cocycle
\[
U_{m+g}(X)=U_g(X+m)U_m(X)

exists and balanced incidence is a fixed codimension-two determinantal/
Plucker condition. The missing ingredient is not rank but compression of clock
complexity: an Apery-specific quotient with bounded-complexity index
translation. Possible mechanisms include higher-order bispectrality, Cartier
semilinearity, Mellin characters, or mixed-Frobenius inner shifts.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[Mesoscopic normalization wall]\label{wall:combinatorial}

$\mathrm{MIDPOINT\text{-}AC}_\rho$.
\end{conjecture}
