# Q7933 audit: anchored-star output-prime mixed energy

## Status

The claimed regression theorem is **not derivable from the currently stated HM3 ingredients alone**. The weighted Cauchy shortcut is valid once a positive lower bound for the total anchored correlation is available, but the required lower bound is not a consequence of individual positivity of the output-prime terms. The missing step is exactly a quantitative global positivity statement for the anchored star correlation.

What follows is the exact implication chain.

## 1. Exact normalization

Let the anchored model have the fixed anchor zero condition at \(m_0\). Write

\[
E_p^*=\sum_{m<M} g_p^*(m)\bigl((G^*(m))^2-G_2^*(m)-2G^*(m)g_p^*(m)+2(g_p^*(m))^2\bigr).
\]

The ordered pair convention is important: the square of \(G^*\) expands over ordered pairs of the two non-output primes. Thus

\[
\sum_p E_p^*=C_X^{\times,*}
\]

with no additional factor \(1/2\). Passing to unordered pairs would insert a factor \(2\), but the proposition uses ordered semiprime blocks.

The weighted quantity is

\[
S_X^*=\sum_{p:Z_p^*>0}\frac p{Z_p^*}(E_p^*)_+^2.
\]

Since \(Z_p^*/p=z_p^*\), the weight is exactly \(1/z_p^*\).

## 2. Weighted Cauchy implication

Let

\[
A_p=(E_p^*)_+.
\]

Then

\[
\sum_p A_p\ge \sum_p E_p^*=C_X^{\times,*}
\]

provided the negative part is controlled or absent. In general this implication is false: cancellation between positive and negative \(E_p^*\) can make the total small while \(S_X^*\) is large or small.

If one has the additional theorem

\[
C_X^{\times,*}\ge c X^3(\lambda_X^*)^3,
\]

and the already-used HM3 upper bound

\[
\sum_p \frac{Z_p^*}{p}\ll X\lambda_X^*,
\]

then weighted Cauchy gives

\[
(C_X^{\times,*})^2
\le
\left(\sum_p\frac p{Z_p^*}A_p^2\right)
\left(\sum_p\frac{Z_p^*}{p}\right).
\]

Hence

\[
S_X^*
\gg
\frac{X^6(\lambda_X^*)^6}{X\lambda_X^*}
=X^5(\lambda_X^*)^5.
\]

Compared with the target normalization

\[
X^4(\lambda_X^*)^5,
\]

this violates the target by a factor \(X\).

## 3. The missing theorem

The required input is not

\[
E_p^*>0\quad\forall p.
\]

That statement is neither proved nor needed. The weakest sufficient replacement is:

**Anchored positivity hypothesis.** There exists \(c>0\) such that

\[
C_X^{\times,*}=\sum_pE_p^*\ge cX^3(\lambda_X^*)^3.
\]

Together with the existing density bound this closes the argument.

This hypothesis is strictly weaker than termwise positivity.

## 4. Why the anchor alone is insufficient

The anchor contributes a deterministic main term to the physical correlation, but the remaining primes contribute signed fluctuations. The identity

\[
\sum_pE_p^*=C_X^{\times,*}
\]

contains cancellation across output primes. A large positive contribution at the anchored point does not by itself imply that the full sum survives after subtracting:

- the \(G_2\) diagonal;
- the self-output corrections;
- non-anchor physical points;
- ordered-pair fluctuation terms.

Therefore a proof must explicitly estimate these errors.

## 5. Finite verification protocol

A finite check should verify only identities, not the asymptotic theorem. The following check confirms conventions:

```python
from itertools import product

# rows[p] is the explicit anchored star indicator array
# pset is the active prime list

M = len(next(iter(rows.values())))

z = {p: sum(rows[p])/M for p in pset}
g = {p: [rows[p][m]-z[p] for m in range(M)] for p in pset}

G = [sum(g[p][m] for p in pset) for m in range(M)]
G2 = [sum(g[p][m]**2 for p in pset) for m in range(M)]

E = {}
for p in pset:
    E[p] = sum(
        g[p][m]*(G[m]**2-G2[m]-2*G[m]*g[p][m]+2*g[p][m]**2)
        for m in range(M)
    )

C = sum(E.values())
print(C, sum(E[p] for p in pset))
```

The equality checked is exact. It does not establish a positive lower bound.

## Final proposition (merge-safe)

**Proposition.** The anchored-star HM3 regression reduces to the single quantitative statement

\[
C_X^{\times,*}\gg X^3(\lambda_X^*)^3.
\]

Assuming this statement and the previously established anchored zero-set density estimate, one obtains

\[
S_X^*\gg X^5(\lambda_X^*)^5,
\]

which exceeds the HM3 sufficient scale \(X^4(\lambda_X^*)^5\) by a factor \(X\).

Without that global positivity estimate, the anchored point and rowwise facts alone do not imply the regression theorem.

The power violation is therefore conditional on a positive total anchored correlation theorem; it is not a consequence of the existing HM3 propositions alone.
