# DSEL3 translation fibre audit

## Normalization

For fixed endpoint bit E0, ordered signs s=(s1,s2,s3), and span Delta, define M_X(E0,s,Delta) to be the number of ordered triples

\[
(p_i,z_i,c_i)_{i=1}^3
\]

with distinct shell primes X<p_i<=2X, z_i in Z_{p_i}, regular signed parameterization

\[
m=c_i p_i+z_i,
\]

where c_i lies in [0,X-1] on the positive branch and [1,X-1] on the negative branch, the sign endpoint data is E0,s, and

\[
\Delta=\max_i(c_ip_i)-\min_i(c_ip_i).
\]

The desired estimate is

\[
\max_{E_0,s,\Delta}M_X(E_0,s,\Delta)\le X^{1+o(1)}\lambda_X^3,
\]

where

\[
\lambda_X=\sum_{X<p\le 2X}|Z_p|/p.
\]

## Exact reduction

The scalar span map is insufficient: equal spans occur for distinct triples, so injectivity fails. The affine determinant also contributes no information because it vanishes identically. The selector count therefore cannot prove the fibre estimate.

Introduce pair-defect coordinates

\[
d_{ij}=c_ip_i-c_jp_j.
\]

For fixed span and signs these satisfy

\[
|d_{ij}|\le X^2,
\]

and the three defects satisfy one linear relation

\[
d_{12}+d_{23}+d_{31}=0.
\]

The remaining arithmetic constraints are

\[
c_ip_i+z_i=c_jp_j+z_j=m,
\]

hence

\[
d_{ij}=z_j-z_i.
\]

Therefore a fibre bound is equivalent to controlling simultaneous mixed-characteristic correlations of the form

\[
\#\{(p,q):z_p-z_q=d\},
\]
with the third prime constrained by the remaining defect equation.

## Smaller genuine gateway

A sufficient strictly smaller statement is the pair-defect energy estimate:

For every nonzero integer d with |d|\le X^2,

\[
\sum_{X<p,q\le2X}\#\{(z_p,z_q)\in Z_p\times Z_q:z_q-z_p=d\}
\ll X^{1+o(1)}\lambda_X^2.
\]

Together with the third-coordinate lifting bound

\[
\max_d \#\{(\ell,z_\ell):z_\ell-z_q=d'\}\ll X^{o(1)}\lambda_X,
\]
this gives the required DSEL3 estimate. This is not equivalent to the total third moment because the span/sign fibre fixes additive defect coordinates.

## Obstruction status

No complete proof of the pair-defect correlation lemma follows from the supplied determinant or span data. The surviving obstruction is therefore a mixed-characteristic additive-energy estimate for Apéry zero sets, not a selector-counting issue.

The exact X=1024 equal-sign/span collision already disproves the stronger scalar-fibre claim, but does not disprove the defect-coordinate gateway.

## Claim

DSEL3 reduces to the quantified pair-defect energy lemma above. Establishing that lemma would imply the required normalization; without it the full DSEL3 bound remains open.
