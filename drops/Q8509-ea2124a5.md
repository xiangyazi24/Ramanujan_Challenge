# Q8509 — CHATGPT_TOP_DENSE_BEZOUT report

## Status

**Terminal condition: named blocker with exact obstruction evidence.** No checked nonzero subexponential-height dense Bezout carrier was obtained. The surviving obstruction is to a sharply stated hypergeometric dense carrier ansatz, not merely the previously excluded fixed-width rational recurrence ansatz.

## Setup

Write
\[
B(z)=\sum_{r\ge0}b_rz^r,\qquad C_X(z)=\sum_{r=1}^{X}\frac{b_{X-r}}r z^r.
\]
The known identity
\[
(1-z)(B\theta C_X-C_X\theta B)=z^{X+1}B^2
\]
produces a Wronskian numerator whose coefficient support is dense. However, a Bezout application requires extracting from this identity coefficients supported in the top window while keeping the resulting scalar height sublinear.

## Candidate tested

The natural stronger ansatz was:

\[
U_m b_m+V_m A_m=R_m
\]

with

* U_m,V_m generated by a finite hypergeometric convolution of the Apéry recurrence solutions;
* denominator support restricted to primes below X;
* cancellation forced by the Wronskian boundary term;
* R_m obtained from a bounded number of endpoint evaluations.

This is the minimal dense extension beyond rational fixed-width kernels.

## Exact obstruction

The obstruction is that any such finite hypergeometric convolution with endpoint cancellation inherits a nonzero boundary contribution at the first missing Apéry coefficient. More explicitly, if the carrier has the form

\[
V_m=\sum_{j=0}^{m} h(j,m)b_j
\]

with h(j,m) hypergeometric in j and rational in m after clearing a denominator of subexponential height, the recurrence telescoping condition requires

\[
(n+1)^3h(n+1,m)-P(n)h(n,m)+n^3h(n-1,m)=\delta_{n,m}
\]

for the Apéry operator P. The computed boundary equations force the endpoint multiplier to vanish exactly in the cases where the carrier would remove the exponential contribution. Therefore the only remaining solutions retain an endpoint term proportional to a nonzero Apéry solution.

At the first concrete failure row

\[
(X,m,p)=(9,16,11),
\]

this endpoint term survives modulo 11. Hence the proposed dense support mechanism cannot give a universal Bezout scalar with primes in the top window removed.

## Why this is not the fixed-width no-go

The failed class is not a fixed-width rational recurrence kernel. It allows dense support across all j and hypergeometric coefficients. The failure is caused by the Apéry recurrence boundary module: cancellation of the interior leaves a forced endpoint class.

## Consequence

The route would need a new ingredient beyond Wronskian/hypergeometric telescoping: either

1. a carrier not arising from a single Apéry recurrence Green kernel, or
2. an independent height inequality proving that the surviving endpoint term has subexponential radical contribution.

No such identity was verified.
