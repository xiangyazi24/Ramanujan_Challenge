## 1. Exact Euler transform

Define

$$
A_0=\frac12,\qquad O_0=1,\qquad B_k=A_kO_k,
$$

$$
A_{k+1}=A_k\frac{k+1}{2k+3},\qquad
O_{k+1}=O_k+\frac1{2k+3},
$$

and

$$
S_K=\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}B_k.
$$

For one P25 column, write

$$
E=qS_K-p,\qquad H=qB_K,\qquad J=qA_K.
$$

One Euler step, from `K=k` to `K=k+1`, is

$$
E^+=E+H,
$$

$$
H^+=\frac{k+1}{2k+3}H+
     \frac{k+1}{(2k+3)^2}J,
$$

$$
J^+=\frac{k+1}{2k+3}J.
$$

Therefore the correct **row-state** transition is

$$
\boxed{
L_k=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
1&\dfrac{k+1}{2k+3}&0\\
0&\dfrac{k+1}{(2k+3)^2}&\dfrac{k+1}{2k+3}
\end{pmatrix}.}
\tag{1}
$$

Indeed,

$$
(E,H,J)L_k=(E^+,H^+,J^+).
$$

The upper-triangular transpose of (1) is the column-state matrix; it must not be used on the right of a row vector.

\boxed{

## 4. Exact reduction to one barycentric coordinate

The existing Lean development proves strict ordering of the three positive ratios:

$$
r_{n,0}>r_{n,1}>r_{n,2},
\qquad
r_{n,j}=\frac{p_{n,j}}{q_{n,j}}.
\tag{12}
$$

Set

$$
w_n=r_{n,0}-r_{n,2}>0
$$

and define

$$
\boxed{
t_n=\frac{S_{6n+12}-r_{n,2}}{w_n}.}
\tag{13}
$$

Then

$$
E_{n,0}=q_{n,0}(S_{6n+12}-r_{n,0})
       =q_{n,0}w_n(t_n-1),

$$
E_{n,2}=q_{n,2}(S_{6n+12}-r_{n,2})

Since the denominators and `w_n` are positive,

$$
\boxed{

This removes eight irrelevant sign coordinates from the logical target.

## 5. Why ordinary moving rational polyhedra cannot close

The decisive feature of (16) is

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial t_{n+1}}{\partial t_n}=\frac1{d_n}.}
\tag{19}
$$

The exact Poincare characters of the normalized P25 module are

$$
\Lambda=17+12\sqrt2,
\qquad 1,
\qquad \Lambda^{-1}=17-12\sqrt2.
$$

The denominator row is in the dominant `Lambda` mode, while a nonzero ratio width is an exterior-square quantity with dominant character `Lambda`. Dividing by two dominant denominators gives

$$
\boxed{
 d_n=\frac{w_{n+1}}{w_n}\longrightarrow
 \Lambda^{-1}=17-12\sqrt2.}
\tag{20}
$$

The existing strict minor cone ensures that the relevant exterior-square coefficient is nonzero, so this is the actual width asymptotic, not only a formal possibility.

Combining (19) and (20),

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\partial t_{n+1}}{\partial t_n}
\longrightarrow17+12\sqrt2>1.}
\tag{21}
$$

### No-go theorem

Consider a family of full-dimensional convex polyhedra `C_n` in any rationally balanced state containing `t`, with the following properties:

1. every facet coefficient and offset is a rational function of `n`;
2. near the limiting denominator/exterior-square state, the fiber of `C_n` in the `t` direction has positive length `ell_n`;
3. the fiber does not collapse identically onto a codimension-one graph.

For rational-function facet data, every nonzero fiber length has

$$
\frac{\ell_{n+1}}{\ell_n}\longrightarrow1.
\tag{22}
$$

Fix the other state variables in the interior of their limiting cross-section. On that vertical fiber, (16) is affine and its image length is exactly `ell_n/d_n`. Forward invariance would require

$$
\ell_{n+1}\ge\frac{\ell_n}{d_n}.
$$

Taking limits and using (20) gives

$$
1\ge17+12\sqrt2,
$$

which is impossible.

Hence:

$$
\boxed{
\text{No full-dimensional moving rational polyhedral cone of
polynomial-scale thickness can prove the Euler squeeze.}}
\tag{23}
$$

This includes fixed and rationally moving rectangles, triangles, simplicial cones, and finite-facet boxes in any finite polynomial balancing of the direct error coordinates.

A possible invariant set must instead be one of the following:

- a codimension-one stable graph with zero `t`-fiber;
- an exponentially thin strip whose width is `O((17-12sqrt(2))^n)`;
- a non-polyhedral analytic comparison identity.

The first two are not describable by nonzero rational-function facet widths.

## 7. The smallest surviving exact alternative

Equation (16) shows precisely what remains to be certified. There is a unique unstable/stable selection problem for the barycentric coordinate. Formally, an invariant graph

$$
t=\Phi_n(x,y,X,\lambda,\rho)
$$

must satisfy

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_{n+1}(x',y',X',\lambda',\rho')
=
\frac{\Phi_n(x,y,X,\lambda,\rho)
 +(U_n+V_n\rho)\lambda-\alpha_{n,2}}
 {\alpha_{n,0}-\alpha_{n,2}}.}
\tag{24}
$$

Equivalently, the backward equation is

$$
\boxed{
\Phi_n=
\alpha_{n,2}-(U_n+V_n\rho)\lambda
+d_n\Phi_{n+1}.}
\tag{25}
$$

Backward iteration is contracting because `0<d_n<2/3` is already available from the ratio-envelope contraction. Thus (25) defines an exact stable graph. What is missing is a closed positive representation of this graph that proves

$$
0<\Phi_n<1.
$$

Finding such a representation is mathematically equivalent to the remaining Catalan connection. A rational polyhedral thickening cannot replace it because of (21)--(23).
