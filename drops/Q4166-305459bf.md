For \(r=t+6d\), equality with the t-branch gives

\[
(k-\ell)q+(6-k)d=0,

For minus,

Define \(\mathscr A_{\rm fix}\) as the class of universal symbolic constructions obtained by a bounded number of:

On every legal noncentral residual state outside the fixed exceptional primes:

The first algebraic class not covered by Theorem 8.1 is a **linear-size growing-prefix marked transfer module**.
