# Conditional route: large Frobenius order implies the needed moment bound

Assume that for all but \(O(x/\log^2x)\) ordinary primes \(p\le x\), the Hecke eigenvalue satisfies

\[
\operatorname{ord}_p(a_p(f))\ge p^{1/2+\varepsilon}.
\]

For the ordinary Frobenius representation of \(f=\eta(2z)^4\eta(4z)^4\), let \(\alpha_p\) be the unit-root eigenvalue. The projective Apéry transfer orbit has period controlled by
\[
d_p=\operatorname{ord}_p(\alpha_p).
\]
The hypothesis gives \(d_p\gg p^{1/2+\varepsilon}\). Since zeros are visits of this cyclic orbit to a fixed point of \(\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p)\),
\[
|Z_p|\ll p/d_p+1\ll p^{1/2-\varepsilon}.
\]

Now suppose generally \(|Z_p|\ll p^c\). The atom moment estimate has the shape
\[
X^{-2}\sum_{m<X^2}(K_X(m))_k\ll \mu_X^k+X^{c+2/k-1+o(1)},
\]
where the second term is the CRT atom contribution. The condition for the atom term to disappear is
\[
c+2/k<1.
\]
With \(c=1/2-\varepsilon\), this becomes
\[
1/2-\varepsilon+2/k<1,
\]
so
\[
k>4/(1+2\varepsilon).
\]
Hence \(k=4\) suffices for every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\). Therefore the conditional orbit-order statement implies the required \((HM)_4\) fourth moment input.

The remaining issue is not the implication \(d_p\Rightarrow Z_p\); that is the cyclic orbit argument. The hard arithmetic input is proving that the Frobenius eigenvalue has large order for almost all primes. This requires a strong Artin/Chebotarev statement for the compatible system attached to \(f\), beyond a formal application of GRH alone.

The conditional chain is therefore:

```text
GRH + Artin/large-image theorem
        -> ord_p(alpha_p) >= p^(1/2+epsilon)
        -> Z_p <= p^(1/2-epsilon)
        -> (HM)_4
        -> fourth moment sieve controls atoms
        -> H(n)=o(n/log n)
        -> log G_n=o(n).
```

Thus the proposed route is a valid conditional strategy: the exponent \(1/2\) is exactly the threshold where the fourth moment becomes strong enough because the CRT atom obstruction contributes the term \(X^{c+2/k-1}\).
