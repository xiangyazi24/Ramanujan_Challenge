# Verdict: PARTIAL

The slowly varying cocycle idea in its archimedean form is not viable at fixed p. The useful surviving object is not local diagonalization, but an exact projective/Wronskian transport invariant that converts long returns into intersections of algebraic graphs with a controlled recurrence complexity.

## 1. Why archimedean slow variation does not transfer

The estimates alpha_{s+1}-alpha_s = O(1/s) and beta_{s+1}-beta_s = O(1/s) are estimates in a chosen real embedding of integer representatives. They do not define a metric structure on F_p.

The obstruction is:

1. There is no canonical small lift of r in F_p. A residue class has representatives of many archimedean sizes.
2. A window of length sqrt(p) in the integer interval is not a small ball in the finite field. Addition wraps globally.
3. Eigenvalues of M_s can resonate in F_p even when the real matrices M_s appear close.
4. A product error estimate of the form product(I+E_s) requires a norm and ordering-compatible topology. F_p has no such topology.

Therefore a p-adic/archimedean adiabatic theorem cannot directly control T_{r,d}.

## 2. The exact replacement invariant

The surviving algebraic object is the projective displacement scalar

D_{r,d}(v)=det(v,T_{r,d}v).

For v=xi_r,

D_{r,d}(xi_r)=0 iff T_{r,d}xi_r=xi_r.

Using the companion recurrence, this is exactly the Casoratian numerator condition

Delta_{r,d}=N_d(r)/prod_{j=1}^d(r+j)^3.

This is stronger than a generic cocycle return condition because the numerator family satisfies the addition law and the dual recurrence in the d-direction.

A concrete experiment should compute, for d in (D/2,D], the normalized polynomial

R_d(r)=prod_{j=1}^d(r+j)^3 Delta_{r,d}

and test whether the coefficient vectors of R_d exhibit rank collapse under d-shifts. Any rank defect would be a genuine ordered-cocycle invariant.

## 3. A plausible theorem weaker than the goal

Proposed invariant:

For a fixed small k, the span over F_p of

{ N_{d+i}(r) : 0 <= i <= k }

has dimension at most 2 after restriction to the solution space generated by the two Apery continuants.

If proved uniformly for d in the mesoscopic range, then a return at d and a return at d+1 impose a bounded-degree algebraic relation independent of r's orbit value.

This does not yet give D^(2-eta), but it is strictly stronger than bounded-window elimination because it couples growing heights.

## 4. Why p-adic lifting also fails

A p-adic lift would require lifting the orbit and matrices to Z_p and comparing nearby parameters. The obstruction is that the parameter s is only defined modulo p and the interval s=1,...,p-2 is not a p-adic neighborhood. The Frobenius action dominates any naive Taylor expansion.

The only meaningful p-adic statement is the recurrence congruence itself, not a small perturbation expansion.

## 5. Alternative ordered-cocycle experiment

Machine-checkable test:

Compute the 2x2 transfer products T_{r,d} and form the commutator defect

C(r,d,e)=T_{r,d} T_{r+d,e} - T_{r,e+d}.

Although the cocycle identity gives equality after correct indexing, the induced projective displacement between different decompositions can be measured by the rank of the coefficient matrices after clearing denominators.

A low-rank pattern would provide a candidate invariant controlling growing-gap correlations.

## 6. Route assessment

Dead:
- archimedean slowly varying diagonalization;
- p-adic adiabatic approximation on the residue interval;
- eigenvalue perturbation arguments.

Viable but unproved:
- recurrence-theoretic low-rank identities among N_d;
- projective displacement/Wronskian correlation estimates;
- a growing-gap trace identity from the companion recurrence.

The target theorem should not be phrased as "the cocycle changes slowly". It should be phrased as a bounded-complexity statement about the algebra generated by the continuant family.

Concrete next computation:

For primes p up to a large bound, compute the matrix rank of the family of coefficient vectors of N_d(x), N_{d+1}(x),...,N_{d+k}(x) after normalization by the forced linear factors for even d. A stable rank-2 pattern would be evidence for the missing ordered invariant.

(i) Least confident step: the proposed low-rank coefficient-family phenomenon. It is a testable conjectural invariant, not a proved consequence of the recurrence.

(ii) Possible blind spot: the question may over-focus on local return rigidity. The missing anti-concentration may live in global Frobenius-compatible identities between many gap levels, not in individual return events.
