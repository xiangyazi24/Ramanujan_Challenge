## 7. Scoped obstruction theorem

The computation illustrates a general obstruction for this exact quadratic construction.

### Proposition

Let a three-hit q=1 row be written in Q2621 base coordinates

```text
Y_i=a_i U-c_i S,
```

and suppose the three bridge minors have the form

```text
Delta_ij=p0*delta_ij
```

with each `delta_ij` a unit at all marked primes. Then

```text
F=product_(i<j) Delta_ij
```

has marked valuation exactly `3` at `p0` and `0` at the other two marked primes. After common denominator clearing the quadratic Veronese pair is

```text
N_F*(U^2,S^2).
```

If `gcd(U,S)=1`, its primitive coefficient normalization is exactly

```text
(U^2,S^2),
```

and the primitive sum is `U^2+S^2`.

Therefore any marked support of the raw scalar `F` is pure coefficient content. The primitive nonlinear carrier carries a marked prime `p` **only if the endpoint state itself satisfies**

```text
U^2+S^2=0 mod p.
```

No gcd with a separate target-preserving endpoint rectangle can change a marked unit into a marked zero.

### Application to n=321

All hypotheses hold. Moreover `179` and `211` are `3 mod 4` and `(U,S)` is nonzero modulo both, so the necessary endpoint-state isotropy is impossible at those two primes. The remaining prime `193` also fails by direct exact evaluation.

Hence the Q2621 quadratic sum is not merely unproved: **this concrete carrier cannot carry the actual three-hit row after primitive normalization.**

The first genuinely different nonlinear possibility would have to make the **primitive symmetric-power form itself** target-dependent and vanish in each marked characteristic. A common scalar product of bridge minors is insufficient; its prime support is precisely what primitive normalization removes.

## Final conclusion

The Q2621 quadratic Veronese construction escapes the **linear algebra** no-go, but it does not escape the arithmetic support barrier.

At the actual row `n=321`, the scalar Veronese factor has only the normalization-dependent marked content `211^3`. Primitive normalization removes it completely. The surviving quadratic endpoint form `U^2+S^2` is a unit modulo `179`, `193`, and `211`; indeed it is automatically anisotropic at `179` and `211` for the actual nonzero endpoint state.

Intersecting with the capped endpoint/Newton rectangle therefore has only two outcomes:

```text
raw:       support collapses to the base prime 211;
primitive: support collapses to 1.
```

The endpoint-state height is not converted into a small three-target carrier. The obstruction is exact.
