# Holonomic Stepanov audit

## 1. Single-polynomial auxiliary construction

The available recurrence and symplectic identities do give extra structure, but they do not currently produce a Stepanov degree win. A candidate is to use the return vector

```
V_h(r)=(N_{h-1}(r),N_h(r),N_{h+1}(r))
```
and impose the recurrence relations at every zero of N_h. The zero condition forces one linear relation among adjacent frame coordinates, but the auxiliary products of shifted N-values still have degree comparable to the sum of shifts. The orbit-return condition is a codimension-one event in the projective orbit, not a high-multiplicity zero of a low-degree polynomial.

The C2/window bounds kill long arithmetic progressions inside one color class, but they do not give a Stepanov auxiliary section with enough vanishing order. The candidate is therefore not a proof of R_h << sqrt(p)/omega(p).

## 2. Resultant/discriminant shadow

A large value of R_h means N_h mod p has many F_p-linear factors. The natural invariant

```
Res(N_h, X^p-X)
```
contains p for every root and is useless because it depends on p. A characteristic-zero invariant independent of p would need to measure splitting type, not just root collisions.

The discriminant does not help: many distinct roots modulo p do not force discriminant divisibility. It only detects repeated roots.

Hence the proposed divisor-counting mechanism is quenched unless one finds an additional arithmetic invariant controlling Frobenius splitting.

## Verdict

No abstract proof from the listed holonomic inputs is obtained. The remaining wall is genuinely arithmetic: controlling how often the Apery gap polynomial has many rational roots modulo p.

Least-confident step: whether a hidden Frobenius-trace invariant exists beyond the current p-curvature rank-one data.

Blind spot: confusing root multiplicity/collision invariants with the number of distinct F_p roots. Distinct modular roots are governed by splitting statistics, not discriminants.
