## 5. Two simultaneous critical cells: exact rank-height tradeoff

```text
 = min(v_i(G(alpha_i)),v_i(J(alpha_i)))               (5.2)
```

### 5.2 Keep the cells separate

Use the block-diagonal Sylvester map



But

```text

 = det(Phi_(A,B)) det(Phi_(B,C)),                     (5.6)

 = log |det(Phi_(A,B))|+log |det(Phi_(B,C))|.         (5.7)
```

Thus the two units of `ell` are purchased with two full height blocks. Dividing the height by the two forced valuations improves only an absolute constant.

```text

blockwise independence: two local directions, two height blocks.     (5.8)
```

Neither unequal gaps, two simultaneous cells, six-minor rank, reflected second Smith, nor bounded reuse removes any positive power of this deficit.

## 11. Smallest successor lemma

The lower recurrence algebra has already forgotten the two pieces of information that distinguish the desired rows:

```text
the common lower projective line is the distinguished q0 line;
the reconstructed upper state is an actual q-zero.                  (11.1)
```

The narrowest successor should therefore measure depletion of those two selectors **inside** the already-paid algebraic tail, rather than ask for yet another unmarked resultant.

For a canonical reversal-paired DA4R block `gamma`, let

```text
rho_ell(gamma)
```

be its mod-`ell` Smith corank after every structural/central factor has been removed. Let

```text
TailCharge(P,L)
 = sum_(P<ell<=2P) (log ell)
     sum_gamma (rho_ell(gamma)-1)_+                  (11.2)
```

with the exact source expansion needed to pay the literal native occurrence weights. The proposed arithmetic side is precisely of the form

```text
TailCharge(P,L) << L^3 P^o(1).                       (11.3)
```

Let `MarkedQ0(P,L)` be the left side `(1.1)`, after the same reversal convention, so that both the lower `q0` predicate and the upper affine mark remain present.

### Successor `DA4R-Q0UP-TAIL_(eta)`

For some fixed `eta>0`, uniformly in the supercritical part of the residual band,

```text
MarkedQ0(P,L)
  << P^(-1/10-eta) TailCharge(P,L) P^o(1).            (11.4)
```

This is the smallest clean new statement exposed by the audit:

- it preserves every literal occurrence and native weight;
- it does not identify different roots, gap words, source labels, or characteristics;
- it asks for no saving on unmarked/wrong-state DA4R roots individually;
- it introduces exactly the information absent from the lower continuants;
- it is strictly stronger than the desired physical estimate, because the right side counts all split second-Smith directions, including phantom, wrong-state, upper-dark, and mask-failing roots.

At the peak, `(11.3)` and `(11.4)` give

```text
MarkedQ0(P,P^(1/2))
  << P^(-1/10-eta) P^(3/2+o(1))
   = P^(7/5-eta+o(1)).                               (11.5)
```

For `alpha=3/5+epsilon` with `epsilon<eta`, this is

```text
P^(2-alpha-(eta-epsilon)+o(1)),                       (11.6)
```

which supplies the required fixed margin. Outside the central supercritical band, the original `L^3` or `P^2/L` side already reaches the endpoint scale.

### Stronger coefficient-only alternative

A cleaner but stronger source theorem would be a reversal-paired second-Fitting depletion:

```text
DA4R-FIT2_(eta):

sum_(gamma in the critical L-band) log Tail_2(gamma)
  << P^(7/5-eta+o(1)),                                (11.7)
```

where `Tail_2(gamma)` is the blockwise product of all Smith layers below the top one. Reflection converts each off-center physical orbit into a tail unit, so `(11.7)` implies `(11.5)` without using the upper mark. It is stronger because it also pays every unmarked and phantom duplicate root. The current generic Hadamard/Smith ledger gives only `P^(3/2+o(1))`; Q6452's diffusion configuration shows why no improvement of `(11.7)` is automatic.

### Cross-characteristic alternative

The other genuinely new route is a selected-upper-state overload theorem. If

```text
k_L(q,t)
```

is the literal weighted number of residual DA4R rows mapping to one actual marked upper state, then a factorial bound on

```text
sum_(q,t:z_q(t)=1) (k_L(q,t)-R)_+                     (11.8)
```

or on its second factorial analogue, at the Q6547 scale, would also break the peak. This is not a consequence of lower resultants; it is a new cross-characteristic theorem.
