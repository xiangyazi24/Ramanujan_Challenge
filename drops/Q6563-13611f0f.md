# G_h absolute irreducibility: exact monodromy reduction, a literal h=1 counterexample, and the one-variable critical-value target

Repository state used: current pushed snapshot `c78d36e5141d02bfa7a3c7338ea921d544bc1ebc`, together with the banked continuant structure in `problems/3.2/chatgpt-answers/Q338.md`, the p-adic factor audit in `Q6490.md`, and the orbit-energy ledger. All displayed formulas are ASCII.

## Executive verdict

There are five conclusions.

1. The literal statement "G_h is absolutely irreducible for all h" is false at `h=1`.

```text
A_h(X) = product_{j=1}^{h} (X+j)^3.

f_h(X) = N_h(X)/A_h(X).

R_h(X,Y) = N_h(X)*A_h(Y) - N_h(Y)*A_h(X).

G_h(X,Y) = R_h(X,Y)/(X-Y).
```

For `h=1`,

```text
G_1(X,Y)
  = -[(X+1)^2 + (X+1)*(Y+1) + (Y+1)^2]

  = -(X+1-omega*(Y+1))*(X+1-omega^2*(Y+1)),

omega^2 + omega + 1 = 0.
```

Thus the correct target starts at `h>=2`. The `h=1` term is explicit and harmless for `[L2-FREQ]`.

2. For `h>=2`, absolute irreducibility has an exact known reformulation:

```text
G_h is absolutely irreducible over Q
    iff
geometric_monodromy(f_h) is 2-transitive.
```

This is the off-diagonal fiber-product criterion for a rational function. It is the right theorem to use; there is no general theorem saying that a nondegenerate second-order recurrence automatically has irreducible value-equality curves.

3. I do not obtain an all-`h` proof today. I do obtain a concrete single-variable sufficient lemma which is substantially weaker than full Morse genericity and is directly machine-certifiable:

```text
[CRIT-2H]

For every h>=2, the rational map f_h has at least 2*h-1
simple nonzero critical values.
```

I prove below that `[CRIT-2H]` implies that `f_h` is indecomposable; one simple critical value then gives a transposition in primitive monodromy, hence full symmetric monodromy and absolute irreducibility of `G_h`.

4. `[CRIT-2H]` is computable without factoring the bivariate `G_h`. It is a gcd/subresultant statement for one polynomial of degree `4*h-4`. This is the sharpest useful minimal remaining lemma I can isolate.

5. Characteristic-zero absolute irreducibility does not by itself give a uniform `h<=p^delta` theorem. An effective height argument gives only a polylogarithmic deterministic window of the shape

```text
h <= c*(log(p)/loglog(p))^(1/3),
```

assuming the all-`h` critical certificate. A positive-power window requires direct mod-`p` nonvanishing of the same critical subresultants, not ordinary height/Bertini specialization.

# 1. Exact rational-map normalization

Define

```text
P(X) = 34*X^3 + 51*X^2 + 27*X + 5.

N_1(X) = 1.
N_2(X) = P(X+1).

N_(h+1)(X)
  = P(X+h)*N_h(X) - (X+h)^6*N_(h-1)(X).

A_h(X) = product_{j=1}^{h} (X+j)^3.

f_h(X) = N_h(X)/A_h(X).
```

The banked leading-coefficient recurrence gives

```text
lc(N_h) = U_h(34,1) != 0,

deg(N_h) = 3*(h-1),

deg(A_h) = 3*h,

deg(f_h) = 3*h,

f_h(X) = lc(N_h)*X^(-3) + O(X^(-4)) at infinity.
```

## 1.1 Exact values at every apparent pole

A useful all-`h` identity, stronger than merely saying `gcd(N_h,A_h)=1`, is

```text
N_h(-j)
  = (-1)^(j-1)
    * ((j-1)!)^3
    * ((h-j)!)^3
    * b[j-1]
    * b[h-j],

1 <= j <= h.
```

Proof. Use the tridiagonal Dirichlet determinant for `N_h`. At `X=-j`, the off-diagonal entry containing `(X+j)^3` vanishes and splits the path determinant into two blocks. The right block is the standard endpoint determinant

```text
((h-j)!)^3*b[h-j],
```

and reversing the left block gives

```text
(-1)^(j-1)*((j-1)!)^3*b[j-1].
```

Their product is the displayed formula.

Since every `b[n]` is a positive integer,

```text
N_h(-j) != 0 over Q,

gcd(N_h,A_h) = 1,
```

so `f_h` really has `h` distinct poles, each of local degree `3`. It also has a zero of local degree `3` at infinity.

This pole profile is a load-bearing part of the indecomposability argument below.

# 2. The exact general theorem: monodromy, not the recurrence by itself

For a reduced rational function

```text
f(X) = F_1(X)/F_2(X),
```

the off-diagonal fiber product is

```text
H_f(X,Y)
  = [F_1(X)*F_2(Y)-F_1(Y)*F_2(X)]/(X-Y).
```

A theorem of Pakovich gives

```text
H_f is irreducible over C
    iff
Mon(f) is 2-transitive on a generic fiber.
```

Applied to `f_h`, this is exactly

```text
H_(f_h) = G_h.
```

Therefore the logically minimal missing statement is

```text
[MON-2TRANS]

For every h>=2, the geometric monodromy group of f_h=N_h/A_h
is 2-transitive.
```

## 2.1 Why no blanket continuant theorem can prove this

The proposed general principle

```text
second-order recurrence
+ nonzero Casoratian
=> irreducible off-diagonal value curve
```

is false. The Chebyshev recurrence is already a countermodel:

```text
T_(n+1)(X) = 2*X*T_n(X)-T_(n-1)(X),
```

with a nondegenerate Wronskian, while

```text
[T_n(X)-T_n(Y)]/(X-Y)
```

has several factors when `n` is composite. The obstruction is decomposition of the underlying rational/polynomial map and the resulting imprimitive monodromy.

Thus one must prove an Apéry-specific monodromy or indecomposability statement.

Reference used: F. Pakovich, "Algebraic curves P(x)-Q(y)=0 and functional equations," arXiv:0804.0736; published in Complex Variables and Elliptic Equations 56 (2011), 199-213. Corollary 2.2 is the 2-transitivity criterion; Proposition 3.4 gives the indecomposable-plus-simple-critical-value criterion used below.

# 3. Mirror involution: exact transformation law

Let

```text
sigma_h(X) = -h-1-X.
```

The banked reflection identity is

```text
N_h(sigma_h(X)) = (-1)^(h-1)*N_h(X).
```

For the denominator,

```text
A_h(sigma_h(X)) = (-1)^h*A_h(X).
```

Hence

```text
f_h(sigma_h(X)) = -f_h(X).
```

Consequently

```text
R_h(sigma_h(X),sigma_h(Y)) = -R_h(X,Y).
```

Since

```text
sigma_h(X)-sigma_h(Y) = -(X-Y),
```

we obtain the exact requested law

```text
G_h(sigma_h(X),sigma_h(Y)) = G_h(X,Y).
```

In centered coordinates

```text
U = 2*X+h+1,
V = 2*Y+h+1,
```

this says

```text
G_h(-U,-V) = G_h(U,V).
```

This restricts a possible factorization: the involution must either preserve every factor or permute factors in pairs. It does not by itself prohibit factorization.

# 4. The h-recurrence for R_h does not close scalarly

Define the symmetric companion

```text
Plus_h(X,Y)
  = N_h(X)*A_h(Y) + N_h(Y)*A_h(X).
```

Put

```text
C = P(X+h)*(Y+h+1)^3,
D = P(Y+h)*(X+h+1)^3,

E = -(X+h)^6*(Y+h)^3*(Y+h+1)^3,
F = -(Y+h)^6*(X+h)^3*(X+h+1)^3.
```

A direct substitution of the two recurrences gives the exact coupled system

```text
R_(h+1)
  = ((C+D)/2)*R_h
    + ((C-D)/2)*Plus_h
    + ((E+F)/2)*R_(h-1)
    + ((E-F)/2)*Plus_(h-1),

Plus_(h+1)
  = ((C-D)/2)*R_h
    + ((C+D)/2)*Plus_h
    + ((E-F)/2)*R_(h-1)
    + ((E+F)/2)*Plus_(h-1).
```

Because `C-D` and `E-F` are antisymmetric in `X,Y`, they are divisible by `X-Y`. After substituting

```text
R_h = (X-Y)*G_h,
```

the quotient coefficients are symmetric. But the `Plus_h` terms remain. Therefore there is no scalar three-term recurrence for `G_h` alone coming for free from the continuant recurrence.

An equivalent compact tensor formulation is

```text
U_h(X)
  = (N_h(X), (X+h)^3*N_(h-1)(X), A_h(X))^T,

U_(h+1)(X) = M_h(X)*U_h(X),

M_h(X) =
[
  [P(X+h), -(X+h)^3, 0],
  [(X+h+1)^3, 0, 0],
  [0, 0, (X+h+1)^3]
].
```

Then the rank-one tensor

```text
K_h(X,Y) = U_h(X)*U_h(Y)^T
```

obeys

```text
K_(h+1)(X,Y)
  = M_h(X)*K_h(X,Y)*M_h(Y)^T.
```

`R_h` is one antisymmetric matrix coefficient of this tensor system. This is useful for symbolic propagation, but it does not establish irreducibility.

# 5. A one-variable critical-value polynomial

This is the main positive reduction.

## 5.1 Reduced critical-point polynomial

Differentiate `f_h=N_h/A_h`. Define

```text
B_h(X)
  = A_h(X)*N_h'(X) - A_h'(X)*N_h(X).
```

At each pole `X=-j`, the exact pole-value formula from Section 1 gives

```text
ord_(X=-j)(B_h) = 2.
```

Therefore define

```text
C_h(X)
  = B_h(X) / product_{j=1}^{h}(X+j)^2.
```

This is an integer/rational polynomial with

```text
deg(C_h) = 4*h-4,

lc(C_h) = -3*lc(N_h).
```

Its roots, with multiplicity, are exactly the finite non-pole critical points of `f_h`. The missing ramification consists of:

```text
h poles, each e=3: total defect 2*h,

X=infinity, a zero with e=3: defect 2.
```

Together with `deg(C_h)=4*h-4`, this gives total ramification

```text
2*h + 2 + (4*h-4) = 6*h-2 = 2*deg(f_h)-2,
```

as required by Riemann-Hurwitz.

## 5.2 Critical-value resultant

Introduce a new variable `T` and define

```text
V_h(T)
  = primitive_part(
      Res_X(C_h(X), N_h(X)-T*A_h(X))
    ).
```

Then

```text
deg(V_h) = 4*h-4.
```

A root `T=a` occurs with multiplicity equal to the total multiplicity in `C_h` of finite critical points `z` satisfying

```text
f_h(z) = a.
```

The mirror law gives

```text
C_h(sigma_h(X)) = C_h(X),

V_h(-T) = V_h(T)
```

after a harmless scalar normalization. Hence

```text
V_h(T) = const*W_h(T^2),

deg(W_h) = 2*h-2.
```

This evenness records the expected pairing of critical values `a <-> -a`; it does not force a factorization of `G_h`.

## 5.3 Counting simple nonzero critical values by gcd only

In characteristic zero, execute

```text
g_h(T)      = gcd(V_h(T), V_h'(T)),
rad_h(T)    = V_h(T)/g_h(T),
repeat_h(T) = gcd(rad_h(T), g_h(T)),
simple_h(T) = rad_h(T)/repeat_h(T).
```

If `T` divides `simple_h`, remove that factor, because the critical point at infinity already lies over value zero and a finite critical point over zero would not give a globally simple branch value.

Define

```text
S_h(T) = simple_h(T) with its factor T removed,

s_h = deg(S_h).
```

Then `s_h` is exactly the number of simple nonzero critical values of `f_h` over the algebraic closure.

This gives a fast exact audit:

```text
[CRIT-2H]

s_h >= 2*h-1 for every h>=2.
```

No bivariate absolute factorization is needed.

# 6. Proof that [CRIT-2H] closes absolute irreducibility

I now prove the implication, including the numerical constant `2*h-1`.

Suppose a proper decomposition exists:

```text
f_h = g o u,

deg(u) = m > 1,
deg(g) = n > 1,

m*n = 3*h.
```

For a value `a`, define the ramification defect

```text
defect_f(a)
  = sum_{x in f_h^(-1)(a)} (e_f(x)-1).
```

For each `y in g^(-1)(a)`, composition gives

```text
contribution over y
  = (e_g(y)-1)*m + defect_u(y).
```

If `a` is a simple critical value of `f_h`, the total defect is exactly `1`. Since `m>1`, this forces

```text
e_g(y) = 1 for every y over a,
```

and exactly one of those `y` is a simple critical point of `u`. Therefore

```text
number of simple critical values of f_h
  <= number of simple critical points of u
  <= 2*m-2.
```

## 6.1 Outer degree n>=3

If `n>=3`, then

```text
m = 3*h/n <= h,
```

so

```text
number of simple critical values of f_h <= 2*h-2.
```

## 6.2 Outer degree n=2

This case can be sharpened using the exact pole profile.

A degree-two rational map `g` has either one double pole or two simple poles. A double pole is impossible: every pole of `f_h` has local degree `3`, while composition through a double pole would make the local degree even.

Thus `g` has two simple poles. For each of them, its full `u`-fiber consists of points with

```text
e_u = 3,
```

because every pole of `f_h` has local degree `3`. Here

```text
m = 3*h/2.
```

Each pole fiber therefore contributes ramification defect

```text
m - m/3 = h.
```

The two pole fibers consume defect `2*h`.

Also `X=infinity` is a zero of `f_h` with local degree `3`. The corresponding point of `g` cannot have local degree `2`, so `u` itself has local degree `3` there, consuming two more units of ramification.

The total ramification of `u` is

```text
2*m-2 = 3*h-2.
```

After the forced pole and infinity ramification, at most

```text
(3*h-2) - (2*h+2) = h-4
```

units remain for simple critical points. Hence the degree-two outer case has at most `h-4` simple critical values. For small `h` where this number is negative, the decomposition is impossible outright.

## 6.3 Conclusion

Every proper decomposition has at most

```text
2*h-2
```

simple nonzero critical values. Thus `[CRIT-2H]` proves that `f_h` is indecomposable.

An indecomposable rational function has primitive geometric monodromy. A simple critical value gives a transposition in that monodromy. A primitive permutation group containing a transposition is the full symmetric group. Therefore

```text
Mon(f_h) = S_(3*h),
```

which is 2-transitive. By the fiber-product criterion,

```text
G_h is absolutely irreducible.
```

This proves:

```text
[CRIT-2H] => [MON-2TRANS] => absolute irreducibility of G_h,

for every h>=2.
```

The constant `2*h-1` is not cosmetic. It is one more than the largest possible number of simple critical values in a proper decomposition.

# 8. A second sufficient package: squarefree N_h plus indecomposability

There is another clean route.

If

```text
gcd(N_h,N_h') = 1,
```

then the fiber `f_h^(-1)(0)` consists of

```text
3*h-3 simple finite zeros,

and X=infinity with local degree 3.
```

The inertia permutation around zero therefore has cycle type

```text
(3)(1)^(3*h-3).
```

If `f_h` is indecomposable, monodromy is primitive. By the standard Jordan theorem, a primitive group of degree `3*h` containing a 3-cycle contains `A_(3*h)`, hence is 2-transitive. Therefore the two-lemma package

```text
[N-SQFREE]
  gcd(N_h,N_h') = 1,

[F-INDECOMP]
  f_h=N_h/A_h is indecomposable
```

also proves absolute irreducibility.

This package may be preferable if the one-variable p-adic machinery in `Q6490` can establish squarefreeness and indecomposability separately. At present neither is proved uniformly all `h` in the repository. `[CRIT-2H]` packages both tasks into one resultant test.

# 9. Specialization in Y: what it proves and what it does not

## 9.1 The best elementary specialization is Y=0

Let

```text
W_h = N_h(0).
```

Since `A_h(0)=(h!)^3`,

```text
G_h(X,0)
  = [(h!)^3*N_h(X) - W_h*A_h(X)]/X.
```

Because `W_h != 0`, this has the full expected `X`-degree

```text
deg_X G_h(X,0) = 3*h-1.
```

After checking primitiveness in `Q[Y][X]`, irreducibility of this specialization in `Q[X]` would prove irreducibility of `G_h` over `Q[X,Y]`: a nontrivial rational factorization would specialize with the same total `X`-degree.

But this proves only irreducibility over `Q`, not absolute irreducibility over `Qbar`. The distinction is essential for Weil/Lang-Weil and `[L2-FREQ]`.

A polynomial may be irreducible over `Q` and split into conjugate factors over an extension. The SymPy statement "one Q-factor" is therefore not yet the desired certificate.

## 9.2 Specializations at poles are maximally bad

For `Y=-j`, the exact pole formula gives

```text
G_h(X,-j)
  = -N_h(-j)*A_h(X)/(X+j).
```

This is visibly reducible. Thus pole specializations cannot prove anything.

## 9.3 The mirror center is also not the right full-degree test

For even `h`, the fixed point

```text
Y = -(h+1)/2
```

is a zero of `N_h`. Specialization there removes the forced central factor of `N_h` and drops the `X`-degree. It tests the primitive factor of `N_h`, not the absolute irreducibility of the full value-equality curve.

## 9.4 A good finite-field specialization can certify absolute irreducibility

For a fixed `h`, one valid certificate is:

```text
choose a prime ell of good reduction;
prove G_h mod ell is absolutely irreducible with the same bidegree;
conclude G_h is absolutely irreducible in characteristic zero.
```

The critical-value certificate is usually cheaper: choose `ell>3*h`, compute `C_h,V_h` modulo `ell`, and verify

```text
s_(h,ell) >= 2*h-1.
```

Then the tame version of the same monodromy proof gives absolute irreducibility modulo `ell`, hence in characteristic zero.

This supplies compact machine certificates for each fixed `h`; it does not by itself prove a uniform all-`h` theorem.

# 10. Small primes and Newton polygons

## 10.1 Characteristic 3 is unusable for direct specialization

The banked identity has the form

```text
N_h(X) = Q_h(X)^3 mod 3
```

for an explicitly recurring `Q_h`. Also

```text
A_h(X) = H_h(X)^3 mod 3,

H_h(X) = product_{j=1}^{h}(X+j).
```

Therefore

```text
R_h(X,Y)
  = [Q_h(X)*H_h(Y)-Q_h(Y)*H_h(X)]^3 mod 3.
```

The bracket is antisymmetric and divisible by `X-Y`. Hence

```text
G_h(X,Y)
  = (X-Y)^2
    * ([Q_h(X)*H_h(Y)-Q_h(Y)*H_h(X)]/(X-Y))^3
    mod 3.
```

So reduction modulo `3` is maximally nonreduced and reducible. It cannot prove characteristic-zero absolute irreducibility by specialization. Its value is in higher 3-adic/Montes analysis, not Bertini.

## 10.2 Characteristic 2 and 17

The repository already records:

```text
mod 2:
  periodic loss of the first coefficient layer/content;

mod 17:
  parity-dependent valuation of lc(N_h)=U_h(34,1).
```

These are useful for the one-variable factor/type tree of `N_h`, but neither directly controls the 2-transitivity of `f_h` or the critical-value resultant `V_h`.

The right mixed-prime experiment is to compute `V_h` or its subresultants modulo auxiliary good primes, not merely factor `N_h` modulo `2,3,17`.

## 10.3 Bertini/Noether specialization verdict

Effective Noether forms can turn characteristic-zero absolute irreducibility into good reduction outside divisors of an explicit integer. Relevant primary sources are:

- W. Ruppert, "Reducibility of polynomials f(x,y) modulo p," arXiv:math/9808021.
- L. Busé, G. Chèze and S. Najib, "Noether's forms for non-composite rational functions and their spectrum," arXiv:0906.2925.
- E. Kaltofen, "Effective Noether Irreducibility Forms and Applications," JCSS 50 (1995), 274-295.

They do not prove that the needed Noether form is nonzero for this deterministic family. That nonvanishing is exactly the present problem, expressed more efficiently by `[CRIT-2H]`.

# 11. Effective h-range in characteristic p

The coefficient heights satisfy the elementary estimates

```text
log height(N_h) = O(h*log(h)),
log height(A_h) = O(h*log(h)),
log height(C_h) = O(h*log(h)).
```

A resultant determinant of dimensions `O(h)` gives

```text
log height(V_h) = O(h^2*log(h)).
```

Since `deg(V_h)=O(h)`,

```text
log |Delta_h^Morse| = O(h^3*log(h)).
```

The same scale applies to a suitable product of principal subresultants certifying only `[CRIT-2H]`; call this nonzero integer `Theta_h`.

If an all-`h` proof establishes

```text
Theta_h != 0,
```

then every prime satisfying

```text
p > 3*h,

p does not divide Theta_h
```

inherits the tame mod-`p` critical-value certificate and hence absolute irreducibility of `G_h mod p`.

A completely deterministic sufficient inequality is

```text
p > |Theta_h|.
```

Using the height estimate, this yields only

```text
h <= c*(log(p)/loglog(p))^(1/3)
```

for a sufficiently small absolute `c`.

Thus:

```text
height + characteristic-zero irreducibility
  => polylogarithmic h-window,

not h <= p^delta.
```

To reach a positive-power window one needs a direct modular statement such as

```text
[CRIT-MOD-p-delta]

There exists delta>0 such that, for every sufficiently large p and
all 2<=h<=p^delta,

    s_(h,p) >= 2*h-1,

apart from an explicitly summable exceptional set of (p,h).
```

This is the correct uniform version of the target. It is still one-variable and is more precise than asking abstractly for "uniform Bertini."

Once `G_h mod p` is absolutely irreducible, the standard affine Weil bound has the shape

```text
#G_h(F_p) = p + O(h^2*sqrt(p)).
```

Pointwise main-term asymptotics are therefore naturally useful at least in the range

```text
h = o(p^(1/4)).
```

A larger frequency window would require averaging the errors, not merely absolute irreducibility.

# 12. Immediate exact computation plan

The following test is more informative and usually cheaper than factoring `G_h(X,Y)` over algebraic closures.

```text
for h = 1,2,...,H:

    build N_h(X)
    build A_h(X) = product_{j=1}^{h}(X+j)^3

    B_h(X) = A_h*N_h' - A_h'*N_h

    C_h(X) = exact_quotient(
               B_h(X),
               product_{j=1}^{h}(X+j)^2
             )

    V_h(T) = primitive_part(
               resultant_X(C_h(X), N_h(X)-T*A_h(X))
             )

    assert degree(C_h) == 4*h-4
    assert degree(V_h) == 4*h-4
    assert V_h(-T) == V_h(T) up to a scalar

    g      = gcd(V_h, derivative(V_h,T))
    rad    = V_h/g
    repeat = gcd(rad,g)
    simple = rad/repeat

    if simple(0) == 0:
        simple = simple/T

    s_h = degree(simple)

    record:
        full_Morse = (V_h(0)!=0 and degree(g)==0)
        crit_2h    = (s_h >= 2*h-1)
```

For larger `h`, do this modulo one or several auxiliary primes `ell>3*h`. Store:

```text
h,
ell,
deg(V_h),
deg(gcd(V_h,V_h')),
valuation_at_T_zero(V_h),
s_h.
```

A modular certificate with `s_h>=2*h-1` is already a proof for that fixed `h` in characteristic zero, provided degree/content preservation is checked.

Also run the direct gates:

```text
Gate 0:
```

# 15. Required final questions

## (a) The step I am least confident about

The least certain substantive point is not the implication

```text
[CRIT-2H] => absolute irreducibility;
```

that implication has a complete ramification/monodromy proof above. The uncertainty is whether `[CRIT-2H]` is actually true for every `h`. The mirror involution forces `a <-> -a`, and hidden modular/contiguity symmetries could create enough repeated critical values at some large `h` to drop `s_h` below `2*h-1`. The supplied checks of `G_h` through `h=6` do not test this failure mode directly; the proposed `V_h` audit does.

The quantitative height exponent `O(h^3*log h)` is also stated at the natural determinant-height scale rather than with optimized explicit constants. It is sufficient to distinguish polylogarithmic from power-sized windows, but a publication-level effective range should compute the constants from the chosen subresultant certificate.

## (b) The likely common blind spot

There are two.

1. We may have been attacking the wrong irreducibility object. Factorization of the one-variable continuant `N_h` and factorization of the off-diagonal curve `G_h` are different questions. Even perfect all-`h` irreducibility of `N_h` would not prove 2-transitivity of `f_h`.

2. We may be conflating three levels:

```text
irreducible over Q,
absolute irreducible over Qbar,
absolute irreducible after reduction mod p uniformly in growing h.
```

The SymPy "single Q-factor" observation addresses only the first. `[L2-FREQ]` needs the third. The critical-value/subresultant formulation makes all three transitions visible and identifies exactly where a bad-reduction divisor can re-enter.

The strategic correction is therefore:

```text
stop treating G_h factorization as a black-box bivariate event;
compute the branch polynomial V_h(T), prove enough simple critical values,
and track its subresultants modulo p.
```
