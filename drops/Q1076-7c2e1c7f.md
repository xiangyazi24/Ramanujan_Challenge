# Audit: Apéry numbers modulo 64 digit transducer

## Executive conclusion

The requested all-length modulo-64 certificate is not currently available from the stated ingredients. The reduction is valid as a research target, but a proof-quality transition table or companion recurrence cannot be honestly supplied without proving additional 2-adic identities for the Apéry sequence.

The correct result of the audit is therefore a narrower missing lemma, not a completed modulo-64 automaton.

## Exact reduction

The binomial sum is

```text
b_n = sum_{k=0}^n C(n,k)^2 C(n+k,k)^2.
```

A binary transducer would need states S such that, after reading the binary digits of n, the state invariant contains enough information to reconstruct `b_n mod 64`.

The invariant cannot merely be

```text
state -> b_n mod 64
```

because proving the transition from `n` to `2n` and `2n+1` requires identities for the whole binomial sum, not interpolation from a prefix.

A valid certificate would require companion quantities

```text
X_n=(x_1(n),...,x_r(n)) mod 64
```

with explicit formulas proving

```text
X_(2n)=T_0(X_n)
X_(2n+1)=T_1(X_n)
```

and initial vector `X_0`.

No such proven finite vector is derived from the Apéry recurrence alone in the supplied material.

## Why ordinary 2-automaticity is insufficient

The statement that `b_n mod 64` is 2-automatic only asserts existence of a finite kernel. It does not provide:

1. the kernel sequences;
2. the transition maps;
3. the invariant proving that the maps preserve the Apéry binomial sum.

A table obtained from a finite prefix would not be a proof certificate.

## What can be proved directly

Modulo small powers of two one can obtain partial information from known Apéry congruences. For example, parity is immediate because every Apéry number is odd.

However lifting successively

```text
mod 8 -> mod 16 -> mod 32 -> mod 64
```

requires controlling the higher 2-adic terms in the binomial summation. The missing step is exactly the modulo-64 companion recurrence.

## Required missing lemma

A sufficient lemma for the later Kummer automaton is:

**Lemma (mod-64 digit companion lemma).** There exist finitely many integer-valued companion sequences `X_i(n)` such that:

```text
X(0) is explicitly known,
X(2n)=A X(n) mod 64,
X(2n+1)=B X(n) mod 64,
first coordinate of X(n)=b_n mod 64.
```

with matrices `A,B` explicitly given and proved from the Apéry recurrence or binomial identity.

This lemma would immediately provide the requested valuation transducer.

## Computer evidence versus proof

A computation of residues through any finite range can test a proposed table but cannot establish it. The requested deliverable specifically excludes this inference.

Therefore the current status is:

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Apéry numbers have structured 2-adic behavior | established |
| A modulo-64 finite automaton exists | plausible/known in principle for this sequence class |
| Explicit transition table with invariant | missing |

The next rigorous step is to derive and prove the smallest companion system closing modulo 64; until then the 2-adic Padé exponent proof remains open.
