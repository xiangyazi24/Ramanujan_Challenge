## PTMA versus SKPMIX

PTMA is a **uniform rowwise** assertion: after undoing the centering, every surviving row has `o(T_*)` targets.

The weaker aggregate statement is the dyadic mean square
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal V_{q,g}(X)
:=\sum_h |S_{q,g,X}(h)|^2
=o_q\!\left(XT_*^2\right).
}
\tag{SKPMIX}

\[
XT_*^2
=\boxed{
\frac{g^2X^3}
{\varphi(g)^2\log^2X(\log\log X)^2},

\tag{2.6}

For the all-`n` theorem one would ultimately need either PTMA or a weighted/adaptive SKPMIX estimate on the already-localized exceptional rows, analogous to the sparse covariance endpoint banked earlier.  The unweighted dyadic statement is still the natural first cross-`N` mixing target.

\log b_r\ll r.
\tag{9.6}
\]
For fixed `1\le a\le Y`, let
\[
k_g(a)
:=\#\{N\in\mathcal N_g(X):p_N\mid b_{ga}\}.
\]
Every such prime is `\gg gX`, and their product divides `b_{ga}`. Hence
\[
k_g(a)\log(gX)\le\log b_{ga}\ll ga,
\]
so, since `g=X^{o(1)}`,
\[
\boxed{
k_g(a)\ll \frac{ga}{\log X}.}
\tag{9.7}
\]
Summing,
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{a\le Y}k_g(a)
\ll \frac{gY^2}{\log X}.}
\tag{9.8}
\]
This is where the actual Apéry sequence enters the fringe estimate.

## 9.2 Number of possible partner moduli

For fixed `N_2`, condition (9.1) is
\[

Thus `N_1` lies in an interval of length `Y/(q+1)+O(1)`.  The primes
\[

therefore lie in an interval of length `\asymp_q gY` in the residue class `1 mod g`.  Brun--Titchmarsh gives
\[
\boxed{

\frac{gY}{\varphi(g)\log Y}.}
\tag{9.9}

Here `Y>=X^epsilon` ensures a uniform positive logarithmic denominator.

Combining (9.8) and (9.9), and dropping the second target condition, gives

\tag{9.10}

The same argument, using reflection (9.4), gives the identical bound for the sums of `B_1` and `B_2` over the fringe.

\tag{9.11}

Insert (3.3):

\tag{9.12}

This proves (FRINGE).

# 12. Logical status relative to PTMA

It is important not to overstate what (11.2) would give.

- **PTMA** would control every exact `(q,g)` row and would directly supply the pointwise small-kernel packet bound needed after Q2989.
- **SKPMIX-BIL**, together with the diagonal and fringe bounds proved here, would prove only the dyadic mean-square SKPMIX statement.
- SKPMIX implies that PTMA-sized spikes occur on `o(X)` rows, but not that they never occur.
- To finish the all-`n` problem from a mean-square route one must combine SKPMIX with the already-known sparse exceptional-row localization and prove the covariance estimate uniformly under that adaptive restriction/weight.  This is analogous to the earlier `EXC-SPARSE-CV` endpoint and is logically stronger than one unweighted signed dyadic identity.

So the covariance attack does not magically turn the current pointwise gap into an average theorem sufficient for all rows.  What it does accomplish is to separate the tasks cleanly:

1. diagonal: **proved negligible**;
2. small-overlap off-diagonal fringe: **proved negligible in the range (9.15)**;
3. primitive-indicator/Ramanujan-sum mechanism: **exact no-go**;
4. central large-overlap covariance of actual Apéry targets: **the sole remaining bilinear estimate**.

The first genuine cross-`N` saving is available, but only because the exact packet geometry plus Apéry reflection forces the small-overlap fringe into small residual indices.  The proof uses the actual Apéry integer height and the prime progression, not primitive Ramanujan sums.

The central range remains open. Proving `Q32-SKPMIX-BIL(q,g)` at the displayed scale is the smallest falsifiable next lemma for the cross-characteristic covariance route.
