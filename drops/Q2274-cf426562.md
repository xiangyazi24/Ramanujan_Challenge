# Direct generating-function audit of `Delta_r` absorption

## Verdict

There is an exact generating-function extension of the BF/MARK pairing, but it does **not** follow by replacing the unweighted sum with the same boundary term.  The weighted Green identity contains an interior current multiplied by `1-y`:

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}w_i b_{r+i}y^i
 =y^{h-1}\mathfrak F_s-y\mathfrak F_{r+1}
 +(1-y)\sum_{i=1}^{h-2}y^i\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}.
}
\tag{WG}
\]

Here \(h=s-r\), and \(\mathfrak F_k\) is the discrete Apéry Lagrange flux attached to the one-sided solution generated by the shifted-Apéry source.  On the marked one-sided branch,

\[
\mathfrak F_{r+1}=0,
\qquad
\mathfrak F_s=s^3b_{s-1}O_{r,s},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}w_i b_{r+i}y^i
 =y^{h-1}s^3b_{s-1}O_{r,s}
 +(1-y)\sum_{i=1}^{h-2}y^i\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}.
}
\tag{WG-MARK}
\]

At \(y=1\), the entire interior term disappears and one recovers BF/MARK.  Thus BF/MARK supplies only one scalar specialization; it does not imply the coefficientwise generating-function identity needed to absorb \(\Delta_r\).

The Racah normalization also needs one correction.  If \(y\) marks the interior index \(i\), the adjacent spectral factor is

\[
r+(r+i)+1=2r+i+1,
\]

so its generating-function operator is

\[
\theta_y+2r+1,
\]

not the constant endpoint factor \(r+s+1\).  The latter appears only at the terminal index.

With the standard Racah series

\[
F_r(y)=\sum_{n\ge0}K(r,n)y^n
      =\frac{Q_r(y)^2}{(1-y)^{2r+1}},
\]

the exact contiguous identity is

\[
\boxed{
\frac{(r+1)Q_r(y)Q_{r+1}(y)}{(1-y)^{2r+2}}
=(\theta_y+r+1)F_r(y)
 +\frac{(2r+1)y}{1-y}F_r(y).
}
\tag{RAC}
\]

The last term is exactly

\[
\Delta_r(y)=\frac{(2r+1)yQ_r(y)^2}{(1-y)^{2r+2}}.
\]

Consequently, if the crossed source has the pure cross image recorded in Q2269, then its residual against the adjacent Racah border is **plus** \(\Delta_r\), not zero.  Absorption requires an additional coefficient row whose Apéry/Racah diagonal transform is exactly \(-\Delta_r\).

The exact remaining theorem is the all-prefix flux identity in §7 below.  It is strictly stronger than BF/MARK at the final endpoint.  No finite scan is used here.

## 1. Exact generating function of the crossed coefficient row

To avoid confusing the source series \(F,G\) with the Racah series \(F_r\), write the former as \(\mathscr F,\mathscr G\).  Put

\[
R_c:=(\theta_x+c)^{-1},
\qquad
A_c=R_c\mathscr F,
\qquad
B_c=R_c\mathscr G-R_c^2\mathscr F,
\]

and abbreviate

\[
S_c:=T_c\mathscr G+U_c\mathscr F,
\qquad
V_c:=T_c\mathscr F.
\]

Then equation (H) is simply

\[
H_{c;d}=A_cS_d-B_cV_d.
\]

Set

\[
a=\frac12,
\qquad
b=\beta=r+1,
\qquad
\delta=b-a=r+\frac12=\frac{2r+1}{2}.
\]

Define divided differences

\[
S[a,b]:=\frac{S_b-S_a}{b-a},
\qquad
V[a,b]:=\frac{V_b-V_a}{b-a}.
\]

Since every \(R_c\) is a rational function of the same Euler operator, the resolvents commute and

\[
R_a-R_b=(b-a)R_aR_b,
\]

\[
R_a^2-R_b^2=(b-a)R_aR_b(R_a+R_b).
\]

Hence

\[
A_a-A_b=\delta R_aR_b\mathscr F,
\]

and

\[
B_a-B_b
=\delta R_aR_b
 \left(\mathscr G-(R_a+R_b)\mathscr F\right).
\]

Now group the crossed difference as

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{a;b}-H_{b;a}
={}&A_a(S_b-S_a)-B_a(V_b-V_a)\\
&+(A_a-A_b)S_a-(B_a-B_b)V_a.
\end{aligned}
\]

Therefore the ordinary generating function of the normalized row

\[
w_i=[x^i]\frac{H_{1/2;\beta}-H_{\beta;1/2}}
                         {8(\beta-1/2)}
\]

is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
W_r(x):=\sum_{i\ge0}w_ix^i
=\frac18\Big[{}
&A_aS[a,b]-B_aV[a,b]\\
&+R_aR_b\mathscr F\,S_a\\
&-R_aR_b\bigl(\mathscr G-(R_a+R_b)\mathscr F\bigr)V_a
\Big],
\end{aligned}}
\tag{W}
\]

with \(a=1/2\), \(b=r+1\).  This is the canonical resolvent-normal form of the crossed source.  It already factors out the full antisymmetric divisor \(8(b-a)\).

## 2. The Apéry-column pairing is a Hadamard/diagonal transform

Let

\[
B(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}b_nz^n
\]

be the Apéry generating series, and let its tail beginning at \(r\) be

\[
B^{\langle r\rangle}(z)
:=\sum_{i\ge0}b_{r+i}z^i
=z^{-r}\left(B(z)-\sum_{n=0}^{r-1}b_nz^n\right).
\]

The desired second-variable series is not the ordinary product
\(W_r(y)B^{\langle r\rangle}(y)\).  It is the coefficientwise product

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_r(y)
:=\sum_{i\ge0}w_i b_{r+i}y^i
=W_r\odot B^{\langle r\rangle}(y).
}
\tag{HAD}
\]

Equivalently, it is the constant-term diagonal

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_r(y)
=\operatorname{CT}_{z}
 \left(W_r(z)B^{\langle r\rangle}(y/z)\right).
}
\tag{DIAG}
\]

For a finite gap \(h=s-r\), the marked interior polynomial is

\[
\mathcal P_{r,s}(y)
:=\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}w_i b_{r+i}y^i.
\tag{Pfin}
\]

This diagonal distinction is essential.  The products in (H) create Cauchy convolutions in the variable \(x\), while the Apéry-column pairing is Hadamard.  Passing from one to the other is a genuine diagonal-evaluation theorem; it is not an automatic operator substitution.

## 3. Weighted discrete Green identity

Let

\[
(Lu)_k=(k+1)^3u_{k+1}-P(k)u_k+k^3u_{k-1}
\]

be the physical Apéry operator, so \(Lb=0\).  Let \(O^{(r)}_k\) denote the one-sided solution produced by the normalized shifted source, so on the interior

\[
(LO^{(r)})_{r+i}=w_i.
\]

Define its Lagrange flux against the Apéry solution by

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F_k
:=k^3\left(
 b_{k-1}O^{(r)}_k-O^{(r)}_{k-1}b_k
\right).
}
\tag{Flux}
\]

A direct subtraction gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak F_{k+1}-\mathfrak F_k
&=b_k(LO^{(r)})_k-O^{(r)}_k(Lb)_k\\
&=b_k(LO^{(r)})_k.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}-\mathfrak F_{r+i}
=w_i b_{r+i}.
}
\tag{local-BF}
\]

Multiply by \(y^i\) and sum from \(i=1\) to \(h-1\).  Discrete summation by parts gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal P_{r,s}(y)
={}&\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}y^i
  (\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}-\mathfrak F_{r+i})\\
={}&y^{h-1}\mathfrak F_s-y\mathfrak F_{r+1}
 +(1-y)\sum_{i=1}^{h-2}y^i\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}.
\end{aligned}
\]

This proves (WG).

On the one-sided marked branch, \(b_r=0\), \(O^{(r)}_r=0\), and \(b_s=0\).  Therefore

\[
\mathfrak F_{r+1}=0,
\]

and

\[
\mathfrak F_s
=s^3b_{s-1}O^{(r)}_s
=s^3b_{s-1}O_{r,s}.
\]

This proves (WG-MARK), and setting \(y=1\) gives exactly

\[
\mathcal P_{r,s}(1)
=s^3b_{s-1}O_{r,s}.
\]

The extension is therefore explicit, but it contains all intermediate fluxes.  The evaluation map

\[
\mathbf Q[y]_{<h}\longrightarrow\mathbf Q,
\qquad R(y)\longmapsto R(1),
\]

has kernel \((1-y)\mathbf Q[y]_{<h-1}\).  BF/MARK kills precisely the part in which a nonzero generating-function residual can hide.

## 4. Exact Racah border and cumulative term

Use the standard Racah kernel

\[
K(r,n)=\sum_{j=0}^{\min(r,n)}
 \binom rj\binom{r+j}{j}
 \binom nj\binom{n+j}{j}.
\]

Put

\[
Q_r(y)=\sum_{j=0}^r\binom rj^2y^j.
\]

Then the row generating function is

\[
\boxed{
F_r(y):=\sum_{n\ge0}K(r,n)y^n
       =\frac{Q_r(y)^2}{(1-y)^{2r+1}}.
}
\tag{Kr}
\]

Let

\[
S_r(n):=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}K(r,j),
\qquad S_r(0)=0.
\]

Its generating function is

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{n\ge0}S_r(n)y^n
=\frac{y}{1-y}F_r(y).
}
\tag{Cum}
\]

The adjacent contiguous identity recorded in Q2269 is

\[
\boxed{
(\theta_y+r+1)F_r(y)
=\frac{(r+1)Q_r(y)Q_{r+1}(y)
      -(2r+1)yQ_r(y)^2}
      {(1-y)^{2r+2}}.
}
\tag{Adj}
\]

Define the pure cross series

\[
C_r(y)
:=\frac{(r+1)Q_r(y)Q_{r+1}(y)}{(1-y)^{2r+2}}.
\]

Then (Adj) is exactly

\[
\boxed{
C_r(y)=(\theta_y+r+1)F_r(y)+\Delta_r(y),
}
\tag{cross=adj+Delta}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_r(y)
=(2r+1)\frac{y}{1-y}F_r(y)
=\frac{(2r+1)yQ_r(y)^2}{(1-y)^{2r+2}}.
}
\tag{Delta}
\]

Coefficientwise,

\[
[y^n](\theta_y+r+1)F_r
=(r+n+1)K(r,n),
\]

\[
[y^n]\Delta_r
=(2r+1)S_r(n),
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
[y^n]C_r
=(r+n+1)K(r,n)+(2r+1)S_r(n).
}
\tag{Ccoef}
\]

Thus the correct coefficient-level absorption formula is

\[
\boxed{
[y^n]C_r-(2r+1)S_r(n)
=(r+n+1)K(r,n).
}
\tag{Abs-coeff}
\]

There is a useful notational warning here.  If the first term in the proposed formula means the **cross coefficient** \([y^s]C_r\), then subtracting the cumulative term is correct and gives \((r+s+1)K(r,s)\).  If it literally means \((r+s+1)K(r,s)\), then subtracting the cumulative term again is one subtraction too many: the adjacent coefficient is already \((r+s+1)K(r,s)\).

## 5. Correct shifted generating operator

The variable in the requested Apéry-column pairing marks the interior displacement \(i\), not the absolute Racah index \(n\).  Define the shifted Racah row

\[
\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)
:=\sum_{i\ge0}K(r,r+i)y^i.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i\ge0}(2r+i+1)K(r,r+i)y^i
=(\theta_y+2r+1)\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y).
}
\tag{Shift-adj}
\]

This is the generating-function replacement for the terminal scalar factor \(r+s+1\).  A constant factor \(r+s+1\) cannot multiply every row coefficient when \(y\) marks varying \(i\).

The shifted cumulative series is also explicit.  Since

\[
S_r(r+i+1)-S_r(r+i)=K(r,r+i),
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i\ge0}S_r(r+i)y^i
=\frac{S_r(r)+y\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)}{1-y}.
}
\tag{Shift-cum}
\]

Consequently the shifted pure-cross row is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal C_r^{\rm sh}(y)
={}&(\theta_y+2r+1)\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)\\
&+(2r+1)
 \frac{S_r(r)+y\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)}{1-y}.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{Shift-cross}
\]

After deleting the \(i=0\) term, the cumulative correction on the actual interior begins as

\[
\boxed{
(2r+1)\sum_{i\ge1}S_r(r+i)y^i
=(2r+1)\frac{y\bigl(S_r(r)+\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)\bigr)}{1-y}.
}
\tag{Shift-Delta}
\]

For a finite endpoint \(s=r+h\), all these series are simply projected to degrees \(1,\ldots,h-1\).

Therefore the corrected generating-function form of the hoped-for cancellation is

\[
\boxed{
\text{shifted cross row}
-(2r+1)\,\text{shifted cumulative row}
=(\theta_y+2r+1)\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y),
}
\tag{Correct-target}
\]

not a constant \((r+s+1)\mathcal K_r^{\rm sh}(y)\).

## 6. What the crossed source would have to prove

Let \(\Pi_{r,s}\) denote projection to the physical interior indices
\(r+1,\ldots,s-1\), followed by the shift \(n=r+i\mapsto y^i\).  Define

\[
\mathcal A_{r,s}(y)
:=\Pi_{r,s}\bigl((\theta+r+1)F_r\bigr),
\]

\[
\mathcal C_{r,s}(y)
:=\Pi_{r,s}(C_r),
\qquad
\mathcal D_{r,s}(y)
:=\Pi_{r,s}(\Delta_r).
\]

Then identically

\[
\mathcal C_{r,s}
=\mathcal A_{r,s}+\mathcal D_{r,s}.
\tag{finite-RAC}
\]

The desired absorption theorem is

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_{r,s}(y)=\mathcal A_{r,s}(y).
}
\tag{ABS}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_{r,s}(y)-\mathcal C_{r,s}(y)
=-\mathcal D_{r,s}(y).
}
\tag{ABS-cross}
\]

Using the exact diagonal expression (DIAG), this becomes the concrete source theorem

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{CT}_{z}
 \left(W_r(z)B^{\langle r\rangle}(y/z)\right)
-\mathcal C_r^{\rm sh}(y)
=-\mathcal D_r^{\rm sh}(y),
}
\tag{Diagonal-Delta}
\]

with the appropriate finite projection for a fixed \(s\).

This is the direct generating-function statement that must be established from the explicit \(T_c,U_c\).  It is not a consequence of (H) merely as an operator template, and it is not a consequence of BF/MARK at \(y=1\).

If one adopts the Q2269 statement that the pure crossed source already transforms to \(C_r\), then

\[
\mathcal P_{r,s}=\mathcal C_{r,s},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal P_{r,s}-\mathcal A_{r,s}
=\mathcal D_{r,s}.
}
\tag{surviving-Delta}
\]

In that normalization, the pure crossed source does **not** absorb \(\Delta_r\); it leaves exactly the previously recorded obstruction.  A mixed square-lift/source row must contribute \(-\mathcal D_{r,s}\).

## 7. Exact all-prefix residual hidden by BF/MARK

The strongest clean formulation uses the Green flux and does not depend on how the omitted \(T_c,U_c\) are written.

Let

\[
q_i:=w_i b_{r+i}
\qquad(1\le i\le h-1)
\]

be the actual paired source coefficients, and let \(a_i\) be the coefficients of the corrected adjacent Racah target.  In the canonical shifted normalization,

\[
a_i=(2r+i+1)K(r,r+i),
\]

or, if one begins with the pure cross row,

\[
a_i=[y^i](\text{cross row})-(2r+1)S_r(r+i).
\]

Define the prefix discrepancy

\[
\boxed{
E_m
:=\mathfrak F_{r+m+1}-\mathfrak F_{r+1}
  -\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_i,
\qquad 0\le m\le h-1,
}
\tag{E}
\]

with \(E_0=0\).  Since

\[
q_i=\mathfrak F_{r+i+1}-\mathfrak F_{r+i},
\]

we have

\[
q_i-a_i=E_i-E_{i-1}.
\]

Therefore the full generating-function residual is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal R_{r,s}(y)
&:=\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}(q_i-a_i)y^i\\
&=y^{h-1}E_{h-1}
 +(1-y)\sum_{i=1}^{h-2}E_i y^i.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{Residual}
\]

If the final scalar BF/MARK identity agrees with the terminal Racah target, then \(E_{h-1}=0\), and

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{r,s}(y)
=(1-y)\sum_{i=1}^{h-2}E_i y^i.
}
\tag{Residual-BF}
\]

This explains exactly why the endpoint test can pass while the generating-function identity fails: all intermediate prefix errors survive, multiplied by \(1-y\).

Hence \(\Delta_r\) is absorbed if and only if

\[
\boxed{
E_m=0
\quad\text{for every }1\le m\le h-2.
}
\tag{ALL-PREFIX}
\]

Equivalently, one must prove the intermediate-flux identities

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak F_{r+m+1}
=\mathfrak F_{r+1}
 +\sum_{i=1}^{m}a_i
\quad(1\le m\le h-1).
}
\tag{Flux-Racah}
\]

BF/MARK is only the last member \(m=h-1\) of this family.

For a correction row \(\rho_i\) intended specifically to remove the pure-cross \(\Delta_r\), its flux \(\Psi_k\) must satisfy the sharper local equation

\[
\boxed{
\Psi_{r+i+1}-\Psi_{r+i}
=-(2r+1)S_r(r+i)
\quad(1\le i\le h-1).
}
\tag{Delta-flux-local}
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\Psi_{r+m+1}
=\Psi_{r+1}
 -(2r+1)\sum_{i=1}^{m}S_r(r+i).
}
\tag{Delta-flux-prefix}
\]

This is the exact transfer/Green form of the missing `Delta_r` row.

## Final conclusion

The direct generating-function computation gives three exact results:

1. the crossed source row is the resolvent/divided-difference series (W);
2. its Apéry-column pairing is the Hadamard diagonal (DIAG);
3. its finite \(y\)-extension is the weighted Green identity (WG-MARK), with a nontrivial \((1-y)\)-times-interior-flux term.

Therefore the BF/MARK value at \(y=1\) does **not** automatically absorb \(\Delta_r\).  The literal proposed generating-function factor \(r+s+1\) is also not the correct row operator; it must be replaced by \(\theta_y+2r+1\) when \(y\) marks the interior displacement.

After that correction, absorption is exactly the all-prefix identity (ALL-PREFIX), or equivalently the diagonal identity (Diagonal-Delta).  In the pure-cross normalization recorded in Q2269, the residual is precisely \(+\Delta_r\), so an additional mixed/square-lift source row with transform \(-\Delta_r\) is still required.
