# 8. Why the natural continuations stop

## Sum over `d` first

The CRT kernel has endpoint width `R~N^2/D`.  Extending the high-column calculation to an interval of that length gives

\[
 \sum_{t\le R}\widetilde m_N(t)^2

After restoring `ND` and the prime weights, this is `N^{23/10+o(1)}`.  Endpoint counting alone cannot close the band.

## Classical spacing

The reduced denominators are `pq~N^2`, so classical spacing is `N^{-4}`.  A length-`D` large sieve pays far too much, even after exact equality is removed.

## Bettin--Chandee for all gaps

For one fixed `(r,s)`, the theorem gives Proposition 3.1.  Summing its scalar bound over all `O(N)` gap values loses a full factor `N`.  The first zero moment and high-column theorem do not replace it.  A tensor-valued theorem controlling all gap-dependent Apéry coefficients would be genuinely new.

## Marginal densities

The off-row TT* term conditions on four actual zero incidences and (7.3).  Neither `Z(p)<<p^{2/3}` nor the first moment permits multiplication of four marginal densities.  The high-column theorem supplies joint information only for coincident/additively close rows, not for the determinant relation.
