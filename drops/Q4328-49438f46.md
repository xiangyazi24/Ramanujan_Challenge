# Q4328 — the determinant-sparse Frobenius detector

## 0. Verdict

The proposed phrase has two materially different formalizations. They must be separated before any construction claim is meaningful.

1. **Exact marked detector.** If a detector may use the two literal lower Apéry-zero tests and must be spectrally degenerate exactly at the actual leaves, then such a detector exists canonically. Its determinant sparsity is nevertheless **logically equivalent to the leaf-count bound**. This is the third certificate collapse. A one-dimensional unit-root detector realizes equality: the total multiplicity of its exceptional Frobenius factors is exactly the fibre size `K`.

2. **Fixed pre-support detector.** There is also a canonical detector defined before factoring or enumerating the lower support: the zero-dimensional arithmetic scheme cut out by the two fixed affine-index Apéry values after honest `q`-primary and fixed-ledger saturation. Its special fibre at a lower prime `p` is nonempty precisely when `p` divides the corresponding fixed two-value gcd. Geometric Frobenius on that fibre has the single eigenvalue `1`. Asking this detector to have at most `D` nontrivial local factors in the admissible shell is **strictly stronger** than the actual leaf bound because it includes false-positive common divisors, but it is exactly the already named support-blind restricted-radical theorem. The Frobenius language adds no new mechanism: all arithmetic information lies in which fibres exist, not in their spectra.

There is no third useful interpretation exposed by the current bank.

- A common-`q` determinant polynomial is CQRD in determinant notation and dies by `q`-local unit erasure; unrestricted existence is equivalent to the root-count target.
- A determinant container modulo an auxiliary prime is the effective auxiliary-class law, already proved equivalent at the matching scale to root descent.
- An integral determinant or perfect-complex rank-jump detector is a Fitting/scalar carrier and contains the marked-prime information floor.
- Separate detectors at the varying lower characteristics live in orthogonal idempotent components, so their determinant defects add and cannot cancel.
- The ordinary determinant or characteristic polynomial of the natural Apéry `F`-crystal is not a detector: Frobenius is invertible, the determinant is row-key blind, and determinants of mixed extensions do not see the extension class.

Therefore the **determinant-sparse Frobenius detector is closed as an independent route**. What survives is one of two old counting statements:

```text
exact labelled exceptional support sparse
    = the desired fibre bound itself;

fixed support-blind exceptional support sparse
    = restricted radical / fixed-gcd shell sparsity.
```

No unconditional positive exponent is proved here. The reusable deliverable is the collapse theorem and the Frobenius/Fitting trichotomy below.

## 3. Packaging check: a determinant is not automatically a conjunction detector

A first tempting matrix is `diag(u,v)`. Its determinant is `uv`, which vanishes when **either** coordinate vanishes. The lower leaf requires **both** coordinates to vanish. Thus the naive `2 by 2` determinant detects the union, not the conjunction.

There are two honest conjunction packages.

### 3.1 Koszul detector

For a field `k` and `u,v` in `k`, take

```text
0 -> k -> k^2 -> k -> 0,
```

with differentials

```text
1 maps to (-v,u),
(x,y) maps to ux+vy.
```

Because `k` is a field, this complex is acyclic whenever `(u,v)` is not `(0,0)`, and every differential is zero when `u=v=0`. Therefore

```text
H*(K(u,v)) is nonzero  iff  u=v=0.
```

This is the canonical derived conjunction detector. Its alternating determinant line has Euler cancellation, however, so one must inspect a chosen cohomology group, total cohomology dimension, or an augmented determinant. The bare superdeterminant is not the desired statistic.

### 3.2 Norm determinant

Let `k=F_p`, let `k_2=F_{p^2}`, and choose `θ` outside `k`. Put

```text
w = (1-m) + m(u+θv).
```

If `m=0`, then `w=1`. If `m=1`, linear independence of `1,θ` over `k` gives

```text
w=0  iff  u=v=0.
```

Multiplication by `w` on the two-dimensional `k`-space `k_2` has determinant

```text
Norm_{k_2/k}(w),
```

and hence

```text
det(multiplication by w)=0
    iff m=1 and u=v=0.
```

Let `φ_p` be absolute Frobenius `z maps to z^p` on `k_2`. It is invertible, so

```text
det((multiplication by w) composed with φ_p)
  = det(multiplication by w) det(φ_p).
```

This is a literal marked-Frobenius determinant detector for the raw conjunction. The identity also exposes the decisive point: **the vanishing comes entirely from the marked multiplication map; Frobenius contributes only a unit**.

A genuine `F`-isocrystal Frobenius is invertible, so its determinant itself never vanishes. A literal detector must use one of:

- `det(1-F)` or another characteristic-polynomial specialization;
- a marked singular operator such as the norm construction;
- rank jump or non-acyclicity of a marked complex.

Every option needs the marking. Once the marking is exact, sparsity collapses to the target count.

## 4. Exact-detector collapse theorem

### 4.1 Abstract detector

Let `Ω` be a finite candidate set and `L` its actual leaf subset. An exact local Frobenius detector is a family `(V_p,F_p)` with invertible local Frobenius operators and a nonnegative additive defect such as

```text
δ_p = dim ker(1-F_p)
```

satisfying

```text
δ_p >= 1  iff  p is in L.
```

Define

```text
Delta = sum over p of δ_p,
Delta_support = number of p with det(1-F_p)=0.
```

### 4.2 Canonical unit-root detector

Let

```text
ell_p = 1_L(p) in {0,1}.
```

Take a one-dimensional line and let Frobenius act by the unit

```text
F_p = 2-ell_p.
```

Because `p>=7`, both possible eigenvalues `1` and `2` are units. Then

```text
det(1-F_p)
  = 1-(2-ell_p)
  = ell_p-1.
```

Therefore

```text
det(1-F_p)=0
    iff ell_p=1
    iff p is an actual leaf.
```

This is a genuine one-dimensional unit-root Frobenius module over a point. It is tautological because the eigenvalue is defined from the exact leaf indicator, but that is precisely the collapse test.

The indicator need not be produced by enumerating a precomputed support list. Section 2 constructs it canonically from the literal recurrence values and masks. Therefore “do not enumerate the support” is not enough to exclude the tautology. A noncollapsed definition must prohibit using the lower zero tests themselves when defining the exceptional spectrum.

### 4.3 Theorem: exact DSFD collapse

For every finite actual leaf set `L` and every `D>=0`, the following are equivalent:

1. `#L <= D`.
2. There exists an exact local Frobenius detector with `Delta_support <= D`.
3. There exists an exact local Frobenius detector with `Delta <= D`.

Moreover, the canonical detector has

```text
Delta_support = Delta = #L.
```

**Proof.** Every exact detector contributes at least one exceptional local factor at every leaf, so

```text
#L <= Delta_support <= Delta.
```

This proves either detector bound implies `#L<=D`. Conversely, the one-dimensional detector above is exceptional exactly on `L`, with multiplicity one at each leaf. If `#L<=D`, it supplies both detector bounds. QED.

### 4.4 One-sided detectors

Suppose only

```text
actual leaf p  implies  det(1-F_p)=0,
```

so false positives are allowed. A bound on the exceptional set is then strictly stronger than the leaf bound. But unrestricted existence still collapses because the exact detector is an allowed one-sided detector.

To obtain a genuinely stronger statement, one must fix the detector **before** the lower zero support is known and restrict its construction class. The canonical such object is the fixed-gcd support scheme.

## 5. Canonical pre-support Frobenius detector

### 5.1 Fixed integers and honest saturation

For an actual selected upper state `(q,t)`, put

```text
A(q,t)   = b_{6q+t},
B_+(q,t) = b_{12q+t},
B_-(q,t) = b_{q-1-t}.
```

Let `E_σ` be the finite fixed row-normalization ledger. Saturate the ideal generated by the two fixed values away from `q E_σ`:

```text
I_σ(q,t) = (A(q,t), B_σ(q,t)) : (q E_σ)^infinity.
```

Because `Z` is a PID, write

```text
I_σ(q,t) = (G_σ(q,t)),
```

where `G_σ` is the fully normalized `q`-free fixed carrier.

The current repository correctly records only

```text
log G_σ(q,t) = O(q)
```

as a general envelope. It does **not** prove `Theta(q)`, and `G_σ` may be `1`. This correction does not change the support argument.

### 5.2 Arithmetic support scheme

Define

```text
S_{σ,q,t} = Spec Z / I_σ(q,t) = Spec Z / G_σ(q,t).
```

This is a zero-dimensional vertical arithmetic scheme constructed from the two fixed Apéry values by ideal operations; no factorization or enumeration of closed points is required.

For a prime `p` not dividing `q E_σ`,

```text
(S_{σ,q,t}) over F_p is nonempty
    iff p divides G_σ(q,t)
    iff p divides A(q,t) and B_σ(q,t).
```

On the literal raw windows, the fixed-index Lucas serialization places every actual lower leaf in this support. Additional residual masks and the plus minus-first exclusion can only delete points. Hence

```text
actual leaf set in I
    is a subset of
support(S_{σ,q,t}) intersect I.
```

### 5.3 Which Frobenius, on which space

Fix an auxiliary coefficient prime `ell_0` outside the lower shell. For each lower prime `p != ell_0`, let

```text
V_p = H_c^0 of the reduced geometric special fibre
      of S_{σ,q,t} at p, with Q_{ell_0} coefficients.
```

Geometric Frobenius at `p` acts on `V_p`.

There are exactly two cases:

- if `p` does not divide `G_σ`, the fibre is empty and `V_p=0`;
- if `p` divides `G_σ`, the reduced fibre is one rational point, `V_p` is one-dimensional, and Frobenius acts as the identity.

Therefore the local determinant is

```text
P_p(Z) = det(1-Z Frob_p | V_p)
       = 1-Z   if p divides G_σ(q,t),
       = 1     otherwise.
```

In particular,

```text
P_p(1)=0  iff  p divides G_σ(q,t).
```

This is a completely precise Frobenius detector:

```text
space:
    H_c^0 of the reduced special fibre of Spec Z/G;

operator:
    geometric Frobenius at the lower characteristic p;

signal:
    det(1-Frob_p)=0;

sparsity:
    few lower primes p for which the local factor is nontrivial;

detection:
    every actual leaf lies in that exceptional support.
```

It avoids `q`-local unit erasure by living at the lower characteristics and packages all lower primes into one arithmetic scheme. But its spectrum is maximally uninformative: every nonempty fibre has the same one-dimensional eigenvalue-`1` spectrum. The entire problem is the external support set.

### 5.4 Determinant sparsity equals restricted-radical sparsity

For a candidate cell or shell `I`, define

```text
S_{σ,q,t}(I) = #{p in I : p divides G_σ(q,t)}.
```

Then

```text
S_{σ,q,t}(I) = #{p in I : P_p(1)=0}.
```

The associated shell radical is

```text
Rad_I(G_σ)
  = product over p in I, p divides G_σ, of p.
```

Since all such `p` are comparable with `T`,

```text
log Rad_I(G_σ)
  = S_{σ,q,t}(I) log T + O_J(S_{σ,q,t}(I)).
```

Therefore

```text
S_{σ,q,t}(I) <<_J H Y^(-ε)
```

is exactly a support-blind restricted-radical theorem for the fixed gcd. It is not supplied by the Frobenius calculation; the calculation merely renames each supporting prime as a nontrivial local Euler factor.

Because of residual masks and minus-first assignment, the support inclusion can be strict. Consequently the support-scheme bound is strictly stronger, at the exposed interface, than the actual bound `K << H Y^(-ε)`. This is the noncollapsed version of DSFD, but it is the route already isolated in Q4300, Q4310, and Q4314.

### 5.5 First exact failed implication

The following implication is false even for the simplest arithmetic support schemes:

```text
bounded local Frobenius rank,
one eigenvalue,
a two-term local determinant,
and bounded local conductor

        imply

few residue characteristics with nonempty fibre.
```

For any finite set `S` of primes, put

```text
N_S = product over p in S of p,
X_S = Spec Z/N_S.
```

At every `p` in `S`, the reduced fibre is one point and

```text
det(1-Z Frob_p)=1-Z.
```

At every `p` outside `S`, the fibre is empty. The local spectral shape is identical for every choice of `S`, while `#S` is arbitrary.

Thus **internal spectral sparsity does not imply external support sparsity**. The smallest premise repairing this failure is the external support theorem itself, namely the restricted-radical bound. Frobenius supplies no smaller premise.

## 6. Why the natural Apéry Frobenius is not the detector

The repository contains a genuine Apéry differential `F`-isocrystal and, when the inhomogeneous companion is retained, a mixed rank-four extension. These are important vertical objects, but neither determinant detects the q6 leaf event.

### 6.1 Invertibility

A Frobenius structure on an overconvergent `F`-isocrystal is an isomorphism after inverting `p`. Therefore

```text
det(Frob_p) is nonzero.
```

A vanishing detector cannot be the determinant of Frobenius itself. One must choose a special value such as `det(1-Frob_p)` or add a marked evaluation map. Neither is automatic.

### 6.2 Row-key blindness

The natural rank-three Apéry `F`-crystal is attached to the Picard-Fuchs equation on the geometric parameter line. Its Frobenius is semilinear over

```text
t maps to t^p.
```

The q6 leaf instead asks whether two specified coefficients of the Apéry solution, at the `p`-dependent recurrence-clock indices from Section 2, vanish modulo `p`. The ordinary Frobenius characteristic polynomial has no slots for

```text
r_σ(p),
a_σ(p),
the raw window,
the residual masks,
the minus-first assignment.
```

To make those coordinates visible, one must add marked evaluation maps. Once inserted, their rank jump is the Koszul/norm detector or the torsion support complex already classified.

### 6.3 Translation is not Frobenius

The recurrence transfer from index `n` to `n+h` is the `h`-fold iterate of the nonautonomous difference cocycle over

```text
n maps to n+1.
```

It is not the `h`-th power of crystalline Frobenius. On `F_p`-points, absolute Frobenius fixes the clock coordinate, while recurrence translation advances it. The earlier Dwork audit in

```text
drops/Q6547-6691acda.md
```

proved this category mismatch explicitly. The two q6 row shifts therefore cannot be reinterpreted as two powers of one Frobenius operator without a new hybrid difference/Frobenius structure.

### 6.4 Determinants ignore mixed extension classes

For a Frobenius-stable extension

```text
0 -> A -> M -> B -> 0,
```

one may choose a block-triangular matrix

```text
F_M = [F_A  *]
      [ 0  F_B].
```

Then

```text
det(1-Z F_M)
  = det(1-Z F_A) det(1-Z F_B),
```

independent of the extension entry. The same is true of `det(F_M)`. Thus the mixed divided-state or normal-function entry is invisible to the ordinary determinant and characteristic polynomial.

A determinant using the marked extension coordinate must be an augmented minor or mapping-cone determinant. That returns to a marked torsion/Fitting object.

## 7. Characteristic trilemma

Every proposed detector must choose a coefficient characteristic. The three possibilities exhaust the natural options.

### 7.1 Upper characteristic q: common field, erased support

Every legal lower prime satisfies `0<p<q`, so its image in `F_q` is a unit. In the recurrence-algebraic presentation, a lower marked divisibility row has the form

```text
F(p) = p Z.
```

After base change to `F_q`, this becomes the split graph equation

```text
Z = p^(-1) F(p),
```

which imposes no condition on `p`. With any number of lower rows, the Smith pivots are units. Honest saturation leaves the upper elimination ideal unchanged.

The fixed-gcd support scheme says the same thing more tersely. Because the complete `q`-primary part was removed,

```text
q does not divide G_σ(q,t),
```

and hence

```text
(Z/G_σ) tensor F_q = 0.
```

There is no lower support in the `q`-fibre for Frobenius to inspect.

Consequences:

- a determinant polynomial in a candidate variable over `F_q` whose roots contain all partners is exactly CQRD;
- in the natural recurrence-algebraic class, the determinant is support-blind after unit saturation;
- unrestricted existence of a low-degree determinant polynomial is equivalent to the partner-set bound.

A common-`q` determinant does not revive the route.

### 7.2 Each lower characteristic p: exact support, orthogonal components

At characteristic `p`, the two lower zero tests are meaningful and the exact detectors exist. But for distinct primes `p` and `p'`, the state spaces live in different fields.

Over the semilocal product

```text
R_I = product over p in I of F_p,
```

there are orthogonal primitive idempotents `e_p`. Every exact detector decomposes componentwise, and Frobenius is componentwise. Every additive rank or multiplicity ledger receives a separate nonnegative contribution from each supporting prime. There is no cross-prime cancellation.

For the canonical detector,

```text
sum over p in I of dim ker(1-F_p)
  = K_{x,I,σ}.
```

Thus the lower-characteristic detector survives unit erasure locally, but its global sparsity is exactly the target count.

If one descends these components to a characteristic-zero finite presentation, the first Fitting divisor contains every supporting prime. If `S` is the actual leaf set, then

```text
product over p in S of p
    divides Fitt_0(C),
```

and hence

```text
h_Fit(C)
  >= sum over p in S of log p
  = K log T + O_J(K).
```

For a DPRM matching with `2R_C` distinct endpoints, this is the banked

```text
2R_C log T + O_J(R_C)
```

information floor. Growing matrix dimensions distribute the cost but do not remove it.

This is not an assertion that the actual fixed gcd has logarithmic height `Theta(q)`. It is a conditional lower bound forced by the presence of marked primes. The Q4310 correction remains fully respected.

### 7.3 Auxiliary characteristic lambda: common comparison, missing transport

A vertical Apéry `F`-crystal at an auxiliary prime `lambda` detects `lambda`-adic or mod-`lambda` recurrence data. An actual leaf is defined by two congruences modulo the independently varying lower characteristic `p`. No banked identity implies that the same row state, or any determinant built from it, is exceptional modulo `lambda`.

A pre-support determinant polynomial or determinantal locus modulo `lambda` containing all actual leaves would provide explicit residue containers. At the matching scale, that is the **effective** auxiliary-class law, already proved equivalent to common-field root descent. The bare numerical `2/3`-class law survives only because it need not exhibit containers.

Therefore an auxiliary determinant has two outcomes:

- if effective and pre-support, it is the closed root-certificate route;
- if it merely asserts that actual exceptional classes are few, it is the bare auxiliary distribution target itself.

Known modularity, Dwork/Lucas, Cartier, CM, Atkin-Swinnerton-Dyer, and mod-`lambda` Galois descriptions remain vertical. Their Frobenius operators do not turn a `p`-special-fibre zero into a `lambda`-special-fibre determinant condition.

## 8. Exhaustion of determinant-shaped variants

### 8.1 Determinant polynomial in the numerical candidate over F_q

Suppose one constructs

```text
Delta_x(X) = det A_x(X) in F_q[X]
```

with

```text
actual leaf p  implies  Delta_x(p)=0.
```

A degree bound

```text
deg Delta_x << H Y^(-ε)
```

is exactly a CQRD root certificate. For an unrestricted detector, existence is equivalent to the cardinality bound. In the recurrence-algebraic class, the saturated candidate elimination ideal is zero. Closed.

### 8.2 Auxiliary determinant residue container

If a determinant vanishes on at most `O(lambda^(2/3))` residue classes and every leaf lies in those classes, this is an effective fibrewise `2/3`-class law. At the matching scale

```text
D = H Y^(-ε),
L = D^(3/2),
```

that object is equivalent to root descent. Closed in the effective recurrence-algebraic class. The bare non-effective class-count statement remains open.

### 8.3 Integral determinant or perfect complex

If a finite-presentation complex becomes non-acyclic modulo every leaf prime, its determinant/Fitting divisor contains their product. This is the fixed-gcd, scalar, or PGMC/Fitting route. It remembers support and pays the information floor. It supplies no automatic upper bound on the number of primes.

### 8.4 Separate local determinants at each p

These detect the event exactly, but their exceptional support has size `K`. A sparse-support theorem is the target; internal spectral shape is irrelevant.

Thus “determinant” does not identify a fifth algebraic category. It selects one of four already classified routes.

## 9. Why alternative notions of spectral sparsity fail

The only useful sparsity is sparsity in the **external prime parameter**. Common internal notions fail.

### 9.1 Few distinct eigenvalues

The identity matrix `I_K` has one distinct eigenvalue and a `K`-dimensional `1`-eigenspace. One eigenvalue can carry arbitrarily many leaves.

### 9.2 Few characteristic-polynomial monomials

A cyclic permutation matrix `C_K` has

```text
det(XI-C_K) = X^K-1,
```

only two monomials, but dimension `K`. Sparse coefficients do not bound spectral multiplicity.

In characteristic `r`, binomial collapse makes this worse:

```text
(1-Z)^(r^m) = 1-Z^(r^m).
```

A root of arbitrarily large multiplicity can be encoded by two terms.

### 9.3 Small determinant set

Symplectic or weight-normalized Frobenius matrices can have a fixed determinant for arbitrarily large rank. A determinant taking one value contains no leaf-count information.

### 9.4 Low rank instead of low corank

A large identity block has rank `K` and `1-F=0`. Counting the corank of `1-F` gives `K`, hence the target. Counting rank points in the wrong direction. This mirrors the banked partial-isometry no-go.

### 9.5 Low local conductor

The schemes `Spec Z/N_S` have identical rank-one local factors at every supporting prime, with arbitrary support size. Bounded local conductor does not control the number of vertical bad fibres without a global support theorem.

### 9.6 Low degree or root multiplicity

The degree of a direct-sum determinant or the order of its root at `1` is at least `K`, and the canonical detector attains `K`. This is the exact collapse theorem.

Every internal sparsity notion is therefore either too weak to count leaves or, once strengthened to count support/multiplicity, equivalent to the desired bound.

## 10. Finite-presentation/Frobenius dichotomy

### Theorem 10.1 — vertical/Fitting dichotomy

Fix an actual selected state `x`, sign `σ`, and finite lower-prime candidate set `I`. Consider a recurrence-algebraic or finite-presentation construction intended to detect the literal lower event.

Exactly one of the following occurs.

1. **Flat or lisse Frobenius object.** The construction remains generically nonzero and gives invertible Frobenius at almost all primes. The natural determinant is independent of the marked coefficient equations. Detecting leaves requires a new horizontal implication placing the Frobenius conjugacy class in a proper determinantal locus. No such implication is banked.

2. **Marked rank-jump object.** Evaluation maps for the two lower zeros are inserted so that cohomology or a kernel appears at leaves. The object is vertical torsion at those primes; its Fitting divisor contains their product. Its support detector is the fixed-gcd/radical object.

3. **Direct q-base change.** The lower prime is a unit, marked rows split, and lower support disappears. The detector is upper-only.

4. **Semilocal lower-characteristic product.** The detector is exact componentwise, but components are orthogonal and total defect is additive, equal to or at least the leaf count.

5. **Auxiliary characteristic.** Detection requires an unproved horizontal `p`-to-auxiliary transport. An effective determinant container is root descent in disguise.

**Proof.** The `q`-local and semilocal claims are the unit-pivot and orthogonal-idempotent calculations above. A generically acyclic finite-presentation complex whose special fibre has excess cohomology at `p` has `p` in the support of its determinant/Fitting ideal; multiplying over distinct primes gives the marked-prime information floor. A flat/lisse object has no such rank-jump support, so the leaf must instead be expressed as a conjugacy-class condition, which the unmarked Apéry Frobenius does not contain. The auxiliary case is the already proved effective-class-law/root-descent equivalence. QED.

This theorem allows arbitrary growth in row count and matrix dimensions. It does not rely on the bounded-transfer impossibility theorem.

## 11. What a genuinely new horizontal detector would have to add

A genuinely new object must avoid both sides of the dichotomy:

- it cannot insert the exact leaf indicator, or existence/sparsity collapses;
- it cannot be the fixed-gcd torsion scheme, or sparsity is restricted radical;
- it cannot live only at `q`, because of unit erasure;
- it cannot be a product of unrelated `p`-adic modules, because defects add;
- it cannot merely give explicit auxiliary residue containers, because that is effective root descent.

The first genuinely new premise would have to be a **support-independent horizontal Frobenius implication**.

For each actual selected state `x`, construct before testing the lower zeros:

1. a fixed finite-type arithmetic parameter space carrying the candidate-prime and row data;

4. a mixed identity proving


5. a short-interval Frobenius theorem showing that the candidate section meets `D_x` at most `H Y^(-ε)` times.

The nontrivial datum is the mixed implication, not the determinant. None of the current vertical theories supplies it. Adding lower evaluation rows to a perfect complex gives the Fitting case.
