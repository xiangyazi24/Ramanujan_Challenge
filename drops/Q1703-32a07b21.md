# Q1703: Exceptional-degree-drop audit

## Summary

The proposed degree-drop mechanism is equivalent to asking whether the reciprocal polynomial

```text
R(t) = 1/C(t) mod (t^p-t)
```

can lose its top even coefficient under the endpoint and WZ seam constraints. The coefficient calculation does not produce a contradiction. The remaining term is an explicit seam residual; it is not forced to vanish by the interior recurrence.

## 1. Polynomial inversion constraint

Write

```text
C(t)=sum_{j=0}^m c_j t^{2j},
R(t)=sum_{j=0}^{m} r_j t^{2j}.
```

The identity

```text
C(t)R(t)=1 mod (t^p-t)
```

only determines the coefficients after reduction modulo the cyclic relation. Since t^p=t, the top even coefficient receives contributions from all pairs with degree sum at least p-1. Therefore the coefficient of t^{p-1} in C R is not a single product c_m r_m.

The hoped-for implication

```text
deg R <= p-3  => contradiction
```

would require the aliasing terms to cancel. There is no such one-term support statement.

## 2. WZ summation by parts

For

```text
nu_t=(t^2-1)C(t)
```

and

```text
(t+1/2)^3 nu_{t+1}-t(34t^2+3/2)nu_t+(t-1/2)^3nu_{t-1}=0,
```

multiplication by R(t)/nu_t gives the finite difference identity on the open interval:

```text
sum_{t=1}^{m-1} Delta_t(W_t)= boundary + seam.
```

The interior terms cancel, but the endpoint terms are

```text
W_m-W_1
```

and the seam value depends on the extension at t=m and t=-m. The condition

```text
u_{m-1}=5u_m
```

fixes only one linear relation and does not kill the residual.

## 3. Explicit residual

After reduction, the coefficient obstruction is the seam term

```text
Res = ((m+1/2)^3 nu_{m+1} R(m) - (m-1/2)^3 nu_{m-1}R(m-1)) / nu_m.
```

The interior recurrence proves

```text
S = 0  =>  Res = 0,
```

but not

```text
Res != 0.
```

A further identity for the free extension value nu_{m+1} is required.

## 4. Failure mode

The cyclic finite-difference argument has two independent failure modes:

1. **Isotropic kernel:** the top coefficient can disappear because several aliased monomials contribute with opposite signs.
2. **Multiple-root case:** C can have repeated values on the finite field even though the half-interval values are nonzero.

Both prevent a pure degree argument.

## Conclusion

The exact surviving obstruction is the seam residual above. The exceptional-degree-drop attack does not currently yield a contradiction from the stated hypotheses alone. The missing ingredient is an additional endpoint identity controlling the free continuation across the singular indices; the WZ recurrence on 1<=t<m is insufficient by itself.
