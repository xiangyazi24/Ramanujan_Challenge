 \right).
\tag{9.6}
\]
Thus, once `nu` is large, the generic factor still has quadratic degree.

This answers the “factors only on classified resonant loci?” question negatively.  Reflection classifies the **nonsquare** root-difference factor, but all generic non-reflection differences survive in the large square `Q_d^2`.

There is also a crucial selected-root issue.  For the distinguished root `r`, being a reflection mate at shift `a` means
\[
 r+a=\rho_d(r)
 \iff
 a+b=0,
\]
which the hard support excludes.  But the scalar value
\[
 L_d(a)=0
\]
may still occur because **some other unmarked root** `x` has `x+a=rho_d(x)`.  Therefore one cannot divide a scalar resultant by `L_d(a)` merely because the distinguished root is not resonant.  That would again convert an aligned marked root statement into an unmarked scalar statement.

The aligned gcd of Section 7 is the only safe object if one needs to remember which root is the SLCCR root.

# 10. Exact finite root-set reformulation

For a fixed gap `d`, let
\[
 Z_{p,d}
 =\{x\in\mathbb F_p:\overline N_d(x)=0\}.
\tag{10.1}
\]
For a shift `A`, put
\[
 g_{p,d}(A)
 =|Z_{p,d}\cap(Z_{p,d}-A)|
 =\deg\Gamma_{p;d,A}.
\tag{10.2}
\]
Then, purely combinatorially,
\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{A\in\mathbb F_p}g_{p,d}(A)=|Z_{p,d}|^2,
}
\tag{10.3}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{A\ne0}g_{p,d}(A)
 =|Z_{p,d}|(|Z_{p,d}|-1).
}
\tag{10.4}
\]
Every ordered pair `(x,y)` of roots has the unique shift `A=y-x`; this proves the identities.

For a marked base root `r in Z_{p,d}`, the polynomial reduction (5.4) gives
\[
 K_x(b,Y)
 \le
 \#\{a:I_x(a,b)=1,\ r+a\in Z_{p,d}\}.
\tag{10.5}
\]
Dropping the physical selector gives
\[
 K_x(b,Y)\le |Z_{p,d}|-1.
\tag{10.6}
\]
This is exact and aligned, but it is not strong enough.  The committed source gives only the polynomial-degree bound for an individual gap polynomial in its nonvanishing range, and no current theorem forces
\[
 |Z_{p,d}|\ll T^{14/31-\eta}
\]
for the long gaps represented by the SLCCR bases.

Grouping bases by `d` gives the legal but presently nonclosing upper bound
\[
 \sum_{\text{bases at }d}K_x(b,Y)
 \le
 B_{p,d}(T)|Z_{p,d}|
 \le
 |Z_{p,d}|^2,
\tag{10.7}
\]
where `B_{p,d}(T)` is the number of hard-range marked base roots at that gap.  Thus a gap-root second-moment theorem
\[
 \sum_{d\in\mathrm{long}}|Z_{p,d}|^2
 \ll T^{76/31-\eta+o(1)}
\tag{10.8}
\]
would be sufficient.  No such local `T`-scale transpose-energy theorem is in the current source.  The proved column energy controls the opposite incidence moment—pairs of return **levels in one fixed column**—and cannot simply be transposed.

This is a useful restatement of the missing input, but not a proof of it.

# 11. Complete degeneracy audit

The restart reduction is exact only after keeping the same regularity distinctions already present in the canonical formal/physical machinery.  Here is the complete list that matters for this attack.

## 11.1 Orientation and wrap

The projective dictionary `N_d(r)=0 iff pi(r)=pi(r+d)` is a nonwrapping statement.  For a formal `+/-Y` pair, choose the sign/order for which the two regular representatives satisfy `s=r+d`.  The opposite lift just exchanges the columns.  No new multiplicity beyond the already present fixed two-lift normalization is created.

If a proposed term crosses the forbidden projective edge, the restart dictionary is not legal.  Such a term cannot be rescued by treating residues as an ordinary integer interval.  Q5304's long-clean residual already excludes the canonical polluted/boundary pieces before SLCCR is invoked.

## 11.2 Singular edge coefficient

If an intervening factor `r+j` vanishes modulo `p`, the transfer product is singular and one may not divide by the neighboring continuant.  The unit conclusions
\[
 N_{d+1}(r)\ne0,
 \qquad
 N_{a+1}(r)\ne0
\]
are therefore asserted only on the regular windows where the projective collision dictionary itself is valid.

This is not a hidden extra hypothesis: it is exactly the same open set used by the committed restart theorem.

## 11.3 Adjacent-zero degeneration

On a regular root `N_d(r)=0`, simultaneous vanishing of `N_{d-1}(r)` or `N_{d+1}(r)` would contradict invertibility of the transfer word.  Hence adjacent continuant vanishing is not an additional generic degeneration on the clean branch.  It can occur only together with a singular edge/bad window already separated above.

## 11.4 Small indices

The formulas with `N_{a-1}` or `N_{d-1}` require the obvious endpoint convention.  AT-P24 has deep positive gaps, so `a,d>=2` in the sector at issue.  Nothing in the exponent argument uses the exceptional `a=1` or `d=1` cases.

## 11.5 Degree drop modulo `p`

The characteristic-zero degree is exactly `3(d-1)`, but the leading coefficient satisfies its own recurrence and can vanish modulo some primes.  Thus `deg overline N_d` may be smaller.  This can only help the direct root bound on that particular `(p,d)`; no current theorem gives the uniform degree-drop frequency required to close the sector.

The committed nonvanishing theorem on the admissible projective-gap range prevents replacing the polynomial by the zero polynomial in the legal collision count, but it does not preserve the full characteristic-zero degree.

## 11.6 Repeated roots / discriminant primes

Uniform characteristic-zero squarefreeness of every `N_d` is not a current canonical theorem.  Reduction modulo `p` can also create repeated roots.  For aligned **distinct** root counting the safe object is
\[
 \gcd(\overline N_d,X^p-X),
\]
not a discriminant-normalized formula that assumes simplicity.

Repeated roots only add resultant/Hasse multiplicity.  They do not produce an upper bound for the marked common-return count without a new valuation theorem.

## 11.7 Diagonal shift

The scalar shift resultant always has the factor `A^n`.  The selected return level has `a>0`, so the literal diagonal `A=0` is absent.  Deflating it is algebraically valid but removes no positive power from the degree.

## 11.8 Marked reflection resonance

For the marked base root, the aligned reflection equation is
\[
 a+b=0\pmod p.
\]
The physical hard support has nonzero nonwrapping `h=a+b`, so this marked resonance is absent.

The **scalar** reflection factor can nevertheless vanish at the same `a` because of another unmarked root.  This is why scalar factor removal cannot replace aligned root tracking.

## 11.9 Forced center

When `d` is even, `F_{d,r}` has the center root `2A+b=0`.  There is at most one such `a` per base pair.  It contributes at most `T^{2+o(1)}` to the complete base sum and is therefore harmless at the `76/31` target.

## 11.10 Formal versus physical companion

The companion root produced by `J_{a,d}` or by `A -> -b-A` is a **formal projective collision root**.  It does not become a zero of the Apéry sequence, does not receive a moving-prime lift, and does not inherit `I_x`.  It may be used only to infer algebraic multiplicity or symmetry of a formal gcd.

This distinction is the same one enforced in Q5304 and `gcdtail_result.tex`.

## 11.11 Integral content

The integer shift resultant has leading and discriminant data depending strongly on `d`; equation (8.6) already exhibits that dependence.  The current source has no bounded-content theorem for `mathscr R_d(A)`.  I therefore do not use a claim such as “all content is `O(1)`” or “all bad primes are classified by reflection.”  For fixed `p`, reduction and squarefree-kernel normalization is safer, but still leaves the large generic degree.

# 13. First literal obstruction, stated as sharply as possible

The obstruction is not merely “the resultant degree is too large.”  It occurs one step earlier.

The SLCCR base condition itself is
\[
 N_d(r)=0.
\]
But exactly on that locus, renewal forces
\[
 N_a(s)
 =N_{d+1}(r)^{-1}N_{a+d}(r).
\tag{13.1}
\]
Therefore a common level is
\[
 N_a(r)=N_{a+d}(r)=0.
\tag{13.2}
\]
The two apparent column sequences have collapsed to a single sequence and a fixed additive lag.

So any argument of the form

```text
large separation of r and s
  => generic algebraic independence of the sequences N_a(r), N_a(s)
  => small gcd/resultant in a
```

fails at its first implication.  The projective base collision is exactly a restart conjugacy between those sequences.

This is a **literal source identity** on every regular base pair, not an exponent-level toy model and not a hypothetical incidence construction.

The missing theorem must therefore see something not contained in that restart identity.  Examples of genuinely new information would be:

- a selected translated-root-set estimate for `Z_{p,d}` which keeps the marked root and the literal `I_x(a,b)` support;

```latex
\begin{lemma}[Restart lock for a regular projective base collision]
\label{lem:slccr-restart-lock}
Let $p\ge 7$, and let $r,s\in\mathbb F_p$ admit regular nonwrapping
representatives with
\[
 s=r+d,\qquad d\ge2,
\]
and suppose
\[
 N_d(r)=0.
\]
Let $a\ge2$ be such that the $a$-windows from $r$ and from $s$ are also
regular and nonwrapping.  Then
\[
 N_{a+d}(r)=N_{d+1}(r)N_a(s),
\]
and $N_{d+1}(r)\ne0$.  Consequently
\[
 \boxed{N_a(s)=0\iff N_{a+d}(r)=0.}
\]
If in addition $N_a(r)=0$, then $N_{a+1}(r)\ne0$ and
\[
 \boxed{
 N_a(r)=N_a(s)=0
 \iff
 N_a(r)=N_d(r+a)=0.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 \boxed{
 N_d(r)=N_a(r)=N_a(s)=0
 \iff
 N_d(r)=N_a(r)=N_{a+d}(r)=0.}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Using $N_h(x)=U_{h-1}(x+1)$ in the renewal identity
\[
U_{m+g+1}(t)
=U_{m+1}(t)U_g(t+m+1)
 -(t+m+1)^6U_m(t)U_{g-1}(t+m+2),
\]
first with $(m,g,t)=(d-1,a-1,r+1)$ gives
\[
N_{a+d}(r)
=N_{d+1}(r)N_a(r+d)
 -(r+d+1)^6N_d(r)N_{a-1}(r+d+1).
\]
Since $r+d=s$ and $N_d(r)=0$, this is the first displayed identity.
Regularity of the transfer word implies that adjacent continuants cannot
vanish together; hence $N_{d+1}(r)\ne0$.

Interchanging $a$ and $d$ in the same renewal identity gives
\[
N_{a+d}(r)
=N_{a+1}(r)N_d(r+a)
 -(r+a+1)^6N_a(r)N_{d-1}(r+a+1).
\]
If $N_a(r)=0$, regularity similarly gives $N_{a+1}(r)\ne0$.
Comparing the two formulas proves the remaining equivalences.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Aligned level polynomial and reflection]
\label{cor:slccr-aligned-level-polynomial}
In the setting of Lemma~\ref{lem:slccr-restart-lock}, suppose additionally
that the centered base parameter $b$ satisfies
\[
 2r+d+1=b\pmod p.
\]
Put
\[
 F_{d,r}(A)=\overline N_d(r+A).
\]
Then, with $n_d=3(d-1)$,
\[
 \boxed{
 2^{n_d}F_{d,r}(A)=\overline M_d(b+2A),}
\]
and therefore
\[
 \boxed{
 F_{d,r}(-b-A)=(-1)^{d-1}F_{d,r}(A).}
\]
In particular
\[
 A(A+b)\mid F_{d,r}(A).
\]
If $d$ is even, then the forced center gives the additional factor
$2A+b$.

For every simultaneous return $N_a(r)=N_a(s)=0$ one has
\[
 F_{d,r}(a)=0.
\]
The marked reflection-mate case is exactly
\[
 a+b=0\pmod p.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The definition
\[
 M_d(Z)=2^{3(d-1)}N_d\!\left(\frac{Z-d-1}{2}\right)
\]
and $2r+d+1=b$ give
\[
2^{3(d-1)}N_d(r+A)=M_d(b+2A).
\]
The reflection identity for $M_d$ gives the second formula.  Since
$F_{d,r}(0)=0$, reflection gives $F_{d,r}(-b)=0$; when $d$ is even,
$M_d$ is odd and hence vanishes at its center.  The final assertion follows
from Lemma~\ref{lem:slccr-restart-lock}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Aligned reflection pairing]
\label{cor:slccr-aligned-reflection-pairing}
For fixed $d,a$ define
\[
\Gamma_{d,a}(X)=
\gcd_{\mathbb F_p[X]}
\bigl(\overline N_d(X),\overline N_d(X+a),X^p-X\bigr).
\]
The involution
\[
 J_{a,d}(x)=-x-a-d-1
\]
permutes the roots of $\Gamma_{d,a}$.  Its fixed points satisfy
\[
 2x+a+d+1=0.
\]
For the marked SLCCR root $x=r$ with $2r+d+1=b$, fixedness is exactly
\[
 a+b=0\pmod p.
\]
Thus on the nonwrapping hard support the marked aligned root has a distinct
formal companion, but no physical or selected predicate is inherited by
that companion.
\end{corollary}
```

\[
 N_d(r+a)=0

together with the original one-column return `N_a(r)=0`.

**Only classified resonant factors: FAIL.**  Diagonal, marked reflection, and center factors are classified and harmless, but the squarefree shift resultant retains a large generic square factor made of ordinary root differences.  Those are not classified AT-P24 resonances.

**Aligned-root preservation: PASS in the gcd formulation.**  The polynomial `Gamma_{d,a}` keeps the distinguished root.  Reflection gives a formal companion but no selected/physical promotion.

**Sector saving to the required exponent: FAIL with current source.**  Forced center/diagonal/reflection loci are paid, but the generic long-clean sector remains at the one-column `T^{2/3}` wall.  No `T^{20/93}` gain follows.
