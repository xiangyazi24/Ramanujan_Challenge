# 4. Why no rational matrix `W_n` exists

Assume

\[
W_nA_n-A_nW_{n+1}=A'_n
\]

with `W_n` rational over `Q(n)`. Right-multiply by `A_n^{-1}` and take traces:

\[
\operatorname{tr}W_n-
 \operatorname{tr}(A_nW_{n+1}A_n^{-1})
 =\operatorname{tr}(A'_nA_n^{-1}).
\]

Trace is invariant under conjugation, so

\[
\operatorname{tr}W_n-\operatorname{tr}W_{n+1}
 =\partial_e\log\det A_n(e)|_{e=0}.
\]

The determinant of the exact Gosper matrix is

\[
\boxed{
\det A_n(e)=
\frac{(n+1)^2(n+1-e)^2(2n+3)^6}
 {8(n+4)^6(n+4-e)(2n+9)(2n+7-2e)^2}. }
\]

Therefore

\[
\partial_e\log\det A_n(e)|_{e=0}
 =\frac{2n^2-n-33}{(n+1)(n+4)(2n+7)}
 =-\frac2{n+1}+\frac1{n+4}+\frac4{2n+7}.
\]

A rational function `f(n)` can be a forward difference `f(n)-f(n+1)` only if the sum of the discrete residues in every `Z`-shift orbit of poles is zero.

Here:

* integer orbit: residues `-2` at `n=-1` and `+1` at `n=-4`; total `-1`;
* half-integer orbit: residue `2` at `n=-7/2`; total `2`.

Both are nonzero. Hence the right-hand side has no rational antidifference. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{there is no }W_n\in M_3(\mathbb Q(n))
\text{ satisfying the matrix correction equation}. }
\]

This conclusion does not depend on a degree bound or a failed search.

# Final proof chain

The independent proof can now be organized as follows.

1. Derive `A_n(e)` by the displayed Gosper identity.
2. Split `R_n` into the finite harmonic part and the positive tail, including the sign at the zero factor.
3. At `n=0`, observe that the finite part vanishes and define
   `J_n = R_0 Phi_{0,n}`.
4. Use the positive-tail formula for `-J_n` and positivity of `A_n(0)`.
5. Add any scalar regularization multiple of `B_n`; it does not change the exterior wedge.
6. Insert the already-verified rational exterior gauge. Restore the cumulative scalar for literal identities, but discard it for projective ratios.
7. Apply the existing factor-`2/3` challenge contraction to obtain exponential projective decay and identify the challenge limit with the Catalan-bearing initial exterior direction.

The matrix `W_n` route is therefore both unnecessary and, over `Q(n)`, impossible.
