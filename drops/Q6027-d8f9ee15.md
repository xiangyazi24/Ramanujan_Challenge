## 7. Why `FJ2G-RAD` is strictly non-equivalent to final pair energy

The statement is strictly narrower in **domain and arithmetic object** than Q5958 `PDD_eta`:

1. it is imposed only after the unconditional deletion (3.6), on the exact `LG` complement;
2. it uses one explicit q-primitive C4 carrier gcd and one explicit same-root continuant certificate;
3. it is directly falsifiable by exact gcd, resultant, and shell-prime computations before enumerating literal second leaves.

It is a sufficient strengthening on that smaller domain, not a renaming of the final count.  Two exact false-positive mechanisms prove non-equivalence.

### 7.1 The C4 coefficient branch

Modulo a candidate \(\ell\) with \(\ell\mid X_s\),

\[
C_{0,s}\equiv b_{6q}Y_s\pmod\ell.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Thus

\[
\ell\mid K_s^{(1)},\quad \ell\nmid Y_s
\]

is possible through

\[
\ell\mid b_{6q}.
\tag{7.2}
\]

Writing \(d=q-\ell\), Lucas identifies this as the genuine coefficient branch

\[
\ell\mid b_{6d},
\tag{7.3}
\]

outside the fixed divisors of \(b_6\).  Such a prime lies in the one-carrier gcd but is not a common divisor of \(X_s,Y_s\).

The second carrier \(C_{1,s}\) would kill this branch by the no-consecutive-zero argument of §4.2, but adding it would restore exact equivalence to common support.  The one-carrier choice in (6.1) is therefore deliberate.

### 7.2 Projective-return phantoms

For \(U\ne V\),

\[
\ell\mid\Theta_s(\ell)
\]

only asserts that \(N_U,N_V\) have **some** common root in \(\mathbb F_\ell\).  It does not assert that the deterministic base \(x_s(\ell)\) is that root, and still less that the first coordinate of the projective orbit vanishes there.  The canonical distinguished-fibre audit calls these extra algebraic roots projective phantoms: the algebraic common-root fibre can be nonempty while the physical Apéry starting-zero fibre is empty.

The midpoint evaluations have the same one-way character: divisibility of \(\mathscr E_u^\sigma\) gives a projective return at the midpoint base but does not by itself provide the literal lower-zero pair.

Therefore one can have

\[
\mathcal R^{\rm FJ2G}_{s,\alpha,D}>0
\]

with no actual partner occurrence.  In particular, the final pair-energy target does not imply (6.5) or (6.9).  Conversely, (6.5) implies the pair bound through the injection (6.4).  The relation is strictly one-way.
