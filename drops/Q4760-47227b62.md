# 2. Cold proof of the reciprocal-fibre second moment

Expand
\[
\sum_{\delta\in J}R(\delta)^2
=
\sum_{\ell_1,\ell_2}
\#\left\{\delta\in J:\
\ell_1,\ell_2\in\mathcal R_{\delta;p,q,p'}

\tag{2.1}
\]
We separate \(\ell_1\ne\ell_2\) and \(\ell_1=\ell_2\).

## 2.1 Distinct moving primes

Fix distinct shell primes \(\ell_1,\ell_2\), both outside \(\{p,q,p'\}\).

The two fixed-\(p\) conditions say
\[
\delta\pmod p
\in p'\ell_1\mathcal Z_p
\cap p'\ell_2\mathcal Z_p.
\tag{2.2}
\]
Each dilate has cardinality \(Z(p)\), so the intersection occupies at most
\[
\boxed{Z(p)}
\tag{2.3}
\]
residue classes modulo \(p\). It is important that the count is \(Z(p)\), not \(Z(p)^2\): we count possible residues of \(\delta\), not ordered pairs of labels.

The moving conditions say
\[
\delta\pmod{\ell_i}
\in -pq\,\mathcal Z_{\ell_i},
\qquad i=1,2.
\tag{2.4}
\]
They occupy exactly \(Z(\ell_i)\) residue classes modulo \(\ell_i\).

The moduli
\[
p,\ell_1,\ell_2
\tag{2.5}
\]
are pairwise coprime. CRT therefore gives at most
\[
Z(p)Z(\ell_1)Z(\ell_2)
\tag{2.6}
\]
classes modulo \(p\ell_1\ell_2\). Every residue class contributes at most
\[
\frac{|J|}{p\ell_1\ell_2}+1
\tag{2.7}
\]
integers in \(J\). Hence
\[
\boxed{
N(\ell_1,\ell_2)
\le
Z(p)Z(\ell_1)Z(\ell_2)
\left(\frac{|J|}{p\ell_1\ell_2}+1\right).}
\tag{2.8}
\]
This is the exact place where the endpoint \(+1\) term enters.

Summing the fractional term gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{\rm off}^{\rm main}
&\ll
|J|\frac{Z(p)}p
\left(\sum_{\ell\asymp N}\frac{Z(\ell)}\ell\right)^2\\
&\ll
|J|\,
N^{2/3-1+2(3/5)+o(1)}\\
&=
\boxed{|J|N^{13/15+o(1)}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.9}
\]

The endpoint term is
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{\rm off}^{\rm end}
&\ll
Z(p)
\left(\sum_{\ell\asymp N}Z(\ell)\right)^2\\
&\ll N^{2/3+16/5+o(1)}\\
&=
\boxed{N^{58/15+o(1)}}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.10}
\]
At the present determinant length,
\[
|J|N^{13/15}
=N^{1337/300+o(1)},
\tag{2.11}
\]
whereas
\[
N^{58/15}=N^{1160/300}.
\tag{2.12}
\]
Thus the endpoint term is smaller by the fixed factor
\[

\tag{2.13}
\]
The advertised theorem would not follow uniformly for a very short determinant interval; here it follows because \(|J|\gg N^3\).

## 2.2 Equal moving prime

For \(\ell_1=\ell_2=\ell\), there is one fixed-\(p\) test and one moving test. The number of \(\delta\in J\) is at most

\begin{proposition}[Determinant second moment for a selected reciprocal fibre]
\label{prop:ad-reciprocal-fibre-second-moment}
Fix pairwise distinct primes $p,q,p'\asymp N$ in fixed ratio boxes,
and let $J\subset\mathbb Z$ be an interval with
$|J|=N^{359/100+o(1)}$.  For $\sigma\in\{\pm1\}$ define
\[
 \mathcal R_{\sigma,\delta;p,q,p'}
 =\left\{\ell\asymp N:\
 \begin{array}{l}
  \ell\text{ prime},\quad \ell\notin\{p,q,p'\},\\
  \sigma\delta\overline{p'\ell}\pmod p\in\mathcal Z_p,\\
  -\sigma\delta\overline{pq}\pmod\ell\in\mathcal Z_\ell
 \end{array}\right\},
 \qquad
 R(\delta)=|\mathcal R_{\sigma,\delta;p,q,p'}|.
\]
Then
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_{\delta\in J}R(\delta)^2
 \ll{}&
 |J|\frac{Z(p)}p
 \left(\sum_{\ell\asymp N}\frac{Z(\ell)}\ell\right)^2
 +Z(p)\left(\sum_{\ell\asymp N}Z(\ell)\right)^2\\
 &+|J|\frac{Z(p)}p
 \sum_{\ell\asymp N}\frac{Z(\ell)}\ell
 +Z(p)\sum_{\ell\asymp N}Z(\ell).
 \end{aligned}
\tag{RF2.1}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sum_{\delta\in J}R(\delta)^2
 \ll |J|N^{13/15+o(1)}.
\tag{RF2.2}
\]
The same bounds hold after imposing arbitrary additional deletion masks;
in particular they hold for the literal four-test sharp fibre.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Expand the left-hand side as a sum over ordered moving-prime pairs
$(\ell_1,\ell_2)$.  Suppose first that $\ell_1\ne\ell_2$.
The two fixed-$p$ tests require
\[
 \delta\pmod p
 \in p'\ell_1\mathcal Z_p\cap p'\ell_2\mathcal Z_p,
\]
which occupies at most $Z(p)$ classes modulo $p$.  The two moving tests
occupy $Z(\ell_i)$ classes modulo $\ell_i$.  Since
$p,\ell_1,\ell_2$ are pairwise coprime, CRT gives at most
$Z(p)Z(\ell_1)Z(\ell_2)$ classes modulo $p\ell_1\ell_2$.
Each class has at most $|J|/(p\ell_1\ell_2)+1$ representatives in $J$.
Summing gives the first two terms of \textup{(RF2.1)}.

\[
 Z(p)Z(\ell)\left(\frac{|J|}{p\ell}+1\right)
\]
possible determinants.  Summing gives the last two terms of
\textup{(RF2.1)}.

\[

 \sum_{\ell\asymp N}Z(\ell)\ll N^{8/5+o(1)},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\ell\asymp N}\frac{Z(\ell)}\ell
 \ll N^{3/5+o(1)}.
\]
The four terms in \textup{(RF2.1)} have respective sizes
\[

 |J|N^{4/15+o(1)},\quad
 N^{34/15+o(1)}.
\]
Since $|J|=N^{359/100+o(1)}$, the first dominates.  Additional masks
only shrink every fibre.
\end{proof}
