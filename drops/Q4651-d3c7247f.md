Let \(\mathscr P_T^\dagger\) be any fixed finite union of lower-shell prime intervals, so \(\ell\asymp_JT\), and assume \(7\Delta<\min\mathscr P_T^\dagger\).  Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\ell\in\mathscr P_T^\dagger}
 (\log\ell)
 \left[
 \min\{v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{2\Delta}),v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{7\Delta})\}
 +\min\{v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{\Delta}),v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{6\Delta})\}
 \right]
 \ll_J\Delta.}
 \tag{6.24}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \boxed{
 \#\{\ell\in\mathscr P_T^\dagger:
 \varepsilon^<_\ell(\Delta)>0
 \text{ or }\varepsilon^>_\ell(\Delta)>0\}
 \ll_J\frac{\Delta}{\log T}.}
 \tag{6.25}
\]

The implication in (6.25) uses only the first valuation level of the lifted residuals: \(\varepsilon>0\) is equivalent to simultaneous divisibility in (6.7) or (6.8).  The theorem does **not** assert that the higher lifted valuation of \(N_m(c\ell)\) equals the valuation of \(u_m\); Hensel/Taylor excess at higher powers is not silently discarded.

This distinction is important: (6.24) retains the common companion valuation multiplicity exactly, while (3.11)/(3.14) retain the full lifted Fitting excess.  Their supports agree, which is enough for the binary occurrence question, but their higher thicknesses need not.

 \frac{H/\log T}{H^2/\Lambda}

## CONDITIONAL

1. \((\mathrm{CFH})_\delta\) remains a valid separate global zero-count gateway; any \(\delta>0\) would beat \(8/5\) on that marginal problem.

3. FAR--SJRT and FG4Z remain valid selected-occurrence gateways, but neither is supplied by Fitting exactness or by the new reduced cofactor theorem.
4. An improvement of the fixed-gap primitive common divisor alone would still not close the pure-Smith-only channel unless accompanied by an actual survival/serialization theorem.

1. **Raw Smith torsion as primitive information.**  The six-unit factor is forced by the singular cut and is present before any primitive short return is tested.
2. **Staircase reuse as independent codegree.**  Q4631's \(K(K-1)\) side-cofactor incidences reuse the same \(K\) polluted tails.

\begin{lemma}[Two-row Smith purification]\label{lem:q6-two-row-smith-purification}
Fix a complete selected minus state $x=(q,t,\ldots)$ and put

 n=q-1-t,
 \qquad B=6q+t.

 v_\ell(\mathscr B_1^<)

\begin{lemma}[Reduced two-row companion cofactor]\label{lem:q6-reduced-companion-cofactor}

\[
 \mathfrak G^<_\Delta
 =\gcd(|u_{2\Delta}|,|u_{7\Delta}|),
 \qquad
 \mathfrak G^>_\Delta
 =\gcd(|u_\Delta|,|u_{6\Delta}|).
\]
Then
\[
 \log\mathfrak G^<_\Delta+\log\mathfrak G^>_\Delta
 \ll \Delta.
\]
For every prime $\ell>7\Delta$,
\[
 v_\ell(\mathfrak G^<_\Delta)
 =\min\{v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{2\Delta}),
          v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{7\Delta})\},
\]
\[
 v_\ell(\mathfrak G^>_\Delta)
 =\min\{v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{\Delta}),
          v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{6\Delta})\}.
\]
Moreover
\[
 \ell\mid N_{2\Delta}(2\ell),N_{7\Delta}(7\ell)
 \iff \ell\mid\mathfrak G^<_\Delta,
\]
\[
 \ell\mid N_{\Delta}(-\ell),N_{6\Delta}(-6\ell)
 \iff \ell\mid\mathfrak G^>_\Delta.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}

 \mathfrak b_m\mathfrak a_{m+h}
 -\mathfrak a_m\mathfrak b_{m+h}
 =(m!)^6N_h(m).

\[
 \min\{v_\ell(\mathfrak a_m),v_\ell(\mathfrak a_{m+h})\}
 \le v_\ell(N_h(m)).

$\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,m)^3$.  Hence for $\ell>7\Delta$ all

\[
 \log|u_m|\ll m.
\]

\end{proof}
