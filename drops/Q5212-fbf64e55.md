## 5.4 Same-characteristic continuant root count

For an actual moving pair with gap \(g=y_2-y_1\), the exact same-characteristic return criterion gives

\[
s\mid N_{|g|}(\min\{y_1,y_2\})

with the appropriate orientation/shift convention, and the root-count theorem gives \(O(|g|)\) possible roots for a **fixed characteristic and fixed gap**.

That does not bind the graph.  The graph asks for only one forced root in each varying characteristic.  One root is compatible with an \(O(|g|)\) capacity for every admissible \(|g|\ge2\).  Moreover \(s\), \(g\), and the root position all vary together.  There is no single finite-field polynomial whose roots are being accumulated across all graph rows.

## 5.5 Factorial gap energy

The gap-energy inequalities are again rowwise.  One graph-selected pair per moving characteristic costs \(O(1)\) gap incidence in that row.  No current gap-energy upper bound forces a positive fraction of rows to have zero such incidences.

Far-gap masks only make this clearer: an upper bound on short-gap collisions cannot be inverted into a saving on a collection that may be supported on far gaps.

## 5.6 Reflection

The exact reflection

\[

is structural inside one characteristic.  It can create a mate of a zero, but it does not make a graph-selected position less likely to be a zero, and it creates no cross-characteristic divisor.  The current nonmidpoint/nonalias masks merely delete some reflected coincidences.

## 5.7 Scalar heights

No power-saving divisor budget appears.

So every currently legal actual-zero mechanism is marginal/vertical.  The missing theorem is horizontal/transversal.

# 10. What would *not* count as a proof of \((\mathrm{AZT})_\delta\)

The following steps are invalid without new input.

1. **Divide by \(s\)** and call \(Z(s)/s\) a probability.  There is no random choice of \(y_1\).
2. **Square the density** and call \(Z(s)(Z(s)-1)/s^2\) the probability of the pair.  The pair is deterministic and its two entries are correlated through the same physical lift.
3. **Transpose the factorial second moment.**  It is a sum of row degrees; the graph asks for selected entries across rows.
4. **Use the continuant degree as a cross-prime count.**  Degree bounds roots only after the characteristic and gap are fixed.
5. **Form a resultant across different \(s\).**  The roots lie in different defining finite fields.
6. **Count primes in the graph and claim a \(1/\log N\) saving.**  This is only \(N^{o(1)}\), and the smooth prime weight carries a compensating logarithm at exponent scale.
7. **Add another \(|u_q|\).**  The outer prime \(q\) is already fixed and paid exactly once in (6.1).
8. **Drop source multiplicity or masks to obtain injectivity.**  The current object is an occurrence multiset; repeated source occurrences remain repeated unless the source itself identifies them.
9. **Use signed cancellation.**  The RR32/Q5189 bound is a positive-energy majorant.  The requested occupancy theorem must work at the nonnegative level.

Any proof containing one of these steps has changed the problem.
