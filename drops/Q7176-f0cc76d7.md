# P32 endpoint-free pair-cap extremizer audit

## Verdict

The endpoint-free modification is valid. It repairs the artificial inclusion of the endpoints 0 and p-1 without changing any local gap statistics. The resulting family satisfies all deterministic axioms currently extracted from Apéry zero sets:

* reflection symmetry `z -> p-1-z`;
* absence of endpoints `0,p-1`;
* no adjacent zeros;
* pair cap `A_Z(h) <= 3(h-1)`;
* the same quadratic number of short four-zero windows.

It therefore shows that these deterministic facts alone still cannot imply the short-window principle.

The construction does not violate any additional deterministic zero-set axiom proved in the P32 setup. The only missing features are genuinely recurrence-specific (for example distinguished orbit constraints coming from the Apéry transfer matrix).

## Construction

Start with the previous left packet.

Let

\[
 m=100k,\qquad R=m/100=k,
\]

and define the periodic gaps

\[
 g_i=3m+(i\bmod m),\qquad 0\le i<mR-1.
\]

Let

\[
P_0=0,\qquad P_j=\sum_{i<j}g_i,
\]

and let

\[
L=P_{mR-1}.
\]

The endpoint-free translation uses

\[
D=1,
\]

and replaces the left packet by

\[
Q_j=D+P_j.
\]

Choose a prime satisfying

\[
4(D+L)+1<p<2(4(D+L)+1).
\]

Finally define

\[
Z=\{Q_j\}\cup\{p-1-Q_j\}.
\]

The two packets are exchanged by reflection.

## 1. Endpoints

Because every left point has the form

\[
Q_j=1+P_j\ge 1,
\]

we have

\[
0\notin Z.
\]

For the reflected side,

\[
p-1-Q_j\le p-2,
\]

so

\[
p-1\notin Z.
\]

Thus both endpoints are absent.

## 2. Reflection

For every left point,

\[
p-1-(D+P_j)=p-1-Q_j
\]

is included by construction. Therefore

\[
z\in Z\iff p-1-z\in Z.
\]

The reflection center is preserved exactly.

## 3. No adjacency

Within one packet,

\[
Q_{j+1}-Q_j=g_j\ge 3m>1.
\]

The reflected packet has the same gaps.

For cross-packet adjacency, the smallest cross distance is at least

\[
(p-1-(D+L))-(D+L)
=p-1-2(D+L).
\]

The prime choice gives

\[
p>4(D+L)+1,
\]

hence

\[
p-1-2(D+L)>2(D+L)>1.
\]

Therefore no adjacent elements occur.

## 4. Short windows

Translation does not change differences:

\[
(Q_{j+3}-Q_j)=(P_{j+3}-P_j).
\]

The previous periodic-gap calculation remains unchanged. Three consecutive gaps satisfy

\[
g_i+g_{i+1}+g_{i+2}
\le (4m-1)+(4m-2)+(4m-3)
<12m=H.
\]

Hence every internal four-point window in either packet has span at most H.

The number of such windows is

\[
2(mR-2)=\frac{m^2}{50}+O(m)
=\frac{H^2}{7200}+O(H),
\]

so the same quadratic lower bound survives.

## 5. Pair-cap verification

Only differences must be checked.

### Within-packet differences

Translation cancels:

\[
Q_b-Q_a=P_b-P_a.
\]

Therefore the within-packet multiplicities are identical to the previous construction. The scale split proof remains:

* for `h<m^2`, a block has fewer than one period and the sawtooth fiber multiplicity is at most 2;
* for `h>=m^2`, the trivial endpoint count is bounded by the available linear budget.

The previous estimate gives

\[
A_{\rm within}(h)\le 2(h-1).
\]

### Cross-packet differences

A cross difference has the form

\[
(p-1-Q_a)-Q_b
=p-1-2D-P_a-P_b.
\]

Equivalently, fixing the difference fixes

\[
P_a+P_b.
\]

Because the left packet is strictly increasing, for each fixed `a` there is at most one possible `b`. Hence the number of representations is at most the packet length.

The previous reflected construction used the same argument, and the translation only subtracts the constant `2D`. Therefore the cross multiplicities are unchanged.

For the possible small cross differences, the prime separation gives

\[
|p-1-2D-P_a-P_b|>2(D+L),
\]

so all cross differences are macroscopic. The linear allowance

\[
3(h-1)
\]

is much larger than the possible packet multiplicity.

Thus

\[
A_Z(h)\le3(h-1)
\]

for every positive h.

## 6. Exceptional central index

The deterministic Apéry reflection law has a possible special point when

\[
z=(p-1)/2.
\]

This construction can avoid it completely. A central point would require

\[
2(D+P_j)=p-1.
\]

The prime is chosen after the packet is fixed. Since there are only finitely many forbidden values of p for each k, choose a prime in the interval avoiding them. The interval length is comparable to D+L, so for large k there are many primes available.

Therefore the construction may be taken without a fixed point under reflection.
