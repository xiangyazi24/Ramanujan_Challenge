## 4. Why the selected upper zero supplies no further power

There are four conceivable uses of `q | b_t`; all four stop at the current interface.

### 4.1 Use the affine invariant

Equation (2.3) reduces a pair of rank choices to a one-dimensional matching. This is already the replacement

\[
DE\rightsquigarrow\min(D,E)
\]

in (3.2). Reusing (2.3) cannot charge a second factor.

### 4.2 Use the upper reflection gap

Equation (2.5) forces a macro upper gap, but (2.8) shows that its ordinary gap determinant is divisible by `q` for structural reasons. It does not distinguish selected from nonselected `t`.

### 4.3 Use separate zero moments at `ell` and `q`

Separate moment bounds do not correlate the affine image

\[
(\ell,u,a)\longmapsto
\left(
q=\frac{8\ell+a-u}{7},
\quad
t=\frac{\ell-u-6a-7}{7}
\right).
\tag{4.1}
\]

The saturator in Section 5 uses only `T^(1-m)` selected digits per upper prime, at most `T^(1/2)`, far below the pointwise `T^(2/3+o(1))` allowance. It also stays below the same cubic-moment budget on the upper shell.

### 4.4 Use a resultant

The lower certificates live in `F_ell`:

\[
N_{\bar h}(u)=0\pmod\ell,
\qquad
N_h(a)=0\pmod\ell.
\]

The upper selected equation and any divided reflection factor live in `F_q`:

\[

\qquad
\widehat N_\gamma(t)\pmod q.
\]

Here `q>ell`, the starts `u,a,t` are different, and no common-root transport has been proved. There is therefore no valid cross-characteristic resultant. The affine relation among integer indices is not an identification of residue fields.

## 7. First missing actual-Apéry lemma

\[
\mathcal E^-_\ell(C)

 \end{array}
 \right\}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

For `(ell,u,a)` in this packet put

\[
\boxed{
q_{\ell,u,a}:=\frac{8\ell+a-u}{7},
\qquad
t_{\ell,u,a}:=\frac{\ell-u-6a-7}{7}.}
\tag{7.2}

These are exactly (1.8), because `bar h=a-u`. Let `Omega_C` denote the product of the literal integrality, range, primality, raw-minus-first, packing, and other already-authorized masks, **excluding only** the selected-upper-zero bit.

```text

cell C,

  sum_{ell in L_z}
  sum_{(u,a) in E^-_ell(C)}
      Omega_C(ell,u,a)
      1_{ q_{ell,u,a} | b_{ t_{ell,u,a} } }
```
