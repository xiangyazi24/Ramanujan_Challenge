# 7. Summation-by-parts no-go for all finite-support integer linear certificates

Let `J_4 subset I_4`, `J_7 subset I_7` be arbitrary finite supports.  Let

Every finite-support integer linear combination of cross determinants and within-slope row combinations has the form `(7.1)` after expansion.

Define its two DC weights

```text

w_7 := sum_(L in J_7) B_L.                           (7.2)
```

Then exactly one of the following occurs.

### Case A: nonzero DC weight

If `(w_4,w_7)!=(0,0)`, the gauge transformation `(6.1)` changes the value by


Therefore no identity



for every pair of integers `c_4,c_7`, impossible unless both weights vanish.

In particular, the displayed recurrence algebra alone gives **no polynomial or `exp(o(m))` height bound** for a nonzero-DC certificate.  The constants may be translated arbitrarily without changing any displayed recurrence.

Assume


