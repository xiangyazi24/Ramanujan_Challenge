# Q1031 audit: q=1 determinantal-divisor carrier

## Verdict

OPEN.

The recovered determinant carrier gives a valid exact support identity for the top-half primes, but it does not by itself prove `log D_n=o(n)`. The smallest missing lemma is a characteristic-zero valuation restriction on the gcd of the full family of terms

\[
T_j=L_j(n)(b_n-b_j),\qquad 0\le j<M.
\]

In particular, one needs a uniform estimate for

\[
\sum_\ell v_\ell(D_n)=\sum_\ell\min_{j<M}(v_\ell(L_j(n))+v_\ell(b_n-b_j)).
\]

No such estimate follows only from the current Lucas/support identities.

## Prime-local formula

For every prime \(\ell\), the exact valuation is

\[
v_\ell(D_n)=\min_{0\le j<M}\{v_\ell(L_j(n))+v_\ell(b_n-b_j)\}.
\]

The binomial factor satisfies

\[
L_j(n)=(-1)^{M-1-j}{n\choose j}{n-j-1\choose M-1-j},
\]

so Kummer gives

\[
v_\ell(L_j(n))=C_\ell(n,j)+C_\ell(n-j-1,M-1-j),
\]

where the right side is the sum of base-\(\ell\) carry counts. This controls the coefficient contribution but not the Apéry difference term.

## Easy large-prime lemma

The proposed lemma for \(\ell>n\) is correct.

If \(\ell>n\), then every binomial coefficient occurring in every \(L_j(n)\) is an \(\ell\)-adic unit. Hence

\[
v_\ell(D_n)=\min_{j<M}v_\ell(b_n-b_j).
\]

For \(j=0\), this requires \(\ell\mid b_n-b_0\), not \(b_1-b_0\). The suggested j=0,1 shortcut is insufficient as stated: the minimum over j is not forced to use j=1. One would need a Bezout combination of the terms or an additional recurrence argument.

Thus the claim "\ell>n cannot divide D_n because j=0,1 force \ell|4" is not proved from the gcd formula alone.

## What remains valid

The top-half support theorem

\[
p\mid D_n\iff p\mid b_{n-p},\qquad n/2<p\le n,
\]

is compatible with the valuation formula. It identifies a subset of the support but gives no cancellation information for small primes.

The finite observation that `log(D_n)/n` decreases through n=1200 is evidence only.

## Required next lemma

A sufficient new result would be one of:

1. A recurrence-derived Bezout identity

\[
\sum_{j<M}u_j(n)L_j(n)(b_n-b_j)=E_n
\]

with controlled coefficients and \(\log|E_n|=o(n)\).

2. A local bound

\[
\sum_{\ell\le n} \min_j(v_\ell(L_j(n))+v_\ell(b_n-b_j))=o(n),
\]

combined with the already known top-half support statement.

3. A direct Apéry-specific restriction on simultaneous zeros

\[
\ell\mid b_n-b_{j_1},\ldots,b_n-b_{j_t}
\]

that bounds their multiplicity across the index interval.

## Finite computation

The existing verifier should be used only to confirm the exact determinant identity:

```python
from math import gcd
D = 0
for j in range(M):
    T = L(j,n)*(b[n]-b[j])
    D = gcd(D, abs(T))
```

The same loop gives the prime valuation data by factoring `D`; it does not establish an asymptotic bound.

## Final status

OPEN.

The missing ingredient is not the top-half radical identification. It is a genuine characteristic-zero Apéry recurrence lemma controlling the common divisibility of all determinant-carrier entries.
