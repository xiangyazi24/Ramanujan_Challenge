&+\#\{(+,-)\}\\
&+\#\{(-,-):h_1<h_2\},
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.3}
\]

with no \((- ,+)\) contribution.

After the proven small-product and thin-height deletions, the exact unresolved weighted moment is

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak U_{\rm rem}(T)
:=
\sum_{\substack{H_1,H_2\text{ dyadic}\\

\min(H_1,H_2)>U_*}}

}
\tag{9.4}
\]

This is an upper-zero-occurrence **signed-height second factorial moment**, with the pair orientation and repeated \(\log q\) weights explicit. It is narrower than an unstructured restatement of FURC: the two unconditional deletions have been removed, the dyadic heights are exposed, and the exact unresolved reuse is concentrated inside each actual \((q,v)\).

\[

\tag{9.5}
\]

\tag{9.6}
