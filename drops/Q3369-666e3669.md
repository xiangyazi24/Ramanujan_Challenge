## 2. Exact p-derivation identity

Work in the localization

\[
A=\mathbf Z[S^{-1}],
\]

where `S` is generated by the core denominators. By hypothesis every target prime in the `q=6` interval is a unit-denominator prime, i.e. `p` divides no element of `S` used as a denominator.

For `a in A` define, in `A_(p)`,

\[
\delta_p(a)=\frac{a-a^p}{p}.
\tag{6}
\]

This is the p-derivation attached to the identity Frobenius lift on the arithmetic coefficients. Fermat gives `a^p = a (mod p)` in the residue field, so (6) is p-integral.

Now suppose `a = p c` in `A_(p)`. Then

\[
\delta_p(a)
 = c-p^{p-1}c^p
 \equiv c\pmod p.
\]

Therefore

\[
\delta_p(a)\equiv a/p\pmod p,
\]

which proves (1). Applying this to `a = R(Omega)=u Gamma` gives

\[
\delta_p(\mathcal R(\Omega))
\equiv \frac{u(\Omega)\Gamma(\Omega)}p
\equiv \bar u(\Omega)\beta_p(\Omega)
\pmod p,
\]

and hence (3).

So there is no mystery about what the first-Witt coordinate *is*: it is the first divided p-adic digit of the already-existing normalized residual.

### Why (3) does not satisfy the requested uniformity

The operation `delta_p` depends on `p` twice: in the division by `p` and in the exponent `p`. After exact cancellation its *value* has only p-unit/core denominators, but this does not make the construction prime-independent. Moreover the naive numerator `a-a^p` has archimedean height on the scale `p h(a)` in general. For target primes `p = Theta(m)` this gives no `o(m)` height theorem; it normally goes in the wrong direction.

Thus (3) is the correct diagnostic identity, not the desired final carrier.

## 3. The decisive obstruction: the first Witt digit does not descend to the special fibre

This is independent of Apéry specifics.

### Proposition 3.1 (first-Witt non-descent)

Let `A` be a ring in which `p` is a non-zero-divisor. There is no function

\[
f:A/pA\longrightarrow A/pA
\]

such that for every `x in pA/p^2A`,

\[
f(x\bmod p)=x/p\bmod p.
\tag{7}
\]

#### Proof

Every `x in pA/p^2A` reduces to `0` in `A/pA`, while the map

\[
pA/p^2A\xrightarrow{/p} A/pA
\]

is an isomorphism. Thus the right side of (7) runs over all of `A/pA`, but the left side is always the single value `f(0)`. Contradiction. `square`

This is exactly the Q3369 situation. Once `p | Gamma`, the ordinary reduction sees

\[
\Gamma(\Omega)\equiv0\pmod p,
\]

but `beta_p` is the class of `Gamma` in the first infinitesimal layer

\[
\boxed{
\beta_p(\Omega)
\in pA_p/p^2A_p\cong A_p/pA_p.
}
\tag{8}
\]

It is therefore **not a function of the special fibre**.

### Corollary 3.2 (core-unit rational expressions cannot manufacture it)

Suppose a proposed formula is a rational expression in the ordinary singular-source data whose denominator is a core unit at `p`. Its reduction modulo `p` is a regular function of the special-fibre data. Consequently it cannot recover `beta_p` unless some *additional Apéry-specific congruence modulo `p^2`* has already made the lift canonical.

A useful tangent form is the following. Let `z` denote all ordinary source coordinates and let `R(z)` be the normalized residual. If two lifts have

\[
z'=z+p v\pmod {p^2},
\]

then they have identical ordinary data, but

\[
\frac{R(z')}{p}
\equiv
\frac{R(z)}p+dR_{\bar z}(v)
\pmod p.
\tag{9}
\]

Thus any allowed lift direction with `dR(v) != 0` changes the first-Witt coordinate without changing the ordinary source. The free mode isolated in Q3362 is precisely why a generic adjacent-lattice/Smith argument cannot supply this coordinate.

This is a **scoped** obstruction: it rules out derivation from the ordinary quotient and its core-unit regular functions. It does not rule out a genuinely Apéry-specific Frobenius congruence modulo `p^2` that canonically fixes the lift.

Then immediately

\[
\boxed{
\beta_p(\Omega)\equiv W(\Omega)\pmod p

Therefore a successful unit-root argument has to prove a genuine compression such as (23); merely invoking “Dwork congruences” does not produce it.

## 8. Scoped obstruction strengthened by Q3362

Proposition 3.1 is already enough to kill any formula that only depends on the special fibre. Q3362 shows why the obvious lattice data cannot secretly provide the missing lift.

The invariant (5) has a free mode not seen by the adjacent hypergeometric `e_K`. In the abstract fixed-`e` family, `Gamma` can vary while the controlled adjacent defect remains fixed. Passing to first order, one may prescribe congruences

\[
\Gamma\equiv p a_p\pmod {p^2}
\]

for finitely many target primes by the Chinese remainder theorem, while all those primes still see the same ordinary condition `Gamma = 0 (mod p)`. Thus the ordinary lattice data place no generic restriction on the first-Witt digits `a_p`.

This does **not** prove that the actual Apéry residual has arbitrary first-Witt digits. It proves the correct negative statement: any solution must use an Apéry-specific `p^2` identity; it cannot be a formal consequence of the one-dimensional ordinary quotient, the adjacent Smith invariant, or core-unit regular algebra alone.

## 10. Bottom line

The first-Witt attack does not currently close, but it reduces to a sharply defined one-coefficient problem.

- The exact local formula is `beta_p = Gamma/p mod p`, equivalently the p-derivation identity (3).
- The ordinary one-dimensional singular quotient cannot determine it: first-Witt data live in `pA/p^2A`, not in `A/pA`.
- The current Dwork source already constructs the divided first ghost `(B^p-psi(B))/p` but uses only its integrality; it does not identify its residue in the `D,J4,J7` singular quotient.
- Q3362 rules out recovering this digit from the adjacent Smith/hypergeometric data alone.
- The minimal extra datum is one scalar: the normalized `D,J4,J7` residual modulo `p^2`, or equivalently its divided residue (19). In Frobenius language it is the projected first ghost / correction (26).
- A true prime-uniform solution must prove the p-independent low-height congruence (23). Without such an Apéry-specific `p^2` identity, no core-unit regular characteristic-zero expression of the ordinary data can recover `beta_p`.

So the mathematically justified status is **scoped obstruction + exact minimal target**, not a claimed unit-root solution.
