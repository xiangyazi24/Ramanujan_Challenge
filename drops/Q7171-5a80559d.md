## 1. Correct object: normalized pencil content

Let the fully deflated adjacent pencil be

\[
C_{a,b,c}=\operatorname{cont}_T\operatorname{Res}_x(F_{a,b,c},G_{a,b,c}+TJ_{a,b,c}).
\]

The DVR formula gives, after normalization,

\[
v_p(C_{a,b,c})=
\sum_i m_i\,\ell_{\mathcal O_i}
(\mathcal O_i/(G(\alpha_i),J(\alpha_i))).
\]

This is a local intersection multiplicity at the same algebraic roots of the adjacent pencil. It is not the valuation of an arbitrary gcd of two endpoint resultants.

Therefore the only admissible charging is:

\[
(p,a,b,c)\mapsto\text{a genuine common root of the normalized pencil}.
\]

Charging a prime merely because it divides two scalar resultants at unrelated roots is invalid.

\[

The direct pencil formulation improves the situation:

If

\[

\quad G=N_b(\cdot+a),

\[

The missing step is then a codegree estimate:

\[

Neither the degree bound of N_h nor the pair-root cap alone gives this. They bound one direction of the incidence relation but not the full three-gap fibre.
