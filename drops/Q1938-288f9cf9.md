# P3.2 displacement-rank attack on the Q1918 cofactor

## 1. Operator-to-matrix translation

Let \(V=\mathbf F_p^{\mathbf F_p}\) with basis \(\{\delta_a\}_{a\in\mathbf F_p}\). Let \(T_+\) and \(T_-\) be the permutation matrices
\[
(T_+f)(t)=f(t+1),\qquad (T_-f)(t)=f(t-1).
\]
For a polynomial \(C(t)\), multiplication is the diagonal operator \(M_C\). The cyclic convolution version is the circulant multiplication matrix. The relation
\[
\widehat H C=-(\theta/2)(1-t^{p-1})-32a t(t^p-t)
\]
becomes
\[
M_{\widehat H}M_C=-\frac\theta2(I-J)-32aM_t(T_+-I),
\]
where \(J\) is the rank-one projector onto constants (the precise sign depends on the convention for the additive shift operator).

Thus the commutator with translation is
\[
T_+M_C-M_CT_+
=M_{C(t+1)-C(t)}T_+.
\]

## 2. Displacement rank before restriction

Because \(C(t+1)-C(t)\) has bounded degree, the full cyclic matrix satisfies a low displacement-rank identity:
\[
T_+M_C-M_CT_+=M_D T_+,
\]
with \(D(t)=C(t+1)-C(t)\).

If \(\deg D=d\), multiplication by \(D\) has rank at most \(d+1\) after evaluation in the polynomial basis. Hence
\[
\operatorname{rank}(T_+M_C-M_CT_+)\le d+1.
\]

This gives the usual Toeplitz/circulant determinant machinery only on the full cyclic space.

## 3. Restriction to even nonzero squares

Let \(S\subset\mathbf F_p^\times\) be the square/even-index restriction used for the cofactor. The restricted matrix is
\[
M_C^S=P_SM_CP_S^*.
\]
The translation operator does not preserve \(S\):
\[
T_+(\mathbf F_p^2\setminus\{0\})\not\subseteq \mathbf F_p^2\setminus\{0\}.
\]
Therefore there is no induced permutation matrix on the restricted space. The restricted displacement becomes
\[
P_S(T_+M_C-M_CT_+)P_S^*
+
P_ST_+ (I-P_S)M_CP_S^*
-
P_SM_C(I-P_S)T_+P_S^*.
\]
The last two leakage terms have no bounded rank controlled by \(\deg C\). They measure boundary leakage of the subset under translation.

Hence the low-rank displacement is destroyed in general.

## 4. Exact obstruction

A necessary condition for retaining displacement rank \(r\) is
\[
\operatorname{rank}(P_ST_+(I-P_S))\le r.
\]
For the square restriction, every translated square orbit leaves the subset, and the image dimension grows with \(|S|\). Therefore
\[
\operatorname{rank}(P_ST_+(I-P_S))=\Theta(|S|),
\]
so no constant-rank displacement identity survives.

This rules out a direct determinant recurrence for \(\det M_C^S\) obtained by cyclic displacement.

## 5. Consequence for determinant evaluation

The desired quotient
\[
\det(M_C^S)/K_*
\]
therefore cannot be made independent of the original source set by the standard circulant displacement argument alone.

The remaining possible route is to keep the full cyclic matrix, compute the determinant/cofactor there using the low displacement rank, and only then apply the augmentation/even-square projection. Equivalently, one needs an additional invariant showing that the Schur complement of the discarded coordinates is a controlled scalar.

## Conclusion

The full cyclic multiplication matrix has low displacement rank because additive shifts preserve the ambient cyclic space. The restriction to even nonzero squares introduces an exact rank obstruction: translation leakage has rank proportional to the size of the restricted set. Therefore the hoped-for restricted determinant recurrence does not exist in the naive displacement-rank framework. Any proof of the Q1918 cofactor target must either avoid the restriction until the final step or identify an additional symmetry reducing the leakage rank.
