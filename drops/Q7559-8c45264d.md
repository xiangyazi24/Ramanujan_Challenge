## 6. Why this is plausibly reachable

The claim is not that existing general theorems already imply FSAC.  They do not.  The plausibility comes from the unusually complete local arithmetic already available.

### 6.1 The recurrence side is exact

For \(x+h<p\), the Apéry three-term recurrence gives a two-dimensional transfer system with nonvanishing denominators.  At a zero \(b_x=0\), no-consecutive-zero rigidity fixes the projective state line.  The numerator of the return entry is the gap polynomial \(N_h(x)\).  Thus every equation in (5.1) is exact, not heuristic.

### 6.2 Formal degeneracies are already classified

The repository has already separated:

- the reflection-center factor;
- the adjacent self-gcd;
- the arithmetic-progression slice \(a=b=c\);
- structural carrier primes;
- skipped-return and endpoint degeneracies.

FSAC does not ask for another geometric classification.  It asks that, after these classifications, the actual prefix cut the remaining formal carrier by one aggregate dimension.

### 6.3 The actual prefix has a canonical arithmetic model

The Hasse–Franel pullback identifies the Apéry coefficients as coordinates of a square in a cover-adapted basis, while the normalized Hasse factorization selects a unique square root.  The Dwork/Franel files provide exact Frobenius and tail congruences rather than numerical fits.  Related primary work supports the same arithmetic framework:

- Mellit–Vlasenko, *Dwork's congruences for the constant terms of powers of a Laurent polynomial*, arXiv:1306.5811;

## 7. Cold-audit failure modes

A purported proof of FSAC must be rejected if it does any of the following.

### 7.1 Counts formal roots instead of actual zeros

Showing that the three polynomials in (5.1) have few common roots is insufficient unless the argument enforces \(b_x=0\).  This is the principal phantom-fibre defect already recorded by the project.

### 7.2 Multiplies scalar resultants over all gap triples

That reproduces the old content mass and loses at least the required factor \(S\).  The saving must occur before the norm/determinant is taken.

### 7.3 Uses generic squareness

The reciprocal-square factorization alone has triangular coefficient freedom.  The proof must use \(B_p(0)=1\), the correct \(C_+\)/\(C_-\) branch, and its universal second-order recurrence.

### 7.4 Discards structural or progression primes without paying them

FSAC is unconditional.  Saturated factors may be removed only after their complete prime-power contribution is bounded inside \(S^{2+o(1)}\).  An empirical list through a fixed \(S\) is not such a bound.

### 7.5 Uses reflection as the main saving

Reflection may remove at most the unique centered adjacent pair, hence at most three sliding quadruples per prime.  That harmless \(O(X)\) term is already absorbed at \(H=X^{2/3}\); it cannot provide the \(S^3	o S^2\) compression.

### 7.6 Uses finite APUC=0 data

An all-unmarked finite box forces every centered fibre negative.  It supplies no estimate for the first populated marked regime and no information toward (4.1).

### 7.7 Proves only the existing square-root zero count

An \(X^{3/2+o(1)}\) average, even if made unconditional, gives

\[
 \sqrt{T^{3/2}T^{13/5}}=T^{41/20}=T^{2.05},
\]

before logarithms.  It cannot yield \(o(B)\).  The theorem must cross exponent \(7/5\); cosmetic improvements to the \(3/2\) route do not close APUC.
