# P3.2 exact half-integer structure audit

## Centered recurrence and known hypergeometric structure

The recurrence

\[
(2t+1)^3u_{t+1}-4t(68t^2+3)u_t+(2t-1)^3u_{t-1}=0
\]

is the Apéry recurrence after the half-integer substitution \(n=t-\frac12\). Equivalently, after clearing the half-step shift, it is the symmetric form of the Picard--Fuchs operator

\[
\theta^3- x(2\theta+1)(17\theta^2+17\theta+5).
\]

The standard analytic solutions are generated by the Apéry generating function and its logarithmic companion. The regular solution has coefficients given by the Apéry numbers

\[
B(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}b_nx^n,
\]

where

\[
b_n=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk^2\binom{n+k}{k}^2.
\]

The half-integer substitution changes the indexing of the recurrence but does not produce a new terminating hypergeometric family.

## Hypergeometric / Wilson possibilities

The recurrence is not one of the Wilson or Racah polynomial recurrences. Those families have a three-term recurrence with spectral parameter appearing linearly in the middle coefficient and possess a finite orthogonality measure. Here the coefficient polynomial

\[
4t(68t^2+3)
\]

comes from the Apéry Picard--Fuchs operator, whose solutions are periods of the associated K3 family, not Wilson functions.

A quadratic transformation does exist at the differential-equation level through the modular parametrization of the Apéry family, but it does not convert the finite field endpoint condition or reciprocal trace into a classical orthogonal-polynomial identity.

## Endpoint condition

The condition

\[
u_{m-1}=5u_m\quad (p=2m+1)
\]

is a finite-field reflection constraint. It is not a standard contiguous relation of a hypergeometric polynomial because the solution is not represented by a terminating \({}_rF_s\) with parameter shift depending on \(m\).

## Reciprocal trace

The quantity

\[
S=\frac13-2\sum_{t=2}^{m}\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}
\]

is a sum over reciprocals of a solution sequence. Christoffel--Darboux identities apply to bilinear kernels of orthogonal polynomial systems:

\[
\sum_k \frac{P_k(x)P_k(y)}{h_k}
\]

or equivalent Wronskian expressions. They do not evaluate sums of \(1/u_t\) for a single non-orthogonal period solution.

The only available discrete Wronskian identity is Abel's identity for the second solution \(z\):

\[
u_tz_{t+1}-z_tu_{t+1}=\frac1{(2t+1)^3},
\]

which gives adjacent-ratio information, not the reciprocal trace.

## Conclusion

No primary-reference hypergeometric, Wilson-function, quadratic-transform, contiguous, or Christoffel--Darboux formula evaluates this reciprocal sum. The obstruction is structural: the Apéry solution is a period solution of a fourth-order Picard--Fuchs geometry, while the proposed identities require a classical finite orthogonal-polynomial system. The half-integer substitution only symmetrizes the recurrence; it does not place it into the Wilson/Racah class.
