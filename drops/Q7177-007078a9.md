# P32 overlap R3: exact sliding-window energy, reflection, and the separated residual

## Verdict

The sliding-window claim is correct, with four conventions made explicit.

1. The repository uses the **linear, nonwrapping** index interval `0,...,p-1`.
2. “Overlap energy” here is the **ordered 0–1 count of pairs of chains whose closed support intervals meet**, as required by the expansion of `V_p`. It is not an intersection-length-weighted energy.
3. Reflection pairs may themselves overlap, so “overlap” and “reflection” are not automatically disjoint classes. Reflection must be removed first, or the overlap/reflection intersection must be recorded explicitly.
4. A residual pair is separated when its closed supports are disjoint. This means occurrence-window index separation at least four; it does **not** imply coordinate separation greater than `H`.

With those conventions, the exact raw ordered overlap count is at most `6 M_p`. After quotienting by the free reflection involution, the quotient ordered overlap count is at most `6 \bar M_p`. Consequently all diagonal, forced-reflection, and overlap terms already have the required dyadic size `O(X^2/log X)`. The only remaining arithmetic term is the ordered energy of two distinct, nonreflection chain orbits in one projective state whose full reflection-invariant supports are disjoint.

The audit below uses the definitions implemented by the exact projective scanner and the reflection-aware scan: projective state occurrence lists, consecutive four-occurrence windows, positive gaps, non-all-equal gaps, all three off-center tests, short span, and carrier cleanliness.

## 1. Exact objects and the nonwrapping convention

Fix an odd prime `p` and write

\[
\pi_p(n)=[B_n:C_n]\in \mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p),
\qquad 0\le n\le p-1.
\]

For a projective state `q`, let its complete linearly ordered occurrence list be

\[
R_p(q)=\{r_0<r_1<\cdots<r_{\nu_q-1}\}.
\]

For every index

\[
0\le i\le \nu_q-4,
\]

define the consecutive four-occurrence window

\[
\gamma_{q,i}=(r_i,r_{i+1},r_{i+2},r_{i+3})
\]

and its closed support interval

\[
J_{q,i}=[r_i,r_{i+3}].
\]

Put

\[
a_i=r_{i+1}-r_i,\quad
b_i=r_{i+2}-r_{i+1},\quad
c_i=r_{i+3}-r_{i+2},\quad
s_i=r_{i+3}-r_i.
\]

Let `I_p(q)` be the subset of starts `i` for which the repository's filters hold:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&a_i,b_i,c_i\ge 2,\\
&\neg(a_i=b_i=c_i),\\
&r_i+r_{i+1}\ne p-1,\quad
r_{i+1}+r_{i+2}\ne p-1,\quad
r_{i+2}+r_{i+3}\ne p-1,\\
&s_i^2\le p,\\
&p\nmid U_{s_i}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The last condition is the carrier-clean test; in the executable scanner it is the prefix condition excluding zeros of the relevant `B_j` and `V_{j+1}` for `j\le s_i`. For prime `p`, the test `s_i^2\le p` is the same short-span convention as `s_i\le\lfloor\sqrt p\rfloor`.

Define

\[
\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}(q)=\{\gamma_{q,i}:i\in I_p(q)\},
\qquad
C_p^{\rm raw}(q)=|I_p(q)|,
\]

and

\[
M_p=\sum_{q\in\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p)}C_p^{\rm raw}(q).
\]

The raw projective-state variance is

\[
V_p^{\rm raw}
=
\sum_q\left(C_p^{\rm raw}(q)-\frac{M_p}{p+1}\right)^2
=
\sum_q C_p^{\rm raw}(q)^2-\frac{M_p^2}{p+1}.
\]

The convention is nonwrapping: a window may not continue from an occurrence near `p-1` through an occurrence near `0`. This is exactly what the scanner does when it processes `0,1,...,p-1`, retaining the three previous occurrences and performing no final cyclic pass.

## 2. Publication-quality sliding-window lemma

### Lemma (exact nonwrapping overlap lemma)

Let

\[
r_0<r_1<\cdots<r_{\nu-1}
\]

be a strictly increasing finite sequence, and let

\[
I\subseteq\{0,1,\ldots,\nu-4\}.
\]

For `i\in I`, put

\[
\gamma_i=(r_i,r_{i+1},r_{i+2},r_{i+3}),
\qquad
J_i=[r_i,r_{i+3}].
\]

Then:

1. for distinct `i,j\in I`,
   \[
   J_i\cap J_j\ne\varnothing
   \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
   |i-j|\le 3;
   \]
2. the ordered overlap count is exactly
   \[
   E_{\rm ov}(I)
   :=
   \#\{(i,j)\in I^2:i\ne j,\ J_i\cap J_j\ne\varnothing\}
   =
   2\sum_{h=1}^{3}\#\{i:i\in I,\ i+h\in I\};
   \]
3. consequently,
   \[
   E_{\rm ov}(I)\le 6|I|.
   \]

#### Proof

Assume `i<j`. Since the sequence is strictly increasing,

\[
J_i\cap J_j\ne\varnothing
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
r_j\le r_{i+3}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
j\le i+3.
\]

This proves the first assertion. Every unordered overlapping pair has a unique difference `h=j-i\in\{1,2,3\}` and contributes both ordered orientations, proving the exact formula. For each fixed `h`, the set

\[
\{i:i\in I,\ i+h\in I\}
\]

has cardinality at most `|I|`, which gives the stated bound. `\square`

For the full unfiltered start set, if

\[
n=(\nu-3)_+
\]

is the number of four-windows, the exact boundary-corrected count is

\[
E_{\rm ov}^{\rm full}
=2\big((n-1)_+ +(n-2)_+ +(n-3)_+\big).
\]

Thus states with fewer than four occurrences contribute no chains, and starts near either end simply have fewer than six neighbors.

### Corollary for exact primitive chains

A primitive four-return chain in state `q` must use four consecutive occurrences of `q`: if it skipped some `r_j`, that skipped occurrence would be an intermediate return and primitivity would fail. Conversely, a consecutive four-window has no intermediate return to `q` inside its support. Therefore every exact primitive chain is one of the `\gamma_{q,i}` above.

All remaining filters only delete indices from the full start set:

- `a,b,c\ge2` deletes windows with adjacent returns;
- non-all-equal deletes the arithmetic-progression slice;
- short span deletes windows with `s>H`;
- carrier cleanliness depends on `p` and `s` and deletes windows;
- the three off-center tests delete windows containing a centered adjacent pair.

None of these operations can create a nonconsecutive primitive window. Hence, with

\[
E_{\rm ov,p}^{\rm raw}
:=
\sum_q
\#\{(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}(q)^2:
\alpha\ne\beta,\ J_\alpha\cap J_\beta\ne\varnothing\},
\]

we obtain the exact deterministic estimate

\[
\boxed{E_{\rm ov,p}^{\rm raw}\le 6M_p.}
\]

This is an ordered **pair-count** estimate. If one instead weights a pair by the cardinality or length of `J_\alpha\cap J_\beta`, the factor `6M_p` is no longer the correct weighted bound. Also, the case `|i-j|=3` shares only the endpoint `r_{i+3}`; it counts for closed-support incidence, while it has zero Lebesgue intersection length.

## 3. Reflection audit

The strong reflection law for both recurrence solutions gives

\[
\pi_p(p-1-n)=\pi_p(n).
\]

Thus every occurrence list is palindromic:

\[
r_k+r_{\nu_q-1-k}=p-1.
\]

Reflection acts on a window by

\[
\rho(r_i,r_{i+1},r_{i+2},r_{i+3})
=
(p-1-r_{i+3},p-1-r_{i+2},p-1-r_{i+1},p-1-r_i).
\]

In occurrence-list indices this is the exact formula

\[
\boxed{\rho(\gamma_{q,i})=\gamma_{q,\nu_q-4-i}.}
\]

Every filter is reflection invariant:

- `(a,b,c)` becomes `(c,b,a)`;
- span and carrier cleanliness are unchanged;
- positive gaps and the non-all-equal condition are unchanged;
- the three centered-pair tests are permuted.

The action is free on the eligible chain set. Indeed, if `\rho(\gamma)=\gamma`, then the middle adjacent pair satisfies

\[
r_{i+1}+r_{i+2}=p-1,
\]

contradicting the off-center filter. Hence every eligible raw chain has one distinct reflected partner in the same projective state.

Let

\[
\overline{\mathcal C}_p(q)
=
\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}(q)/\langle\rho\rangle,
\qquad
\bar C_p(q)=|\overline{\mathcal C}_p(q)|,
\qquad
\bar M_p=\sum_q\bar C_p(q).
\]

Then, state by state,

\[
C_p^{\rm raw}(q)=2\bar C_p(q),
\qquad
M_p=2\bar M_p.
\]

Consequently the quotient variance

\[
\bar V_p
=
\sum_q\left(\bar C_p(q)-\frac{\bar M_p}{p+1}\right)^2
\]

satisfies the exact scaling relation

\[
\boxed{V_p^{\rm raw}=4\bar V_p.}
\]

### Reflection pairs are not automatically disjoint from overlap pairs

A reflected partner has start index

\[
i^\rho=\nu_q-4-i.
\]

By the overlap lemma, `J_i` and `J_{i^\rho}` overlap exactly when

\[
|\nu_q-4-2i|\le3.
\]

The off-center condition rules out a fixed chain, but it does not justify declaring every reflected pair geometrically separated. Therefore a decomposition that simply adds “all overlap pairs” and “all reflection pairs” can double count. The variance decomposition below makes the classes disjoint by giving reflection priority.

## 4. Exact raw variance decomposition

Define the ordered number of overlapping reflection pairs by

\[
R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p)
=
\#\{\alpha\in\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}:J_\alpha\cap J_{\rho\alpha}\ne\varnothing\}.
\]

Since reflection is free, the total ordered reflection-pair count is exactly

\[
\#\{(\alpha,\rho\alpha):\alpha\in\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}\}=M_p.
\]

Define the nonreflection overlap and separated energies by

\[
E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}
=
\sum_q
\#\left\{
(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}(q)^2:
\begin{array}{l}
\beta\notin\{\alpha,\rho\alpha\},\\
J_\alpha\cap J_\beta\ne\varnothing
\end{array}
\right\},
\]

and

\[
E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}
=
\sum_q
\#\left\{
(\alpha,\beta)\in\mathcal C_p^{\rm raw}(q)^2:
\begin{array}{l}
\beta\notin\{\alpha,\rho\alpha\},\\
J_\alpha\cap J_\beta=\varnothing
\end{array}
\right\}.
\]

These are ordered counts. The diagonal, reflection, nonreflection-overlap, and nonreflection-separated classes are now disjoint and exhaustive. Therefore

\[
\sum_q C_p^{\rm raw}(q)^2
=
M_p+M_p+E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr},
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{
V_p^{\rm raw}
=
2M_p+E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}
-
\frac{M_p^2}{p+1}.
}
\]

Because

\[
E_{\rm ov,p}^{\rm raw}
=
R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p)+E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}
\le6M_p,
\]

we have the sharpened local estimate

\[
E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}
\le6M_p-R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p)
\]

and therefore

\[
V_p^{\rm raw}
\le
8M_p-R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p)
+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}
-
\frac{M_p^2}{p+1}
\le
8M_p+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}.
\]

Thus the raw deterministic contributions are exactly:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{class}&\text{ordered contribution}\\ \hline
\text{diagonal}&M_p\\
\text{forced reflection}&M_p\\
\text{nonreflection overlap}&\le6M_p-R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p).
\end{array}
\]

## 5. Exact dyadic raw contribution

Let

\[
\mathcal P_X=\{p:X<p\le2X\},
\qquad
S_M(X)=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}M_p.
\]

Also put

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm sep}^{\rm nr}(X)
=
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr},
\quad
\mathcal R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(X)
=
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p).
\]

Summing the exact identity gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}V_p^{\rm raw}
=
2S_M(X)
+
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}
+
\mathcal E_{\rm sep}^{\rm nr}(X)
-
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}\frac{M_p^2}{p+1}.
}
\]

The overlap lemma yields

\[
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}V_p^{\rm raw}
\le
8S_M(X)
-
\mathcal R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(X)
+
\mathcal E_{\rm sep}^{\rm nr}(X)
-
\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}\frac{M_p^2}{p+1},
\]

and in particular

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{X<p\le2X}V_p^{\rm raw}
\le
8\sum_{X<p\le2X}M_p
+
\mathcal E_{\rm sep}^{\rm nr}(X).
}
\]

A start index supports at most one consecutive four-return window, so

\[
M_p\le p
\]

(and in fact `M_p\le p-1` here because `M_p` is even and `p` is odd). Hence

\[
8S_M(X)
\le
8\sum_{X<p\le2X}p
\le
16X\bigl(\pi(2X)-\pi(X)\bigr)
\ll
\frac{X^2}{\log X}.
\]

Using the prime number theorem more precisely,

\[
\sum_{X<p\le2X}p
=
\left(\frac32+o(1)\right)\frac{X^2}{\log X},
\]

so the worst-case raw local term is

\[
8\sum_{X<p\le2X}p
=
(12+o(1))\frac{X^2}{\log X}.
\]

Thus diagonal, forced reflection, and all actual overlap pairs already lie exactly at the admissible weak-DPLS dyadic scale.

## 6. Reflection quotient and its overlap lemma

For a reflection orbit

\[
\mathcal O=\{\gamma,\rho\gamma\},
\]

define its reflection-invariant support by

\[
K_{\mathcal O}=J_\gamma\cup J_{\rho\gamma}.
\]

This does not depend on the representative. Define the ordered quotient overlap and separated energies by

\[
\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}
=
\sum_q
\#\{(\mathcal O,\mathcal O')\in\overline{\mathcal C}_p(q)^2:
\mathcal O\ne\mathcal O',\ K_{\mathcal O}\cap K_{\mathcal O'}\ne\varnothing\},
\]

and

\[
\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}
=
\sum_q
\#\{(\mathcal O,\mathcal O')\in\overline{\mathcal C}_p(q)^2:
\mathcal O\ne\mathcal O',\ K_{\mathcal O}\cap K_{\mathcal O'}=\varnothing\}.
\]

### Quotient overlap bound

For every orbit `\mathcal O`, choose one representative `\gamma`. If another orbit `\mathcal O'` has

\[
K_{\mathcal O}\cap K_{\mathcal O'}\ne\varnothing,
\]

then some representative of `\mathcal O'` overlaps `J_\gamma`: if the initially witnessed overlap uses `J_{\rho\gamma}`, reflect both intervals. The raw interval `J_\gamma` overlaps at most six distinct raw windows. Distinct quotient orbits supply distinct raw representatives. Hence each quotient orbit has at most six ordered quotient neighbors, and

\[
\boxed{\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}\le6\bar M_p.}
\]

Now distinct ordered quotient pairs are partitioned exactly into overlap and separated pairs, so

\[
\sum_q\bar C_p(q)^2
=
\bar M_p+ar E_{{\rm ov},p}+\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\bar V_p
=
\bar M_p+ar E_{{\rm ov},p}+\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}
-
\frac{\bar M_p^2}{p+1},
}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\bar V_p\le7\bar M_p+\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}.}
\]

The forced mirror pair has disappeared: the quotient diagonal is only `\bar M_p`.

## 7. Exact dyadic quotient contribution

Put

\[
\bar S_M(X)=\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar M_p=\frac12S_M(X),
\]

and

\[
\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X)
=
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}.
\]

Then the exact dyadic identity is

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar V_p
=
\bar S_M(X)
+
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}
+
\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X)
-
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\frac{\bar M_p^2}{p+1}.
}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar V_p
\le
7\bar S_M(X)+\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X).
}
\]

Since `\bar M_p=M_p/2\le p/2`, the quotient local contribution satisfies

\[
7\bar S_M(X)
\le
\frac72\sum_{X<p\le2X}p
=
\left(\frac{21}{4}+o(1)\right)\frac{X^2}{\log X}.
\]

Thus the exact quotient bookkeeping is:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{class}&\text{ordered contribution}\\ \hline
\text{quotient diagonal}&\bar M_p\\
\text{distinct overlapping quotient orbits}&\le6\bar M_p\\
\text{separated quotient orbits}&\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}.
\end{array}
\]

Because `V_p^{\rm raw}=4\bar V_p`, a quotient estimate also gives

\[
\sum_{X<p\le2X}V_p^{\rm raw}
\le
14S_M(X)+4\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X).
\]

This last local constant is weaker than the direct raw `8S_M(X)` bound, but it has the advantage that its residual is reflection invariant and contains no forced mirror duplicates.

One should not assert termwise identities such as

\[
E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}=4\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}
\quad\text{or}\quad
E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}=4\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}.
\]

For two quotient orbits, some of the four ordered raw lifts can overlap while other lifts are disjoint. What is exact is the total relation

\[
E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}
=
4\bigl(\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}+\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}\bigr),
\]

and, independently, `V_p^{\rm raw}=4\bar V_p`.

## 8. The residual separated energy, stated precisely

The clean residual after reflection quotient is

\[
\boxed{
\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X)
=
\sum_{X<p\le2X}
\sum_{q\in\mathbf P^1(\mathbf F_p)}
\sum_{\substack{
\mathcal O,\mathcal O'\in\overline{\mathcal C}_p(q)\\
\mathcal O\ne\mathcal O'\\
K_{\mathcal O}\cap K_{\mathcal O'}=\varnothing
}}
1.
}
\]

It counts **ordered** pairs of two distinct reflection orbits of eligible exact primitive chains in the same projective state, with no overlap between any representative support and any reflected representative support.

At the raw representative level, write

\[
\alpha=(x,x+a,x+a+b,x+s),\qquad s=a+b+c,
\]

and

\[
\beta=(y,y+a',y+a'+b',y+t),\qquad t=a'+b'+c'.
\]

For a particular ordering with `x<y`, disjoint closed intervals mean exactly

\[
x+s<y.
\]

The gap between the two supports is

\[
L=y-(x+s)\ge1.
\]

No stronger deterministic lower bound follows from the sliding-window lemma. In particular, the residual condition is not `L>H`, not `|x-y|>H`, and not `|x-y|\gg H`. What is long or difficult arithmetically is that `L` is unrestricted above and may range through the full ambient interval; geometrically it may also be as small as one.

Equivalently, in occurrence-list indices, a raw separated pair has starts `i<j` with

\[
j-i\ge4.
\]

That is the exact complement of the overlap regime.

For weak DPLS, the remaining desired statement can therefore be isolated as

\[
\boxed{
\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X)
\ll
\frac{X^{2+o(1)}}{\log X}.
}
\]

Indeed, the quotient local term is already `O(X^2/log X)`, and then

\[
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\bar V_p
\ll
\frac{X^{2+o(1)}}{\log X}.
\]

Multiplying by four gives the same weak-DPLS scale for the raw variance.

## 9. Wrap convention audit

All preceding statements use the repository's nonwrapping convention. There are no windows of the form

\[
(r_{\nu-2},r_{\nu-1},r_0+p,r_1+p)
\]

or its two neighboring cyclic starts.

A cyclic variant is possible, but it is a different definition. One must extend

\[
r_{i+\nu}=r_i+p
\]

and define starts modulo `\nu`; the three starts crossing the chosen cut must then be included in `C_p(q)`, `M_p`, and `V_p`. With short lifted arcs and cyclic start distance, the same local argument still gives at most six ordered overlap neighbors per chain. What is invalid is to use cyclic wrap windows while retaining the nonwrapping `M_p` or the linear interval decomposition above.

Reflection causes no boundary defect in the actual convention: the map `n\mapsto p-1-n` sends every ordinary interval inside `[0,p-1]` to another ordinary interval inside `[0,p-1]` and sends the allowed start range to itself via `i\mapsto\nu_q-4-i`.

## Final publication-ready conclusion

For the exact carrier-clean primitive off-center short chains used in P32, chains in a fixed projective state are a filtered subset of consecutive four-windows in that state's ordered nonwrapping occurrence list. Therefore the ordered number of distinct raw chains with intersecting closed supports is at most `6M_p`. Reflection acts freely, contributes exactly one ordered off-diagonal partner per raw chain, and must be separated from overlap without double counting. The exact raw identity is

\[
V_p^{\rm raw}
=
2M_p+E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}+E_{{\rm sep},p}^{\rm nr}
-
\frac{M_p^2}{p+1},
\qquad
E_{{\rm ov},p}^{\rm nr}\le6M_p-R_{\rho,{\rm ov}}(p).
\]

After reflection quotient,

\[
\bar V_p
=
\bar M_p+ar E_{{\rm ov},p}+\bar E_{{\rm sep},p}
-
\frac{\bar M_p^2}{p+1},
\qquad
\bar E_{{\rm ov},p}\le6\bar M_p,
\]

with

\[
V_p^{\rm raw}=4\bar V_p.
\]

On a dyadic block, all local terms are `O(X^2/log X)`. The sole remaining term is the ordered separated nonreflection-orbit energy `\bar{\mathcal E}_{\rm sep}(X)` displayed above. Its separation condition is disjoint reflection-invariant supports, equivalently start-window index distance at least four—not a claimed coordinate gap larger than `H`.
