# Q5914 — beta endpoint cyclotomic Rogers identity

## Status of the exact route

The requested route requires the asserted identification

\[
q+q^{-1}=1+2\cos(2\pi/17).
\]

Let \(\zeta=e^{2\pi i/17}\) and \(s=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}=2\cos(2\pi/17)\). Then

\[
w=q+q^{-1}=1+s.
\]

The real cyclotomic generator satisfies

\[
s^8+s^7-7s^6-5s^5+15s^4+6s^3-10s^2-s+1=0.
\]

Substitution \(s=w-1\) gives

\[
w^8-7w^7+14w^6+w^5-25w^4+9w^3+12w^2-3w-1=0,
\]

so the field generated by \(w\) is exactly the maximal real subfield
\(\mathbb Q(\zeta_{17}+\zeta_{17}^{-1})\).

## Exact generator conversion

\[
t=2\cos(2\pi/17)=\zeta+\zeta^{-1}.

\[
\boxed{w=t+1}.

Conversely,

\[
\boxed{t=w-1}.
