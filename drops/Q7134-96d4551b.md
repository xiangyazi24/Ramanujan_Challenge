# Q7134 audit: fully deflated adjacent certificate

## Verdict

The subsection is **not publication-clean**.  The proposed fully-deflated route is a useful reduction, but `FDAC^tr` cannot currently be treated as the only unproved input: several statements preceding it are missing hypotheses or overstate what has been proved.

The existing adjacent-certificate infrastructure still has the right shape: central factors must be removed before an aligned-content estimate is meaningful.  However, the new claims below need repair before the theorem can be advertised.

## Definition qc-fd / Lemma qc-fd

**Issue 1: nonvanishing after full even-gap deflation is not established.**

Dividing every even-gap polynomial by its reflection-center factor gives a candidate `N_h^circ`, but the argument only shows that the linear factor accounts for one family of roots.  It does not prove that the quotient is nonzero modulo every prime outside the stated carrier.

The required statement is:

```latex
\begin{lemma}[Deflated nonvanishing]
For every even $h$ and every prime $p\notin\mathcal U_H$, the reduction
of $N_h^\circ$ modulo $p$ is nonzero.
\end{lemma}
```

and its proof must use the primitive content of the quotient, not merely
`N_h=(2x+h+1)N_h^circ`.

## Adjacent-pair destruction count

**Issue 2: the "at most three selected sliding quadruples" claim is not automatic.**

A centered adjacent pair can appear as one of the three adjacent pairs inside a sliding quadruple.  For a block containing many zeros, the number of selected quadruples containing a fixed pair is at most three only if the selection convention is exactly the consecutive quadruples

```latex
(z_i,z_{i+1},z_{i+2},z_{i+3})
```

with no re-indexing after deletions.  If the proof deletes central quadruples or changes the selected family, the bound must be reproved.

Patch the statement to:

```latex
A fixed centered adjacent pair destroys at most three members of the
original selected sliding family.
```

not "all selected quadruples" unless the family is frozen first.

## Diagonal exclusion

**Issue 3: excluding `a=b=c` is valid only with an explicit separate charge.**

The current split is acceptable:

```latex
\sum_{a=b=c} t_p(a,a,a) \le \sum_a v_p(S_{a,a}).
```

but the proof must not imply that the diagonal disappears.  It is a separate mass term and needs its own height bound.

Add:

```latex
The diagonal family is not included in $\mathcal A_{tr}(H)$ and is
charged through the self-resultants $S_{a,a}$.
```

## Valuation/content inequalities

**Issue 4: valuation transfer needs primitive contents explicitly.**

The implication

```latex
number of common roots <= v_p(content)
```

requires that the polynomial resultant/content being used is the primitive aligned content after removal of structural factors.  It is not valid for an arbitrary resultant with leading coefficient primes silently removed.

Required patch:

```latex
Let $\mathcal C^*_{a,b,c}$ denote the primitive saturated content
after removing every factor supported on $\mathfrak U_H$.
All valuations below are taken only at primes outside $\mathfrak U_H$.
```

## Ambient-height carrier convention

**Issue 5: the carrier must match the actual deflated family.**

If new even-gap quotients introduce denominators or content factors, the old carrier $\mathfrak U_H$ is insufficient.  The carrier must be defined after deflation.

Patch:

```latex
The structural carrier $\mathfrak U_H^{fd}$ is the product of all
contents and leading coefficients appearing in the deflated adjacent
certificate family.
```

Then all exceptional-prime estimates must use $\mathfrak U_H^{fd}$.

## Level-adaptive bookkeeping

**Issue 6: the extra `-3` is handled numerically but not logically.**

The estimate

```latex
Q_p(H) >= Z(p)-3 ceil(p/H)
```

combined with

```latex
H=ceil(16X/T)
```

gives a loss of about `3T/16`, but the later `T/3` lower bound requires an explicit inequality including the ceiling error.

Patch:

```latex
For sufficiently large $X$,
$3\lceil p/H\rceil+1\le 5T/12$,
so the left side of the certificate inequality is at least $T/3$.
```

Do not hide the `+3` ceiling term.

## H<=32 computational remark

The computational remark must remain support only.  It does not prove `FDAC^tr`.

Required wording:

```latex
These computations verify the stated finite instances of the saturated
content calculation. They provide no implication for the asymptotic
hypothesis $\mathrm{FDAC}^{tr}$.
```

The exact residual-prime claims (157, 431, 653) need a machine-checkable artifact digest: input range, polynomial normalization, saturation convention, and hash.  A list of primes alone is not a reproducible certificate.
