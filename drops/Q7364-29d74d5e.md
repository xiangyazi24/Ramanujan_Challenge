# Q7364 — exact pre-lower quotient-six window occupancy

Let

\[
I=I_T=(T/7,T/3],\qquad
A=A_T=\lfloor T/7\rfloor+1,\qquad
B=B_T=\lfloor T/3\rfloor,
\]

and let \((q,t)\) be a current upper state: \(q>13\) is prime,
\(0\le t<q\), \(q\mid b_t\), and all of the already-fixed \(J\)-gates
in the quotient-six model hold.  Put \(d=q-p\).  No lower condition
\(p\mid b_u\) is imposed anywhere in Sections 1--6 below.

## Terminal verdict

There are two different quantities which Q7344 denoted informally by
\(w\), and they cannot be interchanged.

1.  The number of candidate lower primes in the fixed upper state
    \((q,t)\) is necessarily state- and sign-dependent:

    \[
    \nu_-(q,t;T)=\#\mathcal P_-(q,t;T),\qquad
    \nu_+(q,t;T)=\#\mathcal P_+(q,t;T).
    \]

2.  The q-only quantity needed in Q7344's fixed-q product consumer is
    the number of admissible **upper roots** whose candidate-p union is
    nonempty:

    \[
    w_\vee(q;T,J)
      =\#\{t\in\mathcal Z_J(q):
              \mathcal P_-(q,t;T)\cup\mathcal P_+(q,t;T)\ne\varnothing\},
    \]

    where \(\mathcal Z_J(q)\) is the current \(J\)-admissible subset of
    \(\{t:0\le t<q,\ q\mid b_t\}\).  This is q-only only because \(t\)
    has been aggregated.  It is arithmetic, not a deterministic window
    length.

The exact candidate windows are

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_-(q,t;T)
 =\left\{p\in\mathbb P\cap I:
        \frac{6q+t}{7}<p<q-t\right\},}
\tag{1}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_+(q,t;T)
 =\left\{p\in\mathbb P\cap I:
        \frac{6q+t}{7}<p\le\frac{12q+t}{13}\right\}.}
\tag{2}
\]

They have the same lower endpoint.  For integer \(p\),

\[
\mathcal P_+(q,t;T)\subseteq\mathcal P_-(q,t;T)
      \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q>14t,
\tag{3}
\]

and the reverse inclusion holds when \(q\le14t\).  Consequently

\[
\nu_\vee(q,t;T)
 :=\#(\mathcal P_-\cup\mathcal P_+)
 =\max\{\nu_-(q,t;T),\nu_+(q,t;T)\},
\tag{4}
\]

not their sum.

The sharp geometry-only statewise bounds are

\[
\nu_-(q,t;T)\le N_-(q,t;T)\le T/21+O(1),
\tag{5}
\]

\[
\nu_+(q,t;T)\le N_+(q,t;T)\le T/42+O(1),
\tag{6}
\]

and

\[
\nu_\vee(q,t;T)\le N_\vee(q,t;T)\le T/21+O(1).
\tag{7}
\]

The constants \(1/21\) and \(1/42\) are sharp for integer-window
geometry, up to \(O(1)\).  Thus the local candidate capacity is of
order \(T\), or of order \(T/\log T\) after ordinary prime counting;
it is not \(O(1)\).

The q-only geometric upper-root carrier is also of order \(T\) on a
macroscopic q-range.  Hence its second moment has only the geometry
bound

\[
\sum_q w_\vee(q;T,J)^2
 \le \sum_q G_\vee(q;T)^2
 \ll_J \frac{T^3}{\log T},
\tag{8}
\]

whereas Q7344's support route requires

\[
\sum_q w_\vee(q;T,J)^2
 =o_J\!\left(T^{9/5}(\log T)^{-17/5}\right).
\tag{9}
\]

The geometry bound misses (9) by

\[
T^{6/5}(\log T)^{12/5}.
\tag{10}
\]

Using the banked upper-zero second moment instead gives the Q7344
scale

\[
\sum_q w_\vee(q;T,J)^2
 \le \sum_q Z_J(q)^2
 \ll_J T^{11/5}(\log T)^{-3/5},
\tag{11}
\]

which still misses (9) by

\[
T^{2/5}(\log T)^{14/5}.
\tag{12}
\]

The actual p-window geometry supplies neither factor.  It deletes only
narrow q-boundary and high-t boundary strata; it gives no power or log
saving on the macroscopic central q-stratum.  Therefore Q7344's proposed
\(w\)-second-moment route is not a new geometry consequence.  It remains
a localized arithmetic upper-root second-moment theorem.

## 1. Derivation of the actual minus and plus p-windows

### 1.1 Minus branch

The current minus variables are

\[
a_-=d-t-1=q-p-t-1,
\qquad
r_-=6d+t=6q-6p+t.
\tag{13}
\]

The two lower indices are in the canonical interval \([0,p-1]\) exactly
when

\[
a_-\ge0,\qquad r_-\le p-1;
\tag{14}
\]

the remaining two endpoint inequalities follow from these and
\(0\le t<q\).  Now

\[
a_-\ge0
 \Longleftrightarrow p\le q-t-1
 \Longleftrightarrow p<q-t,
\tag{15}
\]

and

\[
r_-\le p-1
 \Longleftrightarrow 6(q-p)+t\le p-1
 \Longleftrightarrow 7p\ge6q+t+1
 \Longleftrightarrow p>\frac{6q+t}{7}.
\tag{16}
\]

Intersecting with prime \(p\in I\) proves (1).

Equivalently, in the d-coordinate,

\[
\boxed{
D_-(q,t;T)=
\left[
 \max\{q-B,t+1\},
 \min\left\{q-A,\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor\right\}
\right]\cap\mathbb Z .}
\tag{17}
\]

The candidate prime is \(p=q-d\), and the only non-geometric condition
inside (17) is that \(q-d\) be prime.

### 1.2 Plus branch

In the current plus anti-diagonal variables the two lower indices are

\[
z_+=q-7d-t-1=7p-6q-t-1,
\tag{18}
\]

\[
a_+=13d+t-q=12q+t-13p.
\tag{19}
\]

Their nonnegativity gives

\[
z_+\ge0
 \Longleftrightarrow 7p\ge6q+t+1
 \Longleftrightarrow p>\frac{6q+t}{7},
\tag{20}
\]

and

\[
a_+\ge0
 \Longleftrightarrow 13p\le12q+t
 \Longleftrightarrow p\le\frac{12q+t}{13}.
\tag{21}
\]

These inequalities imply \(z_+,a_+\le p-1\).  Indeed,
\(p\le(12q+t)/13<q\), and (20) gives
\(14p\ge12q+2t+2\ge12q+t+1\).  This proves (2).

In the d-coordinate,

\[
\boxed{
D_+(q,t;T)=
\left[
 \max\left\{q-B,\left\lceil\frac{q-t}{13}\right\rceil\right\},
 \min\left\{q-A,\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor\right\}
\right]\cap\mathbb Z .}
\tag{22}
\]

Thus both signs have the same upper d-endpoint and different lower
d-endpoints; equivalently, both p-windows have the same lower p-endpoint
and different upper p-endpoints.

No expression in (13)--(22) contains \(J\).  The parameter \(J\) enters
only through the already-defined admissible upper-state set
\((q,t)\in\mathcal U_J(T)\), and can only shrink the sets below.

## 2. Exact deterministic capacities for fixed \((q,t)\)

Define

\[
\ell(q,t;T)
 =\max\left\{A,
        \left\lfloor\frac{6q+t}{7}\right\rfloor+1\right\},
\tag{23}
\]

\[
u_-(q,t;T)=\min\{B,q-t-1\},
\qquad
u_+(q,t;T)=
 \min\left\{B,\left\lfloor\frac{12q+t}{13}\right\rfloor\right\}.
\tag{24}
\]

To avoid confusing the upper endpoint \(u_\pm\) with the occupancy, set

\[
N_\pm(q,t;T)=[u_\pm(q,t;T)-\ell(q,t;T)+1]_+,
\qquad [x]_+=\max\{x,0\}.
\tag{25}
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal P_\pm(q,t;T)
 =\{p\in\mathbb P:\ell(q,t;T)\le p\le u_\pm(q,t;T)\},
\tag{26}
\]

and, with \(\pi\) denoting the prime-counting function,

\[
\nu_\pm(q,t;T)
 =\begin{cases}
   \pi(u_\pm)-\pi(\ell-1),&u_\pm\ge\ell,\\
   0,&u_\pm<\ell.
  \end{cases}
\tag{27}
\]

The exact integer capacities are also

\[
N_-(q,t;T)
 =\left[
 \min\left\{q-A,\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor\right\}
 -\max\{q-B,t+1\}+1
 \right]_+,
\tag{28}
\]

\[
N_+(q,t;T)
 =\left[
 \min\left\{q-A,\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor\right\}
 -\max\left\{q-B,\left\lceil\frac{q-t}{13}\right\rceil\right\}+1
 \right]_+.
\tag{29}
\]

Before clipping by \(I\), these become

\[
N_-^{\rm raw}(q,t)
 =\left[\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor-t\right]_+,
\tag{30}
\]

\[
N_+^{\rm raw}(q,t)
 =\left[
 \left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor
 -\left\lceil\frac{q-t}{13}\right\rceil+1
 \right]_+.
\tag{31}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
N_-^{\rm raw}
 =\left[q-t-1-\left\lfloor\frac{6q+t}{7}\right\rfloor\right]_+,
\tag{32}
\]

\[
N_+^{\rm raw}
 =\left[
 \left\lfloor\frac{12q+t}{13}\right\rfloor
 -\left\lfloor\frac{6q+t}{7}\right\rfloor
 \right]_+.
\tag{33}
\]

In particular,

\[
N_-^{\rm raw}>0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad q\ge8t+8,
\tag{34}
\]

while, on writing \(n=q-t\),

\[
N_+^{\rm raw}>0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \exists d\ge1:\ 7d+1\le n\le13d.
\tag{35}
\]

Thus the plus raw window is nonempty for \(8\le n\le13\) and for every
\(n\ge15\), and is empty for \(n\le7\) and \(n=14\).

## 3. Exact sign switch and Boolean union

The unclipped integer upper endpoints are

\[
m_-=q-t-1,
\qquad
m_+=\left\lfloor\frac{12q+t}{13}\right\rfloor.
\tag{36}
\]

Since \(m_-\) is an integer,

\[
\begin{aligned}
m_-\ge m_+
&\Longleftrightarrow
 \frac{12q+t}{13}<m_-+1=q-t\\
&\Longleftrightarrow q>14t.
\end{aligned}
\tag{37}
\]

Clipping both upper endpoints by the same \(B\), and both lower
endpoints by the same \(A\), preserves the inclusion.  Therefore

\[
\mathcal P_\vee(q,t;T)
 :=\mathcal P_-(q,t;T)\cup\mathcal P_+(q,t;T)
 =\begin{cases}
   \mathcal P_-(q,t;T),&q>14t,\\
   \mathcal P_+(q,t;T),&q\le14t.
  \end{cases}
\tag{38}
\]

Its exact integer upper endpoint is

\[
u_\vee(q,t;T)
 =\min\left\{B,
       \max\left(q-t-1,
                 \left\lfloor\frac{12q+t}{13}\right\rfloor\right)
       \right\},
\tag{39}
\]

and

\[
N_\vee=[u_\vee-\ell+1]_+=\max\{N_-,N_+\}.
\tag{40}
\]

The same identity holds for prime counts, giving (4).  Hence a
sign-separated estimate which adds the two candidate capacities loses
an avoidable factor and does not represent the Boolean marked union.

## 4. Deterministic q- and t-ranges

If either window contains a candidate integer, then its common lower
endpoint is at most \(B\).  Hence

\[
6q+t\le7B-1.
\tag{41}
\]

Also every candidate satisfies \(A\le p<q\).  Thus

\[
\boxed{A+1\le q\le\left\lfloor\frac{7B-1}{6}\right\rfloor,}
\tag{42}
\]

or, uniformly,

\[
T/7+O(1)\le q\le7T/18+O(1).
\tag{43}
\]

For either sign,

\[
0\le t\le\min\{q-1,\,7B-6q-1\}.
\tag{44}
\]

For the minus sign, (28) gives the exact simpler criterion

\[
N_-(q,t;T)>0
\Longleftrightarrow
\begin{cases}
0\le t<q,\\
t\le q-A-1,\\
t\le7B-6q-1,\\
t\le\lfloor(q-8)/8\rfloor.
\end{cases}
\tag{45}
\]

For the plus sign, the exact criterion is

\[
\max\left\{q-B,\left\lceil\frac{q-t}{13}\right\rceil\right\}
\le
\min\left\{q-A,\left\lfloor\frac{q-t-1}{7}\right\rfloor\right\}.
\tag{46}
\]

Equations (42), (45), and (46), intersected with the current
\(\mathcal U_J(T)\), are the complete deterministic range.  There is no
additional \(J\)-dependent shortening in the p-window geometry.

## 5. Sharp geometry-only statewise bounds

Ignoring endpoint rounding, the minus real window has length

\[
\left(q-t\right)-\frac{6q+t}{7}
 =\frac{q-8t}{7},
\tag{47}
\]

and the plus real window has length

\[
\frac{12q+t}{13}-\frac{6q+t}{7}
 =\frac{6(q-t)}{91}.
\tag{48}
\]

Consequently

\[
N_-^{\rm raw}(q,t)
 \le \left(\frac{q-8t}{7}\right)_++1,
\tag{49}
\]

\[
N_+^{\rm raw}(q,t)
 \le \frac{6(q-t)}{91}+1,
\tag{50}
\]

and, by (38),

\[
N_\vee^{\rm raw}(q,t)
 \le
 \begin{cases}
 (q-8t)/7+1,&q>14t,\\
 6(q-t)/91+1,&q\le14t.
 \end{cases}
\tag{51}
\]

### 5.1 Sharp global minus bound

Write \(n=q-t\).  The minus interval is

\[
\left(t+\frac{6n}{7},\,n\right).
\tag{52}
\]

If \(n\le B\), its length is at most \(n/7\le B/7\).  If \(n>B\),
clipping the upper endpoint at \(B\) gives length at most

\[
B-t-\frac{6n}{7}<B/7.
\tag{53}
\]

Therefore

\[
N_-(q,t;T)\le B/7+1=T/21+O(1).
\tag{54}
\]

Taking \(t=0\) and \(q=B+O(1)\) gives \(B/7+O(1)\) integer points, so
the constant is sharp for geometry.

### 5.2 Sharp global plus bound

Again put \(n=q-t\).  The plus interval is

\[
\left(t+\frac{6n}{7},\,
      t+\frac{12n}{13}\right],
\tag{55}
\]

of raw length \(6n/91\).  After clipping above by \(B\), its real length
is at most

\[
\min\left\{\frac{6n}{91},
             B-t-\frac{6n}{7}\right\}_+.
\tag{56}
\]

The two terms are equal at

\[
n=\frac{13(B-t)}{12},
\tag{57}
\]

where their common value is \((B-t)/14\le B/14\).  Hence

\[
N_+(q,t;T)\le B/14+1=T/42+O(1).
\tag{58}
\]

Taking \(t=0\) and \(q=13B/12+O(1)\) gives \(B/14+O(1)\) integer
points.  Thus (58) is also sharp for geometry.

Since the two windows are nested, (54) and (58) imply (7).

The inequalities \(\nu_\pm\le N_\pm\) are the sharpest bounds obtained
without prime distribution.  Standard prime counting gives
\(\nu_\pm\ll T/\log T\) in the macroscopic windows, but this is not a
power saving and it does not give a \(1/\log T\) factor for the Boolean
event \(\mathcal P_\pm\ne\varnothing\).

## 6. The exact q-only pre-lower upper-root carrier

Let

\[
\mathcal D_{\mathbb P}(q;T)
 =\{d\ge1:q-d\in\mathbb P\cap[A,B]\}.
\tag{59}
\]

For a fixed \(d\in\mathcal D_{\mathbb P}(q;T)\), the minus window is
legal exactly for

\[
0\le t\le\min\{d-1,q-7d-1\},
\tag{60}
\]

and the plus window is legal exactly for

\[
\max\{0,q-13d\}\le t\le q-7d-1.
\tag{61}
\]

Therefore the actual pre-lower prime-window t-carriers are

\[
\mathfrak T_-(q;T)
 =\bigcup_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathbb P}(q;T)}
   [0,\min(d-1,q-7d-1)]_{\mathbb Z},
\tag{62}
\]

\[
\mathfrak T_+(q;T)
 =\bigcup_{d\in\mathcal D_{\mathbb P}(q;T)}
   [\max(0,q-13d),q-7d-1]_{\mathbb Z}.
\tag{63}
\]

The correct q-only quantities are

\[
w_\pm(q;T,J)
 =\#\bigl(\mathcal Z_J(q)\cap\mathfrak T_\pm(q;T)\bigr),
\tag{64}
\]

\[
\boxed{
w_\vee(q;T,J)
 =\#\bigl(\mathcal Z_J(q)\cap
           (\mathfrak T_-(q;T)\cup\mathfrak T_+(q;T))\bigr).}
\tag{65}
\]

In particular,

\[
w_\vee\ne w_-+w_+
\tag{66}
\]

unless the two t-carriers are disjoint.

Equation (65) is pre-lower: it uses the upper zero condition \(q\mid b_t\),
primality and the deterministic p-window, but no condition
\(p\mid b_{a_-},b_{r_-},b_{z_+},b_{a_+}\).  Defining \(w_\vee\) instead
as \(\#\{t:\kappa_\vee(q,t)>0\}\) would use the post-selected marked
load and would be circular in Q7344's consumer.

### 6.1 Geometry-only relaxation

Set

\[
d_0(q)=\max\{1,q-B\},
\qquad
d_1(q)=q-A.
\tag{67}
\]

Replace \(\mathcal D_{\mathbb P}(q;T)\) by every integer
\(d_0\le d\le d_1\), and let \(G_\pm(q;T)\) and \(G_\vee(q;T)\) denote
the corresponding t-carrier cardinalities.  Then

\[
w_\pm(q;T,J)\le G_\pm(q;T),
\qquad
w_\vee(q;T,J)\le G_\vee(q;T).
\tag{68}
\]

Because all minus intervals in (60) begin at zero,

\[
G_-(q;T)
 =\left[
 1+\max_{d_0\le d\le d_1}
       \min\{d-1,q-7d-1\}
 \right]_+,
\tag{69}
\]

with an empty maximum interpreted as \(-1\).  In particular,

\[
G_-(q;T)
 \le\bigl[\min\{d_1,q-7d_0\}\bigr]_+.
\tag{70}
\]

Every plus interval lies inside

\[
[\max(0,q-13d_1),q-7d_0-1]_{\mathbb Z},
\tag{71}
\]

so

\[
G_+(q;T)
 \le\bigl[q-7d_0-\max(0,q-13d_1)\bigr]_+
 \le\min\{13d_1,q-7d_0\}_+.
\tag{72}
\]

Both sign-carriers lie in \([0,q-7d_0-1]\).  Combining (70) and (72)
gives

\[
\boxed{
G_\vee(q;T)
 \le
 \left[
 \min\{14(q-A),\ q-7\max(1,q-B)\}
 \right]_+.}
\tag{73}
\]

Equivalently, up to absolute endpoint constants,

\[
G_\vee(q;T)
 \ll
 \left[
 \min\{q,14(q-A),7B-6q\}
 \right]_+.
\tag{74}
\]

This is a sharp scale description:

* at the lower edge \(q=A+s\), the minus low-t block has size
  \(s+O(1)\), the plus high-t block has size \(13s+O(1)\), and, while
  \(14s<q\), their union has size \(14s+O(1)\);
* if \(q\le B+1\) and \(q-A\ge\lceil q/13\rceil\), the integer plus
  carrier is

  \[
  [0,q-15]_{\mathbb Z}\cup[q-13,q-8]_{\mathbb Z},
  \tag{75}
  \]

  and therefore has \(q-8\) elements;
* at the upper edge, when \(d_0=q-B\ge2\) and
  \(d_1\ge\lceil q/13\rceil\), the plus intervals overlap and

  \[
  G_+(q;T)=q-7d_0=7B-6q.
  \tag{76}
  \]

Thus there is a macroscopic central q-range on which the deterministic
t-carrier is \(q+O(1)\), hence \(\asymp T\).  The exact taper at the two
q-edges does not alter the global T-exponent.

### 6.2 Primality does not yield a logarithm for carrier nonemptiness

For a concrete uniform subregion, take

\[
T/4\le q\le3T/10,
\qquad 0\le t\le T/100.
\tag{77}
\]

The minus window lies inside \(I\), and its real length is at least

\[
\frac{q-8t}{7}\ge\frac{17T}{700}.
\tag{78}
\]

The prime number theorem therefore gives, uniformly in (77),

\[
\#\mathcal P_-(q,t;T)\asymp\frac{T}{\log T}>0
\tag{79}
\]

for all sufficiently large \(T\).  Hence the prime-window carrier itself
has \(\gg T\) admissible t-values for every q in this subrange.  The
condition that p be prime contributes a logarithm to the **number of p's**,
but contributes no logarithm to the Boolean statement that the window is
nonempty.

## 7. Audit of the Q7344 second-moment route

Let \(L=\log T\),

\[
B_0=T^2/L^2,
\qquad
U_*=T^{7/5}L^{-11/5},
\tag{80}
\]

using Q7344's notation for the marked-incidence target and the largest
occupied support closed by the banked factorial moment.  Splitting q by
the q-only carrier (65) gives

\[
\sum_{q:w_\vee(q)\le\Omega}w_\vee(q)
 \ll_J (T/L)\Omega,
\tag{81}
\]

and

\[
\sum_{q:w_\vee(q)>\Omega}w_\vee(q)
 \le \Omega^{-1}\sum_q w_\vee(q)^2.
\tag{82}
\]

The two sides can both be \(o_J(U_*)\) only from the condition

\[
\boxed{
\sum_qw_\vee(q;T,J)^2
 =o_J\left(\frac{U_*^2}{T/L}\right)
 =o_J\left(T^{9/5}L^{-17/5}\right).}
\tag{83}
\]

This consumer is valid only for the q-only root count (65).

### 7.1 Geometry gives no global improvement

There are \(O_J(T/L)\) possible q and, by (73),
\(G_\vee(q;T)=O(T)\).  Thus

\[
\sum_qG_\vee(q;T)^2\ll_JT^3/L.
\tag{84}
\]

Moreover (75) shows that the \(O(T)\) bound is active on a q-interval of
length \(\asymp T\); exact integration of the tapered envelope in (74)
still has order \(T^3/L\).  Comparing (84) with (83) gives the miss
factor (10).

The upper-zero condition gives only

\[
w_\vee(q;T,J)\le Z_J(q).
\tag{85}
\]

On the central q-stratum, the deterministic carrier is \(q+O(1)\), so
the minimum \(\min\{Z_J(q),G_\vee(q;T)\}\) supplies no new power or log
over \(Z_J(q)\).  Substituting the banked second zero-moment yields
(11)--(12), not (83).

Therefore any proof of (83) must control the arithmetic placement of
upper Apéry roots inside the actual prime-window carrier.  It cannot be
deduced from the affine window endpoints.

### 7.2 The only geometry gains are boundary deletions

From (73), for \(q=A+s\),

\[
G_\vee(q;T)\le14s+O(1),
\tag{86}
\]

and, with \(Q_{\max}=\lfloor(7B-1)/6\rfloor\), for
\(q=Q_{\max}-s\),

\[
G_\vee(q;T)\le6s+O(1).
\tag{87}
\]

A q-boundary strip of width \(H=T^{\theta+o(1)}\), \(\theta>0\),
therefore contributes

\[
\sum_{q\ {m in\ the\ strip}}G_\vee(q;T)^2
 \ll_J H^3/L.
\tag{88}
\]

It is automatically below (83) when

\[
H=o\left(T^{3/5}L^{-4/5}\right).
\tag{89}
\]

This removes narrow modulus-edge strata only.  It leaves a macroscopic
central q-stratum on which (85) is the best available inequality.

There is also a direct high-t capacity deletion.  Put \(s=q-t\).  On
\(1\le s\le R\), the plus branch is the union branch and (50) gives

\[
\kappa_\vee(q,t)\le\nu_\vee(q,t)\le6s/91+1.
\tag{90}
\]

Summing over \(O_J(T/L)\) possible q gives

\[
\sum_{q}\sum_{1\le q-t\le R}\kappa_\vee(q,t)
 \ll_J \frac{TR^2}{L}.
\tag{91}
\]

Thus this t-edge is \(o_J(B_0)\) for

\[
R=o\left(T^{1/2}L^{-1/2}\right).
\tag{92}
\]

Again, (92) is a boundary deletion, not a second-moment saving on the
central state space.

## 8. The fixed-q consumer: valid and invalid forms

Let \(\kappa_\vee(q,t)\) be the post-lower Boolean marked load.  Every
occupied t belongs to the pre-lower set counted by (65).  Hence, for any
\(X>0\),

\[
\sum_{\substack{t\in\mathcal Z_J(q)\\
       \kappa_\vee(q,t)w_\vee(q)\le X}}
 \kappa_\vee(q,t)
 \le w_\vee(q)\frac{X}{w_\vee(q)}=X,
\tag{93}
\]

with the \(w_\vee(q)=0\) case empty.  This is the valid Q7344 product
consumer.  It is noncircular because (65) is defined before every lower
zero condition.

By contrast, replacing the q-only root count by the statewise candidate
capacity \(\nu_\vee(q,t)\) gives only

\[
\sum_{\substack{t\\
       \kappa_\vee(q,t)\nu_\vee(q,t)\le X}}
 \kappa_\vee(q,t)
 \le X\sum_t\frac1{\nu_\vee(q,t)},
\tag{94}
\]

and the last sum need not be bounded.  For example, if
\(\nu_\vee(q,t)=\kappa_\vee(q,t)=1\) on \(m\) states and \(X=1\), the
left side of (94) equals \(m\).  Thus \(\nu_\vee(q,t)\) cannot replace
\(w_\vee(q)\) in (93).

Likewise, a sign-specific capacity \(\nu_\pm(q,t)\) cannot be summed as
though it were a number of upper roots.  The two quantities have
different indexing sets and different units.

## Final corrected statement

For the current quotient-six C4 state model, the exact pre-lower
candidate-p occupancies are

```text
nu_-(q,t;T) = # { prime p in (T/7,T/3] :
                   (6q+t)/7 < p < q-t },

nu_+(q,t;T) = # { prime p in (T/7,T/3] :
                   (6q+t)/7 < p <= (12q+t)/13 }.
```

They depend on \((q,t)\) and on the sign.  Their Boolean union is the
minus window for \(q>14t\) and the plus window for \(q\le14t\).  The
windows themselves have no \(J\)-dependence; \(J\) only restricts which
upper states are retained.

The q-only pre-lower carrier needed by Q7344 is instead

```text
w_vee(q;T,J)
  = # { J-admissible upper roots t with q | b_t
        for which nu_vee(q,t;T) > 0 }.
```

Geometry gives the sharp carrier envelope

```text
G_vee(q;T)
 <= [ min(14(q-A_T), q-7 max(1,q-B_T)) ]_+,
A_T=floor(T/7)+1,  B_T=floor(T/3),
```

which is \(\asymp T\) on a macroscopic q-range.  Therefore the proposed
q-only \(w\)-second-moment route saves no global power and no logarithm.
It closes only q-edge strips of width
\(o(T^{3/5}(\log T)^{-4/5})\) and the direct high-t strip
\(q-t=o(T^{1/2}(\log T)^{-1/2})\).  The central remainder still requires
an arithmetic localized upper-root moment of size (83); the actual
minus/plus p-window geometry does not supply it.
