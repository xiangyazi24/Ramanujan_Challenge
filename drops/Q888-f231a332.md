# Q888 Dwork/content divisibility audit

## Conclusion

The proposed implication

```text
v_p(c_n) <= v_p(b_n) for every prime p and every n
```

is **not proved by the stated Dwork congruence plus the triangular family alone**. The missing step is a valuation-lifting argument from the congruence

```text
b_{r+m p^s} b_{⌊r/p⌋} ≡ b_r b_{⌊r/p⌋+m p^{s-1}} (mod p^s)
```

to an inequality on the single valuation `v_p(b_n)`. That step requires division by `b_{⌊r/p⌋}`. The zero-denominator cases are not exceptional technicalities: they are exactly the cases where the induction would need additional information.

## Where the attempted induction fails

Let

```text
T_t(k): e - v_p(k) + v_p(b_{n-k}) >= t
```

be the inequalities forced by `p^t | c_n`. To deduce `p^t | b_n`, one would need to propagate every admissible index `n-k` up to `n`.

Writing `n=r+mp^s`, Dwork gives

```text
b_n b_{⌊r/p⌋} ≡ b_r b_{⌊r/p⌋+mp^{s-1}} mod p^s.
```

If `v_p(b_{⌊r/p⌋})=0`, this can be divided and gives a valuation transfer. However, if

```text
p | b_{⌊r/p⌋},
```

then the congruence only says that a product is congruent modulo `p^s`; it gives no lower bound for the valuation of `b_n`. The triangular conditions do not remove this obstruction because they only provide divisibility of shifted terms `b_{n-k}`, not a nonzero pivot term.

Thus the induction has an unresolved branch whenever the Dwork pivot vanishes.

## Computational status

No exact counterexample to

```text
c_n | b_n
```

was obtained from the available evidence. The data through `n <= 2000` remains consistent with the stronger divisibility. Therefore the correct audit result is:

```text
rad(c_n)|b_n is proved.
full p-adic containment is plausible but not established by the supplied argument.
```

A valid proof would need a separate lemma controlling the zero-pivot cases, for example a theorem of the form

```text
v_p(b_r)>0 => v_p(b_{r+mp^s}) >= v_p(b_r)+s
```

or an equivalent crystalline/Dwork unit-root statement. The quoted congruence alone is insufficient.

## Companion divisibility

The companion claim

```text
c_n | L_n^3 a_n
```

is logically separate. Even if one proved `c_n | b_n`, that would not automatically imply the companion divisibility because the known relation between the Apéry sequence and the companion sequence contains denominator growth. One needs an independent integrality argument showing that the coefficient extraction defining `a_n` absorbs exactly the same prime powers.

The observed identity

```text
c_n | gcd(b_n, L_n^3 a_n)
```

through `n <= 2000` is therefore evidence for a stronger common-content phenomenon, not a consequence of the current Dwork argument.

## Final assessment

The new Dwork/content attack does not currently prove the conjectured valuation inequality. The obstruction is a genuine zero-denominator branch in the Dwork induction. No counterexample is supplied by computation, so the statement remains open from the given ingredients. The companion divisibility requires a separate proof and cannot be obtained by upgrading radical containment alone.
