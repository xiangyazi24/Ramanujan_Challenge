# 10. Final research ruling

The exact centered Fourier calculation does reveal the observed cancellation mechanism, but not through a classical off-the-shelf inequality.

* **Ramanujan/Möbius:** exact, but returns the centered cubic correlation.
* **Poisson:** exact, but is simply the length-\(2M\) interval operator; the sharp tail remains.
* **Additive reciprocity:** gives the useful \(p\) versus \(q\ell\) compression, but opening \(R_p\) creates a modulus-dependent Kloosterman coefficient.
