# Q909 cold audit: signed Padé degree-three no-go

## Verdict

The claimed proof is **not PROVED as written**. The strongest defensible conclusion is a scoped degree-three obstruction, but several displayed steps require repair before they can be promoted to a theorem. In particular, the argument does not establish a valid global convexity/determinant certificate from the stated asymptotic information alone.

## 1. Propagation of the endpoint expansion through three applications of M

The endpoint expansion for the coefficients c_k is not by itself sufficient to justify the claimed expansions of x_k,y_k,z_k after three applications of the moment transform M.

The missing point is remainder bookkeeping. If

```
c_k = c_k^(0)+c_k^(1)H^{-1}+c_k^(2)H^{-2}+O(H^{-3})
```

is substituted into one application of M, the transformed error is multiplied by the k-dependent kernel. After three iterations, terms that were nominally lower order can contribute at the same order as the retained coefficients unless the operator norm of the iterated kernel and the summation range are explicitly bounded.

Therefore the line asserting the final x_k,y_k,z_k expansions from the endpoint expansion has an unproved precision gap. A repair requires an explicit three-step remainder lemma for M.

## 2. Inequalities from s_k,v_k,t_k asymptotics

The asymptotics may indicate the expected ordering, but strict inequalities do not follow unless the error terms are uniform in the full range K <= k <= H-3.

Fixed-k asymptotics are insufficient for a statement quantified over all intermediate k. The proof needs either:

* a uniform error estimate in k, or
* a finite exceptional range check plus a monotonicity argument.

Without that, the strict inequalities are not established.

## 3. Determinant factorization and convex-position step

The factorization (3.7) must be checked independently against the exact definitions of the three coordinates. The convex-position conclusion requires that the signed determinant have the same sign for every admissible k.

The displayed fixed-k leading term does not prove this for all K <= k <= H-3 because the O_k(H^2) term is allowed to depend on k. For k growing with H, it can dominate the cubic main term.

Thus the first invalid inference is the passage:

```
D_(k,H)=2a^6 H^3+O_k(H^2)
therefore D_(k,H)>0 for every K<=k<=H-3.
```

This only proves positivity for each fixed k and sufficiently large H depending on k.

## 4. Fixed-k determinant expansion

The expansion

```
D_(k,H)=2a^6 H^3+O_k(H^2)
```

is compatible with the local calculation if the determinant orientation is the one used in the proof. The sign itself is not the fatal issue; the quantifier upgrade from fixed k to the whole rectangle is.

## 5. Cofactors, rank, integrality, and binomial lower bound

The cofactor construction gives the expected rank obstruction only after the determinant nonvanishing statement is available. The proof still needs:

* a precise orientation convention for the cofactor vector;
* verification that the resulting vector is primitive after clearing denominators;
* a lower bound on the integer size after clearing denominators.

The binomial lower bound cannot be inferred from a rational null vector without this normalization step.

## Scan statement

The requested scan command is a useful consistency check, but finite computation up to limit 300 cannot prove the asymptotic theorem. A passing scan would only support the repaired finite-range component.

## Strongest exact scope currently justified

The valid conclusion is:

> Degree-three signed Padé constructions exhibit a candidate local obstruction and a fixed-k determinant asymptotic with positive leading coefficient, but the proof does not establish a uniform all-k convex-position theorem or the resulting radical bound.

No extension to degree four follows.

## Required repairs

A complete proof would need:

1. a uniform three-iteration M remainder estimate;
2. uniform-in-k asymptotics for the transformed coordinates;
3. a determinant estimate valid over the entire rectangle K <= k <= H-3;
4. an explicit primitive integral cofactor normalization.

Until these are supplied, the degree-three no-go remains a conjectural obstruction rather than a proved dependency-map node.
