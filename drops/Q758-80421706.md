# Q758 audit: corrected valuation of the q=1 carrier

## 1. Setup

Let
\[
A=J+1=\lceil n/3\rceil,
\]
and
\[
B=B_{1,n}=\binom{n+A}{A}\binom{n}{A}
=\frac{(n+A)!}{A!^2(n-A)!}.
\]
Write
\[
n=p+r,
\]
where p is a prime in the q=1 range (roughly n/2<p\le n). Since A<p for large n, only the numerator interval can contain a multiple of p.

The relevant numerator factors are
\[
n+1,\ldots,n+A.
\]
Among these, a multiple of p occurs exactly when
\[
r+i\equiv0\pmod p,
\]
with 1\le i\le A. This gives a multiple precisely when
\[
p-r\le A,
\]
equivalently
\[
r+A\ge p.
\]

The denominator factors A! and (n-A)! contribute a p-factor only through (n-A)! because A<p. A multiple of p occurs in (n-A)! exactly when
\[
n-A\ge p,
\]
that is
\[
r\ge A.
\]

Therefore
\[
v_p(B)=1+\mathbf 1_{r+A\ge p}-\mathbf 1_{r\ge A}.
\]
This is the corrected formula. The previous additive two-indicator formula was wrong because the denominator contribution cancels the numerator contribution.

## 2. Classification of valuation cases

There are three regimes.

### Case I: r<A

The denominator has no p factor. Hence
\[
v_p(B)=1+\mathbf 1_{r+A\ge p}.
\]
Thus:

- if r+A<p, then v_p(B)=1;
- if r+A\ge p, then v_p(B)=2.

### Case II: r\ge A

The denominator contributes one p. Hence
\[
v_p(B)=\mathbf 1_{r+A\ge p}.
\]
Since r\ge A and A\sim n/3, both subcases are possible:

- if r+A<p, then v_p(B)=0;
- if r+A\ge p, then v_p(B)=1.

### No higher valuation

Because A<p and n=p+r<2p in the q=1 range, the numerator interval of length A contains at most one multiple of p. Also A! and (n-A)! contain at most one multiple of p. Therefore
\[
v_p(B)\in\{0,1,2\}.
\]

The valuation-two boundary is exactly
\[
r<A\quad\text{and}\quad r+A\ge p.
\]
Equivalently
\[
p-A\le r<A.
\]

## 3. Explicit residue formulas

Let
\[
C=B/p^{v_p(B)}.
\]
All congruences below are modulo p.

### Valuation one, direct branch

Assume
\[
r<A,\qquad r+A<p.
\]
The unique numerator multiple is absent in the interval after shifting by p, and the only p factor comes from the factor n+A? More explicitly the p-containing factor in the factorial quotient is the unique factor
\[
n+i=p+(r+i)
\]
with i=p-r when this lies in the interval. Removing this factor gives the unit product
\[
\frac Bp\equiv
\frac{(r+1)(r+2)\cdots(r+A)}{A!^2\,(r+1)(r+2)\cdots(r+A-1)}
\pmod p,
\]
which simplifies to the corresponding factorial ratio after cancellation. The exact simplified form depends on which endpoint representation of B is used.

### Valuation two

When
\[
p-A\le r<A,
\]
the numerator contains one p-factor and the denominator does not. The quotient B/p is still divisible by p:
\[
B/p\equiv0\pmod p.
\]
Hence the meaningful jet is
\[
B/p^2\pmod p,
\]
which requires the second-order expansion of the factorial product.

### Denominator-cancelled branch

When r\ge A and r+A\ge p, one numerator p-factor cancels the denominator p-factor, giving
\[
v_p(B)=1.
\]
The remaining unit is obtained by cancelling the two p-factors before reducing modulo p.

## 4. Small-prime check and correction of Q757

The previous claim that the valuation was always the sum of two positive indicators produces false predictions. Direct checks for primes p<100 show counterexamples exactly at the overlap region where the denominator p-factor cancels.

For example, if r\ge A and r+A\ge p, the false formula would predict valuation 2, while the corrected formula gives valuation 1.

The failure mechanism is not rare: it occurs whenever
\[
A\le r\quad\text{and}\quad r+A\ge p.
\]

Thus Q757's valuation-two count was an overcount.

## 5. Interaction with target primes p|b_r

The target condition is
\[
p\mid b_r.
\]
The carrier argument only gives
\[
p\mid B_{1,n}.
\]
To combine them, one needs B/p^v modulo p to be nonzero or a controlled function of b_r.

However, valuation-two cases satisfy
\[
B/p\equiv0\pmod p,
\]
so the first-order quotient contains no information. They saturate the carrier and destroy the hoped-for one-power congruence.

There is no automatic reason that
\[
p\mid b_r
\]
forces
\[
r\notin[p-A,A)
\]
or excludes the valuation-two boundary. Such exclusion would require an additional arithmetic relation between Apéry residues and the factorial carrier.

## Verdict

The corrected valuation identity is:

\[
\boxed{v_p(B_{1,n})=1+\mathbf1_{r+A\ge p}-\mathbf1_{r\ge A}}.
\]

Consequences:

1. Q757's additive two-indicator valuation is false.
2. The valuation-two region is exactly \(p-A\le r<A\).
3. First-order Lucas-type quotient arguments fail on this saturated region.
4. The q=1 carrier remains valid, but a new target-selective congruence is required to exploit it for the gcd problem.
