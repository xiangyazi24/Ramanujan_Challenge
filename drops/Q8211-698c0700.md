## 5. Exact inverse Gram formulas

Define

\[
Q_n(t)=\sum_{k=0}^nU_{n,k}t^k=P_n(1+2t),
\]

where \(P_n\) is the Legendre polynomial. Hence

\[
\int_{-1}^{0}Q_n(t)Q_m(t)\,dt=\frac{\delta_{nm}}{2n+1}.
\]

Rodrigues' formula gives the exact inverse

\[
\boxed{
B_{n,k}=(U^{-1})_{n,k}
=(-1)^{n-k}\frac{(2k+1)(n!)^2}{(n-k)!(n+k+1)!}.
}
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal H^-_{a,b}=\int_{-1}^{0}t^{a+b}\,dt
=\frac{(-1)^{a+b}}{a+b+1}.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
B W^{-1}B^{\mathsf T}=\mathcal H^- ,
\qquad
U^{\mathsf T}WU=(\mathcal H^-)^{-1}.
}
\]

The latter matrix has the explicit positive-integer entries

\[
\boxed{
(U^{\mathsf T}WU)_{a,b}
=(a+b+1)
\binom{N+a+1}{N-b}
\binom{N+b+1}{N-a}
\binom{a+b}{a}^{2}.
}
\]

Since \(M=UU^{\mathsf T}\) and \(G=MWM\),

\[
\boxed{M^{-1}=B^{\mathsf T}B,
\qquad G^{-1}=B^{\mathsf T}\mathcal H^-B.}

Entrywise,

\[
(M^{-1})_{a,b}=(-1)^{a+b}(2a+1)(2b+1)

\frac{(r!)^4}
{(r-a)!(r+a+1)!(r-b)!(r+b+1)!},

\[

={}&(-1)^{a+b}(2a+1)(2b+1)\\

The determinants are

\[
\det U=\prod_{j=0}^{N}\binom{2j}{j},

\[

## 6. Exact midpoint radical

For \(p=2N+1\), the folded nodes are distinct because

\[
\lambda_k-\lambda_j=(k-j)(k+j+1)
e0\pmod p
\quad(0\le j<k\le N).
\]

Also \(U_{j,j}=\binom{2j}{j}\ne0\pmod p\), so \(U,M\) are invertible. The only zero weight is \(W_{N,N}=p=0\), hence \(MWM\) has nullity one.

Using

\[
U_{n,k}=\binom{2k}{k}\binom{n+k}{2k}
\]

and the hockey-stick identity,

\[
\sum_{n=k}^{N}U_{n,k}
=\frac{N+k+1}{2k+1}U_{N,k}.
\]

For \(v=(1,\ldots,1,N+1)^{\mathsf T}\), this yields

\[
\boxed{
(U^{\mathsf T}v)_k
=\frac{(k+1)(2N+1)}{2k+1}U_{N,k}.
}
\]

For \(k<N\) this is zero modulo \(p\); for \(k=N\), cancel in characteristic zero first and obtain \((N+1)U_{N,N}\). Hence

\[
Mv\equiv (N+1)U_{N,N}^2e_N,
\]

which is killed by \(W\). This proves the kernel formula.

The signed Hilbert inverse makes the same phenomenon visible as a unique pole. Its only denominator divisible by \(p\) is

Therefore

\[
\boxed{

with

\[
B^{\mathsf T}e_N
\equiv\frac{(-1)^N}{N+1}(1,\ldots,1,N+1)^{\mathsf T}.

The determinant formula gives the exact diagnostic

Thus the observed corank is exhausted by the midpoint weight.
