## Status

The constrained search shows that the reciprocal-square-root Hasse countermodel is not an Apéry solution. The first obstruction is already the Apéry three-term coefficient recurrence; no higher differential identity is needed.

## Countermodel residual

The F19 construction satisfies the coarse Hasse constraints (degree, central gap, reciprocal symmetry, and the imposed Sigma=0 condition), but the Apéry sequence must satisfy

\[
(j+1)^3 b_{j+1}=(34j^3+51j^2+27j+5)b_j-j^3b_{j-1}.
\]

Substituting the countermodel coefficients gives a nonzero residual

\[
\rho_j=(j+1)^3b_{j+1}-C(j)b_j+j^3b_{j-1}\pmod {19}.
\]

The first failed equation occurs at the smallest available recurrence index. Thus the countermodel is eliminated before any endpoint or derivative constraint is used.

## Minimal violated identity

The minimal Apéry-specific identity is therefore the first local recurrence equation. The coarse Hasse package only controls the geometric shape of the polynomial; it does not enforce that the coefficients come from the Apéry differential equation.
