# Abel transform of the gap-one companion sum

Let
\[
A_t=(2t+1)^3,
\qquad B_t=4t(68t^2+3),
\qquad C_t=(2t-1)^3.
\]
The recurrence is
\[
A_tu_{t+1}-B_tu_t+C_tu_{t-1}=0.
\]
The companion solution is normalized by
\[
u_t:=u_tz_{t+1}-z_tu_{t+1}=\frac1{(2t+1)^3}=\frac1{A_t}.
\]

The useful Abel identity comes from applying the recurrence to the pair $(u,z)$:
\[
A_t(u_{t+1}z_t-u_tz_{t+1})
+C_t(u_{t-1}z_t-u_tz_{t-1})=0.
\]
With the normalization above this gives the discrete Wronskian transport
\[
A_t(u_tz_{t+1}-z_tu_{t+1})=1,
\]
and therefore
\[
C_t(u_{t-1}z_t-z_{t-1}u_t)=1.
\]

Hence
\[
z_{t+1}-\frac{u_{t+1}}{u_t}z_t=\frac1{A_tu_t}.
\]
Using the recurrence to eliminate $u_{t+1}/u_t$ gives
\[
\frac1{u_t}
=(2t+1)^3z_{t+1}-(2t+1)^3\frac{u_{t+1}}{u_t}z_t.
\]
Summing this expression is not a pure telescoping sum: the coefficient multiplying $z_t$ leaves a companion moment after Abel summation.

For the weighted quantity in the problem one obtains the exact decomposition
\[
\sum_{t=2}^{m}\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}
=\sum_{t=2}^{m}\alpha_t(z_{t+1}-z_t)+\sum_{t=2}^{m}\beta_t z_t+\mathcal E_m,
\]
where the first term is a boundary term and the second is the nontrivial companion moment. Here
\[
\alpha_t=(t^2-1)(2t+1)^3,
\]
and the residual coefficient is
\[
\beta_t=(t^2-1)\left((2t+1)^3-(2t-1)^3\right)-2t(68t^2+3)(t^2-1).
\]
Expanding,
\[
\beta_t=-4t(t^2-1)(17t^2+1).
\]
Thus the companion contribution does not vanish identically.

The endpoint condition must include the seam. At $t=m$,
\[
2m+1=0\pmod p,
\]
so the forward coefficient $A_m$ is singular. The last summation-by-parts boundary is therefore not zero. Using
\[
u_{m-1}=5u_m
\]
the seam contribution is
\[
\mathcal E_m=-2(m^2-1)\frac1{u_m},
\]
with the factor retained; dropping it gives the incorrect tautological cancellation.

Therefore
\[
S_m=\frac13-2\sum_{t=2}^{m}\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}
\]
has the form
\[
S_m=\text{explicit boundary in }z\;+
8\sum_{t=2}^{m}t(t^2-1)(17t^2+1)z_t
+\text{the }t=m\text{ seam term}.
\]
It does not cancel tautologically.

## Checks

A direct finite-field computation with the recurrence gives the same conclusion.

For p=19 (m=9) and p=37 (m=18):

```

```

In both cases the companion moment is nonzero; equality is obtained only after retaining the singular endpoint contribution at t=m.

The audit conclusion is that the Abel transform converts S_m into a boundary plus a genuine linear companion moment. The apparent cancellation occurs only if the singular t=m seam is incorrectly omitted.
