# Q904: combined Apéry b-jet and companion p^4 jet audit

## Conclusion

No new fixed characteristic-zero cross-prime carrier follows from the two stated jet layers alone. The scoped result is a no-go: the data supplied by

\[
 b_{qp+r}\pmod {p^2},\qquad p^3a_{qp}\pmod {p^4}
\]

at finitely many target primes form a CRT-saturated local module. Eliminating the local variables can remove the displayed slopes, but it cannot create an integer of logarithmic height o(n) whose divisibility contains a nontrivial product of the target primes unless an additional prime-independent coupling datum is supplied.

The obstruction is not the rank-one first-lift statement. The obstruction is that after the available eliminations, every remaining condition is a congruence in independent local residue coordinates.

## 1. Local coordinates at a target prime

Fix a row n and a hit prime p. Write

\[
 n=q p+r,
\]

with p>q and p>2r. At p|b_r and p∤b_q the mod-p^2 jet gives

\[
 b_n=b_q(b_r+2qpD_r)\pmod {p^2}.
\]

Dividing only after using p|b_r gives

\[
 \frac {b_n}{p}=b_q\left(\frac {b_r}{p}+2qD_r\right)\pmod p.
\]

The variable

\[
 X_p:=\frac {b_r}{p}+2qD_r
\]

is a genuine local coordinate. The term b_r/p cannot be discarded: deleting it would artificially reduce the local module dimension.

For the companion, the given endpoint laws imply

\[
 p^3a_n=C_{p,q}b_n+p^3a_r/b_q\pmod {p^4},
\]

and

\[
 b_qp^3a_n-a_qb_n=p^3a_r\pmod {p^4}.
\]

Since a_r is a p-adic unit at the target, the divided coordinate

\[
 Y_p:=\frac{b_qp^3a_n-a_qb_n}{p^3}\pmod p
\]

is nonzero. This is exactly one transverse local coordinate.

## 2. Attempted elimination over two primes

Take two target primes p_1,p_2. The available equations have the form

\[
 b_n/p_i=b_{q_i}X_i\pmod {p_i},
\]

and

\[
 b_{q_i}p_i^3a_n-a_{q_i}b_n=p_i^3a_{r_i}\pmod {p_i^4}.
\]

Any characteristic-zero expression E(n) fixed before the primes are known must be formed from the global integers b_n,a_n,n and fixed coefficients. Reducing E modulo p_i, the only information supplied by the jets is the residue class of X_i and Y_i. There is no relation between X_1 and X_2: their definitions use different local data

\[
(r_1,q_1,p_1),\qquad(r_2,q_2,p_2).
\]

Consequently the map

\[
(X_1,Y_1,X_2,Y_2)\longmapsto\text{allowed local residues}
\]

has independent CRT factors. Eliminating X_i and Y_i merely projects this product module; it does not create a diagonal constraint.

The same argument applies to four primes. The local solution space is a product

\[
M_{p_1}\times M_{p_2}\times M_{p_3}\times M_{p_4},
\]

and every elimination using only the supplied jets is compatible with changing one factor while fixing the others.

## 3. Equal-gap rectangles do not change the conclusion

Suppose q_i are arranged in an equal-gap rectangle. Then q_i are fixed integer quotients and r_i=n-q_ip_i. The rectangle relates the arithmetic positions of the hits, but it does not give a congruence between

\[
\frac{b_{r_i}}{p_i}+2q_iD_{r_i}
\]

for different primes. The recurrence gives relations in the index variable, and the Casoratian gives

\[
 a_jb_{j-1}-a_{j-1}b_j=6/j^3,
\]

but after specialization at different folded indices it still produces local rational functions with denominators depending on the local primes. Clearing those denominators produces factors of size comparable to the product of the local denominators, not a small fixed carrier.

In particular, the Casoratian does not supply a prime-independent linear form annihilating all local coordinates.

## 4. Height obstruction

A putative carrier H(n), fixed before reading p_i, with

\[
\prod_i p_i\mid H(n)
\]

would need two properties:

1. its reduction modulo each p_i vanishes because of the jet equations;
2. its size remains o(exp(n)), equivalently logarithmic height o(n).

The first property requires a common polynomial relation among the local coordinates. The two jets provide only

\[
X_i\in\mathbf F_{p_i},\qquad Y_i\in\mathbf F_{p_i}^{\times},
\]

with independent CRT variation. Hence no such relation exists.

Any expression that forces cancellation by inserting p_i-dependent coefficients is excluded because the coefficients are chosen after seeing the hit primes. If denominators are cleared to make coefficients integral, their size necessarily contains the missing prime-dependent information and the o(n) height estimate is lost.

## 5. Minimal additional datum required

A new theorem would need one of the following genuinely cross-prime inputs:

1. a prime-independent identity relating the Apéry residues at different folded indices, for example a characteristic-zero formula forcing

\[
F(r_1,q_1)=F(r_2,q_2)=\cdots
\]

for the jet coordinates;

2. a higher jet that couples the same global integer to second-order local slopes in a way not reducible to independent CRT coordinates;

3. an independent bound on a fixed integer linear form in a_n,b_n whose local reductions contain all target primes.

The present mod-p^2 b-jet plus companion mod-p^4 endpoint law provides none of these.

Therefore the strongest valid statement is the scoped no-go: these two layers alone do not yield a fixed characteristic-zero logarithmic-height o(n) multi-prime carrier, even using equal-gap rectangles and the Casoratian. They remain CRT-saturated local data.
