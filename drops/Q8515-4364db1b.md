## claim
Let

\[
f_p(m)=1_{m\bmod p\in Z_p},\qquad g_p=f_p-z_p,
\]

with \(z_p=|Z_p|/p\). For distinct primes,

\[
\sum_{m<M}g_pg_qg_\ell
=\sum_{u\in Z_p\times Z_q\times Z_\ell}U(u),
\]

where

\[
U(u)=\sum_{m<M}\prod_{s\in\{p,q,\ell\}}(1_{m\equiv u_s\pmod s}-1/s).
\]

This follows by expanding the product and summing each indicator intersection. The CRT term is

\[
N U(u)=NI_{p q\ell}-p q I_{pq}-p\ell I_{p\ell}-q\ell I_{q\ell}+pA_p+qA_q+\ell A_\ell-M,
\]

with the notation from the candidate. The formula is an exact inclusion-exclusion expansion.

For a hit residue \(u<M\), the main term is the single CRT representative contribution, and the remaining six correction terms are bounded by the number of prime divisors in the triple window, giving

\[
1-4/X<U(u)<1+3/X.
\]

For a nonhit residue there is no full CRT representative below M, and the same correction estimates give

\[
-4/X<U(u)<4/X.
\]

Therefore the signed triple correlation C satisfies

\[
(1-4/X)H_3+E_-
<C
<(1+3/X)H_3+E_+,
\]

where the CRT error is bounded using the nonhit contribution.

The proposed bound

\[
S_-<32M\lambda^3
\]

is consistent as a sufficient negative-mass estimate if the nonhit sums are bounded with the stated uniform constants. It implies

\[
(1-4/X)H_3-32M\lambda^3<C<(1+3/X)H_3+32M\lambda^3.
\]

However, this does not by itself prove the desired ACF3 third moment. It only transfers control between the signed centered cubic correlation and H3 up to an error of order \(M\lambda^3\).

## second-moment route audit

The definitions

\[
c_m=(K(m))_2,
\]

and

\[
c_m^{(\ell)}=c_m-2(K(m)-1)f_\ell(m)
\]

remove exactly the pairs involving \(\ell\). Hence

\[
H_3=\sum_\ell\sum_{r\in Z_\ell}N_{\ell r}
\]

with

\[
N_{\ell r}=\sum_{m\equiv r(\ell)}c_m^{(\ell)}.
\]

Writing

\[
N_{\ell r}=n_\ell/\ell+(N_{\ell r}-n_\ell/\ell),
\]

Cauchy gives the stated form

\[
H_3\leq 4M\lambda^3+\sqrt{2X\lambda D^2_Z},
\]

provided the baseline contribution is bounded by the corresponding first-moment terms.

The large-sieve estimate

\[
D^2_Z\leq5X\sum_m c_m^2
\]

is an L2 bound only. The identity

\[
c_m^2=(K)_4+4(K)_3+2(K)_2
\]

is exact by the falling-factorial relation.

## correction of overclaims

The route does not prove that every possible L2 method is impossible. It only shows that this particular exclusion-free replacement plus the displayed large-sieve estimate is circular unless higher factorial moments are already controlled.

Likewise, the statement that the negative mass is numerically much smaller than H3 is not justified at the target scale: both quantities can naturally be of order

\[
M\lambda^3.
\]

The valid conclusion is a reduction: proving the desired H3 bound would suffice to control the signed correlation, and the displayed L2 estimate does not independently close the argument.
