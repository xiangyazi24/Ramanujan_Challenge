## 8. Narrow face-local centered successor

Let `k_{3/5}(q,t)` count only rows whose complete lower vertex belongs to the exact `z=3/5` many-`q` residual. Let `k_{2/3}(q,t)` count only rows on the exact surviving asymmetric `z=2/3` many-`q` edge. All physical and source occurrences are retained in these integer loads.

For an integer `R>=1` define

\[
\Psi_R(k)
 =\left(\binom{k}{2}-\binom{R}{2}\right)_+.
\tag{8.1}
\]

For every integer `k`,

\[
\boxed{k\le R+\frac{\Psi_R(k)}R.}
\tag{8.2}
\]

Indeed, for `k>R`,

\[
\Psi_R(k)
 =\frac{(k-R)(k+R-1)}2
 \ge R(k-R).
\]

### 8.1 `z=3/5`

Take

\[
R_{3/5}=T^{2/5-\eta+o(1)}.
\]

Since the number of actual selected upper states is at most `B_1(T)`, (8.2) gives

\[
M_{3/5}
 \le R_{3/5}B_1
 +R_{3/5}^{-1}
  \sum_{q,t\in\mathcal Z_q}
   \Psi_{R_{3/5}}(k_{3/5}(q,t)).
\tag{8.3}
\]

Therefore the sufficient theorem is

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{q\asymp_JT}
\sum_{\substack{0\le t<q\\q\mid b_t}}
\Psi_{R_{3/5}}(k_{3/5}(q,t))
 \ll_J T^{12/5-2\eta+o(1)}.}
\tag{8.4}
\]

It yields

\[
M_{3/5}\ll_JT^{2-\eta+o(1)}.
\tag{8.5}
\]

### 8.2 `z=2/3`

Take

\[
R_{2/3}=T^{3/10-\eta+o(1)}.

This `11/5-2eta` exponent is sharp at the exported interface: the hostile graph has regular state load `T^(3/10)` and factorial mass `T^(11/5)`.

Expanding (1.4), the new object is

\[

\Psi_R\!\left(
