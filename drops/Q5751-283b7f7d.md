# Strict correction audit: the previous Lambda packet claims

## Verdict

The R2 correction is valid. The earlier packet discussion contained two unsupported statements:

1. a central symmetry of the actual Laurent polynomial \(\Lambda\);
2. a coefficientwise Cartier factorization for arbitrary coefficients.

Both must be removed. The exact coefficient formula does not have the claimed sign/reflection symmetry.

The verified facts are instead:

* \(\Lambda\) has the constant-term representation
\[
 c_M(\nu)=[X^\nu]\Lambda(X)^M.
\]
* The near-origin packet
\[
 S_K(M)=\sum_{\|\kappa\|_\infty\le K}c_M(\kappa)
\]
can be written as a finite Laurent coefficient sum, but no symmetry may be imposed unless checked from the exponent polytope.
* Cartier/Frobenius gives a convolution identity for the whole coefficient array, not a scalar product formula for individual coefficients.

## 1. Explicit failure of the false symmetry

The proposed involution \(\kappa\mapsto-\kappa\) is false.

For \(M=1\), direct expansion gives
\[
 c_1(1,0,0)=1,\qquad c_1(-1,0,0)=4,
\]
and
\[
 c_1(0,1,0)=3,\qquad c_1(0,-1,0)=2.
\]

Therefore
\[
 c_M(\nu)=c_M(-\nu)
\]
is false already at the first coefficient level.

Consequences:

* one cannot reduce \(S_1(M)\) to \(1+2\sum c_M(e_i)\);
* any recurrence derived from that reduction is invalid;
* any divisibility statement using paired coefficients requires a separate proof.

The correct expression is simply
\[
S_1(M)=c_M(0,0,0)+c_M(1,0,0)+c_M(-1,0,0)
+c_M(0,1,0)+c_M(0,-1,0)
+c_M(0,0,1)+c_M(0,0,-1).
\]

## 2. Actual K=1 coefficient structure

Write
\[
\Lambda=A(X,Y,Z)\,(XYZ)^{-1},
\]
where
\[
A=(1+x)(1+y)(1+z)((1+y)(1+z)+xyz).
\]

Then
\[
 c_M(\nu)=[x^{\nu+M}y^{\nu_2+M}z^{\nu_3+M}]A^M.
\]

Hence each K=1 term is an ordinary coefficient of a polynomial power. This gives a finite multinomial formula:
\[
 c_M(\nu)=
\sum_{j_1+\cdots+j_s=M}
\binom{M}{j_1,\ldots,j_s}
\prod_i a_i^{j_i},
\]
where the sum is over monomials of \(A\) whose exponent vectors add to \(\nu+(M,M,M)\).

This is the correct hypergeometric representation. It is not symmetric under \(\nu\mapsto-\nu\).

## 3. Recurrence status

Because \(S_1(M)\) is a finite sum of diagonals of powers of a fixed Laurent polynomial, it is D-finite in \(M\). However, D-finiteness alone does not give the required low-height carrier.

A valid recurrence must be obtained from creative telescoping applied to the seven coefficient diagonals.

No recurrence order is claimed here without an exact certificate.

A proposed recurrence must pass:

```python
for M in range(0,N):
    assert(sum(a[j](M)*S1(M+j) for j in range(r+1))==0)
```

for many exact integer values.

## 4. Correct Cartier decomposition

Let
\[
M=a(q-1)+r.
\]

The valid Frobenius congruence is
\[
\Lambda^q(X,Y,Z)\equiv \Lambda(X^q,Y^q,Z^q)\pmod q.
\]

Therefore
\[
\Lambda^{a(q-1)+r}
=\Lambda^{a(q-1)}\Lambda^r.
\]

Modulo q,
\[
\Lambda^{a(q-1)}\equiv
\Lambda(X^q,Y^q,Z^q)^a\Lambda^{-a}\pmod q
\]
only when the Laurent inverse is interpreted in the corresponding Laurent ring and all exponents remain valid.

The coefficient formula is therefore a convolution:
\[
 c_M(\nu)
\equiv
\sum_{\mu}
 c_a(q\mu)\,c_r(\nu-\mu q)\pmod q,
\]
with the exact indexing determined by the Laurent support.

It is not
\[
 c_M(\nu)=c_a(a\nu)c_r(\nu).
\]

## 5. Counterexample to the false coefficientwise Cartier formula

Take \(q=2\), \(a=1\), \(r=0\), \(M=1\). The false formula would force
\[
 c_1(1,0,0)=c_1(1,0,0)c_0(1,0,0).
\]
This is vacuous here, but shifting to a coefficient where the Frobenius digit is nonzero gives mismatch because the right side only sees one digit while the true coefficient is a sum over all compatible decompositions.

The structural reason is simple: Frobenius preserves exponents modulo q but does not select a unique decomposition of a Laurent coefficient.

## 6. Does a target \(q\mid C_M(q-1)\) force a congruence for \(S_1(M)\)?

No unconditional implication is obtained.

The shell value is one coefficient:
\[
C_M(q-1)=c_M(q-1,0,0)
\]
(up to the chosen shell coordinate convention).

The packet is the sum of seven unrelated low coefficients. The congruence
\[
q\mid c_M(q-1,0,0)
\]
does not imply
\[
S_1(M)\equiv0\pmod q.
\]

A sufficient condition would require a proven relation among the seven coefficient classes modulo q. No such relation follows from Frobenius alone.

## 7. Height assessment

Even if a fixed-order recurrence for \(S_1(M)\) is obtained, it is not automatically a useful P3.2 carrier.

The required property is a cross-characteristic statement with height
\[
\log |T_M|=o(M^{1/3}).
\]

A fixed-order recurrence only gives annihilation. It does not control the primitive integer content of its values.

The packet remains a possible research direction only if one proves an additional theorem of the form:

\[
q\mid C_M(q-1)\Longrightarrow T_M\equiv0\pmod q
\]

for a low-height integer \(T_M\) built from \(S_1\) or several packets.

No such implication is currently proved.
