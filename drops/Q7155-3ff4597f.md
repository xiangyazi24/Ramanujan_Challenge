# P32 cubic Mellin certificate R2 audit

## 1. The certificate is exactly the four-zero relation

Let the four offsets be

\[
h_0=0<h_1=a<h_2=a+b<h_3=s,
\]

and let

\[
Q(T)=\sum_{i=0}^3q_iT^{h_i}
\]

be the barycentric polynomial satisfying

\[
(T-1)^3\mid Q(T).
\]

The three vanishing conditions at \(T=1\) are

\[
\sum_iq_i=0,\qquad \sum_iq_ih_i=0,\qquad \sum_iq_ih_i^2=0.
\]

For any scalar sequence \(u_n\), define the Laurent evaluation

\[
\mathcal L_r(Q)=\operatorname{CT}(\Lambda^rQ(\Lambda))
\]

with \(\Lambda\) the shift operator satisfying \(\Lambda^nu_0=u_n\). Then

\[
\mathcal L_r(Q)=\sum_iq_iu_{r+h_i}.
\]

Therefore for the Apéry sequence,

\[
 b_{r+h_i}=0\;(0\le i\le3)\implies \mathcal L_r(Q)=0.
\]

Conversely, without additional information on the four values, \(\mathcal L_r(Q)=0\) is only one linear relation. It is not equivalent to four zeros. The certificate has not created a new congruence; it packages an already-known zero pattern.

## 2. Why the construction works for every sequence

Nothing in the construction uses the Apéry recurrence. For any sequence \(u_n\), if four entries vanish, the same \(Q\) gives

\[
\sum_iq_iu_{r+h_i}=0.
\]

Hence the certificate is a universal annihilator of four prescribed positions, not an Apéry-specific identity.

The recurrence would only enter if one could prove that the same polynomial relation forces additional terms to vanish. No such implication follows from \((T-1)^3\mid Q\).

## 3. Dividing by \((T-1)^3\)

Write

\[
Q(T)=(T-1)^3R(T).
\]

The quotient \(R\) has smaller degree/height, but

\[
\operatorname{CT}(\Lambda^rQ(\Lambda))=0
\]

is still exactly the original barycentric relation. Dividing changes the representation, not the information content.

No extra divisibility statement appears unless there is an independent congruence controlling \(R(\Lambda)\).

## 4. Height and \(p>s^2\)

The coefficient bound \(\|Q\|\ll s^3\) is useful for a determinant/resultant argument, but alone it gives no contradiction modulo \(p\).

A small integer relation

\[
\sum_iq_ib_{r+h_i}\equiv0\pmod p
\]

does not imply any coefficient is zero. The missing ingredient would be an archimedean or algebraic lower bound preventing such relations.

The condition \(p>s^2\) only prevents coefficient wraparound; it does not turn a relation into a zero.

## 5. Cyclotomic conjugates and norms

Taking conjugates or norms of the relation gives

\[
N\left(\sum_iq_ib_{r+h_i}\right)=0.
\]

But this is still the norm of the same zero element. It does not produce independent equations because all conjugates arise from the same relation.

A genuine gain would require a nontrivial Galois orbit of distinct relations. The present \(Q\) has no such source.

## 6. Anti-reciprocity on \(a=c\)

When the gap pattern is symmetric, the polynomial can become anti-reciprocal:

\[
T^sQ(T^{-1})=-Q(T).
\]

This reflects the involution \(r\leftrightarrow p-1-r\). It may identify the reflected chain, but it does not add a new vanishing condition.

The symmetry is therefore a quotient by the reflection orbit, not a new equation.

## 7. Combining with the Picard--Fuchs equation

The Apéry recurrence gives a second-order difference equation. Applying it to the relation only produces identities involving neighboring shifts of the same four-zero data.

To obtain a new constraint one would need to show that

\[
Q(\Lambda)b_r=0
\]

forces another factor of the recurrence operator. The current certificate does not show this.

The recurrence annihilator and the barycentric annihilator are independent operators; their common kernel has not been proved smaller.

## 8. Normalization issue

The fixed Laurent polynomial must be normalized consistently with the oracleC construction. The invariant object is

\[
Q(\Lambda)=\sum_iq_i\Lambda^{h_i},
\]

up to multiplication by a nonzero scalar and powers of \(\Lambda\). Changing the displayed formula for \(\Lambda\) changes the indexing convention but not the underlying relation.

Any comparison with oracleC must first align whether \(\Lambda\) is the forward shift or the recurrence transfer eigenvalue. The Q7145 normalization mixed these conventions; no mathematical strengthening follows from that mismatch.

## 9. Final verdict

The cubic Mellin certificate route is closed in its present form.

**Proved:**

* four zeros imply the cubic-moment relation;
* the construction is sequence-independent;
* self-reciprocity/anti-reciprocity only encodes reflection;
* quotienting by \((T-1)^3\), height bounds, and norms do not create new congruences.

**Not proved:**

* any independent Apéry congruence;
* any forced fifth zero;
* any exclusion of short four-zero chains.

Therefore the certificate is a repackaging of the four equations
\(b_{r+h_i}=0\), not a new obstruction. A successful route must add genuinely arithmetic input (for example a moving-prime estimate, monodromy constraint, or independent recurrence rigidity statement), not further manipulation of the barycentric polynomial alone.
