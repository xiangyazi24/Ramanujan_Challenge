# Q1401 audit: persistent Smith defects and moving height windows

## Classification

**CONCRETE PARTIAL**. The repaired moving-window strategy has a precise sufficient lemma, but the lemma is not currently proved from one-wrap Lucas blocks. The p=379 computations are consistent with the repaired route and rule out only the single-window formulation; they do not prove a universal constant-window theorem.

## 1. The exact repaired theorem that would suffice

Let

\[
M_{K,a}= [U_{s,k}]_{0\le s\le K,0\le k\le a}\mid[-b_sU_{s,k}]_{0\le s\le K,0\le k\le K-a}.
\]

Let \(P_{K,a}\) be the primitive saturated numerator obtained from the integral kernel after dividing by the maximal determinantal divisor. Let \(T_A\) denote the target prime set \(p>2A\).

A sufficient pointwise statement is the following.

**Persistent Smith defect lemma (sufficient form).** There exists an absolute constant \(C\) such that for every large \(H\), every prime \(p\le H+CH^{2/3}\), and every degree \(a\le (H+CH^{2/3})^{2/3}\), define

\[
\Delta_p(H,a)=\{K\in[H,H+C H^{2/3}]:p\mid P_{K,a}\text{ coefficientwise}\}.
\]

Then the weighted persistent defect mass satisfies

\[
\sum_{p\notin T_A} \log p\;\mathbf 1(\Delta_p(H,a)=\text{all window heights})=o(H).
\]

Equivalently, after intersecting all canonical primitive numerators in the window, the remaining nuisance divisor has logarithmic height \(o(H)\).

This statement is intentionally about the **saturated primitive kernel**, not modular kernel dimensions.

## 2. Why p=379 changes the theorem shape

The old claim was:

\[
\exists K\text{ with }K\sim H\implies\text{ exact target support}.
\]

The computation at p=379 disproves this. The corrected quantifier is:

\[
\exists K\in[H,H+CH^{2/3}]
\]

or, for gcd purposes,

\[
\bigcap_{K\in[H,H+CH^{2/3}]}\operatorname{supp}(P_{K,a}).
\]

The observed transition

\[
474\le K\le535\quad\leadsto\quad379\mid P_{K,a},
\]

but

\[
K=540,a=27:\quad P_{K,a}(\tau(1423))\equiv59\pmod{379},
\]

and

\[
K=550,a=3:\quad P_{K,a}(\tau(1423))\equiv45\pmod{379}
\]

shows that a finite persistence interval can terminate. It gives evidence for a finite lifetime of a Smith defect, not an asymptotic bound.

## 3. First required proof step: charging a one-wrap block

For a nuisance prime p, write

\[
K=qp+r,
\]

with \(0\le r<p\). The one-wrap Lucas mechanism gives a decomposition of the triangular carrier into blocks separated by the fold points of

\[
U_{s,k}=\binom{s+k}{2k}.
\]

A possible charging invariant is:

\[
\mathcal C_p(K,a)=\{j:\text{a p-adic Smith pivot remains non-unit after the j-th wrap}\}.
\]

The needed estimate is not merely

\[
v_p(P_{K,a})>0.
\]

It must prove a lifetime bound

\[
\#\{K\le H: v_p(\operatorname{sat}(M_{K,a}))>0\}
\ll H^{2/3+o(1)}
\]

uniformly in p and a.

Summing with the standard split

\[
p\le H^{2/3},\qquad p>H^{2/3}
\]

would then give

\[
\sum_p v_p(D_H)\log p=o(H).
\]

The missing step is precisely the uniform lifetime bound.

## 4. Why fixed-p Lucas information is insufficient

The implication

\[
p\mid b_n\Rightarrow p\mid b_q\ \text{or}\ p\mid b_r
\]

only gives a recursion for membership in the zero set. It does not control the Smith saturation because the primitive kernel depends on the entire interpolation matrix.

A defect can persist through several wraps because the denominator block contributes the same p-adic obstruction to the determinantal quotient. Therefore one needs a statement about invariant factors:

\[
\nu_p(d_i(M_{K,a}))
\]

for the Smith factors, not only about \(b_n\bmod p\).

## 5. Candidate obstruction to a universal C

A no-go would require constructing a family of Apéry-type sequences where, for primes p_i growing with H,

\[
p_i\mid P_{K,a}
\]

for every

\[
K\in[H,H+C H^{2/3}].
\]

The p=379 example does not provide this: its persistence length is approximately 62 heights, while the proposed window length is asymptotically growing like \(H^{2/3}\). Hence it is not a counterexample to any fixed-C theorem.

The obstruction needed would be a sequence of nuisance primes with persistence length

\[
\gg H^{2/3}
\]

or with total weighted contribution

\[
\sum \log p\gg H.
\]

No such family is currently established.

## 6. What must be computed next

The decisive computation is not rank mod p. It is the saturated Smith layer:

1. Compute the p-primary Smith filtration of \(M_{K,a}\).
2. Divide the kernel vector by the exact determinantal divisor.
3. Reduce the primitive numerator modulo p.
4. Record the first K in the window where the residue becomes nonzero.

The p=379 data indicates that this transition can happen quickly, but no proof converts that observation into a uniform bound.

## 7. Final verdict

**CONCRETE PARTIAL.**

The repaired moving-window statement is the correct target. The weakest sufficient lemma is a uniform bound on the persistence lifetime of non-target p-adic Smith defects after saturation. The current one-wrap Lucas information gives a plausible charging framework but does not yet prove the lifetime estimate. The known p=379 computation demonstrates transient persistence and later canonical removal, but neither proves the theorem nor supplies a scalable obstruction.
