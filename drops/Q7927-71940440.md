# Q7927 audit: corrected normalization

## Verdict

The previous formula is not merge-safe as written unless the pairing convention is fixed. The centered transform and semiprime operator factorization survive, but the factor bookkeeping has to be corrected.

## Full-unit versus half-range

Let Q=pqℓ. The primitive sum is a full unit sum

\[
Q^{-1}\sum_{h\bmod Q,(h,Q)=1}W_M(h/Q)\prod_{r=p,q,\ell}R_r(h\overline{Q/r}).
\]

Since R_r(-a)=R_r(a) and W_M(-x)=overline{W_M(x)}, pairing h and -h gives twice the real part of the half-range sum. Therefore:

- the full-unit convention has coefficient 1/Q;
- the paired half-range convention has the factor coming from the two conjugate terms;
- writing 1/(2pqℓ) is valid only after the kernel has already absorbed the pairing factor.

Using 1/(2pqℓ) together with a kernel that is still the unpaired full-unit kernel loses a factor of two (and a second factor of two can appear if the real-part conversion is also counted).

## Ordered semiprime blocks

For fixed output prime p, the variables (q,ℓ) are ordered. The count is

\[
\asymp (X/\log X)^2,
\]

not half of this. The diagonal q=ℓ is excluded but is lower order. Any argument replacing ordered pairs by unordered pairs must restore the missing factor 2.

## Centered energy

For

\[
R_p(a)=\sum_{r\in Z_p}e_p(a(r+1/2)),
\]

Parseval gives

\[
\sum_{a\bmod p}|R_p(a)|^2=p|Z_p|.
\]

On unit frequencies:

\[
\sum_{a\in\mathbb F_p^\times}|R_p(a)|^2=p|Z_p|-|Z_p|^2.
\]

Hence the zero frequency cannot silently be included in a unit-sum estimate. With |Z_p|\ll p^{2/3}, the crude scale is

\[
E_p\ll p^{5/3}.
\]

## Semiprime operator

The factorization

\[
T_{p,v}=(2\sqrt{pv})^{-1}U_pU_v^t
\]

is dimensionally correct under the paired normalization. Consequently

\[
\|T_{p,v}\|_{HS}^2\le {E_pE_v\over4pv}.
\]

The denominator pv is essential; dropping it gives the wrong scale.

## Weighted sufficient estimate

The proposed sufficient condition

\[
\sum_p {p\over Z(p)}(E_p)_+^2\ll X^{4+o(1)}\lambda^5
\]

is valid only with (E_p)_+ interpreted as positive spectral contribution. It is not a consequence of Parseval alone. Parseval gives only

\[
E_p\ll p^{5/3},
\]

which yields a much larger total contribution. Thus this is a genuine arithmetic input.

## Ideal orthogonality gap

Even granting perfect orthogonality between semiprime blocks, the ordered triple count is

\[
(X/\log X)^3.
\]

The square-root gain from orthogonality leaves scale

\[
X^{3/2}/\log^{3/2}X.
\]

Relative to the required HM3 target normalization, the missing gain remains

\[
\boxed{X/(\lambda^3\log X)}.
\]

Thus ideal block orthogonality alone cannot prove the desired estimate.

## Merge-safe proposition

**Proposition.** With the paired half-range convention fixed, the primitive output-prime transform uses the conjugation-paired kernel and not an additional pairing factor. Ordered (q,ℓ) counting must be retained. The centered transforms satisfy Parseval energy on unit frequencies, and the semiprime block operator satisfies

\[
T_{p,v}=(2\sqrt{pv})^{-1}U_pU_v^t,
\qquad
\|T_{p,v}\|_{HS}^2\le E_pE_v/(4pv).
\]

The bound

\[
\sum_p(p/Z(p))(E_p)_+^2\ll X^{4+o(1)}\lambda^5
\]

is a sufficient hypothesis but requires new Apéry-specific cancellation. No proof may replace unit residues by all residues, remove ordered-pair factors, or replace positive spectral parts by unsigned energies.
