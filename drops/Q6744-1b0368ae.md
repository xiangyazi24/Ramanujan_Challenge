## (i) Beacon families

The modular-propagation route is PARTIAL, not a full propagation theorem.

Using only the stated audited inputs, the provable beacon set is:

- r = (p-1)/2: the reflection center. This is forced by the palindrome identity y_{p-1-n}=y_n and controls the parity law for |Z_p|.
- r = (p-1)/4 and its reflected partner 3(p-1)/4: the quarter-value beacon. The given eta-multiplier theorem identifies this point with a weight-3 level-6 modular value.
- Reflections of already known beacons under r -> p-1-r.

I do not have a proved additional family at r=(p-1)/3, (p-1)/6, or general (p+-1)/k. The fact that the underlying motive has level-6 modular structure does not by itself give a rational parameter-line action producing those orbit points.

Therefore the currently banked beacon family is finite. A finite beacon family only gives finitely many incident return curves and cannot attack the growing-gap regime.

## (ii) Does Fricke/Atkin-Lehner give a second involution on the r-line?

No second position-line involution is currently justified.

The reason is structural: the modular involution acts on the modular parameter, while the discrete index r is the truncation coordinate of the Apéry recurrence. To induce a second symmetry on r, one needs an explicit Mobius map sigma(r) satisfying

xi_{sigma(r)} = Gamma(xi_r)

for a fixed projective transformation Gamma and all admissible r.

The only such correspondence supplied by the audited data is reflection:

sigma(r)=p-1-r,

with Gamma equal to the identity on the projective orbit values. This gives Z_d reflection stability but no cross-gap conversion.

The level-6/Fricke information explains the special values, but it does not automatically descend to an automorphism of the discrete recurrence clock. In particular, the missing theorem would have to be a compatibility statement between the modular parameter involution and the Frobenius-indexed recurrence lattice. No such compatibility is among the proved inputs.

Hence the clean death certificate for the stronger propagation claim is:

A modular involution on the motive does not imply an involution of the recurrence index line unless it preserves the chosen integral horizontal section defining xi_r. Reflection has this property; no second one is currently proved.

## Consequence for the LAST-STAND objective

The quarter beacon remains a valuable arithmetic constraint, but it is a local pin, not a growing family.

A return landing on a beacon gives a low-degree condition in the gap variable, but the number of beacon rows is O(1). This cannot produce the required anti-coexistence against the padded scattered construction.

The missing object remains the same: a quenched cross-gap correlation theorem for the growing family of root sets Z_d.

The modular route would become relevant only if one proves one of the following stronger statements:

1. a growing family of explicit beacon rows r_i with controlled density and explicit xi_{r_i}; or
2. a genuine second correspondence sigma on the index line satisfying exact transport relations between Z_d and another gap family.

Neither is presently established.
