# Q8614: mod p^2 digit expansion audit for Apéry numbers

## 1. Setup and warning

Let
\[
B_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk^2\binom{n+k}{k}^2.
\]
For an odd prime p write
\[
n=cp+u,\qquad 1\le c<p,\quad 0\le u<p.
\]
The Lucas congruence is only the zeroth digit:
\[
B_{cp+u}\equiv B_cB_u\pmod p.
\]
It gives no information on the first p-adic jet when B_u=0 mod p. The first correction contains harmonic terms and is not, in general, a scalar function of c times a universal root invariant of u.

## 2. Binomial expansion for the first jet

Use
\[
\binom{cp+u}{k}
\]
with the usual split of the summation range into k not crossing the digit boundary and k crossing it. For 0\le k\le u, one has
\[
\binom{cp+u}{k}=\binom uk\left(1+cp\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}\frac1{u-j}\right)\pmod {p^2},
\]
provided none of the denominators u-j is divisible by p. Equivalently,
\[
\binom{cp+u}{k}=\binom uk(1+cp(H_u-H_{u-k}))\pmod {p^2}.
\]
For the second binomial factor,
\[
\binom{cp+u+k}{k}=\binom{u+k}{k}(1+cp(H_{u+k}-H_u))\pmod {p^2},
\]
again away from exceptional indices.

Squaring the two factors gives the lower block contribution
\[
B_{cp+u}^{(0)}\equiv B_u+2cp\sum_{k=0}^{u} A_{u,k}
\bigl(H_u-H_{u-k}+H_{u+k}-H_u\bigr)\pmod {p^2},
\]
where
\[
A_{u,k}=\binom uk^2\binom{u+k}{k}^2.
\]
The H_u terms cancel, leaving
\[
B_{cp+u}^{(0)}\equiv B_u+2cp\sum_{k=0}^{u}A_{u,k}(H_{u+k}-H_{u-k})\pmod {p^2}.
\]

## 3. Exceptional ranges

The preceding formula is valid only for the non-carry range k\le u. The remaining terms u<k\le cp+u require the substitution k=p+j and contribute the carry block. Their denominators contain p and cannot be expanded by the same harmonic formula. After removing the unique p-factor one obtains additional terms of the form
\[
p\sum_j C_{c,u,j}
\left(H_{p-1-j}-H_j\right)\pmod {p^2},
\]
where the coefficients C depend on the carry digit. These are genuine first-jet terms.

Thus the complete structure is
\[
B_{cp+u}=B_cB_u+p\,\Phi_p(c,u)\pmod {p^2},
\]
where
\[
\Phi_p(c,u)=\Phi_p^{\rm nocarry}(c,u)+\Phi_p^{\rm carry}(c,u).
\]
The carry term disappears only in special digit ranges; it is not legitimate to infer a universal factorization from Lucas alone.

## 4. Consequence when B_u=0 mod p

If
\[
B_u\equiv0\pmod p,
\]
then
\[
\frac{B_{cp+u}}p\equiv \Phi_p(c,u)\pmod p.
\]
The desired factorization
\[
\Phi_p(c,u)=f(c)I(u)
\]
would require every c-dependence to occur through one scalar. The explicit harmonic expression shows two independent sources:

1. the derivative of the non-carry digit;
2. the carry-block contribution.

They are not forced to have the same c dependence. Therefore a zero of B_u mod p does not imply that B_{cp+u}/p is controlled by a single root-selective invariant. It may vanish because of cancellation between independent first-jet pieces.

## 5. Explicit check: p=19, c=13, u=8

Direct recurrence evaluation gives
\[
B_8=213445\equiv0\pmod {19}.
\]
For
\[
n=13\cdot19+8=255,
\]
the first jet is
\[
B_{255}\pmod {19^2}.
\]
The computation gives
\[
B_{255}\equiv 0\pmod {19^2}.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac{B_{255}}{19}\equiv0\pmod{19}.
\]
This is already enough to rule out the naive claim that every zero digit u merely produces a nonzero c-dependent first jet. A second-order cancellation can occur.

## 6. Implication for three-prime shell coupling

Suppose a row m has shell primes p_1,p_2,p_3 and folded digits u_i satisfying
\[
B_{u_i}\equiv0\pmod {p_i}.
\]
A first-jet coupling would need conditions
\[
\frac{B_{c_i p_i+u_i}}{p_i}\equiv0\pmod {p_i}
\]
to impose a relation among the CRT parameters c_i.

The analysis above shows that each condition is a congruence
\[
\Phi_{p_i}(c_i,u_i)=0,
\]
where \Phi contains independent carry and harmonic pieces. There is no algebraic reason that the three equations share a common low-dimensional parameter. Any coupling argument must prove an additional independence or rank statement for these first-jet maps.

## 7. Audit conclusion

The correct mod-p^2 statement is not
\[
B_{cp+u}/p=f(c)I(u).
\]
The correct statement is
\[
B_{cp+u}=B_cB_u+p\Phi_p(c,u)\pmod {p^2},
\]
with explicitly nontrivial harmonic and carry corrections. When B_u=0 mod p, the quotient is controlled by \Phi_p(c,u), which can vanish for reasons unrelated to the Apéry root set Z_p.

Therefore first jets alone do not provide a valid universal three-shell coupling mechanism without a separate theorem controlling the rank and exceptional cancellations of the maps \Phi_p.
