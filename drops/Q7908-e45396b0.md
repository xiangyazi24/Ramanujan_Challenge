# Cold audit of the branch-defect repair

## Verdict

The proposed repair is not yet a valid lemma. It removes the unsafe assertion that the vanishing-cycle line is the exceptional curve, but it silently replaces it by a stronger specialization statement that is not automatic for a singular proper surface.

The correct conclusion is conditional:

> If the minimal resolution is defined over F_p, the ADE configuration is split over F_p, the specialization map identifies the relevant global Tate lattice with a Frobenius-stable rank-19 sublattice of the nearby invariant part, and the local invariant-cycle map is an isomorphism on H^2 after accounting for the rational-double-point local terms, then the primitive stalk trace has no additional branch defect.

Those hypotheses are not consequences of merely saying “split A_1”.

## Audit of the proposed chain

### 1. `H^2(Xtilde)=H^2(X_alpha) direct-sum Q_l(-1)`

This is the first invalid unconditional step.

For a proper surface with a rational double point, there is a canonical relation between the resolution and the singular surface, but the naive direct-sum statement needs hypotheses. The exceptional divisor contributes the root lattice generated by exceptional curves, and its Frobenius action depends on whether the ADE configuration is defined over the residue field.

For a split A_1 point, the exceptional curve is indeed a geometrically rational (-2)-curve defined over F_p, and its cohomology contribution is Q_l(-1). In that special case one obtains a Frobenius-equivariant exact sequence

```
0 -> Q_l(-1)[E] -> H^2(Xtilde) -> H^2(X_alpha) -> 0
```

which splits noncanonically as vector spaces. The formula

```
Tr(H^2(X_alpha)) = Tr(H^2(Xtilde)) - p
```

is therefore valid only after proving that the exceptional divisor is Frobenius-fixed.

It is not valid merely because the singularity is geometrically A_1.

### 2. Proper local invariant cycles

The proposed map

```
H^2(X_alpha) -> H^2(X_generic)^I
```

is not a formal dimension argument.

The invariant cycle theorem gives surjectivity of specialization onto the inertia invariants. For a proper family one has the nearby-cycle sequence and the obstruction is controlled by vanishing cycles. In general:

```
H^2(X_alpha) -> H^2(X_eta)^I
```

is not an isomorphism without excluding extra local terms.

For an ordinary double point degeneration the missing piece is exactly the vanishing-cycle contribution. Over a strictly henselian trait the local monodromy analysis gives a one-dimensional vanishing space. Frobenius on that space is not determined by the exceptional curve; after a quadratic base change the two can acquire different descent data.

Relevant references:

- SGA 7 II, Exp. XIII, especially the invariant cycle theorem and Picard-Lefschetz formalism.
- SGA 7 II, Exp. XV for monodromy and vanishing cycles.
- Illusie, *Autour du théorème de monodromie locale*, for nearby-cycle complexes.

Thus the dimension count “21 equals 21, therefore isomorphism” is incomplete. One must show that the local vanishing contribution has already been removed and that no nontrivial Frobenius-twisted local term remains.

### 3. What “split A_1” must mean

The word split has to be strengthened.

The required hypothesis is not only:

```
X_alpha has an A_1 singularity over F_p.
```

It must mean:

1. the singular point is F_p-rational;
2. the quadratic tangent cone splits over F_p;
3. the exceptional conic in the blow-up has an F_p-rational point;
4. the resulting exceptional P^1 is defined over F_p;
5. Frobenius acts trivially on the one-dimensional root lattice.

Without (4)-(5), the exceptional class contributes p times a nontrivial character, not p.

This is precisely the descent issue avoided by not identifying the vanishing line with the exceptional curve.

## 4. Do H^1/H^3 intervene?

For a K3 surface with rational double points, H^1 and H^3 vanish for the smooth resolution. For the singular fiber, however, the nearby-cycle spectral sequence is the correct object. The question is not ordinary H^1/H^3 of the fiber; it is whether local intersection cohomology or vanishing-cycle terms alter the specialization map.

The relevant triangle is

```
Q_l -> R psi Q_l -> R Phi Q_l ->
```

and the defect is measured by the cohomology of R Phi.

An A_1 gives a rank-one vanishing-cycle object. Its Frobenius action must be computed independently. It cannot be replaced by the exceptional divisor class.

## 5. The 19 Tate classes

The rank-19 lattice argument can work, but compatibility must be checked.

It is not enough that the generic fiber has 19 algebraic classes. One needs:

- divisors extending over the total space near alpha;
- their specializations avoiding ambiguity at the singular point;
- Frobenius-stable classes on the singular/resolved fiber;
- independence after specialization.

If the boundary divisors in `toric_fiber_k3.tex` are explicit curves over F_p and their intersection matrix remains nondegenerate after resolving ADE points, then the specialization of those 19 classes is valid. The document currently gives such a divisor-lattice argument for the resolved K3, but that is a statement about the resolution, not automatically about the singular stalk.

## Correct replacement lemma

A publication-safe version is:

> **Lemma (branch defect criterion).** Let X -> Spec(R) be a proper flat one-parameter degeneration of K3 surfaces over a strictly henselian DVR with special fiber having only split rational double points. Assume the minimal resolution X~ of the special fiber is defined over the residue field, every exceptional component is Frobenius-stable, and the specialization map from the divisor lattice generated by the 19 global Tate classes remains injective and Frobenius-compatible. If the nearby-cycle vanishing contribution is the unique rank-one A_1 vanishing module and its Frobenius eigenvalue is accounted for separately, then the primitive trace obtained from the resolved fiber has no additional branch defect.

The final clause is essential. It does not assert that the vanishing eigenvalue equals p.

## Missing hypothesis in the current argument

The exact missing input is:

```
A Frobenius-equivariant identification of the rank-one local vanishing-cycle contribution at alpha with the removed local term in the singular-fiber trace formula, including its descent character.
```

Equivalently, one must compute the local Frobenius action on the A_1 vanishing cycle or prove that the chosen simultaneous resolution exists over F_p with trivial descent character.

Until that is supplied, the statement “remove the exceptional P^1 contribution and the primitive trace is theta with E_br=0” is not proved.

The repair is therefore directionally correct, but the exact conclusion is conditional rather than established.
