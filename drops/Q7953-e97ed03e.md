# Q7953 Cold audit of K3 relative ADE argument

## 0. Scope

This audit deliberately weakens the previous conclusion. The local blow-up calculation can support a relative-model statement only after explicit chart verification. It does not imply the endpoint middle-extension theorem.

## 1. A2 blow-up audit

Let the local singularity be written in completed local coordinates as

```
xy-z^3=0
```

along the singular section. The ordinary blow-up of the ideal `(x,y,z)` has three affine charts.

### x-chart

Put `y=xY, z=xZ`. The strict transform is

```
Y-Z^3 x^2=0.
```

The derivative with respect to `Y` is 1, so this chart is regular.

### y-chart

Symmetrically, with `x=yX,z=yZ`, the equation is

```
X-Z^3 y^2=0,
```

again regular.

### z-chart

Put `x=zX,y=zY`. The strict transform is

```
XY-z=0.
```

The Jacobian has rank one everywhere, hence this chart is regular.

Therefore a genuine A2 hypersurface singularity of this form is resolved by one blow-up. However this conclusion depends on the local equation actually being analytically equivalent to `xy-z^3`; a persistent family singularity with additional parameter terms must be reduced to this normal form before the claim is valid.

## 2. Branch-root checks

Testing the residual equations at special residue characteristics p=5,7,11 does not by itself prove the henselian statement. The required computation is:

1. reduce the total-space equations modulo each prime;
2. compute the singular ideal `(f,df/dx_i)` on every blow-up chart;
3. saturate by the exceptional coordinate;
4. verify the resulting ideal is the unit ideal.

The symbolic chart calculation above proves the generic A2 model case. It does not certify the specific K3 pencil without the explicit local expansions.

## 3. Global exceptional labeling

A chosen A2 exceptional component is globally labeled over the henselian trait only if the exceptional divisor components form a finite etale (or explicitly split) relative divisor scheme after the blow-up. Local chart labels do not automatically descend to a global label.

The missing hypothesis is a proof that the component permutation action of the henselian base is trivial.

## 4. Specialization audit

Three statements must be separated.

### Relative divisor lattice

A rank-19 relative divisor lattice statement concerns algebraic cycles on the resolved family. It requires:

- regular proper flat total space;
- horizontal Cartier divisors;
- independence of the claimed divisor classes;
- constant intersection matrix.

It does not imply vanishing-cycle statements.

### One-node invariant-cycle statement

The invariant-cycle theorem requires an isolated degeneration with controlled local monodromy. It identifies the invariant part of cohomology; it is not a consequence of the divisor lattice computation.

### Endpoint middle extension

The endpoint trace/middle-extension assertion additionally requires the sheaf-theoretic local monodromy and conductor calculation. Nothing above proves it.

## 5. Minimal publication proposition

```tex
\begin{proposition}[Relative ADE model and branch defect]
Let R be a henselian trait and let X/R be the given K3 pencil. Assume that
at every singular section the completed local equation of the total space is
analytically equivalent to an $A_2$ hypersurface $xy-z^3=0$, and assume that
the exceptional components after blow-up descend as Cartier divisors over R.
Then the single blow-up of the seven singular sections is regular in a
neighbourhood of those sections. The strict transform is a relative Cartier
hypersurface. If the resulting morphism is proper and flat, the exceptional
curves give the predicted local ADE branch defect.
No assertion about the endpoint middle-extension trace or Frobenius
specialization follows from this proposition.
\end{proposition}
```

## 7. Claims not proved by this audit

1. That the concrete K3 pencil has exactly the required A2 normal forms.
2. That all seven sections are simultaneously resolved by one global blow-up.
3. That the exceptional component labeling splits over the original henselian trait.
4. That the 14 boundary transforms plus exceptional curves have constant Gram matrix 48.
5. That the relative lattice calculation implies the endpoint middle-extension theorem.
