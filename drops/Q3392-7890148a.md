# Verdict

The proposed endpoint argument is **not yet a valid unconditional global ledger**.

Its short-prefix arithmetic is mostly sound: once a single, prime-independent endpoint `P` is fixed, the representation

```text
p = P + 1 + h,    0 <= h <= 139
```

has a unique `h`, and the fixed endpoint product

```text
E_P := product_(h=0)^139 (P+1+h)
```

pays one copy of every such prime at total logarithmic height `O(log m)`. Composite values among the 140 factors are harmless overpayment.

The fatal gap is later. To pass from primewise statements to one low-height global carrier, the proof must exhibit **the same nonzero characteristic-zero integer** for every prime in a branch. The following implications are valid only in that universal form:

```text
for every long p:   p^nu_p     | C_long,
for every short p:  p^(nu_p-1) | C_tail,
```

with `C_long` and `C_tail` independent of `p`. If this is true, reuse is legitimate: pairwise coprimality gives the product divisibility automatically. If Proposition 8.1 or `Rstar(K0)` instead supplies a witness depending on `p`, on `h`, on a chosen row, or on a p-divided normalization, then the note has silently replaced a family of witnesses by one carrier; that step is false.

Even under the favorable universal interpretation, the advertised height

```text
O(H + log m)
```

has not been proved. The endpoint product contributes `O(log m)` and `b_(sigma_7)` contributes `O(H)`, but the current project state contains no theorem giving

```text
log |Rstar(K0)| = O(H + log m)
```

for the required fixed divided-tail boundary. The immediately preceding exact audits identify precisely this tail value as an independent Green/integration constant. A recurrence that propagates the tail is not a height bound for its initial value. Any denominator or full-selector normalization hidden in `Rstar` must also be cleared; on slope 7 the known residual selector saturation can have logarithmic size `Theta(sqrt(m))`, not `O(log m)`.

Thus the dichotomy is a correct **support partition**, subject to the endpoint and unit-window qualifications below, but its final global-height conclusion is false as a proved statement. The smallest honest repair is to make the result conditional on one explicit universal divided-tail ledger lemma, stated in Section 8. If the unavoidable slope-7 saturation is retained, the natural corrected target is at best

```text
O(H + sqrt(m) + log m) = o(m)
```

under the usual campaign hypotheses, not `O(H+log m)`.

# 1. The exact global ledger that would be needed

Let `T` be the residual target-prime set and let

```text
nu_p >= 1
```

be the exponent that still has to be paid at `p`. Partition

```text
T = T_long disjoint_union T_short.
```

Let `P` be the fixed endpoint used to define the prefix. Put

```text
E_P = product_(h=0)^139 (P+1+h).
```

The proposed argument is valid if it establishes nonzero integers

```text
C_long in Z \ {0},
C_tail = b_(sigma_7) Rstar(K0) in Z \ {0},
```

which are fixed before a target prime is selected, and proves

```text
v_p(C_long) >= nu_p                  for all p in T_long,       (L)

p = P+1+h_p, 0<=h_p<=139             for all p in T_short,      (S1)

v_p(C_tail) >= nu_p-1                for all p in T_short.      (S2)
```

Then, and only then, the desired product divisibility follows immediately:

```text
product_(p in T_long)  p^nu_p
* product_(p in T_short) p^nu_p
    | C_long * E_P * C_tail.                            (1.1)
```

Indeed, for a short prime,

```text
v_p(E_P C_tail)
 >= 1 + (nu_p-1)
 = nu_p.
```

For distinct primes these valuation inequalities combine because their prime powers are pairwise coprime.

This elementary statement settles the “reuse” question sharply:

> Reusing one carrier is valid if it really is one fixed integer. It is invalid only when the notation suppresses p-dependence, row-choice dependence, or a p-dependent division.

There is no requirement to allocate disjoint factors of `C_tail` to different primes. One integer may simultaneously contain every required coprime prime power.

# 3. The composite-endpoint and uniqueness audit in one lemma

The exact finite statement needed by the short branch is elementary and should replace informal prose.

## Lemma 3.1 — fixed endpoint block

Let `P>=0`, let

```text
E_P = product_(h=0)^139 (P+1+h),
```

and let `S` be a set of distinct primes such that every `p in S` satisfies

```text
P+1 <= p <= P+140.
```

Then:

```text
1. h_p=p-P-1 is the unique integer in [0,139] with p=P+1+h_p;
2. product_(p in S) p divides E_P;
3. log E_P <= 140 log(P+140).
```

If in addition every `p in S` is greater than 139, then `v_p(E_P)=1`.

### Proof

The map `h -> P+1+h` is injective. Each `p` occurs as one displayed factor. Distinct primes are coprime, so their product divides `E_P`. The height estimate is immediate. If `p>139`, two multiples of `p` cannot occur in an interval of length 140, and the one factor equal to `p` contributes valuation one.

Composite factors not belonging to `S` play no role.

This lemma is all that the endpoint block contributes. It does not say anything about the remaining `p^(nu_p-1)`.

# 4. The carrier-reuse audit in one lemma

## Lemma 4.1 — simultaneous valuations in one integer

Let `S` be a finite set of distinct primes, let `a_p>=0`, and let `C` be a nonzero integer. Then

```text
[v_p(C)>=a_p for every p in S]
iff
product_(p in S) p^a_p divides C.                    (4.1)
```

This is simply unique factorization.

Thus the phrase “reuse the same carrier” is mathematically sound provided the object is literally the same integer. The proof must answer the following syntactic questions, not appeal to intuition:

```text
Does K0 contain p or h?
Does the definition of Rstar divide by p?
Does its terminal condition depend on p?
Does Proposition 8.1 choose a p-dependent row?
Is a denominator cleared by a p-dependent unit inverse?
```

Any “yes” means there is a family `C_p`, not one `C`, and `(4.1)` cannot be invoked with the displayed notation.

# 5. Why `p>B7` is not a `Pi` exclusion

The two comparisons concern different axes:

```text
B7: cutoff index,
X7,Phi,p: prime/index scale in the q=6 quotient cell.
```

The exact chain for a core prime is

```text
B7 <= X7-1 < X7 <= p <= Phi.                         (5.1)
```

It proves that cutoff denominators up to `B7` are p-units and that a nonempty interval can exist before `p`. It simultaneously proves that `p` **is in** `Pi_6`, not outside it.

Therefore a sentence of the form

```text
p>B7, hence p is not a Pi prime
```

is reversed. To obtain an off-core endpoint set one must compare `p` directly with `X7` and `Phi`, or explicitly remove `supp(Pi_6)`.

Likewise, if `P` is a cutoff endpoint, the equality `p=P+1+h` mixes the two scales only because they are both integers; it does not by itself identify a q=6 core/off-core position. The note must print whether `P=B7`, a physical node, a prime cutoff such as `Phi`, or another endpoint. The height and disjointness checks differ in those cases.

# 8. Smallest repair

The smallest repair is not to enlarge the endpoint product. The endpoint product already does exactly what it should. The missing statement is a universal divided-tail carrier theorem.

## [UETL] Universal endpoint-tail ledger

Fix the row `(m,H)` and define one endpoint `P`, one legal cutoff `K0`, and disjoint residual target sets `T_long,T_short`, all before choosing `p`. There exist nonzero integers

```text
C_long(m,H),
Rstar(m,H;K0),
```

such that:

```text
(U1) for p in T_long:
     v_p(C_long) >= nu_p;

(U2) for p in T_short:
     p=P+1+h_p with unique 0<=h_p<=139;

(U3) for p in T_short:
     v_p(b_sigma7 Rstar(K0)) >= nu_p-1;

(U4) every denominator cleared in C_long and Rstar is coprime to
     product_(p in T_long union T_short) p;

(U5) C_long * Rstar(K0) != 0;

(U6) log |C_long| + log |Rstar(K0)|
     = O(H+log m).
```

Then Lemmas 3.1 and 4.1 give the complete global statement

```text
product_(p in T_long union T_short) p^nu_p
  | C_long
    * [product_(h=0)^139(P+1+h)]
    * b_sigma7
    * Rstar(K0),                                      (8.1)
```

and

```text
log |right side of (8.1)| = O(H+log m).              (8.2)
```

This is a valid proof.

At present, `(U6)`—and, depending on the exact local definition of `Rstar`, the prime-independence/integrality part of `(U3)-(U4)`—is precisely what has not been established. Therefore the smallest textual repair to `Q32_OFFCORE_ENDPOINT_DIVIDED_TAIL_EXCESS.md` is:

> Replace the unconditional final theorem by “conditional on [UETL]”, and identify [UETL] as the remaining divided Green boundary-compression lemma.

If the proof can establish only p-dependent carriers `C_p`, the honest replacement is

```text
product_p p^nu_p | E_P * product_p C_p,
```

with height

```text
O(log m) + sum_p log |C_p|,                          (8.3)
```

which is not `O(H+log m)` without a new bound on the number and size of the carriers.

If the only unavoidable missing factor is the exact slope-7 saturation `E7=G7/Pi_6`, a weaker but potentially sufficient repair is to state

```text
log global carrier = O(H+sqrt(m)+log m)=o(m),        (8.4)
```

and prove that every other divided-tail factor cancels or has `O(H+log m)` height. One may not drop the `sqrt(m)` term merely because `E7` is a p-unit.

# Final conclusion

The proposed dichotomy does not fail because one fixed integer cannot be reused for several primes—it can. Nor do composite endpoint values invalidate the 140-factor product. The failure is that the note has not yet earned the words **fixed integer** and **low height** for the long-prefix and divided-tail witnesses, and `p>B7` does not perform the claimed `Pi` exclusion.

The smallest repair is the universal carrier lemma [UETL]. Without it, the endpoint construction is a correct primewise support argument but not a global `O(H+log m)` height theorem.
