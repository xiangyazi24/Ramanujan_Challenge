# Q854: fixed-q fourth-factorial attack

## Executive conclusion

The condition

```text
sum_{N<n<=2N} (T_q(n))_4 = o_q(N^4/log^4 N)
```

would indeed exclude a single row with `T_q(n) >> N/log N`, because one spike contributes

```text
(T_q(n))_4 >> N^4/log^4 N.
```

However the currently proved pair-energy/codegree estimates do not establish this fourth factorial bound. The unresolved sector is the four-distinct-prime overlap sector. It is a genuine higher-order correlation and is not equivalent to Q32-LGTQ-AGG-C4.

## Expansion by prime-overlap sectors

Write

```text
T_q(n)=sum_p I_p(n),
```

where `I_p(n)` is the indicator of the fixed-q bad prime event. Then

```text
(T_q)_4 = sum_{p1,p2,p3,p4 distinct} I_{p1}I_{p2}I_{p3}I_{p4}.
```

After dyadic summation, split according to the number of distinct primes.

## Repeated-prime sectors

### One prime

The falling factorial eliminates repeated indices, so there is no pure diagonal contribution.

### Two-prime overlap sector

This is controlled by the existing codegree estimate. For a pair of primes, the simultaneous bad condition gives the affine direct/reflected equations and the common codegree bound

```text
# {n: I_p(n)=I_r(n)=1} = O(h)
```

with the corresponding gap parameter. Summing over admissible pairs gives the known pair-energy scale

```text
E_2(q) = O_q(N^(8/3)/log^2 N).
```

This is far below the fourth moment target, but it only controls two-fold correlations.

### Three-prime sector

A triple sector can be bounded by combining pair codegree with the trivial third-prime count. This gives a bound of the shape

```text
E_3(q) <= E_2(q) * N^{1+o(1)}
```

which is still insufficient to close the fourth factorial estimate. No independent triple cancellation is currently available.

## Four-distinct-prime sector

The remaining term is exactly

```text
E_4(q)=sum_{p1,p2,p3,p4 distinct}
  #{n in (N,2N]: I_p1(n)I_p2(n)I_p3(n)I_p4(n)=1}.
```

This is the missing object.

The known facts provide:

* signed affine laws;
* reflected/direct prime laws;
* quotient spacing;
* pair codegrees;
* individual support bounds `Z(p)<<p^(2/3)`.

They do not give a four-way intersection estimate.

## Natural scale from Z(p)<<p^(2/3)

The individual support estimate gives approximately

```text
sum_p Z(p) = O(N^(5/3+o(1)))
```

at the relevant shell scale. Combining only this with Hölder or Cauchy gives bounds no better than the crude ambient fourth-moment estimates. The four distinct prime sector remains too large.

The previously identified crude estimate

```text
O(N^(14/3)/log^4 N)
```

is consistent with this obstruction: it is larger than the required

```text
o(N^4/log^4 N).
```

The gap is a power

```text
N^(14/3)/N^4 = N^(2/3).
```

Thus the missing saving is roughly `N^(2/3)` in the four-distinct sector.

## Comparison with Q32-LGTQ-AGG-C4

They are related but not identical.

Q32-LGTQ-AGG-C4 controls a structured quotient/branch packet after the LGTQ reductions. It counts configurations with prescribed affine geometry.

The fourth factorial moment asks for

```text
four independent bad primes for the same original n.
```

The missing implication would require a lifting theorem:

```text
four-prime ambient overlap
  => bounded number of LGTQ C4 packets
```

No such lifting is currently proved.

Therefore:

```text
LGTQ-AGG-C4 => structured packet bounds
not => global fourth factorial bound
```

and the converse also does not follow.

## Possible interpolation improvements

### Large sieve

A large sieve inequality would need a family of nearly orthogonal characters/quotient coordinates. The existing affine laws do not supply this automatically because all four primes are coupled through the same n.

No proven saving follows.

### Divisor bound for c_q q = c_p p ± g

This controls the number of compatible quotient pairs after fixing a gap. It improves pair counting, but the fourth sector requires simultaneous control of three independent gap constraints. A divisor estimate alone does not provide the missing three-way independence.

### Interpolation from codegree

Pair codegree plus support size gives at best a generic moment interpolation. It does not reach the needed `N^{-2/3}` saving.

## Final status

```text
(T_q)_4 criterion:
    sufficient for eliminating bad rows

Proved inputs:
    individual Z(p) bounds
    affine direct/reflected laws
    pair codegree
    pair energy

Closed sectors:
    diagonal/repeated-prime sectors
    pair-overlap sector

Open sector:
    four distinct bad primes for one n

Required new lemma:
    a genuine four-prime correlation bound
    or a lifting theorem from global overlaps to LGTQ-C4 packets
```
