Equation (4.5a) does not imply either divisibility, and \(E=0\) would give \(48p_-=49p_+\), impossible for large primes.

### 5.4 No new same-characteristic gap

The only upper zero supplied by either row is the already fixed \(t\in\mathcal Z_q\), together with its deterministic reflection \(q-1-t\in\mathcal Z_q\).  Two rows mapping to the same state repeat this same upper-zero datum; they do not supply a third \(q\)-zero or a new independent \(q\)-gap.

At the lower level, distinct assigned rows have \(p_1\ne p_2\):

```text
same sign and p1=p2  -> identical numerical row;
cross sign and p1=p2 -> raw overlap, assigned only to minus.
```

Hence the lower gap-polynomial equations occur in different characteristics.  Pairwise coprimality or a resultant between two gap polynomials over one field cannot be invoked.  Equality of zero-rank labels is also not forced by (3.3++)–(3.9-+).

## 8. A strictly smaller centered factorial successor for the corrected residual

This section is unnecessary for the literal simultaneous-span face, whose contribution is zero.  It supplies the requested narrower successor if the intended frontier was the larger one-span residual.

Let

\[
\Omega_\sigma^{\rm res}(q,t)
\]

be the exact raw-minus-first packed state-prime rows in that residual, and put

\[
k_\sigma(q,t)=|\Omega_\sigma^{\rm res}(q,t)|.
\]

Take

\[
R=R_\eta=\lfloor T^{2/5-\eta}\rfloor,
\qquad
D=D_\eta=\lfloor R/8\rfloor.
\tag{8.1}
\]

Define

\[
P^{\rm far}_{\sigma,D}(q,t)
=
\#\left\{
\{\omega,\omega'\}\subset\Omega_\sigma^{\rm res}(q,t):
|p(\omega)-p(\omega')|>D
\right\}.
\tag{8.2}
\]

and

\[
\Phi^{\rm far}_{R,D}(q,t)
=
\left(
P^{\rm far}_{\sigma,D}(q,t)-\binom{2R}{2}
\right)_+.
\tag{8.3}
\]

### 8.1 Deterministic domination of the first load

Because there is at most one packed assigned row at each lower prime, every row has at most \(2D\) other rows at integer prime-distance at most \(D\).  Hence the number of near unordered pairs is at most \(kD\), and

\[
P^{\rm far}_{\sigma,D}(q,t)
\ge\binom{k}{2}-kD.
\tag{8.4}
\]

For \(D\le R/8\) and \(k\ge4R\),

\[
\binom{k}{2}-kD-\binom{2R}{2}
\ge Rk.
\tag{8.5}
\]

Indeed the left side minus \(Rk\) is increasing for \(k\ge4R\), and at \(k=4R\) it is at least

\[
\frac32R^2-R\ge0
\]

for all sufficiently large \(R\).  Therefore, for every \(k\),

\[
\boxed{
k\le4R+\frac1R\Phi^{\rm far}_{R,D}(q,t).}
\tag{8.6}
\]

Let

\[
B(T)=\#\{(q,t):q\asymp_JT,\ t\in\mathcal Z_q\}.
\]

The banked first zero moment gives

\[
B(T)\ll_JT^{8/5+o(1)}.
\tag{8.7}
\]

Summing (8.6) over two signs and all actual selected upper states yields

\[
\mathfrak M_{2+1}^{\rm res}(T)
\le8RB(T)
+
\frac1R
\sum_{\sigma,q,t\in\mathcal Z_q}
\Phi^{\rm far}_{R,D}(q,t).
\tag{8.8}
\]

Under (V5), the two terms are respectively

\[
T^{2-\eta+o(1)}
\quad\text{and}\quad
T^{2-\eta+o(1)}.
\]

Thus (V5) implies exactly the Q6547 first-moment target.

### 8.2 Strictness relative to Q6547's successor

For every \(k\),

\[
P^{\rm far}_{\sigma,D}(q,t)\le\binom{k}{2},
\]

and \(\binom{2R}{2}>\binom R2\).  Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Phi^{\rm far}_{R,D}(q,t)
\le
\left(\binom{k}{2}-\binom R2\right)_+
=\Psi_R(k).}
\tag{8.9}
\]

The inequality is strict as an interface: when \(R<k\le2R\), the new observable is identically zero, while \(\Psi_R(k)>0\).  It also ignores every cross-sign pair and every same-sign pair with \(|p-p'|\le D\).

Therefore (V5) is a genuinely smaller centered factorial theorem, not `M21-HZLG-SELTAIL2_eta` renamed.  Its sharp exponent remains

\[
\boxed{12/5-2\eta.}
\tag{8.10}
\]

### 8.3 Occurrence warning

The proof of (8.4) uses one packed row per assigned lower prime.  If a consumer retains several literal occurrences with the same \(p,q,t,\sigma\), put

\[

Then the omitted same-\(p\) pair mass

\[
\sum_p\binom{\nu_p}{2}

must be added to the successor or bounded separately.  The affine equations reconstruct the numerical row but do not control that duplicate fibre.  The Q6547 state-prime load is precisely the packed object for which this extra term is absent up to the already-authorized \(T^{o(1)}\) cell weight.
