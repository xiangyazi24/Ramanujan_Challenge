# Q902: Dirichlet-kernel shell twist audit

## 1. Correct expansion and normalizations

The exact shell energy is

\[
\sum_{N<k\le 2N}|D_P(k)|^2
=\sum_{p_1,p_2}\sum_{a_1,a_2}c_{p_1,a_1}\overline{c_{p_2,a_2}}
K_N(a_1/p_1-a_2/p_2),
\]

where

\[
K_N(t)=\sum_{N<k\le2N}e(-kt).
\]

The sum is ordered in \((p_1,a_1),(p_2,a_2)\); there is no missing factor of two. The diagonal is included exactly once and the off-diagonal sector is paired with its complex conjugate when taking the real part.

For a fixed prime p, periodic Parseval gives

\[
\sum_{N<k\le2N}|\sum_a c_{p,a}e(-ka/p)|^2\le (N/p+1)p\sum_a|c_{p,a}|^2
\ll (N+P)\sum_a|c_{p,a}|^2.
\]

Summing over \(p\asymp P\asymp_q N\) gives the same-prime contribution

\[
\ll NQ_P\ll_q N^{5/3}\log N=o_q(N^2).
\]

Thus only the unequal-prime contribution matters.

## 2. Exact unequal-prime reciprocal fraction form

Let

\[
\Delta=\frac{a_1}{p_1}-\frac{a_2}{p_2}
=\frac{a_1p_2-a_2p_1}{p_1p_2}.
\]

For \(p_1\ne p_2\), the numerator cannot vanish unless the two reduced fractions coincide, which is impossible because both denominators are distinct primes and \(1\le a_i<p_i\).

Hence

\[
K_N(\Delta)=e(-(N+1)\Delta)\frac{1-e(-N\Delta)}{1-e(-\Delta)}.
\]

The unequal-prime piece is therefore exactly

\[
2\Re\sum_{p_1<p_2}\sum_{a_1,a_2}
 c_{p_1,a_1}\overline{c_{p_2,a_2}}
 e(-(N+1)\Delta)
\frac{1-e(-N\Delta)}{1-e(-\Delta)}.
\]

This is the correct oscillatory reciprocal-fraction object. Replacing the kernel by \(\min(N,||\Delta||^{-1})\) destroys the phase and returns to the blocked Fejer argument.

## 3. Slope-discrepancy decomposition

Write

\[
\mathcal R(m)=\sum_{a_1p_2-a_2p_1=m}c_{p_1,a_1}\overline{c_{p_2,a_2}}.
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal E=2\Re\sum_{m\ne0}\mathcal R(m)
 e(- (N+1)m/(p_1p_2))
 \frac{1-e(-Nm/(p_1p_2))}{1-e(-m/(p_1p_2))}.
\]

A summation-by-parts attack would need cancellation in partial sums

\[
S(X)=\sum_{|m|\le X}\mathcal R(m),
\]

at the scale where the reciprocal phase changes by one unit.

After removing the already-small region

\[
|m|\gg P^2/N,
\]

where the kernel contributes only logarithmic weight, the missing estimate is an Apéry-specific discrepancy bound:

\[
\max_X|S(X)|=o_q(N).
\]

This is the sharp remaining statement produced by the exact kernel method.

## 4. Comparison with large sieve and SDC

Generic large sieve gives roughly

\[
\sum_{k\sim N}|D_P(k)|^2\ll (N+P^2)Q_P,
\]

and with \(P\asymp N\), this is

\[
\ll N^2Q_P\ll N^{8/3}\log N.
\]

The required result is only

\[
=o(N^2),
\]

which is smaller by a factor of about \(N^{2/3}\) than the generic estimate.

It is also weaker than full SDC bounds of the form

\[
\Re B_{P,4N}\ll N^{2/3+o(1)},
\]

because the shell argument only needs quadratic average cancellation.

The previous Q901 comparison was incorrect: the shell statement is not stronger than the weak Fejer edge. It is a different sufficient route; no converse relation is known.

## 5. Palindromic cancellation check

Because the Apéry zero set is stable under reflection, one expects

\[
F_p(-a)=\overline{F_p(a)}.
\]

Therefore paired coefficients satisfy

\[
c_{p,a}e(-ka/p)+c_{p,-a}e(ka/p)
=2\Re(c_{p,a}e(-ka/p)),
\]

so the contribution is a cosine-type sum.

However this alone does not remove the anchored main term: cosine symmetry only removes the imaginary part. A four-branch cancellation requires additional reciprocity between the two prime denominators. It is not implied by palindromicity alone.

## Final attackable lemma

The new precise target is:

**Apéry reciprocal discrepancy lemma.** For fixed q and \(P\asymp_qN\), with

\[
\mathcal R(m)=\sum_{a_1p_2-a_2p_1=m}c_{p_1,a_1}\overline{c_{p_2,a_2}},
\]

one has

\[
\max_X\left|\sum_{|m|\le X}\mathcal R(m)\right|=o_q(N).
\]

Together with the exact Dirichlet-kernel decomposition this proves the shell estimate.

The obstruction is that generic bounded-conductor families can have \(\mathcal R(m)\) concentrated on the reciprocal slopes where the kernel has maximal phase alignment. Therefore any proof must use genuine Apéry structure in \(F_p\), not only conductor, size, or symmetry.
