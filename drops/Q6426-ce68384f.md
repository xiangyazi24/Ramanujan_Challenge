## Denominator discipline

Every use of `field_simp` is preceded by a theorem whose left side is the precise displayed denominator before factorial rewriting. The proof never assumes any of


The final half-plane theorem obtains its only denominator condition from:


which is already proved in `Problem27Barnes.lean` for `-1/2 ≤ re s`.

## Translation audit

The numerator translation is not a guessed reindexing. It is proved by induction from the already established shift identity

```text
ctNumerator27 (n+1) (t+1) = t^3 * ctNumerator27 n t.
```

This handles the reversal of the factors

```text
t-1, ..., t-n
```

when `t = s+n+1`. The denominator translation is pointwise and order-preserving. The derivative translation uses the global function equality plus Mathlib's unconditional `deriv_comp_add_const`; no differentiability premise is silently introduced.
