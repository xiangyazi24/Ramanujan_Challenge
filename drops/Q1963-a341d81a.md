# Reciprocal hierarchy summation audit

The requested identities are obtained by first writing the operator in divergence form. With
\[
A_t=(t+1/2)^3(t+2),\qquad B_t=(t-1/2)^3(t-2),
\]
the formal symmetrizer satisfies
\[
\mu_tA_t=\mu_{t+1}B_{t+1}.
\]
Multiplying the equation by \(\mu_t C_t^{-k}\) and summing over the periodic index set gives
\[
\sum_t \mu_t C_t^{-k}(HC)_t=-Q\mu_0 C_0^{-k}.
\]
The source term vanishes for the literal weight \(\mu_t=t(t^2-1)\), since \(\mu_0=0\). Therefore the useful identities require the adjacent regularized weights obtained by removing the factor t before summation.

For k=1 the regularized Picone identity is the quadratic reciprocal-difference identity
\[
\sum_t W_t(R_{t+1}-R_t)^2=\text{seam contribution},\qquad R_t=C_t^{-1},
\]
where the seam contribution is supported only at \(t=0,\pm1\). Over \(\mathbb F_p\) this is not a positivity statement and cannot force nonvanishing.

For k=2 and k=3 the corresponding expansions contain respectively cubic and quartic reciprocal factors:
\[
C_t^{-2}(C_{t+1}-C_t)=C_t^{-1}C_{t+1}^{-1}(R_t-R_{t+1}),
\]
and
\[
C_t^{-3}(C_{t+1}-C_t)=C_t^{-2}C_{t+1}^{-2}(R_t-R_{t+1})(C_t+C_{t+1}).
\]
After periodic summation the interior terms telescope; only the seam rows survive. The endpoint relation \(C_1=-3C_0\) fixes one seam value but does not determine the remaining reciprocal trace.

Consequently the cancellation of the target reciprocal trace does not produce a contradiction from the summation identities alone. The missing implication is an arithmetic statement identifying the seam scalar with a nonzero finite-field invariant. The Picone hierarchy supplies necessary identities but no field-independent sign argument.

The scope is therefore: the k=1,2,3 reciprocal summations reduce the problem to explicit seam scalars; they do not prove \(\Sigma_p\neq0\) over \(\mathbb F_p\). A further Apéry-specific congruence is required.
