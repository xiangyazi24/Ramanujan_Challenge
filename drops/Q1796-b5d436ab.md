Let
\[
N(T)=\prod_{t\in\mathbf F_p}(C(t)-T).
\]
Since \(C(t)\neq0\), \(N(0)\neq0\), and
\[
\frac{N'(0)}{N(0)}=-\sum_{t\in\mathbf F_p}\frac1{C(t)}.
\]
The punctured resultant formulation is obtained from
\[
\operatorname{Res}(C-T,t^p-t)=\prod_t(C(t)-T).
\]
Differentiating the resultant gives the trace of the inverse values.  The operator equation
\[
L(B)=(X^p-X)Q_2
\]
with \(B=(t^2-1)C\) only controls the image of \(C\) under the WZ operator.  It does not by itself determine the logarithmic derivative of the punctured resultant.

The first uncontrolled coefficient is the coefficient corresponding to the inverse trace
\[
\sum_t C(t)^{-1},
\]
which is independent of the polynomial identity for \(LC\) unless an additional adjoint relation is supplied. Therefore singleton nonvanishing of \(C\) does not force \(T=0\) to be a simple noncritical value from the stated WZ data alone.

The strongest statement obtainable from the given equations is the conditional identity:
\[
N'(0)=-N(0)\,\operatorname{Tr}(C^{-1}).
\]
Hence the missing datum is exactly the inverse trace. A proof of simplicity requires an additional recurrence or adjoint identity controlling this trace.
