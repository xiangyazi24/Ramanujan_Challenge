# Audit of complementary Newton stencils for the even-q Apéry support

## 1. Setup

For a consecutive block of nodes `h <= j < h+K`, the proposed weights are

\[
W_j=(-1)^{h+K-1-j}\binom{n-h}{j-h}\binom{n-j-1}{h+K-1-j}.
\]

The second binomial must be audited with its actual arguments.  The unit condition is not obtained by the false identity `j+(M-1-j)=M-1`; instead one must study

\[
v_p\binom{n-j-1}{h+K-1-j}
\]

by Kummer carries in the subtraction

\[
(h+K-1-j)+(n-h-K)=n-j-1.
\]

## 2. General p-unit criterion

For `p^2>n`, write `n=qp+r`, `0<=r<p`. Since every relevant integer is below `p^2`, there is at most one base-p digit above the units digit. Kummer gives

\[
p\nmid W_j
\]

iff both binomial coefficients have no borrow in their base-p subtraction.

The first coefficient requires

\[
j-h\le n-h
\]

and imposes no additional p-obstruction inside the node range.

The second coefficient is the controlling term.  Writing

\[
j=ap+s,
\]

its p-unit condition depends on whether the residue subtraction from `n-j-1` crosses the digit `r-1`.

This gives the same type of half-cutoff strata as the original Newton stencil, but with a shifted residue depending on `h`.  The shift is unavoidable.

## 3. Fixed h obstruction

A fixed translation `h` cannot give a pure even-q detector for all p.

Reason: the second binomial has top argument

\[
n-j-1=(q-a)p+(r-s-1),
\]

while the lower argument is

\[
h+K-1-j.
\]

Changing `r` across the boundary `r=0` changes the residue digit of the top without changing the fixed translation. Therefore a fixed `h` always has at least one residue class where an additional unit stratum survives. This produces nuisance support not determined only by `p|b_r`.

Hence no fixed-h consecutive Newton stencil can universally isolate the even-q target.

## 4. h=1 and reflected variants

For `h=1`,

\[
W_j=(-1)^{K-j}\binom{n-1}{j-1}\binom{n-j-1}{K-j}.
\]

The extra `-1` in the top of the second binomial moves the residue boundary.  In even q blocks this does not align with the desired residue condition `s<floor(r/2)`; instead it creates a shifted residue condition depending on `r-1`.  Reflection exchanges the same obstruction rather than removing it.

## 5. Surviving statement

The valid Apéry-specific conclusion is therefore negative:

* the half-cutoff stencil has the exact odd-q support already established;
* a complementary stencil must have a non-fixed adaptive shift or use additional linear combinations of stencils;
* no fixed consecutive Newton block `h<=j<h+K` supplies a universal even-q detector.

A possible repair is to search for a two-stencil combination where the shifted residue strata cancel. Such a construction requires an explicit identity between the two Newton functionals and cannot be inferred from the individual content supports.

## 6. Required proof target

The remaining lemma is not a generic CRT statement. It is an Apéry-specific cancellation lemma:

For a proposed pair of stencils `W^(1),W^(2)`, prove that for every prime `p^2>n` the union of their p-unit strata outside `p|b_r` cancels in the integer linear functional.  Fixed single stencils fail because their Kummer strata are shifted by the node translation.
