# I. Exact packet algebra

## 2. Branch ranges and injectivity

Write

\[
 M=m-1=gE,
 \qquad s_a=ga.
\]

For the direct branch,

\[
 p_D(a)=m-s_a=g(E-a)+1.
\]

The residue of `m` modulo `p_D(a)` is `s_a`.  The folded condition

\[
 2s_a\le p_D(a)-1
\]

is exactly

\[
 3a\le E.
\]

For the reflected branch,

\[
 p_R(a)=\frac{m+1+s_a}{2}
       =\frac{g(E+a)+2}{2}.
\]

It is integral exactly when `g(E+a)` is even, and

\[
 m=p_R(a)+(p_R(a)-1-s_a).
\]

The strict folded condition

\[
 2s_a<p_R(a)-1
\]

is exactly

\[
 3a<E.
\]

The midpoint belongs to the direct branch.

### Proposition 2.1

For `a>=1`,

\[
 \frac{2m+1}{3}\le p_D(a)<m,
\]

whereas

\[
 \frac{m+1}{2}<p_R(a)<\frac{2m+1}{3}.
\]

Both maps are injective and their images are disjoint.

**Proof.**  If `3a<=E`, then

\[
 p_D(a)=g(E-a)+1\ge\frac{2gE}{3}+1=\frac{2m+1}{3}.
\]

If `3a<E`, then

\[
 p_R(a)>\frac{gE+2}{2}=\frac{m+1}{2}
\]

and

\[
 p_R(a)<\frac{g(4E/3)+2}{2}=\frac{2m+1}{3}.
\]

The direct slope is `-g`; the reflected slope is `g/2` on its parity-compatible domain. ∎

Consequently distinct packet labels are distinct rational primes.  A 16-label packet really contains sixteen defining characteristics; there is no hidden direct/reflected double counting.

## 3. Exact selection theorem

### Theorem 3.1

For every candidate prime `p>=7`,

\[
 \boxed{p\mid b_m\iff p\mid b_{ga}.}
\]

**Direct branch.**  Here `m=p+ga`.  Gessel--Lucas gives

\[
 b_m\equiv b_1b_{ga}=5b_{ga}\pmod p.
\]

**Reflected branch.**  Here

\[
 m=p+(p-1-ga).

\[
 b_m\equiv5b_{p-1-ga}\equiv5b_{ga}\pmod p.
\]

The factor 5 is a unit for `p>=7`. ∎

Thus the selected direct primes are exactly the prime divisors of `b_m` in

 \frac{2m+1}{3}\le p<m,
 \qquad p\equiv1\pmod g,
\]

and the reflected primes are exactly the prime divisors of `b_m` in

 \frac{m+1}{2}<p<\frac{2m+1}{3},
 \qquad 2p\equiv m+1\pmod g.
\]

This is an exact factorization statement for the canonical integer `b_m`, not two independent Apéry equations.

## 4. Exact character orders

The branch equations give

\[
 ga=M-(p_D(a)-1),
 \qquad
 ga=2(p_R(a)-1)-M.
\]

Hence

\[
 ga\equiv M\pmod{p_D(a)-1},
 \qquad
 ga\equiv-M\pmod{p_R(a)-1}.
\]

Let

\[
 d_p=\frac{p-1}{\gcd(p-1,M)}.
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 d_D(a)=\frac{E-a}{\gcd(E,a)},
 \qquad
 d_R(a)=\frac{E+a}{\gcd(E+a,2E)}.
 }
\]

For the direct branch this follows from

\[
 p_D(a)-1=g(E-a),
 \qquad
 \gcd(E-a,E)=\gcd(E,a).
\]

For the reflected branch, doubling the gcd gives

\[
 2\gcd(p_R(a)-1,gE)=g\gcd(E+a,2E).

If `E` is an odd prime and `1<=a<E/3`, then

\[
 d_D(a)=E-a>2E/3.

Also `gcd(E+a,E)=1`, so `gcd(E+a,2E)` is 1 or 2 and

 d_R(a)\ge(E+a)/2>E/2.

The only common symmetry is the inverse-character half-orbit.  Conductor, purity, and a norm do not select the needed prime ideal.

# III. Auditing the special Apéry inputs

## 5. Apéry/Gross--Koblitz unit cancellation

Every admissible label satisfies `2ga<=p-1`.  Hence `ga+k<p` for every `0<=k<=ga`, and all terms in

\[
 b_{ga}=
 \sum_{k=0}^{ga}
 \left(\binom{ga}{k}\binom{ga+k}{k}\right)^2
\]

are `p`-adic units.  In the Gross--Koblitz/Morita language, the minimum-slope packet has exactly `ga+1` terms and there is no positive-slope tail in the folded range.  Selection is exactly additive cancellation among these `ga+1` units.

This is special to the Apéry coefficient, but it supplies no valuation restriction: the number of minimum terms grows with `a`, and actual selected rows exhibit the cancellation.

## 6. Recurrence and simultaneous variation in `p`

The Apéry recurrence can be written

\[
 \binom{b_{n+1}}{b_n}
 =
 \begin{pmatrix}
 P(n)/(n+1)^3&-n^3/(n+1)^3\\
 1&0
 \end{pmatrix}
 \binom{b_n}{b_{n-1}},
\]

where

\[
 P(n)=34n^3+51n^2+27n+5
\]

and the determinant is `n^3/(n+1)^3`.

At a packet prime `p>2ga`, all coefficients around the selected lower index are `p`-units.  A zero `p|b_{ga}` is one projective zero state; the adjacent terms are nonzero modulo `p`, since two consecutive zeros would propagate backward to `b_0=0`.

The fixed-`g` decimation `c_a=b_{ga}` is holonomic through the `g`-step transfer matrix.  But Proposition 2.1 assigns a different prime to every label.  A continuant or resultant between two `a`-rows therefore receives no second **forced** zero in the same characteristic.  The affine identities

\[
 p_D(a)-p_D(b)=g(b-a),
\]

and

\[
 2(p_R(a)-p_R(b))=g(a-b)
\]

are mixed-characteristic physical relations, not same-prime returns.  This is exactly the Q8345 wall.

## 7. Franel/level-6 modular parametrization

\[
 \Psi_{p,r}(x)=x^r(1-8x)^r(1+x)^{p-1-r}

A `g`th-root-of-unity projection merely keeps coordinates whose indices are multiples of `g`; it does not convert one vanished coordinate into a modular evaluation root.  Both the Hasse polynomial and the cover-adapted basis also vary with `p`.

## 9. Product bound

Let `S` be the selected labels and

\[
 P_S=\prod_{i\in S}p_i,
 \qquad K=|S|.

All candidate primes are distinct.  Theorem 3.1 gives

\[
 P_S\mid b_m.

\[
 P_S\mid\prod_{a\in A}b_{ga}.

Every candidate exceeds `(m+1)/2`, proving (1).

The bound

\[

 \binom{n+k}{k}\le4^n.

## 10. Height slack

Let

\[
 P_{\rm top}(m)=\prod_{(m+1)/2<p\le m}p.
\]

Every such prime occurs exactly once in the numerator of the central binomial coefficient and in neither denominator, so

\[
 P_{\rm top}(m)
 \mid\binom m{\lfloor m/2\rfloor}<2^m.
\]

The `k=m` term of the Apéry sum is `binom(2m,m)^2`, and the largest binomial coefficient in `(1+1)^{2m}` is at least the average.  Therefore

\[
 b_m\ge\binom{2m}{m}^2
 \ge\frac{16^m}{(2m+1)^2}.
\]

So the common Apéry value has exponentially more capacity than the entire strict top-strip primorial.  This is an exact obstruction to every argument that uses only magnitude or a norm of comparable or larger height.
