# Deterministic CRT discrepancy audit

## Normalization

Let
\[
N_{pql}(I)=\#\{m\in[0,M):m\bmod p\in Z_p,\ m\bmod q\in Z_q,\ m\bmod l\in Z_l\},\qquad M=X^2.
\]
For distinct primes the CRT image has modulus
\(P=pql\asymp X^3\) and cardinality \(Z(p)Z(q)Z(l)\). The main term in one interval is
\[
\frac{M}{pql}Z(p)Z(q)Z(l).
\]
The HM3 Palm quantity is not controlled by a pointwise discrepancy alone: after conditioning on \(p\), the required object is a signed sum of centered coefficients over \(q,l\), weighted by the output normalization \(1/Z(p)\). Therefore an admissible bound must preserve the positive part:
\[
\sum_p Z(p)^{-1}(E_p)_+^2\ll X^4\lambda^5,
\]
not a stronger unweighted \(L^2\) statement.

## Erdős--Turán route

For one triple, Erdős--Turán gives
\[
|N_{pql}(I)-M Z(p)Z(q)Z(l)/(pql)|
\ll \frac{Z(p)Z(q)Z(l)}{H}
+\sum_{1\le |h|\le H}\frac1{|h|}
\left|\widehat{1_I}(h/P)\right|
\prod_{r=p,q,l}|F_r(h r_r)|.
\]
The available Apéry facts give only
\[
\sum_h |F_p(h)|^2=pZ(p)-Z(p)^2\ll pZ(p).
\]
Using this in the triple product gives no power saving after summing over three primes. The resulting loss is of size comparable to the trivial box boundary error, roughly
\[
X^{3/2}\left(\sum_p Z(p)\right)^{3/2},
\]
which is above the HM3 scale unless an additional fourth-moment estimate is supplied.

## Additive-energy route

The relevant quantity is
\[
\mathcal E(Z_p)=\#\{a+b=c+d:a,b,c,d\in Z_p\}
=\frac1p\sum_h|F_p(h)|^4.
\]
Parseval only gives the trivial bound
\[
\mathcal E(Z_p)\le Z(p)^3.
\]
With this bound, a triple CRT discrepancy summation has no saving over a worst-case structured set. A useful inequality would need
\[
\mathcal E(Z_p)\ll p^{o(1)}Z(p)^2
\]
or at least an averaged version
\[
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\mathcal E(Z_p)\ll X^{o(1)}\sum_p Z(p)^2.
\]
Nothing currently follows from the Hasse ODE alone.

## Resultant/algebraic route

The Hasse polynomial structure controls multiplicative/algebraic relations among zeros. It does not directly control additive relations
\[
r_1+r_2=r_3+r_4\pmod p
\]
or the CRT interval phase. A resultant argument would have to show that a polynomial relation in the Apéry recurrence forbids high additive energy. The current degree and symmetry information does not provide this: the reflection involution already supplies many additive correlations.

## Algebraic-correspondence route

A possible successful theorem would be a Deligne-type bound for the correlation sheaf attached to
\[
(r_p,r_q,r_l)\mapsto 1_{Z_p}(r_p)1_{Z_q}(r_q)1_{Z_l}(r_l)
\]
with the interval cutoff. Such a theorem would require specifying a lisse sheaf, conductor, and singular locus. The Hasse ODE by itself only describes the one-prime Picard-Fuchs system; it does not produce a three-prime CRT sheaf with bounded conductor. Therefore square-root cancellation cannot be claimed.

## Exact missing lemma

A sufficient Apéry-specific input would be one of the following:

1. Fourth moment:
\[
\sum_{X<p\le2X}\frac1{Z(p)}\sum_h |F_p(h)|^4
\ll X^{1+o(1)}\lambda^2.
\]

2. Direct Palm discrepancy:
\[
\sum_p Z(p)^{-1}(E_p)_+^2\ll X^4\lambda^5.
\]

3. Additive-energy plus cross-prime decorrelation:
\[
\sum_{p,q,l}|A_{pql}|^2
\left(Z(p)^{-1}+Z(q)^{-1}+Z(l)^{-1}\right)
\ll X^3\lambda^3
\]
with an additional signed cancellation estimate converting this to the positive-part HM3 quantity.

## Verdict

No deterministic HM3-closing discrepancy estimate follows from the presently available facts \(Z(p)\ll p^{2/3+o(1)}\), reciprocity, Hasse ODE, and degree information. Each standard deterministic route loses a named power:

- Erdős--Turán loses through the missing nontrivial Fourier fourth moment.
- Additive energy loses because Parseval permits \(\mathcal E(Z_p)=Z(p)^3\).
- Resultants control algebraic coincidences but not additive CRT boxes.
- Picard-Fuchs geometry does not currently provide the required multi-prime sheaf.

The weakest realistic new lemma is an averaged fourth Fourier moment/additive-energy estimate for the actual Apéry Hasse zero divisors, not a generic random-root assertion.
