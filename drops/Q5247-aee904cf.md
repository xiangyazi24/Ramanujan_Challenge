## 1. The genuine global \(7/3\) bound

For an actual zero \(u\), define

\[
 K_H^+(u)
 =\#\{1\le d\le H:u+d\le p-1,\ u+d\in\mathcal Z_p\},
\]

\[
 K_H^-(u)
 =\#\{1\le d\le H:u-d\ge0,\ u-d\in\mathcal Z_p\}.
\]

The backward version of (E7/3) follows either by reversing the line or by the actual Apéry reflection; no selected coefficient is reflected or duplicated.

Let \(C_{x,p}(a,b)\) denote the number of physical starts \(u\) for which the four labels in (0.2) are actual zeros and every literal AT-P mask for atom \(x\) holds.  Put

\[
 Q_x(T)=
 \sum_{\substack{a,b\ge2\\T\le a+b<2T}}C_{x,p_x}(a,b).
\]

A chain maps to

\[
 (u,a,h),\qquad h=a+b.
\]

The map is injective, and \(a,h\) are distinct forward return distances from \(u\).  Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
 Q_x(T)
 &\le
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal Z_{p_x}}
 K_{2T}^+(u)igl(K_{2T}^+(u)-1\bigr)\\
 &\le
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal Z_{p_x}}(K_{2T}^+(u))^2\\
 &\ll T^{7/3+o(1)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.1}
\]

This uses all actual zeros, so intervening zeros are counted rather than excluded.  The fourth selected zero and every mask have only been dropped in a positive upper bound.

The canonical selected-Palm absolute-value reduction has exactly one outer atom weight \(w_x\), total mass

\[
 M_0=\sum_xw_x,
\]

coefficient and lift cost \(T^{o(1)}\), finitely many orientations, and

\[
 \frac1{a+b}\ll T^{-1}.
\]

Thus

\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\mathcal R_{\mathrm{int}}(T)|
 &\ll T^{-1+o(1)}\sum_xw_xQ_x(T)\\
 &\ll T^{4/3+o(1)}M_0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{1.2}
\]

No prime average, second outer weight, reflected selected coefficient, or division by \(M_0\) occurs.

 (v,v+b).

 T\le a+b<2T

## Conclusion

The Q5228 closure fails at its first combinatorial step: AT-P1--AT-P4 select four actual zeros in increasing order, not four consecutive elements of the complete actual zero set.  The gap-polynomial equations certify returns at the displayed endpoints but do not certify minimal returns.  The fixed-\(a\) row can therefore contain many middle-gap values.

The correct global replacement is the newly proved actual-return square energy.  It gives \(T^{7/3+o(1)}\) before the tent and \(T^{4/3+o(1)}M_0\) after the one-weight selected-Palm consumer.  This improves the old endpoint but leaves exactly \(10/93\) to the canonical gate.

The strongest clean new deletion is the two-scale product wing

\[
 ab\le T^{414/217-\eta},
\]

which is gate-subcritical and permits the hard core to impose \(a,b>T^{197/217-o(1)}\).  The moderate-imbalance strip can simultaneously be enlarged to \(|a-b|\le T^{21/62-o(1)}\), and the corrected row/column estimates isolate low gap-support and low word-support atoms.  None of these proved reductions empties the remaining balanced, high-support, heavy-fibre selected-point parallelogram sector, so no full closure is asserted.
