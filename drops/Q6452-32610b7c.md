
The same central block of companion roots can be visible from every earlier fixed-left root. Preserving this multiplicity forces the mixed tensor in Q6411 and its aggregate version (3.3).

## 7.2 Many rank-two blocks do not create one common component

The invalid diffusion implication is

```text

  =>
those blocks meet in one affine root, one prime ideal,
or one common low-height subresultant.                (false)             (7.2)
```

The blocks are independent summands. If the active second directions are split and pairwise distinct, their local algebra is a product of copies of `F_p`; its dimension is the number of blocks. Different roots increase, rather than reduce, the number of independent directions.

## 7.3 An LCM pays concentration, not diffusion

For a family of integers `Q_tail(v)`, an LCM has local exponent


The physical global repetition needs the sum

```text

```

Thus an LCM is appropriate only after a proven effective-support bound such as (5.1). It cannot pay a pure diffusion prime with one second direction in each of `Theta(Lambda)` distinct blocks.

## 7.4 A direct-sum determinantal divisor subtracts only once

A blockwise Fitting product is therefore forced. No average determinant saving follows merely from writing the blocks in one large matrix.
