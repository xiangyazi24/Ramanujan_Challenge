# Audit of the upper-half mod-p^2 Apéry-Lucas lift

## 1. The lower-half formula cannot be extended by replacing r with the folded residue

The formula

\[
b_{ap+r}=b_a(b_r+2apD_r)\pmod {p^2}
\]

is a lower-half statement.  The obstruction above \(p/2\) is the reflection term coming from the endpoint binomial factors in the Apéry sum.  Writing

\[
r=p-1-s,\qquad 0\leq s<(p-1)/2,
\]

one has to separate the two effects:

1. the block index \(a\) contribution;
2. the reflection defect of the residue block.

The correct shape is

\[
b_{ap+p-1-s}\equiv b_a\left(b_s+pR_s+2apD_s\right)\pmod {p^2},
\]

where the new reflection jet is

\[
R_s\equiv\frac{b_{p-1-s}-b_s}{p}\pmod p.
\]

Thus the missing term is not absorbed into \(D_s\).  Any proof using only \(b_s,D_s\) above the midpoint is incomplete.

## 2. Explicit reflection jet

Starting from

\[
b_n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk^2\binom{n+k}{k},
\]

put \(n=p-1-s\).  Lucas expansion of the three binomial factors gives the mod-p reflection

\[
b_{p-1-s}\equiv b_s\pmod p.
\]

Keeping the first harmonic correction gives

\[
R_s=\sum_{k=0}^{s}c_{s,k}b_{s,k}\pmod p,
\]

with

\[
c_{s,k}=2(H_s-H_k)+(H_{s+k}-H_k),
\]

and

\[
b_{s,k}=\binom sk^2\binom{s+k}{k}.
\]

All denominators in these harmonic numbers are prime to p because \(s+k\le p-2\).

Therefore \(R_s\) is characteristic-zero data evaluated at the folded index.

## 3. Midpoint residue

For

\[
r=(p-1)/2,
\]

there is no distinct folded partner.  The reflection involution fixes the residue.  The expansion remains

\[
b_{ap+r}=b_a(b_r+2apD_r)\pmod {p^2}
\]

only after defining the midpoint jet using the symmetric correction.  It is not obtained by taking a limit of the upper-half reflection formula.

## 4. Checks

Exact computations from the binomial definition for primes

\[
p=7,11,13,17,19
\]

and all admissible \(a,r\) show:

* \(b_{p-1-s}\equiv b_s\pmod p\);
* the quotient \((b_{p-1-s}-b_s)/p\) agrees with the reflection jet \(R_s\);
* omitting \(pR_s\) gives the first failures already for residues above the midpoint.

The smallest obstruction occurs at upper-half residues, not at the midpoint.

## 5. Consequence for shifted-Newton defects

The aligned shifted-Newton argument needs the full residue class range.  For

\[
r> (p-1)/2,
\]

replace every occurrence of \(b_r\) and \(D_r\) by

\[
(b_s,R_s,D_s),\qquad s=p-1-r.
\]

The first defect row therefore contains two independent characteristic-zero quantities:

\[
2aD_s+R_s.
\]

There is no cancellation theorem forcing \(R_s=0\), so the upper-half contribution is genuinely new.

## Conclusion

The proposed upper-half lift is valid only after adding the reflection jet.  The correct mod-p^2 formula is not the lower-half formula with folded indices substituted.  The first exact obstruction is the nonzero reflection defect

\[
\frac{b_{p-1-s}-b_s}{p}\pmod p.
\]

Any shifted-Newton first-defect proof using only \(b_s\) and \(D_s\) misses this term and is false above the midpoint.
