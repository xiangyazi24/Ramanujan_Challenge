## 4. Complete limiting quadratic-form calculation

The preceding apex test rules out the most obvious Lorentz cone. The following calculation rules out every asymptotically nondegenerate rational quadratic covariance for the balanced cocycle.

Let

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}
h_{11}&h_{12}&h_{13}\\
h_{12}&h_{22}&h_{23}\\
h_{13}&h_{23}&h_{33}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Because the eigenvalues of `L` are `16,16Lambda,16Lambda^{-1}`, a nondegenerate bilinear covariance must have similitude multiplier `256`. Solving

\[
LHL^{\mathsf T}=256H
\tag{17}
\]

over `QQ` gives the complete family

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
h_{13}&=-\frac{4338}{479}h_{12},\\
h_{22}&=\frac{4096}{1437}h_{12},\\
h_{23}&=0,\\
h_{33}&=-\frac{2048}{1437}h_{12},
\end{aligned}}
\tag{18}
\]

with `h11,h12` free.

Scale `h12=1437`. Then

\[
H_h=
\begin{pmatrix}
h&1437&-13014\\
1437&4096&0\\
-13014&0&-2048
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{19}
\]

Two exact identities control its geometry:

\[
\boxed{
\det H_h=-2048(336663423+4096h),}
\tag{20}
\]

and for the neutral eigenvector (11),

\[
\boxed{
v_0H_hv_0^{\mathsf T}
=\frac{336663423+4096h}{4096}.}
\tag{21}
\]

Put `Delta=336663423+4096h`.

- If `Delta>0`, then `det H_h<0`. Since the restriction to the last two coordinates is `diag(4096,-2048)`, the inertia is `(2,1)`. Equation (21) says the neutral ray has positive norm. For signature `(2,1)`, the positive-norm locus is the connected one-sheet side and is not a convex Lorentz cone. The convex two-sheet side has negative norm and excludes the neutral ray.

- If `Delta<0`, then `det H_h>0` and the inertia is `(1,2)`. Equation (21) says the neutral ray has negative norm. For signature `(1,2)`, the negative-norm locus is the connected one-sheet side and is not convex. The convex two-sheet side has positive norm and excludes the neutral ray.

Therefore every nondegenerate covariant quadratic form puts the neutral mode on the wrong, nonconvex side.

The exceptional value

\[
h=-\frac{336663423}{4096}
\tag{22}
\]

makes `H_h` rank two. Its kernel is exactly the neutral ray:

\[
\ker H_h=\mathbb Q\left(
1,-\frac{1437}{4096},-\frac{6507}{1024}
\right).
\tag{23}
\]

If

\[
W=P+\frac{1437}{4096}a,
\qquad
Z=Q+\frac{6507}{1024}a,
\]

then the degenerate form is exactly

\[
YH_hY^{\mathsf T}=-2048(Z^2-2W^2).
\tag{24}
\]

Thus the only invariant quadratic whose boundary contains the neutral character degenerates into the two irrational planes

\[
Z=\pm\sqrt2\,W.
\tag{25}
\]

This is not a rational proper Lorentz cone. It is the stable/unstable line pair already forced by the hyperbolic dynamics.

### Consequence for rational moving forms

Suppose a rational moving symmetric form `H_n` has an exact covariance

\[
B_nH_{n+1}B_n^{\mathsf T}=\mu_nH_n

\]

and, after multiplying by a rational scalar and the balancing used above, its leading coefficient matrix is nonzero and nondegenerate. Taking leading terms in (26) gives (17). The preceding classification then applies. Hence:

> No asymptotically nondegenerate rational quadratic/Lorentz covariance can define a convex sheet containing the Catalan neutral mode.

## 5. Why one Lima tail variable cannot repair the quadratic cone

The normalized challenge characters are

\[
\{1,\Lambda,\Lambda^{-1}\},
\qquad \Lambda=17+12\sqrt2.
\tag{27}
\]

The two-step Lima tail has character

\[
r=\frac1{64}.
\tag{28}
\]

Consider an augmented rank-four system with characters

\[
\mathcal E=\{1,\Lambda,\Lambda^{-1},r\}.
\]

For a nondegenerate symmetric form with similitude character `chi`, every character `alpha` must have a partner `beta` satisfying

\[
\alpha\beta=\chi.
\tag{29}
\]

There are only three pairings of four distinct characters.

1. Pair `Lambda` with `Lambda^{-1}`. Then `chi=1`, so the remaining pair requires `r=1`, false.
2. Pair `1` with `Lambda`. Then `chi=Lambda`, and pairing `Lambda^{-1}` with `r` requires `r=Lambda^2`, false.
3. Pair `1` with `Lambda^{-1}`. Then `chi=Lambda^{-1}`, and the other pair requires `r=Lambda^{-2}`, false.

Since `1/64` is rational while `Lambda^{+-2}` is irrational, these failures are exact.

Therefore a single rational Lima-tail variable cannot create a nondegenerate quadratic covariance. This rules out the smallest augmented-cone repair requested in the question.

## 6. The smallest surviving alternative

The exact no-go does not say the bracket is false. It says that a forward-invariant cone with nonempty interior is the wrong geometric object for the neutral connection coefficient.

The limiting projective dynamics has one expanding and one contracting transverse character. A cone with interior necessarily contains an expanding component. The Catalan error is instead the unique solution for which that component cancels exactly. Geometrically, it lies on a codimension-one nonautonomous stable graph.

The minimal surviving object is therefore a scalar graph

\[
v=\Phi_n(u)
\tag{30}
\]

satisfying the exact invariance equation

\[
\Phi_{n+1}(T_{n,1}(u,\Phi_n(u)))
=T_{n,2}(u,\Phi_n(u)),
\tag{31}
\]

where `T_n` is the rational map (3). The graph is tangent to

\[
v-\frac{13}{16}=-\sqrt2\left(u-\frac18\right)
\]

at infinity and has formal center coefficients listed in Section 2.

A useful equivalent formulation uses the unstable eigen-coordinate

\[
\Xi_n=
(v_n-\bar v_n)+\sqrt2\,(u_n-\bar u_n).
\tag{32}
\]

The desired neutral solution is characterized by the vanishing of the exponentially growing connection coefficient of `Xi_n`. A rigorous graph-transform estimate would express `Xi_n` as a convergent future tail and bound it by the stable coordinate. That is one scalar nonrational tail variable, but it is not a finite rational recurrence variable.

This is the sharp next target:

1. prove an explicit graph-transform contraction for (3) in a shrinking neighborhood of the formal branch;
2. construct rational upper and lower truncations to `Phi_n` with a rigorously bounded analytic remainder;
3. verify the finite initial segment exactly;
4. use the graph side/orientation to deduce `a_n>=0` and `c_n>=0`.

Such a proof can still be Lean-friendly once the analytic tail estimate is isolated, but it cannot be reduced to one `ring/nlinarith` induction on a rational quadratic cone.

## 8. Bottom line for `positiveCatalanError_brackets`

The current `sorry` cannot be closed by:

- a fixed bounded rational polyhedral cone;
- a rational moving Lorentz cone with a nondegenerate limiting form;
- the natural second-order rational apex plus a two-dimensional Lorentz sector;
- one augmented Lima-tail variable with a quadratic covariance.

The sharp algebraic reason is that the quadratic self-duality pairs the dominant and recessive characters, while the Catalan error is the neutral character. Every nondegenerate covariant quadric puts that neutral ray on its nonconvex side. The only boundary form is degenerate and is exactly the irrational stable/unstable line pair.

Accordingly, the smallest viable certificate is an orbit-specific stable-graph/tail identity. Producing that scalar analytic tail, rather than another finite cone, is the remaining mathematical content.
