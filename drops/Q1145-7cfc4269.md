# Audit of the Gamma_n divisibility route

## Conclusion

The requested induction cannot currently be completed from the stated hypothesis. The first obstruction is that the proposed base step through

\[
p^a\mid L_0=\binom{n-1}{\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1}
\]

does not force a sufficiently rigid base-p digit pattern. Therefore the hoped-for witness-selection induction does not start.

The implication that fails is:

> large p-adic valuation of L_0 should force a digit restriction that produces a smaller high-part instance.

Kummer gives the valuation of L_0 as the number of carries in

\[
(M-1)+(n-M)=n-1
\]

in base p. A valuation lower bound only asserts the existence of many carries. It does not determine the location of the carries, and it does not imply that the remaining indices j have a controlled Lucas profile.

## Prime-local formulation

Fix a prime p and suppose

\[
v_p(L_jb_j)\ge a\qquad(0\le j<M).
\]

To prove v_p(b_n)≥a one needs to show that every possible failure of divisibility of b_n is detected by at least one j for which L_j is not sufficiently divisible.

The available Lucas statement has the form

\[
b_{pQ+u}\equiv b_Qb_u\pmod p,
\]

with a residue digit u. This only gives information modulo p and does not supply a linear relation among the weighted residues L_jb_j modulo p^a.

## Failure of the L_0 digit route

The valuation

\[
v_p\binom{n-1}{M-1}
\]

is the number of carries in adding M-1 and n-M. Carries can be concentrated in high digits. They do not imply that all low residue classes j have small valuations or that a fixed j can be chosen recursively.

Thus a statement of the form

\[
v_p(L_0)\ge a\Rightarrow n=pN+r\text{ with a smaller controlled }N
\]

is false without extra hypotheses.

## What would be needed for a valid induction

A successful induction would require an exact lemma of the following stronger type:

For every n and every prime p with v_p(Gamma_n)=a>0, there exists an explicitly constructed j<M such that

1. v_p(L_j)<a, or
2. after removing the forced p-power from L_j, the Lucas quotient gives a smaller instance involving b_{N} with N<n.

The present L_j family does not provide this automatically. The binomial factors in L_j can absorb carries in different digit positions, and the Apéry Lucas congruence only controls the residue decomposition of b_j, not the interaction with the coefficient lattice.

## Exact status

No complete proof of Gamma_n|b_n follows from the supplied ingredients alone. No counterexample to Gamma_n|b_n is exhibited here either; the obstruction is specifically the proposed p-adic induction mechanism. The missing theorem is an integral interpolation/divisibility identity or a genuinely uniform base-p witness construction for all exponents a.
