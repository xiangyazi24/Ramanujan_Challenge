# Q1976 — Cross-degree saturation attack

## Conclusion

The proposed cross-degree saturation mechanism does **not** force a numerator witness. The obstruction is an integral lattice-index/projection phenomenon: the span of the primitive triangular Padé pairs is generally a proper sublattice of the full rectangular interpolation kernel, and its saturation in the ambient rational kernel still projects to the same denominator-only subspace on the terminal bad line.

Thus the rank-3 argument from the enlarged box does not contradict the p=19 gap-one weighted countermodel.

---

## 1. Rectangular kernel lattice

Let \(A_{d,H}\) be the integral interpolation matrix for

\[
\deg P\le d+2,\qquad \deg Q\le H-d.
\]

Let

\[
\Lambda=\ker_{\mathbf Z}(A_{d,H}).
\]

The three primitive triangular pairs for splits \(d,d+1,d+2\) give vectors

\[
v_d,v_{d+1},v_{d+2}\in \Lambda.
\]

Their rational span has dimension three:

\[
V=\Lambda\otimes \mathbf Q.
\]

However, the integral lattice generated by the three primitive pairs is

\[
L=\mathbf Z v_d+\mathbf Z v_{d+1}+\mathbf Z v_{d+2},
\]

and there is no reason for

\[
L^{\rm sat}=\Lambda.
\]

The missing condition is unimodularity of the transition matrix between triangular bases and the rectangular Smith basis.

---

## 2. The exact obstruction

Write the Smith normal form of the rectangular matrix as

\[
U A_{d,H} V=\operatorname{diag}(s_1,\dots ,s_r,0,\dots ,0).
\]

Then

\[
\Lambda=V(0^{r}\oplus \mathbf Z^{3}).
\]

The triangular generators correspond to a matrix

\[
T\in M_{3\times3}(\mathbf Z)
\]

inside this free rank-three part.

Hence

\[
L^{\rm sat}=\Lambda
\]

would require

\[
\det T=\pm1.
\]

The Padé construction only gives primitive vectors in their individual triangular boxes. It does not control the determinant of this transition matrix.

The exact possible defect is

\[
[\Lambda:L^{\rm sat}]=|\det T|.
\]

Therefore dimension counting cannot imply saturation.

---

## 3. Reduction on a q0=0 terminal bad line

On the terminal bad line the reductions satisfy

\[
\bar v_i=(0,\ldots,0,*,*,0,\ldots,0),
\]

where the two nonzero entries are the two interior-zero denominator coordinates.

Let \(\pi_N\) be numerator projection. Then

\[
\pi_N(\bar v_d)=\pi_N(\bar v_{d+1})=\pi_N(\bar v_{d+2})=0.
\]

Because reduction is linear,

\[
\pi_N(\sum a_i\bar v_i)=0
\]

for every integral combination.

Passing to saturation does not change this conclusion. Saturation only allows division by lattice indices:

\[
L^{\rm sat}/pL^{\rm sat}\subseteq \Lambda/p\Lambda.
\]

Any new element obtained from a relation

\[
pw\in L
\]

still satisfies

\[
p\pi_N(w)=0
\]

and therefore over \(\mathbf F_p\)

\[
\pi_N(w)=0
\]

only if \(w\) is already in the saturated denominator subspace. The division step cannot create a numerator coordinate unless the full rectangular lattice contains an additional direction outside the saturated triangular span.

That additional direction is precisely what has not been proved to exist.

---

## 4. Stress test against p=19 countermodel

The reported p=19 model has:

* every individual triangular Padé pair denominator-only modulo \(p\);
* common two-coordinate denominator support;
* no numerator witness from the tested degree/height range.

This is exactly the configuration predicted by the lattice obstruction above.

The three vectors reduce to

\[
\bar v_i=a_i e_1+b_i e_2.
\]

Their determinant may vanish in the denominator plane without producing a third direction:

\[
\dim_{\mathbf F_p}\langle \bar v_d,\bar v_{d+1},\bar v_{d+2}\rangle\le2.
\]

A p-divided combination only detects the kernel of the denominator projection map. It does not manufacture a nonzero numerator projection.

---

## 5. What would be required for the attack to work

A successful proof would need the stronger statement:

\[
\Lambda\otimes\mathbf F_p
\]

contains an element \(w\) such that

\[
\pi_N(w)\ne0
\]

and

\[
w\notin L^{\rm sat}.
\]

Equivalently one must prove a nontrivial Smith factor interaction between the rectangular interpolation matrix and the triangular basis transition.

No such relation follows from the three consecutive primitive pairs alone.

---

## 6. Carrier/height consequence

The cross-degree saturation route therefore gives no new P3.2 carrier bound.

The strongest valid statement is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{three consecutive primitive triangular pairs do not force a numerator witness after saturation.}
}
\]

The missing invariant is an independent rectangular-kernel class with nonzero numerator projection.

Without proving that class exists, the p=19 gap-one weighted countermodel remains compatible with all cross-degree saturation operations.
