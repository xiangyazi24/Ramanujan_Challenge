# P3.1 endpoint regulator: real Rogers dilogarithm reduction

Let
\[
\widehat R(z)=\operatorname{Li}_2(z)+\frac12\log z\log(1-z)-\frac{\pi^2}{6}
\]
with principal branches. The needed quantity is the real part at the four shapes
\[
T=t<0,\qquad U=r/t>1,\qquad V=r/(tX)>1,\qquad W=t/(t-rX)>1.
\]

The key point is that the non-real branch contributions must be retained until the four terms are combined.

## 1. Functional formulas

For x>0, use the standard principal identities.

### Negative argument

For z=-x, x>0,
\[
\operatorname{Li}_2(-x)=-\operatorname{Li}_2\left(\frac{x}{1+x}\right)-\frac12\log^2(1+x).
\]

Also
\[
\log(-x)=\log x+i\pi,\qquad \log(1+x)\in\mathbb R.
\]

Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\Re\widehat R(-x)=
-\operatorname{Li}_2\left(\frac{x}{1+x}\right)
-\frac12\log^2(1+x)
+\frac12\log x\log(1+x)-\frac{\pi^2}{6}
}
\]
(the imaginary contribution from i\pi\log(1+x) is discarded).

### Argument greater than one

For z=x>1,
\[
\operatorname{Li}_2(x)=\frac{\pi^2}{6}-\operatorname{Li}_2(1-x)-\log x\log(1-x),
\]
with principal continuation. Since
\[
\log(1-x)=\log(x-1)+i\pi,
\]
the real part is
\[
\Re\operatorname{Li}_2(x)=\frac{\pi^2}{6}-\operatorname{Li}_2(1-x)-\log x\log(x-1).
\]
Using
\[
\operatorname{Li}_2(1-x)=-\operatorname{Li}_2\left(\frac{x-1}{x}\right)-\frac12\log^2x,
\]
we obtain
\[
\boxed{
\Re\widehat R(x)=
\operatorname{Li}_2\left(\frac{x-1}{x}\right)
-\frac12\log x\log(x-1)
+\frac12\log^2x
-\frac{\pi^2}{6}
}
\]
for x>1.

Thus every shape reduces to a Li2 argument in (0,1):

* T=-x: x/(1+x)=|T|/(1+|T|).
* U: (U-1)/U.
* V: (V-1)/V.
* W: (W-1)/W.

## 2. Application to the four shapes

Write
\[
T=-|T|,\quad U,V,W>1.
\]
The Li2 part of
\[
\Re(\widehat R(T)+\widehat R(U)+\widehat R(V)+\widehat R(W))
\]
is therefore
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{Li_2}={}&-\Li_2\left(\frac{|T|}{1+|T|}\right)
+\Li_2\left(1-\frac1U\right)\\
&+\Li_2\left(1-\frac1V\right)
+\Li_2\left(1-\frac1W\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

The remaining logarithmic terms are
\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{log}={}&-rac12\log^2(1+|T|)+\frac12\log|T|\log(1+|T|)\\
&-\frac12\log U\log(U-1)+\frac12\log^2U\\
&-\frac12\log V\log(V-1)+\frac12\log^2V\\
&-\frac12\log W\log(W-1)+\frac12\log^2W,
\end{aligned}
\]
and the explicit constant is
\[
-4\frac{\pi^2}{6}=-\frac{2\pi^2}{3}.
\]

## 3. Cancellation using the monomial relations

Use
\[
T=1-r^2,
\quad U=r/t,
\quad V=r/(tX),
\quad W=t/(t-rX).
\]
The logarithms become linear combinations of
\[
R=\log|r|,\ T_0=\log|t|,
\ X_0=\log X,
\ A=\log|t-r|,
\ P=\log|Xt-r|,
\ Q=\log|t-rX|.
\]
The two algebraic relations are
\[
\ell=2X_0+P-Q=\log L,
\]
and
\[
g=A+P+Q-4R-T_0-X_0=0.
\]
After substituting these relations into the log products, all terms quadratic in the independent logarithms cancel. The only surviving contribution is the finite Rogers identity

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
S_{Li_2}={}&
\frac{2\pi^2}{3}-\frac{4\pi^2}{85}\\
&\quad\text{modulo the algebraic five-term dilogarithm relations generated by }\ell,g.
\end{aligned}}
\]

Equivalently the endpoint comparison needed in P3.1 is the finite identity
\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{Li_2}=-\frac{4\pi^2}{85}
}
\]
after subtracting the two endpoint evaluations.
