## 4. Exact principal-projector expansion

Write Q_pr,P = I - P_P and Q_pr,Q = I - P_Q, with P_P and P_Q the exact principal projectors.

### Why Route 1 does not close

Three possible orthogonalities fail to produce a new power.

**Principal orthogonality.** P_P Q_pr,P = 0 is an identity in character space. In (3.1) the input has already been cut by X and multiplied by S_d. The four terms in (4.1) recombine exactly to Q_pr, but no individual term contains an adjacent P Q factor that vanishes.

**Complete character orthogonality.** Character orthogonality is exact over the full multiplicative group. Here the sum is over the actual Apéry zero set with a joint source multiplier. Orthogonality therefore produces a centered actual-zero correlation, not a Kronecker delta on source labels. Restoring the full group by moving S_d or the source band outside C would violate the source order.

**d-orthogonality.** The d interval has length D=N^(41/100), while the cleared four-prime CRT modulus below has size about N^4. The signed d-sum is far from a complete residue system. The inner annulus is precisely a near-phase range, so there is no exact delta in the determinant variable.

A useful exact warning is the elementary centered-fibre identity

```text
sum_δ |R_δ - L^(-1) sum_γ R_γ|^2
 = sum_δ R_δ^2 - L^(-1) |sum_δ R_δ|^2.                    (4.3)
```

The bank gives upper bounds for the two quantities on the right, not a lower/asymptotic formula for the mean term. Therefore (4.3) does not yield a power-small variance. In particular, no selected-mass denominator may be inserted to manufacture relative flatness.

**Route 1 verdict:** the sixteen-term expansion is exact, but existing orthogonality leaves the different-determinant centered eight-zero covariance at full difficulty.

Thus the literal same-centered-determinant part is an eight-zero notation for a four-zero diagonal.
