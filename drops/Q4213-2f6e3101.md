# 7. Attacks on the missing covariance

I tested the exact residual rather than replacing it by a generic slogan.

## 7.1 Full CRT periodicity

Over the complete box modulo `d=ell_1 ell_2`, CRT gives exact product density `g_{ell_1}g_{ell_2}`. But

\[
d\asymp T^2,
\]

whereas the physical `(q,t)` box has side `O(T)`. It contains no complete two-dimensional period. The boundary error of naive lattice counting is larger than the predicted main term, and the selected set is not a box anyway.

**Failure:** no complete period; exact ambient density does not imply selected density.

## 7.2 Charge every occupied carrier to an actual anchor leaf

Because

\[
1_{K_{x,j}>0}\le\sum_{p\in I_j}h_{\sigma,x}(p),
\]

one can upper-bound the carrier-pair sum by a triple incidence: one actual anchor prime and two carrier divisors. This preserves first-mass normalization.

It does not create mixing. It conditions the final `ell_2` zero on two additional zero conditions in the anchor characteristic. The CRT modulus becomes effectively of size `T^3`, and the selected box remains `O(T)`. The missing final-coordinate covariance is strengthened, not removed.

## 7.3 Full two-row radical or scalar gcd height

For one selected state, all lower carrier primes divide a q-stripped two-row Apéry gcd of logarithmic height `O_J(T)`. The product of every prime in a cell of length `L=o(T)` has logarithm `O(L)=o(T)`. Even the gifted estimate

\[
\log\gcd=o(T)
\]

would permit a completely rich cell. Scalar height gives no local distribution.

## 7.4 Pairwise primitive bilinears and restart transfer

The exact nonzero cross-characteristic bilinear for a pair has both prime divisors, but its primitive evaluated height remains `Theta_J(T)` and it depends on the pair. Summing pairwise height inequalities gives a quantity proportional to the pair count and no saving.

## 7.5 Same-characteristic gap polynomials

The gap-polynomial root count is powerful when two zeros live in one fixed `F_p`. Here one return condition is in `F_{ell_1}` and the other in `F_{ell_2}`. A Sylvester resultant would require a common root in one field. CRT keeps the two components separate and does not repair the quantifier.

## 7.6 Fourier expansion of the selected mark

One may expand the zero indicator in additive characters, but the modulus changes with `ell_2`, while the selected condition `q|b_t` changes with `q`. No banked estimate controls the resulting three-characteristic trace sum. Applying Cauchy--Schwarz returns to ambient `S_2`-type moments and loses the actual first-mass normalization.

## 7.7 Inverse sieve

The exact occupied residue set for the two-row event has cardinality `|Z_ell|^2<<ell^(4/3)`, not `O(ell)`. More importantly, an inverse-sieve conclusion for an unselected state box would still not control the condition `q|b_t` in the moving characteristic `q`.

None of these routes proves `LG-SH1_0`.
