# Q8595 rc4 — FDTG3 cross-characteristic gap audit

## Status

No proof of the requested uniform estimate
\[
\max_{d,s}\sum_{t,N}\prod_i\eta_i(N-d_i,u_i(t))\le X^{o(1)}\lambda_X^3
\]
was obtained. The existing structural identities reduce the problem to a genuinely mixed-characteristic incidence statement. The missing factor is the three independent Apéry zero densities, not the fibre multiplicity.

A strictly smaller theorem which would close the gap is isolated below.

## Exact reduction

For fixed signs and defects,
\[
z_i=s_i u_i-(1-s_i)/2,
\]
with
\[
t=z_1,\qquad N=n_1,\qquad n_i=N-d_i.
\]
The hit condition is exactly
\[
\eta_i(N-d_i,u_i(t))=1
\]
where \(\eta_i\) asserts that \(N-d_i\) has its unique shell prime divisor \(p_i\) and the corresponding folded residue is an Apéry zero.

Thus the full-defect fibre is
\[
G(d,s)=\sum_{t,N}\prod_{i=1}^3\eta_i(N-d_i,u_i(t)).
\]
The desired cubic moment follows from
\[
\sum_{d,s}G(d,s)\ll X^{2+o(1)}\lambda_X^3
\]
with the finite defect/sign multiplicity accounted for by the exact six defect orderings.

## What injectivity gives and what it does not give

The fact that every \(N-d_i\) has at most one shell divisor gives
\[
G(d,s)\le X^{o(1)}
\]
in observed fibres and is compatible with the data through \(X=4096\).

However this only bounds multiplicity. It gives no factor
\[
\lambda_X^3
=\left(\sum_{X<p\le2X}\frac{|Z_p|}{p}\right)^3.
\]
A single exceptional fibre can have weight much larger than its expected random contribution. Therefore the implication
\[
\text{fibre injective}\Rightarrow G^3\text{-moment}
\]
is false.

## Candidate Apéry carrier

The natural possible replacement is a three-character carrier built from the continuants. For two distinct folded roots \(u,v\), if both correspond to zeros modulo the same prime \(p\), the Casoratian identity
\[
a_uB_v-a_vB_u=\pm 6/(u-v)^3
\]
(after clearing denominators) constrains common characteristics. For three different characteristics one would need an integer carrier
\[
C(u_1,u_2,u_3)
\]
whose prime divisors include every non-reflection triple collision and whose size satisfies
\[
\log |C|\ll X^{o(1)}.
\]
Then summing shell prime divisors would give
\[
\sum_{p,q,r}\mathbf 1_{p,q,r\mid C}
\ll X^{o(1)}
\sum_{p,q,r}\frac{|Z_p||Z_q||Z_r|}{pqr},
\]
which is exactly the missing lambda cube.

No such carrier is currently proved.

## Why the known identities do not finish it

Lucas gives
\[
B_{qp+r}\equiv B_qB_r\pmod p.
\]
Reflection gives only the paired roots
\[
r\leftrightarrow p-1-r.
\]
Those remove the trivial two-point orbit. They do not control distinct folded roots from different characteristics.

The Wronskian/Casoratian gives strong restrictions when two zero conditions share a characteristic, but here the three conditions are deliberately cross-characteristic:
\[
p_i\mid B_{q_i},\qquad p_i\mid B_{r_i}.
\]
No common modulus exists to which one can directly apply the determinant identity.

## Smaller theorem that would suffice

The following is strictly smaller than the desired FDTG3 statement:

**Mixed-characteristic triple carrier lemma.** For every dyadic X there exists an integer carrier assignment \(C_{p,q,r}\) for non-reflection triples such that

1. every rich triple incidence forces
\[
pqr\mid C_{p,q,r};
\]

2. the total carrier height satisfies
\[
\sum_{p,q,r}\log |C_{p,q,r}|
\ll X^{2+o(1)};
\]

3. the number of possible carriers for fixed defects is \(X^{o(1)}\).

This would imply
\[
\sum G(d,s)\ll X^{2+o(1)}\lambda_X^3.
\]

The proof is still missing.

## X=1024 packet check

For the supplied packet
\[
p=1069,1381,1847,2011,\qquad m=15468,
\]
the required identities that can be checked are only the structural ones:

- each prime satisfies \(p\le m\);
- writing \(m=q_pp+r_p\) gives the Lucas fold;
- replacing \(r_p\) by \(u_p=\min(r_p,p-1-r_p)\) preserves the zero condition;
- the four-hit row is a collision of different characteristics, not a reflection collision.

It therefore demonstrates why endpoint-pair lifting is insufficient, but it does not contradict a possible triple-carrier theorem.

## Conclusion

The current blocker is not endpoint bookkeeping, reflection, or fibre injectivity. The missing statement is a genuinely cross-characteristic Apéry arithmetic lemma producing the \(\lambda_X^3\) weight.
