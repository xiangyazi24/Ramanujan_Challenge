# Q8584 tactical audit: DSEL3 shifted-gcd translation

## 1. Exact parameterization

Fix signs and defects
\[
d_1=0, d_2=d_{12}, d_3=d_{13},\qquad |d_i|<2X.
\]
Writing the common translation as \(t=z_1\), one has
\[
z_i=t+d_i,
\]
and the signed residues are
\[
u_i(t)=s_i(t+d_i+\tau_i),\qquad \tau_i=(1-s_i)/2.
\]
If \(n=n_1\), then
\[
n_i=n-d_i,
\]
so the physical integer is \(m=n+t\).

For an interior point, the coefficient condition is
\[
0<n_i<X^2+X.
\]
Since any two distinct primes in \((X,2X]\) have product \(>X^2+X\) (strictly for the interior range), each \(n_i\) has at most one shell prime divisor. Therefore the map from a regular signed triple to the lattice datum
\[
(t,n;p_1,p_2,p_3)
\]
with \(p_i\mid n-d_i\) is injective once the shell factors are required to be distinct and the coefficient/sign constraints are imposed.

The endpoint correction is necessary: if \(c_i=0\), then \(n_i=0\) and there is no unique shell factor. Such points must be separated as boundary terms. They are not represented by the interior lattice bijection.

## 2. Larger translation count

The clean enlargement is
\[
G_X(s,d_{12},d_{13})=
\#\{(t,n): |t|<X,\ |n|<X^2+O(X),
\ p_i(n-d_i),
\ b_{\nu_i(t)}\equiv0\pmod {p_i}\},
\]
where the count sums over shell primes \(X<p_i\le2X\) but drops:

* ordering of primes;
* coefficient positivity;
* regularity exclusions;
* quotient nonzero conditions.

This is strictly larger than DSEL3 but still retains the three shifted divisibility constraints. If one proved uniformly
\[
G_X(s,d_{12},d_{13})\le X^{o(1)}\lambda_X^3
\]
for every fixed sign and defect pair, then summing over the \(O(X)\) admissible full-defect patterns for a fixed span and then over spans gives the required DSEL3 reduction.

The normalization is important: this is not a selector count. The \(\lambda_X^3\) comes from three simultaneous shell-zero incidences.

## 3. Available Apéry identities

The recurrence, reflection, and Wronskian identities provide pair information. They control relations between two shifted zero conditions and give bounds for quantities such as
\[
\gcd(A_u,N_h(u)).
\]
They do not currently supply an independent third-coordinate lifting. A claimed argument that derives the third shell prime solely from two-coordinate recurrence data is only DSEL3 rewritten.

To bound \(G_X\), one needs a genuinely new mixed-characteristic statement: after fixing two shifted divisibilities, the third shifted zero must have an additional saving. Equivalently, one needs a correlation estimate for
\[
(p_1\mid n-d_1),\quad(p_2\mid n-d_2),\quad(p_3\mid n-d_3)
\]
with the Apéry zero conditions retained.

No recurrence/reflection/Wronskian identity supplied by the standard Apéry package proves this third-coordinate independence.

## Terminal conclusion

The lattice bijection is valid after removing and separately treating the \(c_i=0\) endpoints. The natural enlarged count is the shifted-gcd translation count \(G_X(s,d_{12},d_{13})\). A uniform \(X^{o(1)}\lambda_X^3\) bound for it would imply DSEL3, but proving that bound is exactly the remaining arithmetic correlation problem; recurrence/reflection/Wronskian facts alone do not close it.
