# Q32 Apéry companion versus the third polylog moment

## 1. The proposed top-shell congruence

For \(p>n/2\), the only multiple of \(p\) in \(1,\dots,n\) is \(p\) itself. Put \(r=n-p\). Since \(L_n/p\) is a \(p\)-adic unit and

\[
\frac{L_n}{k}\equiv 0\pmod p\qquad(k\ne p),
\]

we have

\[
U_3(n)\equiv b_r(L_n/p)^3\pmod p.
\]

The normalization is important: because \(L_n/p\equiv ((p-1)!)\,((n-p)!)^{-1}\pmod p\), Wilson gives

\[
(L_n/p)^3\equiv (-1)^3(r!)^{-3}\pmod p.
\]

Hence

\[
6U_3(n)\equiv -6b_r(r!)^{-3}\pmod p.
\]

The Frobenius relation for the companion solution gives

\[
p^3c_{p+r}\equiv b_r\pmod p,
\]

but it does not directly identify \(c_n\) with \(-b_r/(r!)^3\). Therefore the claimed congruence

\[
6U_3(n)\equiv 6L_n^3c_n\pmod p
\]

is **not a formal consequence** of the quoted Frobenius congruence. The missing factor is the local normalization converting the Frobenius companion basis to the factorial-scaled polylog moment basis. Without an additional identity

\[
6L_n^3c_n\equiv -6b_{n-p}(n-p)!^{-3}\pmod p,
\]

the statement is unproved and should be treated as false in the current carrier architecture.

## 2. Exact characteristic-zero expression for the defect

By definition,

\[
E_n=6\sum_{k=1}^n b_{n-k}(L_n/k)^3-6L_n^3c_n.
\]

Factor the common scale:

\[
E_n=6L_n^3\left(\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{b_{n-k}}{k^3}-c_n\right).
\]

Define

\[
R_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{b_{n-k}}{k^3}.
\]

Then

\[
E_n=6L_n^3(R_n-c_n).
\]

The recurrence for \(R_n\) is obtained by applying the Apéry recurrence to the convolution with the third polylog coefficients \(1/k^3\). Thus \(R_n\) satisfies the inhomogeneous Apéry recurrence with forcing equal to the third finite difference of \(1/k^3\). The companion \(c_n\) satisfies the homogeneous recurrence, so \(R_n-c_n\) is the canonical particular-minus-companion solution.

## 3. Universal top-shell divisibility

For a top-shell prime \(p>n/2\), the local contribution of the single surviving atom is

\[
R_n\equiv b_{n-p}p^{-3}\pmod p
\]

in the unscaled basis. Multiplication by \(L_n^3\) removes this denominator. The universal factor is therefore only the divisibility already forced by

\[
v_p(L_n^3)=3.
\]

The defect \(E_n\) does not acquire an extra factor merely from subtracting the companion. A target prime \(p\mid b_{n-p}\) gives an additional valuation in the single surviving atom, but this is a conditional valuation, not a universal divisibility of \(E_n\).

## 4. Height obstruction

The possible carrier gain is limited by the size of \(L_n^3\) and the Apéry weights. The integer \(E_n\) contains the factor

\[
6L_n^3R_n
=6\sum_{k=1}^n b_{n-k}(L_n/k)^3.
\]

Using

\[
\log L_n=\psi(n)=n+o(n)
\]

and the Apéry growth

\[
\log b_n=(4\log(1+\sqrt2)+o(1))n,
\]

one obtains

\[
\log |E_n|\ge (3+o(1))n
\]

before cancellations, and the Cauchy convolution contributes the additional Apéry exponential scale. The subtraction of \(c_n\) can remove the Frobenius-leading component but gives no mechanism forcing cancellation of the archimedean height.

Therefore dividing by the universal top-shell factor cannot by itself make

\[
\frac{\log |E_n/\text{(universal factor)}|}{\text{excess target valuation}}
=o(n).
\]

The obstruction is the remaining archimedean term \(6L_n^3R_n\), specifically the \(k=1\) contribution

\[
6b_{n-1}L_n^3,
\]

whose logarithmic size is already

\[
(3+4\log(1+\sqrt2)+o(1))n.
\]

No valuation gain from \(p\mid b_{n-p}\) controls this term globally.

## 5. Combination with \(U_1,U_2\) and Casoratian

The Casoratian identity

\[
b_{n-1}c_n-c_{n-1}b_n=1/n^3
\]

provides a local independence certificate between the two solutions. It does not reduce the height of \(E_n\); it only prevents the companion from being an integral linear combination of the Apéry solution.

Likewise \(U_1,U_2\) give lower polylog moments, but their denominators introduce only fixed powers of \(L_n\) and do not cancel the exponential Apéry coefficient growth.

Hence the proposed triple carrier

\[
(E_n,U_1(n),U_2(n))
\]

is not a sublinear-height target-prime carrier under the present normalization.

The precise obstruction is the surviving Apéry-weight term \(b_{n-1}L_n^3\), not merely determinant or universal-factor size.

## Conclusion

The congruence \(6U_3(n)\equiv C_n\pmod p\) requires an additional factorial-normalized Frobenius identity and is not implied by the stated companion congruence. Even granting such a local identity, the defect integer has archimedean height dominated by \(L_n^3\) times the Apéry growth. Universal top-shell divisibility does not lower this height. The current companion/polylog carrier therefore does not achieve an \(o(n)\) logarithmic-height ratio for target primes.
