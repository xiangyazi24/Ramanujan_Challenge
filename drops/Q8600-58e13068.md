# Q8600 — corrected Apéry gcd interface audit

## 1. Filtered de-anchoring identity

Let
\[
A_m=\{p:X<p\le 2X,\ p\mid B_m\},\qquad
H=\{p\in A_m:p\mid m\}.
\]
For a shell prime with Lucas decomposition
\[
m=qp+r,
\]
Lucas gives
\[
B_m\equiv B_qB_r\pmod p.
\]
Hence a target lower-root hit with p dividing m is in the union
\[
H=U+E-B,
\]
where
\[
U=\{p:p\mid B_m,\ p\nmid B_{m+1}\},
\]
\[
E=\{p:p\mid B_q,\ p\mid B_r\},
\]
and B removes the reflected carry case r=p-1-q.

The target filter is exactly
\[
H^\dagger=H\cap\{p\le m\}.
\]
Define
\[
U^\dagger=U\cap\{p\le m\},
\]
\[
E^\dagger=E\cap\{p\le m\}.
\]
Since every member of H already satisfies p<=m, the filtered identity is
\[
\boxed{H^\dagger=U^\dagger+E-B^\dagger}
\]
with
\[
B^\dagger=B\cap\{p\le m\}.
\]
The correction is only that the carry term must be filtered as well. Writing simply H^dagger=U^dagger+E-B is valid only if B is defined as the already-filtered carry set.

## 2. Physical decomposition

Let
\[
C_{\rm only}=A\setminus H.
\]
Then the disjoint physical partition is
\[
\boxed{A=H^\dagger\sqcup C_{\rm only}}
\]
so, at the counting level,
\[
T=|A|=|U^\dagger|+|E|-|B|+|C_{\rm only}|.
\]
The required point is that C_only is not a moving-root object.

## 3. Why C_only needs no cubic moment

The upper-digit contribution is
\[
A_{\rm upper}=\{p:p\mid B_q\}.
\]
The independent banked theorem gives
\[
\sum_{m<n}\sum_{p\mid B_{\lfloor m/p\rfloor}} w(p,m)
=O(n^{2/3}(\log n)^{1/3})=o(n).
\]
Therefore C_only is controlled by the already established upper-digit estimate. It is not part of the moving-root cubic majorant.

The cubic/factorial moment is required only for
\[
H^\dagger
\]
because only this component contains the simultaneous lower-root condition after quotient stripping.

## 4. Regular folded coefficients

Write a regular hit as
\[
m=cp+z.
\]
For the positive sign,
\[
z=u,
\]
with
\[
1\le c\le X-1.
\]
For the negative sign,
\[
z=-u-1,
\]
and
\[
m+1=cp-u,
\]
with
\[
2\le c\le X-1.
\]
The lower bound c=2 in the negative branch is forced by p<=m and m+1=cp-u:
for c=1 one obtains m=p-u-1<p.

Thus for every regular hit,
\[
 c>0.
\]

## 5. Product-defect nonvanishing

For two hits,
\[
N_i=c_ip_i.
\]
If
\[
c_1p_1=c_2p_2
\]
with distinct primes p_1,p_2, then p_1 divides c_2.
But
\[
1\le c_2\le X-1<p_1,
\]
which is impossible. Therefore
\[
\boxed{c_ip_i-c_jp_j\ne0}
\]
for distinct shell primes.

This is the exact defect exclusion needed in the selector construction.

## 6. Selector count

The count comes from sign sectors and span ranges, not coefficient counting.

A signed endpoint has two sectors. For one coordinate the possible spans are:

- positive sector: c in 1,...,X-1;
- negative sector: c in 2,...,X-1.

For ordered defect differences, the two-sector span count gives
\[
14X-8.
\]

The derivation is sectorwise:

\[
(\text{equal signs})+(\text{mixed signs})
=14X-8.
\]

No extra factor from c-labels is legitimate; c is not the selector variable.

After ordering the three primes, the permutation factor is
\[
3!=6.
\]
The resulting multiplier is therefore
\[
\boxed{6(14X-8)=84X-48}.
\]

## 7. Quantifier gap

The remaining gap is not the identity H^dagger=U^dagger+E-B. The identity is exact after filtering.

The unresolved step is the passage from the target-filtered regular set R^dagger to the E,D,G decomposition:

\[
R^\dagger\subseteq E\cup D\cup G
\]
with all multiplicities and exclusions preserved.

The missing quantifier is:

for every m and every shell prime p satisfying the target condition p<=m, the lower-root representation must be assigned uniquely to exactly one of the classes used in the cubic estimate.

A pointwise upper-digit estimate does not supply this. It only removes C_only. The moving-root theorem must still prove the required cubic bound for H^dagger.

## Conclusion

The corrected interface is:

\[
\boxed{A=H^\dagger\sqcup C_{\rm only}},
\]
\[
\boxed{H^\dagger=U^\dagger+E-B^\dagger}.
\]

The cubic moment obligation applies only to H^dagger. The selector arithmetic is consistent: regular coefficients have the stated ranges, product defects cannot vanish, and the selector multiplier is exactly
\[
\boxed{84X-48}.
\]
The only remaining gap is the quantified reduction from target-filtered lower-root hits to the E/D/G moving-root classes.
