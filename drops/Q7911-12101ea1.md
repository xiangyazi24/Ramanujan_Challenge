# Q7911 adjudication: the branch character does not survive in the primitive invariant stalk

## Verdict

Q7904 is correct under its stated invariant-cycle hypotheses. The Legendre symbol computed from the local Morse model belongs to the **vanishing quotient**
\[
H^2(X_{\bar\eta})/H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I,
\]
not to the invariant quotient that defines the primitive middle-extension stalk. Therefore it is not an additive correction to
\[
\theta_p(\alpha)-t_p(\alpha).
\]
For the explicit toric K3 family, assuming the Q7904 hypotheses (properness, split rational double point, Frobenius-equivariant invariant cycle isomorphism), the branch defect is
\[
\boxed{e_p(\alpha)=0.}
\]

The earlier insertion of a quadratic character into the point-count trace confused the Frobenius action on the vanishing line with the Frobenius action on the invariant rank-two piece.

## The relevant spaces

Let \(X\to S\) be the proper one-parameter family, with generic geometric fiber \(X_{\bar\eta}\), special singular fiber \(Y=X_\alpha\), and minimal resolution \(\widetilde Y\).

For a split A1 singularity:

\[
\dim H^2(X_{\bar\eta})=22.
\]

The local invariant cycle theorem gives the exact sequence
\[
0\to H^2(Y_{\bar{\mathbb F}_p})
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{sp}}
H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I
\to 0
\]
provided the hypotheses of proper local invariant cycles apply. Hence
\[
H^2(Y)\cong H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I,
\]
Frobenius-equivariantly.

The resolution adds the exceptional curve class:
\[
H^2(\widetilde Y)
\cong H^2(Y)\oplus \mathbf Q_\ell(-1).
\]
Therefore
\[
\dim H^2(Y)=21,
\qquad
\dim H^2(\widetilde Y)=22.
\]

The vanishing cycle line is instead
\[
V=H^2(X_{\bar\eta})/H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I,
\]
so
\[
\dim V=1.
\]
It measures the failure of the generic fiber to be invariant; it is not part of the special-fiber invariant stalk.

## Where the quadratic character lives

The local Morse model at the conifold point gives a quadratic singularity
\[
xy+z^2=t
\]
after étale coordinates and a choice of smoothing parameter \(t\).

The vanishing cycle is a rank-one object. Frobenius on this line can acquire the quadratic descent character coming from the discriminant of the quadratic form together with the smoothing parameter. In the explicit family this character is determined by the local Hessian determinant and the derivative of the smoothing coordinate.

This gives a possible formula of the shape
\[
\chi_{\mathrm{van}}(\mathrm{Frob}_p)
=
\left(\frac{\det(q)\,\partial t/\partial\alpha}{p}\right),
\]
up to the usual Tate normalization and the choice of arithmetic versus geometric Frobenius.

This is a statement about \(V\), not about \(H^2(Y)\).

With arithmetic Frobenius, the exceptional divisor class contributes eigenvalue \(p\) on \(\mathbf Q_\ell(-1)\). With geometric Frobenius the eigenvalue is \(p^{-1}\); the trace comparison must use one convention consistently.

## Exact trace comparison

Let

\[
L\subset H^2(\widetilde Y)
\]
be the rank-19 global divisor lattice. Let \(E\) be the exceptional A1 curve class. Then

\[
H^2(\widetilde Y)\cong L\oplus \mathbf Q_\ell(-1)\oplus W,
\]
where
\[
\dim W=2.
\]

The primitive trace used in the toric formula is
\[
t_p(\alpha)=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Frob}_p|W).
\]

Because the exceptional curve is split over \(\mathbb F_p\),
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Frob}_p|E)=p.
\]

Hence
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(H^2(\widetilde Y))
=19p+p+t_p(\alpha).
\]

The singular fiber satisfies
\[
H^2(Y)\cong H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I,
\]
and removing the same rank-19 lattice gives exactly
\[
(H^2(Y)/L)=W.
\]
Thus the primitive middle-extension stalk trace is also
\[
\theta_p(\alpha)=t_p(\alpha).
\]

Therefore
\[
\boxed{e_p(\alpha)=\theta_p(\alpha)-t_p(\alpha)=0.}
\]

## Why no counterexample arises from the Morse character

A nontrivial vanishing character would mean the invariant-cycle map failed to identify the primitive rank-two spaces. The only possible failure points would be:

1. the specialization map
\[
H^2(Y)\to H^2(X_{\bar\eta})^I
\]
not being Frobenius equivariant;

2. the exceptional class not being defined over \(\mathbb F_p\);

3. the rank-19 lattice not specializing compatibly.

Under Q7904 these are excluded:

* proper local invariant cycles gives Frobenius equivariance;
* the explicit toric resolution proves the exceptional conic is split;
* the global toric divisor classes are defined over the base field and specialize.

The quadratic character cannot enter through any of these arrows.

\[
\boxed{
\text{The local Morse Legendre symbol exists, but it is a character of the vanishing quotient; for the toric branch defect }e_p(\alpha)=0.
