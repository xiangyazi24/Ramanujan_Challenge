There is a completely explicit **second MP46 source** that cancels the common full-window Hermite carrier in characteristic zero.

\[
v^{(s)}=(v^{(s)}_K)_{K\in I_s},

\sum y^{(s)}_K=0,

\[
Z^{(s)}_k
:=R^{(s)}_{k+1}-R^{(s)}_k,
\qquad
R^{(s)}_k=(X_s+k)\mathbf e_k+(k+1)\mathbf e_{k+1}.  \tag{3}
\]

Thus explicitly

\[
\boxed{
Z^{(s)}_k
=-(X_s+k)\mathbf e_k

v^{(s)}\!\cdot Z^{(s)}_k=0,

\sum Z^{(s)}_k=2,                                    \tag{5}

\sum a^{(s,k)}=2,

v^{(s)}\!\cdot a^{(s,k)}=2.

# 1. Exact MP46 Hermite decomposition

Fix one slope and suppress the slope label.

The primitive selector satisfies

\[
(K+1)v_{K+1}=-(X+K)v_K.                              \tag{1.1}
\]

MP46 first constructs a full-window Hermite vector \(y\) with

\[
\sum_K y_K=0,
\qquad
v\cdot y=2,                                           \tag{1.2}
\]

and polynomial coefficient height.  The sum is then repaired by a three-coordinate selector-kernel vector.

Define

\[
R_k=(X+k)\mathbf e_k+(k+1)\mathbf e_{k+1}.           \tag{1.3}
\]

By (1.1),

\[
v\cdot R_k=0.                                        \tag{1.4}
\]

Set

\[
Z_k:=R_{k+1}-R_k.                                     \tag{1.5}
\]

Expanding gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
Z_k
&=(X+k+1)\mathbf e_{k+1}+(k+2)\mathbf e_{k+2}\\
&\quad-(X+k)\mathbf e_k-(k+1)\mathbf e_{k+1}\\
&=-(X+k)\mathbf e_k+X\mathbf e_{k+1}
 +(k+2)\mathbf e_{k+2}.
\end{aligned}                                        \tag{1.6}
\]

Hence

\[
v\cdot Z_k=0,                                        \tag{1.7}
\]

while

\[
\sum Z_k
=-(X+k)+X+(k+2)=2.                                  \tag{1.8}
\]

Therefore

\[
a^{(k)}:=y+Z_k                                       \tag{1.9}
\]

satisfies

\[
\sum a^{(k)}=2,
\qquad
v\cdot a^{(k)}=2.                                    \tag{1.10}

This is the exact affine-coset structure of MP46 that matters for the carrier-value problem.

Any two repair-anchor choices satisfy

\[
\boxed{
a^{(k)}-a^{(k')}=Z_k-Z_{k'}.}

In particular their difference has

\[
\sum(a^{(k)}-a^{(k')})=0,
\qquad

Thus changing the repair anchor moves only inside the **zero-sum/zero-link kernel**.

# 7. Why the primitive-link coordinate does not rescue the determinant

One might hope that fixing both MP46 moments

\[
\left(\sum a,\;v\cdot a\right)=(2,2)                \tag{7.1}
\]

provides a second source coefficient that can be used in a \(2\times2\) determinant.

It does not.

All MP46 witnesses lie in one affine coset of the two-moment kernel:

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{MP46}}
=a_0+\ker\left(
 x\mapsto(\sum x,v\cdot x)
\right).                                              \tag{7.2}
\]

Therefore the difference of any two MP46 sources satisfies simultaneously

\[
\sum\delta a=0,
\qquad
v\cdot\delta a=0.                                    \tag{7.3}
\]

The Hermite long component associated with those two affine moments is removed, but the difference has no access to a boundary coordinate that transforms outside that kernel.

In particular, the explicit repair-anchor difference (1.11) lies in exactly this kernel.

So the obstruction is not caused by the particular balanced Euclidean witness \(y\).  Replacing \(y\) by a different full-window Hermite solution only changes the source by another zero-sum/zero-link vector, and the determinant argument above is unchanged.

# 9. Height bookkeeping and what is actually proved

MP46 proves small **source coefficients**, not a small source **value**.

For the full source

\[
H_{K,L}=Y+S_{7,L}-S_{4,K},                            \tag{9.1}
\]

the problematic scalar is

\[
Y=y^{(7)}\cdot T_7-y^{(4)}\cdot T_4.                \tag{9.2}
\]

Even though \(y^{(s)}\) has polynomial coefficients, its support is the full legal window.  Nothing in the Hermite coefficient bound alone implies

\[
\log|Y|=O(H+\log m)                                   \tag{9.3}
\]

or even \(o(m)\).

The determinant (3.4) eliminates \(Y\) exactly.  Its source coefficient height is \(O(\log m)\), and with adjacent anchors it has bounded support.

What I do **not** claim from the connector-visible state is an unconditional \(O(H+\log m)\) bound for the resulting numerical value (3.5) for every legal anchor pair.  Such a value bound requires the chosen bounded-support \(T\)-coordinates to lie on a genuinely \(O(H)\) physical boundary window, or an equivalent Green boundary estimate.  The local MP46 source theorem by itself does not certify that placement for all legal windows.

This does not weaken the obstruction: even if one grants an \(O(H+\log m)\) bound for every bounded correction in (6.3), the determinant \(-18\) still forces the \(C_{47}\) coefficient to vanish.

Thus the failure occurs **before** the desired numerical-height estimate is needed.

\[
\Delta_{47}
=\sum_n(c_rb_n-b_rc_n)h_n                             \tag{10.2}
\]

is a \(p\)-unit.

The MP46 second-source determinant is different.  Its two rows have the **same** affine source moments and hence the same boundary character.  Taking their determinant removes the common character entirely.

That is why Q3408 can in principle retain a transverse mode but the MP46 source-difference determinant cannot: Q3408 requires two linearly independent boundary characters; MP46 gives repeated copies of the same character \((2,-2)\).

The fixed determinant

\[
18=\det\bigl((2,-2),(10,-1)\bigr)                    \tag{10.3}
\]

is the exact algebraic expression of that mismatch.

# 11. Smallest escape from this obstruction

The obstruction theorem covers only combinations built from:

```text
1. MP46 equal-sum/link-two source values;
2. differences of such sources;
3. bounded Newton/vertical/Green corrections with zero slope sums;
4. rational external coefficients with core-unit denominators.
```

To escape, one must add a boundary row whose tail-gauge response is not in the line

\[
\mathbf Q(2,-2).                                      \tag{11.1}
\]

The desired \(2C_{47}\) row has response

\[
(10,-1).                                               \tag{11.2}
\]

The singular target-predecessor recurrence row from the current frontier is of the needed qualitative type because it carries the pure first-Witt intercept rather than an equal-sum tail shift.  Likewise, a genuine characteristic-zero Green source direction \(h\) with nonzero Casoratian determinant, as in Q3408, would escape the MP46 affine coset.

But a second MP46 Hermite source does not.

# 12. Final classification

The requested direct carrier-value attack has a definitive answer for the natural bounded determinant class.

### Proved positive algebra

For the exact MP46 Hermite construction,

\[
a^{(s,k)}=y^{(s)}+Z^{(s)}_k,
\]

and hence

\[
H_{K,L}=Y+S_{7,L}-S_{4,K}.
\]

Changing repair anchors gives the exact long-carrier-free combination

\[
\boxed{
H_{K,L}-H_{K',L'}
=(S_{7,L}-S_{7,L'})-(S_{4,K}-S_{4,K'}).
}
\]

There is no denominator, and the source coefficient height is \(O(\log m)\).

### Proved obstruction

Every MP46 source has tail-gauge character

\[
(2,-2),
\]

while \(2C_{47}\) has character

\[
(10,-1).
\]

The determinant is

\[
\boxed{18.}
\]

It is a unit at every large q=6 core target.  Therefore any linear combination that cancels the common MP46 long carrier necessarily loses the \(C_{47}\) boundary direction as well.

### Not proved

This does **not** rule out a determinant using one MP46 source and one genuinely different singular/Green boundary row.  Such a row must have a gauge character linearly independent of \((2,-2)\) and must itself admit the requested \(O(H+\log m)\) characteristic-zero height bound.

That is the precise remaining opening after MP46; it is narrower than “find a WZ identity,” and the second MP46 source itself is now ruled out exactly.
