## 1. Static invariants versus a dynamical quotient

There are two different questions that must not be conflated.

### 1.1 Static factorization

Given a tuple of matrices and one scalar polynomial function of their entries, the scalar itself defines a map to `A^1`. In that vacuous sense every observable has a one-dimensional quotient.

For example,

```text
(E,M) -> tr(EM)
```

is invariant under simultaneous conjugation

```text
(E,M) -> (gEg^(-1),gMg^(-1)).
```

### 1.2 Dynamical factorization

Definition 0 asks for much more. One needs a fixed state and fixed bounded-degree updates whose iterates produce the observable for every clock value and every gap:

```text
q -> R^r S^h q,
zeta(R^r S^h q)=0  <=>  omega_h(r)=0.
```

The quotient must therefore be closed under both clock operations. Static finite generation of an invariant ring does not imply that a chosen small list of invariants is closed under the update.

This is exactly where the Weyl obstruction returns. In the level-one clock representation,

```text
U^p=I,
minpoly(U)=X^p-1=(X-1)^p.
```

Thus Cayley-Hamilton gives a recurrence of order p, not bounded order. The translated projectors

```text
E_r = U^r E_0 U^(-r),
0<=r<p,
```

are the p independent diagonal matrix units. A generic framed contraction against these projectors has no recurrence shorter than p.

The observable-only loophole is precisely the possibility that the special Apery matrices B and C force an exceptional low-dimensional contraction module. Invariant theory does not prove that exception; it merely provides the language in which it would have to occur.

## 2. Positive controls: when matrix coefficients really do compress

The standard controls are useful because they identify the additional structure that is missing here.

### 2.1 Class functions of one bounded-rank matrix

For `M in GL_d` with d fixed, conjugacy-invariant polynomial functions factor through the characteristic polynomial. Equivalently one may use

```text
tr(M), tr(M^2), ..., tr(M^d)
```

or the d characteristic coefficients.

Individual observables such as `tr(M)` or `det(M)` factor through `A^1` for every d. But the full conjugacy quotient of a p by p matrix has p spectral coordinates; it is not dimension-free as p grows.

### 2.2 A bounded number of bounded-rank generators

For a fixed number m of matrices of fixed rank d, Procesi-Razmyslov invariant theory generates the simultaneous-conjugation invariants by traces of words in characteristic zero; Donkin's characteristic-polynomial formulation handles positive characteristic.

For `SL_2` and two generators A,B, the Fricke-Vogt coordinates

```text
tr A, tr B, tr(AB)
```

determine the generic unframed conjugacy class. Traces of arbitrary words in A and B are polynomials in these three coordinates.

This is a genuine bounded quotient because both quantities are fixed:

```text
matrix rank = 2,
number of free generators = 2.
```

For a free group of rank m, the trace-coordinate set grows with m. Horowitz's classical generating set has size exponential in m in its direct form, and even refined generating systems still have dimension growing linearly with m because

```text
dim Hom(F_m,SL_2)//SL_2 = 3m-3
```

generically.

### 2.3 Framed representations

A matrix element becomes an invariant after adding vectors, covectors, or a rank-one projector to the representation data. For example,

```text
lambda(Wv) = tr((v lambda) W).
```

Under simultaneous change of basis this is invariant.

For a fixed-rank representation of a fixed finitely generated group with a fixed number of framing vectors, the framed invariant ring is finitely generated. Hence every word matrix coefficient is polynomial in a fixed finite set of framed invariants.

This is the closest positive control to the present problem. It works only if the cocycle is actually a word in a fixed bounded set of constant generators. Our letters are

```text
T(r),T(r+1),...,T(r+h-1),
```

and are not words in two constant matrices with parameter-independent exponents.

### 2.4 Common invariant line or triangularization

Suppose every transfer matrix preserves a fixed line L. Then the matrix coefficient along L is a scalar cocycle

```text
f(r+h-1)...f(r).
```

If this scalar cocycle is a shift coboundary

```text
f(r)=c g(r+1)/g(r),
```

then the product telescopes to

```text
c^h g(r+h)/g(r).
```

This is a true endpoint quotient. More generally, simultaneous triangularization gives a bounded extension of scalar endpoint products.

Q6629's rational-coboundary obstruction kills exactly this mechanism at determinant level:

```text
det T(r)=r^6
```

cannot be made constant by a bounded rational gauge.

### 2.5 Constant-coefficient or bounded-period gauge

If

```text
T(r)=G(r+1) A G(r)^(-1)
```

with A constant and G algebraic of bounded complexity, then

```text
T_h(r)=G(r+h) A^h G(r)^(-1),
```

and Cayley-Hamilton for A gives a fixed recurrence in h.

Likewise a coefficient sequence of fixed period L reduces to powers of one L-step monodromy matrix.

The determinant coboundary obstruction excludes a rational constant gauge, and Section 7 below proves that the coefficient family has no nonzero additive period smaller than p.

### 2.6 Canonical spectral framing

If the rank-one projector E is itself an algebraic function of the conjugacy class of M, for example a canonically selected simple-eigenvalue projector on a fixed cover, then

```text
tr(EM)
```

reduces to the selected eigenvalue and hence to spectral invariants.

The projectors `E_r` in the Weyl clock are external clock frames. They are not recoverable from the conjugacy class of `Omega_h` or of the p-curvature.

## 3. Correction to the Cayley-Hamilton/Fricke suggestion

The proposed statement

```text
any GL_2 word's entries are polynomial in traces of subwords and determinants
```

is false without framing.

Let M be a generic two by two matrix and let g vary. Then M and `gMg^(-1)` have identical trace, determinant, and traces of all powers, while their `(1,1)` entries vary. Thus no entry is a function on the unframed character variety.

An entry can be written as a trace pairing:

```text
M_ij = tr(E_ji M).
```

But the matrix unit `E_ji` is a frame. If it is included in the tuple and conjugated with M, then `tr(E_ji M)` is a legitimate framed invariant. It has not been eliminated; it has simply been renamed as an invariant coordinate.

For the collision transfer, one can similarly write, up to the fixed transfer convention and the known nonzero denominator,

```text
Delta_{r,h} = tr(J T_h(r)) / D_{r,h}
```

for a fixed rank-one probe J. This is useful notation but not character-variety compression.

Cayley-Hamilton reduces powers of one fixed two by two matrix:

```text
M^(n+2) = tr(M) M^(n+1) - det(M) M^n.
```

It does not reduce a product of new matrices

```text
T(r+n)...T(r)
```

unless those letters themselves lie in a fixed finite dynamical algebra with fixed structure constants.

## 4. Why the one-parameter algebraic family is not enough

The map

```text
u -> T(u) = [[P(u),-u^6],[1,0]]
```

is an algebraic curve in `Mat_2`. Moreover, every T(u) lies in the three-dimensional affine span

```text
E_21 + P(u) E_11 - u^6 E_12.
```

Thus there is no large matrix-algebra mystery in the rank-two transfer itself. All noncommutative products reduce to four matrix coordinates. The growing complexity sits in the scalar coefficient functions of r.

For fixed h, define

```text
Phi_h : A^1 -> Mat_2^h,
Phi_h(r)=(T(r),T(r+1),...,T(r+h-1)).
```

The image has dimension at most one, as does its image in any simultaneous-conjugation quotient. But its degree grows with h, and the framed holonomy coordinate is

```text
N_h(r),
deg N_h=3h-3.
```

A one-dimensional curve can have arbitrarily large degree. Point-counting conductor and exponential-sum complexity see that degree, not only the dimension of the parameter space.

### 4.1 Scalar death certificate

There cannot be a general invariant-theoretic theorem saying that holonomy of a bounded-degree algebraic family is generated in bounded degree by interval endpoints.

Already in rank one, take

```text
A(u)=u.
```

Then

```text
A(r+h-1)...A(r) = r(r+1)...(r+h-1).
```

Its divisor has h distinct moving zeros. No rational function of bounded degree in r and r+h can equal these products for all h. Endpoint factorization requires the transcendental Gamma function:

```text
r(r+1)...(r+h-1) = Gamma(r+h)/Gamma(r).
```

The determinant of the Apery transfer is exactly the sixth power of this scalar example:

```text
det T_h(r)
  = product_{j=0}^{h-1}(r+j)^6.
```

Thus even the unframed conjugacy invariant `det T_h(r)` already carries linearly many translated zeros. The rational-coboundary obstruction is the algebraic formulation of this death certificate.

So the proposed principle

```text
bounded-degree algebraic family + consecutive holonomy
  => bounded trace ring depending only on endpoints
```

is false in rank one, before matrix elements enter.

## 5. Birkhoff and difference-Galois theory: what they do and do not compress

Consider the additive difference system

```text
Y(z+1)=T(z)Y(z).
```

For any fundamental matrix Y in a Picard-Vessiot or analytic solution ring,

```text
T_h(r)=Y(r+h)Y(r)^(-1).
```

This is the exact endpoint description suggested in the question.

### 5.1 The fixed object is transcendental

Taking determinants gives

```text
d(z+1)=z^6 d(z).
```

Hence, analytically,

```text
d(z)=Gamma(z)^6 q(z),
q(z+1)=q(z).
```

The Gamma factor is unavoidable. It is the determinant-level witness that a fundamental solution does not live on a fixed algebraic variety of bounded degree.

Birkhoff's analytic theory constructs canonical solutions in sectors or half-planes and a periodic connection matrix comparing them. Under the relevant regularity hypotheses, formal data plus this connection matrix classify the analytic difference module. But the connection matrix and canonical solutions are meromorphic/transcendental objects with infinitely many translated singularities. They store the entire growing product rather than removing it.

### 5.2 Difference Galois group versus finite products

The difference module has rank two, so its Picard-Vessiot Galois group is a subgroup of `GL_2`. This is a bounded-rank algebraic object. It controls algebraic relations among solutions and detects special solvability or reducibility.

It does not imply that the values

```text
Y(r+h)Y(r)^(-1)
```

or one framed matrix coefficient of them have bounded algebraic degree in r and h. A large difference Galois group is instead evidence against a hidden algebraic endpoint formula.

A useful positive outcome from difference Galois theory would be one of the following:

```text
- a rank-one submodule;
- a solvable triangular flag;
- an algebraic gauge to constant coefficients;
- a finite difference-Galois orbit;
- an explicit bilinear first integral closing the framed observable.
```

The banked coboundary obstruction removes the simplest gauge/flag route. No Birkhoff connection theorem supplies the remaining framed identity automatically.

## 6. Exact computation of the additive p-curvature

The prompt's block decomposition is correct, but the specialization to rational clock points is far more degenerate than a generic eigenvalue-order analysis suggests.

Let

```text
K=F_p(r),
sigma(r)=r+1,
A(r)=T(r),
C_p(r)=A(r+p-1)...A(r).
```

Since `sigma^p=id` on K, `C_p` is the p-step curvature endomorphism of the reduced difference module.

### 6.1 Exact high-digit block law

Because

```text
A(r+p)=A(r)
```

in characteristic p, one has

```text
T_{mp}(r)=C_p(r)^m
```

and, for `0<=v<p`,

```text
T_{mp+v}(r)
  = T_v(r+mp) T_{mp}(r)
  = T_v(r) C_p(r)^m.                         (6.1)
```

Thus the high digit m is indeed controlled by powers of one two by two matrix.

### 6.2 Covariance and the fixed field

Over K, every A(r) is invertible. A cyclic shift of the p factors gives

```text
C_p(r+1)=A(r) C_p(r) A(r)^(-1).              (6.2)
```

Therefore

```text
tau_p(r)=tr C_p(r),
delta_p(r)=det C_p(r)
```

are sigma-invariant. The fixed field and polynomial invariant ring are

```text
K^sigma=F_p(r^p-r),
F_p[r]^sigma=F_p[r^p-r].
```

Put

```text
s=r^p-r.
```

The determinant is immediate:

```text
delta_p(r)
 = product_{j=0}^{p-1}(r+j)^6
 = (r^p-r)^6
 = s^6.                                      (6.3)
```

### 6.3 The trace is cubic in the Artin-Schreier coordinate

Use the exact transfer formula

```text
T_h(r)=
[[U_h(r),       -r^6 U_{h-1}(r+1)],
 [U_{h-1}(r),   -r^6 U_{h-2}(r+1)]],          (6.4)
```

with `U_-1=0`, `U_0=1`, and the banked recurrence. Since

```text
deg U_j=3j,
```

(6.4) gives

```text
deg_r tau_p <= 3p.
```

Since `tau_p in F_p[s]` and `deg s=p`, there are coefficients in `F_p`

```text
a_0,a_p,b_p,c_p
```

such that

```text
tau_p(r)=a_0+a_p s+b_p s^2+c_p s^3.          (6.5)
```

The constant term vanishes. To see this, define

```text
q_n=(n!)^3 b_n.
```

Then

```text
q_0=1,
q_1=5,
q_{n+1}=P(n)q_n-n^6q_{n-1}.
```

The first column of `C_p(0)` is `(q_p,q_{p-1})^T`, while the second column is zero because the second column of `T(0)` is zero. Modulo p,

```text
q_p=0.
```

Also the Apery binomial formula gives

```text
b_{p-1}=1 mod p:
```

for every `1<=j<=p-1`, the factor

```text
binom(p-1+j,j)
```

is divisible by p, so only the j=0 term survives. Wilson's theorem then gives

```text
q_{p-1}=((p-1)!)^3 b_{p-1}=-1 mod p.
```

Hence

```text
C_p(0)= [[0,0],[-1,0]]                       (6.6)
```

in the present transfer convention, and in particular `tau_p(0)=0`. Therefore

```text
tau_p(r)=s(a_p+b_p s+c_p s^2).               (6.7)
```

This is a genuine bounded-complexity character-variety compression of the symbolic p-curvature: its characteristic polynomial over the fixed field is

```text
X^2-s(a_p+b_p s+c_p s^2)X+s^6.               (6.8)
```

The three coefficients may depend on p, but the degree in the invariant coordinate s is uniformly bounded.

### 6.4 Specialization at every rational clock point

Let `a in F_p`. Among the p factors

```text
T(a),T(a+1),...,T(a+p-1)
```

there is exactly one factor `T(0)`, which has rank one, and all other factors are invertible. Thus

```text
rank C_p(a)=1.                                (6.9)
```

Since `s(a)=0`, equations (6.3) and (6.7) give

```text
tr C_p(a)=0,
det C_p(a)=0.                                 (6.10)
```

Cayley-Hamilton now yields

```text
C_p(a)^2=0.                                   (6.11)
```

So every rational specialization is a nonzero rank-one nilpotent matrix. All such matrices are conjugate in `GL_2(F_p)`. In the unframed character quotient they are one point.

Substituting (6.11) into (6.1) gives the exact rational-fiber block law

```text
T_{mp+v}(a)=
  T_v(a)                  if m=0,
  T_v(a) C_p(a)           if m=1,
  0                        if m>=2.            (6.12)
```

This is stronger than a bounded recurrence in m: the polynomial transfer dies after two complete p-blocks.

### 6.5 Generic versus rational-fiber dynamics

Over the generic field `F_p(r)`, Cayley-Hamilton gives

```text
C_p^(m+2)
  = tau_p(s) C_p^(m+1)-s^6 C_p^m.             (6.13)
```

Thus a generic framed matrix coefficient of `C_p^m` satisfies an order-two recurrence in the high digit m. After adjoining the two eigenvalues, its zeros can be studied through an eigenvalue ratio and an apparition/order problem.

At `r=a in F_p`, both characteristic coefficients vanish. The two eigenvalues are zero and the order problem disappears. The only surviving information is the ordered pair

```text
(image line of C_p(a), kernel line of C_p(a)).
```

Those lines move with a and are invisible to trace and determinant. They are exactly framed data.

This is the invariant-theoretic core of the negative verdict:

```text
The p-curvature character compresses perfectly, but the character is
constant on the rational clock where the collision problem lives.
```

## 7. Why this compression is above the wall horizon

The nonwrapping projective orbit is indexed by

```text
I_p={1,...,p-2}.
```

Therefore an actual collision gap satisfies

```text
1<=h<=p-3.
```

Writing `h=mp+v` forces

```text
m=0,
v=h.
```

Thus the p-curvature factor does not appear in any wall-range transfer product. It becomes available only after a full trip around the characteristic-p clock, which necessarily crosses the singular factor with determinant zero.

The polynomial matrix product remains defined after that crossing, but the normalized recurrence and the projective orbit used in the collision theorem do not. Equation (6.12) is therefore a statement about the singular polynomial continuation, not a new family of regular collision gaps.

### 7.1 No smaller additive period

There is no exact sub-p period to move the mechanism below the horizon. Suppose

```text
T(u+s)=T(u)
```

as functions on `F_p` for some `0<s<p`. Comparing determinants gives

```text
(u+s)^6=u^6
```

for all u. Taking `u=0` gives `s^6=0`, hence `s=0`, a contradiction.

The same argument applies to any nonzero additive step: translation by s has orbit length p in `F_p`. Blocking along an arithmetic progression merely replaces the standard p-curvature by another full p-cycle product; it does not create a shorter period.

The reflection `u -> -u-1` is a useful involution, but it is not an iteratable period and does not turn consecutive products into powers of one fixed block.

## 8. What the p-curvature says about the Weyl observable

The unframed curvature invariants are maximally small on rational points:

```text
(tr,det)=(0,0).
```

Yet a matrix element against a clock projector can distinguish conjugate representatives. If

```text
C_p(a)=x_a y_a^T,
y_a^T x_a=0,
```

then

```text
tr(E_r C_p(a))=y_a^T E_r x_a.
```

This depends on the positions of the image and kernel lines relative to the clock frame.

Adding E_r to the tuple makes this scalar a framed invariant. For a fixed pair `(E,C)` with C nilpotent rank one, the invariant quotient is indeed tiny and includes `tr(EC)` as a coordinate. But the clock update

```text
E_r -> E_{r+1}=U E_r U^(-1)
```

is not closed on that one scalar. To update it one must know further contractions with U, and repeated closure produces the p-step Krylov/Weyl ladder unless the special B,C data satisfy an exceptional relation.

Thus p-curvature provides an especially transparent model of the general obstruction:

```text
static framed quotient: small;
dynamic closure under the clock: p-wide.
```

## 9. Relation to apparition and order structures

For a generic nonsingular two by two curvature matrix C, the scalar sequence

```text
ell(C^m v)
```

is a second-order linear recurrence. If C is semisimple, zeros are controlled by the multiplicative order of the eigenvalue ratio, up to the two framing coefficients. If C is nonsemisimple with nonzero eigenvalue, one obtains a polynomial-times-exponential sequence.

That standard apparition picture applies to (6.13) over the generic Artin-Schreier base or over extensions where `s!=0`.

It does not apply to the rational clock specialization because

```text
C^2=0.
```

There is no nonzero eigenvalue ratio. The only apparition is the singular collapse after one p-block.

A potentially different object could arise by dividing the integral p-block product by its predictable p-adic valuation or by studying its first nonzero layer modulo `p^k`. That would be a divided p-curvature in the depth tower, not the mod-p curvature computed here. This is a genuine blind spot recorded in Section 12.

## 10. Does the collision observable have any remaining positive mechanism?

After the controls, only four mechanisms remain logically possible.

### 10.1 Apery-specific framed trace identity

There could be a fixed set of framed contractions closed under both the p-dilation and low-digit updates, with `omega_h(r)` among them. This would be a genuine `[OBSERVABLE-ONLY-WEYL-FACTOR]`.

No general invariant theorem predicts it. It would have to use the special recurrence, reflection, Casoratian, or another undiscovered bilinear concomitant.

### 10.2 Exceptional low linear complexity of the collision words

The translate/Cartier span of the scalar words

```text
omega_h(r)
```

could have bounded rank even though the ambient Weyl representation has rank p. This is the computational diagnostic proposed in Q6649.

The banked facts `deg N_h=3h-3`, adjacent coprimality, growing irreducibility evidence, and the need for new wall input all point in the opposite direction, but they do not constitute a theorem excluding a nonlinear factor.

### 10.3 Divided p-curvature or p-adic framing

The nilpotent mod-p p-curvature may have a structured first lift modulo `p^2` or after division by the singular factorial factor. The verified depth tower is precisely the place where such information could live.

This would compress the high p-adic digit, but it still must explain how information propagates before the first full p-block if it is to affect `h<p`.

### 10.4 Transcendental but arithmetically tame connection data

A Birkhoff connection matrix or special-function fundamental solution might admit strong finite-field trace-function estimates despite not being algebraic of bounded degree. This would bypass Definition 0 rather than satisfy it.

No current theorem turns the additive difference connection data of this irregular, singular family into the required mesoscopic collision estimate.

## 11. Final verdict on plausibility

The answer depends on what is meant by plausible.

### Plausible as a logical exception

Yes. The full Weyl algebra being `M_p` does not forbid one very special scalar zero set from factoring through a nonlinear fixed quotient. Static invariant theory cannot rule that out.

### Plausible from a known mechanism

No, not at present.

- Fricke coordinates require a bounded number of constant generators; the consecutive transfer family has an unbounded clock.
- Adding the projector makes the matrix element an invariant but does not close its clock update.
- The one-dimensional parameter family has degree growing linearly with h.
- Birkhoff endpoint factorization uses Gamma-type transcendental objects.
- The exact p-curvature factor begins at h=p and becomes nilpotent on `F_p` points.
- There is no shorter additive period.

Therefore the invariant-theory program has not produced leverage independent of the wall. A fixed observable factor, if discovered, would itself be the missing within-digit theorem.

The sharp conclusion is:

```text
[OBSERVABLE-ONLY-WEYL-FACTOR] is not refuted, but it should be classified as
"Apery-specific integrability or nothing," not as a consequence of trace
identities, character varieties, Birkhoff connection theory, or ordinary
p-curvature.
```

The strongest positive theorem obtained in this round is the exact curvature statement:

```text
[APERY-P-CURVATURE]

Over F_p(r), the p-block transfer has

  det C_p=(r^p-r)^6,
  tr C_p=(r^p-r)(a_p+b_p(r^p-r)+c_p(r^p-r)^2),

and for every a in F_p its specialization is nonzero rank-one nilpotent.
Consequently T_{mp+v}(a)=0 for m>=2.
```

This theorem explains why the high-digit compression exists and why it cannot touch the wall.

## 12. Required confidence audit

### (a) Least-confident step

The least-confident substantive step is the negative literature-level conclusion that no specialized additive-difference or framed-character theorem converts the Birkhoff/curvature object into a bounded algebraic zero detector for `h<p`.

The algebraic claims in Section 6 are elementary and exact. The uncertainty is whether a specialized theory of framed difference modules, arithmetic D-groupoids, or recent global-curvature methods contains a non-obvious observable-level compression not captured by the standard character and Picard-Vessiot formulations.

A smaller technical confidence issue is that the three coefficients `a_p,b_p,c_p` in (6.7) have not been computed explicitly. Their existence and the cubic degree bound are proved, and their values do not affect the nilpotent rational-fiber conclusion.

### (b) Blind spot

The main blind spot is a divided or lifted p-curvature in the verified `p^k` depth tower. Mod p, the full-period matrix is nilpotent and loses all spectral information. Modulo `p^k`, after extracting the predictable singular valuation, a nonnilpotent leading operator may survive and may carry framed image/kernel data with bounded Cartier recursion.

The second blind spot is a genuinely Apery-specific bilinear invariant. The Casoratian is one such invariant at adjacent times. There could conceivably be a second concomitant whose finite translation closure contains

```text
b_r c_{r+h}-c_r b_{r+h}
```

without containing the full clock algebra. Current irreducibility and degree-growth evidence argues against this, but does not logically exclude it.

The third blind spot is the newest computational literature on global curvature of linear difference operators. It may offer an efficient way to package curvatures across primes, but no available result currently supplies a wall-range point-count or bounded framed quotient.
