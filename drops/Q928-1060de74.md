# Audit result

**Verdict: the proposed Q923-style collapse is not proved; the first obstruction is that the requested nonzero scalar cannot be produced from the supplied data without an additional transfer identity.**

The important correction is valid: in a canonical same-gap block with

\[
m=a_i+p_i,
\]

one has

\[
q_i=\lfloor m/p_i\rfloor=1
\]

under the stated leading-regular hypothesis, hence the companion coefficient is common:

\[
C_{(p_i,1)}=\frac{a_1}{b_1}=\frac65 \pmod {p_i^3}.
\]

The marked congruence is therefore

\[
5p_i^3a_m-p_i^3a_{a_i}-6b_m\equiv0\pmod {p_i^4},
\]

or

\[
E_m(p_i):=5p_i^3a_m-p_i^3a_{m-p_i}-6b_m\equiv0\pmod {p_i^4}.
\]

This specialization removes the false independent \(C_i\) variables from Q922.

## Exact transfer issue

Let the Apéry companion state be

\[
V_j=(b_j,a_j)^T.
\]

The Apéry recurrence gives a second-order recurrence for each coordinate. Therefore a one-step transfer exists:

\[
V_{j+1}=T_jV_j,
\]

where \(T_j\) is a rational 2 by 2 matrix. After multiplying by the leading recurrence coefficient, one obtains a cleared integral matrix

\[
\widetilde T_j=D_jT_j\in M_2(\mathbb Z[j]).
\]

For a gap \(d\), the exact d-step relation is

\[
V_{a+d}=T^{(d)}_aV_a,
\qquad
T^{(d)}_a=T_{a+d-1}\cdots T_a,
\]

and the cleared form is

\[
\widetilde V_{a+d}=\left(\prod_{r=0}^{d-1}D_{a+r}\right)T^{(d)}_aV_a.
\]

This is the only canonical transfer available. It does not introduce free residues for \(a_{a_i}\).

## Rank count

The marked equations are evaluations of

\[
E_m(x)=x^3(5a_m-a_{m-x})-6b_m.
\]

The common unknown output data are

\[
(a_m,b_m),
\]

while the endpoint state supplying \(a_{m-x}\) has dimension two. Hence the natural augmented state has dimension four.

A block of three primes gives three congruences, but not an overdetermined system. The first possible rank excess occurs at **five marked points** (five equations against four state coordinates), not at three. However, dimension counting alone is insufficient: one needs an explicit 4 by 4 or 5 by 5 minor of the actual transfer matrices.

## Why no scalar is currently obtained

The required scalar would have to be a determinant such as

\[
\Delta_{m,d}=\det(M_{i_1},M_{i_2},M_{i_3},M_{i_4},M_{i_5})
\]

where each row is the coefficient vector of the corresponding transferred equation in the fixed canonical state basis.

The supplied congruence gives only the rows

\[
(-p_i^3,\,-6,\,\text{endpoint-transfer coefficients})
\]

but does not provide the explicit Apéry transfer matrices or their symbolic products. Consequently no factorization

\[
\Delta=(p-d)p(p+d)S_{m,d}
\]

can be claimed.

In particular, the following would be invalid:

- using Casoratian nonvanishing as a determinant evaluation;
- replacing canonical endpoint values by free CRT residues;
- asserting a remaining scalar without computing the transfer minor.

The Casoratian

\[
a_nb_{n-1}-a_{n-1}b_n=6/n^3
\]

only proves transversality of the two fundamental solutions after denominators are cleared. It is not a carrier scalar.

## Height consequence

If an actual scalar \(S_{m,d}\) were produced with \(d=O(\log m)\), its height would need a separate estimate. The transfer denominators contribute at most polynomially many factors in the length-d block, giving a potential bound of order

\[
\log |S_{m,d}|=O(d\log m)=O((\log m)^2),
\]

which would be small enough for a fixed-fibre carrier argument.

But that estimate applies only after the scalar exists. At present the missing determinant calculation is exactly the unresolved step.

## Scoped conclusion

The corrected q=1 specialization eliminates the false local quotient freedom and changes the attack substantially. It does **not** yet prove a canonical collapse theorem and does **not** produce a fixed-fibre carrier.

The strongest justified statement is:

> A canonical same-gap fibre cannot be analyzed by independent p-adic quotients. The first candidate rank-excess block is length five, but no nonzero residual scalar has been derived from the canonical Apéry transfer. Therefore the existence of a second linearly bad future row from one fixed bad anchor remains open under the supplied identities.
