# P32 companion expansion versus reciprocal moments

## Conclusion

The congruence

\[
p^3c_{p+a}\equiv b_a\pmod p
\]

contains only the residue class of the companion solution.  Its higher \(p\)-adic coefficients do not, in general, reduce to the three reciprocal moments

\[
R_s(a)=\sum_{u=1}^a b_{a-u}u^{-s}.
\]

The proposed valuation contradiction cannot be obtained from the known Frobenius congruence alone.

## Recurrence variation

Write the Apéry recurrence as

\[
(n+1)^3y_{n+1}=A(n)y_n-n^3y_{n-1},
\]

where

\[
A(n)=(2n+1)(17n^2+17n+5).
\]

For two solutions \(b,c\), the Casoratian is

\[
b_{m-1}c_m-c_{m-1}b_m=\frac1{m^3}.
\]

Variation of constants expresses a shifted companion value as a sum of forcing terms weighted by the Green kernel.  The kernel involves products of recurrence coefficients, not only the local denominators \(u^{-s}\).  Therefore the expansion of \(c_{p+a}\) has additional boundary terms.

## Expansion obstruction

Let

\[
C_p(a)=p^3c_{p+a}.
\]

The known result gives

\[
C_p(a)=b_a+pE_1(a)+p^2E_2(a)+p^3E_3(a)+\cdots.
\]

The coefficients \(E_i(a)\) depend on:

* harmonic sums coming from expanding recurrence coefficients near indices \(p+u\);
* values of the fundamental solutions at the lower boundary;
* the Green kernel normalization.

They are not equal to \(R_1,R_2,R_3\).  The reciprocal moments appear in a different construction: they arise from the finite convolution

\[
\sum_{u=1}^a b_{a-u}u^{-s},
\]

whereas the companion expansion has recurrence denominators accumulated along the whole path.

## Consequence for the proposed contradiction

Assume

\[
R_1(a)\equiv R_2(a)\equiv R_3(a)\equiv0\pmod p.
\]

This gives no implication that

\[
C_p(a)\equiv0\pmod{p^2}
\]

because the first correction term \(E_1(a)\) is not a linear combination of these three moments alone.  A proof would require an additional identity expressing \(E_1,E_2,E_3\) in the reciprocal-moment span; no such identity follows from the Casoratian.

## Small-index normalization check

For \(a=0\),

\[
p^3c_p\equiv b_0=1\pmod p,
\]

while all reciprocal moments vanish because the sums are empty.  Hence the first coefficient cannot be represented purely by the moments.

For \(a>0\), the same boundary contribution persists: the Green kernel has an initial-value term determined by \((c_0,c_1)=(0,1)\).

## No-go statement

The available structure proves only

\[
p^3c_{p+a}=b_a+O(p).
\]

It does not prove a hierarchy

\[
R_1=R_2=R_3=0\Rightarrow p^2\mid p^3c_{p+a}\Rightarrow\cdots.
\]

The missing ingredient would be an exact connection formula between the companion Green expansion and the polylogarithmic reciprocal-moment convolution. Without such a formula, the proposed hit-selective valuation argument is tautological at the leading congruence and fails at the next \(p\)-adic coefficient.
