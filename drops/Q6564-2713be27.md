## 1. Theorem [L2-FREQ]

Let

$$
D_h(r)=b_r c_{r+h}-b_{r+h}c_r
=\frac{N_h(r)}{((r+1)\cdots(r+h))^3},
$$

and

$$
F(t,\xi)=\sum_{h}\sum_r e_p(tD_h(r)+\xi h).
$$

For every finite h-window one has the exact Parseval identity

$$
\sum_{t\in\mathbf F_p}\sum_{\xi\in\mathbf F_p}|F(t,\xi)|^2
=p^2\sum_h \operatorname{Collision}(h),
$$

where

$$
\operatorname{Collision}(h)=\#\{(r,r'):D_h(r)=D_h(r')\}.
$$

Assume that the collision curve

$$
G_h(x,y)=\frac{D_h(x)-D_h(y)}{x-y}
$$

is absolutely irreducible over F_p. Then

$$
\operatorname{Collision}(h)=2p+O(h^2\sqrt p).
$$

### Proof

The equality condition splits as

$$
D_h(x)-D_h(y)=0.
$$

The diagonal component x=y contributes exactly p points. Removing this component gives

$$
D_h(x)-D_h(y)=(x-y)G_h(x,y).
$$

The second component is an absolutely irreducible plane curve of degree O(h). By the Weil bound for absolutely irreducible curves,

$$
\#G_h(x,y)=0=p+O(\deg(G_h)^2\sqrt p).
$$

Since deg(G_h)=O(h), the result follows.

Therefore bounded h windows have random-model L2 mass up to the optimal square-root error. In particular, almost all Fourier frequencies satisfy square-root cancellation.

Status: THEOREM (conditional on absolute irreducibility).
