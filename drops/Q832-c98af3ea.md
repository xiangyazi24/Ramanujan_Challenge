# Q832 — logarithmic-gap LGTQ-AGG-BDH Fourier audit

## Verdict

The additive-reciprocity/Fourier decomposition gives a useful exact rearrangement, but it does not by itself prove the required

\[
o(N^2/\log^2 N)
\]

bound. The missing input is a nontrivial averaged gap-modulus transform estimate for the Apéry zero sets. The obstruction is not the archimedean twist or incomplete periods; it is the absence of cancellation after the prime and frequency sums.

## 1. Centered edge variable

For a prime pair

\[
l=p+h,\qquad h\le C\log N,
\]

write the centered indicators as

\[
X_p(n)=1_{n\in Z_p}-\alpha_p,
\qquad
X_l(n)=1_{n\in Z_l}-\alpha_l.
\]

The edge correlation is

\[
\sum_{N<n\le2N} X_p(n)X_l(n).
\]

The local residue dependence is through the reductions modulo the gap:

\[
r_p(n)=n\bmod p,
\qquad r_l(n)=n\bmod l.
\]

Using additive reciprocity, the difference between the two characteristics can be expressed through the gap modulus h together with a slowly varying Archimedean phase. Schematically:

\[
e_p(n)-e_l(n)
=
\Phi_h(n)+\Psi_{p,l}(n),
\]

where \(\Phi_h\) is periodic modulo h and \(\Psi_{p,l}\) is the slow twist coming from the reciprocal denominators.

This identity is exact, but it is only a decomposition.

## 2. Summing over a complete gap period

Because

\[
N/h
\]

periods occur for \(h\le C\log N\), the pure periodic part has exact mean cancellation when its Fourier coefficient has zero average.

For the Fourier expansion

\[
\Phi_h(n)=\sum_{a\bmod h}\widehat\Phi_h(a)e(an/h),
\]

we obtain

\[
\sum_{N<n\le2N}\Phi_h(n)
=
O(h\max_a|\widehat\Phi_h(a)|).
\]

However the LGTQ quantity is not a single periodic sum. It contains:

- zero terms;
- mixed terms between centered and mean parts;
- boundary terms from the incomplete final period;
- moving fixed-q cell indicators.

The zero terms contribute the main density terms and cancel only after centering. The mixed terms reduce to first moments, which require the same missing distribution input. Boundary terms are at most O(h), but after summing over many prime pairs they are not automatically negligible.

## 3. Prime and frequency sums

After Fourier expansion the difficult contribution has the form

\[
\sum_{h\le C\log N}
\sum_{p}
\sum_{a\ne0\bmod h}
W(p,h,a)
\widehat Z_p(a)
\widehat Z_{p+h}(-a).
\]

The complete-period argument only controls the n-variable. It gives no unconditional saving for

\[
\sum_p W(p,h,a)\widehat Z_p(a)\widehat Z_{p+h}(-a).
\]

Without cancellation in the prime/frequency variables, the total remains compatible with the forbidden scale

\[
N^2/\log^2 N.
\]

Therefore the Fourier identity alone does not close LGTQ-AGG-BDH.

## 4. Weakest sufficient averaged transform estimate

A sufficient statement is not the full four-distinct-prime C4 estimate. It is enough to assume the following two-point gap-modulus transform bound:

For every fixed C,

\[
\sum_{h\le C\log N}
\sum_{p\le N-h}
\sum_{a\ne0\bmod h}
\frac{1}{p(p+h)}
\left|
\widehat Z_p(a)
\widehat Z_{p+h}(-a)
\right|

=o(1/\log^2 N).
\]

Equivalently, with the natural LGTQ normalization, the weighted off-zero gap Fourier mass must be

\[
o(N^2/\log^2 N).
\]

This is the minimal new ingredient because the surviving expression after exact centering is exactly a two-prime, one-gap transform.

## 5. Why this is weaker than C4

The four-distinct-prime C4 statement controls quantities of the form

\[
\sum_{p_1,p_2,p_3,p_4}
X_{p_1}X_{p_2}X_{p_3}X_{p_4}
\]

with four independent characteristics and all collision patterns removed.

The required gap transform only involves

\[
(p,p+h)
\]

and one Fourier frequency modulo h.

Every C4 bound implies this after Cauchy-Schwarz and discarding variables, but the converse is false: one can have all adjacent-gap Fourier correlations small while maintaining large correlations among separated quadruples.

Hence the estimate is genuinely weaker and not a renamed C4 statement.

## 6. Conclusion

The exact additive-reciprocity identity is structurally useful:

\[
\text{gap edge correlation}
\rightarrow
\text{gap-modulus Fourier modes}
\rightarrow
\text{prime-frequency sum}.
\]

But the final step requires new arithmetic cancellation. Complete gap periods remove the n-average only; they do not control the prime/frequency aggregate.

The sharp missing theorem is the explicit averaged gap-modulus transform estimate above. It is strictly weaker than four-distinct-prime C4 and would directly imply the required LGTQ-AGG-BDH target.
