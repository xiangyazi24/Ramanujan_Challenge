# 3. The exact complement Lucas defect

The relation

\[
A+D=8p-1
\]

is equivalent to saying that \(A\) and \(D\) have quotient digits \(6\) and \(1\), with reflected residue digits \(p-1-x\) and \(x\). Equation (2.2) therefore gives the universal congruence

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_{p,x}:=b_1b_A-b_6b_D\equiv0\pmod p.
}
\tag{3.1}
\]

Importantly, (3.1) does **not** use \(p\mid b_x\). It holds for every integer \(0\le x<p\).

Now let

\[
K_f=\operatorname{rad}\gcd(b_A,b_B,b_C,b_D).
\]

If a prime \(\ell\mid K_f\), then \(\ell\mid b_A\) and \(\ell\mid b_D\), and consequently

\[
\ell\mid b_1b_A-b_6b_D=\Delta_{p,x}.
\]

Thus, whenever \(\Delta_{p,x}\ne0\),

\[
\boxed{K_f\mid\operatorname{rad}|\Delta_{p,x}|.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

This is a genuine characteristic-zero gcd upper carrier. It has the right divisibility direction.

For an all-\(p\) statement with no nonvanishing discussion, one may use the always-positive carrier \(b_A+b_D\); every common prime also divides it. It has the same logarithmic height. The complement-specific carrier (3.1) is nevertheless nonzero throughout the asymptotic shell, as the next subsection shows.

## 3.1 Exact height: no Archimedean cancellation

Put

\[
\alpha=17+12\sqrt2.
\]

The standard Apéry asymptotic is

\[
b_n=c\alpha^n n^{-3/2}(1+O(n^{-1}))
\]

with \(c>0\). Also

\[
A-D=6p-1-2x.
\]

For \(0\le x<p\),

\[
\boxed{4p+1\le A-D\le6p-1.}
\tag{3.3}
\]

Hence

\[
\frac{b_6b_D}{b_1b_A}
\ll
\alpha^{D-A}\left(\frac AD\right)^{3/2}
=e^{-\Theta(p)}.
\tag{3.4}
\]

Therefore

\[
\Delta_{p,x}
=b_1b_A\bigl(1+O(e^{-c_0p})\bigr)
\]

for some \(c_0>0\), and

\[
\boxed{
\log|\Delta_{p,x}|
=A\log\alpha+O(\log p)
=\Theta(p).
}
\tag{3.5}
\]

Thus the special linear combination is not a small Lucas defect in characteristic zero. Its mod-\(p\) cancellation hides an integer of full exponential Apéry size.

## 3.2 Primitive stripping removes the only forced factor

E_{p,x}:=\frac{\Delta_{p,x}}p\in\mathbb Z.

This is the precise primitive-content obstruction: the only guaranteed \(p\)-factor in the complement defect is background content present before any actual flag is imposed.

A fixed-order supercongruence would not solve the occurrence problem. Even \(p^c\mid\Delta_{p,x}\) with an absolute \(c\) would provide only \(O(1)\) capacity at an anchor that can support an unbounded number of plus--minus flag pairs.

# 4. Wronskian and direct-transfer audit

Let \(\mathfrak a_n\) denote the standard companion Apéry numerator solution, and let

\[
d_n=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,n).
\]

After clearing the standard cubic denominators, define

\[
\mathcal W_{A,D}
:=d_A^3d_D^3
  \bigl(\mathfrak a_A b_D-\mathfrak a_D b_A\bigr)
\in\mathbb Z.
\tag{4.1}
\]

The nonadjacent Apéry Casoratian identity gives, for \(A>D\),

\[
\mathfrak a_A b_D-\mathfrak a_D b_A
=
6b_Ab_D
\sum_{j=D+1}^{A}
\frac1{j^3b_{j-1}b_j},
\tag{4.2}
\]

up to the fixed normalization of the companion solution. Every summand is positive, so \(\mathcal W_{A,D}\ne0\).

Every prime common to \(b_A\) and \(b_D\) divides the integer (4.1). Hence

\[
K_f\mid\operatorname{rad}|\mathcal W_{A,D}|.
\tag{4.3}
\]

This direction is valid. But

\[
\log d_n=O(n)
\]

and all Apéry numerator/denominator values involved have logarithmic height \(O(n)\). Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\log|\mathcal W_{A,D}|\ll A+D\ll p.
}
\tag{4.4}
\]

The sum in (4.2) has \(A-D=\Theta(p)\) terms and no alternating cancellation. The fixed-sum relation \(A+D=8p-1\) does not shorten it.

## 4.1 Why the ordinary nonwrapping restart cannot be applied directly

The recurrence transfer from \(D\) to \(A\) crosses the indices

\[
2p,3p,4p,5p,6p.
\]

The normalized recurrence has denominator factors \((j+1)^3\), so these factors are not units modulo \(p\). Therefore one cannot feed the high-index pair \(D,A\) directly into the nonwrapping gap-continuant theorem and cancel all intermediate denominators.

The safe operation is to apply Lucas and reflection first. But that operation sends both high indices back to the same lower zero \(x\). It produces no nonzero lower gap.

A fraction-free transfer matrix can of course be formed. If

\[
g_{AD}:=A-D=6p-1-2x,
\]

then its standard coefficient-height ledger is

\[
\boxed{
\log\|T^{\#}_{D\to A}\|_\infty
\ll g_{AD}\log(A+1)
\ll p\log p.
}
\tag{4.5}
\]

Primitive/content stripping can improve constants and sometimes remove a logarithm, but no current theorem contracts (4.5) to a sub-\(p\) quantity that retains flag multiplicity.

## 4.2 Resultants of two high transfers are degree-square

The other macroscopic coefficient gaps include

\[
\begin{aligned}
B-C&=7p+y_M-y_P,\\
B-A&=7p+x-y_P,\\
C-D&=6p-1-x-y_M.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4.6}
\]

For digits in \([0,p-1]\), each relevant positive gap is \(\Theta(p)\); for example

\[
6p+1\le B-C\le8p-1.
\tag{4.7}
\]

A resultant of two fraction-free transfer polynomials of lengths \(g_1,g_2\asymp p\) therefore has the generic determinant bound

\[
\boxed{
\log|\operatorname{Res}(F_{g_1},G_{g_2})|
\ll g_1g_2\log p
\ll p^2\log p.
}
\tag{4.8}
\]

The Apéry recurrence is not translation invariant and has no proved involution \(n\mapsto8p-1-n\) conjugating its transfer matrices. The scalar equality \(A+D=8p-1\) therefore creates no matrix symmetry that cancels the two macroscopic transfer lengths.

# 5. The exact lower three-zero resultant

Assume first that \(y_P\ne y_M\). The three lower zero positions

\[
\{x,y_P,y_M\}
\]

are distinct. Sort them as

\[
t<t+h<t+h+k.
\tag{5.1}
\]

The no-consecutive-zero theorem gives \(h,k\ge2\). The nonwrapping restart gives

\[
p\mid N_h(t),
\qquad
p\mid N_k(t+h).
\tag{5.2}
\]

Hence

\[
p\mid
S_{h,k}
:=\operatorname{Res}_X
  \bigl(N_h(X),N_k(X+h)\bigr).
\tag{5.3}
\]

The root-strip theorem gives \(S_{h,k}\ne0\), and the current determinant-height theorem gives

\[
\boxed{
\log|S_{h,k}|
\ll hk\log(h+k).
}
\tag{5.4}
\]

This carrier can preserve occurrences when used correctly. Fix one of the at most six label orders assigning \(x,y_P,y_M\) to the sorted triple. Distinct translations \(t\) give distinct common roots modulo \(p\), and the Sylvester valuation inequality yields

\[
\#\{\text{flags of that label order at }p,h,k\}
\le v_p(S_{h,k}).
\tag{5.5}
\]

Therefore, after summing the constant number of label orders,

\[
\sum_p
\#\{\text{flags at }p,h,k\}\log p
\ll\log|S_{h,k}|.
\tag{5.6}
\]

So there is no hidden multiplicity error in the existing three-zero resultant. Its problem is quantitative: (5.4) is degree-square, and the complement relation does not reduce \(hk\).

If \(y_P=y_M\), only two distinct lower zeros remain. Then one obtains one pair-continuant value

\[
p\mid N_{|x-y_P|}(\min\{x,y_P\}),
\]

whose logarithmic height is at best

\[
O\bigl(|x-y_P|\log p\bigr)=O(p\log p).
\tag{5.7}
\]

This is an old second-factorial branch, not a new complement contraction.

\qquad

\quad\text{or}\quad

Accordingly, multiplying by \(q_Pq_M\) is not a valid way to increase the logarithmic weight of \(K_f\). That would reverse the divisibility direction.

# 9. The smallest concrete new contraction lemma

The most economical current exact carriers can be coupled into a raw overcarrier that is strictly larger than the actual flag family.

For a geometrically integral tuple

\[
\tau=(p,x,y_P,y_M),
\]

put

\[
\begin{aligned}
d_P&=\frac{x+y_P+1}{6},
&v_P&=x+7d_P,\\
d_M&=\frac{p-x+y_M}{7},
&v_M&=x+7d_M.
\end{aligned}
\tag{9.1}
\]

For any shift \(d\) and potential reflected upper zero \(v\), define the shifted-divisor pullback

\[
\Sigma_d(v)
:=
\prod_{\substack{q\text{ in the upper shell}\\
                   q\mid b_v,\ q>v\\
                   q-d\text{ in the lower shell}}}
(q-d).
\tag{9.2}
\]

The map \(q\mapsto q-d\) is injective, so

\[
\log\Sigma_d(v)
\le\log\operatorname{rad}|b_v|
\le\log|b_v|
\ll_JT.
\tag{9.3}
\]

For \(x,y_P,y_M\) distinct, let \(S_\tau=S_{h,k}\) be the sorted three-zero resultant from Section 5. Define

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak C_\tau
:=
\operatorname{rad}_{\mathcal P_{J,T}^{\rm low}}
\gcd\!\left(
 |\Delta_{p,x}|,
 |S_\tau|,
 \Sigma_{d_P}(v_P),
 \Sigma_{d_M}(v_M)
\right).
}
\tag{9.4}
\]

In the degenerate branch \(y_P=y_M\), replace \(S_\tau\) by the corresponding nonzero pair-continuant value.

Every actual flag satisfies

\[
p\mid\Delta_{p,x},
\qquad
p\mid S_\tau,
\qquad
p\mid\Sigma_{d_P}(v_P),
\qquad
p\mid\Sigma_{d_M}(v_M).
\tag{9.5}
\]

Hence

\[
p\mid\mathfrak C_\tau.
\tag{9.6}
\]

Because the inverse formulas reconstruct \(q_P,q_M\) uniquely, distinct actual flags have distinct labelled tuples \(	au\). Therefore, for any raw geometry set \(\mathscr T_T\) containing all actual residual flags exactly once,

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{f\in\mathcal F^{\rm act}(T)}\log p_f
\le
\sum_{\tau\in\mathscr T_T}
\log\mathfrak C_\tau.
}
\tag{9.7}
\]

The current marginal inputs give only

\[
\boxed{
\log\mathfrak C_\tau
\le
\min\bigl\{
 O_J(T),
 O(hk\log(h+k))
\bigr\}.
}
\tag{9.8}
\]

This is the exact point at which all standard routes stop.

## `(COMP-2SHIFT)`

A sufficient genuinely new theorem is

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{\tau\in\mathscr T_T}
\log\mathfrak C_\tau
=o_J\!\left(\frac{T^2}{\log T}\right).
}
\tag{9.9}
\]

Here \(\mathscr T_T\) may impose the already-fixed shell, integrality, alignment, and far/core geometry, but it should **not** impose the actual lower-zero and selected-upper incidence tests used to define the target family. The factors in (9.4) already encode relaxed arithmetic supersets.

Thus (9.9) is not the target restated:

- it counts every shell prime in the resultant and shifted-divisor overcarriers, including phantom factors;
- it does not require that the common root of \(S_{h,k}\) is the displayed translation \(x\);
- it does not require that a shifted divisor corresponds to the chosen upper prime;
- it may omit the exact `Xi/Psi`, sign-OR, and liveness tests;
- it bounds a prescribed characteristic-zero gcd of existing raw carriers.

Proving (9.9) would be an actual Apéry correlation/contraction theorem between one same-characteristic three-zero resultant, the complement Lucas defect, and two shifted upper-prime conditions.

## 9.1 Equivalent anchor-level formulation

Conceptually, the minimal occurrence-preserving contraction would provide prescribed nonzero integers \(\mathcal E_{p,x}\), constructed before seeing which raw leaves are actual, such that
