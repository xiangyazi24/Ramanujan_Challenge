# 7. Dyadic summation and cutoff algebra

For dyadic `X<=Y`, geometric summation gives no extra logarithm:

```text
sum_{X dyadic, X<=Y} (T X^2+X^4)
```

```text

Y_2  = floor((M_T/eta_2)^(1/4)).
```

First,

```text

```

because `eta_2->infinity`.

Second,

```text

 <= T^2 sqrt(lambda/(L eta_2)).
```

After division by `M_T=lambda T^2/L`, the ratio is

```text
(T Y_2^2)/M_T
 <= sqrt(L/(lambda eta_2)).
```

Since

```text

 = (1+o(1)) T^(2/5)/(xi L^(6/5)),
```

one has

```text
sqrt(L/(lambda eta_2))
 = (1+o(1))
   xi^(1/2) L^(11/10)/(T^(1/5) eta_2^(1/2))
 = o(1).
```

The last step uses `xi=T^o(1)` and `eta_2=T^o(1)`; the factor `T^(1/5)` dominates every subpower numerator. The defining quantities tend to infinity, so the floors in `lambda` and `Y_2` do not change these asymptotics.

Substituting the formula for `lambda` also gives

```text

 = (1+o(1))
   T^(3/5)/
   (xi^(1/4) L^(11/20) eta_2^(1/4)).
```

Consequently

