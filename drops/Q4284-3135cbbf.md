## 2. The exact two-prime cost

The common primary residue \(r=6d+t\) must satisfy \(r<p=q-d\). Since all quantities are integral,

\[
7d+t\le q-1.
\]

Hence every raw lower prime, on either sign, satisfies

\[
d\le \left\lfloor\frac{q-1-t}{7}\right\rfloor,
\]

and therefore

\[
p\ge P_0(q,t):=q-\left\lfloor\frac{q-1-t}{7}\right\rfloor
=\left\lceil\frac{6q+1+t}{7}\right\rceil.
\]

Consequently, if two distinct admissible primes \(p,p'\) divide the fixed \(G_\sigma(q,t)\), then

\[
G_\sigma(q,t)\ge pp'\ge P_0(q,t)^2,
\]

and the exact radical cost is

\[
\log(pp')\ge 2\log P_0(q,t)
=2\log q+2\log\frac{6+t/q}{7}+o(1).
\]

Thus the elementary size route would close the theorem if one could prove

\[
G_\sigma(q,t)<P_0(q,t)^2.
\]

That is a legitimate strictly stronger premise, not a reformulation of Q6-SLUNI: a state can have at most one admissible prime while \(G_\sigma\) is enormous because of small primes or primes outside the admissible window.

## 3. Height budget first: the precise deficit

Put

\[
\alpha=17+12\sqrt2,\qquad \gamma=\log\alpha=3.52549\ldots.

In particular,

\[
\log A_n=\gamma n-\frac32\log n+\log c_A+o(1).

With \(n_+=6q+t\),

\[

\[
\begin{aligned}

Uniformly in every raw plus state,

\mathcal E_+(q,t)\ge 6\gamma q-\frac92\log q+O(1)

### Minus budget

With \(n_-=q-1-t\),

\[
\log G_-(q,t)
\le \gamma(q-1-t)-\frac32\log(q-1-t)-\log q+\log c_A+o(1).

The raw minus window gives \(d\ge t+1\) and \(7d+t<q\), hence

\[
8t+7<q,\qquad q-1-t\ge \frac78q.

The guaranteed \(q\)-stripping removes only \(\log q\) from the logarithmic height. Two lower primes cost only another \(2\log q+O(1)\). The remaining unconditional upper envelope is still exponential in \(q\).

\[

This answers the first requested calibration decisively:

not \(1\).

## 4. A new unconditional partial: logarithmic boundary vacancy

Although the full fixed-gcd height is much too large, the size of an **individual residual Apéry value** gives a useful pointwise restriction.

Define

\[
L(X):=\min\{m\ge 1:A_m\ge X\}.
\]

The Apéry numbers are positive and strictly increasing. If \(p\mid A_u\) with \(0\le u<p\), then \(A_u\ge p\), hence \(u\ge L(p)\). By the banked reflection symmetry, \(p\mid A_{p-1-u}\) as well, so also

\[
p-1-u\ge L(p).
\]

Thus every \(p\)-zero satisfies

\[
\min(u,p-1-u)\ge L(p).
\]

Since \(p\ge P_0(q,t)\), it is enough to put

\[
L_0=L(P_0(q,t)).
\]

The asymptotic above inverts to

\[
L_0=\frac1\gamma\log q+\frac{3}{2\gamma}\log\log q+O(1).
\]

### Theorem 4.1: raw plus boundary vacancy

A raw plus leaf must satisfy

\[
a=13d+t-q\ge L_0
\]

and, from the reflected primary zero,

\[
p-1-r=q-7d-t-1\ge L_0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{q-t+L_0}{13}\le d\le \frac{q-t-1-L_0}{7}.
\]

For this interval to be nonempty it is necessary that

\[
6(q-t)\ge 20L_0+13.
\]

Hence:

> If
> 
> 
> \[
> 6(q-t)<20L(P_0(q,t))+13,
> \]
> 
> then the raw plus fibre at \((q,t)\) is empty.

Asymptotically this excludes the selected upper boundary strip

\[
q-t<\frac{10}{3\gamma}\log q+\frac5\gamma\log\log q+O(1)
=0.9455\ldots\log q+1.418\ldots\log\log q+O(1).
\]

This is stronger than the trivial upper reflection margin \(q-1-t\ge L(q)\) imposed by \(q\mid A_t\).

### Theorem 4.2: raw minus boundary vacancy

A raw minus leaf must satisfy

\[
a=d-t-1\ge L_0
\]

and

\[
p-1-r=q-7d-t-1\ge L_0.
\]

Therefore

\[
t+1+L_0\le d\le \frac{q-t-1-L_0}{7}.
\]

For this interval to be nonempty it is necessary that

\[
q\ge 8t+8L_0+8.
\]

Hence:

> If
> 
> 
> \[
> q<8t+8L(P_0(q,t))+8,
> \]
> 
> then the raw minus fibre at \((q,t)\) is empty.

Equivalently, raw minus is empty throughout

\[
t>\frac q8-\frac1\gamma\log q-\frac{3}{2\gamma}\log\log q+O(1).
\]

### Scope of the partial

These are literal pointwise exclusions for actual selected states. They retain all raw windows; every residual, shell, primitive, and minus-first predicate only shrinks the raw set further. They use no marked-mass normalization and no lower bound on \(M_\sigma(T)\).

They do **not** close DPRM. Their width is \(O(\log T)\), while

\[
J_*=Y^{1/6}=T^{\beta/6}\gg\log T.
\]

Thus they are substantially shallower than the already banked deep-core scale. They are nevertheless the sharpest unconditional fixed-state support theorem obtained in this round: on the two displayed boundary sectors the horizontal support is not merely a singleton; it is empty.

## 5. The pair-conditioned contradiction attack

Now assume, for contradiction, that one fixed actual selected state and raw sign has two distinct lower primes

\[
p<p'=p+\Delta,\qquad d=q-p,\qquad d'=q-p'=d-\Delta.
\]

This is a **preselected single pair**, not a growing support and not a polynomial asked to control all possible partners. Up to this point the argument is outside the hypotheses of CQRD, PGMC, and the support-compression no-go results.

The exact local conditions are:

```text
plus:
  q | A_t,
  p  | A_{6d+t},       p  | A_{13d+t-q},
  p' | A_{6(d-Delta)+t},
  p' | A_{13(d-Delta)+t-q};

minus:
  q | A_t,
  p  | A_{6d+t},       p  | A_{d-t-1},
  p' | A_{6(d-Delta)+t},
  p' | A_{d-Delta-t-1}.
```

All four lower zero conditions and the raw minus-first assignment are retained.

### 5.1 The fixed-integer observation is necessary but inert

Let \(A,B\) denote the two fixed global Apéry values for the chosen sign. Then

\[
qpp'\mid A,\qquad qpp'\mid B.
\]

Writing

\[
A=qpp'U,\qquad B=qpp'V,
\]

shows exactly what the first divided rows know:

\[
(A/p,B/p)=qp'(U,V),
\]

\[
(A/p',B/p')=qp(U,V),
\]

and

\[
(A/q,B/q)=pp'(U,V).
\]

Every \(2\times2\) determinant of these quotient vectors is identically zero over \(\mathbb Z\). For example,

\[
\det\begin{pmatrix}A/p&B/p\\A/p'&B/p'\end{pmatrix}=0.
\]

This is not an arithmetic restriction produced by the two lower zeros. It is the universal projective-line identity that holds because all rows are scalar multiples of \((U,V)\). Using it as a “mixed tangent” is exactly the equivalence/universality trap already identified by the campaign.

### 5.2 Local recurrence transfer loses the other marking

Working modulo \(p\), the number \(p'=p+\Delta\) is a unit because \(0<\Delta<p\); the fact that \(p'\mid A,B\) has vanished. Working modulo \(p'\) similarly loses the \(p\)-mark. Working modulo \(q\) makes both \(p\) and \(p'\) units, so both lower special-fibre markings disappear.

Working modulo \(pp'\) does not repair this: by CRT,

\[
\mathbb Z/(pp')\cong\mathbb F_p\times\mathbb F_{p'},
\]

and the two lower recurrence states remain in orthogonal idempotent factors. There is no cross-column cancellation.

This is the dichotomy's **forget** branch.

### 5.3 Integral joining pays the pair radical

To retain both local markings one must keep an integral quotient, a determinant, a resultant, a continuant permanent, or a Fitting divisor. Any nonzero \(p,p'\)-safe scalar that remembers both marked special fibres has

\[
pp'\mid C,\qquad \log|C|\ge \log p+\log p'\ge2\log P_0.
\]

That lower bound is exactly the information content of the conclusion one is trying to contradict. The natural recurrence representatives are much larger.

In the deep canonical regime where the banked endpoint permanent applies:

* plus has logarithmic height \(\Theta_J(T)\);
* raw M8\(\pm\) minus has logarithmic height \(\Theta_J(T)\);
* raw M5 minus has
  
  
  \[
  \log B_e=\gamma(7d-\Delta-1)+O_J(\log T).
  \]

For an actual minus pair, the second prime itself obeys

\[
d'=d-\Delta\ge t+1+L(p'),
\]

so

\[
7d-\Delta-1=6d+d'-1\ge7d'-1\ge7L(p')+7t+6.
\]

Thus even the lowest raw M5 geometry naturally has a carrier on a scale far above the two-prime information floor; there is no hidden \(<pp'\) size contradiction. On the deep core the representative-independent lower bound is already \(\gg_JY^{1/6}\gg\log T\).

This is the dichotomy's **pay** branch.

### 5.4 Why recurrence propagation does not produce a new contradiction

At a fixed characteristic, two zeros determine a vanishing transfer continuant. For the pair above one gets one congruence in \(\mathbb F_p\) at indices determined by \(d\), and another in \(\mathbb F_{p'}\) at indices determined by \(d-\Delta\). They are different congruences in different fields. Combining them by CRT leaves them separated; eliminating the two quotient witnesses integrally produces exactly the pair radical and the large continuant/permanent heights described above.

The small Archimedean gap \(\Delta=o(q)\) is not a small \(p\)-adic or \(p'\)-adic displacement. There is therefore no Rolle/Strassmann-type common local field in which the two roots can be compared.

### Pair-attack conclusion

The pair-conditioned attack genuinely begins outside the earlier growing-support no-gos. It fails only when it tries to make the two characteristics interact:

* localize in one field: the other mark is erased;
* retain both integrally: the pair radical is paid;
* compare divided projective rows: the determinant is identically zero before either lower zero is used.

I found no fourth operation in the banked recurrence algebra.

This does **not** prove Q6-SLUNI false or impossible. It proves that the natural conditional-pair recurrence attack reaches the same structural wall, even though it does not begin as a support-compression argument.

## 6. Trap audit

### Equivalence trap

The following would merely restate the desired conclusion and are not accepted as mechanisms:

* a degree-one polynomial over \(\mathbb F_q\) whose roots are all actual partners;
* an assertion that the admissible radical has fewer than two prime factors;
* an adjacent-jet certificate whose coefficients may be chosen after testing whether the selected state has a partner;
* the universal determinant of the divided fixed-value rows.

The sufficient estimate

\[
G_\sigma(q,t)<P_0(q,t)^2
\]

is **not** equivalent to Q6-SLUNI; it is genuinely stronger and therefore a legitimate target. It is simply far beyond the available height estimate.

### Dichotomy trap

The pair setup itself does not trigger the dichotomy. The trap is triggered precisely at the attempted joining step:

* reduction to \(\mathbb F_p\), \(\mathbb F_{p'}\), or \(\mathbb F_q\) forgets at least one marked characteristic;
* CRT preserves two orthogonal components rather than coupling them;
* a characteristic-zero determinant/Fitting/continuant object remembers both only by acquiring the divisor \(pp'\).

Every such occurrence was discarded rather than used as a proof step.

## 7. What remains, quantitatively

A clean strictly stronger theorem that would settle the campaign target is:

> **Fixed-state quadratic gcd bound.** For every actual selected state and assigned raw sign,
> 
> 
> \[
> G_\sigma(q,t)<P_0(q,t)^2.
> \]

A more conventional but slightly stronger asymptotic form would be: for some fixed \(\eta>0\),

\[
G_\sigma(q,t)\ll_J q^{2-\eta}.
\]

Either gives Q6-SLUNI immediately for all large \(q\).

The present unconditional input is instead

\[
G_+(q,t)\le \exp(\gamma(6q+t)+O(\log q)),
\]

\[
G_-(q,t)\le \exp(\gamma(q-1-t)+O(\log q)).
\]

So the genuinely new information required is a cancellation of

\[
\gamma(6q+t)-O(\log q)
\]

on plus, and

\[
\gamma(q-1-t)-O(\log q)
\]

on minus, in the gcd itself. A generic subexponential gcd estimate is not enough; one needs a polynomial bound at exponent below \(2\), or a direct large-prime-support theorem that bypasses total size.

No banked recurrence theorem forces \(G_\sigma\) to be a power, a value of a fixed-degree polynomial, or a product of boundedly many small factors. The Lucas, supercongruence, modular, and known mod-\(\ell\) Galois descriptions are vertical in one characteristic. Known global results on prime factors of individual Apéry numbers do not control the radical of these two moving-index gcds in a short prime window.

The first genuinely adequate datum would therefore be one of:

1. a horizontal theorem proving \(G_\sigma(q,t)\ll q^{2-\eta}\) on actual selected states;
2. a direct theorem that the \(q\)-free gcd has at most one prime factor in \([P_0,q)\), proved by a method not equivalent to that assertion;
3. a non-factoring identity attached to one preselected pair which simultaneously retains the \(p\)- and \(p'\)-marks but yields a contradiction without producing a scalar divisible by \(pp'\).

The third item is logically possible and lies outside the formal scope of the functorial no-gos, but no such identity emerged from the integral recurrence, Lucas/reflection, divided jets, continuants, Wronskians, or the fixed-value projective line.
