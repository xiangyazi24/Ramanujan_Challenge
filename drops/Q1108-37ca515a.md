# Audit of the odd-prime Pade/Newton ratio

## Verdict

The min-plus identity from the gcd triangle is correct, but it does **not** imply
\(v_p(D_n)=v_p(\Gamma_n)\). The remaining assertion is a genuinely new
prime-power statement. The available radical containment automata for fixed
primes do not supply the required uniform lemma.

No counterexample is produced here; however the claimed equality is not proved
from the stated hypotheses.

## 1. Primewise reduction

For a fixed prime \(p\), write

\[
\ell_j=v_p(L_j),\quad
x=v_p(b_n),\quad
u_j=v_p(b_j),\quad
\mu_j=v_p(b_n-b_j).
\]

Then

\[
g=v_p(\Gamma_n)=\min_{j<M}(\ell_j+\nu_j),
\]

and

\[
d=v_p(D_n)=\min_{j<M}(\ell_j+\mu_j).
\]

The divisibility identity

\[
b_n=(b_n-b_j)+b_j
\]

implies

\[
\min(d,g)=\min(d,x)=\min(g,x).
\]

This only gives three possible configurations:

1. \(d=g<x\);
2. \(d=x<g\);
3. \(g=x<d\).

The desired equality excludes cases 2 and 3. The triangle identity alone does not do so.

## 2. Missing condition

A sufficient condition is the following prime-power lifting lemma.

**Lemma (needed).** For every prime \(p\ge5\), every \(n\), and every minimizer
\(j_0\) of \(\ell_j+v_p(b_j)\), there exists a (possibly different)
\(j_1<M\) such that

\[
\ell_{j_1}+v_p(b_n-b_{j_1})
\le \ell_{j_0}+v_p(b_{j_0}).
\]

Symmetrically, every minimizer for \(D_n\) must have a matching term in
\(\Gamma_n\).

This is the exact missing min-plus statement. It is stronger than radical
containment.

## 3. Why Lucas-Dwork and Kummer strata are insufficient by themselves

Lucas/Dwork congruences can show statements of the form

\[
b_m\equiv b_r\pmod {p^s}
\]

when the base-p digits of \(m\) have prescribed blocks.

Kummer gives

\[
v_p\binom ab=\#\{
\text{base-p carries in }b+(a-b)
\}.
\]

Therefore the known finite automata control the radical support and some
low-level valuation strata. They do not automatically prove that the exact
minimum over all \(j<M\) is preserved at every power \(p^e\).

A uniform proof would require a digit-lifting theorem saying that every
p-adic obstruction in \(b_n-b_j\) can be transferred to a witness with the
same or smaller Kummer cost.

## 4. Required stronger lemma

A valid completion would prove:

**Uniform odd-prime transfer lemma.** For all primes \(p\ge5\), all \(e\ge1\),
if

\[
p^e\mid L_jb_j\quad(j<M),
\]

then

\[
p^e\mid L_j(b_n-b_j)\quad(j<M).
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\Gamma_n\mid D_n
\]

at every odd prime power. Together with the reverse radical/content
comparison this gives equality of valuations.

This is the missing induction target.

## 5. What would follow if the lemma were proved

If for every \(p\ge5\)

\[
v_p(D_n)=v_p(\Gamma_n),
\]

then:

* all primes \(p\ge5\) contribute identically to the two contents;
* the only height discrepancy comes from the already isolated 2-adic and
  3-adic quotients;
* the two gcd nodes differ by a bounded factor (after the proved 2,3
  quotient bounds), so the height estimates are equivalent up to \(O(1)\).

The identity

\[
\sum_j L_j=1
\]

also gives

\[
b_n=\sum_j L_jb_n=\sum_jL_j(b_n-b_j)+\sum_jL_jb_j,
\]

which is the correct source of the radical containment relations, but again
not enough for equality of valuations.

## 6. Classification

### Proved from the supplied assumptions

- The min-plus formulas for \(d\) and \(g\).
- The gcd triangle identity.
- The logical consequence of valuation equality if the missing lemma holds.

### Not proved

- Uniform \(p^e\)-equality for every \(p\ge5\).
- A prime-uniform Kummer/Lucas-Dwork transfer theorem.

### No finite-data conclusion

The computation through \(n=1200\) is evidence only. It cannot establish the
prime-power statement.

The smallest rigorous next step is therefore not another scan but a proof of
the uniform odd-prime transfer lemma above, or an explicit tuple
\((p,n,j)\) violating it.
