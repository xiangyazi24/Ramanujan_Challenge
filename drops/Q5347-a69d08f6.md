\sum_{\substack{P<q\le2P\\J\in S_q,\ a_q(J)\in I_L}}
(\log q)a_q(J)m_D(q,J)
\ll
D M(S)P^{-\eta},
\tag{10.4}
\]
which would feed directly into the current proof of `U_D`.  I do not prove either (CURC) or (10.4).

The point of (CURC) is that it is the first place where genuinely new arithmetic is needed.  First-return uniqueness, packet heights, the restart identity, target replication, and the unique next-common-prefix graph have all already been exhausted before it.

The following statements are unconditional consequences of the interfaces audited above.  The final displayed critical-radical estimate is **not** included because it remains open.

```latex
\begin{proposition}[Utilization-depth sector bounds]
\label{prop:cc2q-utilization-depth-sectors}
Retain the literal CC2q medium graph and all current selected masks.  Put
\[
 R_{\le L}(J)=\sum_{a\le L}R(J,a),
 \qquad
 M(S)=\sum_J C_J,
 \qquad
 U_D=\sum_{J,a}aE_{J,a}.

Then, uniformly for every first-return cutoff $L$,
\[
 R_{\le L}(J)\ll L^2P^{o(1)},
\]
\[
 \sum_{J,a\le L}E_{J,a}
 \ll L^2M(S)P^{o(1)},
\]
\[
 \sum_{J,a\le L}aE_{J,a}
 \ll L^3M(S)P^{o(1)},
\]
and
\[
 \sum_{J,a\ge L}E_{J,a}
 \ll \frac{D^2M(S)}{L}P^{o(1)}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathcal E_D
 \ll M(S)P^{o(1)}
 \left(L^2+\frac{D^2}{L}\right),
\]
and choosing $L=D^{2/3}$ gives
\[
 \mathcal E_D\ll M(S)D^{4/3}P^{o(1)}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
If a shell characteristic $q$ occurs in $R(J,a)$, then the literal
selected first-return conditions give
\[
 q\mid b_J,\qquad q\mid N_a(J),\qquad
 q\nmid N_j(J)\quad(1\le j<a).
\]
Thus the packet primes divide the same nonzero continuant value $N_a(J)$,
and the standard continuant height gives
$R(J,a)\ll aP^{o(1)}$.  First-return uniqueness assigns one label to each
$q$, whence
\[
 R_{\le L}(J)\ll\sum_{a\le L}aP^{o(1)}\ll L^2P^{o(1)}.
\]
The unique next-common-prefix graph is simple, so
$E_{J,a}\le C_JR(J,a)$.  Summing gives the two low-label bounds.

 L\sum_{J,a\ge L}E_{J,a}
 \le U_D,
\]
and the current utilization estimate
$U_D\ll D^2M(S)P^{o(1)}$ gives the claimed high-label bound.
The last two assertions follow by adding the disjoint low/high sectors and
balancing the two alternatives.
\end{proof}

 \ll P^{12/5-\delta/3+o(1)}.

 P^{2/5-\delta}<a<P^{2/5+\delta}.

\end{corollary}
```

# Final status

The strongest unconditional **`U_D` defect** obtained by utilization depth is the low-label packet defect (3.3); at the bottom allowed first-return scale and critical `D` it reaches `9/200`.  The strongest high-label result is not a defect in `U_D`, but the three-cap sector endpoint (6.6).  More structurally, all off-critical first-return depths are power-subcritical, and the barrier localizes to
\[
a=D^{2/3}P^{o(1)},
\]
hence `a=P^(2/5+o(1))` at the critical physical scale, simultaneously at source and target for both owner characteristics.

The complementary sector can still carry essentially all of the actual utilization moment; no current theorem gives a fixed fraction less than one for that remaining `U_D`.  The unique-prefix packing and restart identities allow exponent-level saturation there.  What is missing is a power thinning of the selected critical primitive-return radical/covariance such as (CURC), not another multiplication of the existing caps.
