## 6.1 Exact colour support

For a set `S=\{p_1,\ldots,p_r\}` of distinct colours, put

\[
Q_S=\prod_{j=1}^r p_j
\]

and

\[
A_S(h_1,\ldots,h_r)
=\sum_{j=1}^r h_j\frac{Q_S}{p_j}\pmod{Q_S}.

Then

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{j=1}^r g_{p_j}(m)
=\frac1{Q_S}
\sum_{h_j\in\mathbb F_{p_j}^\times}
\left(\prod_j\widehat I_{p_j}(h_j)\right)
 e_{Q_S}(A_S(h)m).}

For every `j`, reducing `A_S` modulo `p_j` gives

\[
A_S\equiv h_j\frac{Q_S}{p_j}\not\equiv0\pmod{p_j}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{\gcd(A_S,Q_S)=1.}
\]

This is the exact colour-aware Fourier statement: centered rank `r` lives on **primitive frequencies of exact denominator containing all r fresh prime colours**. A three-colour centered term cannot silently degenerate into a one- or two-colour Fourier denominator.

# 9. Exact algebraic countermodel to the coarse interfaces

This is **not an Apéry counterexample**. It is an exact countermodel showing that reflection, H-isolation, universal HM2, one-variable shell localization, tiny content, and high-tail truncation do not logically imply I5 unless one uses additional arithmetic specific to the actual Apéry zero sets / actual `Q_X`.

Take

\[
m_0=\left\lfloor\frac X3\right\rfloor,
\qquad y_0=m_0(m_0+1).
\]

For every `p\in\mathcal P_X`, define

\[
I_p^*=\{m_0,\ p-1-m_0\}.
\]

For large `X`, both residues are distinct and nonconsecutive, because `p>X>2m_0+2`. They are a reflection pair, and

\[
Y_p^*=\{m_0\}.
\]

Thus the unique fold is H-isolated for **every H**, vacuously.

Every prime colour hits the same physical integer `m_0`, so

\[
K^*(m_0)=\#\mathcal P_X\asymp\frac X{\log X}.
\]

Since `\eta<1/15`,

\[
\frac{K^*(m_0)}{\tau}
\asymp\frac{X^{1/15-\eta}}{\log X}\to\infty.
\]

Hence `m_0` lies in the requested tail.

The density is

\[
\Lambda^*=2\sum_{X<p\le2X}\frac1p\asymp\frac1{\log X}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\mathcal I_5^*\ge(K^*(m_0))_5
\asymp\frac{X^5}{(\log X)^5},
\]

whereas the proposed right side has scale

\[
X^{o(1)}\left(1+X^2(\Lambda^*)^5\right)
=\frac{X^{2+o(1)}}{(\log X)^5}.
\]

The violation is a factor `X^{3-o(1)}`.

The universal HM2 bound still holds and is of the correct scale here: the single common star already contributes about `X^2/(\log X)^2`, which is exactly the allowed pair scale.

## Shell version of the same countermodel

To show that the *coarse shell interface* does not cure the star, take the standard shell carrier

\[
B_X^*(Y)=\prod_{0\le h<X}\bigl(Y-h(h+1)\bigr)
\]

and the primitive polynomial

\[
Q_X^*(Y)=Y-y_0.

Then `\operatorname{cont}(Q_X^*)=1`. If `m mod p` is either member of `I_p^*`, reflection gives

\[
m(m+1)\equiv m_0(m_0+1)=y_0\pmod p.

Since `m_0<X`, the factor `Y-y_0` occurs in `B_X^*`. Therefore **every hit** satisfies

\[
p\mid\gcd\bigl(Q_X^*(m(m+1)),B_X^*(m(m+1))\bigr).
\]

So the model simultaneously has reflection, perfect H-isolation, the one-variable shell divisibility, primitive content, the universal CRT/HM2 property, and an enormous high-tail five-star.

Again, `Q_X^*` is not the actual Apéry Newton/shell polynomial and `I_p^*` is not the actual Apéry zero set. The conclusion is exactly the intended logical one: **an argument that uses only the listed coarse consequences cannot prove I5. It must invoke an additional actual-Apéry nonalignment property.**

In fresh Hoeffding coordinates the obstruction is transparent. At the common star `m_0`, for most colours

\[
g_p(m_0)=1-\frac2p=1+o(1),
\]

so the fresh rank-3 term has size

\[
C_3\asymp\left(\frac X{\log X}\right)^3,
\]

whereas the independent cubic baseline is only

\[
C_0\asymp(\Lambda^*)^3\asymp(\log X)^{-3}.
\]

The missing gain is literally one factor `X` for each of the three fresh colours.

# 11. Why weaker-looking substitutes do not suffice

1. **Primewise Parseval does not suffice.** It controls `\sum_h |\widehat I_p(h)|^2`, but three different colours can place their large Fourier mass on compatible primitive CRT frequencies. The countermodel makes the physical weight essentially a delta mass, whose Fourier transform has no decay.

2. **Reflection does not suffice.** It makes the centered transforms real/even after a half-shift, but real phases can align just as well as complex ones.

3. **An unweighted trilinear bound is not automatically enough.** The weight `W_S(m)` contains the high-tail event and two physical anchor colours. Removing it changes the problem. One needs a theorem stable under precisely this pair-Palm weight, or a proved reduction that controls the cost of removing it.

4. **HM2 plus a tail count does not suffice.** HM2 sees the number of pair anchors, not the third conditional coefficient on those anchors.

5. **The exact HM3 characterization is not a theorem of small positive excess.** The current repository is explicit on this point; the arithmetic overload remains unproved.

6. **Shell content is not a substitute for cross-characteristic rank.** A single scalar divisibility locus can carry a many-colour star. A second genuinely independent equation, or an equivalent mixed-characteristic trace/nonalignment statement, is what would change the codimension.
