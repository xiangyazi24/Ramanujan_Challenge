## 8.2 A more structural, stronger formulation

For a cycle-state incidence `(C,u)`, let

\[
d_{\rm free}(C,u)
:=\#\{C'\ne C:(C,C';u)\text{ is a free same-sign pair}\}.
\]

The averaged free-partner theorem

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{(C,u)}d_{\rm free}(C,u)
=o_J\!\left(R\frac{T^2}{L^2}\right)}
\tag{SCL}
\]

also suffices, up to the harmless factor two from unordered pairs. A pointwise estimate

\[
d_{\rm free}(C,u)=o_J(R)
\]

on all but a negligible weighted set is stronger and would make the mechanism transparent.

This is the exact kind of theorem that a new deterministic-OR **closure equation** could prove: once one row `(p,u)` of a cycle is fixed, the remaining three rows would have to satisfy a second independent low-complexity congruence when compared with another cycle through `u`.

No such second equation appears in the current frontier. The recurrence, first-return masks, and Q5921 reverse-shift overcarrier constrain each row or each lower-prime pair separately; they do not correlate two complete cycle closures.
