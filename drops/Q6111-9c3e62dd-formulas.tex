\paragraph{Taylor lift and V1--V3.}
In a fixed chamber let
\[
 G_u=N_u(X_c)=N_u(x+\kappa_cp).
\]
Then, as an integer congruence,
\[
 \boxed{G_u\equiv N_u(x)+\kappa_cpN_u'(x)\pmod{p^2}.}
\]
Since $p\mid G_u,N_u(x)$,
\[
 \frac{G_u}{p}\equiv\frac{N_u(x)}p+\kappa_cN_u'(x)\pmod p.
\]
Define
\[
 \eta_x=\frac{b_x}{p}\pmod p,\qquad
 \eta_{x+u}=\frac{b_{x+u}}p\pmod p,
\]
\[
 \lambda_u(x)=\frac{E_u(x)}{A_u(x)}\in\mathbf F_p^\times,
 \qquad
 J_u(x)=\frac{b_{x+1}}{A_u(x)}N_u'(x)\pmod p.
\]
The repaired Q6092 formulas are
\begin{align}
 \boxed{\Theta_c(x,u)
 &=\frac{b_{x+1}}{A_u(x)}\frac{G_u}{p}\pmod p,}
 \tag{V1}\\
 \boxed{\Theta_c(x,u)
 &=\eta_{x+u}+\lambda_u(x)\eta_x+\kappa_cJ_u(x)
 \quad\text{in }\mathbf F_p,}
 \tag{V2}
\end{align}
with
\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 c&x&x+u&\kappa_c\\ \hline
 \mathrm I&a&r&6\\
 \mathrm {II}&a&z&-7\\
 \mathrm {III}&w&r&-2
 \end{array}.}
 \tag{V3}
\]
Indeed, dividing the exact transfer by $p$ gives
\[
 \frac{b_{x+1}}{A_u(x)}\frac{N_u(x)}p
 =\eta_{x+u}+\lambda_u(x)\eta_x\pmod p.
\]
If $p^2\mid G_u$, then $\Theta_c=0$.  If $p^2\mid N_u(x)$,
the term $N_u(x)/p$ is zero modulo $p$.  No formula changes.

The interpretive repair is that $\eta_x,\eta_{x+u},J_u$ are not three
independent coordinates: the divided transfer already fixes the combination
$\eta_{x+u}+\lambda_u\eta_x$.
