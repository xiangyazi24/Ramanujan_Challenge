# P3.2 exact secondary audit

## Transfer matrix

The recurrence is represented by

\[
\binom{u_{t+1}}{u_t}=M_t\binom{u_t}{u_{t-1}},\qquad
M_t=\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{4t(68t^2+3)}{(2t+1)^3}&-\frac{(2t-1)^3}{(2t+1)^3}\\
1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The determinant telescopes:

\[
\det(M_{m-1}\cdots M_1)=\prod_{t=1}^{m-1}\frac{(2t-1)^3}{(2t+1)^3}=\frac1{(2m-1)^3}=-\frac18\in\mathbb F_p.
\]

This gives a monodromy invariant, but it is independent of the potential terms and cannot produce the stated quantity S.

## Floquet derivative obstruction

Consider the natural spectral deformation

\[
M_t(z)=\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{4t(68t^2+3)+z(t^2-1)}{(2t+1)^3}&-\frac{(2t-1)^3}{(2t+1)^3}\\1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The determinant derivative is zero because the perturbation changes only the upper-left entry. For a trace/Floquet discriminant, Jacobi's formula gives sums of bilinear products of fundamental solutions:

\[
\partial_z\operatorname{tr}(M_{m-1}(z)\cdots M_1(z))
=\sum_t\operatorname{tr}(M_{m-1}\cdots M'_t\cdots M_1).
\]

After recurrence reduction these terms necessarily contain neighboring ratios
\(u_{t-1}/u_t\) and \(u_{t+1}/u_t\). The target

\[
S=\frac13-2\sum_{t=2}^m\frac{t^2-1}{u_t}
\]

contains only reciprocal values and cannot arise from this local transfer deformation.

## Cyclic tridiagonal obstruction

For a cyclic tridiagonal determinant, a logarithmic derivative has the form

\[
\partial_z\log\det C=\sum_i(C^{-1})_{ii}.
\]

Continuant inversion forces each diagonal entry to be a combination

\[
\alpha_t\frac{u_{t-1}}{u_t}+\beta_t\frac{u_{t+1}}{u_t}+\gamma_t.
\]

The second-order recurrence cannot remove both neighboring ratios while leaving only \(1/u_t\). Doing so would require a zero off-diagonal term, contradicting the nondegenerate recurrence.

## Audit conclusion

No local 2x2 Floquet discriminant derivative or cyclic tridiagonal determinant derivative identity yields S. The obstruction is structural: derivatives of second-order transfer systems produce adjacent-solution ratios, while S is a pure inverse-value sum. A valid certificate would need an additional nonlocal gauge transformation or a different invariant not present in the stated recurrence and endpoint condition.
