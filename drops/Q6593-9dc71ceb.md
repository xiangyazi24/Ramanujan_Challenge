# Two-clock low-rank carrier for the balanced shifted incidence

## 0. Executive verdict

A fixed-dimensional carrier does exist, in two equivalent forms.

1. The exact transfer carrier is a flat two-clock system in

```text
GL_2 x GL_2.
```

The two conditions

```text
K_m(X)=0,
K_g(X+m+1)=0
```

are two fixed Schubert-divisor conditions on that carrier.

2. The exact Green-kernel carrier is the rank-2 determinant kernel

```text
Delta(i,j)=det(v_i,v_j),

v_n=(b_n,c_n)^T.
```

The balanced incidence is the pullback of the fixed codimension-2 determinantal variety

```text
rank [v_0 v_1 v_2] <= 1.
```

This is the hidden Plucker structure. It is real, exact, and fixed rank.

However, it does not by itself prove MESO-BALANCED-PAIR. The state dimension is fixed, but the map from the clocks `(X,m,g)` into the carrier has growing algebraic complexity: its entries are continuants of degrees `3m` and `3g`. No fixed-conductor, bounded-degree, autonomous, or spectrally gapped realization of this clock map is presently available.

The four candidate routes reduce as follows.

```text
Christoffel-Darboux:
  one new exact Cassini identity exists;
  renewal and the normalized addition law are the same matrix-composition law;
  the full universal bilinear package is the rank-2 Plucker package;
  it yields transitivity, not a new equation cutting the balanced locus.

Bispectrality:
  the ordinary generating-function differential equation is not a
  bispectral operator in the start clock X;
  a standard scalar-gauge Jacobi bispectralization is impossible;
  a higher-order Apery-specific bispectral operator remains unexcluded.

Mixed crystal:
  the rank-4 crystal lives in the geometric variable t;
  the balanced first-block equations are already independent of the
  mixed extension coordinate;
  a new coefficient-extraction/Frobenius bridge would be required.

Plucker/determinantal:
  this candidate succeeds exactly at the fixed-rank level;
  it fails only at the fixed-complexity pullback level.
```

So the correct final sentence is:

> The two-clock carrier has been found, but it is the universal flat `GL_2 x GL_2` / Grassmannian carrier. What is still missing is not rank compression; it is clock-complexity compression.

That distinction should be entered into the single-point convergence map. Searching for another raw bilinear identity among the `K_m` is now low priority. The remaining breakthrough-grade possibility is an Apery-specific quotient of this carrier, most plausibly a higher-order bispectral, Cartier, Mellin, or mixed-Frobenius quotient in which clock translation becomes a bounded-complexity operation.

# 1. Exact transfer package

Put

```text
P(u)=34*u^3+51*u^2+27*u+5
```

and define

```text
T(u) = [ P(u)  -u^6 ]
       [  1      0  ].
```

For `m>=0`, let

```text
U_m(X)=T(X+m-1)*...*T(X),
U_0(X)=I.
```

Use the convention `K_(-1)=0`. Direct induction gives the complete matrix, not only its first entry:

```text
U_m(X)
 = [ K_m(X)       -X^6*K_(m-1)(X+1) ]
   [ K_(m-1)(X)   -X^6*K_(m-2)(X+1) ].              (1.1)
```

For `m=1`, this is exactly `T(X)`. Left multiplication by `T(X+m)` changes the two columns by the defining recurrence, proving (1.1).

The determinant is

```text
det U_m(X)=product_(j=0)^(m-1) (X+j)^6.             (1.2)
```

The ordered products satisfy the exact cocycle law

```text
U_(m+g)(X)=U_g(X+m)*U_m(X).                         (1.3)
```

Splitting after `m+1` steps and taking the `(1,1)` entry gives the banked renewal formula

```text
K_(m+g+1)(X)
 = K_(m+1)(X)*K_g(X+m+1)
   -(X+m+1)^6*K_m(X)*K_(g-1)(X+m+2).                (1.4)
```

Thus renewal is not an additional structure placed on top of the transfer system. It is one matrix entry of the composition law (1.3).

The normalized `delta` addition law in the question is the same composition law after a diagonal factorial gauge and the one-step positional shift. It should not be counted as a second independent bilinear identity.

# 2. The canonical flat two-clock carrier

Set

```text
s=X+m+1,
A=U_m(X),
B=U_g(s).
```

On the regular locus, where every transfer matrix used below is invertible, the state

```text
(A,B) in GL_2 x GL_2                              (2.1)
```

has fixed dimension. The joint incidence is simply

```text
A_11=0,
B_11=0.                                             (2.2)
```

These are two fixed Schubert divisors: `A_11=0` says that `A` maps the restart line to the opposite coordinate line, and similarly for `B`.

## 2.1 Exact clock updates

Increasing `g` by one gives

```text
A' = A,
B' = T(s+g)*B.                                      (2.3)
```

Increasing `m` by one changes both the first segment and the starting clock of the second segment:

```text
A' = T(X+m)*A,
B' = T(s+g)*B*T(s)^(-1).                            (2.4)
```

The formula for `B'` follows from

```text
U_g(s+1)=T(s+g)*U_g(s)*T(s)^(-1).                   (2.5)
```

The two clock updates commute. Indeed, both orders send `B` to

```text
T(s+g+1)*T(s+g)*B*T(s)^(-1).                        (2.6)
```

Thus (2.3)-(2.4) are a flat discrete connection on the two-clock lattice.

There is also an exact start-clock update

```text
X -> X+1:

A' = T(X+m)*A*T(X)^(-1),
B' = T(s+g)*B*T(s)^(-1).                            (2.7)
```

Equations (2.3)-(2.7) are the most economical answer to the request for a two-clock carrier. Projectivizing and fixing the two determinants reduces the geometric state dimension, but does not change the issue below.

## 2.2 Why this carrier does not close MESO-BALANCED-PAIR

The carrier has fixed state dimension, but its transition coefficients retain the absolute clocks `X+m` and `s+g`. Iterating them produces

```text
deg_X A_11=3m,
deg_X B_11=3g.
```

Consequently, the pullback of the fixed codimension-2 set (2.2) to clock space is still the growing-degree pair

```text
K_m(X)=0,
K_g(X+m+1)=0.
```

Nothing has yet reduced its conductor, degree, or arithmetic variation.

This makes the required distinction precise:

```text
fixed state rank:              FOUND;
fixed clock complexity:        NOT FOUND;
fixed-conductor trace object:  NOT FOUND;
uniform mixing/nonconcentration: NOT FOUND.
```

An autonomous fixed matrix realization would be even stronger, but is impossible. If clock translation were represented by powers of one fixed finite matrix and the continuants were fixed linear observables, then the scalar sequences would satisfy constant-coefficient recurrences and have rational generating functions. The Apery period is not rational, and the actual clock evolution is visibly nonautonomous.

The useful new theorem-shaped target in this coordinate is therefore not merely "find a low-rank carrier." It is

```text
[TWO-CLOCK-BRUHAT-MIXING]

For the flat carrier (2.3)-(2.4), the total number of regular balanced
clock triples whose two segment holonomies lie in the two fixed Schubert
divisors is O(p).
```

This is exactly MESO-BALANCED-PAIR in group language. It is a cleaner geometric coordinate, but not yet an easier theorem.

# 3. The exact rank-2 Green kernel

Let

```text
v_n=(b_n,c_n)^T,
Delta(i,j)=det(v_i,v_j).
```

For a regular integer start `X`, define

```text
D_m(X)=K_m(X)/product_(j=1)^m (X+j)^3.               (3.1)
```

Then

```text
D_m(X)=Delta(X-1,X+m)/Delta(X-1,X).                 (3.2)
```

## Proof

For fixed `X`, the right-hand side as a function of `m` has initial values

```text
m=-1: 0,
m=0:  1.
```

The recurrence for `v_n` gives

```text
D_(m+1)
 = P(X+m)/(X+m+1)^3 * D_m
   -(X+m)^3/(X+m+1)^3 * D_(m-1).
```

Dividing the recurrence for `K_m` by `product_(j=1)^m(X+j)^3` gives exactly the same recurrence and initial values. This proves (3.2).

Therefore

```text
K_m(X)=0
 <=> Delta(X-1,X+m)=0                               (3.3)
```

on the regular locus.

The balanced pair is the triple-collinearity condition

```text
Delta(t0,t1)=0,
Delta(t1,t2)=0,                                     (3.4)

where
 t0=X-1,
 t1=X+m,
 t2=X+m+g+1.
```

Since `v_t1` is nonzero, (3.4) is equivalent to

```text
rank [v_t0 v_t1 v_t2] <= 1.                         (3.5)
```

This is a fixed `2 x 3` determinantal variety of codimension 2.

## 3.1 The global determinant matrix has rank exactly 2

The matrix with entries `Delta(i,j)` factors as

```text
(Delta(i,j)) = b*c^T-c*b^T.                         (3.6)
```

Hence its rank is at most 2. The adjacent Casoratian is nonzero on the regular block, so the rank is exactly 2.

Thus the hoped-for rank-bounded compound is not conjectural. It is already present and has the smallest possible nontrivial rank.

## 3.2 Why rank 2 alone gives no collision bound

This is a decisive scoped no-go.

Take any projective color sequence

```text
q_0,q_1,...,q_(N-1) in P^1(F_p)
```

with `q_n!=q_(n+1)`. Choose arbitrary nonzero representatives `u_n` of the colors. Given any prescribed nonzero adjacent Casoratian sequence `w_n`, choose `lambda_0!=0` and recursively set

```text
lambda_(n+1)
 = w_n/(lambda_n*det(u_n,u_(n+1))).                 (3.7)
```

Then

```text
v_n=lambda_n*u_n
```

has

```text
det(v_n,v_(n+1))=w_n                               (3.8)
```

while retaining the arbitrary long-range color coincidences of the original `q_n`.

Therefore the following data, by themselves, do not control MESO:

```text
rank-2 determinant kernel;
Plucker identities;
no consecutive collision;
prescribed nonzero adjacent Casoratian.
```

The Apery recurrence contains more information than these universal invariants. A proof must use how the trajectory is generated, not only the fact that it lies in a two-dimensional solution space.

# 4. Candidate 1: Christoffel-Darboux and bilinear identities

## 4.1 A new exact Cassini identity

Taking the determinant of (1.1) and cancelling the common factor `X^6` gives, for `m>=1`,

```text
K_(m-1)(X)*K_(m-1)(X+1)
 -K_m(X)*K_(m-2)(X+1)
 = product_(j=1)^(m-1)(X+j)^6.                      (4.1)
```

This identity is immediately machine-checkable for `m<=6`. It is the continuant Cassini identity.

It has one useful consequence: at a regular root of `K_m(X)`, the two neighboring entries on the left cannot vanish. This is the local repulsion already observed in the resultant attack.

It does not create propagation of regular common roots in either clock.

## 4.2 The honest Christoffel-Darboux-type sum

Let

```text
u_n=K_n(X),
v_n=K_n(Y),
W_n=u_n*v_(n-1)-u_(n-1)*v_n.
```

Define the divided differences

```text
A_n(X,Y)=[P(X+n)-P(Y+n)]/(X-Y),
B_n(X,Y)=[(X+n)^6-(Y+n)^6]/(X-Y).                  (4.2)
```

A direct subtraction of the two recurrences gives

```text
W_(n+1)-(Y+n)^6*W_n
 =(X-Y)*[
    A_n*u_n*v_n
    -B_n*u_(n-1)*v_n
  ].                                                 (4.3)
```

Put

```text
L_n(Y)=product_(j=0)^(n-1)(Y+j)^6,
L_0=1.
```

Dividing (4.3) by `L_(n+1)(Y)` and summing yields

```text
W_(M+1)/L_(M+1)(Y)
 =(X-Y)*sum_(n=0)^M
   [A_n*u_n*v_n-B_n*u_(n-1)*v_n]/L_(n+1)(Y).        (4.4)
```

Equation (4.4) is the genuine Lagrange/Christoffel-Darboux sum for this recurrence.

## 4.3 Why it does not collapse to the standard CD kernel

For a Jacobi family, the spectral parameter appears in the diagonal as

```text
lambda(X)-a_n
```

and the off-diagonal coefficient is independent of `X`. Subtraction at `X,Y` then produces one scalar factor

```text
lambda(X)-lambda(Y)
```

multiplying one reproducing-kernel sum.

Here both recurrence coefficients depend on the shifted argument:

```text
P(X+n),
(X+n)^6.
```

The second divided difference `B_n` is nonzero and has degree 5 in the clock variables. The bulk term in (4.4) therefore contains two independent product types. It does not reduce to one unweighted positive kernel.

The fact that `A_n` and `B_n` have bounded polynomial degree means that (4.4) can itself be stored in a fixed-dimensional nonautonomous tensor state. That is another fixed-state carrier, not a fixed-complexity counting theorem. The clock dependence remains in every update.

There is an even more basic mismatch for the present problem: (4.4) compares two spectral arguments at the same length `n`, while the balanced incidence uses two different lengths `m,g` and the shifted argument `Y=X+m+1`. Endpoint vanishing of `u_m` and `v_g` does not annihilate the boundary term of one common-length CD formula.

## 4.4 Renewal, addition, Cassini, and Plucker

The complete universal rank-2 package is:

```text
matrix composition          -> renewal and addition laws;
matrix determinant          -> Cassini identity;
rank-2 minors               -> Grassmann-Plucker quadrics.
```

For any four times `i<j<k<l`, the Green minors satisfy

```text
Delta(i,j)*Delta(k,l)
-Delta(i,k)*Delta(j,l)
+Delta(i,l)*Delta(j,k)=0.                            (4.5)
```

Taking `k=j+1` and using the nonzero adjacent Casoratian turns (4.5) into the renewal/transitivity statement. Thus the suspected Plucker structure exists, but its content on the balanced locus is only

```text
two consecutive collinearities imply the total collinearity.             (4.6)
```

It supplies no third independent equation.

For the universal problem with generic `2 x 2` transfer matrices, the determinant is the sole algebraic relation among the four matrix entries. Hence every recurrence-universal bilinear identity among adjacent continuants is generated by composition, determinant, and their Plucker consequences. This statement is deliberately scoped: an Apery-specific identity not valid for generic transfer matrices is not ruled out.

```text
KILLED:

NOT KILLED:
```

# 5. Candidate 2: bispectrality

## 5.1 What would actually be needed

A useful bispectral structure would require one two-variable kernel `Psi_m(X)` and two commuting algebras:

```text
B_X Psi_m(X)=Lambda(m)*Psi_m(X),                    (5.1)

A_m Psi_m(X)=lambda(X)*Psi_m(X),                    (5.2)
```

where:

```text
B_X has fixed differential or difference order;
its coefficients have bounded degree/conductor;
A_m has coefficients depending on m but not on X except through
one fixed spectral coordinate lambda(X);
clock translation has a bounded-degree action on the spectral curve.
```

Only then could the shifted pair `(X,m)` and `(X+m+1,g)` descend to a fixed spectral correspondence.

The known differential operator

```text
theta^3
-t*(2*theta+1)*(17*theta^2+17*theta+5)
+t^2*(theta+1)^3                                    (5.3)
```

acts in the geometric generating-function variable `t`. It is the coefficient-recurrence dual of the Apery sequence. It is not an operator in the start clock `X`, and it does not make `K_m(X)` a joint eigenfunction.

This is the same variable distinction emphasized by the Beukers-Peters and Stienstra-Beukers K3 descriptions: the Picard-Fuchs coordinate is the geometric parameter `t`, whereas `X` here is the initial discrete clock of a Green kernel.

## 5.2 Exact left-end recurrence in X

The continuants also satisfy

```text
K_m(X)
 = P(X)*K_(m-1)(X+1)
   -(X+1)^6*K_(m-2)(X+2).                           (5.4)
```

This follows by expanding the tridiagonal continuant at its first row. It is a useful two-direction identity, but it mixes three lengths. It is not an eigen-equation for one fixed `m`.

## 5.3 A rigorous no-go for standard scalar-gauge Jacobi bispectrality

The following common form can be ruled out.

Assume there exist nonzero rational functions `q_m(X)`, nonzero scalars `alpha_m,gamma_m`, scalars `beta_m`, and one fixed rational spectral coordinate `lambda(X)` such that

```text
psi_m(X)=q_m(X)*K_m(X)
```

satisfies

```text
psi_(m+1)
 = alpha_m*(lambda(X)-beta_m)*psi_m
   -gamma_m*psi_(m-1).                              (5.5)
```

Put

```text
r_m=q_(m+1)/q_m.
```

Comparing (5.5) with the actual recurrence gives

```text
r_m*P(X+m)=alpha_m*(lambda(X)-beta_m),              (5.6)

r_m*r_(m-1)*(X+m)^6=gamma_m.                        (5.7)
```

Taking divisors in the rational function field, (5.7) gives

```text
div(r_m)+div(r_(m-1))=-6[-m].                       (5.8)
```

Hence, outside the fixed finite support of `div(r_0)`, the divisor of `r_m` contains alternating nonzero contributions at every point

```text
X=-1,-2,...,-m.                                     (5.9)
```

Multiplication by `P(X+m)` does not cancel them: `P(j)!=0` for every integer `j`, because the three roots of `P` are not integers. Therefore the left side of (5.6) has an unbounded pole set as `m` grows.

The right side of (5.6) has poles only in the fixed pole set of `lambda(X)`. This is impossible.

Thus no rational scalar gauge converts the continuants to a standard Jacobi/Favard recurrence with one fixed spectral coordinate.

This is a genuine structural death certificate, not a finite computation.

## 5.4 What remains open

The preceding theorem does not exclude a higher-order difference or differential operator in `X`. The exact finite ansatz to test is

```text
sum_(j=-q)^q a_j(X)*K_m(X+j)=Lambda_m*K_m(X),       (5.10)
```

or

```text
sum_(j=0)^q a_j(X)*(d/dX)^j K_m(X)=Lambda_m*K_m(X), (5.11)
```

with fixed `q` and fixed degree bounds for the rational functions `a_j`, independent of `m`.

For proposed `(q,degree)`, substitute `m=0,...,M`, clear denominators, and solve the resulting exact linear system over `Q`. A genuine positive result must persist when `M` exceeds the number of unknown coefficients by a large factor and must verify on new `m` values.

This is the highest-value remaining algebraic search. It is also the least settled part of this audit.

## Candidate-2 verdict

```text
KILLED:
  ordinary generating-function duality as a bispectral involution;
  standard rational scalar-gauge Jacobi bispectrality.

OPEN:
  a higher-order Apery-specific bispectral algebra or a nonlinear
  spectral quotient of the flat GL_2 x GL_2 carrier.
```

For terminology and the standard joint-eigenfunction requirement, the relevant classical sources are Duistermaat and Grunbaum, "Differential equations in the spectral parameter," Comm. Math. Phys. 103 (1986), 177-240, and Wilson, "Bispectral commutative ordinary differential operators," J. reine angew. Math. 442 (1993), 177-204. Their results are context for the definition; they are not being invoked as an automatic classification theorem for the present difference system.

# 6. Candidate 3: mixed-crystal coordinates

The banked mixed connection is

```text
Y=(C,theta*C,theta^2*C,t)^T,
theta Y=G(t)Y,

theta Phi(t)=G(t)Phi(t)-p*Phi(t)G(t^p).             (6.1)
```

This is a fixed rank-4 differential/Frobenius object in the geometric variable `t`.

## 6.1 Why fixed crystal rank does not yet imply fixed clock rank

The balanced incidence is a statement about coefficient indices and discrete start clocks:

```text
K_m(X)=0,
K_g(X+m+1)=0.                                      (6.2)
```

The matrices in (6.1) propagate fibers in `t` and compare `t` with `t^p`. They do not represent

```text
X -> X+1,
m -> m+1,
g -> g+1
```

as bounded-degree operations on crystal fibers.

Passing from a rank-4 differential module to its first `D` Taylor coefficients naturally produces a jet space whose dimension grows with `D`. Fixed differential rank is therefore not the needed fixed clock complexity.

## 6.2 The first-block cancellation is stable under conjunction

The gap continuant is universal for the homogeneous two-dimensional recurrence. It does not depend on the choice of the actual solution pair or on the mixed extension parameter.

The banked first-block theorem goes further: the candidate extension parameter `beta` cancels from the individual collision minor. Algebraically, if

```text
F_(X,m)(beta)=K_m(X)
```

is independent of `beta`, then the ideal generated by two such equations

```text
(F_(X,m),F_(X+m+1,g))                               (6.3)
```

is still independent of `beta`. Taking products, resultants, gcds, or saturation cannot recover a coordinate absent from both generators.

Thus the joint condition does not automatically see more mixed-crystal information than the two single conditions.

This agrees with the banked low-depth tests: projective collisions did not force the `D`, `E`, or `F` jet minors, and `c` was not in the tested linear span of the holomorphic jets.

## 6.3 The exact missing bridge

The mixed crystal could still help only through a genuinely new statement of one of the following forms.

```text
1. A bounded-rank two-point coefficient functor:
   coefficient extraction at X and X+m is represented by fixed crystal
   morphisms, uniformly in the clocks.

2. An affine extension cocycle:
   every balanced collision forces a nontrivial equation in an extension
   coordinate not present in the pure rank-2 transfer.

3. A Frobenius/Cartier quotient:
   clock shifts become bounded-order Frobenius, Hecke, or Cartier actions
   after an arithmetic transform.
```

None is currently banked. Without one, the rank-4 crystal is geometric background, not a carrier for MESO-BALANCED-PAIR.

## Candidate-3 verdict

```text
KILLED:
  the direct first-block linear descent and the idea that merely imposing
  two collision equations restores the cancelled extension coordinate.

OPEN:
  a nonlinear two-point coefficient-extraction or Cartier/Frobenius bridge.
```

The geometric source of the pure period is the K3 framework of Beukers and Peters, "A family of K3 surfaces and zeta(3)," J. reine angew. Math. 351 (1984), 42-54, and Stienstra and Beukers, "On the Picard-Fuchs equation and the formal Brauer group of certain elliptic K3-surfaces," Math. Ann. 271 (1985), 269-304. These works do not identify the coefficient clocks in (6.2) with points of the geometric base.

# 7. Candidate 4: Plucker and determinantal geometry

Let

```text
v_0=(x_0,y_0)^T,
v_1=(x_1,y_1)^T,
v_2=(x_2,y_2)^T,

Delta_ij=det(v_i,v_j).
```

The vector Plucker relation is

```text
v_0*Delta_12-v_1*Delta_02+v_2*Delta_01=0.           (7.1)
```

In coordinates,

```text
x_0*Delta_12-x_1*Delta_02+x_2*Delta_01=0,
y_0*Delta_12-y_1*Delta_02+y_2*Delta_01=0.           (7.2)
```

After saturating by the nonzero middle column,

```text
(Delta_01,Delta_12):(x_1,y_1)^infinity
 = (Delta_01,Delta_02,Delta_12):(x_1,y_1)^infinity. (7.3)
```

Thus the two sequential equations define exactly the rank-one `2 x 3` determinantal locus. Its codimension is

```text
(2-1)*(3-1)=2.                                      (7.4)
```

This is the correct expected codimension: a bounded-complexity transverse pullback from a three-dimensional clock box would have about `p` points, exactly the MESO target.

## 7.1 Fundamental-frame / Borel formulation

Put

```text
Gamma_n=[v_n v_(n+1)] in GL_2,
H_(r,h)=Gamma_r^(-1)*Gamma_(r+h).                   (7.5)
```

Then

```text
pi(r)=pi(r+h)
 <=> (H_(r,h))_21=0
 <=> H_(r,h) lies in the upper Borel B.              (7.6)
```

The cocycle law is

```text
H_(r,a+g)=H_(r+a,g)*H_(r,a).                        (7.7)
```

The balanced incidence is therefore

```text
H_(r,a) in B,
H_(r+a,g) in B.                                     (7.8)
```

Because `B` is a subgroup, (7.7) gives the total return for free. This is renewal in group language.

## 7.2 Exact two-clock symmetry

The pointwise mirror identity gives a bijection reversing every collision triple. If the orbit length is `N`, then

```text
r -> N-1-(r+a+g)
```

interchanges the two internal gaps. Hence, with the same regular/nonwrapping convention,

```text
rho_p(a,g)=rho_p(g,a).                              (7.9)
```

This halves the balanced triangle and is worth using in computations. It changes only a constant.

## 7.3 Why the determinantal carrier is not yet a proof

The target variety in (7.3) has fixed degree and codimension. The pullback map

```text
(r,a,g) -> (v_r,v_(r+a),v_(r+a+g))                 (7.10)
```

is not known to be a bounded-degree algebraic map or a bounded-conductor trace map. In the explicit continuant coordinate its defining equations have degrees `3a` and `3g`.

Consequently, ordinary Bezout, Lang-Weil, Deligne, or fixed-family incidence estimates do not apply uniformly when `a,g` grow to the mesoscopic scale.

The determinantal formulation has therefore located the precise missing theorem:

```text
[TWO-CLOCK-TRANSVERSALITY]

The pullback of the rank-one locus (7.3) along the explicit Apery
trajectory (7.10) has O(p) regular points in the balanced clock region,
uniformly at D=sqrt(p)*L.
```

# 8. What would qualify as a genuine breakthrough carrier

A carrier useful for counting must satisfy more than fixed vector-space dimension. At least one of the following must hold uniformly in `m,g,p`.

```text
1. Autonomous compression:
   clock shifts are powers of finitely many fixed operators with a spectral gap.

2. Algebraic compression:
   the clock-to-state map has bounded degree on a fixed parameter variety.

3. Sheaf compression:
   the incidence is a trace/flag condition in a compatible system of
   bounded rank and conductor.

4. Spectral-curve compression:
   the two clocks act by bounded-degree correspondences on one fixed
   bispectral curve.

5. Arithmetic quotient compression:
   after Mellin, Cartier, Hecke, or Frobenius transform, the shifted
   incidence becomes a bounded-order relation.
```

The canonical carriers in Sections 2, 3, and 7 satisfy none of these extra conditions. They solve the state-rank problem only.

This also explains why a finite Hankel-rank experiment must be interpreted carefully. A nonautonomous order-2 recurrence always has fixed state rank, while its scalar clock table may have Hankel rank growing with the window. A positive experiment must identify a clock-independent quotient, not merely rediscover the two-dimensional solution space.

# 9. Exact machine-check package

The following identities are suitable for direct SymPy verification for `m,g<=6`.

```python
import sympy as sp

X = sp.symbols('X')

def P(z):
    return 34*z**3 + 51*z**2 + 27*z + 5

def T(z):
    return sp.Matrix([[P(z), -z**6], [1, 0]])

def K(m, z):
    if m == -1:
        return sp.Integer(0)
    if m == 0:
        return sp.Integer(1)
    km1 = sp.Integer(1)
    km = P(z)
    if m == 1:
        return sp.expand(km)
    for n in range(1, m):
        kp = P(z+n)*km - (z+n)**6*km1
        km1, km = km, sp.expand(kp)
    return km

def U(m, z):
    A = sp.eye(2)
    for j in range(m):
        A = T(z+j)*A
    return A.applyfunc(sp.expand)

for m in range(1, 7):
    target = sp.Matrix([
        [K(m,X), -X**6*K(m-1,X+1)],
        [K(m-1,X), -X**6*K(m-2,X+1)]
    ])
    assert (U(m,X)-target).applyfunc(sp.expand) == sp.zeros(2)

    cassini = (K(m-1,X)*K(m-1,X+1)
               -K(m,X)*K(m-2,X+1)
               -sp.prod((X+j)**6 for j in range(1,m)))
    assert sp.expand(cassini) == 0

for m in range(0,6):
  for g in range(0,6):
    assert (U(m+g,X)-U(g,X+m)*U(m,X)).applyfunc(sp.expand) == sp.zeros(2)

    renewal = (K(m+g+1,X)
               -K(m+1,X)*K(g,X+m+1)
               +(X+m+1)**6*K(m,X)*K(g-1,X+m+2))
    assert sp.expand(renewal) == 0

for m in range(2,7):
    left_end = (K(m,X)
                -P(X)*K(m-1,X+1)
                +(X+1)**6*K(m-2,X+2))
    assert sp.expand(left_end) == 0
```

```python
assert sp.expand(x0*D12-x1*D02+x2*D01) == 0
assert sp.expand(y0*D12-y1*D02+y2*D01) == 0
```

# 12. Required final answers

## (a) Least-confident step

The least-confident step is the negative conclusion about Apery-specific higher-order bispectrality.

The moving-pole argument rigorously kills the standard scalar-gauge Jacobi form, and the universal matrix/Plucker audit rigorously classifies recurrence-generic bilinear identities. Neither argument excludes a fixed higher-order difference operator, a nonlinear spectral curve, or an arithmetic quotient that exists only for the special cubic `P` and sixth-power determinant.

That remaining possibility is concrete and machine-testable through (5.10)-(5.11), so it should not be declared dead without the exact ansatz computation.

## (b) Likely shared blind spot

The likely shared blind spot is an arithmetic quotient in which the absolute clock is not represented by the raw polynomial variable `X`.

All current no-go arguments work before a transform, where clock translation produces degree growth. A Mellin, Cartier, Hecke, or mixed-Frobenius transform might turn the two shifts into bounded-order actions on a fixed spectral object. Such a quotient would not necessarily appear as a low-degree polynomial identity among the `K_m`, so repeated resultant and continuant searches could miss it.

The correct place to look is the fixed holonomy-pair carrier already found:

```text
(H_(r,a),H_(r+a,g)) in PGL_2 x PGL_2.
```

Search for an Apery-specific bounded-complexity quotient of this pair. If none exists, then MESO-BALANCED-PAIR is indeed the family-compatibility wall in its irreducible two-clock form.
