## 9. Falsifiers for proposed integrality gains

Any claimed improvement should be checked against the following exact witnesses.

```text
F1. Local lattice witness:
    A_t = K z_q(t), with integer K within every selected capacity.
    Then A_q^circ = K g_q and Hilbert Cauchy is equality.

F2. Fixed-total witness:
    M = K Z(q).
    Formula (E) returns K^2 G_q exactly, with no rounding loss.

F3. One-sided reflection witness:
    load one member of each reflected zero pair and take K twice as large.
    The loss is a constant 1/4 before rescaling, not 1/log T.

F4. Capacity transition:
    capacity can help only when G=o(T/log T).
    The current envelope permits G~T^(8/5)log^(-4/5) T.

F5. Boolean-union test:
    a lower prime legal in both signs supplies one unit of capacity,
    not two. Omitting the negative intersection term fabricates load.

F6. Full-defect test:
    C=A-R is not integral. Profile-only rows can have positive PAUC
    even when A=0, so an A-lattice argument cannot be silently applied to C.

F7. Rectangle rounding:
    K_rect~T^(2/5)log^(-7/10) T tends to infinity.
    Replacing it by floor(K_rect) changes energy by relative o(1),
    not by a logarithm.
```
