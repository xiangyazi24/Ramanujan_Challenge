## The irreducible open problem

After the Apéry-Lucas collapse, the conjecture reduces to:

**Bound `Σ_{n=N}^{2N} e(A b_n / Q)` for composite Q = p₁p₂p₃p₄ (product of four primes ~ N).**

This is a character sum of the Apéry recurrence modulo a composite number.  Every reformulation
(Weyl sum, F₂ power saving, 4th moment, CRT anti-resonance, orbit-return, cocycle transversality)
leads to this same statement.  No existing theorem in analytic number theory, algebraic geometry,
arithmetic dynamics, or p-adic analysis provides it.
