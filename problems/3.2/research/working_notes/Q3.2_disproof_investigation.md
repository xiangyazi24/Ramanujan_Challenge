# Q3.2 — the DISPROOF direction: can pointwise `log G_n = o(n)` be FALSE?

## Verdict

**Disproof is not viable, and the attempt produces positive evidence the conjecture is TRUE.**
Not a proof — but the disproof lens converts "open" into "open, with strong numerical + structural
evidence for truth and a clear structural reason no counterexample exists."

## What a disproof would require

Pointwise P3.2 is false ⟺ there is a fixed `ε>0` and infinitely many `n` with `log G_n ≥ ε n`.
Via the support law (§5.9/§5.41), for the dominant top arc `p∈(n/2,n]` this needs, at a single `n`,
about `ε n / log n` **distinct middle primes** `p` simultaneously dividing `b_{n-p}` — a *multi-prime
avalanche at one index* (or, alternatively, one middle prime to multiplicity `~ε n/log n`, the "(T)
full-multiplicity phantom prime").

## Numerical evidence (fresh, this session; exact bignum, `n ≤ 6000`)

Exact `a_n` (mpq), `b_n` (mpz), `d_n=lcm(1..n)^3`, `G_n=gcd(d_n a_n, d_n b_n)`.

1. **The exceptional tail decays.** Per-decade `max log G_n/n`: 0.041 (n≈900) → 0.029 → 0.026 →
   0.023 → 0.013 → 0.012 → 0.011 → 0.0076 (n≈5700). Both max and mean → 0.
2. **Worst-case scaling is exactly `(log n)²`.** The absolute running-max `max_{n≤N} log G_n` stays
   flat (30 → 59) while `N` grows 6000-fold; ratio to `(log N)²` is stable at **0.6–0.9** across three
   orders of magnitude. So `max_{n≤N} log G_n ≈ 0.75(log N)²`, hence `log G_n/n = O((log n)²/n) → 0` —
   precisely the Poisson-independence prediction (§5.31 mean ≈ 1). A counterexample needs this scaling
   to *break* (a positive floor / phase transition); it does not.
3. **Worst cases are diffuse, not spikes.** Factoring `G_n` at the worst-ratio `n`
   (523, 1667, 2098, 3471, 4855): every one spreads `log G_n` over many prime powers with no dominant
   term; the **largest prime factor is always `v_p = 1`** (contributing only ~6–8), and roughly half
   the mass sits in the regular `p ≤ √n` part the density theorem already controls. The (T)-violation
   signature — a middle prime to high multiplicity — **never appears**. This is the "diffuse `v_p=1`
   pencil" the barrier map predicts, not the phantom.

## Structural reason disproof fails (why the numerics are not an accident)

- **The only forcing mechanism is pure-apex.** Forced coincidences come from CM / non-ordinarity
  (§5.26): a prime `p` forces a zero at the *single* index `d=(p-1)/2` (i.e. `p=2d+1`). In the
  (index, prime) plane the forced set is a *line of bounded intercept* — **one forced prime per `n`**,
  never many. This is exactly Lemma R13.2 ("coherent sections are pure-apex: `r(n)=O(polylog)` for
  `n≠apex`"). A structured family cannot avalanche at a generic in-range `n`.
- **ζ(3) is non-CM.** The relevant form 8.4.a.a (`η(2τ)⁴η(4τ)⁴`, weight 4) is non-CM, so even the
  single-index forcing is *rare* (only `p=11` below 2000), versus the ζ(2) sibling where CM by `Q(i)`
  forces half of all primes. The mechanism that makes the ζ(2) *anti-clustering* statement fail is
  absent for ζ(3) — and even for ζ(2) it is one prime per index, not an avalanche.
- **Heights neither forbid nor force.** The Critical Ledger (Gate 2) is *exactly critical* (surplus
  `= (λ-3)n =` the ζ(3) irrationality margin, spent): the adelic budget does not rule a spike out,
  but Partition Blindness shows it cannot *locate* mass to force one either. So the disproof cannot
  borrow an upper-bound obstruction; it must exhibit the phantom *actually realized in-range* — which
  (2)–(3) above refute.

## Honest caveat

`n ≤ 6000` cannot exclude a spike at astronomically large, very sparse `n` driven by an unknown
mechanism (a phase transition in the `(log n)²` law). But: (i) no candidate mechanism exists — every
known forcing is pure-apex; (ii) the `(log n)²` scaling is clean over three decades; (iii) the
concurrent automode session's independent deep numerical search (avenue d, commit 55bf7236) reached
the same conclusion — "zeros confirmed structureless, wall does not dissolve numerically." Convergent
negative results from two independent probes.

## STEELMAN update (n ≤ 15000; search where structure could pile up, not uniformly)

Rather than scan all `n` (Poisson-suppressed deviations), push the disproof at its strongest points.

4. **Small-prime channel provably cannot spike.** `log G_n = (p≤√n part) + (middle part)`. The small
   part is `≤ 3 Σ_{p≤√n} ⌊log_p n⌋ log p = O(√n log n) = o(n)` (a Chebyshev `ψ(√n)` sum). So *only*
   the middle avalanche can disprove.
5. **The actual avalanche count is tiny.** `K(n) = #{middle primes p | G_n}`, computed exactly for
   every `n ≤ 15000`: **max K(n) = 7** (at n=7882). A counterexample needs `K ~ εn/log n` (hundreds).
   It never exceeds 7 — the Poisson `max ~ log n/loglog n` regime. The `(SA)/(Ω)` target is nowhere
   near violated.
6. **No full-multiplicity phantom prime.** Max middle-prime multiplicity over `n ≤ 15000` is 1 (the
   only `v_p ≥ 2` case is `p=3` at `n=6`, the trivial regime). The (T) signature is absent.
7. **`(log n)²` law holds to n=15000:** max `log G_n = 77.8` at n=14213, ratio to `(log n)²` = 0.85 —
   identical constant to n=500. No phase transition.
8. **Targeted CM-apex test (the one structured location).** Non-ordinary primes `p | a_p` of 8.4.a.a
   below 12010 are exactly **{11, 3137}** (density-0 sparsity confirmed). At their forced apex indices
   `d=(p-1)/2` (paper §5.26): `log G_d/d = 0.358` (d=5, trivial regime) and `0.0131` (d=1568 —
   *below* the typical decade max ~0.029, utterly unremarkable). In BOTH cases the forced prime
   **does not even divide `G_d`** (`v_p(G_d)=0`): the §5.26 forcing populates the BAC/anti-clustering
   line, but does not produce a `p | G_n` incidence at the apex. Structure yields no pile-up anywhere.

**Every disproof channel is a dead end:** small-prime part `O(√n log n)`; middle avalanche `K ≤ 7`;
multiplicity `≡ 1`; `(log n)²` law unbroken; the unique structured forcing is pure-apex, non-CM
(2 primes below 12000), and does not even hit `G_n`. The conjecture is almost certainly TRUE.
