[qLOWGENUS — does the continuant structure force low genus on the difference curves? life8]
A hostile audit of the coincidence route just returned this as its least-confident point, i.e. the single place where new structure could revive the pairwise range: the difference curves
  X_{h,h'}: N_h(r) Q_{h'}(r') = N_{h'}(r') Q_h(r),   Q_h(r) = prod_{j=1}^h (r+j)^3,
have bidegree (3h, 3h') in P1 x P1, hence GENERIC arithmetic genus O(h h') and per-pair Weil error O(h h' sqrt p). If the special continuant structure forces the NORMALIZATION of X_{h,h'} to have genus O(h+h') (a "low-genus collapse"), the pairwise range improves from p^{1/6} to p^{1/4}. TASKS:
1. The function Delta_h(r) = N_h(r)/Q_h(r) is the value at distance h of the normalized restart solution: Delta_h = z_h where z_0=0, z_1=1/(r+1)^3, z ranges over a rank-2 recurrence in the shifted variable. The curve X_{h,h'} is the fiber product of the two maps Delta_h, Delta_{h'}: P1 -> P1 of degree 3h, 3h'. The genus of a fiber product of two rational maps is governed by their RAMIFICATION (Riemann-Hurwitz on the correspondence): compute it. Key question: what is the ramification structure of Delta_h : P1 -> P1? Its critical points are zeros of Delta_h' (derivative in r). If Delta_h behaves like a "Belyi-ish"/low-ramification map (few critical values, or critical values in a fixed finite set independent of h), the fiber product genus collapses. If low-genus collapse is FALSE generically, identify any structured SUBFAMILY of pairs (h,h') (e.g. h' = h+1 adjacent via coprimality, or h'=2h via the update/doubling identity) where the fiber product degenerates into components of controlled genus, and state exactly what partial COINC statement that subfamily yields.
