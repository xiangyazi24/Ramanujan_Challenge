[qPAIR-FLAT — the pair-level flat law: |E_{h,k}| ~ sqrt(p) with O(1) constant. Prove or explain the mechanism. life5]
NEW MACHINE DATA (exact, nine (p,H) cells up to p=4001, H=p^{0.66}, all pairs h<k<=H): the centered pair coincidences E_{h,k} = #{(r,r'): delta_h(r)=delta_k(r')} - baselines - components satisfy mean |E_{h,k}| ~ (1.0-1.1) sqrt(p), UNIFORM over pairs — the hk-independent random-function noise floor — even though the fiber-product curve X_{h,k} has genus ~ 6hk and generic Weil error hk sqrt(p). Signs are random (sum over pairs of |E| ~ 0.5 H^2 sqrt(p); signed shell sums A_d/p <= 0.8 with no structured d). This extends the per-h flat law (max_t |S_h(t)| ~ sqrt(2 log p) sqrt(p), h-uniform) down to pairs.
TASKS: 1. Cohomological accounting: E_{h,k} = (1/p) sum_{t!=0} S_h(t) conj(S_k(t))-type. The 2g_{h,k} ~ 12hk Frobenius angles of X_{h,k} would generically produce error ~ sqrt(2g) sqrt(p) ~ sqrt(hk) sqrt(p) if angles were random, or up to 2g sqrt(p) worst case. Observed: O(1) sqrt(p). Write the exact statement of the pair-equidistribution theorem needed and check its plausibility with the known local data (complete local type of G_h determines h — so pairs are never isomorphic — Goursat applies). (b) Distinguish what is provable VERTICALLY (fixed h,k, p -> infty: joint Sato-Tate for two non-isomorphic sheaves — this IS provable by Deligne + big monodromy of the product, if the two geometric monodromy groups have no common quotient acting compatibly — state it precisely) from the HORIZONTAL uniformity needed (h,k up to p^{2/3} at FIXED p — the usual wall). 2.
