[q62 LASTSTAND — cross-correlation rigidity of the root-set family: the same eigenvector mechanism at SHIFTED consecutive positions, aiming at the scattered adversary. life10]

The run-rigidity mechanism (see q61 if cross-referenced; self-contained here) proves: r, r+1 in the SAME Z_d forces xi_r onto an explicit point v(r,d). The scattered adversary avoids same-Z_d runs. But the SAME algebra applies to consecutive positions in DIFFERENT root sets:

SETUP. Suppose r in Z_d and r+1 in Z_{d'} (a "stepped pair", the building block of ANY dense scattered packing: if a positive fraction of a length-2D block is covered by union of Z_d's, then stepped pairs with |d - d'| <= any threshold are forced in abundance by pigeonhole -- quantify this in part (iii)).

(i) Derive the constraint. xi_{r+d} = xi_r =: u and xi_{r+1+d'} = xi_{r+1} = M_r u. The second condition: M_{r+d'} ... careful -- xi_{r+1+d'} = T_{r+1 -> r+1+d'} xi_{r+1} is NOT a single matrix step unless d' relates to d. Work instead with the two Casoratian vanishings as polynomial conditions on the single unknown u = xi_r: Delta_{r,d} == 0 is automatic (defines u's fibre relation), and Delta_{r+1,d'} == 0 pulls back through xi_{r+1} = M_r u. In general each stepped pair gives ONE algebraic condition linking u to the pair (d,d'). A single condition on a 1-dimensional unknown does NOT pin u -- so one stepped pair is not rigid. Determine the minimal LOCAL configuration (how many stepped/nearby pairs in a window of O(1) consecutive positions) that over-determines u and yields an orbit-free identity in (r, d-values), analogous to the 3-run case. Candidates: (r, r+1, r+2) in Z_d, Z_{d'}, Z_{d''} respectively ("staircase triple"); or (r, r+1) in Z_d x Z_{d'} plus (r, r+1) in Z_e x Z_{e'} (double coverage). For each: count unknowns vs equations, derive the identity if over-determined, estimate its degree in the gap variables.

(ii) The packing consequence. If staircase triples (or your minimal rigid configuration) are confined to a bounded-degree variety in (r, d, d', d''), then their total count for gaps <= D is O(D^2) (or compute the right power). A scattered packing achieving P_D = Theta(N) on a positive fraction of blocks must contain how many staircase triples? Pigeonhole: a length-2D block with >= delta * D covered positions, coverage by pairs with gaps in (D/2, D], forces >= f(delta) * D staircase triples per block (compute f, being hostile: the adversary chooses which positions to cover and may leave every third position uncovered to kill triples -- can coverage density 1/3 avoid ALL consecutive covered pairs? YES trivially: cover every other position. So consecutive-position mechanisms cap the provable density at 1/2, NOT at o(1)!). THEREFORE: determine whether the eigenvector mechanism generalizes from CONSECUTIVE positions (r, r+1) to positions at bounded distance (r, r+m), m <= m_0: xi_{r+m} = (M_{r+m-1}...M_r) xi_r is still a single explicit matrix application, so the same cross-product collapse applies with T := M_{r+m-1}...M_r in place of M_r -- BUT T is no longer companion, so the cross product has the full quadratic form Q_{T, T'}(u) = 0 (T' the gap-shifted product), a CONIC through u, not a forced point. Two distinct conic conditions (from two nearby stepped pairs) intersect in <= 4 points => u pinned up to 4 choices => orbit-free identity again. Formalize: any TWO stepped pairs within a window of span m_0 with the four endpoints distinct pin xi to <= 4 explicit points and yield an identity on a bounded-degree variety in the (r, gaps) space. Verify degree bookkeeping (deg of transfer product entries grows like O(m_0) -- keep m_0 an absolute constant, that suffices for density arguments).
(iii) The theorem to aim at: [SPARSE-PAIRS] there is c > 0 and m_0 such that for all but O(D^{2-eta'}) positions r, the window (r, r+m_0] contains at most ONE covered position. Consequence chain: covered density in any block <= 1/m_0-ish + errors => A(D) <= N/m_0 + O(D^{2-eta'}). NOTE: this bounds density by a CONSTANT 1/m_0, not by o(1) -- it does NOT give [FR_eta] directly. But combined with iteration over m_0 = m_0(p) growing slowly? The degree of the identities grows with m_0; count solutions on the variety as a function of m_0 and D and find the largest m_0(D) for which the bound stays o(N). If m_0 can be taken as large as D^c, you get A(D) << N/D^c = power saving = BREAK. This is the sharpest currently-visible path to [FR_eta]. Do the degree/count bookkeeping meticulously -- it is the whole question. If the growth of degrees kills m_0 -> infinity, report the exact crossover and what improvement (e.g. summing identities with cancellation instead of counting solutions one by one) would restore it.
