[q64 LASTSTAND — horizontal large sieve for a nonautonomous 3-term recursion over F_p: literature contact + design. life4]

The wall is now phrased as: prove #{(x,h) : N_h(x) == 0 mod p, D/2 < h <= D, x in window} << D^(2-eta), where for FIXED x the sequence h |-> N_h(x) satisfies an explicit nonautonomous 3-term linear recursion (coefficients rational of bounded degree in x+h), and for FIXED h the function x |-> N_h(x) is a polynomial of degree 3(h-1). Per-row (fixed x) zero counts obey the restart bound O(D^(2/3)); per-column (fixed h) the degree budget 3(h-1). Both marginals are individually sharp-ish; the needed saving is JOINT.

(i) LITERATURE: is there ANY existing technology for joint zero-counting of solution families of linear recurrences over F_p? Search your knowledge for: zeros of linear recurrence sequences mod p (Skolem-Mahler-Lech has no mod-p analogue, but quantitative bounds on zero multiplicity of recurrences over finite fields exist — Lech/Christol? multiplicity of linear recurrences in characteristic p, Derksen's structure theorem for zero sets of recurrences in char p!); large sieve for thin families of polynomials; bilinear forms with polynomial root indicators; Bombieri-Pila/determinant method adapted to mod-p root counting of a POLYNOMIAL FAMILY with recursive structure; Cilleruelo-Garaev-type results on value sets/collisions of polynomial maps over F_p; energy of polynomial orbits (Shkredov-Shparlinski school). For each contact: state the exact theorem shape it gives and whether it survives our regime (degrees growing like D ~ sqrt(p) L, which is the hostile feature).
(ii) DERKSEN ANGLE (potentially decisive, think hard): in characteristic p, zero sets of linear recurrence sequences are p-automatic (Derksen). Our double-indexed object N_h(x) mod p is a recurrence in h whose coefficients depend on x+h — not constant — so Derksen does not apply verbatim. BUT: the Apery sequence b_n mod p has strong Lucas-type/truncated-hypergeometric structure (b_{n} mod p is a product over base-p digits — verify: Apery numbers are known to satisfy the Gessel congruence b_{np+r} == b_n b_r mod p, i.e. b is p-Lucas!). If b is p-Lucas, then the orbit xi_r mod p has digitwise product structure, and collisions xi_{r+d} = xi_r acquire a base-p digit description. Work out: what does the p-Lucas property give for the collision set {(r,d) : xi_{r+d}=xi_r} in the mesoscopic strip d ~ sqrt(p) L? In the window r <= p-2, r has at most two base-p digits' worth of structure... r is a single digit! (r < p). So Lucas structure at ONE digit says nothing directly — UNLESS one lifts to two-digit range or uses the second solution c_n (which is NOT p-Lucas; it involves harmonic-number corrections H_n — the derivative structure). The pair (b,c) mod p relates to (b, b') — a jet. Collisions = vanishing of a Casoratian = Wronskian-type object with derivative structure. Does the jet/derivative formulation give an algebraic description of Z_d beyond "roots of N_d"? Specifically: is there a closed-form for N_d(x) mod p in terms of truncated hypergeometric/harmonic data that makes the FAMILY {N_d}_{d in dyadic} amenable to joint counting (e.g. all N_d divide/relate to values of a SINGLE two-variable polynomial of controlled degree)? THIS — an algebraization of the d-index at fixed p via Lucas/jet structure — is the one route that was never seriously attempted (the old H-algebraization death certificate was about characteristic-zero bounded-degree families; mod-p digit structure is different).
(iii) Give the sharpest theorem you can actually prove today along any of these lines, with proof; and the exact statement of the missing lemma if you get within one lemma of a power saving.
