# 12. Final assessment

The binomial, diagonal, K3, finite-hypergeometric, and modular descriptions are all real and mutually compatible. Their exact contribution is:

```plain text
A_j mod p
 = terminating balanced 4F3 unit cancellation
 = torus Kummer trace for Lambda and omega_p^j
 = Mellin coefficient of the Apéry truncation/Hasse–Witt polynomial
 = constant term of a weight-4 weakly holomorphic modular form.
```

For q=1 and fixed (n), this sharpens further to

```plain text
selected character = omega_p^(n-1),
selected coefficient shell = p-1 inside Lambda^(n-1).
```

What is missing is not another local realization. It is a theorem coupling these realizations across different residue characteristics on one fixed row. The most economical such theorem is ((mathrm{HMC}_2)); the most concrete algebraic route is a low-height close-shell resultant for (E_n(m)).

At present, the honest status is:

- unconditional q=1 pointwise saving: not proved;

- bounded-complexity local Mellin trace: proved by the exact torus formula;

- fixed-(n), 27-shell coefficient carrier: proved above;

- norm or Galois-orbit amplification: fails;

- central fixed modular form: exact but contributes at most one prime per row;

- existing Chebotarev/Sato–Tate/vertical estimates: wrong quantifier or wrong topology;

- narrow remaining theorem: aggregate short-gap horizontal Mellin non-clustering.
