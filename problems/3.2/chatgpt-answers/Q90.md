## 6.2 Why classical discrete Sturm theory does not transfer

Classical Sturm/Jacobi oscillation theory uses:

- an ordered real field;

- signs and sign changes;

- positivity of off-diagonal coefficients;

- self-adjoint spectral parameters;

- interlacing of real zeros/eigenvalues.

None of these structures survives over \mathbf F_p. A finite field has no compatible order, and the variable x changes every diagonal and off-diagonal coefficient rather than entering as a scalar spectral shift.

The Casoratian theorem says that two linearly independent homogeneous solutions cannot have zero Casoratian at one time when the off-diagonal coefficients are nonzero. Our event is different: one endpoint-conditioned solution takes the same affine value at two different times.

Literature on common zeros for recurrence-polynomial sequences also does not directly apply. For example, Wang–Jin, Common zeros of polynomials satisfying a recurrence of order two (arXiv:1712.04231), studies an autonomous recurrence with coefficient polynomials independent of the order index. The Apéry continuant has coefficients P(x+r) and (x+r)^6 changing at every step.

Verdict for (b): no bounded intersection multiplicity or spacing theorem follows from known Sturm/Casoratian theory. The valid replacement is the determinantal descent (1)–(3).
