# Executive verdict

I do not obtain an all-h proof of [CRIT-2H]. I also do not find a structural counterexample to the statement. The machine picture described in the question is entirely compatible with full Morse behavior. What the audit does establish is sharper:

1. The proposed dyadic archimedean separation is false at its leading scale. After the factorial pole denominator is canceled, the pole scale is exactly b_{j-1}b_{h-j}. Its exponential factor is independent of j, its total range is only polynomial in h, adjacent bulk scales have ratio 1+O(h^{-1}), and the two central scales differ only by O(h^{-2}) relatively.

1. The exact two-block formula can nevertheless be made completely explicit. It reduces the archimedean route to a uniform two-sided Jost expansion and a genuinely global cross-branch no-resonance theorem. Leading asymptotics alone cannot supply that theorem.

1. A fixed-prime Newton-slope proof is structurally blocked. At \ell=7 all Apéry numbers are units, so every predicted near-pole scale has valuation zero. At \ell=5 there are exponentially large digit families of pole scales with the same valuation. Moreover the critical-value polynomial is even, so slopes already identify \pm t in pairs. A viable p-adic route must use residual squarefreeness or angular components, not slopes.

1. The differential pencil does propagate in bounded rank before elimination: rank 3 for the pencil, rank 6 for its X-jet, and rank 36 for the two-point tensor. But the projection to the critical-value polynomial forgets the marks needed for the next step. Thus the current tensor system does not yield a scalar recurrence for V_h.

1. All three routes meet the same missing object: the off-diagonal scheme of two critical points having the same value. This is a two-point problem. Pole jets, Newton polygons, and one-point holonomy all stop one categorical level below it.

The best new concrete output is the exact cell formula in Section 1 and the exact bounded-rank pencil in Section 4. The cleanest remaining theorem is the linear collision-degree bound [COLL-2H] in Section 5.

## 2.3 Exact residual for the archimedean route

The route reduces to the following statement.

[ARCH-CELL-ORDER]. The limiting map \Phi has enough nonzero simple critical branches; these branches persist with a complete uniform expansion in 1/r and 1/s; and, for r+s=h-1, at least h of the resulting squared values are pairwise distinct and are not hit by any other critical branch.

The first two clauses are local analytic problems. The third is global and is the actual obstruction.

Even a full C^2 proof of (2.9) does not settle the third clause. There are two reasons.

1. Adjacent same-branch gaps are tiny. A bare relative error O(h^{-1}) is larger than the central O(h^{-2}) gap. One needs a full symmetric expansion and a signed comparison, not an error bar.

1. Different branches overlap in magnitude. If two limiting constants satisfy 0<|\tau_1|<|\tau_2|, the range of q_{h,j} has ratio \asymp h^{3/2} and mesh tending to one. Hence the bands q_{h,j}|\tau_1| and q_{h,k}|\tau_2| necessarily overlap for large h. Size estimates cannot rule out
