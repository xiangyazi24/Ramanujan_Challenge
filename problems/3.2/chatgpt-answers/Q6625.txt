# 10. Required confidence audit

## (a) Least-confident step

The power/log fixed-point calculation and the exact midpoint identity are the most robust parts. The step I would audit first is the global reduction (3.3): both orientations of the adjacent-gap triangle, the dyadic boundary h+d<=X, and overlap at min(h,d)=G_0 must be written with the project's exact nonwrapping conventions.
