The reduction of full pointwise Ramanujan Challenge Problem 3.2 to density zero of the fixed prime set

It does not control the off-center reflection pairs that make up almost all of Z_p, and it does not control the moving condition r=n\bmod p needed for arbitrary n.

The exact conclusions are:

1. For every prime p\ge5,

1. Reflection gives b_{p-1-r}\equiv b_r\pmod p. Since the endpoints are nonzero and the center is the only fixed point,

1. Kilbourn’s modulus-p^3 theorem is for a fixed Rodriguez–Villegas hypergeometric period with four 1/2 parameters. It is not an upgrade of the classical central Apéry number congruence from p^2 to p^3.

1. There is no known elliptic family E_x or fixed K3 family X_x with x=r(r+1) whose fiber trace equals b_r\bmod p for every r. The invariant r(r+1) merely packages the reflection involution; the interpolating polynomial depends on p and has degree comparable with p.

1. A genuine moving geometric lift does exist, but on the character/Mellin axis: r specifies a Kummer character, and b_r is the residual trace of bounded-dimensional Kummer-twisted cohomology. The character order, coefficient field, and selected residual condition vary with p. Current Chebotarev, Sato–Tate, special-cycle, and ordinary-reduction theorems do not match those quantifiers.

1. The central slice is not merely insufficient; it is automatically harmless for each fixed n. If n\bmod p=(p-1)/2, then p\mid 2n+1. If \lfloor n/p\rfloor=(p-1)/2, at most one p can occur. Thus even a positive-density set of central nonordinary primes would contribute only O(\log n) to the support law.

The full every-n problem remains a moving-zero-set/cross-prime dispersion problem. The modular form 8.4.a.a supplies a distinguished diagnostic slice, not a reduction of the problem.

# 10. Audit of proposed unconditional motivic theorems

## 10.1 Density of ordinary primes for the fixed weight-four form

For the separate fixed-form problem, density one of ordinary primes is open. Jin–Ma–Ono construct forms nonordinary at any prescribed finite set; they do not prove a density theorem for one fixed non-CM weight-four form.

Recent results still do not cover 8.4.a.a:

- Suh proves strong results for parallel weight 2 Hilbert modular forms; in higher weight he handles potentially abelian realizations and obtains weaker weight-3 statements.

- Wang–Zhang treat GL$_2$-type abelian varieties and weight-2 modular forms.

Even a future solution of this fixed-form problem would not settle P3.2, for the reasons above.

## 10.2 Ordinary reduction of K3 surfaces

Bogomolov–Zarhin prove that a fixed K3 surface becomes ordinary at density-one primes after a finite extension.

This theorem concerns a fixed K3 surface over a number field. It does not concern:

- all fibers of a K3 pencil with a parameter varying modulo p;

- high-order Taylor coefficients of a Hasse section;

- Kummer characters whose order varies with p;

- the deterministic selector r=n\bmod p.

It cannot be applied to b_r because b_r is not the Hasse scalar of the fiber X_r.
