# Hostile audit verdict: THM-NEAR-WALL-3/7

Overall verdict: the theorem survives the audit. The proof chain has no fatal gap under the stated banked inputs. The only nontrivial dependencies are the already-banked pair-collision estimate Q_p(Y) and the window multiplicity lemma. The claimed corollary needs a corrected exponent/log bookkeeping statement.

## Step (1): midpoint bijection

VERDICT: CONFIRMED.

The substitution u=r+h is injective because r=u-h. The original constraints imply u+d<=p-2 and h in the required interval. Enlarging W_{H,d}(u) to W_H(u) only adds nonnegative terms:

W_{H,d}(u) <= W_H(u).

The later Cauchy step is valid because it is applied to the enlarged nonnegative product sum. Boundary terms h>u are excluded by the definition W_H(u)=0 for those h. No cancellation is used.

## Step (2): nu norm

VERDICT: CONFIRMED with convention check.

For d1<d2 contributing to binom(nu_D(u),2), we have

pi(u-d1)=pi(u)=pi(u+d2-d2)

More directly the midpoint u gives three orbit points

u-d1, u, u+(d2-d1)

with two gaps d1 and d2. The corresponding Q_p(D) triple is based at u-d1 with gaps d1 and d2. Since both gaps are <=D and nonwrapping is preserved, the pair count injects into Q_p(D).

Hence

sum nu_D^2 <= O(D^2)+O(Q_p(D)) << D^(5/2).

## Step (3): W injection

VERDICT: CONFIRMED.

For h1<h2, set

r=u-h2,

g1=h2-h1,

g2=h2.

Then

pi(r)=pi(r+g1)=pi(r+g2).

The map recovers u=r+g2, so distinct pairs cannot collide. Also

g1>=1,

g2<=2H,

g1<g2,

and r>=0 follows from h2<=u. Thus the triple is a valid Q_p(2H) object.

Therefore

sum W_H^2 << H^(5/2).

Similarly

The precise corollary is not K<<HD without qualification; one logarithm remains unless the pair-collision input is strengthened to remove the log.
