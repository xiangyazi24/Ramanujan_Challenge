GARQI-1' remains plausible, but the gap-polynomial/divisor-counting route by itself is capped at the already known

The cap is not caused by coefficient height and not caused by integer zeros of G_h. It is caused by:

- the exact forced mirror factor, which turns the mirror main term back into a one-zero problem;

- the quadratic cumulative degree sum_{h<=H}deg G_h;

- the absence of any valid decreasing bound for all large-gap pairs from local clustering.
