# 8. Relation to Fu–Wan and later effective results

Fu–Wan’s explicit cohomological calculations are relevant to the constant B_m in (1)–(5): they make ranks, weights, local factors, and Betti bounds effective in concrete exponential-sum families. They do not estimate exact residue-characteristic zeros of a Mellin trace.

A related but distinct result is Fu–Lau–Li–Xi, Equidistribution of Kloosterman Sums Over Function Fields, which proves Sato–Tate with explicit errors as places vary in function fields and notes applicability to suitable \mathrm{SL}_2 local systems. That result still concerns archimedean Sato–Tate distribution of Frobenius angles. It does not count values divisible by the residue characteristic.

The common source of the effective error is always Deligne’s Riemann Hypothesis: once a test function is represented by a fixed bounded-complexity sheaf, the nontrivial cohomology contributes O(Q^{1/2}).
