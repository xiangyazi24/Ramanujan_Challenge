# [WITHIN-DIGIT-CARTIER]: exact finite innerization, lambda-one surface, and the residual observable-only quotient

## 0. Executive verdict

The attack produces one genuine construction and one genuine obstruction.

1. The clock-augmented projective recurrence is an exact autonomous birational skew product

```text
sigma(r,[x:y]) = (r+1, [P(r)x-r^6 y : x]).
```

Its h-th iterate is governed by the transfer product

```text
T_h(r) = T(r+h-1) ... T(r),
T(u) = [[P(u),-u^6],[1,0]].
```

The degree of `T_h(r)` is linear in h, in fact `3h+O(1)` in the standard transfer normalization, and the collision entry is `N_h(r)` of degree `3h-3`. Hence the dynamical degree is exactly

```text
lambda(sigma) = 1.
```

This is a real structural gain: the within-digit family is not a collection of unrelated degree-`3h` polynomials; it is a Jonquieres-type lambda-one birational dynamical system.

2. However, `sigma` is not an honest automorphism of `A^1 x P^1`. Its fiber matrix has determinant `r^6`, so the fiber over `r=0` is singular and one point is indeterminate. Restricting away from `r=0` is not stable under translation. Resolving all iterates requires exceptional data along the translated singular fibers, and that data grows linearly with the number of iterates, or through all p clock values in characteristic p. Thus the direct surface does not give Definition 0.

3. The lambda-one condition by itself does not invoke a known finite-field theorem strong enough for the campaign. The Ostafe-Shparlinski slow-degree machinery gives improved orbit exponential-sum estimates for special fixed polynomial or rational systems, often for long orbits or after averaging over initial states. In our two-coordinate situation the available single-orbit thresholds are longer than the length-p within-digit orbit, and the direct complete-sum input remains the ordinary Weil estimate with conductor `O(h)`. Bourgain-Gamburd expansion also does not apply to this deterministic nonstationary word.

4. There is nevertheless an exact positive answer to `[FINITE-CARTIER-LOCAL-DUAL]`. On a finite clock of length `N=p^k`, the cyclic clock shift is inner in the full jet endomorphism algebra. The characteristic-zero boundary defect is exactly `N` times a rank-one projector and therefore vanishes modulo `p^k`. Moreover, the cyclic shift has an exact Cartier carry recursion. This gives a finite autonomous model of the within-digit clock and an exact collision operator.

5. This finite model is not a bounded-complexity quotient. Its module rank is `p^k`; already at level k=1 the clock shift and clock multiplication generate the full matrix algebra `M_p(F_p)`. The recursive description has small carry depth, but its low-digit width is p. Succinct Cartier-circuit complexity is not bounded conductor.

Therefore the full-algebra version of `[WITHIN-DIGIT-CARTIER]` remains dead. The only surviving form is observable-specific:

```text
[OBSERVABLE-ONLY-WEYL-FACTOR]
Can the single Apéry collision matrix coefficient factor through a fixed
algebraic quotient of the finite Weyl-Cartier tower, even though the whole
clock algebra has no small quotient?
```

That is the single sharp question at the end of this answer.

The repository state at commit `58aea7a` is consistent with this reduction. In particular, the verified transfer formula and `deg N_h=3(h-1)` give the exact linear-growth calculation below; the new W_h/Morse material in that commit does not alter the clock-compression obstruction.

## 1. The exact clock-augmented birational system

Write the projective state as `z=[x:y]`. The transfer step is

```text
T(r) z = [P(r)x-r^6 y : x].
```

Adjoin the clock:

```text
sigma : A^1 x P^1 --> A^1 x P^1,
sigma(r,z) = (r+1,T(r)z).
```

As a rational map, this removes the nonautonomy completely. Inductively,

```text
sigma^h(r,z) = (r+h,T_h(r)z),
T_h(r) = T(r+h-1) ... T(r).
```

Up to the fixed transpose/conjugation convention used in `CTX.txt`, the standard product is

```text
G_h(s) =
[[-(s+h)^6 U_{h-2}(s+1), (s+h)^6 U_{h-1}(s)],
 [-U_{h-1}(s+1),             U_h(s)            ]].
```

Since

```text
deg U_j = 3j,
```

the four entry degrees are, for h in the stable range,

```text
3h,
3h+3,
3h-3,
3h.
```

Thus

```text
deg sigma^h = Theta(h),
lim_{h->infinity} (deg sigma^h)^(1/h) = 1.
```

The collision coordinate is smaller still:

```text
pi(r)=pi(r+h)  <=>  N_h(r)=0,
deg N_h=3h-3.
```

So the prompt's proposed `3m` growth is correct up to an additive constant. The full transfer matrix has maximum base degree `3h+3`; the projective collision numerator has degree `3h-3`.

This is exactly the linear-growth branch in the birational surface classification: the map preserves the rational fibration given by the clock coordinate. In the terminology of surface dynamics it is a Jonquieres-type map, and its first dynamical degree is one.

### 1.1 Why it is not an automorphism

The determinant is

```text
det T(r) = r^6.
```

At `r=0`,

```text
T(0) = [[5,0],[1,0]].
```

Hence the point `(0,[0:1])` is indeterminate, while the rest of the fiber is collapsed to `[5:1]`. Therefore `sigma` is neither a morphism nor an automorphism of `A^1 x P^1`.

Its rational inverse is obtained by replacing r by `r-1` and applying `T(r-1)^{-1}`. It has the corresponding singularity over `r=1`. Thus `sigma` is birational, not biregular.

### 1.2 Restricting the base does not repair it

One can make one step invertible on

```text
G_m x P^1 = {r != 0} x P^1.
```

But this open set is not stable under `r -> r+1`, since `r=-1` maps to zero. To obtain an invariant open set in characteristic zero one must remove

```text
..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...,
```

which is not the complement of a finite algebraic divisor. In characteristic p the translation orbit of zero is all of `F_p`, so deleting the full singular orbit deletes every rational base point.

The regular campaign window `1 <= r <= p-2` avoids the singular step, but it is only a partial orbit. Definition 0 asks for a genuine fixed algebraic clock operation, not a partially defined map whose bad fiber is reached just outside the observation window.

### 1.3 Blowups have growing complexity

For the h-step product,

```text
det T_h(r) = product_{j=0}^{h-1} (r+j)^6.
```

The singular fibers and their inverse counterparts therefore move through the translated clock values

```text
r = 0,-1,...,-h+1.
```

Resolving the h-th iterate on the obvious rational-surface models requires exceptional information along a set whose size is `Omega(h)`. In characteristic p, regularizing the complete cyclic clock encounters the entire p-cycle.

This gives a concrete geometric explanation of the same phenomenon seen in Q6629: the lost clock information has not disappeared; it has moved into a growing exceptional divisor configuration.

I do not claim a theorem excluding every possible hidden-state automorphism on every variety of dimension at most four. What is proved here is narrower and exact:

```text
- the direct surface is not an automorphism;
- no finite translation-stable localization repairs it;
- the natural birational regularizations have complexity growing with h or p.
```

An arbitrary automorphic extension with extra hidden coordinates remains the least-settled logical possibility.

## 2. Exhaustion of the natural ways to absorb the clock drift

There are six natural algebraic absorption mechanisms. Five are closed or reduced; the sixth is the residual problem.

### 2.1 Add the affine clock

This gives the exact birational map `sigma` above. It has fixed per-step degree and linear iterate degree, but it is not an automorphism because `det T(r)=r^6` is not a unit.

### 2.2 Replace the affine clock by a fixed finite base

A fixed residue class `r mod L` cannot determine `P(r)` and `r^6` modulo every varying prime p. If two integers differ by L, their polynomial coefficients are not universally equal modulo p. Exact periodization therefore requires a base of size at least p, which is precisely the uncompressed low digit.

### 2.3 Gauge-conjugate the cocycle to a constant matrix

A rational gauge would require a shift coboundary. The determinant normalization already forces a relation of the form

```text
f(X+1)/f(X) = X^6/c,
```

and Q6629's divisor/coboundary argument rules out a rational solution. The same obstruction survives finite rational gauge extensions unless one introduces an infinite translation orbit of zeros and poles. The required normalizer is gamma-like, not algebraic of bounded complexity.

### 2.4 Normalize on a finite algebraic cover

Adjoining a bounded number of roots can change multiplicities but cannot turn the infinite translated divisor of `X^6` into a finite shift coboundary. A finite cover therefore does not make the determinant a constant unit under translation.

### 2.5 Resolve by compactification and blowup

This produces the Jonquieres surface picture, but the translated singular fibers generate linearly many exceptional classes. It is a slow-growth birational model, not a fixed-complexity automorphism model.

### 2.6 Use a p-typical finite clock

This succeeds exactly at finite level. The shift becomes inner after cyclic closure modulo `p^k`, and the carry is Cartier-recursive. However the finite module has size `p^k`, and its low digit has width p. This is the construction in Section 4.

Thus all natural full-clock absorptions reduce to the following dichotomy:

```text
Either retain the affine clock and accept a lambda-one birational map with
linearly growing conductor,

or retain the finite p-typical clock and accept a matrix algebra whose rank
grows with p^k.
```

The only possible bounded quotient must forget almost the entire clock algebra while preserving exactly the collision zero observable.

## 3. Does lambda=1 give useful finite-field cancellation?

### 3.1 What lambda=1 gives immediately

For each fixed h, every rational function or curve obtained from the h-step cocycle has degree and conductor `O(h)`, rather than exponential in h. Consequently a complete additive-character sum has the schematic bound

```text
|sum_{r in F_p} psi(F_h(r))| <= C h sqrt(p),
```

provided `F_h` is not Artin-Schreier-degenerate and after accounting for its poles. Likewise the point-count error for a curve built from the h-th iterate is `O(h sqrt(p))` or the corresponding genus-linear variant.

This is useful bookkeeping, but it is exactly the existing linear-conductor regime. Summing such estimates independently over `h <= H` costs an extra power of H. The dynamical organization does not by itself create cancellation between different h.

### 3.2 The closest slow-degree results

The relevant literature is real but does not presently close this problem.

- Ostafe and Shparlinski study triangular multivariate polynomial systems whose iterate degrees grow polynomially, and use high moments plus Weil bounds to estimate exponential sums along their orbits. Their 2010 Math. Comp. paper explicitly identifies dynamical degree one as the slow-growth setting.

- For a system in `m+1` variables, the single-orbit consequence stated in that paper becomes nontrivial only for orbit segments roughly beyond `p^(m+1/2+epsilon)`. For a two-coordinate surface, this is beyond `p^(3/2+epsilon)`, whereas the within-digit clock supplies only p steps.

- Ostafe's permutation-polynomial results obtain stronger statements on average over all initial values. Our campaign needs one highly structured Apéry orbit, not an average over the full state space.

- Ostafe, Pelican, and Shparlinski give bounds for rather general polynomial dynamical systems when the orbit is sufficiently long. Again, the length-p structured orbit is outside the favorable range.

- Ostafe and Shparlinski treat triangular rational systems and obtain degree-growth, linear-independence, and period results. Gomez-Perez, Ostafe, and Shparlinski explicitly study slow algebraic entropy and rational automorphisms for pseudorandom generation. These are the closest conceptual references, but our map fails the global automorphism/permutation hypothesis at the singular clock fiber.

The literature therefore supports the slogan

```text
slow degree growth can improve orbit sums when combined with long-period,
permutation, averaging, or iterate-independence hypotheses,
```

not the stronger slogan

```text
lambda=1 alone gives square-root cancellation uniformly for a single
length-p nonautonomous projective cocycle.
```

I found no theorem, through the current literature check, that turns the lambda-one property alone into the family-averaged cancellation required here for `h` growing as a power of p.

### 3.3 Why Bourgain-Gamburd is not a black-box substitute

Bourgain-Gamburd expansion concerns random walks or words in a fixed finite generating set whose reductions generate a non-elementary or Zariski-dense subgroup of `SL_2(F_p)`.

Our product is

```text
T(r+h-1) ... T(r),
```

with a deterministically changing generator at every time. It is not a random walk on a fixed Cayley graph. Moreover, before normalization the determinants are `u^6`; after projectivization the sequence remains a rigid polynomial word indexed by consecutive clock values. Expansion of a fixed Cayley graph does not imply that this one prescribed word equidistributes, nor that its specific projective matrix coefficient has few zeros.

A useful Bourgain-Gamburd-style theorem would have to be a new nonstationary expansion statement for polynomially parameterized matrix products, with control of a prescribed matrix coefficient. No such theorem is currently available as a direct input.

### 3.4 The sharp analytic reformulation

The lambda-one route is best summarized by the following new target. For an additive character `psi` and the h-step collision function `Delta_{r,h}`, seek a mean-square estimate such as

```text
sum_{1 <= h <= H} |sum_r psi(t Delta_{r,h})|^2
    << p^(1+epsilon) H
```

uniformly for `t != 0` and in a mesoscopic range of H.

Individual Weil gives only the bound obtained by inserting `O(h sqrt(p))` term by term. Any estimate of the displayed strength must exploit correlation between consecutive iterates of the same lambda-one cocycle. That theorem would bypass clock compression rather than construct it.

So the slow-dynamics viewpoint is legitimate and potentially valuable, but it is not currently a known theorem package.

## 4. [FINITE-CARTIER-LOCAL-DUAL]: exact construction

This section gives the positive finite-level theorem.

### 4.1 Finite jets and the unilateral defect

Let

```text
R_k = Z/p^k Z,
N = p^k,
J_k = direct_sum_{n=0}^{N-1} R_k e_n.
```

Think of `e_n` either as the monomial `t^n` in the truncated jet algebra or as the coefficient functional at index n, with the orientation chosen below.

The unilateral shift is

```text
L e_n = e_{n+1}       for 0 <= n < N-1,
L e_{N-1} = 0.
```

It is nilpotent and noninvertible. Therefore it cannot itself define an inner automorphism of `End_Rk(J_k)`: conjugation is always implemented by a unit.

Now introduce the cyclic shift

```text
U e_n = e_{n+1 mod N},
```

and the finite clock operator

```text
Theta e_n = n e_n.
```

A direct basis calculation gives the exact integral identity

```text
U Theta U^(-1) = Theta - I + N E_0,
```

where `E_0` is the rank-one projector onto `R_k e_0`.

Since `N=p^k=0` in `R_k`, this becomes

```text
Ad_U(Theta) = U Theta U^(-1) = Theta - I.
```

This is exactly the missing inner-shift identity. It fails on finite jets in characteristic zero by the boundary term `N E_0`; it becomes exact modulo `p^k` when the cyclic length is a multiple of `p^k`.

The unilateral and cyclic shifts differ only by the boundary arrow:

```text
L = U (I-E_{N-1}).
```

Consequently, for every nonwrapping pair `n+h<N`,

```text
L^h e_n = U^h e_n.
```

Thus cyclic innerization is exact on every regular campaign window, and its only false data are the wraparound entries, which can be removed by a window projector.

### 4.2 Exact Cartier carry recursion

Split the index into its low digit and high part:

```text
n = p q + a,
0 <= a < p,
0 <= q < p^(k-1).
```

Let

```text
V_a e_q = e_{pq+a},
C_a e_{pq+b} = delta_{a,b} e_q.
```

Then

```text
C_a V_b = delta_{a,b} I,
sum_{a=0}^{p-1} V_a C_a = I.
```

The one-step cyclic clock has the exact carry formula

```text
U_k = sum_{a=0}^{p-2} V_{a+1} C_a
      + V_0 U_{k-1} C_{p-1}.
```

Equivalently,

```text
U_k V_a = V_{a+1}                  for a<p-1,
U_k V_{p-1} = V_0 U_{k-1}.
```

The clock itself decomposes as

```text
Theta_k = sum_{a=0}^{p-1} V_a (a I + p Theta_{k-1}) C_a.
```

Also,

```text
C_a U_k^p V_b = delta_{a,b} U_{k-1}.
```

This is the exact relation between the within-digit clock and the verified p-dilation tower:

```text
- U_k is low-digit increment plus one carry edge;
- U_k^p descends to the high-digit shift;
- Cartier digit extraction sees the carry explicitly.
```

This is a precise form of `[FINITE-CARTIER-LOCAL-DUAL]`.

### 4.3 The finite Weyl algebra at level one

For k=1, work over `F_p` with `N=p`. The operators satisfy

```text
U^p = I,
Ad_U(Theta)=Theta-I.
```

The eigenvalues of Theta are all elements of `F_p`. The polynomial

```text
E_a = I - (Theta-a I)^(p-1)
```

is the rank-one projector onto `F_p e_a`. Hence

```text
U^(i-a) E_a
```

is the matrix unit sending `e_a` to `e_i` and killing the other basis vectors. Therefore

```text
F_p[U,Theta] = End_Fp(F_p^p) = M_p(F_p).
```

This recovers Q6629's finite Weyl obstruction in its cleanest finite-clock form. The inner shift exists, but it exists inside the full p-dimensional irreducible clock representation.

At higher k, Theta alone no longer separates all p-adic digits by bounded-degree polynomial projectors. The Cartier maps `C_a,V_a` supply the missing digit idempotents recursively. Iterating the construction recovers the full `p^k`-state clock, not a fixed state space.

### 4.4 Autonomous finite transfer operator

The nonautonomous Apéry transfer can now be written as one operator. Define

```text
A_k = (U_k tensor I_2) T(Theta_k)
```

on `J_k tensor R_k^2`, where

```text
T(Theta_k) = [[P(Theta_k), -Theta_k^6],[I,0]].
```

Then

```text
A_k(e_n tensor v) = e_{n+1} tensor T(n)v,
```

and induction gives

```text
A_k^h(e_n tensor v)
  = e_{n+h} tensor T(n+h-1)...T(n)v
```

with cyclic indices. On a nonwrapping regular window this is exactly the original transfer product.

The clock part is invertible and inner. The full transfer operator is not globally invertible because `T(n)` is singular when p divides n. This is the finite-algebra version of the singular fiber at `r=0`. The p-typical depth tower organizes those singular layers, but it does not eliminate the p-wide low digit.

### 4.5 Exact collision observable

Let

```text
B_k = diag(b_0,...,b_{N-1}),
C_k = diag(c_0,...,c_{N-1}).
```

Define

```text
Omega_{h,k}
  = B_k (U_k^(-h) C_k U_k^h)
    - C_k (U_k^(-h) B_k U_k^h).
```

It is diagonal, and its n-th diagonal entry is

```text
(Omega_{h,k})_{n,n}
  = b_n c_{n+h mod N} - c_n b_{n+h mod N}.
```

Let

```text
W_{h,k} = sum_{n=0}^{N-h-1} E_n
```

be the nonwrapping window projector. Then for `0 <= n < N-h`,

```text
(W_{h,k} Omega_{h,k})_{n,n} = Delta_{n,h}.
```

On the regular Apéry window, where the projective vectors are nonzero,

```text
pi(n)=pi(n+h)
  <=>
(W_{h,k} Omega_{h,k})_{n,n}=0.
```

Thus the projective collision observable has an exact finite inner-shift realization. No information is missing at finite level.

## 5. Why the finite construction is not Definition 0

The construction has two very different complexity measures.

### 5.1 Succinct recursive complexity

The clock has:

```text
Cartier depth:          k,
carry rank:             1 exceptional carry edge,
transfer gate degree:   6,
iterate degree:         O(h).
```

If digit split, carry, and p-way selection are treated as primitive operations, the formulas have depth `O(k)` and bounded local algebraic degree. This is a very compact circuit description.

### 5.2 Ambient algebraic complexity

The actual state module and endomorphism algebra have ranks

```text
rank J_k = p^k,
rank End(J_k) = p^(2k).
```

At k=1 the low-digit clock already generates `M_p(F_p)`. Therefore any faithful linear quotient of the complete clock algebra has dimension at least p.

This is the precise distinction:

```text
small recursive description != small conductor.
```

Deligne/Weil-type bounds see sheaf rank, degree, Swan data, or geometric conductor. They do not currently accept a p-wide matrix algebra merely because it is generated by a short carry circuit.

A possible replacement notion is `p^k-Cartier circuit complexity`: count depth, local polynomial degree, carry/bond rank, and the number of digit selectors. For the clock this complexity is small in depth but not uniform in p unless the entire p-valued digit register is granted as one primitive affine coordinate. Granting that primitive returns us to the lambda-one surface and its singular fiber.

No existing point-counting theorem is known whose constants depend only on this recursive circuit description and not on the p-wide representation or the resulting geometric conductor.

## 6. The sharp residual quotient

The full finite Weyl-Cartier algebra cannot have a fixed faithful quotient. Definition 0 does not ask for faithfulness; it asks only to preserve one zero observable. Therefore the surviving target must be stated as an observable-only factor problem.

### [OBSERVABLE-ONLY-WEYL-FACTOR]

Let

```text
U_{p,k}, Theta_{p,k}, B_{p,k}, C_{p,k}, Omega_{h,k}
```

be the finite cyclic clock, clock multiplier, Apéry diagonal solutions, and collision operator above, with the verified p-typical depth maps between levels.

Does there exist a fixed finite-type variety Q, fixed bounded-degree maps S and R on Q, a fixed regular/rational observable zeta, and compatible maps from the Apéry finite Cartier states to Q, all independent of p,k,h,r, such that on every regular nonwrapping tuple

```text
zeta(R^r S^h q_{p,k}) = 0
  <=>
(Omega_{h,k})_{r,r} = 0
  <=>
N_h(r)=0 mod p?
```

Compatibility must include both clocks:

```text
S models the p-dilation/depth transition,
R models the low-digit cyclic shift and its carry.
```

Crucially, the quotient need not represent `U` or `Theta` faithfully. It must preserve only the zero set of the single alternating matrix coefficient.

This formulation is sharper than the original abstract Definition 0 because the source object is now explicit and finite. There is no longer any ambiguity about what must be compressed.

### 6.1 A concrete refutation certificate

For computation and a possible negative theorem, define the observable-generated algebra/module from the diagonal words

```text
omega_h(r) = (Omega_{h,1})_{r,r}
```

on the regular low-digit window. Close these words under translation by U and under the Cartier maps coming from higher levels.

A strong negative certificate would be:

```text
for infinitely many p, the smallest translation-Cartier-stable algebra
that preserves the zero loci of the omega_h has complexity Omega(p).
```

An easier linear certificate is growth of the translate span or of the associated Hankel/circulant rank. That would kill all fixed-rank linear and tensor-Cartier quotients, though not every nonlinear variety. A stronger certificate is that a bounded collection of collision diagonals separates the p clock positions and hence generates the full diagonal algebra; adjoining U then recovers `M_p(F_p)`.

### 6.2 A concrete positive certificate

A positive answer would consist of fixed algebraic coordinates

```text
q = q(p-typical boundary state, low digit state)
```

and fixed maps S,R,zeta such that the displayed equivalence holds. It is not necessary to reconstruct the full projective orbit or clock. Any exceptional collapse of the collision determinant alone is sufficient.

This is the only remaining loophole in the finite Weyl obstruction.

## 7. Final verdict

### Task 1: Weyl-algebra escape hatch

The affine-clock skew product is a valid autonomous birational realization of dimension two and has dynamical degree one with linear degree growth. It is not an honest automorphism. The determinant-zero clock fiber is the exact obstruction, and natural localizations or blowups have unbounded orbit complexity.

The slow-degree finite-field literature confirms that lambda one is analytically better than exponential degree growth, but no existing theorem supplies the family-averaged cancellation needed here for a single length-p Apéry orbit. Individual Bombieri-Weil estimates remain linear in h. Bourgain-Gamburd expansion is not directly applicable.

### Task 2: [FINITE-CARTIER-LOCAL-DUAL]

Yes, after cyclic closure at length `N=p^k`, the clock shift is inner:

```text
Ad_U(Theta)=Theta-I mod p^k.
```

The discrepancy from the unilateral jet ladder is a rank-one boundary arrow. The exact Cartier carry recursion is

```text
U_k = sum_{a=0}^{p-2} V_{a+1}C_a + V_0 U_{k-1}C_{p-1}.
```

The exact collision observable is the diagonal operator

```text
Omega_{h,k}
  = B_k Ad_{U_k^(-h)}(C_k)
    - C_k Ad_{U_k^(-h)}(B_k).
```

This completely constructs the finite local dual.

What replaces bounded conductor is only a bounded-depth Cartier/carry circuit with p-wide local digit and ambient rank `p^k`. There is currently no point-counting theory whose constants depend only on this succinct recursive description.

### Task 3: single sharpest next question

The next round should attack exactly this and nothing broader:

```text
[OBSERVABLE-ONLY-WEYL-FACTOR]

Does the scalar zero observable

    (Omega_{h,k})_{r,r} = 0

factor through a fixed algebraic quotient of the finite Weyl-Cartier tower,
uniformly in p,k,h,r, even though the complete clock algebra is M_p at the
first digit?
```

The first computational diagnostic should measure the translation-Cartier rank and the observable-generated diagonal algebra for the Apéry collision words. If they grow like p, the bounded linear/tensor Cartier program is dead and any surviving quotient must be genuinely nonlinear. If they remain bounded or stabilize, they directly expose the candidate quotient coordinates.

## 8. Least-confident step and blind spot

### Least-confident step

The least-confident step is the extrapolation from the explicit singular-fiber/blowup analysis to the belief that no hidden automorphic extension of dimension at most four exists. The direct skew product is certainly not an automorphism, and its natural regularizations have growing exceptional complexity. But I have not proved a universal no-lift theorem saying that it cannot be a factor of some unrelated fixed automorphism carrying extra hidden coordinates.

That missing theorem would have to exclude a reversible algebraic extension that stores exactly the information lost at the determinant-zero fiber without accumulating unbounded history.

### Blind spot

The main blind spot is observable-specific exceptional structure. The finite Weyl algebra is full, but the collision determinant is one alternating matrix coefficient, not a generic observable. It could conceivably lie in a very small Cartier-stable nonlinear factor even though the ambient clock representation is irreducible and p-dimensional.

A secondary blind spot is specialized arithmetic-dynamics literature on deterministic nonstationary `PGL_2` cocycles or arithmetic D-modules/crystalline Cartier modules. The broad slow-degree and expansion literature does not solve the needed estimate, but a specialized theorem about trace functions attached to Jonquieres cocycles could have been missed.
