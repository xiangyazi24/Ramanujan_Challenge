## 1.3 “No finite piece” is not one condition

Three different obstructions must be separated.

# 4. What is and is not excluded by the concrete geometry

## 4.1 Excluded now

- Kummer constituents: impossible because the geometric rank-three representations are irreducible and have non-scalar monodromy at \alpha,\beta and nontrivial unipotent monodromy at 0,\infty.

- Punctual objects: the middle extensions have positive generic rank and nonzero generic Mellin dimension.

- Negligible rank-one objects: the input is not geometrically a Kummer sheaf.

- Unbounded-order Kummer self-twists: local monodromy at 0 already forces the twisting scalar to be 1. The quadratic K_q relating A_+ and A_- is not a Kummer sheaf on \mathbf G_m; it is ramified at \alpha,\beta.

## 4.2 Not excluded by the stated data

- A finite Mellin Tannakian group.

- A torus-normalizer/imprimitive Mellin group.

- The \mathrm{Sym}^3SL_2 subgroup inside Sp_4 for A_-.

- A nontrivial arithmetic component group.

“Many observed Mellin trace values” falsifies small finite candidates numerically, but does not prove infinitude. The theorem-level exclusions must come from Chapters 8 and 16 or from exact moment calculations.

# 5. Pointwise O_3 versus Mellin G_+: the precise separation

There are two tensor categories.

1. The ordinary tensor category generated by A_+ on the t-line. Its fibre is a rank-three stalk and its geometric group is O_3 with determinant K_q.

1. The multiplicative-convolution category generated by the perverse object N_+=j_*A_+[1]. Its fibre functor is generic Mellin cohomology of dimension two, and its group is a subgroup of GL_2.

They are not the same category, representation, or fibre functor. Accordingly:

What passes from the first computation to the second is only specific information:

- local inertia invariants \Rightarrow Euler–Poincaré dimension;

- purity of A_+ \Rightarrow weight of Mellin cohomology;

- pointwise orthogonal duality plus inversion symmetry \Rightarrow convolution autoduality and sign;

- pointwise irreducibility/non-Kummer structure \Rightarrow generic irreducibility and absence of negligible pieces;

- Kummer self-twist and power-map information \Rightarrow Chapter 8 connectedness tests;

- explicit trace function \Rightarrow moment varieties through (1.1).

The disconnected determinant K_q may influence the determinant or component character of the Mellin cohomology through global epsilon factors. It does not say that G_+ is disconnected, and because K_q is not Kummer it does not become a translation \chi\mapsto\chi\eta on the character group. The arithmetic Mellin determinant must be computed separately.

# Least-confident step

The least-confident mathematical step is not the Euler–Poincaré computation; that part is rigid. It is the autoduality upgrade from the orthogonal pointwise systems to a signed self-duality of the actual convolution objects with their Weil structures. The alternating sign on degree-one cohomology is clear once the object-level inversion/duality isomorphism exists, but the supplied local monodromy data do not by themselves construct that isomorphism. It should be proved from the Fricke/inversion geometry before the inclusions in Sp_2 and Sp_4 are treated as theorems.
