## 7.2 The large sieve is an L^2 theorem, not an L^\infty theorem

Formula (2) says that large spikes occupy little total mass. It does not say there are no spikes. A single value of size X/\log X is compatible with the second-moment bound.

Removing all exceptions would require one of:

- a deterministic codegree bound for intersections across distinct primes;

- higher-order correlation bounds uniform in all prime tuples;

- a horizontal compatible-system or reciprocity law constraining the roots;

- or a pointwise large-prime-divisor theorem for the Apéry numbers.
