# 14. Final assessment

The product of same-index jump gcds admits exact and useful algebraic packaging:

- as a Möbius quotient of adjacent cumulative gcds;

- as a first Smith divisor of a CRT-index row;

- as the prime-to-small zeroth Fitting divisor of an explicit graded tensor matrix; and

- through a rank-two family of saturated coefficient minors.

None of these representations supplies o(n) from the presently proved inputs. The obstruction is exact:

- radical jumps give one selective copy;

- raw low/high profiles give at most two selective copies;

- telescoping controls only the total low and high masses, not their diagonal overlap;

- a formal system satisfying every content-level theorem has diagonal mass n/6+o(n).

Therefore the current result is still exact support purification only. The missing theorem is the diagonal-jump entropy estimate (DJ), or an equally strong Apéry-specific cancellation outside the cutoff-content calculus.
