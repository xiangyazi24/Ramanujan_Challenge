# 4. Why the exceptional-set theorem does not propagate

The density theorem implies that only O(X^{2/3+o(1)}) indices in a dyadic shell can carry logarithmic bad-prime weight comparable to X. This does not constrain a fixed high-degree star.

Suppose hypothetically that

The quantifiers are therefore incompatible:

- density one controls how many vertices are heavy;

- the star argument produces many light vertices adjacent to one heavy vertex;

- no theorem presently forces heaviness, or even growing degree, to propagate from the center to its leaves.

Iterating the same reflection does not help: the edge involution sends m_p straight back to n.
