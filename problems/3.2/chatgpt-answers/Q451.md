# 9. Final audit

The actual unresolved phenomenon is therefore extremely narrow:

For a fixed integer n, can the distinguished Apéry direction hit the residual Mellin zero at the single high-order character \omega_p^{n-1} for a positive proportion of primes p\in(n/2,n]?

All known same-prime identities allow such a rainbow alignment. Proving that it does not occur requires either the horizontal second-Fourier estimate (HF) or a genuinely new low-height integer identity such as (IG).
