## 1.3 Cobham does not couple different primes

Cobham's theorem concerns one fixed finite-alphabet sequence which is automatic in two fixed multiplicatively independent bases. Here both the reduction and the base vary with p. There is therefore no Cobham conclusion across primes.

The supercongruences modulo p^k similarly govern lifting and higher-digit propagation. They can show that a zero, once present, has strong descendants; they do not count the one-digit seeds in Z_p.
