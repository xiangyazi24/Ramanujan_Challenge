## Failure 3: principal-subresultant coefficient products

These encode the number of prefix zeros. Multiplying or taking gcds of them reintroduces vertical zero-count information and unmarked saturation. Saturating by the first nonzero principal coefficient returns the marked ideal (F_H(n),N_H(n)).

## Failure 4: recurrence-only simplification

The four-step recurrence reduces storage and proves holonomicity, but no known Bézout identity turns F_H(n) into an integer of sublinear height while preserving all candidate factors.

## Failure 5: comparison with Padé family

The fixed pair and growing family have equal candidate-window support, not a proved integer divisibility. Universal Smith/interpolation algebra cannot convert support equality into a small gcd.
