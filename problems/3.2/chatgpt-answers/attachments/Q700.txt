## 5. Exact limitation of the single-sublevel strategy

For a fixed n, Fekete's theorem gives:

- if t<\ell_n, then \operatorname{cap}E_n(t)<1, so only finitely many complete conjugate sets of algebraic integers can lie entirely in E_n(t);

- if t>\ell_n, the construction in Section 4 gives arbitrarily large degrees.

Thus \ell_n is the exact fixed-n threshold for the all-conjugates-in-one-sublevel strategy.

There is, however, a crucial quantifier warning:

Finiteness for each fixed n does not give a uniform degree bound as n changes.

It therefore does not rule out one exceptional high-degree polynomial P_n inside a subcritical moving lemniscate for every n. A negative solution of Q700 needs a quantitative Fekete theorem uniform in the moving degree-n lemniscates, or the stronger arithmetic equilibrium inequality of Section 8.
