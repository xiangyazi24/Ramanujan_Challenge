## 9. What remains genuinely open

The following routes are closed within the stated classes:

- fixed-order recurrence and adjoint combinations: Section 2;

- mod-p^2 reflection/Hermite matching: Section 3;

- ordinary Newton interpolation: Section 4;

- nonnegative Newton–Padé denominators: Section 5.2;

- full-degree arbitrary Padé compression: Sections 6–7, because it becomes the exact moving-zero gcd.

The surviving non-tautological possibilities are:

### A. Signed square or mildly overdetermined Newton–Padé cancellation

Find degrees a_n+b_n=H+O(1) and an integral pair satisfying

with

The exact computations make this look difficult, but they do not prove impossibility for arbitrary signed growing degree.

### B. An Apéry-specific holonomic gcd theorem

Prove (8.3) using the exact recurrence (8.2), its formal adjoint, and the prime-power factorization of its transfer matrices. A generic P-recursive gcd theorem is unavailable.

### C. A genuinely second local condition

Find a residue independent of A_j that must vanish at every bad direct prime. Reflection and the first derivative do not supply it; Q715 rules out that candidate experimentally and algebraically.

Any successful route must leave the rank-one local profile. Rearranging the existing recurrence, Newton values, or cutoff contents does not do so.
