# 14. What the small exact data do and do not prove

The supplied computations through H=30 show that adjacent and triple gcds can be very small, including for interior b. This is evidence for a genuine arithmetic gcd phenomenon. It is not explained by the adjacent identity:

- small \gcd(\kappa_b,\kappa_{b+1}) only removes good-prime pollution;

- even \gcd(\kappa_b,\ldots,\kappa_{b+r-1})=1 gives only G_{b,r}\mid B_n;

- the target primes are precisely selected prime factors of B_n.

No exact computation, however long, proves that the selected part of B_n has sublinear logarithmic weight.
