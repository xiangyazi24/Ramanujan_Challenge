# 11. Recurrence audit

The latest main commit contains one verified Apéry-specific finite-difference identity for the Newton table entries. It is a 12-term scalar stencil obtained by applying finite differences to the cubic Apéry recurrence.

The working-note audit retracts the claimed continuation from that scalar stencil to recurrences for saturated maximal minors. Determinant multilinearity applies to an entire row or column, whereas the scalar stencil moves one entry with coefficients depending on the column index. No common displacement identity
