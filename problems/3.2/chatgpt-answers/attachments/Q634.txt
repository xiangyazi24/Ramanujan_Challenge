# Final conclusion

The fixed-n shell idea survives as a useful exact reformulation, but not as the requested pair amplifier.

1. The weighted shell in the question is not a hit carrier; the exact carrier is the unweighted 22-shell sum.

1. That carrier has an explicit rational generating function and the exact hypergeometric/Ore system (5.3), (5.7), and (5.10).

1. At two moving primes, Q476 leaves exactly two independent quotient residues.

1. The universal pair ideal is (0.5). Every formal resultant gets the factor (m+1)(m-h+1), and after normalization no additional vanishing remains.

1. Any nonuniversal fixed-sign shell certificate has full N-scale entropy. A signed low-height certificate would require a new cross-prime quotient identity, not more creative telescoping.
