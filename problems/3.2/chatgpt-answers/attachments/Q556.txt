But the ordinary integer polynomials have nominal degree up to n=3p+r. To recover (12.1) one must choose the principal subresultant indexed by the p-dependent effective degree 3p.

This produces two fatal problems:

1. the required subresultant index depends on the unknown candidate prime;

1. the standard height bound for the resulting determinant is \exp(O(n^2)), far larger than the desired \exp(o(n)).

Thus the degree-growing resultant idea does identify a selective local invariant, but not one global low-height integer.
