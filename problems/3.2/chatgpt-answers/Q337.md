## Credible proof direction

Effective Chebotarev/discriminant estimates are not a credible route to (5.1). A proof must use information that is invisible to fixed-field splitting theory, such as:

- a two-variable finite-field description of the entire moving family ((h,x)mapsto N_h(x)) with complexity controlled when (hasympsqrt p);

- strong restrictions on actual Apéry-return roots inside the ambient roots of (N_h);

- a resultant/common-root mechanism which treats the rare high-root primes separately;

- or a trace/monodromy description in the gap variable with conductor subpolynomial in (p).

The numerical law (S_p(H)sim2.5H) may remain correct for ordinary computational-size primes and even for most primes. It cannot be promoted to a uniform all-((p,H)) theorem. The rare completely split primes are a rigorous obstruction, and a pointwise theorem must formulate its scale so that those fixed-(H) exceptions are absorbed rather than denied.
