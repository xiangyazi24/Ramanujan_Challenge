K_{a+g}(X)

N_a(x)=0,
N_g(x+a+1)=0

N_a(x)=0 and N_g(x+a+1)=0
=>
N_{a+g+1}(x)=0.

polynomial identity.

forced. Extension-field roots of N_2(X)=P(X+1) give no identity with

gcd(N_a(X),N_g(X+a)) | gcd(N_a(X),N_{a+g}(X)).

gcd(N_a(X),N_g(X+a+1)) | gcd(N_a(X),N_{a+g+1}(X)).

Hence the shifted family must be reindexed:

sum deg gcd(N_a,N_g(.-a-1))
controls the same-base family
sum deg gcd(N_a,N_{a+g+1}).

Errors only come from:
- cut-edge roots where the cleared denominator vanishes;
- degree drops modulo p;
- roots at infinity after projective compactification.
