## 2.6 Why published “family” results do not supply (BV-R) — CITED

### (i) Number-field Bombieri–Vinogradov

Murty–Petersen, A Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem for all number fields, and Jiang–Lü–Wang, A Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem for number fields, fix a number field/Chebotarev condition and average over arithmetic-progression moduli or related prime variables. They do not average over a deterministic sequence of growing-degree root fields K_h.

### (ii) Large sieve for Frobenius

Kowalski's The large sieve, monodromy and zeta functions of curves treats Frobenius in a fixed algebraic family with controlled monodromy and bounded geometric complexity. Here the permutation degree and field conductor grow linearly/quadratically with h; no fixed-rank compatible system parametrized by h has been constructed.

### (iii) Effective Chebotarev for families of fields

Pierce–Turnage-Butterbaugh–Wood, An effective Chebotarev density theorem for families of number fields, prove strong theorems for almost all fields in appropriate counting families. Their averaging variable is a large family of fields ordered by discriminant, with field-counting and zero-density hypotheses. The Apéry problem supplies one deterministic field for each h, with degree changing with h; there is no ambient field-counting family in which the exceptional fields can be discarded.

### (iv) “N_h is a specialization of a fixed recurrence-defined F” is not enough

There cannot be a fixed polynomial

with

because \deg_XF(h,X)\le\deg_XF is bounded, while \deg_XN_h=3(h-1) tends to infinity. The recurrence is fixed, but its transfer-product length is h. It is an algorithmic/holonomic family of growing complexity, not a bounded-degree algebraic specialization family.

### (v) Linear disjointness is helpful but not sufficient

Pairwise linear disjointness of the splitting fields would help turn mixed Frobenius classes into product classes. It would not by itself prove (BV-R): one still needs uniform control of the degrees and discriminants of composita and a zero-density/large-sieve theorem whose constants survive those composita. Pairwise-coprime discriminants are not established and are implausible literally because the recurrence has persistent small-prime ramification.

### A sufficient new analytic package

A plausible route to (BV-R) would require all of:

1. \kappa_h=O(1);

1. explicit maximal or bounded-index wreath-product Galois groups;

1. controlled intersections of the root fields as h varies;

1. an average zero-density theorem for the associated Artin/Dedekind L-functions with constants uniform in the growing degree;

1. an average conductor bound strong enough to save the X^{1/6} missing at H=X^{1/3}.

No published theorem currently packages these hypotheses for this recurrence family.
