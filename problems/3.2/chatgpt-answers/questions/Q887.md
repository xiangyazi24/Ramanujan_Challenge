Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read latest main commit 9688ca1,
especially Q867, Q885, working-note Sections 159--162, and
q32_pade_zero_absorber.py.

Q867 proves for the primitive numerator-degree-one Padé pair that, for
every prime p>H,

  v_p(content(P_{H,1})) =
  the second-largest value among {v_p(A_s):0<=s<=H}.

There is a natural general theorem. For the primitive integral pair

  deg P_{H,a}<=a, deg Q_{H,a}<=H-a,
  P_{H,a}(s)=A_s Q_{H,a}(s), 0<=s<=H,

conjecture

  v_p(content(P_{H,a})) = e_(a+1),

where e_(1)>=...>=e_(H+1) are the ordered valuations v_p(A_s).
Exact computation already verifies this for every 2<=H<=24, every prime
H<p<=3H+1, and every 0<=a<=H (2640 cases).

Prove or refute this at full p-adic valuation strength. Work over Z_p and
track the primitive normalization of the whole (P,Q) vector; a mod-p root
count proves only the exponent-one shadow and is not enough. Seek an exact
Smith/lattice interpolation lemma valid for arbitrary nonzero node values
u_s with distinct nodes modulo p. State all normality hypotheses and show
they hold for the Apéry pair.

If true, derive the sharp p-adic form of the zero-absorber theorem after
dividing the numerator by its content: describe its roots and residual
valuations at nodes in terms of the order statistics. Then determine
whether this gives a genuinely smaller candidate-window carrier or an
explicit recurrence-specific lemma capable of bounding the growing-family
gcd. Be hostile: a theorem that only repackages |Z_p|>a is useful locally
but does not prove o(H). Record exactly what new global estimate would
still be needed.

Put formulas in fenced plain-text blocks, not Notion equation blocks.
