```plain text
p_j | A_j.
```

Thus the Golyshev--Zagier entire interpolation A(z), or simply the integer sequence A_j, has the property that A_j/(n-j) is an integer at every selected node. Investigate whether many such integer-value nodes can be ruled out by a quantitative interpolation determinant, divided-difference, Pólya/Carlson theorem, auxiliary-function/Siegel lemma, or analytic zero estimate. This must be pointwise for every n.

Every use of integrality must be exact: ordinary divided differences of arbitrary integer values are only rational, and the selected nodes are irregular.
