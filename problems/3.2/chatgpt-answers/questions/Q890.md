Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read latest main commit 9688ca1,
Q867, Q885, and working-note Sections 160--163.

We have now proved for every p>H and every numerator degree a that

  v_p(content(P_{H,a})) =
  the (a+1)-st largest of {v_p(A_s):0<=s<=H}.

Let

  Z_{H,a}(x)=P_{H,a}(x)/content(P_{H,a})

be the globally primitive numerator polynomial. If a target prime
p=3H+1-j>2H has at most a prefix zeros, then p does not divide the removed
content and Z_{H,a}(j)=Z_{H,a}(3H+1)=0 mod p. Thus, once a is above the
vertical zero-count threshold, every target prime is a common-root prime
for a whole tail of the projective polynomials Z_{H,a}.

Attack this common-root formulation. Compute exact resultants,
subresultants, or a simultaneous Bezout/content ideal for

  Z_{H,a}, Z_{H,a+1}, ..., Z_{H,A},

with A about H^(2/3). Seek a nonzero integer divisible by every target
prime whose logarithmic height is o(H), or a strictly smaller explicit
Apéry-specific resultant lemma. Use the Apéry recurrence to simplify the
projective coefficients or the subresultant chain. Track all removed
contents exactly; raw Padé determinants are invalid here.

Q885 already proves that generic interpolation/Smith/resultant identities
admit a full-primorial CRT countermodel. Therefore do not return a generic
Bezout bound or merely say that every target gives a common root. A valid
positive step must use an identity special to the Apéry sequence and
include an actual height estimate. If the route saturates, compute the
first exact saturation factor and prove why the projective resultant
retains linear height. Test claims in exact small cases and record every
failure mechanism. Put formulas in fenced plain-text blocks rather than
Notion equation blocks.
