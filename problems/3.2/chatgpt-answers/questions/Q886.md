Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read the latest main commit of
xiangyazi24/Ramanujan_Challenge, especially working-note Section 160 and
q32_pade_zero_absorber.py. Work on an exact algebraic subproblem, distinct
from a generic height attack.

For each 0<=a<=H let (P_{H,a},Q_{H,a}) be the primitive integral generator
of the rank-one kernel

  deg P<=a, deg Q<=H-a,
  P(s)=A_s Q(s), 0<=s<=H,

written in the integral Newton/binomial bases. Put n=3H+1 and

  G_{H,A}(n)=gcd_{0<=a<=A} P_{H,a}(n).

Construct one explicit integer presentation matrix/module whose first
Fitting ideal, last Smith factor, or determinantal divisor is exactly
(G_{H,A}(n)), up to factors supported on primes <=H that are irrelevant
to the candidate window. The presentation must encode all nested degree
cuts at once, not multiply adjacent Padé identities. Then do one of:

1. derive an exact formula or divisibility bound for its large-prime
   determinantal divisor that gains with A and could imply

     log rad_{2H<p<=3H+1} G_{H,A}(3H+1)=o(H)

   when A is at least C H^(2/3); or

2. give a rigorous saturation/no-go theorem by constructing compatible
   integer data with the same universal interpolation/Smith structure
   for which this radical has linear weight.

Track primitive normalization locally at every p>H. In particular,
explain whether the gcd of the individually primitive kernel generators
is itself a determinantal divisor of a single block matrix, and identify
the exact minors. Explore compound matrices, nested Schur complements,
subresultant chains, exterior powers, and Fitting ideals, but return
actual matrices and proved identities rather than labels. Test any
claimed identity on small H using exact arithmetic. Do not merely repeat
the zero-absorber support equivalence, and do not cite a standard Smith
lemma without matching all dimensions and saturation indices.

Record each failed construction and the exact factor or index that makes
it fail. Put formulas in fenced plain-text blocks rather than Notion
equation blocks.
