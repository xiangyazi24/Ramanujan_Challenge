Ramanujan Challenge Problem 3.2, paper proof only. Read the latest
xiangyazi24/Ramanujan_Challenge main branch, Q850, Q830, and working-note
Sections 147--156. Independently attack the candidate-window support of the
primitive a=1 Padé numerator.

Definitions:

  U_H=sum_{s=0}^H (-1)^s binom(H,s)/A_s,
  L_H=lcm(A_0,...,A_H),
  X_H=L_H U_H,
  Y_H=L_H H(U_H-U_{H-1}),
  h_H=gcd(X_H,Y_H),
  z_H(x)=(Y_H-X_H x)/h_H,
  m_H=lcm_s A_s/gcd(A_s,z_H(s)),
  P_H(x)=m_H z_H(x),
  n=3H+1.

Every direct q=1 target prime lies in

  rad_{2H<p<=n} gcd(P_H(n),binom(n,H+1)).

Try to control this radical directly by reducing the reciprocal moments
modulo a moving candidate prime p=n-j. The obvious reduction of U_H mod p
breaks when p divides one or more A_s; handle those singular nodes
explicitly rather than dividing by zero. Seek a p-integral renormalization,
residue/partial-fraction identity, or exact congruence for the projective
line [X_H:Y_H] mod p. Use that p>2H and z_H is linear. The desired result is
an o(H) logarithmic bound for all candidate primes dividing P_H(n), but a
proof-grade strict reduction or a sharp no-go theorem is acceptable.

Do not repeat Q850's primitive-index lower-bound problem: total height is
irrelevant here. Do not assume zero sets of A_s mod p are independent.
Give exact lemmas, prove them, test delicate congruences on the repository's
code, and clearly mark anything still conjectural.
