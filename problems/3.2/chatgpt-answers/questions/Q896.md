Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Follow Q890 and read latest main.
Replace the entire Padé tail by the fixed pair

  F_H(x)=sum_{k=0}^H Delta^k A_0 binom(x,k),
  N_H(x)=product_{s=0}^H (x-s).

For every p>H, F_H(s)=A_s at the nodes and therefore

  gcd(F_H,N_H) over F_p
   = product_{0<=s<=H, p|A_s}(x-s).

For n=3H+1 and candidate p=n-j>2H, the target condition is exactly that
this modular gcd vanishes at n (equivalently j).

Develop the full integer subresultant chain of the fixed pair F_H,N_H.
Determine whether the varying statement

  first nonzero subresultant modulo p, evaluated at n, is zero

can be encoded by one explicit Fitting/determinantal ideal or a short list
of integer evaluations whose candidate-window radical has subexponential
height. Compare it exactly with the growing Padé family gcd: prove whether
one presentation strictly reduces the other, including all principal
subresultant coefficients and primitive contents.

Ordinary resultants are already ruled out because they contain every
candidate prime having any prefix zero and forget the marked node. Do not
repeat that. A valid advance must retain the evaluation at n through the
varying gcd degree and give an actual height bound, or prove a sharp
equivalence/no-go showing that the fixed subresultant Fitting ideal is
exactly the original marked-root core. Use the Apéry recurrence if it
simplifies F_H coefficients. Test all identities exactly in small H and
record failure mechanisms. Put formulas in fenced plain-text blocks rather
than Notion equation blocks.
