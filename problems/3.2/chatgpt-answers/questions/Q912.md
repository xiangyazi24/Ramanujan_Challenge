Ramanujan Challenge Problem 3.2, direct q=1 branch. Repair and strengthen the failed hit-adaptive Siegel idea using EXACT Apéry interpolation, not modular dimension counting.

Let n=3H+1, let J be a hypothetical set of target nodes j with p_j=n-j prime, p_j>2H, and p_j|A_j, and let R_J=prod_{j in J}p_j. For every integral binomial-basis polynomial P of degree L<min p_j, the congruence lattice collapses exactly:

    [p_j | P(j) for every j in J] iff R_J | P(n).

Since evaluation at n is surjective and its kernel has rank L, every nonzero evaluation already has absolute value at least R_J. Thus Minkowski/Siegel alone is circular no matter how large L is.

The only possible escape is to use the exact equations

    P(s)=A_s Q(s),  0 <= s <= H,

the Apéry recurrence, and the fact that the selected nodes j themselves satisfy p_j|A_j. Investigate whether the affine/module of exact Newton-Pade solutions contains a vector with P(n) nonzero and provably much smaller than the generic determinant height, after primitive Smith/content normalization. You may let the construction depend symmetrically on J, but you may not insert R_J into coefficients or assume a bound on R_J.

Try explicit divided differences, Hermite interpolation with vanishing at J, recurrence telescoping, adjugate/Smith duality, or a linear functional annihilating the evaluation-zero submodule. Determine the exact quotient of the Pade solution lattice by P(n)=0 and its covolume/content. If that quotient is canonically generated by the original fixed Newton carrier F_H(n), prove the equivalence/no-go. If an Apéry-specific cancellation gives a smaller generator, derive it and bound its candidate-window prime support.

Do not return generic suggestions. Give a theorem-grade derivation, an exact obstruction if it fails, and the weakest concrete new identity that would reopen the route. Put every essential formula also in fenced plain text.
