Investigate analytic continuation of F(z)=sum z^s/A_s and whether its singularities suffice for strong binomial-transform asymptotics; do not assume D-finiteness of the reciprocal. Either prove a positive exponential lower bound for Phat, construct a zero-rate subsequence, or state one precise weakest analytic/nonvanishing lemma and prove all reductions to it.
