lem:clique: A a set of occurrences of one color inside an interval of at most 2H consecutive positions, m=|A|, E_H(A)=\sum_{u<v\in A, v-u\le H} (v-u)^{-2}; then \binom{m}{3} \le 11 H^2 E_H(A).
