## Exact submitted text before delivery instructions

Ramanujan Challenge Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read the latest
main branch of xiangyazi24/Ramanujan_Challenge, especially Q802, Q850, and
working-note Sections 147--156.

The fixed a=1 primitive numerator P_H^prim at n=3H+1 contains every direct
q=1 target prime, but has enormous total height. Experiments suggest its
candidate-window support may nevertheless be sparse. Attack this using a
second certificate, not total-height estimates.

Construct the most useful second integral Padé/interpolation numerator
R_H whose value at n is also divisible by every target prime, while its
projective direction is genuinely independent of the a=1 line. Derive an
exact identity/resultant for

  rad_{2H<p<=3H+1} gcd(P_H^prim(n),R_H(n),binom(n,H+1)).

Q802 already shows that an adjacent pair equals the original target core
times pollution supported on kappa_b, so merely restating that identity is
not progress. The goal is to exploit the special a=1 formula, a carefully
chosen nonadjacent degree, derivative, shifted cutoff, or recurrence
companion so that the pollution determinant has provably o(H)
candidate-window radical, or else prove that all such two-certificate
choices necessarily retain an obstruction equivalent to the original
core.

Work with exact primitive normalizations and p>2H. Distinguish target primes
from good-prime pollution. Give a proof-grade identity and explicit height
or support calculation; record exact reasons for failure rather than a
generic no-go discussion.
