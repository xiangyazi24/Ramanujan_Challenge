A1. Claim: [WALL] => row variance. [WALL]: for ALL complex weights u,v on I,J: sum_a |nu_{u,v}(a) - W/p|^2 <= C X^eps E_{u,v}, E_{u,v}=sum_{(r,h)} |u_r v_h|^2. Our derivation: fix v_h=1 for all h (a legal weight choice). Then E_{u,1} = sum_r |u_r|^2 |I|. The a=0 summand of the left side gives, for every u: |sum_r u_r (d_I(r)-q_I(r)/p)|^2 <= C X^eps |I| sum_r |u_r|^2. This holds for ALL u, and the left side is |<u, z>|^2 with z_r = d_I(r)-q_I(r)/p; taking sup over ||u||_2=1 gives ||z||_2^2 <= C X^eps |I|. The audit objected: "dualizing requires operator norm, not Hilbert-Schmidt; accept an extra factor |J|". Who is right? Note the quantifier: [WALL] holds for every u, so sup over u is available. Is there any gap (e.g., complex phases, the a=0 extraction, the X^eps dependence on |J| through X)?

A2. Claim: [NEAR-WALL_eta] substitutes into the box/master machinery.
