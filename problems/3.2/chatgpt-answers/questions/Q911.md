Ramanujan Challenge Problem 3.2, direct q=1 branch. Work theorem-grade and audit every quantifier. Let

    A_m = sum_{r=0}^m binom(m,r)^2 binom(m+r,r)^2
    n = 3H+1
    z_p(H) = #{0 <= s <= H : p | A_s}.

The remaining target is

    sum_{2H < p <= 3H+1, p | A_{3H+1-p}} log p = o(H).

The growing Newton-Pade family shows that a candidate prime with z_p(H) <= b is removed once numerator degrees through b are used. Therefore a potentially decisive missing estimate is a GROWING tail bound: find an explicit B(H) -> infinity, slow enough that the first B(H) primitive Pade numerators have controllable aggregate height, but fast enough that

    sum_{2H < p <= 3H+1, z_p(H) > B(H)} log p = o(H).

Attack this estimate unconditionally. Do not propose the fixed threshold z>=4: complete data through p=2,000,000 gives total log(p)/H around 0.0008 at H=400000 and 600000, consistent with a small positive-density Poisson tail. Use the exact Apéry recurrence, continuants/Casoratians, discriminants, factorial carriers, zero-spacing, p-adic multiplicity, or a double count across p and H. Carefully distinguish:

1. a per-prime bound z_p(H)=O(p^(2/3)), which is far too weak;
2. an average over all roots of a recurrence, which may not control the distinguished Apéry solution;
3. finite-data or random heuristics, which are not proofs;
4. the moving target node, which may allow a stronger target-specific tail than the unmarked tail.

Try both the unmarked tail and the target-conditioned tail

    p | A_{3H+1-p}, z_p(H)>B(H).

Derive the strongest valid weighted inequality with all constants/ranges. If no known identity closes it, give an exact obstruction or adversarial model satisfying every identity you actually used, and state the weakest new lemma sufficient to finish. Also optimize B(H) against the known primitive Newton-Pade content formula: for p>H, the content valuation in degree a is the (a+1)-st largest valuation among A_0,...,A_H. Do not merely restate the desired o(H) estimate.

Put every essential formula also in fenced plain text. Return a proof or a precise failure ledger, not a research-program list.
