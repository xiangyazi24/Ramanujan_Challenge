Problem 3.2 reciprocal-Apery boundary, use a newly identified primary source. Let A(z)=sum_{k>=0} binom(z,k)^2 binom(z+k,k)^2 be the entire Golyshev-Zagier interpolation of the Apery numbers; it satisfies A(z)=A(-z-1) and an explicit inhomogeneous recurrence. Put U_H=sum_{s=0}^H (-1)^s binom(H,s)/A_s. For the fixed numerator-degree a=1 Pade pair, B_0=U_H, B_1=H(U_H-U_{H-1}), the primitive numerator line has root H(1-U_{H-1}/U_H), and the projective value can be written using U_H,U_{H-1},U_{H-2}. Develop a rigorous Norlund-Rice analysis using f(z)=1/A(z). Numerically we independently found simple zeros of A(z) at z approximately 0.14598677311801086651 plus or minus 0.58242614024485315072 i, together with symmetry partners. Verify them at high precision, locate all zeros with maximal real part in a rigorous or certifiable strip, compute residues and the resulting oscillatory power asymptotic for U_H, and bound the shifted-contour remainder using the actual growth of the entire interpolation. Determine whether this proves an eventual or all-H exponential lower bound for the primitive a=1 numerator at n=3H+1, including the sparse H near cosine zeros. Use Golyshev-Zagier Interpolated Apery Numbers explicitly and audit the growth hypotheses of the Rice integral. If full zero-free certification is unavailable, state the exact zero-free strip/residue theorem needed and prove every reduction to it. Do not confuse the ordinary generating function sum z^s/A_s with the complex-index interpolation A(z).
