Let (P_{H,a},Q_{H,a}) be the primitive integral Padé interpolation pair

  deg P_{H,a}<=a, deg Q_{H,a}<=H-a,
  P_{H,a}(s)=A_s Q_{H,a}(s), 0<=s<=H.

For every prime p>H, with Z_p(H)={s<=H:p|A_s}, reduction modulo p gives

  P_{H,a}=0 polynomial  iff |Z_p(H)|>a,

and when a=|Z_p(H)| its interpolation-node roots are exactly Z_p(H).
Therefore, for n=3H+1 and

  G_{H,A}(n)=gcd_{0<=a<=A} P_{H,a}(n),

every candidate prime p=n-j>2H satisfies

  p|G_{H,A}(n) iff (j in Z_p(H)) or (|Z_p(H)|>A).

Using the proved vertical bound |Z_p|<<p^(2/3), a growing but sublinear
A=O(H^(2/3)) removes the second alternative, so the candidate-window
support of G_{H,A}(n) is exactly the direct q=1 target.

Attack the remaining global step: prove

  log rad_{2H<p<=3H+1} G_{H,A}(3H+1)=o(H)

for some A>=C H^(2/3), or reduce it to a strictly smaller explicit
determinantal-divisor lemma. Exploit the entire growing family at once:
Smith/Fitting ideals of the nested Padé kernels, compound matrices,
subresultant chains, Euclidean combinations across a, or a geometry-of-
numbers bound whose gain grows with A. Do not multiply adjacent identities:

restate that the support equals the original target. A valid advance needs

whole family, or a rigorous no-go construction showing that universal
interpolation/Smith data cannot provide it. Track primitive normalization
