Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read the latest main branch of
xiangyazi24/Ramanujan_Challenge, especially Q850 and working-note Sections
147--156. Q850 proves the exact primitive a=1 formula

  z_H(x)=(Y_H-X_H x)/gcd(X_H,Y_H),
  m_H=lcm_{0<=s<=H} A_s/gcd(A_s,z_H(s)),
  P_H^prim(x)=m_H z_H(x).

At n=3H+1 every target prime p=n-j, 0<=j<=H, p|A_j divides
P_H^prim(n). The total size of P_H^prim(n) is quadratic-exponential and
is useless, but exact experiments show that its support in the candidate
window 2H<p<=3H+1 is extremely sparse.

Attack the genuinely sufficient new statement

  log rad_{2H<p<=3H+1} gcd(P_H^prim(3H+1), binom(3H+1,H+1)) = o(H).

This would imply the direct q=1 theorem immediately. Derive an exact local
classification of a candidate prime in this radical using P=mz. Separate
the cases p|m_H and p|z_H(3H+1), and within p|m_H use

  v_p(m_H)=max_s max(0,v_p(A_s)-v_p(z_H(s))).

Exploit p>2H, the fact that a primitive linear z has at most one root mod
p unless both coefficients vanish, Apéry Lucas/Dwork congruences, and the
special relation p=3H+1-j. Determine whether the window-radical statement
can be proved, weakened enough to remain o(H), or rigorously reduced to one
strictly smaller lemma. Do not give a generic theorem survey and do not
confuse a lower bound on m_H with the required upper bound on its
candidate-window prime support. Audit every claimed implication. If the
route fails, exhibit the exact obstruction and a counterexample to the
strongest false intermediate claim.
