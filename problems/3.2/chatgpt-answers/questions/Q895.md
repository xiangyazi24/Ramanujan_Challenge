## Exact submitted text before delivery instructions

Problem 3.2, paper proof only, no Lean. Read latest main commit 1113361,
especially the all-degree content theorem in Section 163.

For the primitive Padé numerator P_{H,a} and every p>H,

  v_p(content(P_{H,a}))=e_(a+1),

where e_(a+1) is the (a+1)-st largest v_p(A_s). Define the explicit node
order-statistic integer

  D_{H,a}
   = lcm over |I|=a+1 of gcd(A_s : s in I).

Then v_p(D_{H,a})=e_(a+1), so content(P_{H,a})/D_{H,a} differs only at
primes <=H (with the quotient direction interpreted valuation by
valuation).

Exploit this explicit normalization. First derive the exact global
divisibility relation between D_{H,a} and content(P_{H,a}), including the
small-prime correction. Construct an integral normalization whose p>H
part is exactly P_{H,a}/D_{H,a}. Then test whether cross-degree
determinants, recurrence identities, or simultaneous Bezout relations
among these normalized numerators have saturation quotients supported
only on primes <=H.

The goal is an Apéry-specific integer divisible by every direct target
prime but with logarithmic height o(H), or a strictly smaller explicit
large-prime saturation lemma. Generic interpolation is ruled out by
Q885's CRT model; use the actual gcd structure of the Apéry values and
their recurrence. If the D-normalization does not help, identify the first
large prime or exact valuation where cross-degree saturation reappears and
prove the failure. Include exact small computations, but do not infer an
asymptotic from them. Put formulas in fenced plain-text blocks rather than
Notion equation blocks.
