  A=ceil(H^(2/3)),

nontrivial-target data and an exact structural conjecture, not another
small-range heuristic.

primitive normalized values P_{H,a}(3H+1), avoiding a separate large
cofactor expansion for every a. Compute G at least through the first target
heights and, if feasible, through H=200 or more. Verify primitive
normalization, factor G, separate primes <=H, H<p<=2H, and 2H<p<=3H+1,
and check the zero-absorber support exactly.

terms of A_{3H+1}, binom(3H+1,H+1), Smith factors, subresultants, or a
short recurrence in H. Search for a reproducible invariant that explains
the collapse. Finite decay is not a proof: return either a symbolic lemma
