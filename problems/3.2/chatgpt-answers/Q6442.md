# A residual local-limit / phase-modulus program for Apéry Mellin traces

## Executive conclusion

The missing theory should not be formulated as another equidistribution theorem for characteristic-zero Frobenius conjugacy classes. It should be a rare-event local-limit theory for an exact residual object, with resolution (1/p), and with enough quantitative uniformity to control moments whose order grows slowly with (p).

I see three serious candidate objects:

1. Transfer-cocycle phase modulus: encode the Apéry recurrence as an exact rational (mathrm{SL}_2(mathbf F_p))-cocycle on (mathbf P^1(mathbf F_p)). Zeros are visits to one projective point. This is the best candidate for a first theorem within months.

1. Gross–Koblitz critical phase cloud: retain, before summation, the minimal-valuation Gauss-sum terms together with their residual (p)-adic Gamma phases. This exactly determines (b_rmod p), but there is no scalar “modulus” whose vanishing controls the answer; the missing theorem is decorrelation of a marked unit-phase cloud.

1. Framed residual crystalline Mellin display: retain the integral Mellin cohomology and its divided Frobenius modulo (p), not merely its Hodge polygon, Newton polygon, or unframed (F)-zip. This is the most canonical long-term object, but it requires the largest amount of new theory.

My research-priority ranking is therefore

[

oxed{	ext{transfer cocycle} ;>; 	ext{Gross–Koblitz phase cloud} ;>; 	ext{residual crystalline display}.}

]

The first concrete target should be a codegree/flattening lemma for the one-step Apéry transfer family. It is explicit enough to test immediately, is plausibly accessible by an absolute-irreducibility classification plus Weil bounds, and would give a rigorous Poisson law for an annealed random-matrix model. The genuinely new and hardest bridge would then be a quenched ordered-cocycle theorem transferring that law to the deterministic word (0,1,ldots,p-2).

## Least-confident step

The least-confident step is [GAP-QUENCHED-ORDER]. Even if the one-step codegree lemma yields an excellent spectral gap for the annealed kernel, the deterministic sequence of increments (0,1,ldots,p-2) lies on one rigid algebraic word and is not sampled from that kernel. There is presently no justified principle saying that expansion of the averaged family forces rare-event independence along this ordered word. The codegree lemma is therefore the right first experiment and theorem, but the ordered-cocycle transference—not random-matrix mixing itself—is the true phase-modulus barrier.
