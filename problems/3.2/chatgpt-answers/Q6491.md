# 1. Expansion and spectral gap in SL_2(\mathbf F_p)

The Bourgain–Gamburd machine proves expansion for Cayley graphs of SL_2(\mathbf F_p) generated by a fixed finite set S satisfying:

1. S generates a Zariski-dense subgroup;

1. the group has a product-growth property;

1. the random walk uses the same step distribution at every time.

# 5. Final verdict

## Ranking

### 1. Holonomy/algebraic correspondence — best

The reason is that the color event is genuinely a statement about a rank-two transfer object. The right theorem is a new geometric equidistribution theorem for a nonautonomous SL_2 cocycle.

First attack:

Construct the two-parameter Apéry transfer sheaf and prove its geometric monodromy is large.

### 2. Second moment / dispersion — second best

It avoids pointwise mixing and only asks for aggregate cancellation.

First attack:

Prove the colored BDH variance bound above.

### 3. Expansion — unlikely but transformative

It would solve the problem immediately, but current expansion theory does not cover deterministic time-dependent generators.

First attack:

Prove a replacement theorem for polynomially varying generators in SL_2(\mathbf F_p).

### 4. Polynomial dynamics — least promising

The Apéry system is not an iteration of a fixed map, so the existing orbit machinery is structurally mismatched.
