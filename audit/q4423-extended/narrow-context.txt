560-

The cited Breuillard–Varjú cutoff result likewise has i.i.d. noise.
562-
563-### 2. Is Bourgain–Gamburd non-concentration automatic for a polynomial cocycle?
564-
565-**No.** Generation is not a spectral gap, and polynomiality is not escape from subgroups. In the corrected \(\mathrm{SL}_2\) gauge, every one-step Apéry matrix sends \([0:1]\) to \([1:0]\), so all one-step matrices lie in one Borel coset and all pairwise differences lie in a proper Borel subgroup. 568-
